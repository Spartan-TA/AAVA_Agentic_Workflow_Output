Title: Safety Incident - Record Incident

User Story: As a SUPERVISOR I want to record safety incidents with all required details So that investigations can be tracked.

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Record incident
Given an incident occurs
When I POST to /safety/incidents with severity, location, description, and involved employees
Then the incident is logged and status is Open

Additional Details:
- Priority: High
- Story Points: 2
- Dependencies: E02, E03
- Notes: Validation on required fields

Title: Safety Incident - Investigation Workflow

User Story: As a SAFETY OFFICER I want to manage the investigation workflow for incidents So that corrective actions are documented.

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Advance incident status
Given an incident is Open
When I update the status to Investigating or Resolved
Then the workflow progresses and actions are logged

Additional Details:
- Priority: High
- Story Points: 3
- Dependencies: E02, E03
- Notes: Status history is auditable

Additional Details:
- Priority: High
- Story Points: 2
- Dependencies: E02, E03
