Title: Safety Incident Reporting

User Story: As a warehouse employee I want to report safety incidents So that hazards are documented and corrective actions are tracked

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Incident reporting
Given I witness a safety incident
When I submit a report through the system
Then the incident is logged and notifications are sent to safety officers

Additional Details:
- Priority: High
- Story Points: 8
- Dependencies: Notification module, reporting module
- Notes: Support for photo attachments and follow-up tracking
