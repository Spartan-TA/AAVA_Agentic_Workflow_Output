Title: RBAC - Row-Level Security
User Story: As a supervisor, I want to access only my team's employee records so that privacy and data protection are enforced.
Acceptance Criteria:
  - Given a SUPERVISOR user, When they GET /employees, Then only employees in their team are returned.
  - Given a WORKER user, When they access employee data, Then only their own record is visible.
Additional Details:
  Priority: High
  Story Points: 2
  Dependencies: RBAC
  Notes: Row-level constraints.
