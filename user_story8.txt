Title: Safety Incidents & OSHA Reporting

User Story: As a safety officer I want to record and manage safety incidents and near-misses so that we can improve safety culture and meet regulatory reporting requirements.

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Incident recording and workflow
  Given an incident occurs in the warehouse
  When I record the incident with severity, location, description, and involved employees
  Then the status workflow (Open, Investigating, Resolved) is followed, OSHA 300/300A fields are exportable, and metrics dashboard endpoints are available

Additional Details:
- Priority: High
- Story Points: 7
- Dependencies: Employee Master Data (CRUD), Role Based Access Control (RBAC)
- Notes: Validation required for incident data; workflow must be auditable.
