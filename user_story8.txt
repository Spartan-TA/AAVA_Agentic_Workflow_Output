Title: Safety Incidents & OSHA Reporting

User Story: As a safety officer I want to record and manage safety incidents So that investigations and regulatory reporting are streamlined and compliance is ensured

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Record safety incident
Given an incident occurs
When I submit incident details (severity, location, description, involved employees)
Then the incident is logged and status workflow is initiated

Scenario: Investigate incident
Given an incident is open
When I update the status to Investigating
Then corrective actions can be tracked and documented

Scenario: Export OSHA summary
Given incidents are recorded
When I request an OSHA 300/300A export
Then I receive a report with required fields

Scenario: Metrics dashboard
Given incidents exist
When I access the dashboard endpoint
Then I see safety KPIs and incident metrics

Additional Details:
- Priority: High
- Story Points: 8
- Dependencies: Employee Master Data CRUD APIs, Role Based Access Control (RBAC)
- Notes: Status workflow (Open, Investigating, Resolved); export OSHA fields; metrics dashboard endpoints.
