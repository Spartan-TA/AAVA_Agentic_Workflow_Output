Title: Safety Incidents & OSHA Reporting

User Story: As a safety officer I want to record incidents and generate OSHA reports So that we improve safety culture and meet regulatory requirements.

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Incident recording
Given an incident occurs
When I submit details via the safety module
Then the incident is logged with severity, location, and involved employees

Scenario: OSHA report export
Given incidents are recorded
When I request an OSHA summary
Then the system exports OSHA 300/300A fields

Additional Details:
- Priority: High
- Story Points: 8
- Dependencies: E02, E03
- Notes: Status workflow (Open, Investigating, Resolved); metrics dashboard endpoints.
