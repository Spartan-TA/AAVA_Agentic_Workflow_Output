Title: Safety Incidents & OSHA Reporting
User Story: As a safety officer I want to record incidents and generate OSHA reports so that safety culture is improved and regulatory requirements are met.
Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Incident recording and OSHA reporting
Given an incident occurs in the warehouse
When I record the incident with severity, location, description, and involved employees
Then the status workflow (Open, Investigating, Resolved) is tracked, OSHA 300/300A fields are exportable, and metrics dashboard endpoints are available.
Additional Details:
- Priority: High
- Story Points: 8
- Dependencies: Employee Master Data (CRUD), Role Based Access Control (RBAC)
- Notes: Workflow for investigation and corrective actions.
