### E08: Safety Incidents & OSHA Reporting

**User Story 1**
Title: Safety Incident Recording

User Story: As a SUPERVISOR I want to record safety incidents and near-misses So that safety data is tracked for compliance.

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Record Incident
Given incident details
When I POST to /safety/incidents
Then the incident is recorded with validation

Additional Details:
- Priority: High
- Story Points: 2
- Dependencies: E02, E03
- Notes: Severity, location, description required

---

**User Story 2**
Title: Incident Investigation Workflow

User Story: As a SAFETY OFFICER I want to manage investigation and corrective actions for incidents So that root causes are addressed.

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Incident Workflow
Given a recorded incident
When I update status to Investigating or Resolved
Then the workflow progresses and actions are logged

Additional Details:
- Priority: High
- Story Points: 2
- Dependencies: Story 1
- Notes: Status transitions: Open, Investigating, Resolved

---

**User Story 3**
Title: OSHA Report Generation

User Story: As an ADMIN I want to export OSHA 300/300A summary reports So that regulatory requirements are met.

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: OSHA Export
Given incident data
When I request an OSHA report
Then the export includes required fields

Additional Details:
- Priority: High
- Story Points: 2
- Dependencies: Story 1, Story 2
- Notes: Export format matches OSHA standards

---

**User Story 4**
Title: Safety Metrics Dashboard

User Story: As a SUPERVISOR I want to view safety KPIs on a dashboard So that trends and risks are visible.

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: View Safety Dashboard
Given incident data
When I access the dashboard endpoint
Then safety metrics are displayed

Additional Details:
- Priority: High
- Story Points: 1
- Dependencies: Story 1
- Notes: Metrics include incident rates, severity, resolution time
