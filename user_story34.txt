Title: Certification Tracking - Status Visibility and Proof Upload
User Story: As an employee, I want to view my certification status and upload proof documents so that my qualifications are visible and verifiable.
Acceptance Criteria:
  Given I am authenticated as an employee
  When I view my profile
  Then I see all certifications with status and can upload proof documents
Additional Details:
  Priority: High
  Story Points: 5
  Dependencies: E02, E03
  Notes: Uploaded documents must be securely stored and linked to certification records.
