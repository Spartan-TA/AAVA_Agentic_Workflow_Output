Title: Alerts for Expiring Certifications and Scheduling Checks

User Story: As a supervisor I want to receive alerts for certifications expiring soon and have the system block scheduling of unqualified employees so that only certified staff are assigned to sensitive tasks.

Acceptance Criteria:

Scenario: Alert for expiring certification
Given a certification is expiring in 30 or 7 days
When the date approaches
Then the system sends alerts to the employee and supervisor

Scenario: Scheduling check blocks unqualified assignment
Given an employee's certification is expired
When I attempt to assign them to a restricted shift or task
Then the system prevents the assignment

Scenario: Certification status visible on schedule
Given a scheduled shift
When I view the assignment
Then the certification status is visible to the supervisor

Additional Details:
- Priority: High
- Story Points: 3
- Dependencies: E02, E03
- Notes: Prevents safety incidents and regulatory violations.
