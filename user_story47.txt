Title: Review Cycles and Assignment to Employees

User Story: As an HR manager I want to create quarterly and annual review cycles and assign them to employees so that performance reviews are conducted on schedule.

Acceptance Criteria:

Scenario: Create review cycle
Given review period details
When I POST to /reviews/cycles
Then a new review cycle is created

Scenario: Assign review to employee
Given a review cycle and employee
When I POST to /reviews/assign
Then the review is assigned to the employee and supervisor

Scenario: Review cycle visible to employee and supervisor
Given a review is assigned
When the employee or supervisor logs in
Then the review is visible in their dashboard

Additional Details:
- Priority: Medium
- Story Points: 3
- Dependencies: E02, E03
- Notes: Ensures timely and consistent performance management.
