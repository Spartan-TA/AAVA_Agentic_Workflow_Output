Title: Performance Reviews - Assign Review Cycle and Workflow
User Story: As a supervisor, I want to assign review cycles to employees and manage the submission/acknowledgement workflow so that performance reviews are completed and signed off.
Acceptance Criteria:
  Given I am authenticated as a supervisor
  When I assign a review cycle and employees submit their reviews
  Then the workflow tracks submission, acknowledgement, and sign-off
Additional Details:
  Priority: Medium
  Story Points: 8
  Dependencies: E02, E03
  Notes: History must be immutable after sign-off and visible to both supervisor and employee.
