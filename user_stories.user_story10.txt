Title: Clock In/Out Event Capture

User Story: As a warehouse worker I want to clock in and out of my shift So that my worked hours are accurately recorded for payroll

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Clock In/Out
Given I am scheduled for a shift
When I clock in or out using the system
Then my attendance event is recorded with timestamp and device info

Additional Details:
- Priority: High
- Story Points: 3
- Dependencies: Employee CRUD, RBAC
- Notes: Optionally capture geofence and device details; validate shift association.
