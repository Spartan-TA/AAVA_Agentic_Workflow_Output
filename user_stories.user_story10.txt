Title: Performance Reviews & Goals

User Story: As a supervisor I want to create and manage performance review cycles and goals so that structured feedback and productivity improvements are achieved.

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Review cycle management
Given a new review cycle is initiated
When I assign reviews to employees and submit ratings and comments
Then the workflow supports supervisor/employee acknowledgements, PDF export, role-based visibility, and immutable history after sign-off.

Additional Details:
- Priority: Medium
- Story Points: 5
- Dependencies: Employee Master Data (E02), Role Based Access Control (E03)
- Notes: Support quarterly/annual templates and competencies tracking.
