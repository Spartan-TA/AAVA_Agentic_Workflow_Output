Title: Track Employee Certifications and Expirations

User Story: As an HR manager I want to record required certifications for employees and track their expiration dates so that only qualified staff are assigned to sensitive roles.

Acceptance Criteria:

Scenario: Add certification to employee
Given an employee profile
When I POST a certification record
Then the certification and expiration date are saved for the employee

Scenario: Certification expiration tracked
Given a certification with an expiration date
When the date approaches (30/7 days before)
Then the system sends alerts to the employee and supervisor

Scenario: Expired certifications block assignments
Given a certification is expired
When I attempt to assign the employee to a restricted task
Then the system prevents the assignment

Additional Details:
- Priority: High
- Story Points: 5
- Dependencies: E02, E03
- Notes: Ensures compliance and workplace safety.
