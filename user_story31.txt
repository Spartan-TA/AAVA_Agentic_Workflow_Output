Title: Certification Tracking - CRUD for Certifications
User Story: As an HR manager, I want to create, update, and delete employee certifications so that compliance and training requirements are tracked accurately.
Acceptance Criteria:
  Given I am authenticated as an HR manager
  When I manage certifications for an employee
  Then I can add, update, or remove certification records
Additional Details:
  Priority: High
  Story Points: 5
  Dependencies: E02, E03
  Notes: Certification records must include type, issue/expiry dates, and status.
