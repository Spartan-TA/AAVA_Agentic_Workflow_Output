Title: Asset Assignment - Overdue Return Reports
User Story: As an asset manager, I want to generate reports of overdue asset returns so that I can follow up and ensure all equipment is accounted for.
Acceptance Criteria:
  Given assets are checked out
  When the return date is past due
  Then the system includes the asset in the overdue report with employee and due date details
Additional Details:
  Priority: Medium
  Story Points: 5
  Dependencies: E02, E03, E07
  Notes: Overdue report must be exportable and filterable by asset type and employee.
