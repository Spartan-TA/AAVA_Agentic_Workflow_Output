Title: Training Module Assignment

User Story: As a warehouse supervisor, I want to assign mandatory training modules to new employees so that they are prepared for their roles.

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Assigning training modules
Given a list of available training modules
When the supervisor selects modules for an employee
Then the employee is notified and their completion status is tracked

Additional Details:
- Priority: High
- Story Points: 3
- Dependencies: Training content repository, notification system
- Notes: Training modules may include forklift operation, safety compliance, and inventory handling
