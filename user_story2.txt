Title: Employee Master Data (CRUD)
User Story: As an HR manager I want to manage employee records with CRUD operations so that the warehouse has a single source of truth for employee data.
Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Employee CRUD operations
Given I have access to the employee management API
When I POST, GET, PUT, PATCH, or DELETE employee records
Then badgeId is unique, soft-delete is supported, pagination and filtering are available, and OpenAPI schemas are documented with examples.
Additional Details:
- Priority: High
- Story Points: 8
- Dependencies: Project Scaffolding & Domain Setup
- Notes: Ensure department, role, shiftGroup, and status fields are validated.
