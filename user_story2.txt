Title: User Login - Email and Password

User Story Narrative:
As a registered user, I want to log in using my email and password so that I can securely access my account.

Acceptance Criteria (Gherkin):
Given I am on the login page,
When I enter a valid email and password,
Then I am logged into my account,
And I am redirected to the dashboard.

Additional Details:
- Priority: High
- Story Points: 2
- Dependencies: User Registration
- Notes: Lock account after 5 failed attempts.
