Title: Add Product to Shopping Cart

User Story: As a registered customer I want to add products to my shopping cart So that I can review and purchase multiple items in a single transaction

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Adding Available Product to Cart
Given I am logged in and viewing a product detail page for an in-stock item
When I click the "Add to Cart" button
Then the product is added to my cart and the cart icon updates to reflect the new item count

Additional Details:
- Priority: High
- Story Points: 3
- Dependencies: Product catalog module, Shopping cart service
- Notes: If the product is out of stock, the "Add to Cart" button should be disabled.
