Title: Shift & Schedule Management
User Story: As a supervisor I want to manage shift templates and assignments So that I can ensure adequate staffing and reduce conflicts
Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Schedule creation and conflict detection
Given I create or modify a schedule
When I assign shifts to employees
Then conflicts are detected/prevented, workers see their shifts, and audit entries are generated
Additional Details:
- Priority: High
- Story Points: 8
- Dependencies: Employee Master Data, RBAC, Time & Attendance
- Notes: Supports recurring templates, rotations, blackout dates
