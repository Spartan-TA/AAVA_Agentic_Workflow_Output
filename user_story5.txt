Title: Shift & Schedule Management

User Story: As a supervisor I want to create and assign shift schedules to employees So that staffing levels are maintained and scheduling conflicts are minimized

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Create shift template
Given I am a supervisor
When I define a recurring shift template
Then the template is saved and available for assignment

Scenario: Assign shifts to employees
Given a shift template exists
When I bulk-assign shifts to employees
Then the assignments are created and conflicts are detected/prevented

Scenario: View personal schedule
Given I am a worker
When I view my schedule
Then I see my upcoming shifts and blackout dates

Scenario: Audit shift assignments
Given shifts are assigned
When I review the audit log
Then I see entries for all assignments and changes

Additional Details:
- Priority: High
- Story Points: 8
- Dependencies: Employee Master Data CRUD APIs, Role Based Access Control (RBAC), Time & Attendance (Clock In/Out)
- Notes: Support recurring templates, rotations, overtime rules, blackout dates, and operation calendars.
