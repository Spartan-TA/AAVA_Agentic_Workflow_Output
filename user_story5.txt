Title: Shift & Schedule Management

User Story: As a supervisor I want to create and assign recurring shift templates and manage blackout dates So that staffing is optimized and scheduling conflicts are minimized.

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Shift template creation
Given I am a supervisor
When I create a new shift template
Then the template is saved and available for assignment

Scenario: Conflict detection
Given overlapping shifts are assigned
When I save the schedule
Then the system detects and prevents conflicts

Additional Details:
- Priority: High
- Story Points: 13
- Dependencies: E02, E03, E04
- Notes: Bulk-assign capability; audit entries generated.
