Title: Shift & Schedule Management
User Story: As a supervisor I want to create and manage shift templates, rotations, and assignments so that warehouse staffing is adequate and scheduling conflicts are minimized.
Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Shift and schedule management
Given I have access to the scheduling module
When I create, update, or assign shifts
Then conflicts are detected and prevented, workers see their upcoming shifts, supervisors can bulk-assign, and audit entries are generated.
Additional Details:
- Priority: High
- Story Points: 8
- Dependencies: Employee Master Data (CRUD), Role Based Access Control (RBAC), Time & Attendance (Clock In/Out)
- Notes: Handle blackout dates and warehouse operation calendars.
