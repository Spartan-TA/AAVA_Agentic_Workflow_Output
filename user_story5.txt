### E05: Shift & Schedule Management

**User Story 1**
Title: Shift Template Creation

User Story: As an HR I want to create recurring shift templates So that scheduling is efficient and consistent.

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Create Shift Template
Given valid shift details
When I POST to /shifts/templates
Then a new shift template is created

Additional Details:
- Priority: High
- Story Points: 3
- Dependencies: E02, E03, E04
- Notes: Supports rotations and overtime rules

---

**User Story 2**
Title: Shift Assignment to Employees

User Story: As a SUPERVISOR I want to assign shifts to employees in bulk So that staffing is managed effectively.

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Bulk Shift Assignment
Given a list of employees
When I assign a shift template
Then all selected employees receive the assignment

Additional Details:
- Priority: High
- Story Points: 2
- Dependencies: Story 1
- Notes: Audit entries generated for assignments

---

**User Story 3**
Title: Conflict Detection

User Story: As a SYSTEM I want to detect and prevent scheduling conflicts So that employees are not double-booked.

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Conflict Prevention
Given overlapping shift assignments
When I attempt to assign a conflicting shift
Then the system blocks the assignment and notifies the user

Additional Details:
- Priority: High
- Story Points: 2
- Dependencies: Story 1, Story 2
- Notes: Blackout dates and operation calendars considered

---

**User Story 4**
Title: Personal Shift View

User Story: As a WORKER I want to view my upcoming shifts So that I can plan my schedule.

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: View Shifts
Given assigned shifts
When I access my schedule
Then my personal upcoming shifts are displayed

Additional Details:
- Priority: High
- Story Points: 1
- Dependencies: Story 2
- Notes: Mobile-friendly view required
