Title: Performance Reviews & Goals

User Story: As an HR manager I want to create and manage performance review cycles and goals so that structured feedback and employee development are supported.

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Review cycle creation and workflow
  Given a new review cycle is initiated
  When reviews are assigned to employees and supervisors
  Then the submit/acknowledge workflow is followed, PDF export is available, role-based visibility is enforced, and history is immutable after sign-off

Additional Details:
- Priority: Medium
- Story Points: 6
- Dependencies: Employee Master Data (CRUD), Role Based Access Control (RBAC)
- Notes: Templates for quarterly/annual reviews and goal tracking required.
