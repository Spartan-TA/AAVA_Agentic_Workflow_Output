Title: Performance Reviews

User Story: As a manager I want to conduct and record employee performance reviews So that feedback and development plans are documented

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Completing a review
Given I am logged in as a manager
When I submit a performance review for an employee
Then the review is stored and accessible to HR and the employee

Additional Details:
- Priority: Medium
- Story Points: 8
- Dependencies: Employee profile, HR module
- Notes: Support for review templates and goal tracking
