### E10: Performance Reviews & Goals

**User Story 1**
Title: Review Cycle Creation

User Story: As an HR I want to create quarterly and annual review cycles So that employee performance is evaluated regularly.

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Create Review Cycle
Given review template details
When I create a cycle
Then employees are assigned to the review

Additional Details:
- Priority: Medium
- Story Points: 2
- Dependencies: E02, E03
- Notes: Templates include competencies, goals

---

**User Story 2**
Title: Review Submission Workflow

User Story: As a SUPERVISOR I want to submit and acknowledge performance reviews So that feedback is documented.

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Submit Review
Given an assigned review
When I submit ratings and comments
Then the review is recorded and acknowledged by employee

Additional Details:
- Priority: Medium
- Story Points: 2
- Dependencies: Story 1
- Notes: Workflow includes supervisor and employee sign-off

---

**User Story 3**
Title: PDF Export of Reviews

User Story: As an HR I want to export completed reviews as PDFs So that records are archived and shared.

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Export Review PDF
Given a completed review
When I request a PDF export
Then the review is generated as a PDF

Additional Details:
- Priority: Medium
- Story Points: 1
- Dependencies: Story 2
- Notes: Immutable history after sign-off

---

**User Story 4**
Title: Role-Based Review Visibility

User Story: As an ADMIN I want to control visibility of reviews So that only authorized users access sensitive feedback.

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Review Access Control
Given review data
When I access reviews as different roles
Then visibility is restricted based on role

Additional Details:
- Priority: Medium
- Story Points: 1
- Dependencies: Story 1
- Notes: HR and supervisor access only
