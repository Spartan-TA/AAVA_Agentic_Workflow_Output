Title: Multi-Factor Authentication (MFA)

User Story Narrative:
As a security-conscious user, I want to enable multi-factor authentication so that my account is more secure.

Acceptance Criteria (Gherkin):
Given I am logged in,
When I enable MFA in my account settings,
Then I am prompted to set up a second authentication factor,
And I must use it on subsequent logins.

Additional Details:
- Priority: Low
- Story Points: 5
- Dependencies: User Login
- Notes: Support for authenticator apps (e.g., Google Authenticator).
