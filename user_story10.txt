Title: Performance Reviews & Goals
User Story: As a supervisor I want to create and track performance reviews and goals so that employee productivity and retention are improved through structured feedback.
Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Performance review cycle
Given a review template and employee assignment
When a review cycle is created and submitted
Then employees and supervisors can acknowledge, PDF export is available, role-based visibility is enforced, and history is immutable after sign-off.
Additional Details:
- Priority: Medium
- Story Points: 5
- Dependencies: Employee Master Data (CRUD), Role Based Access Control (RBAC)
- Notes: Track goals, competencies, ratings, and comments.
