Title: Performance Reviews & Goals

User Story: As a supervisor I want to conduct performance reviews and set goals for employees So that feedback is structured and productivity is improved

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Create review cycle
Given review templates exist
When I initiate a quarterly or annual review cycle
Then employees are assigned reviews and notified

Scenario: Submit review and acknowledge
Given a review is assigned
When supervisor and employee submit ratings and comments
Then both parties acknowledge and the review is finalized

Scenario: Export review to PDF
Given a review is completed
When I request an export
Then I receive a PDF of the review and ratings

Scenario: Immutable history after sign-off
Given a review is signed off
When I attempt to edit
Then the review remains immutable

Additional Details:
- Priority: Medium
- Story Points: 8
- Dependencies: Employee Master Data CRUD APIs, Role Based Access Control (RBAC)
- Notes: Role-based visibility; structured feedback loop; immutable history after sign-off.
