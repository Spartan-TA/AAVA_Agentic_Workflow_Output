Title: Bulk Assign Shifts to Employees

User Story: As a supervisor I want to bulk assign shifts to multiple employees So that scheduling is streamlined

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Bulk assignment Given I select multiple employees and a shift template When I assign the shift Then all selected employees are scheduled and conflicts are detected

Additional Details:
- Priority: High
- Story Points: 2
- Dependencies: Shift templates
- Notes: Conflict detection and prevention
