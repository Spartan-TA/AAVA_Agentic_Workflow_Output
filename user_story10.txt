Title: Clock In/Out Attendance

User Story: As a worker I want to clock in and out via an endpoint So that my attendance is accurately recorded

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Clock in/out event
Given I am scheduled for a shift
When I clock in/out
Then the event is recorded with timestamp and device info

Additional Details:
- Priority: High
- Story Points: 2
- Dependencies: Employee CRUD, RBAC
- Notes: Optional geofence validation
