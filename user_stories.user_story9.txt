Title: Equipment & Asset Assignment

User Story: As an asset manager I want to assign and track equipment and PPE to employees so that accountability and safe operation are ensured.

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Asset assignment and tracking
Given an asset registry and employee certifications
When I assign or check out equipment to an employee
Then the system blocks assignment if certifications are invalid, maintains asset condition state, logs history per asset and employee, and reports overdue returns.

Additional Details:
- Priority: Medium
- Story Points: 5
- Dependencies: Employee Master Data (E02), Role Based Access Control (E03), Training & Certification Tracking (E07)
- Notes: Support check-in/out endpoints and asset registry CRUD.
