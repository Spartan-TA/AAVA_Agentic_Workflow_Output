Title: Shift Management - Personal Upcoming Shifts Visibility
User Story: As a worker, I want to view my upcoming scheduled shifts so that I can plan my attendance and manage my time.
Acceptance Criteria:
  Given I am authenticated as a WORKER
  When I access my schedule
  Then I see a list of my upcoming shifts with details
Additional Details:
  Priority: High
  Story Points: 3
  Dependencies: E02, E03, E04
  Notes: Shifts must be filterable by date and include location and role.
