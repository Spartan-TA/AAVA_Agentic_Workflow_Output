Title: Define Overtime Rules and Shift Rotations

User Story: As an HR manager I want to configure overtime rules and shift rotations so that the system can automatically apply overtime pay and rotate employees through different shifts.

Acceptance Criteria:

Scenario: Overtime rule configuration
Given the overtime policy is defined
When I configure overtime thresholds and rates
Then the system applies overtime pay when thresholds are exceeded

Scenario: Shift rotation setup
Given a set of employees and shifts
When I configure a rotation schedule
Then employees are automatically rotated through shifts as per the schedule

Scenario: Overtime flagged in reports
Given overtime is worked
When I generate a schedule or attendance report
Then overtime hours and pay are clearly indicated

Additional Details:
- Priority: High
- Story Points: 5
- Dependencies: E02, E03, E04
- Notes: Ensures compliance with labor laws and fair scheduling.
