Title: Assign Shifts to Employees

User Story: As a supervisor I want to assign shifts to employees in bulk So that I can efficiently manage staffing for warehouse operations

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Bulk Shift Assignment
Given a set of employees and available shift templates
When I perform a bulk assignment
Then employees receive their upcoming shift schedules and conflicts are detected/prevented

Additional Details:
- Priority: High
- Story Points: 3
- Dependencies: Shift templates, Employee CRUD
- Notes: Workers can view their personal upcoming shifts; audit entries generated for assignments.
