Title: Shift Management - Conflict Detection and Prevention
User Story: As a scheduler, I want the system to detect and prevent scheduling conflicts so that employees are not double-booked or assigned during blackout dates.
Acceptance Criteria:
  Given I am assigning shifts
  When a conflict is detected (overlap, blackout date, or unavailable employee)
  Then the system prevents the assignment and displays a warning
Additional Details:
  Priority: High
  Story Points: 8
  Dependencies: E02, E03, E04
  Notes: Conflict rules must be configurable and auditable.
