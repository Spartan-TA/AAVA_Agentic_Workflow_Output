Title: Notifications - Opt-In/Out Per Channel
User Story: As an employee, I want to opt-in or out of in-app, email, and SMS notifications so that I receive updates in my preferred way.
Acceptance Criteria:
  Given I am authenticated as an employee
  When I update my notification preferences
  Then I only receive notifications via selected channels
Additional Details:
  Priority: Medium
  Story Points: 5
  Dependencies: E03, E05, E06, E07
  Notes: Preferences must be saved and respected for all notification types.
