Title: Pagination and Filtering for Employee Records

User Story: As an HR manager I want to paginate and filter employee records So that I can efficiently search and manage large employee datasets

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Paginated and filtered employee list
Given a large number of employee records
When I send a GET request to /employees with pagination and filter parameters
Then I receive a paginated and filtered list of employees matching the criteria

Additional Details:
- Priority: High
- Story Points: 2
- Dependencies: E01
- Notes: Support filtering by department, role, status, and shiftGroup.
