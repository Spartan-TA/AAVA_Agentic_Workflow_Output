Title: Reconciliation and Error Handling for Payroll Exports

User Story: As a payroll administrator I want to reconcile payroll exports with attendance and leave reports and handle errors gracefully so that payroll is accurate and issues are resolved quickly.

Acceptance Criteria:

Scenario: Totals reconcile with reports
Given a payroll export is generated
When I compare totals with attendance and leave reports
Then the values match and discrepancies are flagged

Scenario: Export errors handled gracefully
Given an export fails
When the system retries or aborts
Then errors are logged and notifications are sent to payroll admin

Scenario: Export status visible
Given exports are generated
When I view the export history
Then the status (success/failure) is visible for each export

Additional Details:
- Priority: High
- Story Points: 3
- Dependencies: E04, E06
- Notes: Ensures payroll accuracy and compliance.
