Title: Payroll Export - Export Audit Trail and Access
User Story: As an auditor, I want to export a log of all payroll exports by date, user, and entity so that I can review export activity and ensure compliance.
Acceptance Criteria:
  Given payroll exports have been generated
  When I export the audit log
  Then the log includes export date, user, entity, delivery status, and any errors
Additional Details:
  Priority: High
  Story Points: 5
  Dependencies: E04, E06
  Notes: Audit log export must be access controlled and filterable by date range and user.
