Title: Request Leave

User Story: As an employee I want to request PTO, sick, or unpaid leave So that my time off is tracked and approved according to policy

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Leave Request Submission
Given I am logged in as an employee
When I submit a leave request with required details
Then the request is routed to my supervisor for approval and my accrual balance is checked

Additional Details:
- Priority: Medium
- Story Points: 2
- Dependencies: Employee CRUD, RBAC, Shift Management
- Notes: Validate leave type, dates, and balance; support attachments if required.
