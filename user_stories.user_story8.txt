Title: Safety Incidents & OSHA Reporting

User Story: As a safety officer I want to record and manage safety incidents and near-misses so that investigations and OSHA reporting are accurate and timely.

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Incident recording and workflow
Given a safety incident occurs
When I submit the incident with severity, location, description, and involved employees
Then the status workflow (Open, Investigating, Resolved) is followed, OSHA 300/300A fields are exportable, and metrics dashboard endpoints are available.

Additional Details:
- Priority: High
- Story Points: 8
- Dependencies: Employee Master Data (E02), Role Based Access Control (E03)
- Notes: Support validation and corrective action workflow.
