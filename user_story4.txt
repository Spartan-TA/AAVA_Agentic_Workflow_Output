Title: Shift Scheduling Creation

User Story: As a warehouse manager, I want to create and publish shift schedules so that employees know when they are expected to work.

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Publishing a shift schedule
Given a list of employees and shift requirements
When the manager creates and publishes a schedule
Then employees receive notifications about their assigned shifts

Additional Details:
- Priority: High
- Story Points: 5
- Dependencies: Employee database, notification system
- Notes: Schedule conflicts and overtime should be flagged
