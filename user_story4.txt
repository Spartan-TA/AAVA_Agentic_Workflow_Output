Title: Profile Management

User Story Narrative:
As a logged-in user, I want to update my profile information so that my account details are accurate and up to date.

Acceptance Criteria (Gherkin):
Given I am logged in,
When I navigate to my profile page and update my information,
Then my changes are saved,
And I see a confirmation message.

Additional Details:
- Priority: Medium
- Story Points: 3
- Dependencies: User Login
- Notes: Email change requires re-verification.
