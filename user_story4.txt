Title: Time & Attendance Clock In/Out

User Story: As a warehouse worker I want to clock in and out with device and location capture So that my working hours are accurately recorded for payroll and compliance.

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Valid clock-in
Given I am scheduled for a shift
When I clock in via the app
Then my attendance is recorded with device and location data

Scenario: Missed punch correction
Given I missed a clock-in
When I submit a correction request
Then an approval task is created for my supervisor

Additional Details:
- Priority: High
- Story Points: 8
- Dependencies: E02, E03
- Notes: Automatic shift association; daily totals computed; exportable reports.
