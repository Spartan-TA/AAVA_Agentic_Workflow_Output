Title: Time & Attendance (Clock In/Out)
User Story: As a warehouse worker I want to clock in and out with device and location validation so that my worked hours are accurately captured for payroll and compliance.
Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Clock in/out event recording
Given I am at the warehouse with a valid device
When I clock in or out using the endpoint
Then the event is validated, shift association is automatic, daily totals are computed, corrections create approval tasks, and reports are exportable as CSV.
Additional Details:
- Priority: High
- Story Points: 8
- Dependencies: Employee Master Data (CRUD), Role Based Access Control (RBAC)
- Notes: Handle missed punches and corrections workflow; optional geofence validation.
