Title: Time & Attendance - Clock In

User Story: As a WORKER I want to clock in at the start of my shift So that my attendance is accurately recorded.

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Successful clock-in
Given a scheduled shift and valid geofence
When I POST to /attendance/clock-in
Then my clock-in event is recorded and associated with my shift

Additional Details:
- Priority: High
- Story Points: 2
- Dependencies: E02, E03
- Notes: Device and location are captured

---

Title: Time & Attendance - Clock Out

User Story: As a WORKER I want to clock out at the end of my shift So that my worked hours are tracked.

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Successful clock-out
Given an active shift
When I POST to /attendance/clock-out
Then my clock-out event is recorded and daily total hours are computed

Additional Details:
- Priority: High
- Story Points: 2
- Dependencies: E02, E03
- Notes: Handles missed punches

Title: Time & Attendance - Missed Punch Correction

User Story: As a SUPERVISOR I want to correct missed or erroneous clock events So that attendance data remains accurate.

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Correction workflow
Given a missed punch is detected
When a correction is submitted
Then an approval task is created and the correction is logged

Additional Details:
- Priority: High
- Story Points: 3
- Dependencies: E02, E03
- Notes: All corrections are auditable
