Title: Time & Attendance (Clock In/Out)
User Story: As a warehouse worker I want to clock in and out So that my hours are accurately tracked for payroll and compliance
Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Clock in/out and corrections
Given I am scheduled for a shift
When I clock in/out via the endpoint
Then my hours are calculated, missed punches trigger corrections workflow, and reports are exportable
Additional Details:
- Priority: High
- Story Points: 8
- Dependencies: Employee Master Data, RBAC
- Notes: Geofence/device capture optional; corrections create approval tasks
