Title: Time & Attendance Tracking

User Story: As a warehouse employee I want to record my clock-in and clock-out times So that my attendance and working hours are accurately tracked

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Successful time entry
Given I am logged into the system
When I clock in or clock out
Then my attendance record is updated and visible in my profile

Additional Details:
- Priority: High
- Story Points: 5
- Dependencies: User authentication, employee profile module
- Notes: Support for multiple shifts and overtime calculations
