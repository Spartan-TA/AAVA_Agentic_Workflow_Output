Title: Time & Attendance (Clock In/Out)

User Story: As a warehouse worker I want to clock in and out with validation and device capture so that my working hours are accurately recorded for payroll and compliance.

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Clock in/out and hours calculation
  Given I am an authenticated employee
  When I clock in or out using the provided endpoint
  Then my shift association is automatic, daily totals are computed, and corrections create approval tasks
  And missed punches are handled, and reports are exportable as CSV

Additional Details:
- Priority: High
- Story Points: 8
- Dependencies: Employee Master Data (CRUD), Role Based Access Control (RBAC)
- Notes: Geofence and device capture are optional; ensure corrections workflow is implemented.
