Title: Time & Attendance (Clock In/Out)

User Story: As a warehouse worker I want to clock in and out with device and location validation so that my worked hours are accurately tracked for payroll and compliance.

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Clock in/out event
Given I am scheduled for a shift
When I clock in or out using the provided endpoint
Then my event is validated, associated with my shift, totals are computed, corrections trigger approval tasks, and reports are exportable as CSV

Additional Details:
- Priority: High
- Story Points: 8
- Dependencies: Employee Master Data (E02), Role-Based Access Control (E03)
- Notes: Geofence and device capture are optional but recommended for compliance.
