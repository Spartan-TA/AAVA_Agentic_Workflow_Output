Title: Time & Attendance (Clock In/Out)

User Story: As a warehouse worker I want to clock in and out of my shift So that my working hours are accurately recorded for payroll and compliance

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Clock-in event
Given I am scheduled for a shift
When I submit a clock-in event with valid credentials
Then my attendance is recorded and associated with my shift

Scenario: Clock-out event
Given I am clocked in
When I submit a clock-out event
Then my total hours worked are calculated and recorded

Scenario: Missed punch correction
Given I missed a clock-in or clock-out
When I submit a correction request
Then an approval task is created for my supervisor

Scenario: Attendance report export
Given attendance data exists
When I request a report export
Then I receive a CSV file with daily totals and corrections

Additional Details:
- Priority: High
- Story Points: 8
- Dependencies: Employee Master Data CRUD APIs, Role Based Access Control (RBAC)
- Notes: Support geofence/device capture; automatic shift association; corrections workflow; exportable reports.
