Title: Time & Attendance - Corrections Workflow
User Story: As a supervisor, I want to approve or deny attendance corrections so that time records are accurate and auditable.
Acceptance Criteria:
  - Given a correction request, When I review it, Then I can approve or deny the change.
  - Given an approved correction, When payroll is processed, Then the corrected hours are used.
Additional Details:
  Priority: High
  Story Points: 2
  Dependencies: Time & Attendance
  Notes: Approval tasks generated.
