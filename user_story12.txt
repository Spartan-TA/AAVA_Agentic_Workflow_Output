Title: Row-Level Security for Supervisors
User Story: As a Supervisor I want to access and manage only the records of employees in my team So that I cannot view or modify data outside my scope
Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Supervisor access limited to team
Given a supervisor is authenticated
When they access employee records
Then only employees in their team are visible and editable
Scenario: Attempt to access out-of-scope records
Given a supervisor is authenticated
When they attempt to access records outside their team
Then a 403 Forbidden response is returned
Additional Details:
- Priority: High
- Story Points: 5
- Dependencies: E01, E02
- Notes: Implement row-level security checks for supervisor role
