Title: Notifications & Announcements
User Story: As a supervisor I want to send notifications and announcements So that workers are informed in real time
Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Notification delivery and opt-in/out
Given a notification is sent
When users have configured preferences
Then delivery is tracked, rate limits applied, and announcements visible on dashboard
Additional Details:
- Priority: Medium
- Story Points: 5
- Dependencies: RBAC, Shift & Schedule Management, Leave & Absence Management, Training & Certification Tracking
- Notes: In-app, email/SMS, quiet hours
