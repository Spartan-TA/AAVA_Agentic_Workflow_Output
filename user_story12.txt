Title: Notifications - Shift Change Notification

User Story: As a WORKER I want to receive notifications for shift changes So that I am always aware of my schedule.

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Notify on shift change
Given my shift is updated
When the change is saved
Then I receive a notification via my preferred channel

Additional Details:
- Priority: Medium
- Story Points: 2
- Dependencies: E03, E05, E06, E07
- Notes: Users can opt-in/out per channel
