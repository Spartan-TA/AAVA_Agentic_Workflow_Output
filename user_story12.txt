### E12: Notifications & Announcements

**User Story 1**
Title: Notification Channel Opt-In/Opt-Out

User Story: As a WORKER I want to opt-in or out of notification channels So that I control how I receive updates.

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Channel Preferences
Given notification settings
When I update my preferences
Then notifications are sent only to selected channels

Additional Details:
- Priority: Medium
- Story Points: 1
- Dependencies: E03, E05, E06, E07
- Notes: Supports in-app, email, SMS

---

**User Story 2**
Title: Localized Notification Templates

User Story: As an ADMIN I want notification templates to be localized So that users receive messages in their preferred language.

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Localized Notification
Given a user language preference
When a notification is sent
Then the template matches the user's language

Additional Details:
- Priority: Medium
- Story Points: 1
- Dependencies: Story 1
- Notes: Localization for all supported languages

---

**User Story 3**
Title: Delivery Status Tracking

User Story: As a SYSTEM I want to track notification delivery status So that failed deliveries can be retried.

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Delivery Status
Given a sent notification
When I check delivery status
Then success or failure is logged

Additional Details:
- Priority: Medium
- Story Points: 1
- Dependencies: Story 1
- Notes: Retries for failed deliveries

---

**User Story 4**
Title: Announcement Dashboard Visibility

User Story: As a WORKER I want to view announcements on my dashboard So that I stay informed about important updates.

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: View Announcements
Given active announcements
When I access my dashboard
Then announcements are displayed

Additional Details:
- Priority: Medium
- Story Points: 1
- Dependencies: Story 1
- Notes: Rate limits applied during quiet hours
