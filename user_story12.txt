Title: Notifications & Announcements
User Story: As a warehouse worker I want to receive in-app and email/SMS notifications for shift changes, expiring certs, approvals, and announcements so that I am informed in real time.
Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Notification delivery and management
Given I am subscribed to notification channels
When an event occurs (shift change, cert expiry, approval, announcement)
Then users can opt-in/out per channel, templates are localized, delivery status is tracked, rate limits are applied, and announcements are visible on the dashboard.
Additional Details:
- Priority: Medium
- Story Points: 5
- Dependencies: Role Based Access Control (RBAC), Shift & Schedule Management, Leave & Absence Management, Training & Certification Tracking
- Notes: Quiet hours configuration; delivery via email/SMS/in-app.
