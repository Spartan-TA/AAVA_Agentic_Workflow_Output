Title: Schedule Conflict Detection

User Story: As a SYSTEM I want to detect and prevent scheduling conflicts So that employees are not double-booked

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Overlapping Shifts
Given an employee with an existing shift
When I assign a new overlapping shift
Then the system blocks the assignment

Additional Details:
- Priority: High
- Story Points: 3
- Dependencies: Shift Template Management
- Notes: Conflict rules must be configurable
