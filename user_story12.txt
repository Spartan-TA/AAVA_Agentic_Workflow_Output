Title: Notifications

User Story: As a warehouse user I want to receive timely notifications about important events So that I am informed and can take necessary actions

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Receiving notifications
Given an event occurs (e.g., shift assignment, leave approval)
When the system processes the event
Then relevant users receive notifications via email and in-app alerts

Additional Details:
- Priority: High
- Story Points: 5
- Dependencies: All modules generating events
- Notes: Support for notification preferences and escalation
