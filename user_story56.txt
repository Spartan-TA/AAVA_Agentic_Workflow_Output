Title: In-App and Email/SMS Notifications for Key Events

User Story: As a warehouse worker or supervisor I want to receive in-app and email/SMS notifications for shift changes, expiring certifications, approvals, and announcements so that I am always informed of important updates.

Acceptance Criteria:

Scenario: Notification for shift change
Given a shift is changed
When the change is saved
Then affected employees receive a notification via selected channels

Scenario: Notification for expiring certification
Given a certification is expiring soon
When the system detects the expiration window
Then the employee and supervisor are notified

Scenario: Notification for leave approval
Given a leave request is approved
When the status changes
Then the employee is notified

Additional Details:
- Priority: Medium
- Story Points: 5
- Dependencies: E03, E05, E06, E07
- Notes: Improves communication and reduces missed events.
