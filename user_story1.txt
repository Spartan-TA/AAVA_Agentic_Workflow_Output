Title: Employee Onboarding Workflow

User Story: As an HR manager, I want to initiate onboarding for new warehouse employees so that they can quickly become productive and compliant with company policies.

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Successful onboarding initiation
Given a new employee record exists in the system
When the HR manager starts the onboarding process
Then the employee receives onboarding tasks and documentation, and their progress is tracked

Additional Details:
- Priority: High
- Story Points: 5
- Dependencies: Employee database, onboarding module
- Notes: Onboarding tasks include safety training, equipment assignment, and policy acknowledgment
