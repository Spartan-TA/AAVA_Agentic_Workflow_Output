Title: User Registration via Email

User Story: As a new user I want to register for an account using my email address So that I can securely access and personalize my experience on the platform

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Successful Email Registration
Given I am on the registration page and have not previously registered
When I enter a valid email address, a strong password, and submit the form
Then I receive a verification email and my account is created in a pending state until I verify my email

Additional Details:
- Priority: High
- Story Points: 5
- Dependencies: User authentication module, Email service integration
- Notes: Password must meet security requirements (min 8 chars, 1 uppercase, 1 number). Verification link expires in 24 hours.
