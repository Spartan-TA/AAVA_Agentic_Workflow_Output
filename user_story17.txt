### E17: Onboarding & Offboarding Workflow

**User Story 1**
Title: Automated Onboarding from HRIS

User Story: As an HR I want new hires to appear in EMS from HRIS So that onboarding tasks are triggered automatically.

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: New Hire Onboarding
Given a new hire in HRIS
When the sync job runs
Then the employee is created and onboarding tasks generated

Additional Details:
- Priority: Medium
- Story Points: 2
- Dependencies: E02, E03, E05, E07, E09, E13
- Notes: Includes schedule and training assignment

---

**User Story 2**
Title: Training and Asset Assignment for New Hires

User Story: As a SUPERVISOR I want onboarding tasks to include training and asset assignment So that new hires are fully provisioned.

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Onboarding Task Completion
Given onboarding tasks
When I complete training and asset assignment
Then the employee is ready for work

Additional Details:
- Priority: Medium
- Story Points: 2
- Dependencies: Story 1
- Notes: Checklist visible to supervisor

---

**User Story 3**
Title: Automated Offboarding Workflow

User Story: As an HR I want offboarding to revoke access and collect assets So that terminated employees are properly deprovisioned.

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Offboarding Completion
Given a terminated employee
When I trigger offboarding
Then access is revoked, assets collected, and schedules updated

Additional Details:
- Priority: Medium
- Story Points: 2
- Dependencies: Story 1, Story 2
- Notes: Integration with asset registry and scheduling
