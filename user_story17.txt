Title: Attendance Reports Export (CSV)
User Story: As a Supervisor I want to export attendance reports in CSV format So that I can analyze and share attendance data for compliance and payroll
Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Export attendance report
Given attendance data is available
When I request a CSV export
Then the report is generated and downloadable within 10 seconds
Scenario: Export includes corrections
Given corrections have been approved
When I export the report
Then corrections are reflected in the exported data
Additional Details:
- Priority: High
- Story Points: 3
- Dependencies: E02, E03
- Notes: Ensure export includes all relevant fields and corrections
