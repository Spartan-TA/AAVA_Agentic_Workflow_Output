Title: Onboarding/Offboarding

User Story: As an HR manager I want to manage employee onboarding and offboarding processes So that transitions are smooth and compliance is maintained

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Onboarding a new employee
Given a new hire is added to the system
When onboarding tasks are assigned
Then the employee receives instructions and progress is tracked

Additional Details:
- Priority: Medium
- Story Points: 8
- Dependencies: HR module, notification module
- Notes: Support for customizable onboarding checklists
