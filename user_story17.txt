Title: Leave Management - Leave Balance Accrual
User Story: As an HR manager, I want leave balances to accrue according to policy so that employees have accurate records.
Acceptance Criteria:
  - Given an accrual policy, When time passes, Then employee leave balances update automatically.
  - Given a leave export, When I generate it, Then only approved leaves are included.
Additional Details:
  Priority: Medium
  Story Points: 2
  Dependencies: Leave Management
  Notes: Integration hooks for scheduling/payroll.
