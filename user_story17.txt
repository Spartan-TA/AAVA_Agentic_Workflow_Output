Title: Attendance - Clock Out and Shift Association
User Story: As a worker, I want to clock out and have my hours automatically associated with my scheduled shift so that my worked time is accurately tracked.
Acceptance Criteria:
  Given I am authenticated as a WORKER
  When I POST to /attendance/clock-out
  Then my clock-out event is validated and linked to my shift, and total hours are calculated
Additional Details:
  Priority: High
  Story Points: 5
  Dependencies: E02, E03
  Notes: Shift association must be automatic based on schedule and clock-in time.
