Title: Onboarding & Offboarding Workflow
User Story: As an HR manager I want automated onboarding/offboarding workflows So that employee lifecycle changes are efficient and low risk
Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: New hire and termination automation
Given a new hire appears from HRIS
When onboarding/offboarding tasks are triggered
Then accounts, training, assets, and schedules are provisioned/revoked as appropriate
Additional Details:
- Priority: Medium
- Story Points: 8
- Dependencies: Employee Master Data, RBAC, Shift & Schedule Management, Training & Certification Tracking, Equipment & Asset Assignment, Integration Layer
- Notes: Deprovision access and collect assets on termination
