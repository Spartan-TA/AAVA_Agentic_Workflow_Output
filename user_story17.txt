Title: Onboarding & Offboarding Workflow
User Story: As an HR manager I want to automate onboarding and offboarding workflows so that employee lifecycle changes are handled efficiently and risks are reduced.
Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Automated onboarding and offboarding
Given a new hire or termination event
When onboarding or offboarding is triggered
Then new hires appear from HRIS, tasks are generated for training and asset assignment, offboarding revokes access, collects assets, and updates schedules.
Additional Details:
- Priority: Medium
- Story Points: 8
- Dependencies: Employee Master Data (CRUD), Role Based Access Control (RBAC), Shift & Schedule Management, Training & Certification Tracking, Equipment & Asset Assignment, Integration Layer (HRIS/WMS APIs)
- Notes: Provisioning of accounts, initial schedule, required training; deprovision access and assets.
