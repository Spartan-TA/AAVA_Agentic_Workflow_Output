Title: Onboarding & Offboarding Workflow

User Story: As an HR manager I want to automate onboarding and offboarding workflows so that employee lifecycle changes are efficient, compliant, and risk is reduced.

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Automated onboarding and offboarding
  Given a new hire appears from HRIS or an employee is terminated
  When onboarding or offboarding tasks are triggered
  Then accounts are provisioned/deprovisioned, initial schedules and training are assigned, assets are collected/assigned, and schedules are updated

Additional Details:
- Priority: Medium
- Story Points: 8
- Dependencies: Employee Master Data (CRUD), Role Based Access Control (RBAC), Shift & Schedule Management, Training & Certification Tracking, Equipment & Asset Assignment, Integration Layer (HRIS/WMS APIs)
- Notes: Ensure all tasks are tracked and completion is auditable.
