Title: Equipment Maintenance Scheduling

User Story: As a warehouse manager, I want to schedule equipment maintenance so that tools remain safe and operational.

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Scheduling maintenance
Given equipment maintenance requirements
When the manager schedules maintenance
Then maintenance tasks are assigned and tracked

Additional Details:
- Priority: Medium
- Story Points: 4
- Dependencies: Equipment management module, maintenance module
- Notes: Maintenance history should be reportable
