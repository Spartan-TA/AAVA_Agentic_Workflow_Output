Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Receiving AI suggestions
Given I am creating a new schedule
When I request AI suggestions
Then the system should propose shift assignments based on historical data and constraints

Additional Details:

- Story Points: 8
- Dependencies: Scheduling module, ML engine
