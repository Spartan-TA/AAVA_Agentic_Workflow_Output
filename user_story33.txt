Title: Certification Tracking - Scheduling Block for Expired Certifications
User Story: As a scheduler, I want the system to block assignment of tasks to employees with expired certifications so that compliance and safety are maintained.
Acceptance Criteria:
  Given an employee's certification is expired
  When I attempt to assign them to a task requiring that certification
  Then the system prevents the assignment and displays a warning
Additional Details:
  Priority: High
  Story Points: 8
  Dependencies: E02, E03
  Notes: Scheduling checks must run automatically and be auditable.
