Title: Approve or Deny Leave Requests

User Story: As a supervisor I want to approve or deny employee leave requests So that I can ensure adequate staffing and compliance with leave policies

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Leave Approval Workflow
Given a pending leave request
When I review and approve or deny it
Then the employee is notified and the schedule is updated accordingly

Additional Details:
- Priority: Medium
- Story Points: 2
- Dependencies: Leave request submission
- Notes: Approved leaves auto-flag scheduled shifts for coverage; update accrual balances.
