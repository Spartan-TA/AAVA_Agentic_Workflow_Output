Title: Shift Management - Bulk Assignment and Audit Logging
User Story: As a supervisor, I want to bulk-assign shifts to multiple employees and have all actions logged for audit purposes so that scheduling is efficient and traceable.
Acceptance Criteria:
  Given I am authenticated as a SUPERVISOR
  When I bulk-assign shifts
  Then all assignments are completed and audit entries are generated for each action
Additional Details:
  Priority: High
  Story Points: 8
  Dependencies: E02, E03, E04
  Notes: Audit log must include actor, timestamp, and affected records.
