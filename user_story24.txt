Title: Personal Shift Visibility for Workers and Supervisors

User Story: As a worker or supervisor I want to view upcoming personal shifts and team schedules so that I can plan my work and manage staffing.

Acceptance Criteria:

Scenario: Worker views personal shifts
Given an authenticated worker
When they access /schedules/my-shifts
Then they see a list of their upcoming assigned shifts

Scenario: Supervisor views team schedule
Given an authenticated supervisor
When they access /schedules/team
Then they see the schedule for all team members

Scenario: Shifts reflect blackout dates
Given blackout dates are configured
When I view my shifts
Then no shifts are shown on blackout dates

Additional Details:
- Priority: High
- Story Points: 3
- Dependencies: E02, E03, E04
- Notes: Improves transparency and reduces absenteeism.
