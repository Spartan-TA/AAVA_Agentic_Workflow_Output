Title: Leave Management - Export and Coverage Flagging
User Story: As an HR manager, I want to export approved leave records and have scheduled shifts auto-flagged for coverage so that reporting and shift management are streamlined.
Acceptance Criteria:
  Given leave requests are approved
  When I export leave data or review schedules
  Then approved leaves are included in exports and affected shifts are flagged for coverage
Additional Details:
  Priority: Medium
  Story Points: 5
  Dependencies: E02, E03, E05
  Notes: Export format must include leave type, dates, approval status, and coverage flags.
