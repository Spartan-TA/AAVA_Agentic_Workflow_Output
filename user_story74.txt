Title: Automate Onboarding Provisioning (Accounts, Schedule, Training)

User Story: As an HR manager I want new hires from HRIS to trigger automated provisioning of user accounts, initial schedules, and required training so that onboarding is efficient and consistent.

Acceptance Criteria:

Scenario: New hire appears from HRIS
Given a new hire is added in HRIS
When the integration job runs
Then the employee is created in the system and onboarding tasks are generated

Scenario: Accounts provisioned automatically
Given a new hire
When onboarding tasks are processed
Then user accounts are created and access is granted

Scenario: Initial schedule and training assigned
Given a new hire
When onboarding is processed
Then an initial schedule and required training are assigned

Additional Details:
- Priority: Medium
- Story Points: 5
- Dependencies: E02, E03, E05, E07, E09, E13
- Notes: Reduces manual effort and onboarding time.
