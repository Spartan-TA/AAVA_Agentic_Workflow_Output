Title: Certification Tracking - Expiry Alerts
User Story: As an employee, I want to receive alerts when my certifications are about to expire so that I can renew them in time and avoid being blocked from assignments.
Acceptance Criteria:
  Given my certification is expiring in 30 or 7 days
  When the system detects the upcoming expiry
  Then I receive an alert via my preferred notification channel
Additional Details:
  Priority: High
  Story Points: 5
  Dependencies: E02, E03
  Notes: Alerts must be configurable and include renewal instructions.
