Title: Payroll Export - Totals Reconciliation
User Story: As a payroll administrator, I want payroll export totals to reconcile with attendance and leave reports so that payroll is accurate and auditable.
Acceptance Criteria:
  Given I generate a payroll export
  When I compare totals with attendance and leave reports
  Then all values match and discrepancies are flagged
Additional Details:
  Priority: High
  Story Points: 5
  Dependencies: E04, E06
  Notes: Reconciliation must be automated and discrepancies must trigger alerts.
