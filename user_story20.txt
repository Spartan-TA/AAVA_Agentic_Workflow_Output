Title: Safety Incidents - Record Incident
User Story: As a safety officer, I want to record safety incidents and near-misses with all relevant details so that investigations can be tracked and OSHA reporting is accurate.
Acceptance Criteria:
  - Given incident details, When I POST to /safety/incidents, Then the incident is recorded with severity, location, description, and involved employees.
  - Given incomplete data, When I submit, Then validation errors are shown.
Additional Details:
  Priority: High
  Story Points: 2
  Dependencies: Employee CRUD, RBAC
  Notes: OSHA summary generation.
