Title: Absence Notification Workflow

User Story: As a warehouse employee, I want to notify my supervisor of planned absences so that shift coverage can be arranged.

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Notifying absence
Given an employee plans to be absent
When they submit an absence notification
Then the supervisor receives the request and can arrange coverage

Additional Details:
- Priority: Medium
- Story Points: 2
- Dependencies: Notification system, shift scheduling module
- Notes: Absence notifications should be submitted in advance
