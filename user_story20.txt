Title: Attendance - Corrections Audit and Export
User Story: As an HR manager, I want all attendance corrections to be auditable and exportable so that compliance and review processes are supported.
Acceptance Criteria:
  Given corrections have been submitted and approved
  When I export attendance data
  Then all corrections are included with audit details (who, when, what changed)
Additional Details:
  Priority: High
  Story Points: 5
  Dependencies: E02, E03
  Notes: Export format must include correction history and approval status.
