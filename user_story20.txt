### E20: Self-Service Portal

**User Story 1**
Title: Employee Self-Service Portal

User Story: As a WORKER I want a portal to view my data, request leave, and see announcements So that I manage my employment independently.

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Employee Portal Access
Given valid credentials
When I log in to the portal
Then I can view my profile, schedule, leave, and announcements

Additional Details:
- Priority: High
- Story Points: 3
- Dependencies: E02, E04, E05, E06, E12
- Notes: Mobile and desktop support

---

**User Story 3**

Additional Details:
- Priority: High

**User Story 5**
Title: Portal Accessibility Compliance

User Story: As an ADMIN I want the portal to meet accessibility standards So that all employees can use it.

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Accessibility Verification
Given the portal UI
When tested with accessibility tools
Then it meets WCAG 2.1 AA standards

Additional Details:
- Priority: High
- Story Points: 1
- Dependencies: Story 1
- Notes: Includes keyboard navigation and screen reader support
