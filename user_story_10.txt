Title: List and Filter Employees

User Story: As an HR Manager I want to list and filter employees by various criteria So that I can quickly find relevant employee records

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Employee list filtering
Given I am on the employee list page
When I apply filters (e.g., department, status)
Then only employees matching the criteria are displayed

Additional Details:
- Priority: Medium
- Story Points: 2
- Dependencies: Employee record creation
- Notes: Support pagination and sorting
