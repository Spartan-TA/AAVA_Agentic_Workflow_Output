Title: Track Employee Certifications

User Story: As an HR manager I want to record and track employee certifications and expirations So that only qualified staff are assigned to equipment-sensitive roles

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Certification Tracking
Given an employee profile
When I add or update a certification
Then the certification status and expiry date are visible and alerts are generated 30/7 days before expiry

Additional Details:
- Priority: High
- Story Points: 2
- Dependencies: Employee CRUD, RBAC
- Notes: Block assignment to tasks requiring expired certifications; upload proof documents.
