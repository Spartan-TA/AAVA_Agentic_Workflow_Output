Title: Safety Compliance Checklist Assignment

User Story: As a warehouse supervisor, I want to assign safety compliance checklists to employees so that safety standards are maintained.

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Assigning safety checklists
Given a list of safety checklists
When the supervisor assigns a checklist to an employee
Then the employee receives the checklist and completion is tracked

Additional Details:
- Priority: High
- Story Points: 3
- Dependencies: Safety compliance module, notification system
- Notes: Checklist completion should be mandatory before shift start
