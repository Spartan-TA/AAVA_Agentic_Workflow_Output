Title: Employee Clock In/Out Event Capture
User Story: As a Warehouse Worker I want to clock in and out using a secure endpoint So that my working hours are accurately recorded for payroll and compliance
Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Successful clock-in
Given the worker is authenticated and within the allowed geofence
When they POST to /attendance/clock-in with device info
Then the event is recorded with timestamp and device details
Scenario: Successful clock-out
Given the worker is authenticated and clocked in
When they POST to /attendance/clock-out
Then the event is recorded and shift hours are calculated
Additional Details:
- Priority: High
- Story Points: 5
- Dependencies: E02, E03
- Notes: Device capture and optional geofence validation
