Title: Shift Management - Conflict Detection
User Story: As a scheduler, I want the system to detect and prevent shift conflicts so that employees are not double-booked.
Acceptance Criteria:
  - Given overlapping shifts, When I attempt to assign them, Then the system blocks the assignment and shows an error.
  - Given a blackout date, When I schedule a shift, Then the system prevents scheduling.
Additional Details:
  Priority: High
  Story Points: 2
  Dependencies: Shift Management
  Notes: Blackout dates and operation calendars.
