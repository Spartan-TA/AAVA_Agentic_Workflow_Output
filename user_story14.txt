Title: Audit Trail & Compliance
User Story: As a compliance officer I want centralized audit logging for sensitive changes so that audit/compliance needs are met and forensic analysis is enabled.
Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Audit logging and export
Given sensitive changes (employee PII, schedules, approvals, payroll)
When a create, update, or delete occurs
Then actor, timestamp, before/after are logged, log table is immutable, export by date/user/entity is available, and tests validate coverage.
Additional Details:
- Priority: High
- Story Points: 5
- Dependencies: Role Based Access Control (RBAC)
- Notes: Tamper-evident storage; centralized audit logging.
