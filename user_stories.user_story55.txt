Title: Scheduling AI Feedback Loop

User Story: As a scheduler I want to provide feedback on AI-generated shift suggestions So that the system can learn and improve future recommendations

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Providing feedback on suggestions
Given AI-generated shift assignments are displayed
When I accept, modify, or reject suggestions
Then the system should record my feedback and use it to refine the scheduling model

Additional Details:
- Priority: Medium
- Story Points: 5
- Dependencies: Scheduling module, ML engine
- Notes: Feedback options should be simple and actionable.
