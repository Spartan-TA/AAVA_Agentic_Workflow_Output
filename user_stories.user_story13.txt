Title: Create Shift Templates

User Story: As a scheduler I want to create recurring shift templates So that I can efficiently assign standard schedules to employees

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Shift Template Creation
Given I have scheduler access
When I define a new shift template
Then it is saved and available for assignment to employees

Additional Details:
- Priority: High
- Story Points: 2
- Dependencies: Employee CRUD, RBAC, Attendance
- Notes: Support for rotations, overtime rules, and blackout dates.
