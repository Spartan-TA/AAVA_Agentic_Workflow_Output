Title: Offboarding Workflow for Deprovisioning Access and Asset Collection

User Story: As an HR manager I want offboarding to automatically revoke access, collect assets, and update schedules so that terminated employees are removed from the system efficiently and securely.

Acceptance Criteria:

Scenario: Offboarding triggered by HRIS
Given an employee is terminated in HRIS
When the integration job runs
Then offboarding tasks are generated in the system

Scenario: Access revoked automatically
Given offboarding is in progress
When tasks are processed
Then user accounts are disabled and access is revoked

Scenario: Assets collected and schedules updated
Given offboarding is in progress
When tasks are processed
Then assigned assets are collected and future schedules are updated to remove the employee

Additional Details:
- Priority: Medium
- Story Points: 5
- Dependencies: E02, E03, E05, E07, E09, E13
- Notes: Reduces risk and ensures compliance during offboarding.
