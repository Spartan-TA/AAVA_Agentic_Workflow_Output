Title: Safety Incident Investigation Workflow

User Story: As a safety officer I want to initiate and track investigation workflows for reported incidents So that each incident is properly reviewed and corrective actions are documented

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Initiating investigation
Given a safety incident is recorded
When I assign an investigator and set due dates
Then the system should notify the investigator and track investigation status

Additional Details:
- Priority: High
- Story Points: 5
- Dependencies: Incident recording, notification module
- Notes: Investigation outcomes must be linked to the original incident record.
