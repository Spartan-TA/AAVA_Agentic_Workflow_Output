Title: Onboarding Progress Tracking

User Story: As an HR manager, I want to monitor onboarding progress for each employee so that I can ensure timely completion.

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Viewing onboarding progress
Given an employee is undergoing onboarding
When the HR manager views their profile
Then the onboarding progress and outstanding tasks are displayed

Additional Details:
- Priority: Medium
- Story Points: 2
- Dependencies: Onboarding module, employee profile
- Notes: Progress tracking should highlight overdue tasks
