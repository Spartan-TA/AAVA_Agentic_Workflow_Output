Title: Safety Incidents - OSHA Summary Export
User Story: As an HR manager, I want to export OSHA 300/300A summary fields from recorded incidents so that regulatory reporting is accurate and efficient.
Acceptance Criteria:
  Given incidents are recorded
  When I export OSHA summary data
  Then the export includes all required fields and matches regulatory format
Additional Details:
  Priority: High
  Story Points: 5
  Dependencies: E02, E03
  Notes: Export must be available in CSV and PDF formats.
