Title: Safety Incidents - Corrective Action Audit Trail
User Story: As a compliance officer, I want all corrective actions from incident investigations to be logged and auditable so that regulatory requirements are met.
Acceptance Criteria:
  Given a corrective action is taken during an investigation
  When the action is logged
  Then the audit trail includes actor, timestamp, action details, and related incident
Additional Details:
  Priority: High
  Story Points: 5
  Dependencies: E02, E03
  Notes: Audit trail must be exportable and tamper-evident.
