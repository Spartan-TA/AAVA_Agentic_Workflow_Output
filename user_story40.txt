Title: Audit Logging and Notifications for Safety Incidents

User Story: As a compliance officer I want all safety incident actions to be logged and relevant parties notified so that there is a clear audit trail and timely response to incidents.

Acceptance Criteria:

Scenario: Incident actions logged
Given an incident is created, updated, or resolved
When the action occurs
Then an audit entry is created with user, timestamp, and action

Scenario: Notifications sent to stakeholders
Given a new incident or status change
When the action occurs
Then relevant parties (safety manager, supervisor, HR) are notified

Scenario: Audit log exportable
Given audit entries exist
When I request an export
Then I receive a CSV file with all relevant audit data

Additional Details:
- Priority: High
- Story Points: 3
- Dependencies: E02, E03
- Notes: Supports compliance and rapid response to safety issues.
