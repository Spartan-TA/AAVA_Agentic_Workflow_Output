Title: Content Moderation
User Story: As a moderator I want to review and moderate content So that I can ensure platform guidelines are followed
Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Successful content moderation
  Given I am logged in as a moderator
  When I access the moderation queue
  Then I should be able to approve, reject, or flag content for review
Additional Details:
  Priority: High
  Story Points: 5
  Dependencies: Authentication module, Content management system, Admin panel
  Notes: Implement automated flagging for suspicious content.
