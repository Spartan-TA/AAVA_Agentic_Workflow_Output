Title: Leave Management - Request & Approve Leave
User Story: As an employee, I want to request PTO, sick, or unpaid leave so that my time off is managed according to policy.
Acceptance Criteria:
  - Given a leave request, When I submit it, Then my supervisor is notified for approval.
  - Given an approval/denial, When my supervisor acts, Then my leave balance is updated and scheduled shifts are flagged for coverage.
Additional Details:
  Priority: Medium
  Story Points: 2
  Dependencies: Employee CRUD, RBAC, Shift Management
  Notes: Accrual balances and policies.
