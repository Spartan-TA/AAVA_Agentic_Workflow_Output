Title: Certification Assignment Blocking

User Story: As a SYSTEM I want to block assignment of tasks to employees with expired certifications So that compliance is enforced

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Assignment Attempt
Given an expired certification
When I assign a restricted task
Then the system blocks the assignment and notifies the supervisor

Additional Details:
- Priority: High
- Story Points: 3
- Dependencies: Certification Tracking
- Notes: Applies to equipment and shift assignments
