Title: Equipment Assignment to Employees

User Story: As a warehouse supervisor, I want to assign equipment to employees so that each has the tools needed for their tasks.

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Assigning equipment
Given available equipment and employee list
When the supervisor assigns equipment
Then the employee receives assignment details and equipment status is updated

Additional Details:
- Priority: Medium
- Story Points: 3
- Dependencies: Equipment management module, employee database
- Notes: Equipment assignment should consider maintenance schedules
