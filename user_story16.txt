Title: Attendance - Clock In Endpoint with Validation
User Story: As a worker, I want to clock in using a secure endpoint with device and optional geofence validation so that my attendance is accurately recorded and verified.
Acceptance Criteria:
  Given I am authenticated as a WORKER
  When I POST to /attendance/clock-in with required data
  Then my clock-in event is validated and recorded, including device and location info
Additional Details:
  Priority: High
  Story Points: 5
  Dependencies: E02, E03
  Notes: Geofence validation is optional and configurable per site.
