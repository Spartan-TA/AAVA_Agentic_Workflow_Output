Title: Automatic Shift Association and Daily Totals
User Story: As a Payroll Specialist I want attendance events to be automatically associated with shifts and daily totals computed So that payroll calculations are accurate and efficient
Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Automatic shift association
Given a worker clocks in/out
When the attendance event is recorded
Then it is linked to the correct shift based on schedule
Scenario: Daily totals computation
Given attendance events are recorded
When daily totals are computed
Then hours worked per shift are available for payroll
Additional Details:
- Priority: High
- Story Points: 3
- Dependencies: E02, E03
- Notes: Ensure shift association logic is robust
