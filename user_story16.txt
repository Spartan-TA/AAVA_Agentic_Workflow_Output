Title: Mobile Access (PWA)

User Story: As a warehouse worker I want to access core workflows (clock-in/out, view schedules, request leave, see announcements) on mobile devices so that I can perform essential tasks anywhere, even offline.

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Mobile usability and offline support
  Given I am using a mobile device
  When I access the application
  Then core flows are usable, the app is installable as a PWA, offline queue for clock events is supported with conflict resolution, and Lighthouse PWA score is met

Additional Details:
- Priority: Medium
- Story Points: 8
- Dependencies: Time & Attendance (Clock In/Out), Shift & Schedule Management, Leave & Absence Management, Notifications & Announcements
- Notes: Responsive design and offline-first architecture required.
