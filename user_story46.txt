Title: Performance Reviews - Create Review Templates
User Story: As an HR manager, I want to create quarterly and annual review templates with goals, competencies, and rating scales so that performance reviews are standardized.
Acceptance Criteria:
  Given I am authenticated as an HR manager
  When I create a review template
  Then the template includes goals, competencies, and rating scales and is available for assignment
Additional Details:
  Priority: Medium
  Story Points: 5
  Dependencies: E02, E03
  Notes: Templates must be editable until assigned to a review cycle.
