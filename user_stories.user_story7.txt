Title: Training & Certification Tracking

User Story: As a safety manager I want to track employee certifications, expirations, and renewals so that only qualified staff are assigned to equipment-sensitive roles and compliance is maintained.

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Certification tracking and enforcement
Given an employee profile with certifications
When a certification is nearing expiry or is expired
Then alerts are sent 30/7 days before expiry, scheduling checks block unqualified assignments, and certification status is visible on the employee profile.

Additional Details:
- Priority: High
- Story Points: 5
- Dependencies: Employee Master Data (E02), Role Based Access Control (E03)
- Notes: Support uploading proof documents and CRUD for certifications.
