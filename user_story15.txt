### E15: Reporting & Analytics

**User Story 1**
Title: Attendance and Overtime Reports

User Story: As an HR I want to generate attendance and overtime reports So that staffing decisions are data-driven.

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Generate Attendance Report
Given attendance data
When I filter by date, department, shift
Then the report is generated and exportable

Additional Details:
- Priority: Medium
- Story Points: 2
- Dependencies: E04, E05, E06, E07, E08, E14
- Notes: Export within 10s for up to 50k rows

---
