Title: Record Safety Incidents

User Story: As a safety officer I want to record incidents and near-misses with severity, location, and involved employees So that safety data is tracked and reported

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Incident recording Given I submit a safety incident When all required fields are validated Then the incident is logged and status workflow begins

Additional Details:
- Priority: High
- Story Points: 3
- Dependencies: Employee CRUD, RBAC
- Notes: OSHA summary export; metrics dashboard endpoints
