Title: Shift Management - Bulk Assignment & Audit
User Story: As a supervisor, I want to bulk-assign shifts and have audit entries generated so that staffing is efficient and changes are tracked.
Acceptance Criteria:
  - Given a list of employees, When I bulk-assign a shift, Then all assignments are created and audit logs are generated.
  - Given an audit request, When I view the logs, Then I see who assigned which shifts and when.
Additional Details:
  Priority: High
  Story Points: 2
  Dependencies: Shift Management
  Notes: Audit entries required.
