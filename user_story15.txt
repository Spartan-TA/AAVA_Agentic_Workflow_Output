Title: Automatic Shift Association for Clock Events

User Story: As a warehouse worker I want my clock-in and clock-out events to be automatically associated with my scheduled shift So that my attendance is accurately tracked

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Shift association
Given a scheduled shift for an employee
When they clock in or out
Then the event is linked to the correct shift and daily totals are computed

Additional Details:
- Priority: High
- Story Points: 2
- Dependencies: Employee CRUD API, RBAC, Shift Management
- Notes: Handle missed punches and corrections workflow
