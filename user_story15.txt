Title: Reporting & Analytics
User Story: As an operations manager I want to generate operational reports and dashboards so that data-driven decisions for staffing, safety, and compliance can be made.
Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Report generation and export
Given attendance, overtime, leave, certification, and safety data
When I filter and export reports
Then reports filter by date, department, shift, exports complete within 10s for 50k rows, access is controlled, and metrics endpoints are available for BI.
Additional Details:
- Priority: Medium
- Story Points: 8
- Dependencies: Time & Attendance (Clock In/Out), Shift & Schedule Management, Leave & Absence Management, Training & Certification Tracking, Safety Incidents & OSHA Reporting, Audit Trail & Compliance
- Notes: Export CSV/PDF; basic role-based dashboards.
