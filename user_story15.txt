Title: Certification Tracking

User Story: As an HR I want to track required certifications, expirations, and renewals for employees So that only qualified staff are assigned to sensitive roles

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Add Certification
Given an employee
When I add a certification
Then it appears on their profile

Scenario: Expiry Alert
Given a certification expiring in 30 or 7 days
When the date approaches
Then an alert is sent to the employee and supervisor

Additional Details:
- Priority: High
- Story Points: 5
- Dependencies: Employee CRUD API, Role-Based Endpoint Security
- Notes: Upload proof documents
