Title: Safety Incident Reporting

User Story: As a warehouse employee, I want to report safety incidents so that hazards are addressed quickly.

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Reporting a safety incident
Given an employee witnesses a safety issue
When they submit an incident report
Then the supervisor is notified and the incident is logged

Additional Details:
- Priority: High
- Story Points: 2
- Dependencies: Safety compliance module, notification system
- Notes: Incident reports should be auditable
