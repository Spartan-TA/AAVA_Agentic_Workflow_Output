Title: Reporting & Analytics

User Story: As an operations manager I want to generate and export operational reports and dashboards so that data-driven decisions for staffing, safety, and compliance are enabled.

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Report generation and export
  Given access to reporting tools
  When I filter and export reports by date, department, or shift
  Then exports complete within 10s for up to 50k rows, access is controlled, and metrics endpoints are available for BI

Additional Details:
- Priority: Medium
- Story Points: 7
- Dependencies: Time & Attendance (Clock In/Out), Shift & Schedule Management, Leave & Absence Management, Training & Certification Tracking, Safety Incidents & OSHA Reporting, Audit Trail & Compliance
- Notes: Support for CSV/PDF export and role-based dashboards required.
