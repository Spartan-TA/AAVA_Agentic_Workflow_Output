Title: Onboarding Training Tasks

User Story: As a new employee I want to see and complete required onboarding training tasks So that I can quickly become productive and compliant

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Completing onboarding tasks
Given I am a new hire with assigned training tasks
When I view my onboarding checklist and complete tasks
Then the system should track my progress and notify HR upon completion

Additional Details:
- Priority: High
- Story Points: 3
- Dependencies: Task management, notification module
- Notes: Support for both mandatory and optional tasks.
