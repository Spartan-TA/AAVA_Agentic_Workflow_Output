Title: Integration Hooks to Exclude Approved Leave from Scheduling and Payroll

User Story: As a system integrator I want approved leave to automatically exclude employees from scheduling and payroll calculations so that staffing and pay are accurate.

Acceptance Criteria:

Scenario: Approved leave blocks shift assignment
Given an employee has approved leave for a date
When the scheduler runs
Then the employee is not assigned to any shifts on that date

Scenario: Approved leave excluded from payroll hours
Given an employee has approved unpaid leave
When payroll is calculated
Then hours for the leave period are not included in pay

Scenario: Integration hooks documented
Given the system exposes integration points
When I review the documentation
Then I see how leave status affects scheduling and payroll modules

Additional Details:
- Priority: Medium
- Story Points: 3
- Dependencies: E02, E03, E05
- Notes: Ensures data consistency across modules.
