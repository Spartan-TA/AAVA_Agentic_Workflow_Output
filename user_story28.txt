Title: Leave Management - Accrual Balance Update
User Story: As an employee, I want my leave balances to update automatically when requests are approved or denied so that I always see my current available leave.
Acceptance Criteria:
  Given a leave request is approved or denied
  When the status changes
  Then the employee's accrual balance is updated accordingly
Additional Details:
  Priority: Medium
  Story Points: 5
  Dependencies: E02, E03, E05
  Notes: Balance updates must be reflected in scheduling and payroll integration.
