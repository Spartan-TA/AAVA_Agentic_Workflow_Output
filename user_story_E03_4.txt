Title: SUPERVISOR Role Constraints

User Story: As a SUPERVISOR I want to manage only my team's records So that I can oversee my direct reports without accessing other teams' sensitive data

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: SUPERVISOR access
Given an authenticated SUPERVISOR user
When they access employee management endpoints
Then they can only view and edit records for employees assigned to their team

Additional Details:
- Priority: High
- Story Points: 2
- Dependencies: E01, E02
- Notes: Enforce row-level security for team-based access.
