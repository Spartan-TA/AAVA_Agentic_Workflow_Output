Title: Incident Investigation and Corrective Actions Workflow

User Story: As a safety manager I want to manage the workflow for incident investigation and corrective actions so that all incidents are properly reviewed and resolved.

Acceptance Criteria:

Scenario: Incident status workflow
Given a new incident is recorded
When investigation begins
Then the status changes to 'Investigating'

Scenario: Corrective actions documented
Given an incident is under investigation
When corrective actions are identified
Then they are recorded and linked to the incident

Scenario: Incident resolved
Given corrective actions are completed
When the incident is closed
Then the status changes to 'Resolved' and all actions are logged

Additional Details:
- Priority: High
- Story Points: 5
- Dependencies: E02, E03
- Notes: Ensures accountability and continuous improvement.
