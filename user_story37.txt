Title: Safety Incidents - Investigation Workflow
User Story: As a safety officer, I want to manage incident investigation and corrective actions so that all safety events are properly resolved and documented.
Acceptance Criteria:
  Given an incident is recorded
  When I update the status to Investigating or Resolved
  Then the workflow tracks progress and corrective actions taken
Additional Details:
  Priority: High
  Story Points: 8
  Dependencies: E02, E03
  Notes: Status workflow must include Open, Investigating, and Resolved states.
