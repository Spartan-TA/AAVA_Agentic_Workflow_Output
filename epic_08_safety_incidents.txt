EPIC 08: SAFETY INCIDENTS & OSHA REPORTING

===========================================

Title: Record Safety Incidents
User Story: As a supervisor I want to record safety incidents and near-misses So that investigations and reporting are accurate
Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Incident recording
Given incident details
When I submit a safety incident
Then the incident is validated and stored

Additional Details:
- Priority: High
- Story Points: 2
- Dependencies: Employee CRUD, RBAC
- Notes: Severity, location, involved employees

---

Title: Safety Incident Workflow
User Story: As a safety officer I want a workflow for investigating and resolving incidents So that corrective actions are tracked
Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Incident status workflow
Given an open incident
When I update the status
Then the workflow progresses from Open to Investigating to Resolved

Additional Details:
- Priority: High
- Story Points: 2
- Dependencies: Record Safety Incidents
- Notes: Status changes logged

---

Title: OSHA Summary Export
User Story: As a compliance manager I want to export OSHA summary reports So that regulatory requirements are met
Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: OSHA report export
Given recorded incidents
When I request an export
Then OSHA 300/300A fields are included in the report

Additional Details:
- Priority: High
- Story Points: 1
- Dependencies: Record Safety Incidents
- Notes: Export format matches OSHA requirements

---

Title: Safety Metrics Dashboard
User Story: As a manager I want a dashboard of safety metrics So that I can monitor KPIs and trends
Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Metrics dashboard endpoint
Given safety incident data
When I access the dashboard
Then metrics and KPIs are displayed

Additional Details:
- Priority: High
- Story Points: 1
- Dependencies: Record Safety Incidents
- Notes: Endpoint for BI integration
