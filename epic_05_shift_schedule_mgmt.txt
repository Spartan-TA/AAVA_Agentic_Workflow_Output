EPIC 05: SHIFT & SCHEDULE MANAGEMENT

===========================================

Title: Create Shift Templates
User Story: As an HR manager I want to create recurring shift templates So that scheduling is efficient and consistent
Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Shift template CRUD
Given shift template data
When I create, update, or delete a template
Then the template is stored and available for assignment

Additional Details:
- Priority: High
- Story Points: 2
- Dependencies: Employee CRUD, RBAC, Attendance
- Notes: Support rotations and overtime rules

---

Title: Assign Shifts to Employees
User Story: As a supervisor I want to assign shifts to employees So that coverage is ensured for warehouse operations
Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Shift assignment
Given available shift templates
When I assign shifts to employees
Then assignments are validated and conflicts prevented

Additional Details:
- Priority: High
- Story Points: 2
- Dependencies: Shift Templates
- Notes: Bulk-assign supported

---

Title: Detect and Prevent Schedule Conflicts
User Story: As a scheduler I want the system to detect and prevent scheduling conflicts So that employees are not double-booked
Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Conflict detection
Given existing assignments
When I assign a new shift
Then the system checks for conflicts and blocks invalid assignments

Additional Details:
- Priority: High
- Story Points: 2
- Dependencies: Shift Templates, Assign Shifts
- Notes: Audit entries generated

---

Title: Personal Upcoming Shifts View
User Story: As a worker I want to view my upcoming shifts So that I can plan my work schedule
Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Upcoming shifts display
Given assigned shifts
When I access my schedule
Then my personal upcoming shifts are listed

Additional Details:
- Priority: High
- Story Points: 1
- Dependencies: Shift Templates, Assign Shifts
- Notes: Accessible via web/mobile
