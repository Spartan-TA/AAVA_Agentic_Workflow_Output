Title: Certification Tracking - Block Assignment for Expired Certs
User Story: As a supervisor, I want the system to block assignment of tasks to employees with expired certifications so that only qualified staff are scheduled for safety-critical work.
Acceptance Criteria:
  Given an employee's certification is expired
  When I attempt to assign them to a safety-critical task
  Then the system prevents the assignment and displays a compliance warning
Additional Details:
  Priority: High
  Story Points: 8
  Dependencies: E02, E03
  Notes: Blocking logic must be enforced for all certification-dependent tasks.
