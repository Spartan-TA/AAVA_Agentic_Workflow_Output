Title: Training & Certification Tracking

User Story: As a safety manager I want to track employee certifications and block assignments for expired certs So that only qualified staff operate equipment.

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Certification expiry alert
Given a certification is expiring in 30/7 days
When the date approaches
Then the system sends an alert to the employee and supervisor

Scenario: Assignment block
Given an employee's certification is expired
When I try to assign them to a restricted task
Then the system blocks the assignment

Additional Details:
- Priority: High
- Story Points: 8
- Dependencies: E02, E03
- Notes: Certification status visible on profile; proof documents uploadable.
