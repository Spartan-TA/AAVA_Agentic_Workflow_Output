### E07: Training & Certification Tracking

**User Story 1**
Title: Certification CRUD Operations

User Story: As an HR I want to manage employee certifications So that compliance and safety are ensured.

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Manage Certifications
Given employee certification data
When I create, update, or delete a certification
Then the changes are reflected in the employee profile

Additional Details:
- Priority: High
- Story Points: 2
- Dependencies: E02, E03
- Notes: Proof documents can be uploaded

---

**User Story 2**
Title: Certification Expiry Alerts

User Story: As a SUPERVISOR I want to receive alerts for expiring certifications So that renewals are handled proactively.

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Expiry Alert
Given a certification expiring in 30 or 7 days
When the alert job runs
Then notifications are sent to relevant users

Additional Details:
- Priority: High
- Story Points: 1
- Dependencies: Story 1
- Notes: Alerts visible in-app and via email/SMS

---

**User Story 3**
Title: Assignment Blocking for Expired Certifications

User Story: As a SYSTEM I want to block assignment to tasks requiring valid certifications So that only qualified staff are assigned.

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Assignment Block
Given an expired certification
When I attempt to assign a task requiring certification
Then the system blocks the assignment

Additional Details:
- Priority: High
- Story Points: 2
- Dependencies: Story 1
- Notes: Status visible on employee profile

---

**User Story 4**
Title: Certification Status Visibility

User Story: As an HR I want to view certification status on employee profiles So that qualification is easily verified.

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: View Certification Status
Given an employee profile
When I view the profile
Then all certifications and their status are displayed

Additional Details:
- Priority: High
- Story Points: 1
- Dependencies: Story 1
- Notes: Expiry dates and proof documents shown
