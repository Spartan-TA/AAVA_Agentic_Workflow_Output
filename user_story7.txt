Title: Training & Certification Tracking
User Story: As a safety manager I want to track employee certifications So that only qualified staff are assigned to equipment-sensitive roles
Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Certification expiry and assignment block
Given a certification is expiring
When it is within 30/7 days of expiry
Then alerts are sent, and scheduling blocks unqualified assignments
Additional Details:
- Priority: High
- Story Points: 5
- Dependencies: Employee Master Data, RBAC
- Notes: Upload proof documents; status visible on profile
