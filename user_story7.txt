Title: Task Assignment to Employees

User Story: As a warehouse supervisor, I want to assign specific tasks to employees during their shifts so that operations run smoothly.

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Assigning tasks
Given a list of tasks and available employees
When the supervisor assigns tasks
Then employees receive task notifications and can update status

Additional Details:
- Priority: High
- Story Points: 4
- Dependencies: Task management module, notification system
- Notes: Task assignments should consider employee skills and workload
