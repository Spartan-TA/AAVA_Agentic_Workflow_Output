Title: Training & Certification Tracking

User Story: As a safety manager I want to track employee certifications and expirations so that only qualified staff are assigned to equipment-sensitive roles and compliance is maintained.

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Certification tracking and assignment blocking
  Given an employee with required certifications
  When a certification is nearing expiry or is expired
  Then alerts are sent 30/7 days before expiry, scheduling checks block unqualified assignments, and certification status is visible on the employee profile
  And proof documents can be uploaded

Additional Details:
- Priority: High
- Story Points: 6
- Dependencies: Employee Master Data (CRUD), Role Based Access Control (RBAC)
- Notes: Ensure CRUD for certifications and alerting logic is implemented.
