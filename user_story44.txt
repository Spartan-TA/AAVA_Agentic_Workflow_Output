Title: Asset Assignment - Asset and Employee History Log
User Story: As an asset manager, I want to view the history log for each asset and employee so that all check-in/out and condition changes are traceable.
Acceptance Criteria:
  Given I am authenticated as an asset manager
  When I view an asset or employee profile
  Then I see a complete history log of asset assignments and condition changes
Additional Details:
  Priority: Medium
  Story Points: 5
  Dependencies: E02, E03, E07
  Notes: History log must be exportable and include timestamps, actions, and actors.
