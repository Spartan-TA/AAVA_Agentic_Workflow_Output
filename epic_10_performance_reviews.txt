EPIC 10: PERFORMANCE REVIEWS & GOALS

===========================================

Title: Create Performance Review Templates
User Story: As an HR manager I want to create quarterly and annual review templates So that performance evaluations are standardized
Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Review template creation
Given template data
When I create a review template
Then it is available for assignment to employees

Additional Details:
- Priority: Medium
- Story Points: 2
- Dependencies: Employee CRUD, RBAC
- Notes: Track goals and competencies

---

Title: Assign Performance Reviews to Employees
User Story: As a supervisor I want to assign performance reviews to employees So that evaluations are conducted on schedule
Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Review assignment
Given review templates
When I assign reviews to employees
Then the review cycle is initiated

Additional Details:
- Priority: Medium
- Story Points: 2
- Dependencies: Create Performance Review Templates
- Notes: Role-based visibility

---

Title: Submit and Acknowledge Reviews
User Story: As an employee I want to submit and acknowledge my performance review So that feedback is documented
Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Review submission and acknowledgement
Given an assigned review
When I submit or acknowledge the review
Then the review is marked as completed and history is immutable

Additional Details:
- Priority: Medium
- Story Points: 2
- Dependencies: Assign Performance Reviews
- Notes: PDF export available
