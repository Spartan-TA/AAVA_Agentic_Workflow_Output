Title: Training & Certification - Track Certifications
User Story: As a safety manager, I want to track employee certifications and expirations so that only qualified staff are assigned to sensitive roles.
Acceptance Criteria:
  - Given a certification, When I add it to an employee, Then it appears on their profile.
  - Given an expiring certification, When it is 30 or 7 days from expiry, Then an alert is generated.
  - Given an expired certification, When scheduling assignments, Then the system blocks unqualified assignments.
Additional Details:
  Priority: High
  Story Points: 3
  Dependencies: Employee CRUD, RBAC
  Notes: Upload proof documents.
