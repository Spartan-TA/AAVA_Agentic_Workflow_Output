Title: Time Tracking for Shifts

User Story: As a warehouse employee, I want to clock in and out for shifts so that my working hours are accurately recorded.

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Clocking in and out
Given an employee is scheduled for a shift
When they clock in at the start and out at the end
Then their time is logged and available for payroll

Additional Details:
- Priority: High
- Story Points: 2
- Dependencies: Time tracking module, shift scheduling module
- Notes: Time logs should be tamper-proof
