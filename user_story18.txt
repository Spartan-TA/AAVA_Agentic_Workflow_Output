Title: Attendance - Missed Punch Correction Workflow
User Story: As a worker, I want to submit corrections for missed clock-in/out events so that my attendance record is accurate and approved by my supervisor.
Acceptance Criteria:
  Given I missed a clock-in or clock-out event
  When I submit a correction request
  Then an approval task is generated for my supervisor and my record is updated upon approval
Additional Details:
  Priority: High
  Story Points: 8
  Dependencies: E02, E03
  Notes: Correction workflow must be auditable and exportable.
