Title: Performance Reviews - Immutable History After Sign-Off
User Story: As an HR manager, I want review history to be immutable after supervisor and employee sign-off so that records are tamper-proof and compliant.
Acceptance Criteria:
  Given a review is signed off by both supervisor and employee
  When I view the review history
  Then no further changes are allowed and the record is locked
Additional Details:
  Priority: Medium
  Story Points: 5
  Dependencies: E02, E03
  Notes: Locked history must be visible to authorized roles and exportable.
