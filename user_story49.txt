Title: Goal Tracking, Competencies, and Ratings

User Story: As a supervisor I want to track employee goals, competencies, and ratings during reviews so that feedback is structured and actionable.

Acceptance Criteria:

Scenario: Add goals and competencies to review
Given a review template
When I assign goals and competencies
Then they are included in the review for scoring

Scenario: Supervisor rates employee
Given a review is in progress
When the supervisor enters ratings and comments
Then the data is saved and visible to the employee after submission

Scenario: Goals tracked over time
Given multiple review cycles
When I view an employee's profile
Then I see historical goals and progress

Additional Details:
- Priority: Medium
- Story Points: 3
- Dependencies: E02, E03
- Notes: Supports employee development and retention.
