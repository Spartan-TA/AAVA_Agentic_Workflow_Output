Title: RBAC - SUPERVISOR Team Constraints
User Story: As a SUPERVISOR, I want to access and manage records only for my assigned team so that I can fulfill my responsibilities without overreaching access.
Acceptance Criteria:
  Given I am authenticated as a SUPERVISOR
  When I access employee or attendance records
  Then I can only view and manage records for my team members
Additional Details:
  Priority: High
  Story Points: 5
  Dependencies: E01, E02
  Notes: Row-level security must enforce team-based constraints.
