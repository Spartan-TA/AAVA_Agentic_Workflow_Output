Title: Integration Layer - HRIS Sync

User Story: As an ADMIN I want to sync employee data with the HRIS So that records are consistent across systems.

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: HRIS sync job
Given new hires in HRIS
When the sync job runs
Then new employees are created or updated in EMS

Additional Details:
- Priority: High
- Story Points: 3
- Dependencies: E01, E02, E03
- Notes: Idempotent sync
