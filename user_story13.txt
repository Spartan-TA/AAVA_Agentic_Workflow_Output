Title: Certification Tracking and Alerts

User Story: As a safety manager I want to track employee certifications and receive alerts before expiration So that compliance is maintained

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Certification expiry alert Given a certification is nearing expiry When it is 30 or 7 days before expiry Then an alert is sent to the employee and supervisor

Additional Details:
- Priority: High
- Story Points: 3
- Dependencies: Employee CRUD, RBAC
- Notes: Upload proof documents; block assignment to tasks requiring expired certs
