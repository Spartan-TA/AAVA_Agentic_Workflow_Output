Title: Shift Management - Create Shift Templates
User Story: As an HR manager, I want to create recurring shift templates so that scheduling is efficient and consistent.
Acceptance Criteria:
  - Given shift details, When I POST to /shifts/templates, Then a new template is created.
  - Given a template, When I assign it to employees, Then their schedules are updated accordingly.
Additional Details:
  Priority: High
  Story Points: 2
  Dependencies: Employee CRUD, RBAC, Time & Attendance
  Notes: Supports rotations and overtime rules.
