Title: Assign Shifts to Employees and Bulk Assignment

User Story: As a supervisor I want to assign shifts to individual employees or in bulk so that I can efficiently manage team schedules and ensure adequate staffing.

Acceptance Criteria:

Scenario: Assign shift to employee
Given a valid employee and shift template
When I POST to /schedules/assign
Then the shift is assigned to the employee

Scenario: Bulk assign shifts
Given a list of employees and a shift template
When I POST to /schedules/bulk-assign
Then the shift is assigned to all selected employees

Scenario: Assignment conflicts detected
Given an employee already has a shift assigned
When I attempt to assign an overlapping shift
Then the system prevents the assignment and returns a conflict error

Additional Details:
- Priority: High
- Story Points: 5
- Dependencies: E02, E03, E04
- Notes: Reduces manual scheduling errors and ensures coverage.
