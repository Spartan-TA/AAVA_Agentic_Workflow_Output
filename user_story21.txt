Title: Shift Management - Create Recurring Shift Templates
User Story: As an HR manager, I want to create recurring shift templates and assign them to employees so that scheduling is efficient and consistent.
Acceptance Criteria:
  Given I am authenticated as an HR manager
  When I create a shift template with recurrence rules
  Then the template is saved and can be assigned to employees
Additional Details:
  Priority: High
  Story Points: 5
  Dependencies: E02, E03, E04
  Notes: Recurrence options must include daily, weekly, and custom intervals.
