Title: Leave Management - PTO Request Submission
User Story: As an employee, I want to submit paid time off (PTO) requests so that my leave is tracked and processed according to company policy.
Acceptance Criteria:
  Given I am authenticated as an employee
  When I submit a PTO request
  Then my request is recorded and routed for supervisor approval
Additional Details:
  Priority: Medium
  Story Points: 3
  Dependencies: E02, E03, E05
  Notes: PTO requests must validate against accrual balances and policies.
