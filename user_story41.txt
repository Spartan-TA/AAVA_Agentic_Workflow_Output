Title: Assign Equipment and PPE to Employees

User Story: As a supervisor I want to assign scanners, forklifts, and PPE to employees so that asset usage is tracked and only qualified staff use equipment.

Acceptance Criteria:

Scenario: Assign asset to employee
Given an available asset and qualified employee
When I POST to /assets/assign
Then the asset is checked out to the employee

Scenario: Asset assignment blocked by missing certification
Given an employee lacks required certification
When I attempt to assign a restricted asset
Then the system prevents the assignment

Scenario: Asset status updated
Given an asset is assigned or returned
When the action occurs
Then the asset status is updated accordingly

Additional Details:
- Priority: Medium
- Story Points: 5
- Dependencies: E02, E03, E07
- Notes: Ensures accountability and safety compliance.
