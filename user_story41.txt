Title: Asset Assignment - Asset Registry CRUD
User Story: As an asset manager, I want to create, update, and delete asset records so that equipment and PPE are tracked and managed efficiently.
Acceptance Criteria:
  Given I am authenticated as an asset manager
  When I manage asset records
  Then I can add, update, or remove assets from the registry
Additional Details:
  Priority: Medium
  Story Points: 5
  Dependencies: E02, E03, E07
  Notes: Asset records must include type, condition, assignment status, and history.
