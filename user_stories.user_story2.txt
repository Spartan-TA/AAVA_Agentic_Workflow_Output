Title: Employee Master Data (CRUD)

User Story: As an HR manager I want to manage employee records with CRUD operations so that I maintain a single source of truth for all warehouse staff.

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Employee record management
Given I have access to the employee management module
When I create, read, update, patch, or delete employee records
Then unique badgeId is enforced, soft-delete is supported, pagination and filtering are available, and OpenAPI schemas with examples are provided.

Additional Details:
- Priority: High
- Story Points: 8
- Dependencies: Project Scaffolding & Domain Setup (E01)
- Notes: Ensure badgeId uniqueness and support for soft-delete.
