In the boardroom, a sly little fox,
Always scheming, yet dressed in fine socks.
His motives opaque,
Decisions he'll fake,
Yet he's first to take home the gift box.

The cubicle dwellers all cheer,
For the coffee pot sits rather near.
But beware the brigade,
Who plot masquerade,
And claim all the donuts each year.

The manager struts down the hall,
With memos that threaten a brawl.
"Deadlines are tight,
Stay late every night!"
But it's clear he just wants us to stall.

So here's to the office charade,
Where alliances and schemes are displayed.
If you play it just right,
You'll escape every fight,
And still make it to lunchtime parade.
