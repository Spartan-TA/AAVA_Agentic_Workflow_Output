Title: Notification Templates and Localization

User Story: As a system administrator I want to manage notification templates and support localization so that messages are consistent and available in multiple languages.

Acceptance Criteria:

Scenario: Manage notification templates
Given notification templates exist
When I create, update, or delete a template
Then the changes are reflected in all outgoing notifications

Scenario: Localize notification templates
Given multiple languages are supported
When I configure a template in a new language
Then users receive notifications in their preferred language

Scenario: Templates versioned and auditable
Given templates are updated
When I review the template history
Then all changes are logged and auditable

Additional Details:
- Priority: Medium
- Story Points: 3
- Dependencies: E03, E05, E06, E07
- Notes: Supports global workforce and compliance.
