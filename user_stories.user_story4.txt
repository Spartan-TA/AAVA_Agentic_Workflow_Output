Title: Time & Attendance (Clock In/Out)

User Story: As a warehouse worker I want to clock in and out with device and location validation so that my worked hours are accurately captured for payroll and compliance.

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Accurate time capture
Given I am scheduled for a shift
When I clock in or out using the provided endpoint
Then my event is validated, automatically associated with my shift, daily totals are computed, corrections create approval tasks, and reports are exportable in CSV format.

Additional Details:
- Priority: High
- Story Points: 8
- Dependencies: Employee Master Data (E02), Role Based Access Control (E03)
- Notes: Handle missed punches and corrections workflow.
