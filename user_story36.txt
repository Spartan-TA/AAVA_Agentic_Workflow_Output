Title: Safety Incidents - Record Incident/Near-Miss
User Story: As a supervisor, I want to record safety incidents and near-misses with severity, location, and involved employees so that compliance and corrective actions are tracked.
Acceptance Criteria:
  Given I am authenticated as a supervisor
  When I POST to /safety/incidents with required details
  Then the incident is validated and recorded with all relevant fields
Additional Details:
  Priority: High
  Story Points: 5
  Dependencies: E02, E03
  Notes: Incident records must include severity, location, description, and employee list.
