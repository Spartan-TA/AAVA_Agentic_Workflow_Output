Title: Record Safety Incidents and Near-Misses

User Story: As a safety manager I want to record incidents and near-misses with severity, location, and involved employees so that I can track safety trends and take corrective action.

Acceptance Criteria:

Scenario: Record new incident
Given an incident occurs
When I POST to /safety/incidents
Then the incident is saved with all required details

Scenario: Incident includes severity and location
Given an incident is recorded
When I view the incident details
Then severity and location are present

Scenario: Involved employees linked
Given an incident involves employees
When the incident is saved
Then all involved employees are linked to the record

Additional Details:
- Priority: High
- Story Points: 5
- Dependencies: E02, E03
- Notes: Foundation for OSHA compliance and safety improvement.
