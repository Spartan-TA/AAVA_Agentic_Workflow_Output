EpicID: E12
Title: Notifications & Announcements
User Story: As a worker, I want to receive notifications about shifts, certifications, and announcements so that I stay informed in real time.
Acceptance Criteria:
  Scenario: Opt-In/Out
    Given notification channels
    When I update preferences
    Then my choices are saved
  Scenario: Localized Templates
    Given a notification
    When sent
    Then template is localized to user language
  Scenario: Delivery Status
    Given a sent notification
    When I view status
    Then delivery is tracked
  Scenario: Rate Limits
    Given high notification volume
    When limits are exceeded
    Then excess notifications are queued or dropped
Additional Details:
  Priority: Medium
  Story Points: 8
  Dependencies: E03, E05, E06, E07
  Notes: Announcements must be visible on dashboard
