EPIC E04: Time & Attendance (Clock In/Out)

Title: Clock-In Endpoint
User Story: As a warehouse worker I want to clock in at the start of my shift So that my attendance is accurately recorded
Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Clock-in
Given I am scheduled for a shift
When I POST to /attendance/clock-in
Then my clock-in event is recorded with timestamp and device info
Additional Details:
- Priority: High
- Story Points: 2
- Dependencies: E02, E03
- Notes: Optional geofence validation

---

Title: Clock-Out Endpoint
User Story: As a warehouse worker I want to clock out at the end of my shift So that my worked hours are tracked
Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Clock-out
Given I am clocked in
When I POST to /attendance/clock-out
Then my clock-out event is recorded and hours are calculated
Additional Details:
- Priority: High
- Story Points: 2
- Dependencies: E02, E03
- Notes: Validate shift association
