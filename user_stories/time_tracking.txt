Title: Time Tracking

User Story: As a warehouse employee, I want to clock in and clock out So that my work hours are accurately recorded.

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Clock in/out
Given I am scheduled for a shift
When I clock in at the start and clock out at the end
Then my hours are logged in the system

Additional Details:
- Priority: High
- Story Points: 2
- Dependencies: Shift Scheduling
- Notes: Supports biometric or badge-based clocking
