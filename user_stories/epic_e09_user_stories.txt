EPIC E09: Equipment & Asset Assignment

===========================================

Title: Equipment & Asset Assignment

User Story: As a warehouse supervisor I want to assign equipment and PPE to employees So that asset usage is tracked

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Asset assignment
Given assets are registered
When I assign to an employee
Then the assignment is logged

Additional Details:
- Priority: Medium
- Story Points: 2
- Dependencies: Employee CRUD, Certification tracking
- Notes: Check-in/out endpoints

---

Title: Prevent Asset Use with Invalid Certification

User Story: As a supervisor I want to prevent asset use if certification is missing So that safety is maintained

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Asset use block
Given an employee lacks certification
When I assign equipment
Then the system blocks the assignment

Additional Details:
- Priority: Medium
- Story Points: 2
- Dependencies: Asset assignment, Certification tracking
- Notes: History log per asset
