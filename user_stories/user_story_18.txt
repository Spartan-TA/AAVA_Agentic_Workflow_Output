Title: Employee Views Schedule

User Story: As an employee I want to view my assigned shifts So that I can plan my work and personal commitments

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Viewing assigned shifts
Given I am logged in as an employee
When I access my schedule page
Then I should see all my upcoming shifts with dates and times

Additional Details:
- Priority: High
- Story Points: 2
- Dependencies: Shift assignment
- Notes: Display schedule in both calendar and list formats.
