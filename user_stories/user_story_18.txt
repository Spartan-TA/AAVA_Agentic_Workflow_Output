Title: Employee Clock In

User Story: As an employee I want to clock in at the start of my shift So that my attendance is recorded

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Successful clock in
  Given I am logged in
  When I click the clock in button
  Then my start time should be recorded

Additional Details:
- Priority: High
- Story Points: 2
- Dependencies: Attendance module
- Notes: Prevent duplicate clock-ins
