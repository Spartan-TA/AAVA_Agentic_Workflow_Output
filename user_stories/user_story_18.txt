Title: Assign Shifts

User Story: As a scheduler I want to assign shifts to employees using templates So that I can efficiently manage workforce coverage

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Assign shift using template
Given I have a list of employees and available shift templates
When I select employees and assign a template
Then the shifts should be reflected in the schedule and visible to assigned employees

Additional Details:
- Priority: High
- Story Points: 5
- Dependencies: Shift Templates, Employee Directory
- Notes: Bulk assignment should be supported.
