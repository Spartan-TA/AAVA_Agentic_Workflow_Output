Title: Bulk Shift Assignment
User Story: As a supervisor I want to assign multiple employees to shifts in bulk So that scheduling large teams is fast and error-free
Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Bulk assignment Given I select multiple employees and a shift template When I confirm the assignment Then all selected employees are scheduled and audit entries are generated
Additional Details:
- Priority: High
- Story Points: 2
- Dependencies: Shift templates, employee master data
- Notes: Bulk actions should be logged for compliance.
