Title: Employee Requests Leave

User Story: As an employee I want to request paid or unpaid leave through the system so that my absence is tracked and approved efficiently

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Submitting a leave request
Given I am logged in as an employee
When I fill out and submit a leave request form specifying dates and type (PTO, sick, unpaid)
Then the request should appear in my pending requests and notify my supervisor for approval

Additional Details:
- Priority: High
- Story Points: 3
- Dependencies: Employee Master Data, RBAC
- Notes: Validation for overlapping leave and sufficient accrual balance required.
