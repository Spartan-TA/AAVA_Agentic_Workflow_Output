Title: Assign Shifts to Employees

User Story: As a supervisor I want to assign shifts to employees in bulk or individually So that I can ensure adequate staffing and reduce scheduling conflicts

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Bulk assign shifts
Given a list of employees and a shift template
When I assign the shift
Then all selected employees receive the assignment

Scenario: Conflict detection
Given an employee with an existing shift
When I assign an overlapping shift
Then the system prevents the assignment and notifies me of the conflict

Additional Details:
- Priority: High
- Story Points: 5
- Dependencies: Employee CRUD, RBAC, Attendance, Shift Templates
- Notes: Audit entries must be generated for assignments
