Title: Detect and Prevent Shift Assignment Conflicts

User Story: As a supervisor I want the system to detect and prevent scheduling conflicts when assigning shifts so that employees are not double-booked

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Assigning overlapping shifts
Given an employee is already assigned to a shift
When I attempt to assign another overlapping shift
Then the system blocks the assignment and notifies me of the conflict

Additional Details:
- Priority: High
- Story Points: 2
- Dependencies: E02, E03, E04, E05
- Notes: Conflict detection must be enforced for blackout dates and warehouse calendars.
