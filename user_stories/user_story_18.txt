Title: Supervisor Approval of Missed Punch Corrections

User Story: As a supervisor I want to review and approve or deny missed punch correction requests So that attendance records remain accurate and auditable

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Correction request received
Given an employee submits a missed punch correction
When I review the request
Then I can approve or deny it, and the employee is notified of the outcome

Scenario: Audit trail
Given a correction is approved or denied
When I view the attendance record
Then the correction and decision are logged with timestamps

Additional Details:
- Priority: High
- Story Points: 2
- Dependencies: Attendance module, RBAC
- Notes: Approval workflow must be tracked for compliance.
