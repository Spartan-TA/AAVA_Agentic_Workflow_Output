Title: Approve/Reject Leave

User Story: As a supervisor I want to approve or reject leave requests So that I can manage team availability and workload

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Approve or reject leave request
Given a leave request is pending
When I review and take action
Then the employee should be notified of the decision

Additional Details:
- Priority: High
- Story Points: 3
- Dependencies: Leave management module, notification system
- Notes: Capture reason for rejection.
