EpicID: E04
Title: Time & Attendance (Clock In/Out)
User Story: As a warehouse worker, I want to clock in and out with device and location capture so that my hours are accurately tracked for payroll.
Acceptance Criteria:
  Scenario: Clock In
    Given I am scheduled for a shift
    When I POST to /attendance/clock-in
    Then my clock-in is recorded with device and location
  Scenario: Clock Out
    Given I am clocked in
    When I POST to /attendance/clock-out
    Then my clock-out is recorded and hours are calculated
  Scenario: Missed Punch Correction
    Given a missed punch
    When I submit a correction
    Then an approval task is created
  Scenario: Attendance Report
    Given attendance data exists
    When I export the report
    Then I receive a CSV file
Additional Details:
  Priority: High
  Story Points: 13
  Dependencies: E02, E03
  Notes: Automatic shift association required
