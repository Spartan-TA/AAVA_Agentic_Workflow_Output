Title: Timezone-Aware Scheduling

User Story: As a supervisor I want schedules to display in the local time zone of each warehouse so that shift times are accurate for all users

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Viewing schedules in local time
Given I am assigned to a warehouse in a specific timezone
When I view my schedule
Then all shift times are displayed in my local timezone

Additional Details:
- Priority: Low
- Story Points: 2
- Dependencies: Scheduling module, timezone library
- Notes: Timezone must be set per tenant and respected in all scheduling views
