Title: Initiate Offboarding Workflow

User Story: As an HR manager I want to initiate an offboarding workflow for departing employees so that all exit tasks are tracked and compliance is ensured

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Starting offboarding
Given I am logged in as an HR manager
When I mark an employee for offboarding
Then the system generates a checklist and assigns tasks to relevant stakeholders

Additional Details:
- Priority: High
- Story Points: 4
- Dependencies: Employee master data, task management
- Notes: Support for custom offboarding templates by role or location.
