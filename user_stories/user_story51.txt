Title: Create Quarterly/Annual Review Templates

User Story: As an HR manager I want to create standardized quarterly and annual review templates So that performance reviews are consistent and aligned with company goals

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Template creation
Given I am on the performance review admin page
When I select 'Create New Template' and define review periods, competencies, and rating scales
Then the template should be saved and available for assignment to employees

Additional Details:
- Priority: Medium
- Story Points: 5
- Dependencies: E02, E03
- Notes: Templates must support custom competencies and rating scales.
