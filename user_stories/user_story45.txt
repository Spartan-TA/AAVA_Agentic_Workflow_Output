Title: Expiring Certification Notification

User Story: As an employee I want to be notified when my certifications are about to expire So that I can renew them in time and remain compliant

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Certification expiry notification
Given my certification is expiring within the configured threshold
When the system detects the upcoming expiry
Then I receive a notification via my selected channel and the notification is visible on my dashboard

Additional Details:
- Priority: Medium
- Story Points: 3
- Dependencies: E03, E05, E06, E07
- Notes: Notification templates must be localized; users can configure quiet hours
