Title: Manage Notification Preferences

User Story: As a user I want to manage my notification preferences So that I only receive relevant alerts through my preferred channels

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Updating notification preferences
Given I am logged into my account
When I access notification settings and update my preferences
Then my choices should be saved and notifications sent accordingly

Additional Details:
- Priority: Medium
- Story Points: 2
- Dependencies: User profile, notification service
- Notes: Support opt-in/opt-out for each channel and notification type.
