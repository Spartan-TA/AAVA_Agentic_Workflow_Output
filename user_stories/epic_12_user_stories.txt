### E12: Notifications & Announcements

**User Story 12.1**
Title: Shift Change Notifications

User Story: As an Employee I want to receive notifications for shift changes So that I am always aware of my schedule.

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Shift notification
Given my shift changes
When the change is saved
Then I receive a notification via my preferred channel

Additional Details:
- Priority: Medium
- Story Points: 3
- Dependencies: E03, E05
- Notes: In-app, email, and SMS supported

---

**User Story 12.2**
Title: Certification Expiry Alerts

User Story: As an Employee I want to be notified when my certifications are about to expire So that I can renew them in time.

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Expiry notification
Given my certification is expiring
When the alert is triggered
Then I receive a notification per my preferences

Additional Details:
- Priority: Medium
- Story Points: 2
- Dependencies: E03, E07
- Notes: Quiet hours respected

---

**User Story 12.3**
Title: Approval Workflow Notifications

User Story: As a Supervisor I want to receive notifications for pending approvals (leave, corrections, etc.) So that I can act promptly.

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Approval notification
Given I have pending approvals
When a new item is assigned
Then I am notified and can access the item directly

Additional Details:
- Priority: Medium
- Story Points: 2
- Dependencies: E03, E06
- Notes: Notification templates localized

---

**User Story 12.4**
Title: Announcements Dashboard

User Story: As an Employee I want to view company announcements on my dashboard So that I stay informed.

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: View announcements
Given announcements are posted
When I log in
Then I see the latest announcements with read/unread status

Additional Details:
- Priority: Medium
- Story Points: 2
- Dependencies: E03
- Notes: Announcements can be targeted by role or department
