Title: Upload Proof Documents for Certifications

User Story: As an employee I want to upload proof documents for certifications So that my qualifications are validated and stored securely

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Uploading certification proof
Given I am updating my certification
When I upload a document
Then it should be stored and linked to my profile

Additional Details:
- Priority: Medium
- Story Points: 2
- Dependencies: Document storage
- Notes: Support PDF, image formats, and expiration reminders.
