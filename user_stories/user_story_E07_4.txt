Title: Upload Proof of Certification Documents
User Story: As an employee I want to upload proof documents for my certifications So that HR can verify my qualifications and maintain compliance records
Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Document upload
Given I have a valid certification
When I upload the proof document
Then the system should store the file securely and link it to my employee profile
Additional Details:
- Priority: High
- Story Points: 2
- Dependencies: Employee module, document storage
- Notes: Uploaded documents must be accessible to HR and protected per privacy standards.
