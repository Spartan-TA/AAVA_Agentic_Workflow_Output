Title: Dashboard Announcements and Quiet Hours

User Story: As an employee I want to see important announcements on my dashboard and configure quiet hours for notifications So that I stay informed without being disturbed outside work hours

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Announcement visibility and quiet hours
Given an announcement is published
When I log in to my dashboard
Then the announcement is visible and notifications respect my configured quiet hours

Additional Details:
- Priority: Medium
- Story Points: 5
- Dependencies: E03, E05, E06, E07
- Notes: Announcements must be localized; quiet hours configurable per user
