Title: Record Safety Incidents and Near-Misses

User Story: As a warehouse supervisor I want to record safety incidents and near-misses So that I can track workplace safety and initiate investigations

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Record incident
Given I witness or am notified of a safety incident
When I submit an incident report with severity, location, description, and involved employees
Then the incident is logged and assigned a status of 'Open'

Additional Details:
- Priority: High
- Story Points: 4
- Dependencies: E02, E03
- Notes: Support validation for required fields and attach supporting documents.
