Title: View Announcement History and Audit Trail

User Story: As an HR administrator I want to view the history and audit trail of sent announcements So that I can verify delivery and review communication compliance

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Viewing announcement history
Given multiple announcements have been sent
When I access the announcement audit log
Then I should see details of each announcement, recipients, and delivery status

Additional Details:
- Priority: Medium
- Story Points: 2
- Dependencies: Notification service, audit trail module
- Notes: Include filters by date, sender, and recipient group.
