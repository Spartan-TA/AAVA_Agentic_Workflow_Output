Title: Employee Clock-Out

User Story: As an employee I want to clock out at the end of my shift So that my worked hours are accurately tracked

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Successful clock-out
Given I am clocked in for a shift
When I clock out via the web or mobile app
Then my clock-out time is recorded and my total hours are calculated

Additional Details:
- Priority: High
- Story Points: 2
- Dependencies: Attendance module, user authentication
- Notes: Prevent clock-out if not clocked in.
