Title: View Assigned Training & Certification Status

User Story: As an employee I want to view my assigned training modules and certification status So that I know what is required and what is completed

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Viewing training status
Given I am logged into the system
When I navigate to my training page
Then I should see all assigned modules and certification expiry dates

Additional Details:
- Priority: Medium
- Story Points: 2
- Dependencies: Training assignment logic
- Notes: Highlight overdue or expiring certifications.
