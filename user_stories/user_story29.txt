Title: Record Safety Incidents

User Story: As a safety officer I want to record safety incidents and near-misses with required fields so that we can track, investigate, and report workplace safety issues.

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Incident record creation
Given I am on the safety incident entry form
When I submit incident details including severity, location, description, and involved employees
Then the system validates required fields and saves the incident

Additional Details:
- Priority: High
- Story Points: 5
- Dependencies: E02, E03
- Notes: Validation for mandatory fields; supports attachments.
