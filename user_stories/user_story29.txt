Title: Initiate Performance Review Cycle

User Story: As an HR manager I want to initiate a performance review cycle so that employees and managers can complete evaluations on schedule

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Launching a review cycle
Given I am logged in as an HR manager
When I initiate a new review cycle
Then all relevant employees and managers are notified and review forms are assigned

Additional Details:
- Priority: High
- Story Points: 3
- Dependencies: Notification module, employee master data
- Notes: Support for annual, semi-annual, and ad-hoc review cycles.
