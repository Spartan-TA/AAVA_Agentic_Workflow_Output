Title: Track Required Certifications

User Story:
As a Training Coordinator, I want to track required certifications (e.g., forklift, first aid) for each role so that only qualified employees are assigned to specific tasks.

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario 1: Assign certification requirements to roles
  Given I am on the role management page
  When I assign required certifications to a role
  Then only employees with valid certifications can be assigned to that role

Scenario 2: View certification status for employees
  Given certification requirements are set
  When I view an employee profile
  Then their certification status is displayed

Scenario 3: Block assignment if certification is missing
  Given an employee lacks a required certification
  When I attempt to assign them to a restricted role
  Then the system prevents the assignment and displays a warning

Priority: High
Story Points: 8
Dependencies: E02, E03
Notes: Must support multiple certifications per role and integrate with asset assignment.
