Title: Safety Incident Investigation Workflow

User Story: As a safety officer I want to manage the investigation workflow for reported incidents So that I can track status and ensure corrective actions are taken

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Incident investigation status
Given an incident is reported
When I update the status to Investigating or Resolved
Then the workflow is tracked and corrective actions can be documented

Additional Details:
- Priority: High
- Story Points: 2
- Dependencies: Safety Incident Recording
- Notes: Status options should include Open, Investigating, and Resolved.
