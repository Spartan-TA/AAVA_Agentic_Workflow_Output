Title: Certification Expiry Alerts and Renewals

User Story:
As an employee, I want to receive alerts 30 and 7 days before my required certifications expire so that I can renew them in time and remain eligible for assignments.

Acceptance Criteria:
- Scenario: Employee receives 30-day expiry alert
  Given a certification is expiring in 30 days
  When the alert job runs
  Then the employee and supervisor are notified

- Scenario: Employee receives 7-day expiry alert
  Given a certification is expiring in 7 days
  When the alert job runs
  Then the employee and supervisor are notified

Additional Details:
- Alerts must be sent via in-app notification and email/SMS (if configured)
- Alert templates must be localized
- All alerts must be logged for audit
