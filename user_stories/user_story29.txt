Title: Training & Certification Reporting

User Story: As a COMPLIANCE OFFICER I want to generate reports on training and certification status So that I can ensure regulatory compliance and prepare for audits

Acceptance Criteria:

Scenario: Compliance officer generates report
Given training and certification data is available
When I run a compliance report
Then I see all employees with expired, expiring, or missing certifications

Scenario: Export report
Given a report is generated
When I choose export
Then the report is available in CSV and PDF formats

Additional Details:
- Priority: Medium
- Story Points: 5
- Dependencies: E07-1, E07-2, E07-3
- Notes: Reports should be filterable by department, location, and date.
