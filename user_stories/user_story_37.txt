Title: Set and Track Employee Goals

User Story: As an employee I want to set personal and professional goals within the performance review system so that I can track my progress and align with company objectives

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Goal setting and progress tracking
Given I am participating in a review cycle
When I add or update my goals in the system
Then my goals should be saved and visible to my manager and HR, with progress tracking enabled

Additional Details:
- Priority: Medium
- Story Points: 2
- Dependencies: Employee portal, Review cycle management
- Notes: Allow comments and updates on goals throughout the year
