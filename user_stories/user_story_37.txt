Title: Investigate Safety Incidents

User Story: As a safety officer I want to investigate reported safety incidents So that I can determine root causes and recommend corrective actions

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Safety incident investigation
Given a safety incident is reported
When I review the incident details
Then I should be able to document findings and assign corrective actions

Additional Details:
- Priority: High
- Story Points: 3
- Dependencies: Incident reporting
- Notes: Support for attaching evidence and notes
