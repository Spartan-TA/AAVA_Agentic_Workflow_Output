Title: Create Performance Review Cycles and Templates

User Story: As an HR administrator I want to create quarterly and annual performance review cycles and templates so that employee evaluations are standardized and repeatable

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Create review cycle and template
  Given I am an HR administrator
  When I define a new review cycle and template with goals, competencies, and rating scales
  Then the template is available for assignment to employees

Additional Details:
- Priority: Medium
- Story Points: 3
- Dependencies: E02 (User Management), E03 (Scheduling)
- Notes: Templates must be version-controlled
