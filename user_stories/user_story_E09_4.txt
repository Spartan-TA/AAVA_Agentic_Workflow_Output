Title: Asset Condition State and History Log

User Story: As an Asset Manager I want to maintain a condition state and history log for each asset so that equipment usage and maintenance can be tracked over time

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Update asset condition
Given an asset is checked out or returned
When I update its condition (e.g., damaged, repaired)
Then the change is logged in the asset history

Scenario: View asset history
Given an asset has multiple condition updates
When I view the asset details
Then I see a chronological log of all changes with timestamps and actors

Scenario: Export asset history
Given I am viewing an asset's history
When I export the log
Then I receive a CSV or PDF file of all condition changes

Additional Details:
- Priority: Medium
- Story Points: 3
- Dependencies: Employee Master Data (E02), RBAC (E03), Certification Tracking (E07)
- Notes: History log must be immutable and auditable
