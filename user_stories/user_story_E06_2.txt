Title: Leave Accrual Balances and Policy Enforcement

User Story: As an HR manager I want leave accrual balances and policies to be enforced automatically So that employees' leave requests are validated against their available balances and company rules

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Leave request exceeds balance
Given an employee submits a leave request
When the requested days exceed their available balance
Then the system blocks the request and displays an error message

Scenario: Accrual policy update
Given company leave policies change
When I update the accrual rules
Then all future leave calculations use the new policy

Additional Details:
- Priority: Medium
- Story Points: 3
- Dependencies: E02, E03, E05
- Notes: Accruals must be calculated monthly; policy changes must not retroactively affect approved leave.
