Title: Supervisor Leave Approval Workflow

User Story: As a supervisor I want to review and approve or deny employee leave requests so that staffing plans remain accurate and compliant

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Approve or deny leave request
Given a pending leave request is assigned to me
When I review the request and select approve or deny
Then the employee is notified of the decision and the leave balance is updated accordingly

Additional Details:
- Priority: Medium
- Story Points: 2
- Dependencies: Employee Master Data, RBAC, Shift & Schedule Management
- Notes: Approval/denial reasons must be logged; notifications sent via preferred channel.
