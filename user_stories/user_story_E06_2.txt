Title: Approve or Deny Leave Requests

User Story: As a supervisor I want to approve or deny leave requests So that I can manage team availability and ensure coverage

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Reviewing a leave request
Given a leave request is pending
When I review and take action
Then the employee should be notified of the decision and the schedule updated accordingly

Additional Details:
- Priority: High
- Story Points: 2
- Dependencies: Notification system
- Notes: Support bulk approval and comments.
