Title: Approve Leave Requests

User Story: As a supervisor I want to review and approve or deny employee leave requests So that I can manage team coverage and ensure compliance with policy

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Approve leave request
Given I am logged in as a supervisor and have pending leave requests
When I review a request and select approve or deny
Then the employee should receive a notification and the leave status should update accordingly

Additional Details:
- Priority: Medium
- Story Points: 2
- Dependencies: E02 (Employee Profiles), E03 (Scheduling), E05 (Attendance Tracking)
- Notes: Approval should trigger integration hooks to exclude approved leave from scheduling and payroll calculations.
