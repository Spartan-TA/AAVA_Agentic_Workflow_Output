Title: Safety Incident Investigation Workflow

User Story: As a Safety Officer I want to manage the investigation workflow for reported incidents so that corrective actions can be tracked and resolved

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Start investigation
Given an incident is reported and status is Open
When I assign an investigator and change status to Investigating
Then the incident is updated and visible in the investigation queue

Scenario: Add corrective actions
Given an incident is under investigation
When I add corrective actions and assign responsible parties
Then actions are tracked and marked complete when resolved

Scenario: Resolve incident
Given all corrective actions are completed
When I change status to Resolved
Then the incident is closed and archived

Additional Details:
- Priority: High
- Story Points: 4
- Dependencies: Employee Master Data (E02), RBAC (E03)
- Notes: Status transitions must be logged; corrective actions should be auditable
