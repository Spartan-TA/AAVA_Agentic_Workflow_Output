Title: Safety Incident Investigation Workflow

User Story: As a safety officer I want to manage the investigation workflow for reported incidents so that corrective actions and status updates are tracked

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Investigate safety incident
Given an incident is in Open status
When I assign it for investigation and update findings
Then the status changes to Investigating and corrective actions are logged

Additional Details:
- Priority: High
- Story Points: 3
- Dependencies: Employee Master Data, RBAC
- Notes: All status changes timestamped; corrective actions linked to incident record.
