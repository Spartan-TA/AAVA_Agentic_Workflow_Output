Title: Incident Investigation Workflow

User Story: As a safety manager I want an incident investigation workflow So that root causes are identified and corrective actions tracked

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Investigating an incident
Given an incident is reported
When I initiate an investigation
Then the workflow should guide me through steps and record findings

Additional Details:
- Priority: High
- Story Points: 3
- Dependencies: Workflow engine
- Notes: Support assignment, status tracking, and corrective action logging.
