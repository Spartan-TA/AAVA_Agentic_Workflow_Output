Title: Investigate Safety Incident and Assign Corrective Actions

User Story: As a safety officer I want to initiate and track investigations for reported safety incidents So that corrective actions are assigned and compliance is maintained

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Initiate investigation and assign corrective actions
Given a safety incident has been reported
When I review the incident and assign investigation tasks and corrective actions
Then the system should track progress and completion of each action

Additional Details:
- Priority: High
- Story Points: 3
- Dependencies: E02 (Employee Profiles), E03 (Scheduling)
- Notes: Investigation status and corrective actions should be visible to relevant stakeholders and included in compliance reports.
