Title: Safety Incident Investigation Workflow
User Story: As a safety officer I want a workflow to investigate safety incidents So that corrective actions can be tracked and resolved
Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Investigation workflow
Given a safety incident is recorded
When I initiate the investigation
Then the system should track status (Open, Investigating, Resolved) and allow documentation of corrective actions
Additional Details:
- Priority: High
- Story Points: 3
- Dependencies: Incident recording, workflow engine
- Notes: Status changes and actions must be logged and exportable for OSHA compliance.
