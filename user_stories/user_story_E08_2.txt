Title: Investigation Workflow and Corrective Actions for Safety Incidents

User Story: As a safety officer I want to manage the investigation workflow and assign corrective actions for reported incidents So that all safety events are properly addressed and resolved

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Incident status workflow
Given a new incident is reported
When the safety officer reviews the incident
Then they can update the status to Open, Investigating, or Resolved and assign corrective actions

Scenario: Corrective action completion
Given corrective actions are assigned
When they are marked as completed
Then the incident status is updated and all actions are logged

Additional Details:
- Priority: High
- Story Points: 5
- Dependencies: E02, E03
- Notes: Workflow must be auditable; notifications sent to involved employees and supervisors.
