Title: Assign Equipment to Employees
User Story: As a supervisor I want to assign equipment (scanners, forklifts, PPE) to employees So that asset usage is tracked
Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Assign equipment
  Given equipment and employee exist
  When I assign equipment
  Then the assignment is recorded
Scenario: Return equipment
  Given equipment is assigned
  When the employee returns it
  Then the assignment is closed
Additional Details:
- Priority: Medium
- Story Points: 4
- Dependencies: Employee CRUD, RBAC, Certification tracking
- Notes: Track asset condition
