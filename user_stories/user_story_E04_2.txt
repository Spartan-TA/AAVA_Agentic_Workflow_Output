Title: Employee Clock-Out

User Story: As a warehouse worker I want to clock out at the end of my shift so that my working hours are accurately recorded and shift completion is tracked

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Successful clock-out
Given I am scheduled for a shift and have clocked in
When I submit a clock-out event via the app
Then my attendance record should show the clock-out time and calculate total hours worked

Additional Details:
- Priority: High
- Story Points: 2
- Dependencies: E02, E03
- Notes: Device ID captured for audit; automatic shift association required.
