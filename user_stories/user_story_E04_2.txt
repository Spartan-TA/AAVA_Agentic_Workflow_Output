Title: Record Employee Clock-Out

User Story: As an employee I want to record my clock-out time So that my work hours are accurately tracked

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Employee clock-out
Given I am scheduled to work
When I clock out via the time & attendance module
Then my clock-out time should be recorded and visible to HR

Additional Details:
- Priority: High
- Story Points: 2
- Dependencies: Time & attendance module
- Notes: Support overtime and early clock-out scenarios.
