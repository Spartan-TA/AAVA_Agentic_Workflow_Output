Title: Automated Hours Calculation and Reporting

User Story: As a Supervisor I want the system to automatically calculate total hours worked for each employee so that payroll and compliance reporting are accurate and efficient.

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Accurate calculation of hours
Given employees have clocked in and out for their shifts
When the Supervisor views the attendance report
Then the total hours worked for each employee are displayed correctly

Scenario: Handling missed punches
Given an employee missed a clock-in or clock-out
When the Supervisor reviews the attendance
Then the system flags the record for manual correction

Additional Details:
- Priority: High
- Story Points: 4
- Dependencies: Attendance module, Payroll integration
- Notes: Include overtime and break deductions in calculations.
