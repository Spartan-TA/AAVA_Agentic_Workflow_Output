Title: Calculate Hours Worked

User Story: As a supervisor I want the system to automatically calculate hours worked for each employee based on clock-in/out events So that payroll and overtime can be accurately tracked

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Accurate calculation of hours
Given an employee has valid clock-in and clock-out events for a shift
When the system processes attendance data
Then the total hours worked should be calculated and visible in reports

Additional Details:
- Priority: High
- Story Points: 2
- Dependencies: E02, E03
- Notes: Handle overnight shifts and multiple clock events per day.
