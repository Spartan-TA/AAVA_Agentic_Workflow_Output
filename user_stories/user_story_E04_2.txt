Title: Record Employee Clock-Out

User Story: As an employee I want to clock out at the end of my shift So that my work hours are accurately tracked

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Employee clock-out
Given I am scheduled for a shift
When I clock out using the system
Then my attendance should be updated with end timestamp

Additional Details:
- Priority: High
- Story Points: 2
- Dependencies: Shift schedule
- Notes: Prevent early clock-out
