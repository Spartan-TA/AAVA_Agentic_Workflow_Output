Title: Automatic Shift Association and Daily Totals Computation

User Story: As a supervisor I want clock-in/out events to be automatically associated with scheduled shifts and daily totals computed So that I can accurately track hours worked for payroll and compliance

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Clock events linked to shifts
Given a worker clocks in and out during their scheduled shift
When the system processes attendance events
Then the events are linked to the correct shift and daily hours are calculated

Scenario: Out-of-schedule clock events
Given a worker clocks in or out outside their scheduled shift
When the system processes attendance events
Then the event is flagged for supervisor review

Additional Details:
- Priority: High
- Story Points: 5
- Dependencies: E02, E03
- Notes: Shift association logic must handle overnight and overlapping shifts; daily totals available for reporting.
