### EPIC 6 (E06): Leave & Absence Management

**User Story 6.1: Request Leave**
- Title: Request Leave (PTO, Sick, Unpaid)
- User Story: As an employee, I want to request PTO, sick, or unpaid leave so that my absences are tracked and approved.
- Acceptance Criteria:
    - Given the leave request form,
      When I submit a request,
      Then it is recorded and routed for approval.
- Additional Details: Priority: Medium, Story Points: 3, Dependencies: E02, E03, E05, Notes: Core leave flow.

**User Story 6.2: Approve or Deny Leave Requests**
- Title: Approve or Deny Leave Requests
- User Story: As a supervisor, I want to approve or deny leave requests so that staffing is managed.
- Acceptance Criteria:
    - Given a pending leave request,
      When I approve or deny,
      Then the employee is notified and the leave balance is updated.
- Additional Details: Priority: Medium, Story Points: 2, Dependencies: 6.1, Notes: Ensures proper workflow.

**User Story 6.3: Update Accrual Balances**
- Title: Update Leave Accrual Balances and Policies
- User Story: As an HR user, I want leave accrual balances and policies to be updated automatically so that balances are accurate.
- Acceptance Criteria:
    - Given a leave approval,
      When it is processed,
      Then the employee's accrual balance is updated per policy.
- Additional Details: Priority: Medium, Story Points: 3, Dependencies: 6.2, Notes: Ensures compliance.

**User Story 6.4: Exclude Approved Leave from Scheduling and Payroll**
- Title: Exclude Approved Leave from Scheduling and Payroll
- User Story: As a system, I want approved leave to be excluded from scheduling and payroll calculations so that staffing and pay are accurate.
- Acceptance Criteria:
    - Given an approved leave,
      When schedules or payroll are generated,
      Then the employee is flagged for coverage and excluded from payroll hours as appropriate.
- Additional Details: Priority: Medium, Story Points: 3, Dependencies: 6.2, Notes: Integration hooks.

**User Story 6.5: Export Approved Leaves**
- Title: Export Approved Leaves for Reporting
- User Story: As an HR user, I want to export approved leaves so that I can report on absences.
- Acceptance Criteria:
    - Given the leave management module,
      When I request an export,
      Then a report of all approved leaves is generated.
- Additional Details: Priority: Medium, Story Points: 2, Dependencies: 6.2, Notes: Supports reporting.
