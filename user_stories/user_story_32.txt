Title: Track Training Completion

User Story: As a manager I want to track training completion for my team So that I can ensure compliance and identify skill gaps

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Training completion tracking
Given training modules are assigned
When employees complete modules
Then the system should update their training records and notify the manager

Additional Details:
- Priority: High
- Story Points: 3
- Dependencies: Training assignment, notifications
- Notes: Support for automated reminders
