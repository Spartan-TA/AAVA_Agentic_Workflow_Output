Title: Create and Manage Review Templates

User Story: As an HR manager I want to create and manage quarterly and annual performance review templates so that reviews are consistent and aligned with company standards.

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Creating a new review template
Given I am on the review template management page
When I define review periods, competencies, and rating scales
Then the template should be saved and available for assignment to employees

Additional Details:
- Priority: Medium
- Story Points: 3
- Dependencies: E02, E03
- Notes: Templates should support both quarterly and annual cycles.
