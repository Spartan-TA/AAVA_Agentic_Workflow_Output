Title: View Safety Dashboard

User Story: As a safety officer I want to view a dashboard of open and closed safety incidents So that I can monitor trends and prioritize follow-up actions

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Safety officer views dashboard
Given I am logged into the system
When I access the safety dashboard
Then I should see a summary of incidents by status, location, and type

Additional Details:
- Priority: Medium
- Story Points: 2
- Dependencies: Reporting & Analytics module
- Notes: Dashboard should support filtering and export.
