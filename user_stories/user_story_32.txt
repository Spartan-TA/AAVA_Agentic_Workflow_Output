Title: Safety Incident Recording

User Story: As a supervisor I want to record safety incidents in the system So that all events are documented and can be investigated

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Recording a new incident
Given I am on the incident reporting page
When I enter incident details and submit
Then the incident should be saved and visible in the incident log

Scenario: Validation error on incomplete submission
Given I am on the incident reporting page
When I leave required fields blank and submit
Then I should see validation errors and the incident should not be recorded

Additional Details:
- Priority: High
- Story Points: 3
- Dependencies: Incident log, user authentication
- Notes: Incident records should be timestamped and linked to location/department.
