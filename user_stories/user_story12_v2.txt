Title: Row-Level Security Constraints

User Story: As a supervisor I want to access only my team's records So that I can manage my team without viewing other teams' sensitive data

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Row-level access Given multiple teams exist When a supervisor accesses employee records Then only records for their assigned team are visible

Additional Details:
- Priority: High
- Story Points: 2
- Dependencies: Employee Master Data (E02), Role Assignment
- Notes: Ensure row-level constraints are enforced and covered by tests
