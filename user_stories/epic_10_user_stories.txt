### E10: Performance Reviews & Goals

**User Story 10.1**
Title: Performance Review Cycle Creation

User Story: As an HR Admin I want to create quarterly and annual review cycles So that structured feedback is collected.

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Review cycle creation
Given I am an HR Admin
When I create a review cycle
Then it is available for assignment to employees

Additional Details:
- Priority: Medium
- Story Points: 3
- Dependencies: E02, E03
- Notes: Templates for goals, competencies, and ratings

---

**User Story 10.2**
Title: Assign and Complete Performance Reviews

User Story: As a Supervisor I want to assign and complete reviews for my team So that performance is documented.

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Review completion
Given a review cycle is active
When I submit a review
Then the employee can acknowledge and the review is locked

Additional Details:
- Priority: Medium
- Story Points: 5
- Dependencies: E02, E03
- Notes: Supervisor and employee must both acknowledge

---

**User Story 10.3**
Title: PDF Export of Completed Reviews

User Story: As an Employee I want to export my completed reviews to PDF So that I can keep a personal record.

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: PDF export
Given a review is completed
When I export
Then the PDF matches the review content and is downloadable

Additional Details:
- Priority: Medium
- Story Points: 2
- Dependencies: E02, E03
- Notes: Export must be immutable after sign-off

---

**User Story 10.4**
Title: Role-Based Review Visibility

User Story: As an HR Admin I want to control who can view and edit reviews So that privacy is maintained.

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Access control
Given reviews exist
When accessed by different roles
Then only authorized users can view or edit per policy

Additional Details:
- Priority: Medium
- Story Points: 2
- Dependencies: E02, E03
- Notes: Immutable history after sign-off
