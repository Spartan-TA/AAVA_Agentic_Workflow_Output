Title: Clock In/Out Attendance
User Story: As a worker I want to clock in and out of my shift So that my working hours are accurately recorded
Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Clock In/Out Events
Given I am scheduled for a shift
When I clock in and out via the endpoint
Then my attendance is recorded and associated with my shift
Additional Details:
- Priority: High
- Story Points: 3
- Dependencies: Employee CRUD, RBAC
- Notes: Geofence/device capture optional
