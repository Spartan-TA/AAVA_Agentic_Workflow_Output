Title: Time & Attendance (Clock In/Out) - Record Clock-In Event
User Story: As a warehouse worker I want to clock in at the start of my shift So that my attendance is accurately recorded

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Valid clock-in
Given my badgeId and location are valid
When I POST to /attendance/clock-in
Then my clock-in event is recorded
And associated with my shift

Scenario: Geofence validation
Given geofence is enabled
When I clock in outside allowed area
Then the request is rejected

Additional Details:
- Priority: High
- Story Points: 3
- Dependencies: Employee CRUD, RBAC
- Notes: Device capture required

Title: Time & Attendance (Clock In/Out) - Record Clock-Out Event
User Story: As a warehouse worker I want to clock out at the end of my shift So that my worked hours are calculated

Acceptance Criteria:

And hours worked are calculated

- Priority: High
- Story Points: 2

Title: Time & Attendance (Clock In/Out) - Handle Missed Punches
User Story: As a supervisor I want to correct missed clock-in/out events So that attendance records are accurate

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Correction workflow
Given a missed punch is detected
When a correction request is submitted
Then an approval task is created
And upon approval, the record is updated

Additional Details:
- Priority: High
- Story Points: 2
- Dependencies: Clock-in/out events
- Notes: Corrections must be auditable
