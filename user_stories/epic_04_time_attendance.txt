### E04: Time & Attendance (Clock In/Out)

**User Story 1**
- Title: Clock In Endpoint
- User Story: As a worker, I want to clock in at the start of my shift so that my attendance is recorded.
- Acceptance Criteria in Gherkin format:
  Scenario: Successful clock-in
  Given a worker is at the warehouse location
  When they POST to /attendance/clock-in with valid credentials
  Then the clock-in is recorded and associated with their shift
- Additional Details:
  * Priority: High
  * Story Points: 3
  * Dependencies: E02, E03
  * Notes: Optional geofence validation.

**User Story 2**
- Title: Clock Out Endpoint
- User Story: As a worker, I want to clock out at the end of my shift so that my worked hours are calculated.
- Acceptance Criteria in Gherkin format:
  Scenario: Successful clock-out
  Given a worker is clocked in
  When they POST to /attendance/clock-out
  Then the clock-out is recorded and hours worked are calculated
- Additional Details:
  * Priority: High
  * Story Points: 2
  * Dependencies: E02, E03
  * Notes: Device capture for audit.

**User Story 3**
- Title: Handle Missed Punches and Corrections
- User Story: As a supervisor, I want to correct missed punches so that attendance records are accurate.
- Acceptance Criteria in Gherkin format:
  Scenario: Correction workflow
  Given a missed punch is detected
  When a correction is submitted
  Then an approval task is created for supervisor review
- Additional Details:
  * Priority: High
  * Story Points: 3
  * Dependencies: E02, E03
  * Notes: Audit trail required.

**User Story 4**
- Title: Attendance Reports Export
- User Story: As a payroll admin, I want to export attendance reports so that payroll can be processed.
- Acceptance Criteria in Gherkin format:
  Scenario: Export attendance as CSV
  Given attendance data exists
  When I request an export
  Then a CSV file is generated with daily totals
- Additional Details:
  * Priority: High
  * Story Points: 2
  * Dependencies: E02, E03
  * Notes: Include shift association.
