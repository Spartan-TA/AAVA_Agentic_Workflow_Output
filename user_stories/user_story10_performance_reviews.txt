EPIC E10: Performance Reviews & Goals

---

Title: Create Performance Review Templates
User Story: As an HR admin I want to create templates for quarterly and annual reviews So that reviews are consistent
Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Create review template
Given I have HR admin access
When I define a new template
Then it is saved and available for use

Additional Details:
- Priority: Medium
- Story Points: 2
- Dependencies: E02, E03
- Notes: Support competencies, ratings, comments

---

Title: Assign Reviews to Employees
User Story: As a supervisor I want to assign performance reviews to employees So that feedback cycles are initiated
Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Assign review
Given a review cycle is open
When I assign a review to an employee
Then the employee is notified and can complete the review

Additional Details:
- Priority: Medium
- Story Points: 2
- Dependencies: E02, E03
- Notes: Notification sent to employee

---

Title: Submit and Acknowledge Reviews
User Story: As an employee I want to submit my self-review and acknowledge supervisor feedback So that the process is transparent
Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Submit and acknowledge review
Given a review is assigned
When I submit my self-review and acknowledge supervisor comments
Then the review is marked complete and history is immutable

Additional Details:
- Priority: Medium
- Story Points: 2
- Dependencies: E02, E03
- Notes: PDF export available

---

Title: Track Goals and Competencies
User Story: As a supervisor I want to track employee goals and competencies So that development is monitored
Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Update goals and competencies
Given an employee profile
When I add or update goals
Then the changes are saved and visible in the review cycle

Additional Details:
- Priority: Medium
- Story Points: 2
- Dependencies: E02, E03
- Notes: Goals visible to employee and supervisor

---

Title: Role-Based Visibility for Reviews
User Story: As an HR admin I want role-based access to reviews So that only authorized users can view or edit them
Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Review access control
Given I am logged in
When I access reviews
Then I see only those I am authorized to view/edit

Additional Details:
- Priority: Medium
- Story Points: 1
- Dependencies: E02, E03
- Notes: Immutable after sign-off
