Title: Safety Incident Investigation Workflow

User Story: As a safety manager I want to manage the investigation workflow for reported safety incidents so that corrective actions are tracked and incidents are resolved efficiently

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Update incident status and actions
  Given a safety incident is reported
  When I update the status to Investigating or Resolved and log corrective actions
  Then the workflow and history are visible in the incident record

Additional Details:
- Priority: High
- Story Points: 3
- Dependencies: E02 (User Management), E03 (Scheduling)
- Notes: Status changes and actions must be auditable
