Title: Investigate and Close Incident

User Story: As a safety officer I want to investigate and close reported safety incidents So that corrective actions are documented and compliance is maintained

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Safety officer closes incident
Given a safety incident has been reported
When I review the details and add investigation notes
Then I can mark the incident as closed and notify relevant stakeholders

Additional Details:
- Priority: High
- Story Points: 3
- Dependencies: Notifications module
- Notes: Closure should require root cause and corrective action documentation.
