Title: Prevent Assignment Without Certification

User Story: As a warehouse supervisor I want the system to prevent equipment assignment to employees lacking required certifications so that safety and compliance are enforced.

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Attempting to assign equipment to uncertified employee
Given an employee does not have the required certification for a forklift
When I attempt to assign the forklift to the employee
Then the system should block the assignment and display an error message indicating missing certification

Additional Details:
- Priority: Medium
- Story Points: 2
- Dependencies: E02, E03, E07
- Notes: Certification requirements should be configurable per asset type.
