EPIC 06: LEAVE & ABSENCE MANAGEMENT

===========================================

Title: Leave Request Workflow

User Story: As a warehouse worker I want to request PTO, sick, or unpaid leave So that my time off is tracked and approved

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Submit leave request
Given I am authenticated as WORKER
When I POST to /leave/requests with valid data
Then a leave request is created

Scenario: Supervisor approval
Given a leave request is pending
When a supervisor reviews and approves/denies
Then the request status is updated and balances are adjusted

Additional Details:
- Priority: Medium
- Story Points: 5
- Dependencies: Employee CRUD API, RBAC, Shift Management
- Notes: Scheduled shifts auto-flag for coverage

---

Title: Leave Accrual and Integration

User Story: As an HR staff member I want leave balances and policies integrated with scheduling and payroll So that compliance and accurate staffing are maintained

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Accrual balance update
Given a leave is approved
When the payroll export is generated
Then the leave balance is updated and reflected in payroll

Scenario: Export approved leaves
Given approved leave requests exist
When I export leave data
Then only approved leaves are included

Additional Details:
- Priority: Medium
- Story Points: 8
- Dependencies: Employee CRUD API, RBAC, Shift Management
- Notes: Integration hooks for scheduling and payroll
