Title: Report on Training Completion Rates

User Story: As an HR administrator I want to generate reports on training completion rates So that I can monitor compliance and identify gaps

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Generate training completion report
Given I am logged in as an HR administrator
When I select a training module and reporting period
Then I should see a report of assigned, completed, and overdue trainings by department

Additional Details:
- Priority: Medium
- Story Points: 3
- Dependencies: Reporting & Analytics module
- Notes: Reports should be exportable to CSV.
