Title: Approve or Reject Leave Requests

User Story: As a manager I want to approve or reject leave requests So that I can ensure adequate staffing and fairness

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Leave request approval/rejection
Given I am logged in as a manager
When I review a leave request
Then I should be able to approve or reject it and the employee should be notified

Additional Details:
- Priority: High
- Story Points: 2
- Dependencies: Leave request submission, notifications
- Notes: Include reason for rejection
