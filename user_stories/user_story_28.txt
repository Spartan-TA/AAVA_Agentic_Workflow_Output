Title: Assign Equipment to Employees

User Story: As a warehouse supervisor I want to assign scanners, forklifts, and PPE to employees so that I can track asset responsibility and ensure proper usage.

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Assigning equipment to an employee
Given an employee is certified for the equipment
When I assign a scanner, forklift, or PPE to the employee
Then the system should record the assignment and update the asset's status to 'Checked Out' under the employee's profile

Additional Details:
- Priority: Medium
- Story Points: 5
- Dependencies: E02, E03, E07
- Notes: Only certified employees should be eligible for assignment.
