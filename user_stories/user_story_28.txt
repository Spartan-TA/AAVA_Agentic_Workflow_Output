Title: Export OSHA Summary and Reporting

User Story: As a safety officer I want to export OSHA 300/300A summary reports from recorded incidents so that regulatory reporting requirements are met

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Exporting OSHA summary
Given I am logged in as a safety officer
When I select a reporting period and export OSHA summary
Then the system generates a file with all required OSHA fields populated from incident records

Additional Details:
- Priority: High
- Story Points: 2
- Dependencies: Incident Recording, Reporting Module
- Notes: Export format must match OSHA requirements.
