Title: Record Safety Incidents

User Story: As a supervisor I want to record safety incidents in the system So that compliance and reporting are streamlined

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Record safety incident
Given I am on the safety incident entry page
When I submit incident details
Then the incident should be logged and available for investigation

Additional Details:
- Priority: High
- Story Points: 3
- Dependencies: Incident management module
- Notes: Should support attachments and OSHA categories.
