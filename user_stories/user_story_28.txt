Title: Certification Expiration Alerts

User Story: As an employee I want to receive alerts when my certifications are about to expire So that I can renew them in time and avoid assignment blocks

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: 30-day expiration alert
Given my certification is expiring in 30 days
When the system checks expiration dates
Then I should receive a notification via email and in-app

Scenario: 7-day expiration alert
Given my certification is expiring in 7 days
When the system checks expiration dates
Then I should receive a high-priority notification

Additional Details:
- Priority: High
- Story Points: 2
- Dependencies: Notification system, certification tracking
- Notes: Alerts should be configurable for frequency and channel.
