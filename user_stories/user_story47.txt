Title: Goal Tracking and Comments in Performance Reviews

User Story:
As an employee or supervisor, I want to track goals, add comments, and update progress in performance reviews so that feedback is actionable and development is continuous.

Acceptance Criteria:
- Scenario: Add or update goals and comments
  Given a review is in progress
  When the employee or supervisor adds goals or comments
  Then the updates are saved and visible to both parties

- Scenario: Progress updates are tracked
  Given goals are updated over time
  When the review is viewed
  Then all progress updates and comments are shown in chronological order

Additional Details:
- Goals must support status (not started, in progress, completed)
- Comments must be timestamped and attributed
- All updates must be auditable and included in the final review export
