Title: Real-Time Location Tracking

User Story: As a warehouse manager I want to track the real-time location of assets and shipments So that inventory movement is visible and losses are minimized

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Location tracking
Given assets and shipments with tracking devices
When I view the tracking dashboard
Then the system should display current locations and movement history

Additional Details:
- Priority: High
- Story Points: 5
- Dependencies: Equipment Asset Registry CRUD
- Notes: Support integration with GPS and RFID devices.
