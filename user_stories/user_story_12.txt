Title: Record Clock-In Event

User Story: As a warehouse worker I want to clock in at the start of my shift so that my working hours are accurately recorded for payroll and compliance

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Successful clock-in
Given I am a registered employee and my shift is scheduled for today
When I access the clock-in endpoint and provide my badge ID and location
Then my clock-in event is recorded with timestamp and geofence validation, and my shift association is updated

Additional Details:
- Priority: High
- Story Points: 3
- Dependencies: Employee Master Data, RBAC
- Notes: Geofence is optional but should be captured if available; device ID should be logged for audit
