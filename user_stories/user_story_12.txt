Title: Enforce Row-Level Security

User Story: As a supervisor I want to access only the records of my assigned team So that I do not see or modify data outside my responsibility

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Team data restriction
Given I am logged in as a supervisor
When I view employee records
Then I should only see records for employees in my team

Scenario: Unauthorized access attempt
Given I am logged in as a supervisor
When I attempt to access or modify records outside my team
Then the system should return a 403 Forbidden error

Additional Details:
- Priority: High
- Story Points: 3
- Dependencies: Employee Master Data, RBAC core
- Notes: Row-level constraints must be covered by automated tests.
