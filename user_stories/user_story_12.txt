Title: Schedule Assignment and Conflict Detection
User Story: As a supervisor I want to assign employees to shifts and detect conflicts So that staffing is adequate and errors are minimized.
Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Assign employee to shift
  Given an employee and a shift
  When I assign the employee
  Then the schedule is updated

Scenario: Conflict detection
  Given overlapping or unavailable shifts
  When I assign an employee
  Then the system prevents the conflict

Scenario: Bulk assignment
  Given a list of employees
  When I assign them to a shift
  Then all are scheduled if no conflicts

Additional Details:
- Priority: High
- Story Points: 5
- Dependencies: E02, E03, E04
- Notes: Audit entries generated for assignments.
