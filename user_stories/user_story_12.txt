Title: Supervisor Attendance Review

User Story: As a supervisor I want to review attendance records for my team So that I can monitor punctuality and address issues promptly

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Review team attendance
Given I am logged in as a supervisor
When I access the attendance dashboard
Then I should see attendance summaries and details for my direct reports

Additional Details:
- Priority: Medium
- Story Points: 3
- Dependencies: Attendance module, supervisor role
- Notes: Include filters for date range and shift.
