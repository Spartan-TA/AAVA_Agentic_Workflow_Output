Title: Implement Row-Level Security Constraints

User Story: As a SUPERVISOR I want to access only records related to my team so that data privacy and security are maintained

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Accessing team records
Given I am a SUPERVISOR
When I request employee records
Then I only receive records for employees in my assigned team

Additional Details:
- Priority: High
- Story Points: 2
- Dependencies: E01, E02, E03
- Notes: Row-level constraints must be enforced in queries and validated by tests.
