Title: Enforce Row-Level Security for Supervisors

User Story: As a supervisor I want to access only the records of my assigned team so that I cannot view or modify data outside my responsibility

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Supervisor accessing employee records
Given a supervisor is logged in
When they request employee data
Then only records for their assigned team are returned; access to other teams is forbidden (403)

Additional Details:
- Priority: High
- Story Points: 2
- Dependencies: Employee Master Data, RBAC core
- Notes: Must be covered by automated security tests and documented in OpenAPI.
