Title: AI-Assisted Shift Assignment Suggestions

User Story: As a supervisor I want the system to suggest optimal shift assignments based on skills, availability, and demand So that I can efficiently staff shifts and improve performance

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: AI shift suggestion
Given employee skills, availability, and historical data
When I request shift assignment suggestions
Then the system returns a ranked list with rationale and allows manual override

Additional Details:
- Priority: Low
- Story Points: 5
- Dependencies: E02, E05, E15
- Notes: Fallback to manual assignment if AI fails; rationale must be logged.
