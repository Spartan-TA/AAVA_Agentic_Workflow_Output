Title: Track Employee Goals and Competencies

User Story: As a supervisor I want to set and track goals and competencies for my team members so that I can monitor progress and provide targeted feedback.

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Setting goals for an employee
Given I am viewing an employee's profile
When I add or update goals and competencies for the review period
Then the system should save the entries and display them in the employee's review dashboard

Additional Details:
- Priority: Medium
- Story Points: 3
- Dependencies: E02, E03
- Notes: Goals should be visible to both supervisor and employee.
