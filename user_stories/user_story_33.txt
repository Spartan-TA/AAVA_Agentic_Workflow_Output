Title: Asset Registry CRUD

User Story: As an asset manager I want to create, update, and delete records for equipment and PPE so that all assets are tracked and their condition is maintained

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Manage asset registry
  Given I have asset manager access
  When I add, edit, or remove an asset
  Then the registry is updated and the asset is available for assignment

Additional Details:
- Priority: Medium
- Story Points: 3
- Dependencies: E02 (User Management), E03 (Scheduling), E07 (Certifications)
- Notes: Asset types include scanners, forklifts, PPE
