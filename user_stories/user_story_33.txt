Title: Prevent Equipment Use Without Cert

User Story: As a supervisor I want the system to block equipment assignment to employees lacking required certifications So that safety and compliance are enforced

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Block equipment assignment without cert
Given an employee lacks a required certification
When I attempt to assign restricted equipment
Then the system should prevent the assignment and display a warning

Additional Details:
- Priority: High
- Story Points: 2
- Dependencies: Certification tracking, asset management
- Notes: Should support multiple equipment types and certs.
