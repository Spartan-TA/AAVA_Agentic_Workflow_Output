Title: Assign Asset to Employee

User Story: As an asset manager I want to assign equipment or assets to employees So that asset responsibility and tracking are maintained

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Assign asset
Given I am logged in as an asset manager
When I select an asset and assign it to an employee
Then the assignment should be recorded and the employee notified

Additional Details:
- Priority: High
- Story Points: 3
- Dependencies: Notifications module, Employee Master Data
- Notes: Asset assignment history should be auditable.
