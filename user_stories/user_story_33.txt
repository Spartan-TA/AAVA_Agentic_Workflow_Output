Title: Automated Asset Assignment Notifications

User Story: As an employee I want to receive notifications when equipment is assigned to me so that I am aware of my responsibilities and can prepare accordingly

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Notification upon asset assignment
Given an asset is assigned to my profile
When the assignment is completed
Then I should receive an email and in-app notification with asset details and pickup instructions

Additional Details:
- Priority: Medium
- Story Points: 1
- Dependencies: Notification service, Asset inventory module
- Notes: Notification template should include asset type, serial number, and contact for support
