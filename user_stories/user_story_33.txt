Title: View Certification Status

User Story: As an employee I want to view my certification status So that I know which certifications are current and which need renewal

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Certification status viewing
Given I am logged in as an employee
When I access my training dashboard
Then I should see a list of my certifications and their expiration dates

Additional Details:
- Priority: Medium
- Story Points: 2
- Dependencies: Training completion
- Notes: Support for automated renewal reminders
