### E08 - Safety Incidents & OSHA Reporting

#### User Story 1
Title: Record Safety Incident
User Story: As a SUPERVISOR, I want to record safety incidents and near-misses so that investigations can be initiated.
Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Incident Recording
Given a safety event
When SUPERVISOR submits incident details
Then the incident is recorded with validation
Additional Details:
- Priority: High
- Story Points: 2
- Dependencies: E02, E03
- Notes: Severity, location, description required
