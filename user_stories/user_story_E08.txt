### E08 - Safety Incidents & OSHA Reporting

**User Story 1: Record Safety Incidents**
- Title: Record Safety Incidents and Near-Misses
- Narrative: As a supervisor, I want to record safety incidents and near-misses so that risks are tracked.
- Acceptance Criteria:
  ```
  Given an incident,
  When I POST /safety/incidents,
  Then the incident is validated and saved.
  ```
- Priority: High
- Story Points: 3
- Dependencies: E02, E03
- Notes: Safety tracking.

**User Story 2: Investigation Workflow**
- Title: Safety Incident Investigation Workflow
- Narrative: As a safety officer, I want a workflow for investigating incidents so that corrective actions are taken.
- Acceptance Criteria:
  ```
  Given an open incident,
  When investigation is started,
  Then status updates to Investigating and actions are logged.
  ```
- Priority: High
- Story Points: 2
- Dependencies: Story 1
- Notes: Compliance.

**User Story 3: OSHA 300/300A Export**
- Title: Export OSHA Reports
- Narrative: As a compliance officer, I want to export OSHA 300/300A reports so that regulatory requirements are met.
- Acceptance Criteria:
  ```
  Given incident data,
  When I request an export,
  Then OSHA 300/300A fields are generated.
  ```
- Priority: High
- Story Points: 2
- Dependencies: Story 1
- Notes: Regulatory reporting.

**User Story 4: Safety Metrics Dashboard**
- Title: Safety Metrics Dashboard
- Narrative: As a manager, I want a dashboard of safety metrics so that I can monitor trends.
- Acceptance Criteria:
  ```
  Given incident data,
  When I view the dashboard,
  Then metrics are displayed (counts, severity, status).
  ```
- Priority: High
- Story Points: 2
- Dependencies: Story 1
- Notes: Data-driven safety.
