Title: View Personal Upcoming Shifts

User Story: As a warehouse worker I want to view my upcoming shifts So that I can plan my schedule and avoid missed assignments

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Upcoming shifts display
Given I am logged into my account
When I navigate to the schedule section
Then I should see a list of all my upcoming shifts with dates and times

Additional Details:
- Priority: High
- Story Points: 2
- Dependencies: Shift Assignment, Employee Master Data
- Notes: Display should be mobile-friendly and include shift location.
