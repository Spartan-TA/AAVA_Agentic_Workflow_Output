Title: Track Leave Balances

User Story: As an employee I want to view my current leave balances so that I can plan my time off accordingly

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Viewing leave balances
Given I am logged in as an employee
When I navigate to my leave balance page
Then I see up-to-date balances for all leave types

Additional Details:
- Priority: Medium
- Story Points: 2
- Dependencies: Leave request/approval process
- Notes: Balances should update in real time after approvals or denials.
