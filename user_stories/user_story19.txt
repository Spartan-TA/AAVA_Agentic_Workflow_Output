Title: Training & Certification Tracking - Certification Expiry Alerts

User Story: As a supervisor, I want to receive alerts before employee certifications expire so that renewals are not missed.

Acceptance Criteria:

Scenario: Alert 30/7 days before expiry
Given a certification is expiring in 30 or 7 days
When the system checks certification dates
Then an alert is sent to the employee and supervisor

Additional Details:
- Priority: High
- Story Points: 3
- Dependencies: Certification CRUD
- Notes: Alerts must be sent via in-app and email/SMS if configured.
