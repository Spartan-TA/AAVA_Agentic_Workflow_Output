Title: Safety Incidents & OSHA Reporting - Incident Recording

User Story: As a safety officer, I want to record incidents and near-misses with severity, location, and description so that safety data is tracked.

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Record Incident
Given incident details
When I POST /safety/incidents
Then the incident is recorded with validation

Scenario: Status Workflow
Given an incident
When I update status to Investigating or Resolved
Then workflow progresses accordingly

Additional Details:
- Priority: High
- Story Points: 4
- Dependencies: E02, E03
- Notes: OSHA summary exportable
