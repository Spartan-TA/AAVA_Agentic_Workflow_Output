### E19 - Advanced Scheduling Optimization

**Title:** Optimize Shift Scheduling
**User Story:** As a scheduler, I want the system to suggest optimal shift assignments so that staffing is efficient.
**Acceptance Criteria:**
Scenario: Scheduling optimization
Given employee availability and rules
When I generate a schedule
Then the system suggests assignments minimizing overtime and conflicts
**Additional Details:** Priority: Medium, Story Points: 5, Dependencies: E05, Notes: Use optimization algorithm.

**Title:** Blackout Date Handling
**User Story:** As a scheduler, I want to define blackout dates so that no shifts are scheduled during warehouse closures.
**Acceptance Criteria:**
Scenario: Blackout date enforcement
Given a blackout date
When I schedule shifts
Then the system prevents assignments on those dates
**Additional Details:** Priority: Medium, Story Points: 2, Dependencies: E05, Notes: Sync with warehouse calendar.
