Title: Certification CRUD and Expiry Alerts

User Story: As an HR manager I want to create, update, and delete employee certifications and receive alerts before they expire so that compliance and safety requirements are met

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Create certification
Given an employee profile exists
When I add a new certification with expiry date
Then the certification is saved and tracked

Scenario: Expiry alert
Given a certification is expiring in 30 or 7 days
When the system detects the upcoming expiry
Then an alert is sent to the employee and supervisor

Additional Details:
- Priority: High
- Story Points: 3
- Dependencies: Employee Master Data, RBAC
- Notes: Certification status must be visible on employee profile; alerts sent via preferred channels.
