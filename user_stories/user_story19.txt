Title: Create Recurring Shift Templates

User Story:
As a Scheduling Manager, I want to create recurring shift templates (daily, weekly, custom patterns) so that I can efficiently plan and assign shifts for employees over extended periods.

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario 1: Create a weekly recurring shift template
  Given I am on the shift template creation page
  When I select 'weekly recurrence' and specify days/times
  And I save the template
  Then the template is available for future shift assignments

Scenario 2: Apply a recurring shift template to a department
  Given a recurring shift template exists
  When I assign it to a department
  Then all employees in that department see the assigned shifts in their schedules

Scenario 3: Edit a recurring shift template
  Given a recurring shift template exists
  When I edit the template's pattern or times
  Then all future unassigned shifts are updated accordingly

Priority: High
Story Points: 8
Dependencies: E02, E03, E04
Notes: Must support exceptions for holidays and blackout dates.
