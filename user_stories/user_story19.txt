Title: Send In-App and Email/SMS Notifications

User Story: As a system I want to send notifications for shift changes, expiring certifications, approvals, and announcements so that users are informed in real time

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Sending a notification
Given a triggering event occurs (e.g., shift change)
When the event is processed
Then a notification is sent to the relevant user(s) via their preferred channel

Additional Details:
- Priority: Medium
- Story Points: 3
- Dependencies: RBAC, Shift Management, Certification Tracking
- Notes: Support in-app, email, and SMS
