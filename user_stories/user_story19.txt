Title: Review and Resolve Safety Incident
User Story: As a safety officer I want to review and resolve reported safety incidents So that workplace hazards are mitigated
Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Incident resolution
Given I am logged in as safety officer
When I review a reported incident and mark it as resolved
Then the reporting employee should be notified and the incident status updated
Additional Details:
- Priority: High
- Story Points: 2
- Dependencies: Notifications, Incident module
- Notes: Resolution actions must be logged for audit.
