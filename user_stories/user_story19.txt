Title: Observability & Monitoring

User Story: As a DevOps engineer I want structured logging (JSON), distributed tracing (OpenTelemetry), metrics (Prometheus), and alerting (PagerDuty/Slack) So that I can monitor system health, troubleshoot issues, and respond to incidents quickly.

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Monitoring and alerting setup
Given the platform is running in production
When an error, performance issue, or threshold breach occurs
Then logs, traces, and metrics are captured and alerts are sent to PagerDuty/Slack for immediate action.

Additional Details:
- Priority: High
- Story Points: 8
- Dependencies: E01
- Notes: Ensure monitoring covers all critical services and supports dashboard visualization.
