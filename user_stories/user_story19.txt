Title: Audit Entries and Blackout Date Handling
User Story: As an HR manager I want all schedule changes and blackout dates to be logged in the audit trail So that compliance and operational integrity are maintained
Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Log schedule changes and blackout dates
Given I make changes to shifts or add blackout dates
When the changes are saved
Then audit entries are generated with actor, timestamp, and details
Additional Details:
- Priority: High
- Story Points: 3
- Dependencies: E02, E03, E04, E05
- Notes: Audit log must be immutable and exportable for compliance.
