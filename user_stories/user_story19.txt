Title: Deliver Notifications & Announcements

User Story: As an employee I want to receive in-app and email/SMS notifications for important events So that I am always informed about schedule changes, approvals, and announcements

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Receive notification for schedule change
Given my shift schedule is updated
When the scheduler publishes the change
Then I receive a notification via my preferred channel

Additional Details:
- Priority: Medium
- Story Points: 5
- Dependencies: E03 (RBAC), E05 (Shift & Schedule Management), E06 (Leave & Absence Management), E07 (Training & Certification Tracking)
- Notes: Support notification preferences and announcement broadcasts.
