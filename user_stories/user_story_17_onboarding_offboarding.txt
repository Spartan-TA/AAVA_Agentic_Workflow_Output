Title: Onboarding & Offboarding Workflow - New Hire Provisioning

User Story: As an HR user, I want new hires to appear from HRIS and provision accounts, initial schedule, and required training so that onboarding is automated.

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: New hire onboarding
Given a new hire in HRIS
When sync occurs
Then account, schedule, and training tasks are created

Additional Details:
- Priority: Medium
- Story Points: 3
- Dependencies: Employee CRUD, RBAC, Shift Management, Certification Tracking, Asset Assignment, Integration Layer
- Notes: Task generation
