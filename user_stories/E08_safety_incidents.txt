EpicID: E08
Title: Safety Incidents & OSHA Reporting
User Story: As a safety officer, I want to record incidents and generate OSHA reports so that we improve safety and meet regulatory requirements.
Acceptance Criteria:
  Scenario: Record Incident
    Given incident details
    When I POST to /safety/incidents
    Then the incident is logged with all required fields
  Scenario: Incident Workflow
    Given an open incident
    When investigation progresses
    Then status updates to Investigating or Resolved
  Scenario: OSHA Export
    Given incident data
    When I export OSHA summary
    Then I receive 300/300A formatted data
  Scenario: Metrics Dashboard
    Given incidents exist
    When I view the dashboard
    Then safety KPIs are displayed
Additional Details:
  Priority: High
  Story Points: 13
  Dependencies: E02, E03
  Notes: Metrics endpoints must be available
