Title: Leave & Absence Management

User Story: As an employee I want to request and track leave So that my time off is managed accurately and supervisors can plan coverage

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Leave request approval
Given I have available leave balance
When I submit a leave request
Then my supervisor can approve or deny and my balance updates accordingly

Additional Details:
- Priority: Medium
- Story Points: 5
- Dependencies: Employee Master Data, RBAC, Shift & Schedule Management
- Notes: PTO, sick, unpaid leave supported; scheduled shifts auto-flagged for coverage; exports show approved leaves
