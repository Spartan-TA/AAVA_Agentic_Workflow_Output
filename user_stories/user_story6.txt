Title: Leave & Absence Management

User Story: As an employee I want to request PTO, sick, or unpaid leave So that my time off is tracked and staffing plans remain accurate

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Request Leave
Given I am an employee
When I submit a leave request
Then my supervisor receives it for approval

Scenario: Approve/Deny Leave
Given a leave request is pending
When my supervisor reviews it
Then they can approve or deny and my balance updates accordingly

Scenario: Scheduled Shift Coverage
Given approved leave overlaps with scheduled shifts
When the system processes the leave
Then affected shifts are flagged for coverage

Scenario: Leave Export
Given leave data exists
When I request an export
Then I receive a report of approved leaves

Additional Details:
- Priority: Medium
- Story Points: 5
- Dependencies: Employee Master Data (E02), Role-Based Access Control (E03), Shift & Schedule Management (E05)
- Notes: Accrual balances and policies must be enforced; integration hooks for scheduling and payroll.
