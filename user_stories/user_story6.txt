Title: Leave & Absence Management

User Story: As an employee I want to request and track leave so that my time off is handled compliantly and my schedule is updated accordingly.

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Leave request and approval
Given I have leave balance available
When I submit a PTO, sick, or unpaid leave request
Then my supervisor can approve or deny the request
And my leave balance updates
And scheduled shifts are flagged for coverage
And approved leaves are included in exports

Additional Details:
- Priority: Medium
- Story Points: 5
- Dependencies: Employee Master Data (CRUD), Role-Based Access Control (RBAC), Shift & Schedule Management
- Notes: Accrual policies and integration hooks for scheduling and payroll hours.
