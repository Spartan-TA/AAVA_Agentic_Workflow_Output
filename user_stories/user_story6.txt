Title: Delete Employee Record

User Story: As an HR manager I want to delete an employee record So that I can remove employees who have left the organization

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Delete employee record
Given I am on the employee profile page
When I click the delete button and confirm
Then the employee record should be removed from the system

Additional Details:
- Priority: Medium
- Story Points: 2
- Dependencies: Employee CRUD module
- Notes: Ensure deletion is irreversible and audit log is updated.
