Title: Leave & Absence Management

User Story: As an employee I want to request and track leave so that my time off is managed according to company policies and staffing plans remain accurate.

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Request leave
Given I have available leave balance
When I submit a leave request
Then the request is logged and pending supervisor approval

Scenario: Approve/deny leave
Given a leave request is pending
When my supervisor reviews the request
Then they can approve or deny it and my balance updates accordingly

Scenario: Auto-flag scheduled shifts
Given I have approved leave overlapping scheduled shifts
When the leave is approved
Then affected shifts are flagged for coverage

Scenario: Export approved leaves
Given approved leave records exist
When I request an export
Then the system generates a report of approved leaves

Additional Details:
- Priority: Medium
- Story Points: 5
- Dependencies: Employee Master Data (CRUD), Role Based Access Control (RBAC), Shift & Schedule Management
- Notes: Accrual balances and policies must be enforced; integration hooks for scheduling and payroll.
