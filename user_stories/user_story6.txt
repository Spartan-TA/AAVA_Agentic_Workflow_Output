Title: Leave & Absence Management

User Story: As an employee I want to request and track leave (PTO, sick, unpaid) so that my time off is compliant and my schedule is updated accordingly.

Acceptance Criteria:

Scenario: Request leave
Given I am an employee
When I submit a leave request
Then my supervisor is notified for approval

Scenario: Approve/deny leave
Given a pending leave request
When my supervisor reviews it
Then they can approve or deny the request and my accrual balances update

Scenario: Schedule integration
Given an approved leave
When I am scheduled for a shift
Then the shift is auto-flagged for coverage

Scenario: Leave reporting
Given leave data exists
When I export leave records
Then only approved leaves are shown in the export

Additional Details:
- Priority: Medium
- Story Points: 5
- Dependencies: Employee Master Data (E02), Role-Based Access Control (E03), Shift & Schedule Management (E05)
- Notes: Integration hooks to exclude from scheduling and payroll hours.
