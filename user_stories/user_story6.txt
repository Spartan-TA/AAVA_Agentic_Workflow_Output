Title: Manage Leave & Absence Requests

User Story: As an employee I want to request PTO, sick, or unpaid leave So that my time off is handled compliantly and my schedule is updated accordingly

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Leave request and approval
Given I am an employee with available leave balance
When I submit a leave request
Then my supervisor can approve or deny, balances update, scheduled shifts are flagged for coverage, and exports show approved leaves

Additional Details:
- Priority: Medium
- Story Points: 5
- Dependencies: Employee Master Data, Role-Based Access Control, Shift & Schedule Management
- Notes: Accrual balances and policies must be enforced; integration hooks exclude from scheduling and payroll hours.
