Title: Leave & Absence Management

User Story: As an employee I want to request and track leave (PTO, sick, unpaid) so that my time off is handled compliantly and my schedule is updated accordingly.

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Leave request and approval
Given I have leave balance available
When I submit a leave request
Then my supervisor can approve or deny, balances update, scheduled shifts are flagged for coverage, and approved leaves are included in exports

Additional Details:
- Priority: Medium
- Story Points: 5
- Dependencies: Employee Master Data (E02), Role Based Access Control (E03), Shift & Schedule Management (E05)
- Notes: Accrual balances and policies supported; integration hooks for scheduling and payroll.
