Title: Leave & Absence Management

User Story: As an employee I want to request and track leave so that my time off is properly managed and reflected in schedules.

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Leave request and approval
Given I have available leave balance
When I submit a leave request
Then my supervisor can approve/deny, balances update, and scheduled shifts are flagged for coverage

Additional Details:
- Priority: Medium
- Story Points: 5
- Dependencies: Employee Master Data, RBAC, Shift & Schedule Management
- Notes: PTO, sick, unpaid leave; integration hooks for scheduling/payroll
