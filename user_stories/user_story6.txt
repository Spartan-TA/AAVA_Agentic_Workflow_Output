### E06 â Leave & Absence Management

**User Story 1: Request Leave**
- **User Story:** As an employee, I want to request PTO, sick, or unpaid leave so that my time off is tracked.
- **Acceptance Criteria:**
  - Given I am logged in,
  - When I submit a leave request,
  - Then it is recorded and pending approval.
- **Additional Details:** Priority: Medium; Story Points: 2; Dependencies: E02, E03, E05; Notes: Validate leave type.

**User Story 2: Approve/Deny Leave**
- **User Story:** As a supervisor, I want to approve or deny leave requests so that staffing is managed.
- **Acceptance Criteria:**
  - Given a pending request,
  - When I take action,
  - Then the request status updates and the employee is notified.
- **Additional Details:** Priority: Medium; Story Points: 2; Dependencies: E06-1; Notes: Record reason for denial.

**User Story 3: Update Accrual Balances**
- **User Story:** As an HR user, I want leave balances updated automatically so that accruals are accurate.
- **Acceptance Criteria:**
  - Given an approved leave,
  - When the leave is processed,
  - Then the employee's balance is reduced accordingly.
- **Additional Details:** Priority: Medium; Story Points: 2; Dependencies: E06-2; Notes: Support multiple policies.

**User Story 4: Exclude from Scheduling**
- **User Story:** As a scheduler, I want approved leaves to auto-flag shifts for coverage so that absences are covered.
- **Acceptance Criteria:**
  - Given an approved leave,
  - When shifts overlap,
  - Then those shifts are flagged for reassignment.
- **Additional Details:** Priority: Medium; Story Points: 2; Dependencies: E05, E06-2; Notes: Notify supervisors.

**User Story 5: Export Approved Leaves**
- **User Story:** As an HR user, I want to export approved leaves so that payroll and compliance are supported.
- **Acceptance Criteria:**
  - Given approved leaves,
  - When I export data,
  - Then a CSV is generated with all relevant details.
- **Additional Details:** Priority: Medium; Story Points: 1; Dependencies: E06-3; Notes: Include leave type and dates.
