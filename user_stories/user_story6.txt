Title: Leave Request and Approval Workflow

User Story:
As an employee, I want to request PTO, sick, or unpaid leave and have it approved or denied by my supervisor so that my leave balances are accurately tracked and schedules are updated.

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario 1: Request leave
  Given I am an employee
  When I submit a leave request
  Then my supervisor is notified for approval

Scenario 2: Approve/deny leave
  Given a leave request is pending
  When my supervisor reviews it
  Then they can approve or deny the request

Scenario 3: Update balances
  Given leave is approved
  When the approval is processed
  Then my leave balance is updated accordingly

Scenario 4: Flag scheduled shifts
  Given approved leave overlaps with scheduled shifts
  When the leave is approved
  Then affected shifts are flagged for coverage

Scenario 5: Export approved leaves
  Given approved leave records exist
  When I export leave data
  Then the export includes all approved leaves

Additional Details:
Priority: Medium
Story Points: 8
Dependencies: E02, E03, E05
Notes: Accrual policies must be enforced.
