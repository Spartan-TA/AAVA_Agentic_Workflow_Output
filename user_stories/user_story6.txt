#### User Story 6: Delete (Soft-Delete) Employee Record
**As an** HR user  
**I want** to soft-delete employee records  
**so that** historical data is preserved for compliance.

**Acceptance Criteria:**
- **Given** an employee record,  
  **When** I DELETE `/employees/{id}`,  
  **Then** the record is marked as inactive but not physically removed.

**Additional Details:**  
Priority: High  
Story Points: 1  
Dependencies: E02  
Notes: Ensure filtering excludes inactive records by default.
