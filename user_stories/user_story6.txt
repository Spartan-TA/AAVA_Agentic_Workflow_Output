Title: Leave & Absence Management

User Story: As an employee I want to request and track PTO, sick, and unpaid leave so that my time off is managed compliantly and my schedule is updated accordingly.

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Leave Request and Approval
Given I have available leave balance
When I submit a leave request
Then my supervisor can approve or deny, balances update, scheduled shifts are flagged for coverage, and exports show approved leaves.

Additional Details:
- Priority: Medium
- Story Points: 5
- Dependencies: Employee Master Data, Role Based Access Control, Shift & Schedule Management
- Notes: Support accrual balances and integration hooks to scheduling and payroll.
