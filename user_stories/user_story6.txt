Title: Leave & Absence Management

User Story: As a warehouse employee I want to request and track leave (PTO, sick, unpaid) so that my time off is managed compliantly and staffing plans remain accurate.

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Leave request and approval
Given I am an employee
When I submit a leave request
Then supervisors can approve or deny, balances update automatically, scheduled shifts are flagged for coverage, and exports show approved leaves.

Additional Details:
- Priority: Medium
- Story Points: 5
- Dependencies: Employee Master Data (E02), Role-Based Access Control (E03), Shift & Schedule Management (E05)
- Notes: Accrual balances and policies must be enforced, and integration hooks should exclude leave from scheduling and payroll hours.
