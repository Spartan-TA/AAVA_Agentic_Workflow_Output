Title: Manage Leave & Absence Requests and Approvals

User Story: As an employee I want to request PTO, sick, or unpaid leave and have my supervisor approve or deny the request so that my leave balances are accurately tracked and my scheduled shifts are updated accordingly.

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Request leave
  Given I am an active employee
  When I submit a leave request with type and dates
  Then the request is created and pending supervisor approval

Scenario: Approve or deny leave
  Given a pending leave request
  When my supervisor reviews the request
  Then they can approve or deny it, and my leave balance is updated

Scenario: Update scheduled shifts
  Given approved leave overlaps with scheduled shifts
  When the leave is approved
  Then affected shifts are flagged for coverage

Scenario: Export leave records
  Given leave records exist
  When I export leave data
  Then only approved leaves are included in the export

Additional Details:
- Priority: Medium
- Story Points: 5
- Dependencies: Employee Master Data, Role-Based Access Control, Shift & Schedule Management
- Notes: Accrual balances and policies must be enforced; integration hooks for scheduling and payroll required.
