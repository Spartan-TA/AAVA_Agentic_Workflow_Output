Title: Leave & Absence Management
User Story: As an employee I want to request and track leave (PTO, sick, unpaid) so that my time off is handled compliantly and my schedule is updated accordingly.
Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Leave request and approval
Given I am authenticated as an employee
When I submit a leave request
Then my supervisor can approve or deny the request
And my leave balance updates
And scheduled shifts are auto-flagged for coverage
And approved leaves are included in exports
Additional Details:
- Priority: Medium
- Story Points: 5
- Dependencies: Employee Master Data (CRUD), Role-Based Access Control (RBAC), Shift & Schedule Management
- Notes: Accrual balances and policies must be supported; integration hooks for scheduling and payroll.
