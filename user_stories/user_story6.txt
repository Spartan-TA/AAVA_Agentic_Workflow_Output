Title: Leave & Absence Management

User Story: As an employee I want to request and track leave (PTO, sick, unpaid) So that my time off is managed compliantly and my schedule is updated accordingly

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Leave request
Given I have available leave balance
When I submit a leave request
Then the request is sent to my supervisor for approval

Scenario: Leave approval/denial
Given a pending leave request
When my supervisor reviews and approves or denies
Then my leave balance is updated and my schedule is flagged for coverage

Scenario: Leave export
Given approved leave records
When I request an export
Then I receive a file showing all approved leaves

Additional Details:
- Priority: Medium
- Story Points: 5
- Dependencies: Employee Master Data (E02), Role Based Access Control (E03), Shift & Schedule Management (E05)
- Notes: Accrual balances and policies must be enforced; integration hooks for scheduling and payroll.
