Title: Configure Training Requirements

User Story: As an HR manager I want to configure training requirements for roles So that employees receive appropriate training for their positions

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Training requirement configuration
Given I am logged in as an HR manager
When I set training requirements for a role
Then the system should enforce these requirements for all employees in that role

Additional Details:
- Priority: Medium
- Story Points: 3
- Dependencies: Role management, training module
- Notes: Support for mandatory and optional requirements
