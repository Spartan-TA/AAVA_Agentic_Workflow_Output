Title: Supervisor and Employee Review Acknowledgements

User Story: As an employee I want to acknowledge my performance review and add comments so that I can confirm receipt and provide feedback.

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Employee acknowledges review
Given a completed performance review is assigned to me
When I review the ratings and comments and submit my acknowledgement
Then the system should record my acknowledgement and allow me to add optional comments

Additional Details:
- Priority: Medium
- Story Points: 2
- Dependencies: E02, E03
- Notes: Supervisor must also acknowledge completion of the review.
