Title: Submit Self-Assessment for Performance Review

User Story: As an employee I want to submit a self-assessment as part of my performance review so that I can reflect on my achievements and areas for growth

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Completing self-assessment
Given a performance review cycle is active
When I access my review form and complete the self-assessment section
Then my responses should be saved and visible to my manager for review

Additional Details:
- Priority: High
- Story Points: 2
- Dependencies: Review cycle management, Employee portal
- Notes: Allow employees to save drafts before final submission
