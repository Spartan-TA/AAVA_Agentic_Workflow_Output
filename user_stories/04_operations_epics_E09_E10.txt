### EPIC E09: Equipment & Asset Assignment

**User Story 40**

**Title:** Register Asset

**User Story:** As an asset manager, I want to register equipment and PPE So that I can track assignments and condition.

**Acceptance Criteria:**

Scenario: Adding an asset
Given I am logged in as asset manager
When I enter asset details and save
Then the asset appears in the registry

**Additional Details:**
- Priority: Medium
- Story Points: 2
- Dependencies: E02, E03, E07
- Notes: Includes asset type, serial, condition.

---
