---
Epic E05: Shift & Schedule Management

Title: Create Shift Templates
User Story: As a scheduler, I want to create recurring shift templates so that I can efficiently assign shifts.
Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Shift Template Creation
  Given shift parameters
  When I create a template
  Then the template is saved and reusable

Additional Details:
Priority: High
Story Points: 3
Dependencies: E02, E03, E04
Notes: Support rotations and overtime rules.

---

Title: Assign Shifts to Employees
User Story: As a supervisor, I want to assign shifts to employees so that staffing needs are met.
Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Shift Assignment
  Given a shift template
  When I assign employees
  Then assignments are reflected in their schedules

Additional Details:
Priority: High
Story Points: 5
Dependencies: Create Shift Templates
Notes: Bulk-assign supported.

---

Title: Detect and Prevent Scheduling Conflicts
User Story: As a scheduler, I want the system to detect and prevent scheduling conflicts so that employees are not double-booked.
Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Conflict Detection
  Given existing schedules
  When I assign a new shift
  Then conflicts are flagged and prevented

Additional Details:
Priority: High
Story Points: 5
Dependencies: Assign Shifts to Employees
Notes: Include blackout dates.

---

Title: Audit Shift Assignments
User Story: As a compliance officer, I want audit entries generated for shift assignments so that changes are traceable.
Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Shift Assignment Audit
  Given a shift assignment
  When the assignment is made
  Then an audit entry is created

Additional Details:
Priority: Medium
Story Points: 2
Dependencies: Assign Shifts to Employees
Notes: Include actor and timestamp.
