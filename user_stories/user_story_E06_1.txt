Title: Employees Request Leave, Supervisors Approve/Deny

User Story: As an employee I want to request PTO, sick, or unpaid leave and have my supervisor approve or deny the request So that my absences are tracked and managed according to company policy

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Submit leave request
Given I am authenticated as an employee
When I submit a leave request with type, dates, and reason
Then the system creates a pending approval task for my supervisor

Scenario: Supervisor reviews leave request
Given a leave request is pending
When my supervisor approves or denies it
Then the employee is notified and the leave balance is updated if approved

Additional Details:
- Priority: Medium
- Story Points: 5
- Dependencies: E02, E03, E05
- Notes: Leave types and policies must be configurable; all actions must be logged for audit.
