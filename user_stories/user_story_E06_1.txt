Title: Request Leave (PTO, Sick, Unpaid)

User Story: As an employee I want to request leave for PTO, sick, or unpaid time So that my absences are tracked and approved

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Submitting a leave request
Given I am logged into the portal
When I submit a leave request with type and dates
Then the request should be routed to my supervisor for approval

Additional Details:
- Priority: High
- Story Points: 2
- Dependencies: Employee profile, approval workflow
- Notes: Support attachments and comments.
