Title: Employee Leave Request Workflow

User Story: As an employee I want to request PTO, sick, or unpaid leave so that my time off is tracked and approved according to company policy

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Submit leave request
Given I am logged into the system
When I fill out and submit a leave request form specifying type, dates, and reason
Then my request should be sent to my supervisor for approval and my leave balance should be updated accordingly

Additional Details:
- Priority: Medium
- Story Points: 3
- Dependencies: Employee Master Data, RBAC, Shift & Schedule Management
- Notes: Request types and balances must match company policy; validation for overlapping leave and minimum notice period.
