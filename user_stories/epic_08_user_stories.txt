### E08: Safety Incidents & OSHA Reporting

**User Story 8.1**
Title: Safety Incident Submission

User Story: As an Employee I want to report safety incidents or near-misses So that hazards are addressed promptly.

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Incident report
Given I witness or am involved in an incident
When I submit a report
Then the incident is logged with required fields and status set to Open

Additional Details:
- Priority: High
- Story Points: 3
- Dependencies: E02, E03
- Notes: Severity, location, and involved employees required

---

**User Story 8.2**
Title: Incident Investigation Workflow

User Story: As a Safety Officer I want to manage the investigation and resolution of incidents So that corrective actions are tracked.

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Investigation workflow
Given an incident is Open
When I update the status to Investigating or Resolved
Then the workflow and corrective actions are recorded

Additional Details:
- Priority: High
- Story Points: 5
- Dependencies: E02, E03
- Notes: Status transitions must be auditable

---

**User Story 8.3**
Title: OSHA Summary Report Generation

User Story: As an Admin I want to generate OSHA 300/300A summary reports So that regulatory requirements are met.

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: OSHA report
Given incidents are recorded
When I generate a report
Then the output matches OSHA field requirements and is exportable

Additional Details:
- Priority: High
- Story Points: 3
- Dependencies: E02, E03
- Notes: Export formats: CSV, PDF

---

**User Story 8.4**
Title: Safety Metrics Dashboard

User Story: As a Supervisor I want to view safety KPIs and incident trends So that I can monitor and improve safety culture.

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Metrics dashboard
Given I access the dashboard
When I filter by date or department
Then relevant metrics and trends are displayed

Additional Details:
- Priority: Medium
- Story Points: 3
- Dependencies: E02, E03
- Notes: Dashboard endpoints must be performant
