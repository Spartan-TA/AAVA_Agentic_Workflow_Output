EpicID: E06
Title: Leave & Absence Management
User Story: As an employee, I want to request leave and track my accruals so that my time off is managed and compliant with policy.
Acceptance Criteria:
  Scenario: Request Leave
    Given sufficient accrual balance
    When I submit a leave request
    Then the request is created and pending approval
  Scenario: Approve/Deny Leave
    Given a supervisor
    When they review a request
    Then they can approve or deny it
  Scenario: Update Balances
    Given approved leave
    When the leave is processed
    Then accrual balances are updated
  Scenario: Flag Scheduled Shifts
    Given approved leave overlaps with scheduled shifts
    When the schedule is viewed
    Then affected shifts are flagged for coverage
Additional Details:
  Priority: Medium
  Story Points: 8
  Dependencies: E02, E03, E05
  Notes: Exports must show approved leaves
