Title: Expiring Certification Notifications

User Story: As a supervisor I want to receive notifications when employee certifications are about to expire So that I can proactively schedule renewals and maintain compliance

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Certification expiry alert
Given an employee's certification is within 30 days of expiry
When the system detects the upcoming expiration
Then a notification is sent to the supervisor and the employee via configured channels

Additional Details:
- Priority: Medium
- Story Points: 2
- Dependencies: E03, E05, E06, E07
- Notes: Delivery status must be tracked; rate limits applied to prevent spam.
