Title: Notifications for Expiring Certifications

User Story: As a supervisor I want to receive notifications when employee certifications are about to expire so that I can ensure compliance and schedule renewals proactively.

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Certification expiry notification
Given an employee's certification is within 30 days of expiry
When the system detects the upcoming expiration
Then a notification should be sent to the supervisor and the employee

Additional Details:
- Priority: Medium
- Story Points: 3
- Dependencies: RBAC (E03), Training & Certification Tracking (E07)
- Notes: Notification templates must be localized and delivery status tracked.
