Title: Assign Required Training
User Story: As a training coordinator I want to assign required training to employees So that compliance and skill requirements are met
Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Training assignment
Given I am on the training management page
When I assign a training to an employee
Then the employee should be notified and the assignment tracked
Additional Details:
- Priority: High
- Story Points: 3
- Dependencies: Notification module, training catalog
- Notes: Support recurring and one-time trainings.
