Title: Certification Expiry Alerts
User Story: As an employee I want to receive alerts before my certifications expire So that I can renew them and remain eligible for equipment-sensitive roles
Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Expiry alert Given my certification is expiring in 30 or 7 days When the system checks expiry dates Then I receive an in-app and email notification with renewal instructions
Additional Details:
- Priority: High
- Story Points: 2
- Dependencies: Certification records, notification system
- Notes: Alerts should be configurable and support multiple channels.
