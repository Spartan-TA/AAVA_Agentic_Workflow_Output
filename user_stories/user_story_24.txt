Title: View Team Leave Calendar

User Story: As a manager I want to view a calendar of team leave So that I can plan resources and avoid scheduling conflicts

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Manager views team leave calendar
Given I am logged in as a manager
When I open the team leave calendar
Then I should see all approved and pending leaves for my direct reports

Additional Details:
- Priority: Medium
- Story Points: 2
- Dependencies: Calendar module, Employee Master Data
- Notes: Calendar should support filtering by team, department, and leave type.
