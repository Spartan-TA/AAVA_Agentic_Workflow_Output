Title: Exclude Leaves from Scheduling

User Story: As a scheduler I want approved leaves to automatically block shift assignments So that employees are not scheduled during their time off

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Prevent scheduling during approved leave
Given an employee has approved leave
When I attempt to assign a shift during their leave
Then the system should prevent the assignment and display a warning

Additional Details:
- Priority: High
- Story Points: 2
- Dependencies: Leave approval, shift assignment logic
- Notes: Should work for partial day leaves.
