Title: Leave Approval Workflow

User Story: As a manager I want to review and approve or reject leave requests So that I can ensure adequate staffing and compliance

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Approving a leave request
Given a pending leave request is assigned to me
When I review the details and click 'Approve'
Then the employee should be notified and the leave recorded in the schedule

Scenario: Rejecting a leave request
Given a pending leave request is assigned to me
When I click 'Reject' and provide a reason
Then the employee should be notified with the rejection reason

Additional Details:
- Priority: High
- Story Points: 3
- Dependencies: Notification module, scheduling module
- Notes: Approval actions should be logged for audit purposes.
