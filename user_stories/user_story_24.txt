Title: Record Safety Incident

User Story: As a supervisor I want to record safety incidents so that workplace hazards are documented and addressed

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Record new safety incident
Given I witness or am informed of an incident
When I fill out the incident form with required details
Then the incident should be logged and available for investigation

Additional Details:
- Priority: High
- Story Points: 3
- Dependencies: Safety module
- Notes: Support attachments and witness statements.
