## Project Overview

The Warehouse Employee Management System (WEMS) is a comprehensive platform designed to streamline and automate all aspects of employee management within a warehouse environment. The system covers the full employee lifecycle, from onboarding and scheduling to compliance, safety, and payroll integration. This document provides an executive summary of the transformation process from high-level epics to actionable user stories, a master index of all epics and stories, and a roadmap for implementation.

---

## Transformation Methodology

### Process Overview

1. **Epic Identification:** 20 high-level epics were defined, each representing a major capability or functional area.
2. **Epic Decomposition:** Each epic was broken down into detailed user stories, ensuring coverage of all acceptance criteria and business needs.
3. **User Story Detailing:** Stories were written in the INVEST format (Independent, Negotiable, Valuable, Estimable, Small, Testable), with Gherkin-style acceptance criteria for clarity and testability.
4. **Story Pointing:** Each story was estimated using story points, reflecting complexity and effort.
5. **Dependency Mapping:** Dependencies between epics and stories were identified to inform sequencing.
6. **Prioritization:** Epics and stories were prioritized based on business value, compliance, and technical dependencies.

## Risk and Considerations

- **Integration Complexity:** HRIS, WMS, and payroll integrations may require custom adapters and extensive testing.
- **Compliance:** OSHA, GDPR, and labor law requirements must be validated with legal/compliance teams.
- **Change Management:** Significant process changes for warehouse staff; training and communication plans required.
- **Data Migration:** Legacy data import and cleansing may impact timelines.
- **Performance:** High concurrency and mobile/offline use cases require robust performance and resilience engineering.
- **Security:** RBAC and audit trail must be airtight to protect PII and meet regulatory standards.
