Title: Mobile Clock-In/Out and Schedule Viewing

User Story: As a warehouse worker I want to clock in/out and view my schedule on my mobile device So that I can manage my work from anywhere

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Mobile clock-in/out and schedule
Given I have access to the mobile PWA
When I clock in/out or view my schedule
Then the actions are processed and displayed responsively on my device

Additional Details:
- Priority: Medium
- Story Points: 3
- Dependencies: E04, E05, E06, E12
- Notes: Must support offline queue for clock events and conflict resolution.
