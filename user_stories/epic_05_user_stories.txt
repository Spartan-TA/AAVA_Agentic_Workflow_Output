### E05: Shift & Schedule Management

**User Story 5.1**
Title: Create and Manage Shift Templates

User Story: As an HR user I want to define recurring shift templates So that scheduling is efficient and consistent.

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Shift template CRUD
Given the shift management UI
When I create/edit/delete a template
Then the template is persisted and available for assignment

Additional Details:
- Priority: High
- Story Points: 2
- Dependencies: E02, E03, E04
- Notes: Support for rotations and overtime rules

---

**User Story 5.2**
Title: Assign Shifts to Employees

User Story: As a supervisor I want to assign shifts to employees So that staffing requirements are met.

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Shift assignment
Given available employees and shifts
When I assign shifts
Then assignments are saved and visible to employees

Additional Details:
- Priority: High
- Story Points: 2
- Dependencies: E05.1
- Notes: Bulk assignment supported

---

**User Story 5.3**
Title: Detect and Prevent Scheduling Conflicts

User Story: As a scheduler I want the system to prevent overlapping or conflicting shifts So that employees are not double-booked.

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Conflict detection
Given an employee with a scheduled shift
When I try to assign an overlapping shift
Then the system blocks the action and shows an error

Additional Details:
- Priority: High
- Story Points: 2
- Dependencies: E05.2
- Notes: Includes blackout dates and operation calendars

---

**User Story 5.4**
Title: View Personal Upcoming Shifts

User Story: As a worker I want to view my upcoming shifts So that I can plan accordingly.

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: View schedule
Given assigned shifts
When I log in
Then I see a list/calendar of my upcoming shifts

Additional Details:
- Priority: High
- Story Points: 1
- Dependencies: E05.2
- Notes: Mobile-friendly view

---

**User Story 5.5**
Title: Audit Shift Assignment Changes

User Story: As an auditor I want all shift assignment changes logged So that compliance is ensured.

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Audit log entry
Given a shift assignment change
When the change is made
Then an audit entry is created with actor, timestamp, and details

Additional Details:
- Priority: High
- Story Points: 1
- Dependencies: E05.2
- Notes: Integrate with central audit trail
