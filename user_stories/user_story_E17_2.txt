Title: Generate Onboarding Tasks for Training and Asset Assignment

User Story: As a supervisor I want onboarding tasks for training and asset assignment to be automatically generated for new hires So that all compliance and operational requirements are met

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Onboarding task generation
Given a new employee record is created
When onboarding is initiated
Then tasks for required training and asset assignment are generated and tracked until completion

Additional Details:
- Priority: Medium
- Story Points: 5
- Dependencies: E02, E03, E05, E07, E09, E13
- Notes: Task completion status must be visible to HR and supervisors.
