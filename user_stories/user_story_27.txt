Title: Upload Certification Documents

User Story: As an employee I want to upload certification documents So that HR can verify and store proof of compliance

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Upload certification document
Given I am on my profile page
When I upload a certification file
Then HR should be notified and the document should be stored securely

Additional Details:
- Priority: Medium
- Story Points: 2
- Dependencies: Employee profile, document storage
- Notes: Support PDF, image formats, and expiration reminders.
