Title: Safety Officer Reviews and Closes Incident

User Story: As a safety officer I want to review reported safety incidents and close them after resolution So that compliance and workplace safety are maintained

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Closing a safety incident
Given a safety incident is reported and assigned to me
When I review the details and mark it as resolved
Then the incident status should update to closed and a resolution note is recorded

Additional Details:
- Priority: High
- Story Points: 3
- Dependencies: Incident reporting, user notifications
- Notes: All actions should be timestamped for audit.
