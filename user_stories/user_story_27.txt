Title: Employee Views Training Status

User Story: As an employee I want to view my assigned training and certification status So that I can track my compliance and progress

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Employee checks training status
Given I am logged into the system
When I navigate to my training dashboard
Then I should see all assigned, completed, and overdue training modules and certifications

Additional Details:
- Priority: Medium
- Story Points: 2
- Dependencies: Employee Master Data
- Notes: Dashboard should highlight overdue or expiring items.
