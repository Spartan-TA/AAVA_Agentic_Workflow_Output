Title: Incident Investigation Workflow

User Story: As a safety officer I want to manage the workflow for investigating safety incidents so that corrective actions are tracked and incidents are resolved efficiently

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Advancing incident status through investigation
Given an incident is recorded with status 'Open'
When I assign investigators and add findings or corrective actions
Then the incident status updates to 'Investigating' and finally to 'Resolved' upon completion

Additional Details:
- Priority: High
- Story Points: 3
- Dependencies: Incident Recording, RBAC
- Notes: All status changes and actions should be auditable.
