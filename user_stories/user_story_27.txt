Title: Safety Incident Workflow
User Story: As a safety officer I want to manage the investigation and resolution workflow for reported incidents So that corrective actions are tracked and compliance is maintained
Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Incident workflow Given an incident is reported When I update the status to Investigating or Resolved Then the workflow history is logged and visible for audit
Additional Details:
- Priority: High
- Story Points: 2
- Dependencies: Safety incident recording
- Notes: Workflow should support status changes and corrective action documentation.
