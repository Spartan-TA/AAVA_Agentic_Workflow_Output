Title: Soft-Delete Employee Record

User Story: As an HR user I want to soft-delete employee records So that historical data is preserved and inactive employees are excluded from active lists

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Employee soft-delete
Given I am authenticated as HR
When I request to delete an employee record
Then the record is marked as inactive and excluded from active queries

Additional Details:
- Priority: High
- Story Points: 2
- Dependencies: Employee domain
- Notes: Ensure deleted records are not physically removed and can be restored if needed
