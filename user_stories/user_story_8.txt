Title: Clock-In/Out Endpoints with Validation and Shift Association

User Story: As a warehouse worker I want to clock in and out using validated endpoints So that my attendance and shift hours are accurately recorded

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Clock-in/out event capture
Given I am scheduled for a shift
When I POST a clock-in or clock-out event with required validation
Then my attendance is recorded and associated with the correct shift

Additional Details:
- Priority: High
- Story Points: 3
- Dependencies: Employee CRUD, RBAC
- Notes: Device and geofence capture optional; automatic shift association required.
