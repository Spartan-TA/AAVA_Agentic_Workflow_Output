Title: Soft Delete Employee

User Story: As an HR manager I want to soft-delete employee records so that terminated employees are not shown in active lists but their history is retained for compliance.

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Soft-delete employee
Given I am authenticated as HR and viewing an employee profile
When I choose to delete the employee
Then the employee is marked as inactive, not removed from the database, and excluded from active queries

Additional Details:
- Priority: High
- Story Points: 2
- Dependencies: Employee Master Data (E02)
- Notes: Soft-deleted employees must be filterable in admin views.
