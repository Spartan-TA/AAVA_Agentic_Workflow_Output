Title: Support Pagination and Filtering for Employee List

User Story: As an HR Manager I want to paginate and filter the employee list So that I can efficiently find and manage employee records in large warehouses

Acceptance Criteria:

Scenario: Paginated employee list
Given more than 20 employees exist
When I GET /employees?page=1&size=10
Then I receive only 10 employee records and metadata about total pages

Scenario: Filter by department
Given employees exist in multiple departments
When I GET /employees?department=Shipping
Then only employees in the Shipping department are returned

Scenario: Filter by status
Given employees with status 'Active' and 'Inactive' exist
When I GET /employees?status=Active
Then only active employees are returned

Additional Details:
- Priority: High
- Story Points: 3
- Dependencies: E01, E02
- Notes: Filtering should support department, role, status, and shiftGroup.
