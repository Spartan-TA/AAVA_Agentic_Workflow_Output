### Epic E06: Leave & Absence Management

**User Story 1**
Title: Request Leave
User Story: As an employee, I want to request PTO, sick, or unpaid leave so that my time off is tracked.
Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Leave Request
Given I have available leave balance
When I submit a leave request
Then the request is recorded and pending approval
Additional Details:
- Priority: Medium
- Story Points: 2
- Dependencies: Employee API, RBAC, Shift management
- Notes: Accrual policies applied

**User Story 2**
Title: Approve/Deny Leave
User Story: As a supervisor, I want to approve or deny leave requests so that staffing is maintained.
Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Leave Approval
Given a pending leave request
When I approve or deny
Then the employee is notified
And balances are updated
Additional Details:
- Priority: Medium
- Story Points: 2
- Dependencies: Leave request
- Notes: Approval workflow

**User Story 3**
Title: Auto-Flag Scheduled Shifts for Coverage
User Story: As a scheduler, I want scheduled shifts to be auto-flagged when leave is approved so that coverage can be arranged.
Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Shift Coverage
Given approved leave overlaps with scheduled shifts
When leave is approved
Then affected shifts are flagged for coverage
Additional Details:
- Priority: Medium
- Story Points: 2
- Dependencies: Leave approval, shift management
- Notes: Export shows approved leaves
