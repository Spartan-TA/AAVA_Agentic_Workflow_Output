EPIC E06 - LEAVE & ABSENCE MANAGEMENT

===========================================

Title: Submit Paid Time Off (PTO) Request

User Story: As an employee I want to submit a PTO request so that I can take planned time off and have it tracked against my accrual balance

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: PTO request submission
Given I am logged in and have available PTO balance
When I fill out and submit a PTO request form
Then my request should be recorded, my balance updated, and my supervisor notified for approval

Additional Details:
- Priority: Medium
- Story Points: 3
- Dependencies: E02, E03, E05
- Notes: PTO balance must be checked before submission

---

Title: Approve or Deny Leave Requests

User Story: As a supervisor I want to review and approve or deny leave requests so that I can ensure adequate shift coverage

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Supervisor reviews leave requests
Given I have pending leave requests from my team
When I approve or deny a request
Then the employee is notified and the schedule is updated accordingly

Additional Details:
- Priority: Medium
- Story Points: 2
- Dependencies: E02, E03, E05
- Notes: Approval/denial must trigger notifications and update scheduling

---

Title: Track Leave Accrual Balances

User Story: As an employee I want to view my current leave accrual balances so that I can plan my time off

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Viewing leave balances
Given I am logged in
When I navigate to my leave balance page
Then I see up-to-date PTO, sick, and unpaid leave balances

Additional Details:
- Priority: Medium
- Story Points: 2
- Dependencies: E02, E03
- Notes: Balances must reflect recent accruals and deductions

---

Title: Exclude Approved Leave from Scheduling and Payroll

User Story: As a system I want to automatically exclude approved leave periods from shift assignments and payroll calculations so that staffing and pay are accurate

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Leave exclusion integration
Given an employee has approved leave
When schedules and payroll are generated
Then the leave period is excluded from shift assignments and payroll hours

Additional Details:
- Priority: Medium
- Story Points: 3
- Dependencies: E02, E03, E05
- Notes: Integration hooks for scheduling and payroll modules
