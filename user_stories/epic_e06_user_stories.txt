EPIC E06: Leave & Absence Management

===========================================

Title: Leave & Absence Management

User Story: As an employee I want to request PTO, sick, or unpaid leave So that my time off is tracked

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Leave request
Given I am logged in
When I submit a leave request
Then my supervisor is notified for approval

Additional Details:
- Priority: Medium
- Story Points: 2
- Dependencies: Employee CRUD, Schedule management
- Notes: Accrual balances shown

---

Title: Supervisor Approves/Deny Leave

User Story: As a supervisor I want to approve or deny leave requests So that staffing plans are maintained

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Leave approval
Given a leave request is pending
When I approve or deny
Then the employee is notified and balances updated

Additional Details:
- Priority: Medium
- Story Points: 2
- Dependencies: Leave management
- Notes: Scheduled shifts auto-flagged for coverage
