### EPIC E14: Audit Trail & Compliance

**User Story 65**

**Title:** Log Sensitive Changes

**User Story:** As a compliance officer, I want all sensitive changes logged So that I can audit system activity.

**Acceptance Criteria:**

Scenario: Logging a change
Given a sensitive action occurs
When the change is made
Then actor, timestamp, and before/after values are logged

**Additional Details:**
- Priority: High
- Story Points: 2
- Dependencies: E03
- Notes: Applies to PII, schedules, approvals.
