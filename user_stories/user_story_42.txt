Title: Schedule Automated Payroll Exports

User Story: As a payroll administrator I want to schedule automated payroll exports So that payroll data is sent to providers on a recurring basis without manual intervention

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Schedule payroll export
Given I am logged in as a payroll administrator
When I configure export frequency and destination
Then the system should automatically export payroll data as scheduled

Additional Details:
- Priority: Medium
- Story Points: 3
- Dependencies: Integration Layer, Time & Attendance
- Notes: Support for error notifications if export fails.
