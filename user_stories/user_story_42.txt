Title: Configure Quiet Hours for Notifications

User Story: As an employee I want to configure quiet hours for notifications so that I am not disturbed outside of my preferred time window.

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Setting quiet hours
Given I am on my notification preferences page
When I set a quiet hours window (e.g., 10pm-6am)
Then the system should suppress non-urgent notifications during that period and deliver them after quiet hours end

Additional Details:
- Priority: Medium
- Story Points: 2
- Dependencies: E03, E05, E06, E07
- Notes: Urgent notifications (e.g., safety alerts) may bypass quiet hours.
