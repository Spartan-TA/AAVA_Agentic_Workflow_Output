Title: Update Schedules and Revoke Access in Linked Systems During Offboarding

User Story: As an HR manager I want offboarding to update schedules and revoke access in all linked systems So that employee termination is reflected across HRIS, EMS, and asset management

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Linked system offboarding
Given an employee is terminated
When offboarding is processed
Then schedules are updated and access is revoked in all integrated systems

Additional Details:
- Priority: Medium
- Story Points: 4
- Dependencies: E02, E03, E05, E07, E09, E13
- Notes: Offboarding must trigger sync jobs to all connected systems.
