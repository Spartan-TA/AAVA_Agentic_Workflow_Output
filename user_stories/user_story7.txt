Title: Training & Certification Tracking
User Story: As a safety manager I want to track employee certifications and expirations so that only qualified staff are assigned to equipment-sensitive roles.
Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Certification management
Given I am authenticated as a safety manager
When I create, update, or delete certifications for employees
Then alerts are generated 30 and 7 days before expiry
And scheduling checks block assignment to unqualified employees
And certification status is visible on employee profiles
Additional Details:
- Priority: High
- Story Points: 5
- Dependencies: Employee Master Data (CRUD), Role-Based Access Control (RBAC)
- Notes: Proof documents must be uploadable; expired certifications block task assignment.
