Title: Training & Certification Tracking

User Story: As a safety manager I want to track employee certifications and expirations so that only qualified staff are assigned to sensitive tasks.

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Certification expiry alert
Given an employee's certification is expiring soon
When the expiry is within 30 or 7 days
Then an alert is generated and unqualified assignments are blocked

Additional Details:
- Priority: High
- Story Points: 3
- Dependencies: Employee Master Data, RBAC
- Notes: Upload proof documents; certification status on profile
