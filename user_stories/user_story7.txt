Title: Assign Training to Employee
User Story: As a supervisor I want to assign training modules to employees so that compliance is maintained
Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Assign training
Given I am on the training management page
When I select an employee and assign a module
Then the employee should be notified and the assignment recorded
Additional Details:
- Priority: High
- Story Points: 2
- Dependencies: Training module
- Notes: Track assignment date
