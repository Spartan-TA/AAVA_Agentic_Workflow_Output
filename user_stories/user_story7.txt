Title: Training & Certification Tracking

User Story: As a safety manager I want to track employee certifications, expirations, and renewals so that only qualified staff are assigned to equipment-sensitive roles and compliance is maintained.

Acceptance Criteria:

Scenario: Add certification
Given an employee
When I add a new certification
Then it is recorded with type, issue date, and expiry date

Scenario: Expiry alerts
Given a certification nearing expiry
When it is 30 or 7 days from expiration
Then an alert is sent to the employee and supervisor

Scenario: Assignment block
Given an expired or missing certification
When assigning to a restricted task
Then the assignment is blocked

Scenario: Certification status visibility
Given an employee profile
When viewed by authorized users
Then all certifications and statuses are visible

Additional Details:
- Priority: High
- Story Points: 5
- Dependencies: Employee Master Data (E02), Role-Based Access Control (E03)
- Notes: Upload proof documents and block assignments if certs are expired.
