Title: Certification CRUD and Alerts

User Story: As a training coordinator I want to manage employee certifications and receive expiry alerts So that compliance is maintained.

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Certification management and alerts
Given a certification is added or updated
When the expiry date approaches (30/7 days)
Then alerts are sent and expired certifications block assignments

Additional Details:
- Priority: High
- Story Points: 4
- Dependencies: E02, E03
- Notes: Certification status visible on employee profile.

Title: Certification Assignment Blocking

User Story: As a scheduler I want to prevent assignment of tasks to employees with expired certifications So that only qualified staff are assigned.

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Assignment blocking for expired certs
Given an employee's certification is expired
When I attempt to assign them to a restricted task
Then the system blocks the assignment and notifies me

Additional Details:
- Priority: High
- Story Points: 2
- Dependencies: E02, E03
- Notes: Proof documents can be uploaded.
