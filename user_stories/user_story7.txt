Title: Time & Attendance Clock-In
User Story: As a worker I want to clock in and out via the app So that my worked hours are accurately recorded for payroll
Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Valid clock-in event
Given I am scheduled for a shift today
When I POST to /attendance/clock-in with my badgeId
Then my clock-in time is recorded and associated with my shift
Additional Details:
- Priority: High
- Story Points: 3
- Dependencies: E02, E03
- Notes: Device and geofence capture optional
