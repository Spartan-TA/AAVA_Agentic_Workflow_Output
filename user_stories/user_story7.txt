### E07 â Training & Certification Tracking

**User Story 1: Track Employee Certifications**
- **User Story:** As an HR user, I want to record certifications for employees so that compliance is tracked.
- **Acceptance Criteria:**
  - Given an employee,
  - When I add a certification,
  - Then it is visible on their profile.
- **Additional Details:** Priority: High; Story Points: 2; Dependencies: E02, E03; Notes: Support multiple certs per employee.

**User Story 2: Certification Expiry Alerts**
- **User Story:** As a supervisor, I want alerts for expiring certifications so that renewals are not missed.
- **Acceptance Criteria:**
  - Given a cert expiring in 30/7 days,
  - When the date approaches,
  - Then alerts are sent to employee and supervisor.
- **Additional Details:** Priority: High; Story Points: 2; Dependencies: E07-1; Notes: In-app and email/SMS.

**User Story 3: Block Assignment to Expired Cert Roles**
- **User Story:** As a scheduler, I want to prevent assignment to tasks requiring valid certs so that safety is enforced.
- **Acceptance Criteria:**
  - Given an expired cert,
  - When I assign a restricted task,
  - Then the system blocks the assignment.
- **Additional Details:** Priority: High; Story Points: 2; Dependencies: E05, E07-1; Notes: Show error message.

**User Story 4: Upload Proof Documents**
- **User Story:** As an HR user, I want to upload proof of certifications so that records are auditable.
- **Acceptance Criteria:**
  - Given a certification,
  - When I upload a document,
  - Then it is stored and linked to the cert.
- **Additional Details:** Priority: High; Story Points: 2; Dependencies: E07-1; Notes: Support PDF, image formats.

**User Story 5: Certification Status on Profile**
- **User Story:** As a supervisor, I want to view certification status on employee profiles so that I can verify qualifications.
- **Acceptance Criteria:**
  - Given an employee,
  - When I view their profile,
  - Then all certifications and statuses are visible.
- **Additional Details:** Priority: High; Story Points: 1; Dependencies: E07-1; Notes: Show expiry dates.
