Title: Clock In/Out Endpoint for Employees
User Story: As a warehouse employee I want to clock in and out via an endpoint So that my attendance is recorded

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Clock in/out event
Given I am authenticated
When I submit a clock in or out request
Then my attendance event is recorded with timestamp

Additional Details:
- Priority: High
- Story Points: 3
- Dependencies: Employee Master Data (CRUD)
- Notes: Prevent duplicate clock-ins without clock-out
