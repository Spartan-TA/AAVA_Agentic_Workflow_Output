Title: Training & Certification Tracking

User Story: As a safety manager I want to track employee certifications and expirations so that only qualified staff are assigned to equipment-sensitive roles and compliance is maintained.

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Add certification
  Given an employee
  When I add a certification with expiry date and proof document
  Then the certification is saved and visible on the employee profile

Scenario: Expiry alerts
  Given a certification is nearing expiry
  When the expiry is within 30 or 7 days
  Then an alert is generated for the employee and supervisor

Scenario: Assignment check
  Given a task requires certification
  When scheduling is performed
  Then unqualified employees are blocked from assignment

Additional Details:
- Priority: High
- Story Points: 5
- Dependencies: Employee Master Data (E02), RBAC (E03)
- Notes: Certification status must be visible; scheduling checks required; upload proof documents.
