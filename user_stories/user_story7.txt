Title: Employee Domain CRUD - List & Filter Employees
User Story: As an HR user I want to list, paginate, and filter employee records So that I can quickly find and manage employees.
Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: List and filter employees
Given multiple employee records exist
When I GET /employees with filters and pagination
Then I receive a paginated, filtered list of employees
Additional Details:
- Priority: High
- Story Points: 2
- Dependencies: Create Employee
- Notes: Support filtering by department, status, role
