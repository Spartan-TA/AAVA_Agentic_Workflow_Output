Title: Employee Soft-Delete Functionality

User Story: As an HR manager I want to soft-delete employee records So that I can remove employees from active lists without losing historical data

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Soft-delete employee
  Given I am viewing an employee record
  When I choose to delete the employee
  Then the employee should be marked as inactive and hidden from active lists but retained in the database
Scenario: Restore soft-deleted employee
  Given an employee is soft-deleted
  When I choose to restore the employee
  Then the employee should reappear in active lists

Additional Details:
- Priority: High
- Story Points: 2
- Dependencies: Employee database
- Notes: Ensure audit trail for delete/restore actions
