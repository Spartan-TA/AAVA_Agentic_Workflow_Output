Title: Training & Certification Tracking

User Story: As a supervisor I want to track employee certifications and expirations So that only qualified staff are assigned to equipment-sensitive roles and compliance is maintained

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Certification expiry alert
Given an employee has a certification expiring soon
When the expiry date is within 30 days
Then the system sends an alert and blocks assignment to restricted tasks

Additional Details:
- Priority: High
- Story Points: 5
- Dependencies: Employee Master Data, RBAC
- Notes: CRUD for certifications; alerts 30/7 days before expiry; certification status visible on employee profile
