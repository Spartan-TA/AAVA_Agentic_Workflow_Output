Title: Create Shift Templates and Rotations

User Story: As an HR manager I want to define recurring shift templates and rotations so that scheduling is efficient and consistent

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Creating a shift template
Given I am logged in as HR manager
When I create a new shift template with recurrence rules
Then the template is saved and available for assignment

Additional Details:
- Priority: High
- Story Points: 3
- Dependencies: Employee master data, RBAC, Attendance
- Notes: Supports overtime rules and blackout dates

---

Title: Assign Shifts to Employees

User Story: As a supervisor I want to bulk-assign shifts to employees so that staffing plans are implemented quickly

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Bulk shift assignment
Given a list of employees and a shift template
When I assign the template to the group
Then all employees receive their personal shift schedules

Additional Details:
- Priority: High
- Story Points: 2
- Dependencies: Shift templates
- Notes: Audit entries generated for assignments

---

Title: Detect and Prevent Scheduling Conflicts

User Story: As a scheduler I want the system to detect and prevent shift conflicts so that employees are not double-booked

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Conflict detection
Given an employee has an existing shift
When I attempt to assign an overlapping shift
Then the system blocks the assignment and notifies me of the conflict

Additional Details:
- Priority: High
- Story Points: 2
- Dependencies: Shift assignment
- Notes: Must handle blackout dates and operation calendars

Title: View Upcoming Shifts

User Story: As a warehouse worker I want to view my upcoming shifts so that I can plan my work schedule

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Viewing personal schedule
Given I am logged in as a worker
When I access my schedule page
Then I see a list of all upcoming assigned shifts

Additional Details:
- Priority: High
- Story Points: 1
- Dependencies: Shift assignment
- Notes: Must be mobile-friendly
