#### User Story 7: List and Filter Employees
**As a** supervisor  
**I want** to list and filter employees by department, role, and status  
**so that** I can quickly find relevant staff.

**Acceptance Criteria:**
- **Given** filter parameters,  
  **When** I GET `/employees?department=X&role=Y`,  
  **Then** only matching employees are returned with pagination.

**Additional Details:**  
Priority: High  
Story Points: 2  
Dependencies: E02  
Notes: Support pagination and OpenAPI schema documentation.
