### E07 - Training & Certification Tracking

**Title:** Add Employee Certification
**User Story:** As an HR user, I want to add certifications to an employee profile so that compliance is tracked.
**Acceptance Criteria:**
Scenario: Add certification
Given an employee
When I add a certification
Then it appears on their profile with expiry date
**Additional Details:** Priority: High, Story Points: 2, Dependencies: E02, E03, Notes: Upload proof documents.

**Title:** Certification Expiry Alerts
**User Story:** As a supervisor, I want to receive alerts before certifications expire so that renewals are timely.
**Acceptance Criteria:**
Scenario: Expiry alert
Given a certification is expiring in 30 days
When the system runs the alert job
Then the supervisor and employee are notified
**Additional Details:** Priority: High, Story Points: 2, Dependencies: E02, E03, Notes: Alerts at 30 and 7 days.

**Title:** Block Assignment for Expired Certification
**User Story:** As a scheduler, I want to prevent assignment of tasks to employees with expired certifications so that safety is ensured.
**Acceptance Criteria:**
Scenario: Assignment block
Given an employee's certification is expired
When I assign them to a restricted task
Then the system blocks the assignment
**Additional Details:** Priority: High, Story Points: 3, Dependencies: E02, E03, Notes: Visible status on profile.
