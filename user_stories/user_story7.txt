Title: Track Training & Certification Status

User Story: As a safety manager I want to track required certifications, their expirations, and ensure only qualified employees are assigned to equipment-sensitive roles so that safety and compliance are maintained in the warehouse.

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Add certification
  Given an employee needs a certification
  When I add a certification record with expiry date
  Then the certification is saved and visible on the employee profile

Scenario: Expiry alerts
  Given a certification is expiring in 30 or 7 days
  When the system checks certification status
  Then an alert is generated for the employee and supervisor

Scenario: Block unqualified assignments
  Given a certification is expired or missing
  When an assignment to a sensitive task is attempted
  Then the system blocks the assignment and notifies the supervisor

Scenario: Upload proof documents
  Given a certification is added or renewed
  When I upload proof documents
  Then the documents are stored and linked to the certification record

Additional Details:
- Priority: High
- Story Points: 5
- Dependencies: Employee Master Data, Role-Based Access Control
- Notes: Certification status must be visible on employee profile; scheduling checks required for compliance.
