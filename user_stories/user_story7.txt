Title: Employee Clock Out

User Story: As an employee I want to clock out at the end of my shift So that my working hours are accurately recorded

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Successful clock out
Given I am logged into the system and have clocked in for my shift
When I select 'Clock Out' on the dashboard
Then my clock-out time is recorded and I receive a confirmation notification

Additional Details:
- Priority: High
- Story Points: 3
- Dependencies: Shift & Schedule Management, Employee Master Data
- Notes: Prevent clock-out if clock-in was not performed.
