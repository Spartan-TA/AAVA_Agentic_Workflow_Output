Title: Training & Certification Tracking

User Story: As a safety manager I want to track employee certifications and expirations so that only qualified staff are assigned to equipment-sensitive roles and compliance is maintained.

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Certification tracking and assignment
Given an employee profile
When a certification is added or updated
Then alerts are generated 30/7 days before expiry
And scheduling checks block unqualified assignments
And certification status is visible on the profile
And proof documents can be uploaded

Additional Details:
- Priority: High
- Story Points: 5
- Dependencies: Employee Master Data (CRUD), Role-Based Access Control (RBAC)
- Notes: Block assignment to tasks requiring expired certifications; CRUD for certifications.
