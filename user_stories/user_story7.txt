Title: Training & Certification - Track Certifications and Expiry
User Story: As a safety manager, I want to track employee certifications, expirations, and renewals so that only qualified staff are assigned to sensitive roles.
Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Add certification
  Given an employee completes training
  When I add a certification
  Then it is visible on their profile

Scenario: Expiry alerts
  Given a certification is expiring in 30/7 days
  When the system checks expirations
  Then an alert is sent

Additional Details:
Priority: High
Story Points: 3
Dependencies: E02, E03
Notes: Ensures compliance
