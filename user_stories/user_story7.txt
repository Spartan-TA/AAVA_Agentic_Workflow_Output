Title: Record Clock-In Event
User Story: As a warehouse worker I want to clock in at the start of my shift So that my working hours are accurately tracked
Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Successful clock-in Given I am scheduled for a shift When I clock in via the system Then my clock-in time is recorded and associated with my shift
Additional Details:
- Priority: High
- Story Points: 2
- Dependencies: Employee Master Data CRUD (E02), RBAC (E03)
- Notes: Geofence/device capture optional

Title: Record Clock-Out Event
User Story: As a warehouse worker I want to clock out at the end of my shift So that my shift completion is logged for payroll
Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Successful clock-out Given I am clocked in When I clock out via the system Then my clock-out time is recorded and hours worked are calculated
Additional Details:
- Priority: High
- Story Points: 2
- Dependencies: Time & Attendance (E04)
- Notes: Automatic shift association

Title: Handle Missed Punches & Corrections
User Story: As a supervisor I want to correct missed clock-in/out events So that attendance data remains accurate
Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Correction workflow Given a missed punch is detected When I submit a correction request Then an approval task is created and the record is updated upon approval
Additional Details:
- Priority: High
- Story Points: 3
- Dependencies: Time & Attendance (E04)
- Notes: Approval workflow required

Title: Export Attendance Reports

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Export attendance Given attendance records exist When I request a report export Then a CSV file is generated with daily totals and corrections
Additional Details:
- Priority: Medium
- Story Points: 2
- Dependencies: Time & Attendance (E04)
- Notes: Totals computed; exportable format
