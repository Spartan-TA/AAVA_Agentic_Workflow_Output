### E07 - Training & Certification Tracking

**User Story 1**
Title: Manage Employee Certifications
User Story: As an HR user I want to create, update, and delete employee certifications So that compliance is tracked.
Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Certification CRUD
Given I have HR access
When I manage certifications
Then changes are persisted and visible on employee profiles
Additional Details:
- Priority: High
- Story Points: 2
- Dependencies: E02, E03
- Notes: Support document uploads

**User Story 2**
Title: Alert on Expiring Certifications
User Story: As a supervisor I want to receive alerts 30 and 7 days before a certification expires So that renewals can be arranged.
Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Expiry alert
Given a certification is expiring soon
When the alert window is reached
Then an alert is sent to the employee and supervisor
Additional Details:
- Priority: High
- Story Points: 1
- Dependencies: E07-1
- Notes: Notification via email/SMS/in-app

**User Story 3**
Title: Block Assignment to Tasks Requiring Valid Certification
User Story: As a scheduler I want to prevent assignment of employees to tasks requiring certifications if their cert is expired So that only qualified staff are assigned.
Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Assignment blocked
Given an employee's certification is expired
When I try to assign them to a restricted task
Then the system blocks the assignment with an error
Additional Details:
- Priority: High
- Story Points: 2
- Dependencies: E07-1
- Notes: Applies to equipment and shift assignment

**User Story 4**
Title: Display Certification Status on Employee Profile
User Story: As an employee I want to view my certification status So that I am aware of upcoming renewals.
Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: View certification status
Given I am logged in
When I view my profile
Then all certifications and their statuses are displayed
Additional Details:
- Priority: High
- Story Points: 1
- Dependencies: E07-1
- Notes: Show expiry dates and document links
