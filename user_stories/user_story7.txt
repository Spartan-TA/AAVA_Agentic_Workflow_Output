Title: Retrieve Employee Records with Pagination and Filtering

User Story: As an HR user I want to retrieve a paginated and filterable list of employees So that I can efficiently manage large employee datasets

Acceptance Criteria:

Scenario: Paginated employee list
Given there are more than 50 employees in the system
When I GET /employees?page=2&size=20
Then I receive 20 employee records for page 2

Scenario: Filter by department and status
Given employees exist in multiple departments and statuses
When I GET /employees?department=Shipping&status=Active
Then only active employees in the Shipping department are returned

Additional Details:
- Priority: High
- Story Points: 5
- Dependencies: E01
- Notes: Support filtering by department, role, status, and shiftGroup.
