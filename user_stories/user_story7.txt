Title: Track Employee Training & Certifications

User Story:
As a compliance officer, I want to track required certifications, expirations, and renewals for employees so that only qualified staff are assigned to sensitive equipment and roles.

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario 1: Add certification
  Given an employee profile
  When I add a certification with expiry date
  Then the certification is saved and visible

Scenario 2: Expiry alerts
  Given a certification is expiring in 30 or 7 days
  When the system checks expirations
  Then an alert is sent to the employee and supervisor

Scenario 3: Block unqualified assignments
  Given a certification is expired
  When a supervisor tries to assign the employee to a restricted task
  Then the assignment is blocked

Scenario 4: Upload proof
  Given a certification is added
  When I upload a document
  Then the proof is attached to the employee profile

Additional Details:
Priority: High
Story Points: 8
Dependencies: E02, E03
Notes: Certification status must be visible on profiles.
