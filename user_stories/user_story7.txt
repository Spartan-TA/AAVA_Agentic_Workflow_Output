Title: Track Training & Certifications

User Story: As a safety manager I want to track employee certifications and expirations So that only qualified staff are assigned to equipment-sensitive roles and compliance is maintained

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Certification tracking and assignment
Given employees have certification records
When a certification is nearing expiry or is expired
Then alerts are sent 30/7 days before expiry, scheduling checks block unqualified assignments, and certification status is visible on employee profiles

Additional Details:
- Priority: High
- Story Points: 5
- Dependencies: Employee Master Data, Role-Based Access Control
- Notes: CRUD for certifications; upload proof documents; block assignment to tasks requiring expired certs.
