Title: Employee Data Pagination & Filtering

User Story: As an HR manager I want to paginate and filter employee records So that I can efficiently search and manage large datasets

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Pagination
  Given more than 20 employees
  When I GET /employees?page=1&size=20
  Then I receive 20 employee records

Scenario: Filtering
  Given employees in multiple departments
  When I filter by department
  Then only employees from that department are returned

Additional Details:
- Priority: Medium
- Story Points: 3
- Dependencies: user_story5
- Notes: Ensure performance for large datasets.
