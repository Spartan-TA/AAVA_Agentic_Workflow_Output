Title: Implement Training & Certification Tracking

User Story: As a Safety Officer I want to track employee certifications, expirations, and renewals so that only qualified staff are assigned to equipment-sensitive roles.

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Add certification to employee
  Given an employee profile exists
  When I add a certification with expiry date
  Then the certification is saved and visible on the profile
Scenario: Alert for expiring certification
  Given a certification is expiring in 30 or 7 days
  When the system checks certification status
  Then an alert is sent to the employee and supervisor
Scenario: Block assignment for expired cert
  Given an employee has an expired certification
  When I attempt to assign them to a restricted task
  Then the assignment is blocked
Scenario: Upload proof document
  Given a certification is added or renewed
  When I upload a proof document
  Then the document is stored and linked to the certification

Additional Details:
- Priority: High
- Story Points: 8
- Dependencies: Employee CRUD (E02), RBAC (E03)
- Notes: Certification status must be visible on employee profile.
