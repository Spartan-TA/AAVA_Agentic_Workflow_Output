EPIC E08: SAFETY INCIDENTS & OSHA REPORTING

===========================================

Title: Safety Incident Recording

User Story: As a safety officer I want to record safety incidents and near-misses So that investigations and corrective actions can be tracked

Acceptance Criteria:

Scenario: Record incident
Given an incident occurs
When I POST to /safety/incidents with required fields
Then the incident is logged and status is Open

Scenario: Incident validation
Given incident data is incomplete
When I submit the record
Then validation errors are returned

Additional Details:
- Priority: High
- Story Points: 5
- Dependencies: Employee Master Data, Role-Based Access Control
- Notes: Capture severity, location, description, involved employees

---

Title: Safety Incident Workflow

User Story: As a safety manager I want to manage incident investigation and resolution So that corrective actions are documented

Acceptance Criteria:

Scenario: Investigate incident
Given an incident is Open
When investigation starts
Then status changes to Investigating

Scenario: Resolve incident
Given investigation is complete
When corrective actions are taken
Then status changes to Resolved

Additional Details:
- Priority: High
- Story Points: 5
- Dependencies: Safety Incident Recording
- Notes: Generate OSHA summary and metrics dashboard

---

Title: OSHA Reporting and Metrics

User Story: As a compliance officer I want to export OSHA 300/300A reports and view safety metrics So that regulatory requirements are met

Acceptance Criteria:

Scenario: Export OSHA report
Given incidents are recorded
When I request an export
Then OSHA 300/300A fields are included

Scenario: Metrics dashboard
Given incidents exist
When I access the dashboard endpoint
Then safety KPIs are visible

Additional Details:
- Priority: High
- Story Points: 3
- Dependencies: Safety Incident Recording
- Notes: Dashboard endpoints available
