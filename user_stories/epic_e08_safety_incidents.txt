EPIC E08: Safety Incidents & OSHA Reporting

---

Title: Record Safety Incidents and Near-Misses

User Story: As a safety officer I want to record incidents and near-misses So that safety data is captured for analysis

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Incident recording
Given an incident occurs
When I POST to /safety/incidents
Then the event is validated and stored

Additional Details:
- Priority: High
- Story Points: 2
- Dependencies: Employee Master Data, RBAC
- Notes: Severity, location, description required

---

Title: Workflow for Investigation and Corrective Actions

User Story: As a safety officer I want a workflow for investigating incidents So that corrective actions are tracked

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Investigation workflow
Given an incident is recorded
When I change status from Open to Investigating to Resolved
Then each step is logged

Additional Details:
- Priority: High
- Story Points: 2
- Dependencies: Record Safety Incidents and Near-Misses
- Notes: Status transitions

---

Title: Generate OSHA Summary Reports

User Story: As a compliance officer I want to generate OSHA summary reports So that regulatory requirements are met

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: OSHA report export
Given incidents are recorded
When I export OSHA 300/300A fields
Then the report matches required format

Additional Details:
- Priority: High
- Story Points: 1
- Dependencies: Record Safety Incidents and Near-Misses
- Notes: Exportable CSV/PDF

---

Title: Metrics Dashboard for Safety KPIs

User Story: As a manager I want a dashboard for safety KPIs So that I can monitor trends

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Dashboard view
Given incidents are logged
When I access the dashboard
Then metrics are displayed by severity, location, and time

Additional Details:
- Priority: Medium
- Story Points: 1
- Dependencies: Record Safety Incidents and Near-Misses
- Notes: Filterable by date
