Title: Notification Delivery Tracking and Rate Limiting

User Story: As an admin I want to track notification delivery status and enforce rate limits so that users are not spammed and we can audit notification effectiveness

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Notification delivery is tracked and rate limited
  Given a notification is sent to a user
  When the notification is delivered via in-app, email, or SMS
  Then the delivery status is logged and no user receives more than the configured number of notifications per hour

Additional Details:
- Priority: Medium
- Story Points: 3
- Dependencies: E03, E05, E06, E07
- Notes: Delivery logs must be queryable; rate limits configurable per channel.
