Title: Audit Log for Payroll Exports

User Story: As a compliance officer I want every payroll export to be logged with delivery status and audit details so that all payroll data transfers are traceable and verifiable

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Log payroll export event
  Given a payroll export is generated and delivered
  When I review the audit log
  Then I see the export details, delivery status, and any retry attempts

Additional Details:
- Priority: High
- Story Points: 2
- Dependencies: E04 (Attendance), E06 (Leave)
- Notes: Audit log must be exportable by date
