Epic E07: Training & Certification Tracking

---

Title: Track Employee Certifications and Expirations

User Story: As an HR manager I want to track required certifications and their expiration dates So that compliance is maintained

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Adding a certification
Given I am on an employee's profile
When I add a forklift certification with expiration date
Then the certification is tracked and flagged for renewal

Scenario: Expired certification
Given a certification has expired
When I view the employee's profile
Then the system highlights the expired status

Additional Details:
- Priority: High
- Story Points: 3
- Dependencies: E02, E03
- Notes: Supports multiple certification types

---

Title: Block Assignments for Expired Certifications

User Story: As a supervisor I want to prevent assignment of tasks requiring valid certifications to employees with expired certs So that safety and compliance are enforced

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Assignment blocked due to expired cert
Given an employee's certification is expired
When I try to assign them to forklift operation
Then the system prevents the assignment

Additional Details:
- Priority: High
- Story Points: 5
- Dependencies: E02, E03
- Notes: Assignment logic integrated with scheduling

---

Title: Upload Proof of Certification Documents

User Story: As an employee I want to upload proof documents for certifications So that HR can verify compliance

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Uploading a document
Given I have a valid certification
When I upload the document
Then HR is notified and the document is stored securely

Additional Details:
- Priority: High
- Story Points: 3
- Dependencies: E02, E03
- Notes: Support PDF, image formats
