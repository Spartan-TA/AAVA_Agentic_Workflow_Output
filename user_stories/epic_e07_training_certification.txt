EPIC E07: TRAINING & CERTIFICATION TRACKING

===========================================

Title: Certification CRUD
User Story: As an HR user, I want to create, read, update, and delete certifications for employees so that compliance is tracked.
Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Certification CRUD
Given employee certifications
When CRUD operations are performed
Then certifications are updated.
Additional Details:
- Priority: High
- Story Points: 3
- Dependencies: E02, E03
- Notes: Compliance.

===========================================

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Expiry Alert

- Priority: High
- Story Points: 2
- Dependencies: E02, E03
- Notes: Notification.
