EPIC E07: TRAINING & CERTIFICATION TRACKING

===========================================

Title: Certification Tracking CRUD

User Story: As a training coordinator I want to track employee certifications So that only qualified staff are assigned to sensitive tasks

Acceptance Criteria:

Scenario: Add certification
Given an employee needs a certification
When I POST to /certifications
Then the certification is added and linked to the employee

Scenario: Edit certification
Given a certification exists
When I PUT/PATCH to /certifications/{id}
Then the certification is updated

Scenario: Delete certification
Given a certification exists
When I DELETE /certifications/{id}
Then the certification is removed

Additional Details:
- Priority: High
- Story Points: 5
- Dependencies: Employee Master Data, Role-Based Access Control
- Notes: Support document upload for proof

---

Title: Certification Expiry Alerts

User Story: As a supervisor I want to receive alerts for expiring certifications So that renewals are handled proactively

Acceptance Criteria:

Scenario: Alert 30 days before expiry
Given a certification expires in 30 days
When the system checks certifications
Then an alert is sent to the employee and supervisor

Scenario: Alert 7 days before expiry
Given a certification expires in 7 days
When the system checks certifications
Then a high-priority alert is sent

Additional Details:
- Priority: High
- Story Points: 3
- Dependencies: Certification Tracking CRUD
- Notes: Block assignment to tasks requiring expired certs

---

Title: Certification Status on Profile

User Story: As an employee I want to view my certification status on my profile So that I am aware of my compliance

Acceptance Criteria:

Scenario: View certifications
Given I am logged in
When I access my profile
Then all certifications and expiry dates are visible

Additional Details:
- Priority: High
- Story Points: 2
- Dependencies: Certification Tracking CRUD
- Notes: Scheduling checks block unqualified assignments
