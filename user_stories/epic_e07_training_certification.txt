## User Story 1: CRUD for Certifications
**User Story:**  
As an HR user, I want to create, update, and delete employee certifications so that compliance is tracked.

**Acceptance Criteria (Gherkin):**  
Given the certification module  
When I manage certifications  
Then changes are saved and visible on employee profiles

**Additional Details:**  
- Priority: High  
- Story Points: 2  
- Dependencies: E02, E03  
- Notes: Certification types configurable

---

## User Story 2: Alert on Expiring Certifications
**User Story:**  
As a supervisor, I want to receive alerts 30 and 7 days before a certification expires so that renewals are not missed.

**Acceptance Criteria (Gherkin):**  
Given a certification nearing expiry  
When the alert period is reached  
Then a notification is sent to the employee and supervisor

**Additional Details:**  
- Priority: High  
- Story Points: 2  
- Dependencies: E07-1, E12  
- Notes: Alerts via email/SMS/in-app

---

## User Story 3: Block Assignment to Tasks Requiring Valid Certifications
**User Story:**  
As a scheduler, I want to prevent assignment of employees to tasks requiring certifications if their cert is expired.

**Acceptance Criteria (Gherkin):**  
Given an expired certification  
When assigning a task  
Then the system blocks the assignment and displays a warning

**Additional Details:**  
- Priority: High  
- Story Points: 2  
- Dependencies: E07-1, E05  
- Notes: Certification requirements defined per task

---

## User Story 4: Upload Proof Documents for Certifications
**User Story:**  
As an employee, I want to upload proof of certification so that my records are complete.

**Acceptance Criteria (Gherkin):**  
Given a certification  
When I upload a document  
Then it is stored and linked to my profile

**Additional Details:**  
- Priority: Medium  
- Story Points: 1  
- Dependencies: E07-1  
- Notes: Support PDF/JPG/PNG
