EPIC 16: MOBILE ACCESS (PWA)

===========================================

Title: Mobile Clock-In/Out (PWA)

User Story: As a warehouse worker I want to clock in and out using my mobile device So that I can accurately record my work hours even when offline

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Clock-in/out online
Given I am logged into the mobile app and have an active internet connection
When I tap the clock-in or clock-out button
Then my event is recorded and reflected in my schedule immediately

Scenario: Offline queue for clock events
Given I am logged into the mobile app and have no internet connection
When I tap the clock-in or clock-out button
Then my event is queued locally and submitted automatically when connectivity is restored, with conflict resolution if duplicate events exist

Additional Details:
- Priority: Medium
- Story Points: 5
- Dependencies: E04 (Scheduling), E05 (Authentication), E06 (Time Tracking), E12 (Notifications)
- Notes: Offline queue must handle edge cases such as overlapping events
