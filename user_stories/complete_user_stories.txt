Executive Summary

This document presents a comprehensive breakdown of 20 high-level epics for a Warehouse Employee Management System (EMS) into detailed, actionable user stories. Each epic has been decomposed into 2-5 user stories, ensuring clarity, testability, and alignment with agile best practices. Every user story includes a clear title, role-based narrative, Gherkin-formatted acceptance criteria, priority, story points, dependencies, and relevant notes. The stories are organized by epic and designed to facilitate efficient development, quality assurance, and stakeholder alignment.

---
