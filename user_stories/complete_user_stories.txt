Executive Summary

This document presents a comprehensive breakdown of 20 Warehouse Employee Management System (EMS) epics into actionable, testable user stories. Each epic has been decomposed into 2-5 detailed user stories, ensuring coverage of all relevant user roles (ADMIN, HR, SUPERVISOR, WORKER, System Administrator, Developer). Every user story includes a clear title, narrative, Gherkin-style acceptance criteria, and additional details such as priority, story points, dependencies, and notes. This format supports agile development teams in planning, implementation, and quality assurance. The stories are organized by epic for clarity and traceability.

---
