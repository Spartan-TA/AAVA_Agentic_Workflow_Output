Title: Shift & Schedule Management

User Story: As a supervisor I want to create and manage shift templates, rotations, and assignments so that warehouse staffing is adequate and scheduling conflicts are minimized.

Acceptance Criteria:

Scenario: Create shift template
Given I am a supervisor
When I create a new shift template
Then it is available for assignment to employees

Scenario: Assign shifts
Given available employees and shift templates
When I bulk-assign shifts
Then assignments are created and conflicts are detected/prevented

Scenario: View personal schedule
Given I am a worker
When I view my schedule
Then I see all upcoming assigned shifts

Scenario: Audit entries
Given scheduling changes
When assignments are created or modified
Then audit entries are generated

Additional Details:
- Priority: High
- Story Points: 8
- Dependencies: Employee Master Data (E02), Role-Based Access Control (E03), Time & Attendance (E04)
- Notes: Handle blackout dates and warehouse operation calendars.
