#### User Story 5: Update Employee Information
**As an** HR user  
**I want** to update employee details  
**so that** records remain current and accurate.

**Acceptance Criteria:**
- **Given** an existing employee,  
  **When** I PUT/PATCH to `/employees/{id}`,  
  **Then** the employee record is updated accordingly.

**Additional Details:**  
Priority: High  
Story Points: 2  
Dependencies: E02  
Notes: Support partial updates via PATCH.
