Title: Shift & Schedule Management

User Story: As a supervisor I want to create and manage shift templates, rotations, and assignments so that staffing is adequate and scheduling conflicts are minimized.

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Shift creation and conflict detection
Given shift templates and schedules are created
When assignments are made
Then conflicts are detected/prevented, workers see upcoming shifts, supervisors can bulk-assign, and audit entries are generated

Additional Details:
- Priority: High
- Story Points: 8
- Dependencies: Employee Master Data, RBAC, Time & Attendance
- Notes: Handle blackout dates and warehouse calendars
