### E05 - Shift & Schedule Management

**Title:** Create Shift Templates
**User Story:** As an HR user, I want to create recurring shift templates so that scheduling is efficient.
**Acceptance Criteria:**
Scenario: Shift template creation
Given I provide shift details
When I POST to `/shifts/templates`
Then a new template is created
**Additional Details:** Priority: High, Story Points: 3, Dependencies: E02, E03, E04, Notes: Support rotations and overtime rules.

**Title:** Assign Shifts to Employees
**User Story:** As a supervisor, I want to assign shifts in bulk so that staffing is managed efficiently.
**Acceptance Criteria:**
Scenario: Bulk assignment
Given a list of employees
When I assign a shift
Then all selected employees are scheduled and conflicts are checked
**Additional Details:** Priority: High, Story Points: 3, Dependencies: E02, E03, E04, Notes: Prevent double-booking.

**Title:** View Personal Shift Schedule
**User Story:** As a worker, I want to view my upcoming shifts so that I can plan accordingly.
**Acceptance Criteria:**
Scenario: View schedule
Given I am logged in
When I access my schedule
Then I see all upcoming assigned shifts
**Additional Details:** Priority: High, Story Points: 2, Dependencies: E02, E03, E04, Notes: Mobile-friendly view.
