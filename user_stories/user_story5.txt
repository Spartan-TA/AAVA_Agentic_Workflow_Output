Title: Shift & Schedule Management

User Story: As a supervisor I want to create and manage shift templates, rotations, and assignments so that staffing is adequate and scheduling conflicts are minimized.

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Shift Template and Assignment
Given the scheduling module is available
When I create or update shift templates, assign employees, and set blackout dates
Then conflicts are detected and prevented, workers see their upcoming shifts, supervisors can bulk-assign, and audit entries are generated for all changes.

Additional Details:
- Priority: High
- Story Points: 8
- Dependencies: Employee Master Data, Role Based Access Control, Time & Attendance
- Notes: Support recurring shifts, overtime rules, and warehouse operation calendars.
