Title: Shift & Schedule Management

User Story: As a supervisor I want to create and manage shift templates, rotations, and assignments So that warehouse staffing is optimized and scheduling conflicts are minimized

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Shift template creation
Given I have access to scheduling tools
When I create a new shift template
Then the template is saved and available for assignment

Scenario: Conflict detection
Given existing shift assignments
When I assign a new shift to an employee
Then the system checks for conflicts and prevents overlapping assignments

Scenario: Bulk assignment
Given multiple employees
When I select and assign shifts in bulk
Then all selected employees receive their shift assignments

Scenario: Audit entry generation
Given a shift assignment or change
When the action is performed
Then an audit entry is generated for tracking

Additional Details:
- Priority: High
- Story Points: 8
- Dependencies: Employee Master Data (E02), Role Based Access Control (E03), Time & Attendance (E04)
- Notes: Blackout dates and warehouse operation calendars must be supported; workers see personal upcoming shifts.
