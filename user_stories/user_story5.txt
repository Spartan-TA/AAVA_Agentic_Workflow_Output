Title: Shift & Schedule Management

User Story: As a supervisor I want to create and manage shift templates, rotations, and assignments so that staffing is adequate and scheduling conflicts are minimized.

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Shift scheduling and assignment
Given the scheduling interface
When I create or assign shifts
Then conflicts are detected and prevented
And workers see their upcoming shifts
And supervisors can bulk-assign shifts
And audit entries are generated for changes

Additional Details:
- Priority: High
- Story Points: 8
- Dependencies: Employee Master Data (CRUD), Role-Based Access Control (RBAC), Time & Attendance (Clock In/Out)
- Notes: Support recurring templates, overtime rules, blackout dates, and warehouse operation calendars.
