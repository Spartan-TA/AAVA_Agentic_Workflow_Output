EPIC E05 - Shift & Schedule Management

===========================================

User Story 1: CRUD for Shift Templates
---------------------------------------------
Title: Manage Shift Templates (CRUD)

User Story: As an HR user, I want to create, update, and delete shift templates so that recurring schedules are easy to manage.

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Shift template management
  Given the shift template endpoint
  When CRUD operations are performed
  Then templates are updated accordingly

Additional Details:
- Priority: High
- Story Points: 3
- Dependencies: E02, E03, E04
- Notes: Support rotations.

===========================================

User Story 2: Conflict Detection and Prevention
---------------------------------------------
Title: Detect and Prevent Scheduling Conflicts

User Story: As a scheduler, I want conflicts to be detected and prevented so that employees are not double-booked.

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Conflict detection
  Given a schedule assignment
  When a conflict exists
  Then the system blocks the assignment and shows an error

Additional Details:
- Priority: High
- Story Points: 3
- Dependencies: E02, E03, E04
- Notes: Include blackout dates.

===========================================

Additional Details:
