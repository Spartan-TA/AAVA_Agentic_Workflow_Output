---
Epic: E05 - Shift & Schedule Management

User Story 1: Shift Template CRUD
Title: Manage Shift Templates
User Story: As an HR manager, I want to create, update, and delete shift templates so that scheduling is consistent.
Acceptance Criteria (Gherkin):
Given access to shift templates,
When I perform CRUD operations,
Then templates are created, updated, or deleted.
Additional Details:
Priority: High
Story Points: 2
Dependencies: E02, E03, E04
Notes: Supports recurring shifts.

User Story 2: Shift Assignment
Title: Assign Shifts to Employees
User Story: As a supervisor, I want to assign shifts to employees so that staffing is planned.
Acceptance Criteria (Gherkin):
Given employees and shift templates exist,
When I assign shifts,
Then assignments are recorded and conflicts are detected.
Additional Details:
Priority: High
Story Points: 2
Dependencies: E02, E03, E04
Notes: Prevents overbooking.

User Story 3: Conflict Detection
Title: Prevent Scheduling Conflicts
User Story: As a scheduler, I want the system to detect and prevent shift conflicts so that employees are not double-booked.
Acceptance Criteria (Gherkin):
Given an employee has a shift,
When I assign another overlapping shift,
Then the system blocks the assignment.
Additional Details:
Priority: High
Story Points: 2
Dependencies: E02, E03, E04
Notes: Ensures compliance.

User Story 4: Bulk Assignment
Title: Bulk Assign Shifts
User Story: As a supervisor, I want to bulk-assign shifts to multiple employees so that scheduling is efficient.
Acceptance Criteria (Gherkin):
Given a list of employees,
When I bulk-assign a shift,
Then all assignments are created and audit entries generated.
Additional Details:
Priority: High
Story Points: 2
Dependencies: E02, E03, E04
Notes: Supports large teams.

User Story 5: Blackout Dates
Title: Manage Blackout Dates
User Story: As an operations manager, I want to set blackout dates so that shifts are not scheduled during warehouse closures.
Acceptance Criteria (Gherkin):
Given a blackout date is set,
When I schedule a shift,
Then the system prevents assignments on blackout dates.
Additional Details:
Priority: High
Story Points: 1
Dependencies: E02, E03, E04
Notes: Supports operational calendar.

User Story 6: Employee Shift View
Title: View Upcoming Shifts
User Story: As a worker, I want to view my upcoming shifts so that I can plan my schedule.
Acceptance Criteria (Gherkin):
Given I am logged in,
When I access my shift schedule,
Then my upcoming shifts are displayed.
Additional Details:
Priority: High
Story Points: 1
Dependencies: E02, E03, E04
Notes: Improves transparency.
