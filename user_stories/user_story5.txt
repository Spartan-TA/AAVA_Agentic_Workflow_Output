Title: Shift & Schedule Management

User Story: As a supervisor I want to create and manage recurring shift templates, rotations, and overtime rules so that employees are scheduled efficiently and conflicts are minimized.

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Create shift template
  Given I am a supervisor
  When I create a new shift template
  Then the template is saved and available for assignment

Scenario: Assign shifts
  Given shift templates exist
  When I assign shifts to employees (individually or in bulk)
  Then assignments are created and visible to workers

Scenario: Detect scheduling conflicts
  Given an employee is already scheduled
  When I attempt to assign an overlapping shift
  Then the system prevents the conflict and notifies me

Scenario: View personal schedule
  Given I am a worker
  When I view my upcoming shifts
  Then I see my assigned schedule

Scenario: Audit trail
  Given shift assignments are changed
  When I view the audit log
  Then all changes are recorded with actor and timestamp

Additional Details:
- Priority: High
- Story Points: 8
- Dependencies: Employee Master Data (E02), Role Based Access Control (E03), Time & Attendance (E04)
- Notes: Support blackout dates, warehouse operation calendars, and bulk assignment.
