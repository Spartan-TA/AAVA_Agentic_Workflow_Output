Title: Create Shift Templates
User Story: As an HR manager I want to create recurring shift templates so that scheduling is consistent and efficient.
Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Shift Template Creation
Given I have HR manager access
When I submit a new shift template
Then the template is saved and available for assignment
Additional Details:
- Priority: High
- Story Points: 3
- Dependencies: Employee Master Data, Role-Based Access Control, Time & Attendance
- Notes: Support for recurring and custom templates.

Title: Assign Shifts to Employees
User Story: As a supervisor I want to assign shifts to employees so that staffing levels meet operational needs.
Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Bulk Shift Assignment
Given a list of employees and available shifts
When I perform a bulk assignment
Then employees are scheduled and notified
Additional Details:
- Priority: High
- Story Points: 5
- Dependencies: Create Shift Templates
- Notes: Audit entries generated for assignments.

Title: Detect and Prevent Scheduling Conflicts
User Story: As a scheduler I want the system to detect and prevent scheduling conflicts so that employees are not double-booked or assigned during blackout dates.
Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Conflict Detection
Given existing schedules and blackout dates
When a new shift is assigned
Then conflicts are detected and prevented
Additional Details:
- Priority: High
- Story Points: 5
- Dependencies: Assign Shifts to Employees
- Notes: Include warehouse operation calendar checks.

Title: View Personal Upcoming Shifts
User Story: As a warehouse worker I want to view my upcoming shifts so that I can plan my work schedule.
Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Personal Shift View
Given I am logged in as a worker
When I access my schedule
Then my upcoming shifts are displayed
Additional Details:
- Priority: High
- Story Points: 2
- Dependencies: Assign Shifts to Employees
- Notes: Role-based visibility for workers.
