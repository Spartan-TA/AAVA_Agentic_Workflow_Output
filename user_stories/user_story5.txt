Title: Shift & Schedule Management

User Story: As a supervisor I want to create and assign shift templates and schedules So that I can ensure adequate staffing and prevent scheduling conflicts

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Shift assignment
Given I am authenticated as supervisor
When I create or assign shifts
Then conflicts are detected and prevented, workers see personal upcoming shifts, and audit entries are generated

Additional Details:
- Priority: High
- Story Points: 5
- Dependencies: Employee Master Data (E02), RBAC (E03), Time & Attendance (E04)
- Notes: Support recurring templates, rotations, overtime rules, blackout dates, and bulk assignment.
