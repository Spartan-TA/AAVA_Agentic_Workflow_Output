Title: Shift Management - Create and Assign Shift Templates
User Story: As an operations manager, I want to create recurring shift templates and assign them to employees so that scheduling is efficient.
Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Create shift template
  Given I have shift details
  When I POST to /shifts/templates
  Then a new template is created

Scenario: Assign shift
  Given a template exists
  When I assign it to employees
  Then their schedules are updated

Additional Details:
Priority: High
Story Points: 3
Dependencies: E02, E03, E04
Notes: Reduces manual scheduling
