Title: Shift & Schedule Management

User Story: As a supervisor I want to create and manage shift templates, rotations, and assignments so that warehouse staffing is adequate and scheduling conflicts are minimized.

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Shift template and schedule management
Given I am a supervisor
When I create, update, or assign shifts and schedules
Then CRUD operations are available for shift templates and schedules, conflicts are detected and prevented, workers see their upcoming shifts, supervisors can bulk-assign, and audit entries are generated for changes.

Additional Details:
- Priority: High
- Story Points: 8
- Dependencies: Employee Master Data (E02), Role-Based Access Control (E03), Time & Attendance (E04)
- Notes: Include blackout dates and warehouse operation calendars in scheduling logic.
