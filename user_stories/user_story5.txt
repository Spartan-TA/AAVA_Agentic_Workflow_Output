Title: Manage Shifts and Schedules

User Story: As a supervisor I want to create and assign recurring shift templates and manage employee schedules So that staffing is adequate and scheduling conflicts are minimized

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Shift and schedule management
Given I am a supervisor with access to scheduling tools
When I create or assign shifts and schedules
Then conflicts are detected and prevented, workers see their upcoming shifts, supervisors can bulk-assign, and audit entries are generated

Additional Details:
- Priority: High
- Story Points: 8
- Dependencies: Employee Master Data, Role-Based Access Control, Time & Attendance
- Notes: CRUD for shift templates and schedules; handle blackout dates and warehouse operation calendars.
