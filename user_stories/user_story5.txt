Title: Shift & Schedule Management

User Story: As a supervisor I want to create and manage shift templates, rotations, and assignments so that warehouse staffing is optimized and scheduling conflicts are minimized.

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Create shift template
Given I have access to scheduling tools
When I create a new shift template
Then the template is saved and available for assignment

Scenario: Assign shifts to employees
Given a shift template exists
When I bulk-assign employees to shifts
Then assignments are created and conflicts are detected/prevented

Scenario: View personal shifts
Given I am a worker
When I access my schedule
Then I see my upcoming shifts and blackout dates

Scenario: Audit shift changes
Given shift assignments are modified
When I review the audit log
Then all changes are tracked with timestamps and actors

Additional Details:
- Priority: High
- Story Points: 8
- Dependencies: Employee Master Data (CRUD), Role Based Access Control (RBAC), Time & Attendance (Clock In/Out)
- Notes: Blackout dates and warehouse operation calendars must be supported.
