Title: Shift Management - Create Shift Templates

User Story: As an HR user I want to create recurring shift templates so that scheduling is efficient.

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Create shift template
Given I am authenticated as HR
When I POST to /shifts/templates with valid data
Then a new shift template is created

Additional Details:
- Priority: High
- Story Points: 3
- Dependencies: E02, E03, E04
- Notes: Supports rotations and overtime rules.

---

Title: Shift Management - Assign Shifts

User Story: As a supervisor I want to assign shifts to employees so that staffing is planned.

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Assign shift to employee
Given a shift template exists
When I assign it to an employee
Then the employee sees the upcoming shift in their schedule

Scenario: Bulk-assign shifts
Given multiple employees
When I bulk-assign a shift
Then all selected employees receive the assignment

Additional Details:
- Priority: High
- Story Points: 3
- Dependencies: E02, E03, E04
- Notes: Conflict detection required.

---

Title: Shift Management - Prevent Scheduling Conflicts

User Story: As a scheduler I want to prevent overlapping or conflicting shifts so that employees are not double-booked.

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Detect scheduling conflict
Given an employee has a shift assigned
When I try to assign an overlapping shift
Then the system prevents the assignment and shows an error

Additional Details:
- Priority: High
- Story Points: 2
- Dependencies: E02, E03, E04
- Notes: Blackout dates and operation calendars respected.

---

Title: Shift Management - Audit Shift Assignments

User Story: As an auditor I want all shift assignments and changes to be logged so that I can review scheduling history.

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Audit entry for shift assignment
Given a shift is assigned or changed
When the action occurs
Then an audit entry is generated with actor, timestamp, and details

Additional Details:
- Priority: High
- Story Points: 2
- Dependencies: E02, E03, E04
- Notes: Required for compliance.
