Title: Shift & Schedule Management

User Story: As a supervisor I want to create and manage recurring shift templates, rotations, and overtime rules so that I can ensure adequate staffing and reduce scheduling conflicts.

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: CRUD for shift templates and schedules
  Given I have supervisor access
  When I create, update, or delete shift templates or schedules
  Then the changes should be saved and reflected in the system

Scenario: Conflict detection
  Given I am assigning shifts
  When a conflict is detected (e.g., double booking)
  Then the system should prevent the assignment and display an error

Scenario: Worker visibility
  Given I am a worker
  When I view my schedule
  Then I should see all my upcoming shifts

Scenario: Bulk assignment and audit
  Given I am a supervisor
  When I bulk-assign shifts
  Then the assignments are made and audit entries are generated

Additional Details:
- Priority: High
- Story Points: 8
- Dependencies: Employee Master Data (E02), Role Based Access Control (E03), Time & Attendance (E04)
- Notes: Support blackout dates and warehouse operation calendars.
