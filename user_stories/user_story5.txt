Title: Shift & Schedule Management

User Story: As a supervisor I want to create and assign recurring shift templates so that staffing is planned and conflicts are minimized.

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Shift assignment
Given I am a supervisor
When I assign shifts to employees
Then conflicts are detected/prevented, workers see their schedules, and audit entries are generated

Additional Details:
- Priority: High
- Story Points: 5
- Dependencies: Employee Master Data, RBAC, Time & Attendance
- Notes: Support blackout dates, bulk assignment, and operation calendars
