Title: Shift & Schedule Management

User Story: As a supervisor I want to create and manage shift templates, rotations, and assignments so that staffing is adequate and scheduling conflicts are minimized.

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Managing shifts and schedules
Given I have access to the scheduling module
When I create, update, or assign shifts
Then conflicts are detected and prevented, workers see their upcoming shifts, supervisors can bulk-assign, and audit entries are generated

Additional Details:
- Priority: High
- Story Points: 8
- Dependencies: Employee Master Data (E02), Role Based Access Control (E03), Time & Attendance (E04)
- Notes: Support recurring templates, overtime rules, blackout dates, and warehouse operation calendars.
