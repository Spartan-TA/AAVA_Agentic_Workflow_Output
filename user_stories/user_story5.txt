Title: Manage Shift & Schedule Templates and Assignments

User Story: As a supervisor I want to create recurring shift templates, manage rotations, set overtime rules, and assign shifts to employees so that warehouse staffing is adequate and scheduling conflicts are minimized.

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Create shift template
  Given I have shift details
  When I create a new shift template
  Then the template is saved and available for assignment

Scenario: Assign shifts to employees
  Given a shift template exists
  When I assign shifts to employees
  Then assignments are recorded and visible to workers

Scenario: Detect scheduling conflicts
  Given employees have assigned shifts
  When a new assignment overlaps with an existing one
  Then the system prevents the conflict and notifies the supervisor

Scenario: Bulk assignment
  Given a group of employees
  When I perform a bulk shift assignment
  Then all selected employees are assigned to the shift

Scenario: Audit entries
  Given shift assignments are made
  When I review audit logs
  Then all changes are recorded with actor and timestamp

Additional Details:
- Priority: High
- Story Points: 8
- Dependencies: Employee Master Data, Role-Based Access Control, Time & Attendance
- Notes: Workers should see their upcoming shifts; supervisors can bulk-assign; blackout dates and operation calendars must be supported.
