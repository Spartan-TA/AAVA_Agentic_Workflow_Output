Title: Shift & Schedule Management

User Story: As a supervisor I want to create and manage shift templates, rotations, and assignments So that staffing is adequate and scheduling conflicts are minimized

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Create Shift Template
Given I am a supervisor
When I create a new shift template
Then it is saved and available for assignment

Scenario: Assign Shifts
Given shift templates exist
When I assign shifts to employees
Then assignments are reflected in their schedules

Scenario: Conflict Detection
Given overlapping shifts
When I attempt to assign conflicting shifts
Then the system prevents the assignment and notifies me

Scenario: Audit Entries
Given shift changes
When I view the audit log
Then I see entries for all assignments and modifications

Additional Details:
- Priority: High
- Story Points: 8
- Dependencies: Employee Master Data (E02), Role-Based Access Control (E03), Time & Attendance (E04)
- Notes: Support bulk-assign and personal shift visibility for workers.
