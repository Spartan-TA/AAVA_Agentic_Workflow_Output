Title: Shift & Schedule Management

User Story: As a supervisor I want to create and assign recurring shift templates and schedules So that staffing is adequate and scheduling conflicts are minimized

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Assigning a shift template
Given a valid shift template and employee
When I assign the template to the employee
Then the employee sees their upcoming shifts and conflicts are detected/prevented

Additional Details:
- Priority: High
- Story Points: 8
- Dependencies: Employee Master Data, RBAC, Time & Attendance
- Notes: Bulk assignment supported; blackout dates and operation calendars handled; audit entries generated
