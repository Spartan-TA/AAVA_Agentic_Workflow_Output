Title: Shift & Schedule Management

User Story: As a supervisor I want to create and manage shift templates, rotations, and assignments so that warehouse staffing is adequate and scheduling conflicts are minimized.

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Create shift template
  Given I am a supervisor
  When I submit a new shift template
  Then the template is saved and available for assignment

Scenario: Assign shifts to employees
  Given a set of employees and shift templates
  When I bulk-assign shifts
  Then assignments are created and conflicts are detected/prevented

Scenario: View personal schedule
  Given I am a worker
  When I log in
  Then I can view my upcoming shifts

Scenario: Audit trail
  Given shift assignments are made
  When I review the audit log
  Then all changes are recorded with actor and timestamp

Additional Details:
- Priority: High
- Story Points: 8
- Dependencies: Employee Master Data (E02), RBAC (E03), Time & Attendance (E04)
- Notes: Support recurring shifts, blackout dates, and operation calendars.
