Title: Shift & Schedule Management
User Story: As a supervisor I want to create and manage shift templates, rotations, and assignments so that warehouse staffing is adequate and scheduling conflicts are minimized.
Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Manage shifts and schedules
Given I am a supervisor
When I create, update, or assign shifts
Then conflicts are detected and prevented, workers see personal upcoming shifts, and bulk-assignments are supported
And audit entries are generated for all changes
Additional Details:
- Priority: High
- Story Points: 8
- Dependencies: Employee Master Data (CRUD), Role Based Access Control (RBAC), Time & Attendance (Clock In/Out)
- Notes: Handle blackout dates and operation calendars.
