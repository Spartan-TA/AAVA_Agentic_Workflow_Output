Title: Manage Shift Templates and Schedules

User Story:
As a supervisor, I want to create and assign recurring shift templates, rotations, and overtime rules to employees so that scheduling is efficient and conflicts are minimized.

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario 1: Create shift template
  Given I am a supervisor
  When I POST a new shift template
  Then the template is saved and available for assignment

Scenario 2: Assign shifts
  Given shift templates exist
  When I assign shifts to employees
  Then assignments are created and visible to workers

Scenario 3: Detect conflicts
  Given overlapping shifts are assigned
  When I attempt to save the schedule
  Then the system prevents the conflict and displays an error

Scenario 4: Bulk assignment
  Given multiple employees
  When I bulk-assign shifts
  Then all assignments are processed and audit entries are generated

Additional Details:
Priority: High
Story Points: 13
Dependencies: E02, E03, E04
Notes: Blackout dates and operation calendars must be supported.
