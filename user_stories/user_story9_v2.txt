Title: Retrieve Employees with Pagination and Filtering

User Story: As an HR administrator I want to retrieve employee records with pagination and filtering So that I can efficiently manage and view large employee datasets

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Retrieve paginated and filtered employees Given multiple employee records exist When I send a GET request to /employees with pagination and filter parameters Then I receive a paginated, filtered list of employees matching the criteria

Additional Details:
- Priority: High
- Story Points: 2
- Dependencies: Project Scaffolding (E01)
- Notes: Support filtering by department, role, status, and shiftGroup
