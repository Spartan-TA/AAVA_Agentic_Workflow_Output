Title: Clock In/Out Endpoint
User Story: As a worker I want to clock in and out via a mobile or web interface So that my attendance is accurately recorded
Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Successful clock in
  Given I am at the warehouse location
  When I clock in
  Then my clock-in time and location are recorded
Scenario: Geofence enforcement
  Given I am outside the warehouse geofence
  When I attempt to clock in
  Then the system should deny the clock-in
Scenario: Missed punch handling
  Given I missed a clock out
  When I attempt to clock in the next day
  Then the system should prompt for correction
Additional Details:
- Priority: High
- Story Points: 5
- Dependencies: Employee CRUD, RBAC
- Notes: Integrate with geolocation services

Title: Calculate Hours Worked
User Story: As a supervisor I want the system to calculate total hours worked per employee So that payroll and compliance are accurate
Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Regular hours calculation
  Given clock in and out times exist
  When I view attendance records
  Then total hours worked should be displayed
Scenario: Overtime calculation
  Given hours exceed standard shift
  When I view the report
  Then overtime hours should be calculated
Additional Details:
- Priority: High
- Story Points: 3
- Dependencies: Clock In/Out
- Notes: Handle overnight shifts
