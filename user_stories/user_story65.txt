Title: Opt-In/Out Per Notification Channel and Localized Templates

User Story: As an employee I want to manage my notification preferences per channel and receive messages in my preferred language So that I have control over how and when I am notified

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Channel preference and localization
Given I access my notification settings
When I update my channel preferences and language
Then notifications are sent only via selected channels and in my chosen language

Additional Details:
- Priority: Medium
- Story Points: 3
- Dependencies: E03, E05, E06, E07
- Notes: Support English and Spanish templates; preferences editable at any time.
