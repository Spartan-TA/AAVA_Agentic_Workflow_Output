Title: Retrieve Employee Records

User Story: As an HR user I want to retrieve employee records with filtering and pagination So that I can efficiently manage large employee datasets

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Employee record retrieval
Given I am authenticated as HR
When I request employee records with filters and pagination
Then I receive a paginated and filtered list of employee DTOs

Additional Details:
- Priority: High
- Story Points: 3
- Dependencies: Employee domain
- Notes: Support filtering by department, role, status, and shiftGroup
