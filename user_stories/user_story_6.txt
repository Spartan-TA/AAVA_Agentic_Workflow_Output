Title: Implement Leave & Absence Management

User Story: As an Employee I want to request paid time off, sick leave, or unpaid leave so that my absences are properly tracked and compliant with company policy.

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Request leave
  Given I have available leave balance
  When I submit a leave request
  Then my supervisor receives the request for approval
Scenario: Approve/deny leave
  Given a leave request is pending
  When my supervisor reviews and approves/denies
  Then my leave balance updates and scheduled shifts are flagged for coverage
Scenario: Leave balance update
  Given a leave is approved
  When the leave period starts
  Then my accrual balance is updated accordingly
Scenario: Export approved leaves
  Given approved leave records exist
  When I request an export
  Then I receive a report showing all approved leaves

Additional Details:
- Priority: Medium
- Story Points: 5
- Dependencies: Employee CRUD (E02), RBAC (E03), Shift & Schedule (E05)
- Notes: Integration hooks to exclude from scheduling and payroll hours.
