Title: Soft Delete Employee Record

User Story: As an HR user I want to soft-delete employee records So that terminated employees are not shown in active lists but their history is retained

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Soft-deleting an employee
Given I am viewing an employee's details
When I choose to delete the record
Then the employee is marked as inactive and excluded from active queries

Additional Details:
- Priority: High
- Story Points: 2
- Dependencies: Employee CRUD (E02)
- Notes: Soft-deleted employees can be restored by ADMIN
