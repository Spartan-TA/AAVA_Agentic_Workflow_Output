Title: Leave Request Submission
User Story: As an employee I want to submit PTO or sick leave requests So that my absences are tracked and approved
Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Leave request
Given I am logged in
When I submit a leave request
Then my supervisor should be notified and able to approve or reject
Additional Details:
- Priority: Medium
- Story Points: 2
- Dependencies: Employee domain, RBAC, Shift management
- Notes: Integrate with scheduling to prevent conflicts

Title: Leave Accrual Tracking
User Story: As an HR manager I want to track leave accrual balances So that employees know their available PTO
Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Accrual tracking
Given leave policies are defined
When employees request leave
Then their accrual balances should be updated accordingly
Additional Details:
- Priority: Medium
- Story Points: 2
- Dependencies: Leave request, Shift management
- Notes: Display balances in employee profile
