Title: Assign Equipment to Employee

User Story: As an asset manager I want to assign equipment to employees So that asset responsibility and tracking is maintained

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Assigning equipment
Given I am on the asset management page
When I select equipment and assign it to an employee
Then the assignment should be recorded and visible in the employee profile

Additional Details:
- Priority: High
- Story Points: 2
- Dependencies: Employee list, asset inventory
- Notes: Support bulk assignment for multiple assets.
