Title: Record Safety Incidents and Near-Misses

User Story:
As a Safety Officer, I want to record safety incidents and near-misses in the system so that we can track, investigate, and prevent future occurrences.

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario 1: Submit a new incident report
  Given I am on the safety incident reporting page
  When I submit a new incident or near-miss
  Then the report is logged and assigned a unique ID

Scenario 2: Attach photos and documents to report
  Given I am submitting an incident report
  When I upload supporting photos or documents
  Then they are securely stored and linked to the report

Scenario 3: Notify relevant parties
  Given a new incident is reported
  When the report is submitted
  Then safety and HR teams are notified for follow-up

Priority: High
Story Points: 8
Dependencies: E02, E03
Notes: Must support mobile submission and secure document storage.
