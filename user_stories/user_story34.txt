Title: Assign Equipment to Employee

User Story:
As an HR administrator, I want to assign equipment and assets to employees so that each employee has the necessary tools for their role and asset tracking is maintained.

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario 1: Assign equipment to employee
  Given an employee profile exists
  When I select equipment from the available assets and assign it to the employee
  Then the equipment is listed under the employee's assigned assets

Scenario 2: Prevent duplicate assignment
  Given an asset is already assigned to an employee
  When I attempt to assign the same asset to another employee
  Then the system displays an error and prevents duplicate assignment

Additional Details:
Priority: High
Story Points: 3
Dependencies: Employee profile management, asset inventory
Notes: Must integrate with asset tracking system.
