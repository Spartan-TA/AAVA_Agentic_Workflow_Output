Title: View Personal Upcoming Shifts

User Story: As a warehouse worker I want to view my upcoming shifts So that I can plan my work and personal time effectively

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Worker views upcoming shifts
Given I am logged into my account
When I navigate to the schedule section
Then I see a list of my assigned upcoming shifts with dates, times, and locations

Additional Details:
- Priority: High
- Story Points: 3
- Dependencies: E02, E03, E04
- Notes: Include shift details and highlight any changes or cancellations.
