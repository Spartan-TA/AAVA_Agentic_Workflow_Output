Title: Export Approved Leaves for Reporting and Payroll

User Story: As a payroll administrator I want to export approved leaves So that I can reconcile payroll and provide compliance reports

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Exporting approved leave data
Given there are approved leave requests in the system
When I generate a leave export
Then the export should include employee details, leave type, dates, and approval status in CSV format

Additional Details:
- Priority: Medium
- Story Points: 3
- Dependencies: Leave Request, Payroll Export Integration
- Notes: Export should support filters by date range, department, and leave type.
