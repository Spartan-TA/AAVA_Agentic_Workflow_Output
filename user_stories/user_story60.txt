Title: Onboarding Tasks for Training and Asset Assignment

User Story: As a supervisor I want onboarding tasks to be generated for training and asset assignment So that new hires are fully prepared and equipped

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Onboarding task generation
Given a new hire appears in the system
When onboarding is triggered
Then tasks for required training and asset assignment are created and tracked

Additional Details:
- Priority: Medium
- Story Points: 5
- Dependencies: E02, E03, E05, E07, E09, E13
- Notes: Task completion must be auditable
