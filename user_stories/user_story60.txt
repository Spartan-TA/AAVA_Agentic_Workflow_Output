Title: Alert on Compliance Violations

User Story:
As a compliance officer, I want the system to alert me when potential compliance violations are detected in the audit trail so that I can take immediate action to resolve issues.

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario 1: Detect compliance violation
  Given audit logs are being monitored
  When a suspicious or unauthorized action is detected
  Then the system generates an alert for compliance officers

Scenario 2: View violation details
  Given an alert is received
  When I access the alert
  Then I see details of the violation, affected records, and recommended actions

Additional Details:
Priority: High
Story Points: 3
Dependencies: Audit trail, notification system
Notes: Alerts should be logged and tracked for resolution.
