Title: Support Employee Soft-Delete

User Story: As an HR Manager I want to soft-delete employee records So that terminated employees are excluded from active queries but historical data is retained for compliance

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Soft-delete operation
  Given an employee exists
  When I DELETE /employees/{id}
  Then the employee is marked as inactive and excluded from active lists
Scenario: Historical data retention
  Given an employee is soft-deleted
  When I query for historical records
  Then the employee data is available for audit and reporting

Additional Details:
- Priority: High
- Story Points: 3
- Dependencies: E01, E02_1
- Notes: Soft-deleted employees must not appear in active scheduling or payroll exports
