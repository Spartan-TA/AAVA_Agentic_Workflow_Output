Title: List and Filter Employees

User Story: As an HR manager I want to list and filter employees So that I can quickly find relevant personnel based on criteria

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Employee list and filter
Given I am on the employee list page
When I apply filters (e.g., department, status)
Then the displayed list should update to match the criteria

Additional Details:
- Priority: Medium
- Story Points: 2
- Dependencies: Employee records
- Notes: Support pagination
