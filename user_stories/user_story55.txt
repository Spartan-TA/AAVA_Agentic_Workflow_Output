Title: Location-Based Time Zone and Holiday Calendars

User Story: As a scheduler I want to assign time zones and holiday calendars to each warehouse location so that schedules and leave calculations are accurate

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Assigning time zone and holiday calendar
Given I am logged in as a scheduler
When I configure a warehouse location
Then I can select the appropriate time zone and holiday calendar for that site

Additional Details:
- Priority: Medium
- Story Points: 3
- Dependencies: Localization module, schedule management
- Notes: Support for regional and country-specific holidays.
