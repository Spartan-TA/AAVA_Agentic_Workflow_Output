### E04: Time & Attendance (Clock In/Out)

#### User Story 1
Title: Employee Clock-In Endpoint
User Story: As a worker, I want to clock in at the start of my shift so that my attendance is recorded.
Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Clock-in event
  Given I am authenticated
  When I POST to /attendance/clock-in with my badgeId
  Then my clock-in time is recorded and associated with my shift
Additional Details:
Priority: High
Story Points: 2
Dependencies: E02, E03
Notes: Optionally capture device and geofence.

#### User Story 2
Title: Employee Clock-Out Endpoint
User Story: As a worker, I want to clock out at the end of my shift so that my worked hours are calculated.
Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Clock-out event
  Given I am clocked in
  When I POST to /attendance/clock-out
  Then my clock-out time is recorded and hours are computed
Additional Details:
Priority: High
Story Points: 2
Dependencies: E02, E03
Notes: Validate shift association.

#### User Story 3
Title: Missed Punch Correction Workflow
User Story: As a supervisor, I want to approve corrections for missed punches so that attendance records are accurate.
Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Correction request
  Given an employee missed a punch
  When a correction is submitted
  Then an approval task is created for the supervisor
  And upon approval, the record is updated
Additional Details:
Priority: High
Story Points: 3
Dependencies: E02, E03
Notes: Track correction history.

#### User Story 4
Title: Attendance Reporting
User Story: As an HR user, I want to export attendance reports in CSV format so that I can process payroll.
Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Attendance export
  Given attendance data exists
  When I request a report export
  Then I receive a CSV file with daily totals
Additional Details:
Priority: High
Story Points: 2
Dependencies: E02, E03
Notes: Include filters for date range.
