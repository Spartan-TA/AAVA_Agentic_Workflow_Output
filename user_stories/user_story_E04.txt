EPIC E04: Time & Attendance (Clock In/Out)

===========================================

Title: Time & Attendance (Clock In/Out) - Clock-In Endpoint
User Story: As a worker I want to clock in for my shift So that my attendance is accurately recorded.
Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Successful clock-in
Given I am scheduled for a shift
When I POST to /attendance/clock-in with valid data
Then my clock-in event is recorded and associated with my shift

Additional Details:
- Priority: High
- Story Points: 2
- Dependencies: Employee API, RBAC
- Notes: Validate location/device if geofence enabled.

---

Title: Time & Attendance (Clock In/Out) - Clock-Out Endpoint
User Story: As a worker I want to clock out at the end of my shift So that my worked hours are tracked.
Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Successful clock-out
Given I am clocked in
When I POST to /attendance/clock-out
Then my clock-out event is recorded and hours are calculated

Additional Details:
- Priority: High
- Story Points: 2
- Dependencies: Clock-in endpoint
- Notes: Validate shift association.

---

Title: Time & Attendance (Clock In/Out) - Missed Punch Correction
User Story: As a worker I want to request corrections for missed punches So that my attendance records are accurate.
Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Correction request
Given I missed a clock-in/out
When I submit a correction request
Then an approval task is created for my supervisor

Additional Details:
- Priority: High
- Story Points: 3
- Dependencies: Clock-in/out endpoints
- Notes: Track correction workflow.

Title: Time & Attendance (Clock In/Out) - Automatic Shift Association
User Story: As a worker I want my clock-in/out events to be automatically associated with my scheduled shift So that payroll and compliance are accurate.
Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Shift association
Given I clock in/out
When the event is processed
Then it is linked to my scheduled shift

Additional Details:
- Priority: High
- Story Points: 2
- Dependencies: Shift management
- Notes: Handle overlapping shifts.
