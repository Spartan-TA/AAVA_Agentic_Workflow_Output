User Story 1: Time & Attendance - Clock In
===========================================

Title: Time & Attendance - Clock In

User Story: As a worker, I want to clock in at the start of my shift (optionally with geofence/device capture) so that my attendance is accurately recorded.

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Clock in
Given a scheduled shift
When I POST to /attendance/clock-in
Then my clock-in is recorded with timestamp and location/device info

Additional Details:
- Priority: High
- Story Points: 3
- Dependencies: Employee CRUD, RBAC
- Notes: Validate shift association.

User Story 2: Time & Attendance - Clock Out
===========================================

Title: Time & Attendance - Clock Out

User Story: As a worker, I want to clock out at the end of my shift so that my worked hours are calculated correctly.

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Clock out
Given an active shift
When I POST to /attendance/clock-out
Then my clock-out is recorded and daily totals are computed

Additional Details:
- Priority: High
- Story Points: 2
- Dependencies: Clock in
- Notes: Handle missed punches.
