EPIC E04: Time & Attendance (Clock In/Out)

===========================================

Title: Time & Attendance - Clock In
User Story: As a worker, I want to clock in with device and optional geofence so that my attendance is accurately recorded.
Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Clock In
Given I am scheduled for a shift
When I POST to /attendance/clock-in with valid data
Then my clock-in is recorded with device and location info
Additional Details:
- Priority: High
- Story Points: 3
- Dependencies: Employee CRUD, RBAC
- Notes: Geofence optional.

===========================================

Title: Time & Attendance - Clock Out
User Story: As a worker, I want to clock out so that my worked hours are tracked.
Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Clock Out
Given I am clocked in
When I POST to /attendance/clock-out
Then my clock-out is recorded and shift hours are calculated
Additional Details:
- Priority: High
- Story Points: 2
- Dependencies: Clock In
- Notes: Validates open clock-in.

===========================================

Title: Time & Attendance - Missed Punch Correction
User Story: As a worker, I want to request a correction for missed punches so that my attendance is accurate.
Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Missed Punch Correction
Given I missed a clock-in/out
When I submit a correction request
Then an approval task is created for my supervisor
Additional Details:
- Priority: High
- Story Points: 3
- Dependencies: Clock In/Out
- Notes: Supervisor approval required.

===========================================

Title: Time & Attendance - Attendance Reports
User Story: As a supervisor, I want to export attendance reports in CSV so that I can review and share attendance data.
Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Export Attendance
Given attendance data exists
When I request a CSV export
Then I receive a file with daily totals per employee
Additional Details:
- Priority: High
- Story Points: 2
- Dependencies: Attendance data
- Notes: Export includes corrections.

===========================================

Title: Time & Attendance - Automatic Shift Association
User Story: As a system, I want clock events to be automatically associated with scheduled shifts so that reporting is accurate.
Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Shift Association
Given a clock-in/out event
When it is recorded
Then it is linked to the correct scheduled shift
Additional Details:
- Priority: High
- Story Points: 2
- Dependencies: Shift Management
- Notes: Handles overlapping shifts.
