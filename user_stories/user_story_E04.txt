EPIC E04: Time & Attendance (Clock In/Out)

===========================================

Title: Time & Attendance - Clock In/Out
User Story: As a worker, I want to clock in and out so that my attendance is accurately recorded.
Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Clock in/out
Given I am scheduled
When I POST to /attendance/clock-in or /clock-out
Then my event is recorded with timestamp
Additional Details:
- Priority: High
- Story Points: 3
- Dependencies: Employee CRUD, RBAC
- Notes: Validate shift association.

---

Title: Time & Attendance - Geofence & Device Capture
User Story: As a supervisor, I want to capture device and location on clock events so that attendance is verified.
Acceptance Criteria:

Additional Details:
- Priority: Medium
