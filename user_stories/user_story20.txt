Title: Assign Required Training

User Story: As a training manager I want to assign required training modules to employees so that compliance and skill requirements are met

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Assigning training
Given I am logged in as a training manager
When I assign a training module to an employee or group
Then the assignment appears in their training dashboard and they are notified

Additional Details:
- Priority: High
- Story Points: 3
- Dependencies: Notification module, employee master data
- Notes: Support for both mandatory and optional training.
