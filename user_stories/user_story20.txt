Title: Leave Integration with Scheduling and Payroll

User Story: As a payroll manager I want leave requests and approvals to integrate with scheduling and payroll So that employee absences are reflected in shift assignments and pay calculations

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Leave integration
Given an approved leave request
When payroll and scheduling are processed
Then the employee's shifts and pay should be adjusted accordingly

Additional Details:
- Priority: High
- Story Points: 5
- Dependencies: Leave Approval and Balances Update
- Notes: Support retroactive adjustments and reporting.
