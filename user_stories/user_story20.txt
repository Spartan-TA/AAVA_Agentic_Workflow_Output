Title: Notify on Certification Expiry

User Story: As an HR manager I want to receive notifications when employee certifications are about to expire So that I can take timely action to renew them

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Certification expiry notification
  Given an employee's certification is nearing expiry
  When the expiry date is within the notification threshold
  Then the system should send a notification to the HR manager and the employee

Additional Details:
- Priority: High
- Story Points: 2
- Dependencies: Certification tracking, notification system
- Notes: Support configurable notification thresholds (e.g., 30 days before expiry).
