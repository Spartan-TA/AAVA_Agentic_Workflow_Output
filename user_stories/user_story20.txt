EPIC E20 - Document Management

===========================================

User Story 1: Upload/Download Employee Documents
---------------------------------------------
Title: Upload and Download Employee Documents

User Story: As an HR user, I want to upload and download employee documents so that compliance records are centralized.

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Document management
  Given employee record
  When document is uploaded/downloaded
  Then file is stored/retrieved securely

Additional Details:
- Priority: Low
- Story Points: 3
- Dependencies: E02, E03, E07
- Notes: Support contracts, I-9, certs.
