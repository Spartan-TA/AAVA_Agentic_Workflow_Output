Title: View and Download Pay Stubs
User Story: As an employee I want to view and download my pay stubs from the portal so that I can access my payment records anytime.
Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Pay stub access
Given I am logged in
When I navigate to the pay stubs section
Then I should see and be able to download my pay stubs
Additional Details:
- Priority: Low
- Story Points: 2
- Dependencies: E02, E03, E11
- Notes: Pay stubs available in PDF format
