Title: Review Submission and Acknowledgement
User Story: As a supervisor I want to submit and acknowledge performance reviews with employees So that feedback is documented and immutable after sign-off
Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Submit and sign-off review
Given a completed review form
When both supervisor and employee acknowledge
Then the review is locked and PDF exportable
Additional Details:
- Priority: Medium
- Story Points: 3
- Dependencies: E02, E03
- Notes: Role-based visibility
