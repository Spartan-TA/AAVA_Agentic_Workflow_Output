Title: Admin Sends Announcement

User Story: As an admin I want to send company-wide announcements So that all employees are informed of important updates

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Sending announcement
Given I am logged in as an admin and have access to the announcements module
When I compose and send an announcement
Then all employees receive a notification and the message is archived for future reference

Additional Details:
- Priority: High
- Story Points: 2
- Dependencies: Notifications Module, Employee Master Data
- Notes: Support attachments and scheduling future announcements.
