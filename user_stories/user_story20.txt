Title: Notification Preferences & Delivery
User Story: As a user, I want to configure notification preferences and receive in-app/email/SMS alerts so that I stay informed.
Acceptance Criteria:
  Given notification channels,
  When I opt in/out,
  Then my preferences are saved.

  Given a shift change or expiring cert,
  When triggered,
  Then I receive a notification per my settings.

  Given quiet hours are set,
  When a notification is sent,
  Then it is deferred until allowed.

Additional Details:
  Priority: Medium
  Story Points: 5
  Dependencies: RBAC, Shift, Leave, Certification
  Notes: Delivery status tracked.
