Title: Leave Request and Approval Workflow
User Story: As a WORKER I want to request PTO, sick, or unpaid leave So that my supervisor can review and approve my absence
Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Submit leave request
Given I am logged in as a worker
When I submit a leave request with type and dates
Then my supervisor receives an approval task and my accrual balance updates upon approval
Additional Details:
- Priority: Medium
- Story Points: 5
- Dependencies: E02, E03, E05
- Notes: Leave requests must integrate with scheduling and payroll modules.
