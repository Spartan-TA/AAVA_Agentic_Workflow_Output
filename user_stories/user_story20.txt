Title: Reporting & Analytics
User Story: As a manager I want to view and export operational reports on attendance, overtime, and safety So that I can make data-driven decisions
Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Filter and export report
Given I select filters (date, department, shift)
When I generate a report
Then results are returned within 10 seconds and exportable as CSV/PDF

Additional Details:
- Priority: Medium
- Story Points: 8
- Dependencies: Attendance, Schedule Management, Leave Management, Certification Tracking, Safety Incidents, Audit Trail
- Notes: Role-based dashboard access; metrics endpoints for BI.
