Title: Assign Asset to Employee
User Story: As an asset manager I want to assign assets to employees So that equipment usage is tracked and managed
Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Asset assignment
Given I am logged in as asset manager
When I select an asset and assign it to an employee
Then the asset record should reflect the assignment and the employee notified
Additional Details:
- Priority: Medium
- Story Points: 2
- Dependencies: Employee CRUD, Notifications
- Notes: Track assignment history for each asset.
