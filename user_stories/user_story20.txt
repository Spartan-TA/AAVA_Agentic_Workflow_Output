Title: Block Unqualified Assignments and Upload Proof Documents

User Story: As a supervisor I want the system to block assignment of tasks to employees with expired certifications and allow uploading of proof documents so that only qualified staff operate equipment and compliance is documented

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Block assignment
Given an employee's certification is expired
When I attempt to assign them to a task requiring certification
Then the system prevents the assignment and notifies me

Scenario: Upload proof document
Given a certification record exists
When I upload a proof document
Then the document is stored and linked to the certification

Additional Details:
- Priority: High
- Story Points: 2
- Dependencies: Certification CRUD, Employee Master Data
- Notes: Scheduling checks must enforce qualification; document uploads must be auditable.
