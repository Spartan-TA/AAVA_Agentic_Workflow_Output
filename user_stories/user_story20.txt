Title: Shift Conflict Detection and Prevention

User Story: As a scheduler I want the system to detect and prevent scheduling conflicts so that employees are not double-booked or assigned during blackout dates

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Scheduling conflict
Given an employee is already assigned to a shift or blackout date
When I attempt to assign another overlapping shift
Then the system blocks the assignment and displays an error

Additional Details:
- Priority: High
- Story Points: 2
- Dependencies: E02, E03, E04
- Notes: Include audit entry for blocked assignments; support warehouse operation calendar.
