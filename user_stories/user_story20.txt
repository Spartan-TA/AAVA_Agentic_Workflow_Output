Title: Notifications and Announcements

User Story: As a user, I want to receive in-app and email/SMS notifications for shift changes, expiring certifications, approvals, and announcements so that I am informed in real time.

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Notification delivery
Given I have opted in to a notification channel
When a relevant event occurs
Then I receive a notification via my chosen channel
And I can opt out at any time

Additional Details:
- Priority: Medium
- Story Points: 5
- Dependencies: RBAC, Shift Management, Leave Management, Certification Tracking
- Notes: Support quiet hours and localized templates.
