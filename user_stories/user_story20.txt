Title: Submit Leave Request

User Story: As an employee I want to submit a leave request for PTO, sick, or unpaid leave So that I can formally notify my supervisor and update my schedule

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Leave request submission
Given I am logged into my account
When I fill out and submit a leave request form
Then the request should be sent to my supervisor for approval and my accrual balance updated

Additional Details:
- Priority: Medium
- Story Points: 2
- Dependencies: Employee Master Data, Shift & Schedule Management
- Notes: Form should validate leave type and dates.
