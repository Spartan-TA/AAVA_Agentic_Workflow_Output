Title: Mobile Clock In/Out
User Story: As an employee I want to clock in and out using my mobile device so that attendance is convenient
Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Mobile clock in
  Given I am logged in on my mobile device
  When I tap 'Clock In'
  Then my attendance is recorded
Scenario: Offline clock in
  Given I have no internet connection
  When I clock in
  Then my attendance is stored locally and synced when online
Additional Details:
- Priority: High
- Story Points: 3
- Dependencies: Time & Attendance
- Notes: Support offline mode

Title: Mobile Shift View

  Then I see my upcoming shifts
Additional Details:
- Priority: Medium
- Story Points: 2
- Dependencies: Shift & Schedule Management

Title: Push Notifications on Mobile
User Story: As an employee I want to receive push notifications on my mobile device so that I am alerted to important updates
Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Receive push notification
  Given a key event occurs (e.g., shift change)
  When I am logged in on mobile
  Then I receive a push notification
Additional Details:
- Priority: High
- Story Points: 2
- Dependencies: Notifications & Announcements
- Notes: Support iOS and Android
