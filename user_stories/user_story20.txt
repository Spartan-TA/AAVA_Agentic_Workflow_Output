Title: Employee Views Schedule

User Story: As an EMPLOYEE I want to view my assigned shifts So that I can plan my work and personal time

Acceptance Criteria:

Scenario: Employee views schedule
Given I am logged in as EMPLOYEE
When I navigate to my schedule page
Then I see all upcoming assigned shifts

Scenario: Employee receives schedule update
Given my schedule changes
When the update is made
Then I receive a notification of the change

Additional Details:
- Priority: Medium
- Story Points: 3
- Dependencies: E05-2
- Notes: Schedule should be accessible via mobile and desktop.
