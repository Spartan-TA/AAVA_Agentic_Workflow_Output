Title: Training & Certification Tracking - Block Unqualified Assignments

User Story: As a scheduler, I want to prevent assignment of tasks to employees with expired or missing certifications so that only qualified staff are assigned.

Acceptance Criteria:

Scenario: Block assignment to unqualified employee
Given an employee's certification is expired
When I attempt to assign them to a task requiring that certification
Then the assignment is blocked with an error

Additional Details:
- Priority: High
- Story Points: 5
- Dependencies: Certification CRUD, Scheduling
- Notes: Applies to equipment-sensitive roles.
