Title: In-App and Email/SMS Notifications for Shift Changes

User Story: As a worker I want to receive notifications via app, email, or SMS when my shift changes so that I am always informed and can adjust my schedule accordingly.

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Shift change notification
Given my shift assignment is updated
When the change is approved
Then I should receive a notification through my selected channels

Additional Details:
- Priority: Medium
- Story Points: 3
- Dependencies: RBAC (E03), Shift & Schedule Management (E05)
- Notes: Users must be able to opt-in/out per channel.
