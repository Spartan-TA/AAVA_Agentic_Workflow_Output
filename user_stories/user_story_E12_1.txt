Title: Shift Change Notifications

User Story: As a worker I want to receive in-app and SMS/email notifications for shift changes So that I am always aware of my updated schedule

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Notification for shift change
Given my shift is changed by a supervisor
When the change is approved
Then I receive a notification via my selected channels and can see the update in the app

Additional Details:
- Priority: Medium
- Story Points: 3
- Dependencies: E03, E05, E06, E07
- Notes: Users can opt-in/out per channel; templates must be localized.
