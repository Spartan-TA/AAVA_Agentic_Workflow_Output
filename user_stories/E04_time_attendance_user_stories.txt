EPIC E04: TIME & ATTENDANCE (CLOCK IN/OUT)

---
Title: Time & Attendance Clock-In
User Story: As a worker I want to clock in at the start of my shift So that my attendance is recorded accurately
Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Successful clock-in
Given I am scheduled for a shift
When I clock in via the endpoint
Then my attendance is recorded and associated with the shift

Additional Details:
- Priority: High
- Story Points: 3
- Dependencies: Employee CRUD, RBAC
- Notes: Validate geofence and device capture

---
Title: Time & Attendance Clock-Out
User Story: As a worker I want to clock out at the end of my shift So that my worked hours are calculated
Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Successful clock-out
Given I am clocked in
When I clock out via the endpoint
Then my shift hours are computed

Additional Details:
- Priority: High
- Story Points: 3
- Dependencies: Clock-in
- Notes: Handle missed punches

---
Title: Attendance Correction Workflow
User Story: As a supervisor I want to approve corrections for missed punches So that attendance records remain accurate
Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Correction approval
Given a correction request exists
When I approve or deny the request
Then the attendance record is updated accordingly

Additional Details:
- Priority: High
- Story Points: 4
- Dependencies: Time & Attendance
- Notes: Create approval tasks

---
Title: Attendance Reporting
User Story: As a payroll manager I want to export attendance reports So that payroll processing is accurate
Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Attendance report export
Given attendance data exists
When I request an export
Then a CSV file is generated with daily totals

Additional Details:
- Priority: High
- Story Points: 2
- Dependencies: Time & Attendance
- Notes: Support export for payroll
