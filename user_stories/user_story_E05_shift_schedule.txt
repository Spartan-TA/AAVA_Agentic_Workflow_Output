Title: Create & Manage Shift Templates
User Story: As an HR admin, I want to create and edit recurring shift templates so that scheduling is efficient.
Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Shift template is created
Given I have access to shift management
When I create or edit a shift template
Then the template is saved and available for assignment
Additional Details:
- Priority: High
- Story Points: 3
- Dependencies: E02, E03
- Notes: Templates include start/end times, breaks, roles.

---

Title: Assign Shifts to Employees (Bulk & Individual)
User Story: As a supervisor, I want to assign shifts to employees individually or in bulk so that staffing is managed efficiently.
Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Shifts are assigned
Given I have a list of employees
When I assign a shift template
Then the assignment is visible in each employee's schedule
Additional Details:
- Priority: High
- Story Points: 5
- Dependencies: US05.1
- Notes: Bulk assignment supports CSV upload.

---

Title: Detect & Prevent Scheduling Conflicts
User Story: As a scheduler, I want the system to detect and prevent scheduling conflicts so that employees are not double-booked.
Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Scheduling conflict is detected
Given an employee has an existing shift
When I attempt to assign an overlapping shift
Then the system blocks the assignment and displays a conflict warning
Additional Details:
- Priority: High
- Story Points: 3
- Dependencies: US05.2
- Notes: Includes blackout dates and operation calendar.

---

Title: Overtime Rules & Calculation
User Story: As a payroll admin, I want overtime rules to be configurable and overtime hours calculated automatically.
Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Overtime is calculated
Given overtime rules are set
When an employee exceeds standard hours
Then overtime is calculated and flagged in reports
Additional Details:
- Priority: Medium
- Story Points: 3
- Dependencies: US05.1, E04
- Notes: Rules per department/role.

---

Title: Employee Shift View
User Story: As an employee, I want to view my upcoming shifts so that I can plan my work schedule.
Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Employee views shifts
Given I am logged in
When I access my schedule
Then I see all assigned shifts for the next 30 days
Additional Details:
- Priority: Medium
- Story Points: 2
- Dependencies: US05.2
- Notes: Mobile and web views.
