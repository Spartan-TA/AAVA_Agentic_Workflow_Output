User Story: As an HR manager I want to create, update, and delete shift templates and schedules so that warehouse operations are efficiently planned

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Creating a new shift template
Given I am an HR manager
When I submit a new shift template
Then the template is saved and available for assignment

- Story Points: 3
- Dependencies: E02, E03, E04
