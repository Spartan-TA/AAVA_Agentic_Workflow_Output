Title: Assign Shifts to Employees

User Story: As a supervisor I want to assign employees to shifts using templates so that I can ensure adequate staffing and reduce scheduling conflicts

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Assign employees to shift
Given I have created shift templates and have a list of available employees
When I select a shift and assign employees
Then the assignments are saved, conflicts are detected and prevented, and employees are notified of their upcoming shifts

Additional Details:
- Priority: High
- Story Points: 3
- Dependencies: Employee Master Data, Shift Templates, RBAC
- Notes: Bulk assignment and conflict detection must be supported; audit entries generated for all assignments
