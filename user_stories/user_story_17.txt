Title: Manager Assigns Shifts

User Story: As a manager I want to assign shifts to employees So that work coverage is ensured for all operational hours

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Assigning a shift
Given I am on the shift scheduling page
When I select an employee and assign a shift
Then the employee should receive a notification and the shift should appear on their schedule

Additional Details:
- Priority: High
- Story Points: 5
- Dependencies: Employee master data
- Notes: Prevent overlapping shifts for the same employee.
