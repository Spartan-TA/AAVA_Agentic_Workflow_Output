Title: Performance Review Workflow
User Story: As a supervisor I want to create and complete performance reviews for employees So that feedback and goals are documented and acknowledged.
Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Create review cycle
  Given a review period
  When I create a review
  Then employees are assigned review tasks

Scenario: Submit and acknowledge review
  Given a completed review
  When supervisor and employee sign off
  Then the review is locked and archived

Scenario: PDF export
  Given a completed review
  When I export
  Then a PDF is generated

Additional Details:
- Priority: Medium
- Story Points: 5
- Dependencies: E02, E03
- Notes: Immutable history after sign-off.
