Title: Employee Clock-Out Event Capture

User Story: As an employee I want to clock out at the end of my shift So that my working hours are accurately recorded and shift completion is tracked

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Successful clock-out
Given I am on the clock-out page and my shift is scheduled
When I submit my clock-out event
Then the system records my end time and calculates total hours worked

Scenario: Missed punch correction
Given I missed a clock-out
When I submit a correction request
Then the system creates an approval task for my supervisor

Additional Details:
- Priority: High
- Story Points: 3
- Dependencies: Employee Master Data, RBAC, Attendance module
- Notes: Corrections workflow must be auditable.
