Title: Shift Templates

User Story: As a scheduler I want to create reusable shift templates So that I can quickly assign standard shift patterns to multiple employees

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Create shift template
Given I am on the shift template creation page
When I enter shift details and save the template
Then the template should be available for future shift assignments

Additional Details:
- Priority: Medium
- Story Points: 3
- Dependencies: Shift management module
- Notes: Templates should support variable start/end times and roles.
