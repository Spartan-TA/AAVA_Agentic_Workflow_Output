Title: View Attendance Records

User Story: As a manager I want to view attendance records for my team So that I can monitor punctuality and identify attendance issues

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Attendance record viewing
Given I am logged in as a manager
When I access the attendance dashboard
Then I should see a list of attendance records for my team members

Additional Details:
- Priority: Medium
- Story Points: 2
- Dependencies: Attendance data capture
- Notes: Support filtering by date and employee
