Title: Shift Conflict Detection
User Story: As a supervisor I want the system to detect and prevent scheduling conflicts So that employees are not double-booked and staffing is adequate
Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Schedule conflict Given I attempt to assign an employee to overlapping shifts When the system validates the schedule Then a conflict warning is displayed and the assignment is blocked
Additional Details:
- Priority: High
- Story Points: 2
- Dependencies: Shift templates, employee master data
- Notes: Conflict detection should consider blackout dates and warehouse calendars.
