Title: Soft-Delete Employee Record

User Story: As an HR manager I want to soft-delete employee records So that terminated employees are excluded from active lists but their data is retained for compliance

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Employee soft-delete
Given an active employee record
When I submit a DELETE request to /employees/{id}
Then the employee status should be updated to 'inactive' and the record retained in the database

Additional Details:
- Priority: High
- Story Points: 2
- Dependencies: Employee creation
- Notes: Ensure soft-deleted records are excluded from active queries.
