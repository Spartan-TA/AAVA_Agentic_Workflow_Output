Title: Enforce Row-Level Data Access Constraints

User Story: As a supervisor I want to access only the employee records within my department So that I can manage my team without viewing sensitive data from other departments

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Row-level data restriction
Given I am logged in as a supervisor
When I view employee records
Then I should only see employees assigned to my department

Additional Details:
- Priority: High
- Story Points: 2
- Dependencies: E01, E02
- Notes: Ensure similar constraints for workers and HR roles as per policy.
