Title: Create Review Cycles and Templates

User Story: As an HR manager I want to create quarterly and annual performance review cycles and templates So that employee evaluations are standardized and repeatable

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Create review template
Given I am authenticated as HR or ADMIN
When I create a new review template with goals, competencies, and rating scales
Then the template is saved and available for assignment

Scenario: Assign review cycle
Given a review cycle is active
When I assign it to employees
Then the system notifies supervisors and employees of the upcoming review

Additional Details:
- Priority: Medium
- Story Points: 5
- Dependencies: E02, E03
- Notes: Templates must be versioned; review cycles must support custom timeframes.
