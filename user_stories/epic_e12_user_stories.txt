EPIC E12: Notifications & Announcements

===========================================

Title: Notifications & Announcements

User Story: As a supervisor I want to send in-app and email/SMS notifications for shift changes and announcements So that employees are informed in real time

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Notification delivery
Given a shift change occurs
When I send a notification
Then users receive it via selected channels

Additional Details:
- Priority: Medium
- Story Points: 2
- Dependencies: RBAC, Schedule management
- Notes: Opt-in/out per channel
