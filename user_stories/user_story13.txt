Title: Leave Request and Approval

User Story: As an employee, I want to request PTO, sick, or unpaid leave, and as a supervisor, I want to approve or deny these requests so that leave is managed compliantly.

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Leave request submission
Given I am an employee
When I submit a leave request
Then the request is pending supervisor approval

Scenario: Leave approval/denial
Given I am a supervisor
When I approve or deny a request
Then the employee is notified and balances are updated

Additional Details:
- Priority: Medium
- Story Points: 5
- Dependencies: Employee CRUD API, RBAC, Shift Management
- Notes: Integrate with scheduling and payroll.
