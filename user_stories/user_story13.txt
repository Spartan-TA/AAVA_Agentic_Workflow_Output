Title: Shift Template Management
User Story: As an HR manager I want to create and manage recurring shift templates So that scheduling is efficient and consistent
Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Shift template CRUD
Given I am authenticated as HR
When I create, update, or delete a shift template
Then the template is saved and available for assignment

Additional Details:
- Priority: High
- Story Points: 3
- Dependencies: Employee CRUD, RBAC, Attendance
- Notes: Templates support rotations and overtime rules
