Title: Create New Shift

User Story: As a shift manager I want to create new shifts So that I can schedule employees according to operational needs

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Create shift
Given I am on the shift management page
When I enter shift details and save
Then a new shift should be added to the schedule

Additional Details:
- Priority: High
- Story Points: 3
- Dependencies: Shift Management module
- Notes: Validate shift overlaps and employee availability.
