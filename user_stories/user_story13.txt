Title: Clock-Out Event Recording
User Story: As a WORKER I want to clock out at the end of my shift So that my total hours worked are accurately calculated
Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Successful clock-out
Given I am scheduled for a shift and on the clock-out page
When I submit a clock-out event
Then my clock-out is recorded, associated with my shift, and daily totals are updated
Additional Details:
- Priority: High
- Story Points: 3
- Dependencies: E02, E03, E04
- Notes: Automatic shift association must handle overlapping or missed punches.
