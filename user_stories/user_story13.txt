Title: Assign Shifts to Employees

User Story: As a scheduler I want to assign shifts to employees using templates So that scheduling is efficient and consistent

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Shifts assigned to employees
  Given shift templates exist
  When I assign a shift to an employee
  Then the employee's schedule should reflect the new assignment

Additional Details:
- Priority: High
- Story Points: 3
- Dependencies: Shift templates, employee entity
- Notes: Prevent overlapping shift assignments.
