Title: Training & Certification Tracking

User Story: As an HR manager I want to track employee certifications and expirations So that only qualified staff are assigned to equipment-sensitive roles and compliance is maintained

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Certification expiry alert
Given an employee's certification is expiring within 30 days
When the system checks certification status
Then an alert is sent to the employee and supervisor

Scenario: Block unqualified assignment
Given an employee's certification is expired
When a supervisor tries to assign them to a restricted task
Then the system blocks the assignment and displays a warning

Additional Details:
- Priority: High
- Story Points: 5
- Dependencies: Employee Master Data, RBAC
- Notes: Upload proof documents; certification status visible on profile; scheduling checks enforced
