Title: Shift Assignment and Conflict Detection
User Story: As a supervisor, I want to assign shifts to employees and detect conflicts so that staffing is adequate and errors are prevented.
Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Assign shift
Given an employee is available
When I assign a shift
Then the assignment is successful

Scenario: Conflict detected
Given an employee is already scheduled
When I assign an overlapping shift
Then a conflict is detected and prevented

Additional Details:
- Priority: High
- Story Points: 4
- Dependencies: Shift Template Management
- Notes: Bulk assignment supported
