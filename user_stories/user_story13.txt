Title: Request Leave (PTO, Sick, Unpaid)

User Story: As an employee I want to request different types of leave so that my time off is properly recorded and approved

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Submitting a leave request
Given I am logged in
When I submit a leave request for PTO, sick, or unpaid leave
Then the request is sent to my supervisor for approval

Additional Details:
- Priority: Medium
- Story Points: 2
- Dependencies: Employee Master Data, RBAC, Shift Management
- Notes: Leave types and policies configurable
