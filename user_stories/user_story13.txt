Title: Manage Leave & Absence Requests

User Story: As an employee I want to submit PTO and sick leave requests So that my absences are tracked and approved efficiently

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Submit and approve leave request
Given I am logged into the system
When I submit a PTO request
Then my manager is notified and can approve or reject the request

Additional Details:
- Priority: Medium
- Story Points: 5
- Dependencies: E02 (Employee Master Data CRUD), E03 (RBAC), E05 (Shift & Schedule Management)
- Notes: Include leave balance validation and approval workflow.
