Title: Time & Attendance - Clock In

User Story: As a worker I want to clock in with device and optional geofence capture so that my attendance is accurately recorded.

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Clock in event
Given I am scheduled to work
When I POST to /attendance/clock-in
Then my clock-in is recorded with device and location info

Additional Details:
- Priority: High
- Story Points: 3
- Dependencies: Employee CRUD, RBAC
- Notes: Validate geofence if enabled.
