Title: Request Leave
User Story: As an employee I want to request PTO, sick, or unpaid leave So that my time off is tracked and approved
Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Request leave Given I am logged in When I submit a leave request Then my supervisor is notified for approval
Additional Details:
- Priority: Medium
- Story Points: 2
- Dependencies: Employee Master Data, RBAC, Shift Management
- Notes: Leave types and balances enforced

Title: Approve/Deny Leave
User Story: As a supervisor I want to approve or deny leave requests So that staffing plans remain accurate
Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Approve leave Given a pending leave request When I approve or deny Then the employee's leave balance and schedule are updated
Additional Details:
- Priority: Medium
- Story Points: 2
- Dependencies: Employee Master Data, RBAC, Shift Management
- Notes: Scheduled shifts auto-flagged for coverage

Title: Export Approved Leaves
User Story: As an HR manager I want to export approved leaves So that payroll and compliance reports are accurate
Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Export leaves Given approved leave records exist When I export leaves Then a report is generated showing all approved leaves and coverage status
Additional Details:
- Priority: Medium
- Story Points: 1
- Dependencies: Employee Master Data, RBAC, Shift Management
- Notes: Export includes leave type, dates, and approval status
