Title: Manager Assigns Training

User Story: As a manager I want to assign training modules to employees So that compliance and skill requirements are met

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Assigning training
Given I am logged in as a manager and have access to employee profiles
When I select an employee and assign a training module
Then the employee receives a notification and the training is added to their required list

Additional Details:
- Priority: Medium
- Story Points: 3
- Dependencies: Employee Master Data, Notifications
- Notes: Allow bulk assignment for multiple employees.
