Title: Clock In Functionality
User Story: As an employee I want to clock in at the start of my shift So that my working hours are accurately recorded
Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Successful clock in
Given I am logged into the system and my shift is scheduled for today
When I select the 'Clock In' button
Then my clock in time is recorded and I receive a confirmation notification
Additional Details:
- Priority: High
- Story Points: 3
- Dependencies: User authentication, shift schedule
- Notes: Must prevent multiple clock-ins for the same shift.
