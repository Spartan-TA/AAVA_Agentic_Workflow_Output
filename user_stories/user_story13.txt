Title: Leave Request & Approval
User Story: As an employee, I want to request PTO, sick, or unpaid leave so that my time off is tracked and approved.
Acceptance Criteria:
  Given a leave request,
  When I submit it,
  Then my supervisor can approve or deny.

  Given an approved leave,
  When I view my balance,
  Then it updates accordingly.

  Given a scheduled shift during leave,
  When leave is approved,
  Then the shift is flagged for coverage.

Additional Details:
  Priority: Medium
  Story Points: 5
  Dependencies: Employee CRUD, RBAC, Shift Management
  Notes: Exports show approved leaves.
