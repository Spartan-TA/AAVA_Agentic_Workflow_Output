Title: Shift Template and Schedule Management

User Story: As a scheduler, I want to create and manage recurring shift templates so that staffing is consistent and efficient.

Acceptance Criteria:

Scenario: Create shift template
Given the UI/API
When I create a shift template
Then it is saved and visible in the schedule list

Scenario: Assign shifts to employees
Given a template
When I assign to employees
Then their schedules update accordingly

Scenario: Detect scheduling conflicts
Given overlapping shifts
When detected
Then a conflict error is shown

Additional Details:
- Priority: High
- Story Points: 5
- Dependencies: E02, E03, E04
- Notes: Blackout dates supported
