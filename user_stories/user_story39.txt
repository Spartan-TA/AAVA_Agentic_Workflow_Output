Title: Manager Evaluation Submission

User Story: As a manager I want to submit my evaluation of direct reports during the review cycle So that HR can aggregate performance data for organizational insights

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Submitting manager evaluation
Given the review cycle is active and my direct reports have submitted self-assessments
When I complete and submit my evaluations
Then the results should be recorded and accessible to HR

Additional Details:
- Priority: High
- Story Points: 4
- Dependencies: Employee self-assessment, review cycle initiation
- Notes: Allow managers to provide qualitative and quantitative feedback.
