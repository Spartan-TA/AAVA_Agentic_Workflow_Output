Title: Auto-Flag Scheduled Shifts for Coverage

User Story: As a scheduler I want approved leave requests to automatically flag scheduled shifts for coverage So that I can quickly identify and fill gaps in the schedule

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Leave approval triggers coverage flag
Given an employee's leave request is approved
When the employee has scheduled shifts during the leave period
Then those shifts are flagged for coverage and notifications are sent to the scheduler

Additional Details:
- Priority: Medium
- Story Points: 4
- Dependencies: E02, E03, E05
- Notes: Integrate with scheduling and notification modules.
