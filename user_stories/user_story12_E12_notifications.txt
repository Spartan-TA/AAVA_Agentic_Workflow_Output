### Epic E12: Notifications & Announcements

**User Story 1**
Title: Opt-In/Out Notification Channels
User Story: As a WORKER, I want to opt-in/out of notification channels so that I control how I'm notified.
Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Channel preference
Given notification settings
When I update preferences
Then notifications are sent only to selected channels
Additional Details:
Priority: Medium
Story Points: 2
Dependencies: E03, E05, E06, E07
Notes: In-app, email, SMS.

**User Story 2**
Title: Localized Notification Templates
User Story: As an HR, I want notification templates localized so that messages are clear for all regions.
Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Localization
Given a template
When I select a locale
Then the notification is sent in the correct language
Additional Details:
Priority: Medium
Story Points: 2
Dependencies: E03, E05, E06, E07
Notes: Supports all active locales.
