[E12] Notifications & Announcements

Title: Opt-In/Out for Notification Channels
User Story: As a user I want to opt-in or out of notification channels (email, SMS, in-app) so that I control how I receive updates.
Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Notification Channel Preferences
Given my user settings
When I change notification preferences
Then my choices are saved and respected

Additional Details:
- Priority: Medium
- Story Points: 2
- Dependencies: E03, E05, E06, E07
- Notes: Support per-event preferences
