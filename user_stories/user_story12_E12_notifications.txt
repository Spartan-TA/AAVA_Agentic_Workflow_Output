Epic E12: Notifications & Announcements

---

User Story 1: In-App and Email/SMS Notifications
As a worker, I want to receive notifications for shift changes, expiring certs, approvals, and announcements so that I am informed.

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Notification delivery
  Given a relevant event
  When notification is triggered
  Then I receive it via selected channel

Additional Details:
- Priority: Medium
- Story Points: 5
- Dependencies: E03, E05, E06, E07
- Notes: Users opt-in/out per channel

---

User Story 2: Quiet Hours Configuration
As a worker, I want to configure quiet hours so that notifications do not disturb me outside work hours.

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Quiet hours set
  Given my preferences
  When quiet hours are configured
  Then notifications are suppressed during those times

Additional Details:
- Priority: Medium
- Story Points: 2
- Dependencies: E03, E05, E06, E07
- Notes: Quiet hours per user

Additional Details:
- Priority: Medium
- Story Points: 2
- Dependencies: E03, E05, E06, E07
