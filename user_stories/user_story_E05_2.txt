Title: Assign Shifts to Employees

User Story: As a shift manager I want to assign shifts to employees So that work schedules are clearly communicated and managed

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Shift assignment
Given I have created shift templates
When I assign shifts to employees
Then the assignments should be visible to employees and managers

Additional Details:
- Priority: High
- Story Points: 3
- Dependencies: Shift management module, employee master data
- Notes: Support bulk assignment and conflict detection.
