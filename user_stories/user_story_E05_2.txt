Title: Assign Shifts to Employees

User Story: As a supervisor I want to assign shifts to employees so that staffing needs are met and schedules are visible to all team members

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Assigning a shift
Given I am logged in as supervisor
When I select employees and assign them to a shift template
Then the schedule should reflect the assignments and notify affected employees

Additional Details:
- Priority: High
- Story Points: 2
- Dependencies: E02, E03, E04
- Notes: Bulk assignment supported; notifications triggered upon assignment.
