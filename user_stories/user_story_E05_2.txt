Title: Workers View Personal Upcoming Shifts

User Story: As a warehouse worker I want to view my upcoming scheduled shifts on my dashboard So that I can plan my attendance and work-life balance

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: View upcoming shifts
Given I am authenticated as a WORKER
When I access my dashboard
Then I see a list of all my upcoming scheduled shifts with dates, times, and assigned locations

Scenario: No upcoming shifts
Given I have no scheduled shifts in the future
When I access my dashboard
Then the system displays a message indicating no upcoming shifts

Additional Details:
- Priority: High
- Story Points: 3
- Dependencies: E02, E03, E04
- Notes: Dashboard must update in real-time as schedules are assigned or changed.
