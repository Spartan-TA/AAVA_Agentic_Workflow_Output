Title: Manage Shift Rotations

User Story: As a scheduling manager I want to manage shift rotations So that employees can be assigned to rotating schedules efficiently

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Assigning employees to a rotation
Given I have defined a rotation pattern
When I assign employees to the rotation
Then the system should automatically update their schedules according to the pattern

Additional Details:
- Priority: High
- Story Points: 3
- Dependencies: Employee roster
- Notes: Support for weekly, bi-weekly, and custom rotations.
