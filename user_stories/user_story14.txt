Title: Create and Manage Shift Templates

User Story: As an HR manager I want to create recurring shift templates and assign them to employees so that warehouse staffing is consistent and conflicts are minimized

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Create shift template
Given I am an HR manager
When I define a new shift template with start/end times and recurrence
Then the template is saved and available for assignment

Scenario: Assign shift template
Given a shift template exists
When I assign it to one or more employees
Then their schedules are updated accordingly

Additional Details:
- Priority: High
- Story Points: 3
- Dependencies: Employee Master Data, RBAC, Attendance
- Notes: Must support rotations, overtime rules, and blackout dates.
