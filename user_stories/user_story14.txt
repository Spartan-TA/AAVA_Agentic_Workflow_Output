Title: Safety Incidents & OSHA Reporting

User Story: As a safety officer I want to record and track safety incidents So that investigations can be conducted and OSHA reporting requirements are met

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Record safety incident
Given an incident occurs in the warehouse
When I submit a new incident report with required details
Then the system validates and stores the report

Scenario: Investigation workflow
Given an incident is reported
When the status is updated to 'Investigating'
Then corrective action tasks are generated and tracked

Additional Details:
- Priority: High
- Story Points: 6
- Dependencies: Employee Master Data, RBAC
- Notes: OSHA 300/300A export supported; metrics dashboard endpoints available
