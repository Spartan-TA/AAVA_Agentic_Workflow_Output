Title: Block Unqualified Assignment
User Story: As a scheduler I want to prevent assignment of tasks to employees with expired certifications So that safety and compliance are ensured
Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Assign task to unqualified employee
Given an employee with expired forklift certification
When I try to assign them to a forklift shift
Then the system blocks the assignment and notifies the scheduler
Additional Details:
- Priority: High
- Story Points: 2
- Dependencies: E02, E03
- Notes: Certification status visible on profile
