Title: Time & Attendance - Clock Out

User Story: As a worker I want to clock out so that my shift hours are calculated for payroll.

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Clock out event
Given I am clocked in
When I POST to /attendance/clock-out
Then my clock-out is recorded and hours are computed

Additional Details:
- Priority: High
- Story Points: 2
- Dependencies: Clock In
- Notes: Associate with correct shift.
