Title: Track Training & Certifications

User Story: As a compliance manager I want to track employee certifications and expirations So that only qualified employees are assigned to tasks and compliance is maintained

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Block assignment for expired certification
Given an employee's certification has expired
When a scheduler attempts to assign the employee to a restricted task
Then the system blocks the assignment and notifies the scheduler

Additional Details:
- Priority: High
- Story Points: 6
- Dependencies: E02 (Employee Master Data CRUD), E03 (RBAC)
- Notes: Include automated reminders for upcoming expirations.
