Title: Record Safety Incidents
User Story: As a safety officer I want to record safety incidents So that OSHA compliance is maintained

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Incident recording
Given a safety incident occurs
When I submit an incident report
Then the incident is logged with all required details

Additional Details:
- Priority: High
- Story Points: 3
- Dependencies: Employee Master Data (CRUD)
- Notes: Include location, time, involved employees
