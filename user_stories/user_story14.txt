Title: Schedule Conflict Detection
User Story: As a scheduler I want the system to detect and prevent shift conflicts So that employees are not double-booked
Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Shift conflict prevention
Given an employee is assigned to a shift
When I try to assign them to an overlapping shift
Then the system prevents the assignment and displays an error

Additional Details:
- Priority: High
- Story Points: 3
- Dependencies: Shift Template Management
- Notes: Blackout dates and operation calendars supported
