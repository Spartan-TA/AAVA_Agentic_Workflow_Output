Title: Employee Clock-In/Clock-Out

User Story: As an EMPLOYEE I want to clock in and clock out So that my working hours are accurately recorded

Acceptance Criteria:

Scenario: Employee clocks in
Given I am logged in as EMPLOYEE
When I click the clock-in button
Then my start time is recorded for the day

Scenario: Employee clocks out
Given I am logged in as EMPLOYEE and have clocked in
When I click the clock-out button
Then my end time is recorded for the day

Additional Details:
- Priority: High
- Story Points: 3
- Dependencies: E01, E02
- Notes: Must support mobile and desktop clock-in/out.
