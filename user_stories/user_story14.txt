Title: Track Employee Certifications

User Story: As an HR manager I want to record and track employee certifications so that compliance with safety and operational requirements is maintained

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Adding a certification
Given I am on an employee's profile
When I add a new certification with expiry date
Then the certification is saved and tracked for renewal

Additional Details:
- Priority: High
- Story Points: 2
- Dependencies: Employee Master Data, RBAC
- Notes: Certification types configurable

---

Title: Alert for Expiring Certifications

User Story: As a supervisor I want to receive alerts for certifications expiring soon so that renewals can be scheduled in advance

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Certification expiry alert
Given a certification is expiring in 30 or 7 days
When the system checks certification dates
Then an alert is sent to the employee and supervisor

Additional Details:
- Priority: High
- Story Points: 2
- Dependencies: Certification tracking, Notifications
- Notes: Configurable alert periods

---

Title: Block Assignment to Tasks Requiring Valid Certifications

User Story: As a scheduler I want the system to prevent assignment of employees with expired certifications to sensitive tasks so that only qualified staff are assigned

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Assignment with expired certification
Given an employee's certification is expired
When I attempt to assign them to a restricted task
Then the system blocks the assignment and notifies me

Additional Details:
- Priority: High
- Story Points: 3
- Dependencies: Scheduling, Certification tracking
- Notes: Applies to equipment and safety-sensitive roles

Title: Display Certification Status on Employee Profile

User Story: As a supervisor I want to view certification status on employee profiles so that I can quickly verify qualifications

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Viewing certification status
Given I am viewing an employee profile
When I check the certifications section
Then I see all certifications, expiry dates, and status (valid/expiring/expired)

Additional Details:
- Priority: Medium
- Story Points: 1
- Dependencies: Employee profile, Certification tracking
- Notes: Status color-coded for clarity
