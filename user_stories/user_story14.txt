Title: Leave Accrual and Balance Update

User Story: As a system, I want to update leave balances and auto-flag scheduled shifts for coverage when leave is approved so that staffing plans remain accurate.

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Leave balance update
Given a leave is approved
When the approval is processed
Then the employee's leave balance is updated
And scheduled shifts during the leave are flagged for coverage

Additional Details:
- Priority: Medium
- Story Points: 3
- Dependencies: Leave Request and Approval
- Notes: Export approved leaves for payroll.
