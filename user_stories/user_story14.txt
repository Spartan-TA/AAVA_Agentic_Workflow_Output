Title: Employee Reports Safety Incident

User Story: As an employee I want to report safety incidents through the system So that hazards are documented and addressed promptly

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Reporting a safety incident
Given I am logged into the system
When I submit a safety incident report with details and optional attachments
Then the report is logged and relevant personnel are notified

Additional Details:
- Priority: High
- Story Points: 4
- Dependencies: Notifications, Employee Master Data
- Notes: Ensure anonymity option and mandatory fields for incident description.
