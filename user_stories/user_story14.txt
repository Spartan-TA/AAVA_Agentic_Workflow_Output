Title: Send Shift Change Notifications

User Story: As a warehouse worker I want to receive notifications for shift changes so that I am always informed of my schedule

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Shift change notification
Given my shift is updated
When the change is saved
Then I receive an in-app and email/SMS notification

Additional Details:
- Priority: Medium
- Story Points: 2
- Dependencies: Shift management, Notification module
- Notes: Users can opt-in/out per channel
