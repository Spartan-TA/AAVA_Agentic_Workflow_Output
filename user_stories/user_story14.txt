Title: Clock Out Functionality
User Story: As an employee I want to clock out at the end of my shift So that my working hours are accurately recorded and payroll can be processed
Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Successful clock out
Given I am logged into the system and have clocked in for my shift
When I select the 'Clock Out' button
Then my clock out time is recorded and I receive a confirmation notification
Additional Details:
- Priority: High
- Story Points: 3
- Dependencies: User authentication, clock in record
- Notes: Must prevent clock out without a prior clock in.
