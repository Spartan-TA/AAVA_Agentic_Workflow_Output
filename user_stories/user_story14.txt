Title: Shift & Schedule Management - Audit Entries for Scheduling

User Story: As an auditor, I want all schedule changes to generate audit entries so that changes are traceable.

Acceptance Criteria:

Scenario: Audit entry on schedule change
Given a shift assignment is created, updated, or deleted
When the action occurs
Then an audit entry is recorded with actor, timestamp, and before/after state

Additional Details:
- Priority: High
- Story Points: 3
- Dependencies: Shift assignment
- Notes: Audit log must be immutable.
