Title: Equipment & Asset Assignment
User Story: As an asset manager I want to assign and track equipment issued to employees So that accountability and safety are ensured
Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Assign asset
Given an available asset and a qualified employee
When I assign the asset
Then the assignment is recorded and visible on both profiles

Scenario: Block assignment if certification missing
Given an employee lacks required certification
When I attempt to assign restricted equipment
Then the assignment is blocked

Additional Details:
- Priority: Medium
- Story Points: 5
- Dependencies: Employee CRUD API, RBAC, Certification Tracking
- Notes: Track check-in/out and asset condition.
