Title: Create and Assign Shifts

User Story: As a scheduler I want to create shifts and assign them to employees so that workforce coverage is planned and communicated

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Creating and assigning a shift
Given I am logged in as a scheduler
When I create a new shift and assign employees
Then the shift appears on the schedule and assigned employees are notified

Additional Details:
- Priority: High
- Story Points: 5
- Dependencies: Employee master data, notification module
- Notes: Support for recurring and one-time shifts.
