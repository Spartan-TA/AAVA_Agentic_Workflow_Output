Title: Retrieve Employee List (GET)

User Story: As an HR user, I want to retrieve a paginated and filtered list of employees so that I can manage large workforces efficiently.

Acceptance Criteria:

Scenario: Paginated results
Given multiple employees exist
When I GET `/employees?page=1&size=20`
Then I receive 20 employees and pagination metadata

Scenario: Filter by department
Given employees in different departments
When I GET `/employees?department=Shipping`
Then only employees from Shipping are returned

Additional Details:
- Priority: High
- Story Points: 3
- Dependencies: E01
- Notes: Filtering by role, status, shiftGroup.
