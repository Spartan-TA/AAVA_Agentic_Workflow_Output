# Epic E08: Safety Incidents & OSHA Reporting

## User Story 1: Record Safety Incidents/Near-Misses
**Title:** Record Safety Incidents  
**Narrative:** As a safety officer, I want to record incidents and near-misses with details so that safety is tracked and improved.  
**Acceptance Criteria (Gherkin):**
```
Given the safety module
When I POST a new incident
Then severity, location, description, and involved employees are captured
```
**Priority:** High  
**Story Points:** 3  
**Dependencies:** E02, E03  
**Notes:** Validate required fields.
