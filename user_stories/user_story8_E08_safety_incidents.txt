[E08] Safety Incidents & OSHA Reporting

Title: Record Safety Incidents
User Story: As a safety officer I want to record incidents and near-misses with severity, location, description, and involved employees so that safety data is tracked.
Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Incident Recording
Given the safety module
When I submit an incident report
Then the incident is validated and stored

Additional Details:
- Priority: High
- Story Points: 3
- Dependencies: E02, E03
- Notes: Support attachments

---

Title: Incident Investigation Workflow
User Story: As a safety officer I want a workflow for investigating incidents so that corrective actions are documented.
Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Investigation Workflow
Given an open incident
When I start an investigation
Then the status changes to Investigating and actions can be logged

Additional Details:
- Priority: High
- Story Points: 2
- Dependencies: Record Safety Incidents
- Notes: Track investigation steps

---

Title: Generate OSHA Summary Reports
User Story: As a compliance officer I want to generate OSHA 300/300A summary reports so that regulatory requirements are met.
Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: OSHA Report Generation
Given incident data
When I request a summary
Then the report includes all required OSHA fields

Additional Details:
- Priority: High
- Story Points: 2
- Dependencies: Record Safety Incidents
- Notes: Support PDF/CSV export

Title: Status Workflow for Incidents
User Story: As a safety officer I want incident status to progress from Open to Investigating to Resolved so that lifecycle is tracked.
Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Incident Status Progression
Given an incident
When investigation and corrective actions are completed
Then status changes to Resolved

Additional Details:
- Priority: High
- Story Points: 2
- Dependencies: Incident Investigation Workflow
- Notes: Prevent edits after resolution
