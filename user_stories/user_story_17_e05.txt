Title: Assign Shifts to Employees

User Story: As a scheduler I want to assign shifts to employees So that work coverage is planned and communicated

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Assign shift to employee
Given a shift template and employee list
When I select employees and assign them to a shift
Then the assignments are saved and employees are notified

Additional Details:
- Priority: High
- Story Points: 2
- Dependencies: Scheduling module, notification system
- Notes: Prevent overlapping assignments for the same employee.
