Title: Shift Notification

User Story: As a warehouse employee, I want to receive notifications about upcoming shifts So that I am reminded to report on time.

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Shift notification
Given I am assigned to a shift
When the shift is approaching
Then I receive a notification via email or SMS

Additional Details:
- Priority: High
- Story Points: 2
- Dependencies: Shift Scheduling
- Notes: Notification timing configurable
