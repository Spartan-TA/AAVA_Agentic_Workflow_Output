Title: Assign Training Modules

User Story: As a training manager I want to assign training modules to employees So that required skills and certifications are tracked and completed

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Training assignment
Given I am logged in as a training manager
When I assign a training module to an employee
Then the employee should be notified and able to access the module

Additional Details:
- Priority: High
- Story Points: 3
- Dependencies: Training content, notifications
- Notes: Support for mandatory and optional training
