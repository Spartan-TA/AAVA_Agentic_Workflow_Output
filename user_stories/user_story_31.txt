Title: Assign Equipment

User Story: As a supervisor I want to assign equipment to employees for specific shifts So that asset usage is tracked and accountability is maintained

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Assign equipment to shift
Given I am scheduling a shift
When I select equipment and assign it to an employee
Then the assignment should be logged and visible in the schedule

Additional Details:
- Priority: Medium
- Story Points: 3
- Dependencies: Asset management, shift scheduling
- Notes: Should support multiple assets per shift.
