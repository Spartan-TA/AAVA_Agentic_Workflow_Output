Title: Maintain Asset Condition State and Audit

User Story: As an asset manager I want to perform periodic audits of equipment condition so that I can ensure assets are safe, functional, and compliant with regulations.

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Conducting an asset audit
Given a scheduled audit period
When I review the condition of all assets and update their status in the system
Then the system should log the audit, update asset records, and flag any assets needing maintenance

Additional Details:
- Priority: Medium
- Story Points: 3
- Dependencies: E02, E03, E07
- Notes: Audit logs should be accessible for compliance reviews.
