Title: Export OSHA Summary Reports

User Story: As a compliance officer I want to export OSHA 300/300A summary reports from recorded safety incidents so that regulatory requirements are met and audits are supported

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Generate OSHA export
  Given safety incidents are recorded in the system
  When I generate an OSHA summary
  Then the export includes all required OSHA fields and is available for download

Additional Details:
- Priority: High
- Story Points: 2
- Dependencies: E02 (User Management), E03 (Scheduling)
- Notes: Export must match OSHA schema and be filterable by date
