Title: Upload Proof Documents for Certifications

User Story: As an employee I want to upload proof documents for my certifications So that my qualification status is verified and stored securely

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Upload certification proof
Given I have obtained a new or renewed certification
When I upload the proof document to my profile
Then the document is stored and linked to my certification record

Additional Details:
- Priority: High
- Story Points: 2
- Dependencies: E02, E03
- Notes: Support PDF, image formats, and secure storage.
