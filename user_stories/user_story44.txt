Title: Shift Change Notification

User Story: As an employee I want to receive notifications for shift changes via my preferred channel So that I am always aware of updates to my schedule

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Shift change notification delivery
Given my shift has been changed
When the change is approved
Then I receive a notification via my selected channel (in-app, email, SMS) and delivery status is tracked

Additional Details:
- Priority: Medium
- Story Points: 5
- Dependencies: E03, E05, E06, E07
- Notes: Users can opt-in/out per channel; templates must be localized
