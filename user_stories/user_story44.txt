Title: Assign Review Cycles and Submit/Acknowledge Workflow

User Story:
As a supervisor, I want to assign performance review cycles to employees and manage the submission and acknowledgement workflow so that reviews are completed on time and acknowledged by all parties.

Acceptance Criteria:
- Scenario: Assign review cycle to employee
  Given a review cycle is created
  When the supervisor assigns it to an employee
  Then the employee receives a notification and can access the review

- Scenario: Employee and supervisor submit and acknowledge review
  Given a review is in progress
  When both parties submit and acknowledge
  Then the review is marked as complete and history is locked

Additional Details:
- Workflow must support draft, submitted, acknowledged, and locked statuses
- Notifications must be sent at each stage
- All actions must be auditable
