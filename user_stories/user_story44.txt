Title: Damage Reporting Workflow

User Story: As a warehouse associate I want to report damaged goods and assets So that inventory records are updated and corrective actions are initiated

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Damage report submission
Given a damaged item
When I submit a damage report
Then the system should update inventory and notify relevant personnel

Additional Details:
- Priority: Medium
- Story Points: 3
- Dependencies: Equipment Asset Registry CRUD
- Notes: Support attachment of photos and incident details.
