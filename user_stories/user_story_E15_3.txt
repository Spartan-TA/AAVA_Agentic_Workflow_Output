Title: CSV/PDF Export of Reports Within 10 Seconds for Up to 50,000 Rows

User Story: As a business analyst I want to export operational reports to CSV or PDF formats within 10 seconds for up to 50,000 rows So that I can share and analyze data externally without delays

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Fast report export
Given a report contains up to 50,000 rows
When I request a CSV or PDF export
Then the file is generated and available for download within 10 seconds

Additional Details:
- Priority: Medium
- Story Points: 5
- Dependencies: E04, E05, E06, E07, E08, E14
- Notes: Export must preserve all report filters and formatting.
