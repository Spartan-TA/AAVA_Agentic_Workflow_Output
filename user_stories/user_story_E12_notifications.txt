Title: Notify Shift Changes

User Story: As a Warehouse Worker I want to receive notifications for shift changes So that I am aware of my updated schedule

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Shift change notification
Given a shift is updated
When the change is saved
Then affected employees should receive notifications

Additional Details:
- Priority: Medium
- Story Points: 2
- Dependencies: Shift Management (E05), Notification System
- Notes: Support in-app and email/SMS
