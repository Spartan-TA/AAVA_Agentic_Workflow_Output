Title: Send Notifications for Shift Changes
User Story: As a worker I want to receive notifications for shift changes So that I am aware of my schedule
Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Shift change notification
  Given my shift is updated
  When the change is saved
  Then I receive an in-app and email/SMS notification
Additional Details:
- Priority: Medium
- Story Points: 3
- Dependencies: RBAC, Shift management, Notification module
- Notes: Support opt-in/out
