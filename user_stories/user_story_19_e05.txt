Title: View Personal Shift Calendar

User Story: As an employee I want to view my personal shift calendar So that I can see my upcoming work schedule and plan accordingly

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Display personal shift calendar
Given I am logged in as an employee
When I navigate to the shift calendar page
Then my assigned shifts for the month are displayed in a calendar view

Additional Details:
- Priority: Medium
- Story Points: 2
- Dependencies: Scheduling module, calendar UI component
- Notes: Support mobile and desktop views.
