EPIC E05: SHIFT & SCHEDULE MANAGEMENT

===========================================

Title: Shift Management - Create Shift Templates
User Story: As a scheduler, I want to create recurring shift templates and rotations so that scheduling is efficient.
Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Create shift template
  Given shift details
  When I POST to /shifts/templates
  Then the template is created and visible
Additional Details:
- Priority: High
- Story Points: 3
- Dependencies: E02,E03,E04
- Notes: Support weekly, biweekly, etc.

---

Title: Shift Management - Assign Shifts
User Story: As a supervisor, I want to assign shifts to employees in bulk so that staffing is streamlined.
Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Bulk assign shifts
  Given a list of employees
  When I assign a shift
  Then all assignments are created and conflicts are detected
Additional Details:
- Priority: High
- Story Points: 3
- Dependencies: E02,E03,E04
- Notes: Prevent double-booking.

---

Title: Shift Management - Blackout Dates & Calendars
User Story: As a scheduler, I want to manage blackout dates and operation calendars so that shifts are not assigned on holidays or closures.
Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Blackout date
  Given a blackout date
  When scheduling shifts
  Then no shifts are assigned on that date
Additional Details:
- Priority: High
- Story Points: 2
- Dependencies: E02,E03,E04
- Notes: Calendar visible to all users.

---

Title: Shift Management - View Personal Shifts
User Story: As a worker, I want to view my upcoming shifts so that I can plan my schedule.
Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: View shifts
  Given assigned shifts
  When I log in
  Then my upcoming shifts are listed
Additional Details:
- Priority: High
- Story Points: 1
- Dependencies: E02,E03,E04
- Notes: Mobile-friendly.

Title: Shift Management - Audit Shift Changes
User Story: As an auditor, I want all shift assignments and changes logged so that compliance is ensured.
Acceptance Criteria:

Additional Details:
- Priority: High
- Story Points: 2
