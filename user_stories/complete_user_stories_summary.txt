The user stories have been organized with clear dependency chains:

- **Core Data Layer** (E02, E03): Employee data and security - depends on E01
- **Operational Layer** (E04-E10): Time tracking, scheduling, leave, training, safety, equipment, performance - depends on E02, E03
- **Integration Layer** (E11-E13): Payroll, notifications, external systems - depends on operational layer
- **Compliance Layer** (E14): Audit trail - depends on E03
- **Analytics Layer** (E15): Reporting - depends on operational and compliance layers
- **User Experience Layer** (E16): Mobile access - depends on operational layer

- **Scale Layer** (E18): Multi-tenant and localization - depends on foundation and core layers
