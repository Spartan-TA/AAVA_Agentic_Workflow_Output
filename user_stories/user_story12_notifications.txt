EPIC E12: Notifications & Announcements

---

Title: Send Shift Change Notifications
User Story: As a worker I want to receive notifications for shift changes So that I am always informed of my schedule
Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Shift change notification
Given my shift is updated
When the change is saved
Then I receive an in-app and email/SMS notification

Additional Details:
- Priority: Medium
- Story Points: 2
- Dependencies: E03, E05
- Notes: Opt-in/out per channel
