Title: Request Paid/Unpaid Leave
User Story: As an employee, I want to request PTO, sick, or unpaid leave so that my absences are tracked.
Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Leave request is submitted
Given I have available leave balance
When I submit a leave request
Then the request is routed to my supervisor for approval
Additional Details:
- Priority: Medium
- Story Points: 3
- Dependencies: E02, E03, E05
- Notes: Leave types configurable.

Title: Supervisor Approve/Deny Leave
User Story: As a supervisor, I want to approve or deny leave requests so that staffing is maintained.
Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Leave request is processed
Given I have pending leave requests
When I approve or deny a request
Then the employee is notified and the schedule is updated
Additional Details:
- Priority: Medium
- Story Points: 2
- Dependencies: US06.1
- Notes: Approval workflow with comments.

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Leave balance is displayed
Given I am logged in
When I view my leave balance
Then I see current accruals and usage
Additional Details:
- Priority: Medium
- Story Points: 2
- Dependencies: US06.1

Title: Exclude Approved Leave from Scheduling & Payroll
User Story: As a scheduler, I want approved leaves to automatically exclude employees from shift assignments and payroll calculations.
Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Leave is excluded from scheduling
Given an employee has approved leave
When generating schedules or payroll
Then the system excludes those dates from assignment and pay
Additional Details:
- Priority: Medium
- Story Points: 3
- Dependencies: US06.2, E05
- Notes: Integration hooks for payroll export.

Additional Details:
- Priority: Low
- Story Points: 2
