Title: Request and Approve Leave
User Story: As a worker I want to request PTO or sick leave So that my absence is recorded and approved
Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Submit leave request
  Given I am logged in
  When I submit a leave request
  Then the request should be sent to my supervisor
Scenario: Approve leave
  Given a pending request
  When the supervisor approves
  Then the leave is recorded and the schedule updated
Additional Details:
- Priority: Medium
- Story Points: 5
- Dependencies: Employee CRUD, RBAC, Shift management
- Notes: Track leave balances
