Title: Implement Clock-In/Clock-Out Endpoints
User Story: As a warehouse worker, I want to clock in and out via endpoints so that my work hours are accurately recorded.
Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Clock-In/Out
  Given valid credentials
  When I clock in or out
  Then the event is recorded with timestamp

Additional Details:
Priority: High
Story Points: 5
Dependencies: E02, E03
Notes: Capture device and geofence if available.
