### EPIC 7 (E07): Training & Certification Tracking

**User Story 7.1: Manage Certifications for Employees**
- Title: Manage Employee Certifications (CRUD)
- User Story: As an HR user, I want to create, update, and delete certifications for employees so that compliance is tracked.
- Acceptance Criteria:
    - Given the certification API,
      When I perform CRUD operations,
      Then certifications are managed for each employee.
- Additional Details: Priority: High, Story Points: 3, Dependencies: E02, E03, Notes: Core certification flow.

**User Story 7.2: Alert on Expiring Certifications**
- Title: Alert Employees and Supervisors on Expiring Certifications
- User Story: As a system, I want to send alerts 30 and 7 days before a certification expires so that renewals are not missed.
- Acceptance Criteria:
    - Given a certification nearing expiry,
      When it is 30 or 7 days from expiration,
      Then an alert is sent to the employee and supervisor.
- Additional Details: Priority: High, Story Points: 2, Dependencies: 7.1, Notes: Prevents lapses.

**User Story 7.3: Block Assignment to Unqualified Tasks**
- Title: Block Assignment to Tasks Requiring Valid Certifications
- User Story: As a supervisor, I want the system to block assignment of employees to tasks requiring certifications if their certs are expired.
- Acceptance Criteria:
    - Given an assignment,
      When the employee's certification is expired,
      Then the assignment is blocked and a warning is shown.
- Additional Details: Priority: High, Story Points: 3, Dependencies: 7.1, Notes: Ensures safety.

**User Story 7.4: Upload Proof of Certification**
- Title: Upload Proof Documents for Certifications
- User Story: As an employee, I want to upload proof documents for my certifications so that compliance is documented.
- Acceptance Criteria:
    - Given a certification,
      When I upload a document,
      Then it is stored and linked to my certification record.
- Additional Details: Priority: High, Story Points: 2, Dependencies: 7.1, Notes: Supports audits.

**User Story 7.5: Display Certification Status on Profile**
- Title: Display Certification Status on Employee Profile
- User Story: As an employee or supervisor, I want to view certification status on the employee profile so that qualifications are visible.
- Acceptance Criteria:
    - Given an employee profile,
      When I view it,
      Then all current certifications and their statuses are displayed.
- Additional Details: Priority: High, Story Points: 2, Dependencies: 7.1, Notes: Improves transparency.
