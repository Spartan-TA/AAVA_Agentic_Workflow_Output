Title: New Hire Provisioning on HRIS Sync

User Story: As an HR manager I want new hires from the HRIS to be automatically provisioned with accounts and initial schedules so that onboarding is seamless

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Automatic new hire provisioning
Given a new hire record appears in the HRIS
When the sync job runs
Then the employee is created in the system with a user account and initial schedule

Additional Details:
- Priority: Medium
- Story Points: 3
- Dependencies: HRIS integration, account management, scheduling
- Notes: Must trigger onboarding workflow
