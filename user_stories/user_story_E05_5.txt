Title: Audit Entries for Schedule Changes

User Story: As a compliance officer I want all schedule changes to generate audit entries so that we can track who made changes and ensure accountability

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Auditing a schedule change
Given a shift assignment is created, updated, or deleted
When the change is saved
Then an audit entry should be generated with actor, timestamp, and before/after details

Additional Details:
- Priority: High
- Story Points: 2
- Dependencies: E02, E03, E04
- Notes: Audit log must be immutable and exportable by date/user/entity.
