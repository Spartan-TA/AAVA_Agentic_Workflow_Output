Title: Handle Blackout Dates and Operation Calendars

User Story: As a scheduler I want to define blackout dates and operation calendars So that shifts are not assigned on non-working days

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Adding blackout dates
Given I am on the calendar settings page
When I add a blackout date
Then the system should prevent shift assignments on those dates

Additional Details:
- Priority: Medium
- Story Points: 2
- Dependencies: Calendar module
- Notes: Support holidays, maintenance days, and custom events.
