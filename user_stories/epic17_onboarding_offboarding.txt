### EPIC 17 (E17): Onboarding & Offboarding Workflow

**User Story 17.1: Automate Provisioning for New Hires**
- Title: Automate Provisioning for New Hires from HRIS
- User Story: As an HR user, I want new hires from HRIS to be automatically provisioned with accounts, initial schedules, and required training so that onboarding is efficient.
- Acceptance Criteria:
    - Given a new hire in HRIS,
      When synced,
      Then an account, schedule, and training tasks are created.
- Additional Details: Priority: Medium, Story Points: 3, Dependencies: E02, E03, E05, E07, E09, E13, Notes: Reduces manual work.

**User Story 17.2: Generate Tasks for Training and Asset Assignment**
- Title: Generate Tasks for Training and Asset Assignment
- User Story: As a supervisor, I want onboarding tasks for training and asset assignment to be generated automatically so that nothing is missed.
- Acceptance Criteria:
    - Given a new hire,
      When onboarding starts,
      Then tasks for required training and asset assignment are created and tracked.
- Additional Details: Priority: Medium, Story Points: 2, Dependencies: 17.1, Notes: Ensures completeness.

**User Story 17.3: Automate Offboarding to Revoke Access and Collect Assets**
- Title: Automate Offboarding to Revoke Access and Collect Assets
- User Story: As an HR user, I want offboarding to automatically revoke system access, collect assigned assets, and update schedules so that terminations are secure and complete.
- Acceptance Criteria:
    - Given a termination,
      When offboarding is triggered,
      Then access is revoked, assets are flagged for return, and schedules are updated.
- Additional Details: Priority: Medium, Story Points: 3, Dependencies: 17.1, Notes: Reduces risk.
