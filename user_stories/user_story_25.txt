Title: Accrual Balance Tracking

User Story: As an employee I want to view my current leave accrual balances So that I can plan my time off responsibly

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Viewing accrual balances
Given I am logged into my account
When I navigate to the leave balance section
Then I should see my current PTO, sick leave, and other accrual balances

Scenario: Accrual update after leave approval
Given my leave request is approved
When the leave is processed
Then my accrual balance should be updated accordingly

Additional Details:
- Priority: Medium
- Story Points: 2
- Dependencies: Leave approval workflow, accrual calculation engine
- Notes: Balances should be updated in real-time and reflect pending requests.
