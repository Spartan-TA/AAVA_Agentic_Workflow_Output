Title: Assign Training to Employee

User Story: As a manager I want to assign mandatory training to employees So that compliance and skill requirements are met

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Manager assigns training
Given I am logged in as a manager
When I select an employee and assign a training module
Then the employee should receive a notification and the assignment should be tracked

Additional Details:
- Priority: High
- Story Points: 3
- Dependencies: Notifications module, Employee Master Data
- Notes: Training assignments should be visible in employee profiles.
