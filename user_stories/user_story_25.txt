Title: Manager Tracks Training Completion

User Story: As a manager I want to track the completion status of assigned training modules So that I can ensure compliance and identify gaps

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Viewing training completion status
Given I am logged in as a manager
When I access the training dashboard
Then I should see the completion status for all assigned modules by employee

Additional Details:
- Priority: High
- Story Points: 3
- Dependencies: Training assignment, employee profiles
- Notes: Provide export option for compliance reporting.
