Title: Manage Employee Certifications

User Story: As an HR administrator I want to create, update, and delete employee certifications so that compliance with required training and safety standards is tracked and maintained

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Certification CRUD operations
  Given I have HR admin access
  When I add, edit, or remove a certification for an employee
  Then the certification record is updated and visible on the employee profile

Additional Details:
- Priority: High
- Story Points: 3
- Dependencies: E02 (User Management), E03 (Scheduling)
- Notes: Certification types and expiration dates must be validated
