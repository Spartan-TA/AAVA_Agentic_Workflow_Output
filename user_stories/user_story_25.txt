Title: Incident Investigation Workflow

User Story: As a safety officer I want to manage the investigation workflow for safety incidents so that root causes are identified and corrective actions are tracked

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Investigate safety incident
Given an incident is logged
When I assign investigators and track investigation steps
Then the workflow status and findings should be recorded and reportable

Additional Details:
- Priority: High
- Story Points: 5
- Dependencies: Safety module, Workflow engine
- Notes: Support attachments and corrective action tracking.
