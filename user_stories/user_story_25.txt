Title: View Training and Certification Status

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Viewing training status
Given I am logged in
When I navigate to my training dashboard
Then I should see all completed, pending, and expired certifications
Additional Details:

- Story Points: 2
- Dependencies: Training records module
- Notes: Include alerts for upcoming expirations.
