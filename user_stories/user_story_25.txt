Title: Certification Status Visible on Employee Profile

User Story: As a supervisor or HR user I want to view the current certification status for each employee on their profile so that I can quickly verify qualifications and compliance

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Viewing certification status
Given I am viewing an employee profile
When I navigate to the certifications section
Then I see a list of all required certifications, their status (valid/expiring/expired), and any uploaded proof documents

Additional Details:
- Priority: Medium
- Story Points: 2
- Dependencies: Employee Master Data, Certification Upload
- Notes: Status should be color-coded for quick reference.
