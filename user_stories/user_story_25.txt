Title: Record Safety Incidents and Near-Misses
User Story: As a supervisor I want to record safety incidents and near-misses with severity, location, description, and involved employees so that we can track and investigate workplace safety issues
Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Record new incident
Given an incident occurs
When I submit details including severity, location, description, and involved employees
Then the incident is saved and visible in the safety dashboard
Scenario: Validation on submission
Given I am submitting an incident
When required fields are missing
Then the system displays validation errors
Additional Details:
- Priority: High
- Story Points: 3
- Dependencies: E02, E03
- Notes: Support both incidents and near-misses
