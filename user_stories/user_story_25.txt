Title: Publish Shift Schedules

User Story: As a scheduler I want to publish finalized shift schedules So that employees are notified and can prepare for their shifts

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Schedule publication
Given I have created or updated a shift schedule
When I publish the schedule
Then all affected employees should receive a notification and see the schedule in their dashboard

Additional Details:
- Priority: High
- Story Points: 2
- Dependencies: Notifications module
- Notes: Support for bulk notifications
