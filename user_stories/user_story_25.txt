Title: Certification Assignment Blocking
User Story: As a supervisor I want the system to block assignment of employees to equipment-sensitive tasks if their certifications are expired So that safety and compliance are maintained
Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Block unqualified assignment Given I attempt to assign an employee with an expired certification to a forklift shift When the system validates the assignment Then the action is blocked and a warning is displayed
Additional Details:
- Priority: High
- Story Points: 2
- Dependencies: Certification records, shift assignment
- Notes: Blocking logic should be enforced for all equipment-sensitive roles.
