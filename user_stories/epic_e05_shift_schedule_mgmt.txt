EPIC E05: Shift & Schedule Management

---

Title: Create Shift Templates

User Story: As an HR user I want to create recurring shift templates So that scheduling is efficient

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Template creation
Given I define a shift template
When I save it
Then it is available for assignment

Additional Details:
- Priority: High
- Story Points: 2
- Dependencies: Employee Master Data, RBAC, Time & Attendance
- Notes: Support rotations

---

Title: Assign Shifts to Employees

User Story: As a supervisor I want to assign shifts to employees So that staffing is adequate

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Shift assignment
Given a shift template exists
When I assign it to employees
Then assignments are visible in their schedules

Additional Details:
- Priority: High
- Story Points: 2
- Dependencies: Create Shift Templates
- Notes: Bulk-assign supported

---

Title: Detect and Prevent Scheduling Conflicts

User Story: As a scheduler I want conflicts detected and prevented So that double-booking is avoided

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Conflict detection
Given overlapping shifts
When I try to assign them
Then the system blocks the assignment

Additional Details:
- Priority: High
- Story Points: 2
- Dependencies: Assign Shifts to Employees
- Notes: Alert user on conflict

---

Title: Handle Blackout Dates and Calendars

User Story: As an HR user I want to manage blackout dates and operation calendars So that scheduling respects warehouse closures

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Blackout date management
Given blackout dates are set
When I schedule shifts
Then those dates are excluded

Additional Details:
- Priority: Medium
- Story Points: 1
- Dependencies: Create Shift Templates
- Notes: Calendar integration

---

Title: Audit Shift Assignments

User Story: As a compliance officer I want audit entries for shift assignments So that changes are traceable

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Audit logging
Given a shift assignment is made
When I view the audit log
Then actor, timestamp, and details are recorded

Additional Details:
- Priority: Medium
- Story Points: 1
- Dependencies: Assign Shifts to Employees
- Notes: Immutable log

---

Title: Workers View Personal Upcoming Shifts

User Story: As a worker I want to view my upcoming shifts So that I can plan my schedule

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Shift visibility
Given I am logged in
When I access my schedule
Then my assigned shifts are displayed

Additional Details:
- Priority: Medium
- Story Points: 1
- Dependencies: Assign Shifts to Employees
- Notes: Mobile-friendly view
