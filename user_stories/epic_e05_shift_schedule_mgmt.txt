Epic E05: Shift & Schedule Management

---

Title: Create Recurring Shift Templates

User Story: As an HR manager I want to define recurring shift templates So that scheduling is efficient and consistent

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Creating a weekly shift template
Given I am on the shift management page
When I create a template for Mon-Fri, 8am-4pm
Then the template is saved and available for assignment

Additional Details:
- Priority: High
- Story Points: 3
- Dependencies: E02, E03, E04
- Notes: Support daily, weekly, and custom recurrence

---

Title: Assign Shifts to Employees

User Story: As a supervisor I want to assign shifts to employees So that coverage and compliance are maintained

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Assigning a shift
Given a shift template exists
When I assign it to an employee
Then the employee's schedule is updated

Scenario: Blackout date enforcement
Given a blackout date is set
When I try to assign a shift on that date
Then the system prevents the assignment

Additional Details:
- Priority: High
- Story Points: 5
- Dependencies: E02, E03, E04
- Notes: Blackout dates configurable per site

---

Title: Configure Overtime Rules

User Story: As an HR manager I want to set overtime rules So that overtime pay is calculated according to policy

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Overtime calculation
Given an employee works more than 40 hours in a week
When payroll is processed
Then overtime hours are calculated and flagged

Additional Details:
- Priority: High
- Story Points: 3
- Dependencies: E02, E03, E04
- Notes: Support federal and local overtime regulations
