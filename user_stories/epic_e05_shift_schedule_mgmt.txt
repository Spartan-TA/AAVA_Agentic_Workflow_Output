EPIC E05: SHIFT & SCHEDULE MANAGEMENT

===========================================

Title: Shift Template Management

User Story: As a scheduler I want to create and manage recurring shift templates So that I can efficiently assign shifts to employees

Acceptance Criteria:

Scenario: Create shift template
Given valid shift details
When I POST to /shifts/templates
Then a new template is created

Scenario: Edit shift template
Given a template exists
When I PUT/PATCH to /shifts/templates/{id}
Then the template is updated

Scenario: Delete shift template
Given a template exists
When I DELETE /shifts/templates/{id}
Then the template is removed

Additional Details:
- Priority: High
- Story Points: 5
- Dependencies: Employee Master Data, Role-Based Access Control, Attendance
- Notes: Support recurring and rotating shifts

---

Title: Shift Assignment and Conflict Detection

User Story: As a supervisor I want to assign shifts and detect scheduling conflicts So that staffing is adequate and errors are prevented

Acceptance Criteria:

Scenario: Assign shift
Given employees and templates exist
When I assign shifts
Then conflicts are detected and prevented

Scenario: Bulk assignment
Given multiple employees
When I bulk-assign shifts
Then assignments are processed and audit entries generated

Additional Details:
- Priority: High
- Story Points: 8
- Dependencies: Shift Template Management
- Notes: Workers see personal upcoming shifts
