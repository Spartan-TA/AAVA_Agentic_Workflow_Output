Title: Automated Offboarding to Revoke Access, Collect Assets, and Update Schedules

User Story: As an HR manager I want employee offboarding to automatically revoke system access, collect assigned assets, and update schedules So that terminated employees are securely and efficiently removed from operations

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Offboarding automation
Given an employee is marked for termination
When offboarding is initiated
Then all system access is revoked, asset collection tasks are generated, and future schedules are updated to remove the employee

Additional Details:
- Priority: Medium
- Story Points: 5
- Dependencies: E02, E03, E05, E07, E09, E13
- Notes: Offboarding must be auditable and handle edge cases (e.g., missing assets).
