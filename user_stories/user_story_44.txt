Title: Schedule Performance Reviews

User Story: As an HR manager I want to schedule performance reviews for employees So that feedback and development plans are provided regularly

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Performance review scheduling
Given I am logged in as an HR manager
When I schedule a performance review
Then the employee and manager should be notified and the review added to the calendar

Additional Details:
- Priority: High
- Story Points: 3
- Dependencies: Employee database, notifications
- Notes: Support for recurring and ad-hoc reviews
