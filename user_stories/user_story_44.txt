Title: Announcements on Dashboard and Quiet Hours

User Story: As a user I want to view important announcements on my dashboard and configure quiet hours so that I am informed but not disturbed outside my preferred time

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Announcements are visible and quiet hours respected
  Given an announcement is published
  When I log in to my dashboard
  Then the announcement is visible until dismissed or expired
  And notifications are not sent during my configured quiet hours

Additional Details:
- Priority: Medium
- Story Points: 2
- Dependencies: E03, E05, E06, E07
- Notes: Quiet hours configurable per user; announcements must support rich text and expiry.
