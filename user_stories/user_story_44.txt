Title: User Notification Channel Preferences

User Story: As a worker I want to opt-in or opt-out of in-app, email, and SMS notifications so that I only receive relevant communications through my preferred channels

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Managing notification preferences
Given I am on the notification settings page
When I select or deselect notification channels for shift changes, approvals, or announcements
Then my preferences are saved and only selected channels are used for notifications

Additional Details:
- Priority: Medium
- Story Points: 3
- Dependencies: Notification service, user profile module
- Notes: Preferences must be respected for all notification types; default is all channels enabled
