### EPIC 8 (E08): Safety Incidents & OSHA Reporting

**User Story 8.1: Record Safety Incidents and Near-Misses**
- Title: Record Safety Incidents and Near-Misses
- User Story: As a supervisor, I want to record safety incidents and near-misses with severity, location, and involved employees so that safety data is tracked.
- Acceptance Criteria:
    - Given the safety incident form,
      When I submit a new incident,
      Then it is validated and recorded with all required fields.
- Additional Details: Priority: High, Story Points: 3, Dependencies: E02, E03, Notes: Core incident tracking.
