### EPIC E05: Shift & Schedule Management

**User Story 21**
Title: Create Shift Templates  
User Story: As an HR user I want to create recurring shift templates So that scheduling is efficient  
Acceptance Criteria:  
Scenario: Shift Template Creation  
Given I have HR role  
When I create a shift template  
Then the template is saved and reusable  
Additional Details:  
- Priority: High  
- Story Points: 2  
- Dependencies: E02, E03, E04  
- Notes: Support rotations

**User Story 22**
Title: Assign Shifts to Employees  
User Story: As a supervisor I want to assign shifts to employees So that staffing is adequate  
Acceptance Criteria:  
Scenario: Shift Assignment  
Given shift templates exist  
When I assign shifts  
Then employees see their upcoming shifts  
Additional Details:  
- Priority: High  
- Story Points: 2  
- Dependencies: User Story 21  
- Notes: Bulk-assign supported

**User Story 23**

Additional Details:  
- Priority: High  
- Story Points: 2
