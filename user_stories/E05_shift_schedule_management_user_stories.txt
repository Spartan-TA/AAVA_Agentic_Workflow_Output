EPIC E05: SHIFT & SCHEDULE MANAGEMENT

---
Title: Shift Template Management
User Story: As a scheduler I want to create and manage recurring shift templates So that staffing is consistent and efficient
Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Shift template CRUD
Given I have scheduler access
When I create, update, or delete a shift template
Then the template is saved and available for assignment

Additional Details:
- Priority: High
- Story Points: 4
- Dependencies: Employee CRUD, RBAC, Time & Attendance
- Notes: Support rotations and overtime rules

---
Title: Assign Shifts to Employees
User Story: As a supervisor I want to bulk-assign shifts to employees So that scheduling is streamlined
Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Bulk shift assignment
Given multiple employees are selected
When I assign shifts in bulk
Then assignments are created and conflicts are detected

Additional Details:
- Priority: High
- Story Points: 3
- Dependencies: Shift template management
- Notes: Prevent scheduling conflicts

---
Title: Blackout Dates and Calendar Integration
User Story: As a scheduler I want to manage blackout dates and operation calendars So that shifts are not assigned during warehouse closures
Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Blackout date enforcement
Given blackout dates are configured
When assigning shifts
Then the system prevents assignments on those dates

Additional Details:
- Priority: High
- Story Points: 2
- Dependencies: Shift template management
- Notes: Integrate with warehouse calendar
