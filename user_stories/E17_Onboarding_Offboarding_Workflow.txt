### E17: Onboarding & Offboarding Workflow

#### 1. Title: Automated Onboarding from HRIS
User Story: As an HR user I want new hires from HRIS to trigger onboarding tasks So that provisioning is automated
Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: New hire onboarding
Given a new hire appears in HRIS
When synced
Then onboarding tasks are generated for accounts, schedule, and training

Additional Details:
- Priority: Medium
- Story Points: 3
- Dependencies: E02, E03, E05, E07, E09, E13
- Notes: Notify responsible parties

---

#### 2. Title: Initial Schedule Assignment for New Hires
User Story: As a supervisor I want new hires to receive an initial schedule automatically So that they are ready to work
Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Assign schedule
Given a new hire
When onboarding is triggered
Then an initial schedule is assigned based on role and shift group

Additional Details:
- Priority: Medium
- Story Points: 2
- Dependencies: E02, E03, E05, E07, E09, E13
- Notes: Configurable templates

---

#### 3. Title: Required Training Assignment for New Hires
User Story: As a supervisor I want required training tasks assigned to new hires So that compliance is ensured
Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Assign training
Given a new hire
When onboarding is triggered
Then required training and certifications are assigned

Additional Details:
- Priority: Medium
- Story Points: 2
- Dependencies: E02, E03, E05, E07, E09, E13
- Notes: Track completion

---

#### 4. Title: Automated Offboarding on Termination
User Story: As an HR user I want terminated employees to be deprovisioned automatically So that access and assets are recovered
Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Offboarding triggered
Given a termination in HRIS
When synced
Then access is revoked, assets are collected, and schedules updated

Additional Details:
- Priority: Medium
- Story Points: 3
- Dependencies: E02, E03, E05, E07, E09, E13
- Notes: Notify IT and asset managers

---

#### 5. Title: Offboarding Task Tracking
User Story: As a supervisor I want to track completion of all offboarding tasks So that nothing is missed
Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Track tasks
Given an offboarding is in progress
When I view the checklist
Then all tasks and statuses are visible

Additional Details:
- Priority: Medium
- Story Points: 2
- Dependencies: E02, E03, E05, E07, E09, E13
- Notes: Escalate overdue tasks
