## E17 - Onboarding & Offboarding Workflow

### Story 1: Automated Onboarding from HRIS
- **User Story:** As an HR user, I want new hires from HRIS to be onboarded automatically so that accounts and schedules are provisioned.
- **Acceptance Criteria:**
    ```
    Given a new hire in HRIS
    When the sync job runs
    Then the employee is created in EMS and onboarding tasks are generated
    ```
- **Additional Details:**  
    Priority: Medium  
    Story Points: 4  
    Dependencies: E02, E03, E05, E07, E09, E13  
    Notes: Tasks include training and asset assignment.

### Story 2: Training and Asset Assignment Tasks
- **User Story:** As a Supervisor, I want onboarding tasks for training and asset assignment so that new hires are ready to work.
- **Acceptance Criteria:**
    ```
    Given a new hire
    When onboarding tasks are generated
    Then I can assign required training and assets
    ```
- **Additional Details:**  
    Priority: Medium  
    Story Points: 3  
    Dependencies: E02, E03, E05, E07, E09, E13  
    Notes: Task tracking required.

### Story 3: Automated Offboarding on Termination
- **User Story:** As an HR user, I want terminated employees to be offboarded automatically so that access is revoked and assets are collected.
- **Acceptance Criteria:**
    ```
    Given a termination in HRIS
    When the sync job runs
    Then the employee's access is revoked, assets are flagged for return, and schedules are updated
    ```
- **Additional Details:**  
    Priority: Medium  
    Story Points: 4  
    Dependencies: E02, E03, E05, E07, E09, E13  
    Notes: Integrate with asset and schedule modules.
