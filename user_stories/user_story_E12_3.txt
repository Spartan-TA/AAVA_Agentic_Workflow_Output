Title: Notifications for Approvals and Announcements

User Story: As a supervisor I want to send and receive notifications for approvals (leave, shift swaps) and company-wide announcements so that all relevant parties are informed promptly.

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Approval and announcement notification
Given an approval or announcement is created
When the event occurs
Then notifications should be sent to the appropriate users and visible on their dashboard

Additional Details:
- Priority: Medium
- Story Points: 4
- Dependencies: RBAC (E03), Leave & Absence Management (E06), Shift & Schedule Management (E05)
- Notes: Announcements must support scheduling and quiet hours configuration.
