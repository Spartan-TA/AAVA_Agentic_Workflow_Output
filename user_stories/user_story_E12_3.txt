Title: Dashboard Announcements and Quiet Hours

User Story: As a worker I want to see important announcements on my dashboard and configure quiet hours for notifications So that I stay informed without being disturbed during off-hours

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Announcement visibility and quiet hours
Given an announcement is published by HR or supervisor
When I log into the dashboard
Then the announcement is visible and notifications respect my configured quiet hours

Additional Details:
- Priority: Medium
- Story Points: 2
- Dependencies: E03, E05, E06, E07
- Notes: Announcements must be localized; quiet hours configurable per user.
