Title: Generate AI-Assisted Shift Schedules

User Story: As a scheduler I want the system to generate AI-assisted shift schedules so that workforce coverage is optimized and manual effort is reduced

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Generating AI-assisted schedule
Given I am logged in as a scheduler
When I request an AI-generated schedule for a location and date range
Then the system proposes a schedule based on staffing rules, availability, and demand

Additional Details:
- Priority: High
- Story Points: 5
- Dependencies: Scheduling module, AI engine
- Notes: Scheduler can review and adjust the proposed schedule before publishing.
