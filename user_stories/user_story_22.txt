Title: Approve or Deny Leave Request

User Story: As a supervisor I want to review and approve or deny employee leave requests so that I can ensure adequate shift coverage and compliance with policy

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Supervisor reviews leave request
  Given I am a supervisor with pending leave requests
  When I view a request and select approve or deny
  Then the employee is notified and the leave balance and schedule are updated accordingly

Additional Details:
- Priority: Medium
- Story Points: 2
- Dependencies: E02 (User Management), E03 (Scheduling), E05 (Attendance)
- Notes: Approval/denial reasons must be logged
