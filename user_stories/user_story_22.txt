Title: Manage Shift Rotations

User Story: As a scheduling manager I want to configure shift rotations So that employees are assigned to different shifts in a fair and predictable manner

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Creating a rotation pattern
  Given I am on the schedule management page
  When I define a rotation pattern and assign employees
  Then the system should automatically rotate assignments per the pattern
Scenario: Editing a rotation
  Given a rotation pattern exists
  When I update its details
  Then future assignments should reflect the changes

Additional Details:
- Priority: Medium
- Story Points: 3
- Dependencies: Shift templates, employee profiles
- Notes: Rotations should support weekly, bi-weekly, and custom intervals.
