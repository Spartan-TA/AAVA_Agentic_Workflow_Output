Title: Leave Balance Update
User Story: As an employee I want my leave balance to update automatically after requests are approved or denied So that I always see my current available time off
Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Balance update Given a leave request is approved or denied When the action is completed Then my leave balance reflects the change and is visible on my profile
Additional Details:
- Priority: Medium
- Story Points: 1
- Dependencies: Leave approval workflow
- Notes: Balances should be recalculated in real time and support accrual policies.
