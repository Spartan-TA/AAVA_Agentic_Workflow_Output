Title: Certification Expiry Alerts
User Story: As an HR manager I want to receive alerts for certifications expiring in 30 or 7 days so that I can proactively manage renewals and compliance
Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Alert for expiring certification
Given an employee has a certification expiring in 30 or 7 days
When the system checks certification dates
Then an alert is sent to the HR manager and employee
Scenario: No alert for valid certifications
Given a certification is not expiring soon
When the system checks certification dates
Then no alert is sent
Additional Details:
- Priority: High
- Story Points: 2
- Dependencies: E02, E03
- Notes: Alerts should be configurable for timing
