Title: Certification Expiry Alerts

User Story: As an HR administrator I want to receive alerts for upcoming certification expiries so that renewals are not missed

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Certification expiry alert
Given a certification is expiring within 30 days
When the system checks certification dates
Then an alert should be sent to the employee and HR administrator

Additional Details:
- Priority: High
- Story Points: 2
- Dependencies: Notification module, Certification tracking
- Notes: Configurable alert window.
