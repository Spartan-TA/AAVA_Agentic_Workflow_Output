Title: Employee Uploads Certification Proof

User Story: As an employee I want to upload proof of my required certifications so that my qualifications are validated and tracked for compliance

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Uploading certification proof
Given I am logged in as an employee
When I upload a document for a required certification
Then the document is stored, linked to my profile, and visible to supervisors and HR

Additional Details:
- Priority: High
- Story Points: 2
- Dependencies: Employee Master Data, RBAC
- Notes: Supported file types and size limits enforced.
