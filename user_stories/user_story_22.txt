Title: Training Completion Reporting

User Story: As a training coordinator I want to generate reports on training completion So that I can monitor compliance and identify gaps

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Generate training completion report
Given training records exist
When I run a completion report
Then I should see a summary of completed, pending, and overdue trainings by employee

Additional Details:
- Priority: Medium
- Story Points: 5
- Dependencies: Training management module, reporting system
- Notes: Exportable to CSV and PDF.
