EPIC E13: Integration Layer (HRIS/WMS APIs)

---

Title: Integration Layer - HRIS Sync
User Story: As a System I want to sync new hires and terminations from HRIS So that employee data is always current.
Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: HRIS sync job
Given new hires/terms in HRIS
When the sync job runs
Then employees are created/updated in EMS
Additional Details:
- Priority: High
- Story Points: 5
- Dependencies: E01, E02, E03
- Notes: Idempotent sync, OpenAPI documented
