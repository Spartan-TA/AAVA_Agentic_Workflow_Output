EpicID: E05
Title: Shift & Schedule Management
User Story: As a supervisor, I want to create shift templates and assign schedules so that staffing is optimized and conflicts are avoided.
Acceptance Criteria:
  Scenario: Create Shift Template
    Given shift details
    When I POST to /shifts/templates
    Then a new template is created
  Scenario: Assign Schedule
    Given employees and templates
    When I assign schedules
    Then conflicts are detected and prevented
  Scenario: View Personal Shifts
    Given a worker
    When they view their schedule
    Then upcoming shifts are displayed
  Scenario: Bulk Assignment
    Given a supervisor
    When they bulk-assign shifts
    Then assignments are created and audited
Additional Details:
  Priority: High
  Story Points: 13
  Dependencies: E02, E03, E04
  Notes: Audit entries must be generated
