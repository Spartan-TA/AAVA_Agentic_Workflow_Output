Title: Automate Offboarding Workflow for Access and Asset Revocation

User Story: As an HR manager I want offboarding to automatically revoke access and collect assets So that terminated employees no longer have system access and assets are returned

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Automated offboarding
Given an employee is terminated
When offboarding is initiated
Then system access is revoked, assets are collected, and schedules updated

Additional Details:
- Priority: Medium
- Story Points: 5
- Dependencies: E02, E03, E05, E07, E09, E13
- Notes: Offboarding tasks must be tracked and completed within 24 hours.
