Title: Auditability of Onboarding and Offboarding Actions

User Story: As a compliance officer I want all onboarding and offboarding actions to be logged so that we can demonstrate compliance and investigate issues

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Auditing onboarding/offboarding
Given onboarding or offboarding actions are performed
When I review the audit log
Then I see entries for each action with actor, timestamp, and outcome

Additional Details:
- Priority: Medium
- Story Points: 2
- Dependencies: Audit log service, onboarding/offboarding workflows
- Notes: Log entries must be exportable for compliance review
