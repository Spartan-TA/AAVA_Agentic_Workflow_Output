Title: Generate Operational Reports
User Story: As a supervisor I want to generate operational reports for attendance, overtime, and leave So that I can make data-driven decisions
Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Report Generation
Given operational data
When I request a report
Then it is generated with filters and exported in CSV/PDF
Additional Details:
- Priority: Medium
- Story Points: 3
- Dependencies: Attendance, Shift assignment, Leave management, Certification tracking, Safety incidents, Audit logging
- Notes: Exports within 10s for 50k rows
