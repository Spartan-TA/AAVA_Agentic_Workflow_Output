### E15: Reporting & Analytics

**User Story 1**
- Title: Attendance and Overtime Reports
- User Story: As an operations manager, I want reports on attendance and overtime so that I can optimize staffing.
- Acceptance Criteria in Gherkin format:
  Scenario: Generate attendance report
  Given attendance data exists
  When I filter by date, department, or shift
  Then the report is generated and exportable as CSV/PDF
- Additional Details:
  * Priority: Medium
  * Story Points: 3
  * Dependencies: E04, E05, E06, E07, E08, E14
  * Notes: Export within 10s for 50k rows.

**User Story 2**
- Title: Certification and Safety KPI Dashboards
- User Story: As a safety manager, I want dashboards for certifications and safety KPIs so that compliance is visible.
- Acceptance Criteria in Gherkin format:
  Scenario: View certification/safety dashboard
  Given certification and incident data exists
  When I access the dashboard
  Then I see up-to-date metrics with role-based access
- Additional Details:
  * Priority: Medium
  * Story Points: 2
  * Dependencies: E04, E05, E06, E07, E08, E14
  * Notes: Metrics endpoints for BI.
