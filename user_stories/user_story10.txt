Title: Calculate Hours Worked

User Story: As a payroll manager I want the system to automatically calculate hours worked from clock in/out data So that payroll processing is accurate

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Hours worked calculated
  Given employees have clocked in and out
  When I view payroll or attendance reports
  Then the system should display total hours worked for each employee

Additional Details:
- Priority: High
- Story Points: 3
- Dependencies: Clock in/out endpoints
- Notes: Handle overnight shifts and time zone differences.
