Title: Performance Reviews & Goals

User Story: As a supervisor I want to create and manage performance reviews and goals for employees so that structured feedback and productivity improvements are tracked.

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Performance review cycle
Given a review template is available
When I assign and submit a review for an employee
Then the employee can acknowledge the review
And the workflow is immutable after sign-off
And reviews can be exported as PDF
And visibility is role-based

Additional Details:
- Priority: Medium
- Story Points: 5
- Dependencies: Employee Master Data (CRUD), Role-Based Access Control (RBAC)
- Notes: Support quarterly/annual cycles, competencies, ratings, comments, and history tracking.
