### E10 â Performance Reviews & Goals

**User Story 1: Create Review Templates**
- **User Story:** As an HR user, I want to define review templates so that performance reviews are standardized.
- **Acceptance Criteria:**
  - Given template details,
  - When I create a template,
  - Then it is available for review cycles.
- **Additional Details:** Priority: Medium; Story Points: 2; Dependencies: E02, E03; Notes: Support quarterly/annual.

**User Story 2: Assign Reviews to Employees**
- **User Story:** As a supervisor, I want to assign reviews to my team so that feedback is structured.
- **Acceptance Criteria:**
  - Given a review cycle,
  - When I assign employees,
  - Then reviews are created and visible to both parties.
- **Additional Details:** Priority: Medium; Story Points: 2; Dependencies: E10-1; Notes: Notify employees.

**User Story 3: Submit and Acknowledge Reviews**
- **User Story:** As a supervisor/employee, I want to submit and acknowledge reviews so that the process is complete.
- **Acceptance Criteria:**
  - Given a review,
  - When both parties sign off,
  - Then the review is locked and archived.
- **Additional Details:** Priority: Medium; Story Points: 2; Dependencies: E10-2; Notes: Immutable after sign-off.

**User Story 4: Track Goals and Competencies**
- **User Story:** As a supervisor, I want to track goals and competencies so that development is monitored.
- **Acceptance Criteria:**
  - Given a review,
  - When goals are set/updated,
  - Then progress is visible in the review.
- **Additional Details:** Priority: Medium; Story Points: 2; Dependencies: E10-2; Notes: Support comments and ratings.

**User Story 5: Export Reviews to PDF**
- **User Story:** As an HR user, I want to export reviews to PDF so that records are kept for compliance.
- **Acceptance Criteria:**
  - Given a completed review,
  - When I export,
  - Then a PDF is generated with all details.
- **Additional Details:** Priority: Medium; Story Points: 1; Dependencies: E10-3; Notes: Include signatures.
