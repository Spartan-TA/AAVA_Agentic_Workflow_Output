Title: Record Employee Clock-In

User Story: As an employee I want to clock in at the start of my shift So that my attendance is accurately tracked

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Clock-in
Given I am on the time & attendance page
When I click the clock-in button
Then my clock-in time should be recorded in the system

Additional Details:
- Priority: High
- Story Points: 2
- Dependencies: Time & Attendance module
- Notes: Prevent multiple clock-ins for the same shift.
