Title: Performance Reviews & Goals

User Story: As an HR manager I want to create and manage performance reviews so that employee feedback and development are documented.

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Review cycle
Given a review period is open
When I assign and complete reviews
Then employees and supervisors acknowledge, PDF export is available, and history is immutable after sign-off

Additional Details:
- Priority: Medium
- Story Points: 5
- Dependencies: Employee Master Data, RBAC
- Notes: Track goals, competencies, ratings, comments
