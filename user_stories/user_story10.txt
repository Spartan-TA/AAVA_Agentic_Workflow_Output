Title: Manage Performance Reviews & Goals

User Story: As an HR manager I want to create quarterly and annual review templates, track goals and competencies, and manage supervisor/employee acknowledgements so that structured feedback improves productivity and retention.

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Create review cycle
  Given review templates exist
  When I assign a review cycle to employees
  Then the cycle is created and visible to both supervisor and employee

Scenario: Submit and acknowledge review
  Given a review cycle is active
  When supervisor and employee submit ratings and comments
  Then both parties acknowledge the review and it becomes immutable

Scenario: PDF export
  Given reviews are completed
  When I export a review
  Then the review is available as a PDF document

Scenario: Role-based visibility
  Given reviews exist
  When I access reviews as HR, supervisor, or employee
  Then visibility is restricted based on role

Additional Details:
- Priority: Medium
- Story Points: 5
- Dependencies: Employee Master Data, Role-Based Access Control
- Notes: Immutable history after sign-off; competencies and goals must be tracked.
