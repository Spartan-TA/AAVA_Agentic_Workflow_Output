Title: Time & Attendance Clock-In

User Story: As a warehouse worker I want to clock in with device and location capture so that my attendance is accurately recorded.

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Clock-in event
Given I am scheduled for a shift
When I POST to /attendance/clock-in
Then my clock-in is validated and recorded with device info

Additional Details:
- Priority: High
- Story Points: 3
- Dependencies: E02,E03
- Notes: Geofence optional.
