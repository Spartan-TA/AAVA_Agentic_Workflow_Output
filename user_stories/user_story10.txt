Title: Performance Reviews & Goals
User Story: As a supervisor I want to create and manage performance reviews and goals for employees so that structured feedback and productivity improvements are tracked.
Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Review cycle management
Given I am authenticated as a supervisor
When I create or assign review cycles and goals
Then employees can submit and acknowledge reviews
And the workflow supports PDF export
And role-based visibility is enforced
And review history is immutable after sign-off
Additional Details:
- Priority: Medium
- Story Points: 5
- Dependencies: Employee Master Data (CRUD), Role-Based Access Control (RBAC)
- Notes: Templates for quarterly/annual reviews must be supported; competencies and ratings tracked.
