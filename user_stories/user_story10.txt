Title: Shift & Schedule Management
User Story: As a supervisor I want to create and assign recurring shift templates So that scheduling is efficient and consistent
Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Create shift template
Given I am a supervisor
When I POST a new shift template
Then the template is saved and available for assignment

Scenario: Bulk-assign shifts
Given a group of employees
When I assign a shift template
Then all selected employees are scheduled accordingly

Additional Details:
- Priority: High
- Story Points: 5
- Dependencies: Employee CRUD API, RBAC, Attendance
- Notes: Detect and prevent scheduling conflicts.
