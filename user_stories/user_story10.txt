Title: Time & Attendance Clock In/Out

User Story: As a warehouse worker I want to clock in and out using my device So that my working hours are accurately recorded for payroll and compliance

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Successful clock-in
Given I am scheduled for a shift and at the warehouse location
When I clock in using the app
Then my attendance event is recorded and associated with my shift

Scenario: Missed punch correction
Given I missed a clock-in or clock-out
When I submit a correction request
Then an approval task is created for my supervisor

Additional Details:
- Priority: High
- Story Points: 5
- Dependencies: Employee Master Data, RBAC
- Notes: Geofence validation optional; exportable attendance reports; automatic shift association
