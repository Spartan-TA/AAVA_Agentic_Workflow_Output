Title: Record Safety Incident or Near-Miss

User Story: As a supervisor I want to record safety incidents and near-misses so that investigations and corrective actions can be tracked

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Recording an incident
Given an incident occurs in the warehouse
When I submit a report with severity, location, and involved employees
Then the incident is logged and status is set to Open

Additional Details:
- Priority: High
- Story Points: 2
- Dependencies: Employee master data, RBAC
- Notes: Validation required for all fields
