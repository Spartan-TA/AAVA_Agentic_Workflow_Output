Title: Clock In/Out Event Recording
User Story: As a warehouse worker I want to clock in and out via the system So that my attendance is accurately recorded
Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Successful clock-in and clock-out
Given I am authenticated and within my scheduled shift
When I submit a clock-in or clock-out event
Then the event is recorded and associated with my shift

Additional Details:
- Priority: High
- Story Points: 3
- Dependencies: Employee CRUD, RBAC
- Notes: Device and geofence capture optional
