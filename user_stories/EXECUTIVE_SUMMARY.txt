## 1. TRANSFORMATION PROCESS OVERVIEW

The transformation from high-level epics to actionable user stories followed a rigorous process:

- **Epic Decomposition:** Each epic was analyzed for core business objectives and functional scope.
- **User Story Creation:** Stories were written in the "As a [role], I want [functionality], so that [benefit]" format, ensuring clarity and alignment with stakeholder needs.
- **Acceptance Criteria:** Each story includes Gherkin-style scenarios (Given, When, Then) for testability and completeness.
- **Additional Details:** Priority (High/Medium/Low), Story Points (Fibonacci 1â13), Dependencies, and Notes were captured for each story.
- **Quality Assurance:** Stories were reviewed for completeness, clarity, and alignment with industry best practices.

7. Reserve 20% capacity for technical debt and bug fixes
