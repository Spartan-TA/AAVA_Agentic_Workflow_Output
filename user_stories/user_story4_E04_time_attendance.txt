## E04: Time & Attendance (Clock In/Out)

### Story 1: Implement Clock-In/Clock-Out Endpoints
- **User Story:** As a worker, I want to clock in and out via API so that my attendance is accurately recorded.
- **Acceptance Criteria (Gherkin):**
  ```
  Scenario: Valid Clock-In
    Given I am within geofence and on shift
    When I POST to /attendance/clock-in
    Then my clock-in is recorded with timestamp and device info

  Scenario: Valid Clock-Out
    Given I am clocked in
    When I POST to /attendance/clock-out
    Then my clock-out is recorded
  ```
- **Priority:** High
- **Story Points:** 5
- **Dependencies:** E02, E03
- **Notes:** Geofence optional.

### Story 2: Calculate Hours Worked per Shift
- **User Story:** As a payroll admin, I want hours worked to be calculated per shift so that payroll is accurate.
- **Acceptance Criteria (Gherkin):**
  ```
  Given a worker has clocked in and out
  When I view their attendance record
  Then total hours worked for the shift are displayed
  ```
- **Priority:** High
- **Story Points:** 3
- **Dependencies:** E04-1
- **Notes:** Handle overnight shifts.

### Story 3: Handle Missed Punches and Corrections
- **User Story:** As a supervisor, I want to correct missed punches so that attendance records are accurate.
- **Acceptance Criteria (Gherkin):**
  ```
  Scenario: Missed Punch Correction
    Given a worker missed a clock-in/out
    When a correction is submitted
    Then an approval task is generated for supervisor
    And upon approval, the record is updated
  ```
- **Priority:** High
- **Story Points:** 5
- **Dependencies:** E04-1
- **Notes:** Audit corrections.

### Story 4: Export Attendance Reports
- **User Story:** As an HR user, I want to export attendance data as CSV so that I can analyze and share records.
- **Acceptance Criteria (Gherkin):**
  ```
  Given attendance data exists
  When I request an export
  Then a CSV file is generated with clock-in/out and hours worked
  ```
- **Priority:** High
- **Story Points:** 2
- **Dependencies:** E04-2
- **Notes:** Export endpoint required.
