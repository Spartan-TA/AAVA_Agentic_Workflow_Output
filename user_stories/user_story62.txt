Title: Notify Users of Expiring Certifications

User Story: As an employee I want to receive notifications when my certifications are about to expire So that I can renew them on time and remain compliant

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Certification expiry notification
Given my certification is nearing expiry
When the system detects the upcoming expiration
Then I receive a notification via my preferred channels

Additional Details:
- Priority: Medium
- Story Points: 3
- Dependencies: E03, E05, E06, E07
- Notes: Notification templates must be localized.
