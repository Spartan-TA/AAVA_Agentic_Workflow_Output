EPIC E07: Training & Certification Tracking

---

Title: Training & Certification - Add Certification
User Story: As an HR I want to add certifications to employee profiles So that compliance is tracked.
Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Add certification
Given an employee profile
When I add a certification
Then the certification appears with expiry date and document upload
Additional Details:
- Priority: High
- Story Points: 3
- Dependencies: E02, E03
- Notes: Supports document upload

---

Title: Training & Certification - Expiry Alerts
User Story: As a SUPERVISOR I want to receive alerts for expiring certifications So that renewals are not missed.
Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Certification expiry alert
Given a certification is expiring in 30 or 7 days
When the system checks expirations
Then an alert is sent to the supervisor and employee
Additional Details:
- Priority: High
- Story Points: 2
- Dependencies: E02, E03
- Notes: Alerts via in-app and email

---

Title: Training & Certification - Block Unqualified Assignment
User Story: As a System I want to block assignment of staff to tasks requiring expired certifications So that only qualified staff are assigned.
Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Block assignment
Given a required certification is expired
When I assign the employee to a task
Then the system prevents the assignment and shows an error
Additional Details:
- Priority: High
- Story Points: 5
- Dependencies: E02, E03
- Notes: Enforced in scheduling and asset assignment

---

Title: Training & Certification - Certification Status View
User Story: As an HR I want to view certification status on employee profiles So that I can audit compliance.
Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: View certification status
Given an employee profile
When I view certifications
Then I see all current and expired certifications with status
Additional Details:
- Priority: High
- Story Points: 2
- Dependencies: E02, E03
- Notes: Status visible to HR and supervisors
