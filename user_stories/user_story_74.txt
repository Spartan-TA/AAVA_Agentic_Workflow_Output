Title: Onboard New Employees

User Story: As an HR manager I want to onboard new employees So that they have access to required systems, training, and resources from day one

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Employee onboarding
Given a new employee is hired
When I initiate the onboarding process
Then the employee should receive access credentials, training assignments, and equipment as needed

Additional Details:
- Priority: High
- Story Points: 4
- Dependencies: User management, training, equipment assignment
- Notes: Support for onboarding checklist
