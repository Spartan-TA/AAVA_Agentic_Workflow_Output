Title: Notifications & Announcements - Notification Preferences

User Story: As a user, I want to opt-in/out of in-app, email, and SMS notifications so that I control my communication channels.

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Preference update
Given notification settings
When I update preferences
Then notifications are sent only via selected channels

Additional Details:
- Priority: Medium
- Story Points: 2
- Dependencies: RBAC, Shift Management, Leave Management, Certification Tracking
- Notes: Channel support
