Title: Notification Delivery
User Story: As an employee I want to receive notifications for shift changes and expiring certifications So that I am informed of important updates
Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Notification delivery
Given a shift change or certification expiration
When the event occurs
Then the system should send in-app and email/SMS notifications
Additional Details:
- Priority: Medium
- Story Points: 2
- Dependencies: RBAC, Shift management, Leave management, Certification tracking
- Notes: Support opt-in/out preferences
