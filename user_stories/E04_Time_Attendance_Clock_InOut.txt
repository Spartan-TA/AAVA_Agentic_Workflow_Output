### E04: Time & Attendance (Clock In/Out)

#### 1. Title: Employee Clock-In Endpoint
User Story: As a worker I want to clock in at the start of my shift So that my attendance is recorded
Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Successful clock-in
Given I am scheduled for a shift
When I POST to `/attendance/clock-in` with my badgeId
Then my clock-in time is recorded and associated with my shift

Scenario: Geofence validation
Given I am outside the warehouse geofence
When I attempt to clock in
Then I receive a 400 error

Additional Details:
- Priority: High
- Story Points: 3
- Dependencies: E02, E03
- Notes: Capture device info

---

#### 2. Title: Employee Clock-Out Endpoint
User Story: As a worker I want to clock out at the end of my shift So that my worked hours are calculated
Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Successful clock-out
Given I am clocked in
When I POST to `/attendance/clock-out`
Then my clock-out time is recorded and hours worked are computed

Scenario: Missed clock-in
Given I did not clock in
When I try to clock out
Then I receive an error and instructions to request correction

Additional Details:
- Priority: High
- Story Points: 2
- Dependencies: E02, E03
- Notes: Validate shift association

---

#### 3. Title: Handle Missed Punches and Corrections
User Story: As a worker I want to request corrections for missed punches So that my attendance is accurate
Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Submit correction
Given I missed a clock-in/out
When I submit a correction request
Then an approval task is created for my supervisor

Scenario: Supervisor approval
Given a pending correction
When the supervisor approves
Then the attendance record is updated

Additional Details:
- Priority: High
- Story Points: 3
- Dependencies: E02, E03
- Notes: Track correction history

---

#### 4. Title: Attendance Reports Export
User Story: As an HR user I want to export attendance data to CSV So that I can analyze and share reports
Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Export report
Given attendance records exist
When I request a CSV export
Then a file is generated with all relevant fields

Additional Details:
- Priority: High
- Story Points: 2
- Dependencies: E02, E03
- Notes: Include filters for date, department

---

#### 5. Title: Automatic Shift Association
User Story: As a worker I want my clock-in/out events automatically linked to my scheduled shift So that hours are calculated correctly
Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Shift association
Given I clock in during my scheduled shift
When the event is recorded
Then it is linked to the correct shift

Additional Details:
- Priority: High
- Story Points: 2
- Dependencies: E02, E03
- Notes: Handle overlapping shifts
