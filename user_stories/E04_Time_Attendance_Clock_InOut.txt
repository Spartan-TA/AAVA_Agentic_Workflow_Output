## E04 - Time & Attendance (Clock In/Out)

### Story 1: Clock-In Endpoint
- **User Story:** As a Worker, I want to clock in at the start of my shift so that my attendance is recorded.
- **Acceptance Criteria:**
    ```
    Given a valid worker and shift
    When I POST to '/attendance/clock-in' with my badgeId
    Then my clock-in time is recorded and associated with my shift

    Given an invalid badgeId
    When I attempt to clock in
    Then the request is rejected
    ```
- **Additional Details:**  
    Priority: High  
    Story Points: 3  
    Dependencies: E02, E03  
    Notes: Optionally capture device and geofence.

### Story 2: Clock-Out Endpoint
- **User Story:** As a Worker, I want to clock out at the end of my shift so that my worked hours are tracked.
- **Acceptance Criteria:**
    ```
    Given a valid clock-in event
    When I POST to '/attendance/clock-out'
    Then my clock-out time is recorded and hours worked are calculated

    Given no prior clock-in
    When I attempt to clock out
    Then the request is rejected with an error
    ```
- **Additional Details:**  
    Priority: High  
    Story Points: 3  
    Dependencies: E02, E03  
    Notes: Handle missed punches.

### Story 3: Missed Punch Correction Workflow
- **User Story:** As a Worker, I want to request corrections for missed punches so that my attendance is accurate.
- **Acceptance Criteria:**
    ```
    Given a missed clock-in or clock-out
    When I submit a correction request
    Then a task is created for supervisor approval

    Given a supervisor
    When I approve or reject a correction
    Then the attendance record is updated accordingly
    ```
- **Additional Details:**  
    Priority: High  
    Story Points: 5  
    Dependencies: E02, E03  
    Notes: Approval workflow required.

### Story 4: Attendance Reporting
- **User Story:** As a Supervisor, I want to export attendance reports so that I can review and share attendance data.
- **Acceptance Criteria:**
    ```
    Given attendance records exist
    When I request a report export (CSV)
    Then I receive a file with daily totals per employee
    ```
- **Additional Details:**  
    Priority: Medium  
    Story Points: 2  
    Dependencies: E02, E03  
    Notes: Include shift association.
