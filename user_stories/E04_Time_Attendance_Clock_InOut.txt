### E04: Time & Attendance (Clock In/Out)

**User Story 1**
Title: Employee Clock-In Endpoint

User Story: As a WORKER I want to clock in at the start of my shift So that my attendance is recorded

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Valid clock-in
Given my shift has started
When I POST to /attendance/clock-in with my badgeId
Then my clock-in time is recorded

Additional Details:
- Priority: High
- Story Points: 2
- Dependencies: E02, E03
- Notes: Geofence validation optional

---

**User Story 2**
Title: Employee Clock-Out Endpoint

User Story: As a WORKER I want to clock out at the end of my shift So that my work hours are tracked

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Valid clock-out
Given I am clocked in
When I POST to /attendance/clock-out
Then my clock-out time is recorded and hours are calculated

Additional Details:
- Priority: High
- Story Points: 2
- Dependencies: E02, E03
- Notes: Prevent double clock-out

---

**User Story 3**
Title: Handle Missed Punches

User Story: As a SUPERVISOR I want to correct missed punches So that attendance records are accurate

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Missed punch correction
Given a worker missed a clock-in
When I submit a correction request
Then an approval task is created for review

Additional Details:
- Priority: High
- Story Points: 3
- Dependencies: E02, E03
- Notes: Corrections must be auditable

---

**User Story 4**
Title: Attendance Report Export

User Story: As an HR I want to export attendance data to CSV So that I can analyze attendance trends

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Exporting attendance
Given attendance records exist
When I request a CSV export
Then a CSV file is generated with all relevant data

Additional Details:
- Priority: High
- Story Points: 2
- Dependencies: E02, E03
- Notes: Include filters for date range

---

**User Story 5**
Title: Geofence Validation for Clock-In

User Story: As a WORKER I want to be prevented from clocking in outside the warehouse geofence So that only on-site attendance is recorded

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Clock-in outside geofence
Given I am outside the allowed location
When I attempt to clock in
Then I receive an error and my attendance is not recorded

Additional Details:
- Priority: High
- Story Points: 3
- Dependencies: E02, E03
- Notes: Geofence is optional but must be configurable
