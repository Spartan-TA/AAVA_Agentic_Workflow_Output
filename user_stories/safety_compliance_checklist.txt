Title: Safety Compliance Checklist

User Story: As a warehouse employee, I want to complete safety compliance checklists So that I can ensure my work area meets safety standards.

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Checklist completion
Given a safety checklist assigned to me
When I complete and submit the checklist
Then the safety officer is notified and the checklist is archived

Additional Details:
- Priority: Medium
- Story Points: 2
- Dependencies: Safety Incident Reporting
- Notes: Checklist templates configurable
