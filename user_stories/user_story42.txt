Title: Search and Export Audit Logs

User Story: As a compliance officer I want to search and export audit logs so that I can support investigations and compliance reporting

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Searching and exporting logs
Given I am logged in as a compliance officer
When I search audit logs by user, date, or action
Then I see matching entries and can export the results in CSV or PDF format

Additional Details:
- Priority: High
- Story Points: 2
- Dependencies: Audit trail module
- Notes: Exported logs must include all relevant metadata.
