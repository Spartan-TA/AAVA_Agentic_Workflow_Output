Title: Alert for Expiring Certifications

User Story: As an employee I want to receive alerts before my certifications expire So that I can renew them in time and avoid being blocked from assignments

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Certification expiry alert
Given my certification is set to expire in 30 or 7 days
When the system detects the upcoming expiry
Then I receive an in-app and email notification with renewal instructions

Additional Details:
- Priority: High
- Story Points: 3
- Dependencies: E02, E03
- Notes: Alerts should be sent to both employee and supervisor.
