Title: Asset Return and Reassignment

User Story: As an asset manager I want to record the return and reassignment of equipment so that asset inventory remains accurate

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Returning and reassigning an asset
Given an employee returns an asset
When I record the return and assign it to another employee
Then the asset assignment history is updated and both employees are notified

Additional Details:
- Priority: Medium
- Story Points: 3
- Dependencies: Notification module, asset assignment
- Notes: Maintain a full audit trail of asset assignments and returns.
