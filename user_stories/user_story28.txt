Title: Supervisor Assigns Equipment
User Story: As a supervisor I want to assign equipment and assets to employees So that resources are distributed efficiently and tracked
Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Assign equipment
Given I am logged into the system as a supervisor
When I select an employee and assign equipment
Then the assignment is logged and the employee is notified
Additional Details:
- Priority: High
- Story Points: 2
- Dependencies: Asset management module, notifications
- Notes: Assignment history should be tracked for compliance.
