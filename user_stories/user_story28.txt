Title: Upload Certification Proof Documents

User Story: As an employee I want to upload proof documents for my certifications so that HR can verify my qualifications and maintain compliance records.

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Upload certification proof
Given I have a new or renewed certification
When I upload the proof document to my profile
Then the document is stored securely and linked to the certification record

Additional Details:
- Priority: High
- Story Points: 3
- Dependencies: E02, E03
- Notes: Support for PDF, image formats; access restricted to HR and employee.
