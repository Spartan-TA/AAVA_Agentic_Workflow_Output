Title: Record Safety Incidents and Near-Misses

User Story: As a safety officer I want to record incidents and near-misses with severity, location, and involved employees So that I can track safety events and initiate investigations

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Safety incident recording
Given I am on the safety incident form
When I submit details about an incident or near-miss
Then the record is saved and available for investigation workflow

Additional Details:
- Priority: High
- Story Points: 2
- Dependencies: Employee Master Data, RBAC
- Notes: Form should validate required fields and support attachments.
