Title: Assign Training Modules

User Story: As a supervisor I want to assign training modules to employees So that required certifications are tracked and completed

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Assigning training
Given I am a supervisor
When I select employees and assign a training module
Then the employees should be notified and the assignment tracked

Additional Details:
- Priority: High
- Story Points: 2
- Dependencies: Notification system
- Notes: Support recurring and ad-hoc training assignments.
