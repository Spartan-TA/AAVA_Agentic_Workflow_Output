Title: Safety Incident Workflow

User Story: As a safety officer I want a workflow for incident review and follow-up So that incidents are investigated, resolved, and tracked to closure

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Assign incident for review
  Given an incident is recorded
  When I assign it to a reviewer
  Then the reviewer should be notified and the assignment logged
Scenario: Record follow-up actions
  Given an incident is under review
  When follow-up actions are taken
  Then they should be documented in the incident record
Scenario: Close incident
  Given all actions are complete
  When I mark the incident as closed
  Then its status should be updated and archived

Additional Details:
- Priority: High
- Story Points: 3
- Dependencies: Safety module, workflow engine
- Notes: Support for notifications and audit trail
