Title: Scheduling Integration for Coverage Needs

User Story: As a scheduler I want leave requests to automatically flag coverage gaps So that I can proactively assign replacements and maintain operational continuity

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Coverage gap flagged
Given an employee submits a leave request
When the system detects a shift without coverage
Then a coverage gap notification should be sent to the scheduler

Scenario: No coverage gap
Given an employee submits a leave request
When the system detects all shifts are covered
Then no notification is sent

Additional Details:
- Priority: High
- Story Points: 3
- Dependencies: Scheduling module, notification system
- Notes: Coverage gaps should be visible on the scheduling dashboard.
