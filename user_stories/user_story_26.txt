Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Shift attendance reporting
Given a shift has ended
When I submit attendance for the shift
Then the system should update attendance records and notify payroll

Additional Details:

- Story Points: 2
- Dependencies: Attendance module, shift schedule
- Notes: Support for bulk attendance reporting
