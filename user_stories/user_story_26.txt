Title: Record Safety Incident with Validation

User Story: As a supervisor or safety officer I want to record safety incidents and near-misses with required details so that all events are documented for investigation and compliance

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Recording a safety incident
Given I am logged in as a supervisor or safety officer
When I submit a new incident report with severity, location, description, and involved employees
Then the system validates required fields and saves the incident with status 'Open'

Additional Details:
- Priority: High
- Story Points: 3
- Dependencies: Employee Master Data, RBAC
- Notes: Validation for required fields and duplicate detection.
