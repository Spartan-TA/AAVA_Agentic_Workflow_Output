Title: Certification Expiry Alerts

User Story: As an employee I want to receive alerts when my required certifications are about to expire so that I can renew them in time and remain eligible for assignments

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Certification expiry notification
  Given my certification is expiring in 30 or 7 days
  When the system checks certification statuses
  Then I receive an alert via my chosen notification channel

Additional Details:
- Priority: High
- Story Points: 2
- Dependencies: E02 (User Management), E03 (Scheduling)
- Notes: Alerts must be sent for all required certifications
