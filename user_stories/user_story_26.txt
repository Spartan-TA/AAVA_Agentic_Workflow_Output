Title: Safety Incident Recording
User Story: As a supervisor I want to record safety incidents and near-misses with details So that investigations and corrective actions can be tracked for compliance
Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Record incident Given I am on the safety module When I enter incident details and submit Then the incident is logged and assigned a status for follow-up
Additional Details:
- Priority: High
- Story Points: 2
- Dependencies: Employee master data, safety module
- Notes: Incident records should support severity, location, and involved employees.
