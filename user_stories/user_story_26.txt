Title: Employee Reports Safety Incident

User Story: As an employee I want to report a safety incident So that hazards are documented and addressed promptly

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Submitting a safety incident report
Given I am logged in as an employee
When I fill out and submit the safety incident form
Then the incident should be logged and the safety officer notified

Additional Details:
- Priority: High
- Story Points: 3
- Dependencies: Employee master data, notifications
- Notes: Support photo/document upload with report.
