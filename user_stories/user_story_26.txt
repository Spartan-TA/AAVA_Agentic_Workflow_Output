Title: Track Certification Expiry

User Story: As an HR administrator I want to track employee certification expiry dates So that I can ensure all required certifications are current

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Certification expiry alert
Given an employee's certification is nearing expiry
When the expiry date is within 30 days
Then the system should notify the employee and their manager

Additional Details:
- Priority: High
- Story Points: 3
- Dependencies: Notifications module, Employee Master Data
- Notes: Expiry thresholds should be configurable.
