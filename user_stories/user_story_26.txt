Title: Record Safety Incident

User Story: As a safety manager I want to record incidents and near-misses with severity, location, description, and involved employees so that we can track and investigate workplace safety events.

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Incident creation and validation
Given I am on the incident reporting page
When I submit incident details including severity, location, description, and involved employees
Then the system should validate the input and create a new incident record with status 'Open'

Additional Details:
- Priority: High
- Story Points: 5
- Dependencies: E02, E03
- Notes: Must support both incidents and near-misses; validation required for mandatory fields.
