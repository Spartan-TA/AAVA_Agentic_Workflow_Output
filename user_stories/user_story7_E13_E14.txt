Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Audit log
Given a create/update/delete action

Additional Details:
Priority: High
Story Points: 2
Dependencies: E03
Notes: Exportable by date/user/entity

Additional Details:
Priority: High
Story Points: 2
Dependencies: E03
Notes: Hashing or blockchain-based

Story Points: 1
