Title: Delete Employee Record

User Story: As an HR manager I want to delete employee records So that I can remove staff who are no longer with the company

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Employee record deletion
Given I am viewing an employee record
When I select delete and confirm
Then the employee record should be removed from the system and not appear in the employee list

Additional Details:
- Priority: Medium
- Story Points: 2
- Dependencies: Employee record creation
- Notes: Require confirmation and log deletion for audit
