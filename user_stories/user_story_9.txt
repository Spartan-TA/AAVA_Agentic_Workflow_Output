Title: Create Shift Templates
User Story: As an HR manager I want to create recurring shift templates so that scheduling is efficient and consistent
Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Shift template creation
Given I have access to the scheduling module
When I create a new shift template with required fields
Then the template is saved and available for assignment
Additional Details:
- Priority: High
- Story Points: 3
- Dependencies: Employee Master Data, RBAC
- Notes: Templates support rotations and overtime rules

User Story: As a supervisor I want to assign shifts to employees so that staffing needs are met
Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Bulk shift assignment
Given a list of employees and available shifts
When I assign shifts in bulk
Then employees receive their schedules and conflicts are detected/prevented
Additional Details:
- Priority: High
- Story Points: 3
- Dependencies: Employee Master Data, RBAC, Attendance
- Notes: Audit entries generated for assignments

- Priority: High

Title: Prevent Scheduling Conflicts

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Conflict detection
Given I attempt to assign overlapping shifts
When the system checks for conflicts
Then I am notified and the assignment is blocked
Additional Details:
- Priority: High
- Story Points: 2
- Dependencies: Scheduling module
- Notes: Conflict logic covers blackout dates and overtime
