Title: Employee List Filtering & Pagination

User Story: As an HR manager I want to filter and paginate the employee list so that I can efficiently find and manage employee records in large warehouses.

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Filter and paginate employees
Given I am authenticated as HR and on the employee list page
When I apply filters (e.g., department, status) and navigate pages
Then the results update accordingly and performance remains acceptable for large datasets

Additional Details:
- Priority: High
- Story Points: 2
- Dependencies: Employee Master Data (E02)
- Notes: Support filtering by department, status, and role; page size configurable.
