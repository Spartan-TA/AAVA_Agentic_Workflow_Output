Title: Assign and Track Equipment & Assets

User Story: As a warehouse supervisor I want to assign equipment and assets to employees So that asset accountability and safe operation are ensured

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Asset checkout
Given an employee with valid certification
When they check out an asset
Then the asset is assigned and history is logged

Additional Details:
- Priority: Medium
- Story Points: 5
- Dependencies: E02, E03, E07
- Notes: Block checkout if certification is missing; overdue returns reportable
