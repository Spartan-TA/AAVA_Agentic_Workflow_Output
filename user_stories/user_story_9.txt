Title: Support Pagination and Filtering for Employee Records

User Story: As an HR manager I want to paginate and filter employee records So that I can efficiently find and manage employees in large organizations.

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Paginated employee list
  Given more than 50 employees
  When I GET /employees?page=1&size=20
  Then I receive 20 employee records and pagination metadata
Scenario: Filter by department
  Given employees in multiple departments
  When I GET /employees?department=Shipping
  Then only employees in the Shipping department are returned
Scenario: Combined filters
  Given employees with various statuses and roles
  When I GET /employees?status=active&role=SUPERVISOR
  Then only active supervisors are returned

Additional Details:
- Priority: High
- Story Points: 3
- Dependencies: user_story_6.txt
- Notes: Filtering must support department, role, status, and shiftGroup.
