Title: Clock In/Out Endpoint
User Story: As a warehouse worker I want to clock in and out via a secure endpoint So that my attendance is accurately recorded for payroll.
Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Clock in event
  Given I am scheduled to work
  When I POST to /attendance/clock-in with my badgeId
  Then my clock-in time is recorded

Scenario: Clock out event
  Given I am clocked in
  When I POST to /attendance/clock-out
  Then my clock-out time is recorded and shift hours calculated

Scenario: Geofence validation (optional)
  Given geofencing is enabled
  When I clock in/out outside the allowed area
  Then the request is rejected

Additional Details:
- Priority: High
- Story Points: 5
- Dependencies: E02, E03
- Notes: Device capture included if available.
