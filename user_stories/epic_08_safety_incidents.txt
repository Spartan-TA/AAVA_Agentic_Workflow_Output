Title: Safety Incidents & OSHA Reporting - Record Safety Incident
User Story: As a supervisor I want to record safety incidents and near-misses So that investigations and reporting are possible

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Record incident
Given valid incident details
When I POST to /safety/incidents
Then the incident is recorded with status Open

Additional Details:
- Priority: High
- Story Points: 2
- Dependencies: Employee CRUD, RBAC
- Notes: Severity, location, description required

Title: Safety Incidents & OSHA Reporting - Incident Investigation Workflow
User Story: As a safety officer I want to manage the investigation workflow for incidents So that corrective actions are tracked

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Investigate incident
Given an incident is Open
When I update status to Investigating
Then investigation details are recorded

Scenario: Resolve incident
Given investigation is complete
When I update status to Resolved
Then corrective actions are documented

Additional Details:
- Priority: High
- Story Points: 2
- Dependencies: Incident recording
- Notes: Workflow must be auditable
