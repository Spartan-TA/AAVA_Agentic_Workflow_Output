Title: Handle Missed Punches and Corrections Workflow

User Story: As a warehouse employee I want to request corrections for missed clock-in/out events so that my attendance records are accurate and payroll is correct

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Employee requests correction
Given an employee notices a missed punch
When they submit a correction request
Then a supervisor is notified to approve or deny the correction and the attendance record is updated accordingly

Additional Details:
- Priority: High
- Story Points: 2
- Dependencies: Clock-in/out event recording, RBAC
- Notes: Corrections must create approval tasks and be auditable.
