Title: Missed Punch Notification
User Story: As an employee I want to receive notifications when I miss a clock-in or clock-out So that I can promptly correct my attendance records
Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Missed punch alert Given I fail to clock-in or clock-out for a scheduled shift When the system detects the missed punch Then I receive an in-app and email notification with instructions to submit a correction
Additional Details:
- Priority: High
- Story Points: 2
- Dependencies: Attendance module, notification system
- Notes: Notifications should be configurable by channel.
