Title: Audit Role Changes

User Story: As a compliance officer I want to audit all role changes So that I can ensure regulatory compliance and traceability

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Role change audit
Given role changes occur in the system
When I access the audit log
Then I should see details of who made the change, when, and what was changed

Additional Details:
- Priority: Medium
- Story Points: 2
- Dependencies: Role management, audit trail module
- Notes: Audit logs should be immutable
