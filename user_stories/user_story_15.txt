Title: Employee Clock Out

User Story: As an employee I want to clock out at the end of my shift So that my work hours are accurately tracked

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Successful clock out
Given I am clocked in for my shift
When I access the clock out page and submit my clock out
Then my attendance record should show the correct clock out time and calculate total hours worked

Additional Details:
- Priority: High
- Story Points: 3
- Dependencies: Clock in functionality
- Notes: Prevent clock out if not clocked in.
