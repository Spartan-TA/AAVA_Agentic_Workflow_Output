Title: Supervisor Approval of Attendance Corrections

User Story: As a supervisor I want to review and approve or deny attendance correction requests so that only valid changes are made to employee attendance records

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Approve or deny correction
Given there are pending attendance correction requests for my team
When I review the request details and supporting information
Then I can approve or deny the request, and the employee is notified of the outcome

Additional Details:
- Priority: High
- Story Points: 2
- Dependencies: Employee Master Data, RBAC
- Notes: All actions should be logged for audit; approvals update attendance and trigger recalculation of hours
