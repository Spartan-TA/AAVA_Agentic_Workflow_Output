Title: Detect and Prevent Scheduling Conflicts

User Story: As a scheduler I want the system to detect and prevent scheduling conflicts so that employees are not double-booked

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Schedule conflict detection
Given I am assigning a shift
When there is a conflict with an existing assignment
Then the system should alert me and prevent the conflict

Additional Details:
- Priority: High
- Story Points: 5
- Dependencies: Scheduling module
- Notes: Include overlapping shifts, overtime, and policy violations.
