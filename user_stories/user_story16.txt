Title: Bulk Assignment of Shifts and Audit Entries

User Story: As a supervisor I want to bulk-assign shifts to multiple employees and ensure audit entries are generated so that staffing changes are efficient and traceable

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Bulk assign shifts
Given a list of employees and a shift template
When I perform a bulk assignment
Then all selected employees are assigned the shift and audit entries are created for each change

Additional Details:
- Priority: High
- Story Points: 2
- Dependencies: Shift templates, Employee schedules
- Notes: Audit entries must include actor, timestamp, and before/after state.
