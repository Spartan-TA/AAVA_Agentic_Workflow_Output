Title: View Upcoming Shifts

User Story: As a worker I want to view my upcoming shifts So that I can plan my work schedule

Acceptance Criteria:

Scenario: Worker sees personal shift schedule
Given I am logged in as a worker
When I access my schedule
Then I see a list of my upcoming shifts

Scenario: Supervisor sees team shifts
Given I am a supervisor
When I access the team schedule
Then I see upcoming shifts for my team members

Additional Details:
- Priority: Medium
- Story Points: 3
- Dependencies: Shift Templates and Schedules
- Notes: Support mobile and web views; filter by date range
