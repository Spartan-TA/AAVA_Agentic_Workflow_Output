Title: Leave Request Submission
User Story: As an employee I want to request PTO, sick, or unpaid leave So that my absences are tracked and approved
Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Leave request submission
Given I am authenticated as an employee
When I submit a leave request
Then the request is recorded and pending approval

Additional Details:
- Priority: Medium
- Story Points: 2
- Dependencies: Employee CRUD, RBAC, Shift Management
- Notes: Accrual balances must be checked
