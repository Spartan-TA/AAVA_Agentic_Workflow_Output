Title: Manager Assigns Equipment

User Story: As a manager I want to assign equipment and assets to employees So that resources are tracked and accountability is maintained

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Assigning equipment
Given I am logged in as a manager and have access to the asset inventory
When I select an employee and assign equipment
Then the assignment is logged and the employee receives a notification

Additional Details:
- Priority: Medium
- Story Points: 3
- Dependencies: Employee Master Data, Notifications
- Notes: Track asset return dates and condition.
