Title: Mobile Access (PWA)

User Story: As a warehouse worker I want to access core workflows (clock-in/out, schedules, leave requests, announcements) on mobile devices so that I can perform tasks easily from anywhere, even offline.

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Mobile usability and offline support
Given I have installed the PWA on my device
When I use core flows (clock-in/out, view schedules, request leave, see announcements)
Then the experience is responsive and offline-friendly
And clock events are queued offline and resolved on reconnect
And the PWA manifest is installable
And Lighthouse PWA score is at least 80

Additional Details:
- Priority: Medium
- Story Points: 8
- Dependencies: Time & Attendance (Clock In/Out), Shift & Schedule Management, Leave & Absence Management, Notifications & Announcements
- Notes: Ensure core flows are usable on mobile and offline.
