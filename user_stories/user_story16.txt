Title: Create Recurring Shift Templates

User Story: As an HR manager I want to create recurring shift templates So that I can efficiently schedule employees and reduce manual entry

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Shift template creation
Given I am on the shift management page
When I create a new shift template with recurrence rules
Then the template should be saved and available for future assignments

Additional Details:
- Priority: High
- Story Points: 3
- Dependencies: Employee Master Data, RBAC
- Notes: Support daily, weekly, and custom recurrence options.
