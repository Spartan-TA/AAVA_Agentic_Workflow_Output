Title: Mobile Access (PWA)

User Story: As a warehouse worker I want to access core workflows (clock-in/out, schedules, leave requests, announcements) on my mobile device so that I can perform my job efficiently from anywhere in the warehouse.

Acceptance Criteria:

Scenario: Responsive views
Given I access the application on a mobile device
When I use core workflows
Then all views are responsive and usable

Scenario: PWA installation
Given I am on a supported device
When I add the app to my home screen
Then it installs as a PWA with a manifest

Scenario: Offline clock events
Given I am offline
When I clock in or out
Then the event is queued and synced with conflict resolution when online

Scenario: PWA quality score
Given the PWA is deployed
When I run Lighthouse
Then the PWA score is 80 or higher

Additional Details:
- Priority: Medium
- Story Points: 8
- Dependencies: Time & Attendance (E04), Shift & Schedule Management (E05), Leave & Absence Management (E06), Notifications & Announcements (E12)
- Notes: Core flows must be offline-friendly and installable.
