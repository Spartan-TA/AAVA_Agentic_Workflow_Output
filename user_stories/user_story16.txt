Title: Employee Request Shift Swap

User Story: As an employee I want to request a shift swap with a coworker so that I can manage personal commitments without disrupting coverage

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Requesting and approving a shift swap
Given I am assigned to a shift
When I submit a shift swap request to a coworker
Then the coworker and supervisor are notified and the swap is pending approval

Additional Details:
- Priority: Medium
- Story Points: 3
- Dependencies: Notification module, supervisor approval
- Notes: All swaps must be logged and require supervisor approval.
