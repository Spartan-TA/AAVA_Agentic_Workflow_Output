Title: Performance Reviews & Goals

User Story: As a supervisor I want to conduct structured performance reviews and set goals for employees So that feedback is documented and productivity is improved

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Create review cycle
Given a new review period is starting
When I assign review templates to employees
Then the system tracks goals, competencies, and ratings

Scenario: Submit and acknowledge review
Given a review is completed
When supervisor and employee submit acknowledgements
Then the review is locked and history is immutable

Additional Details:
- Priority: Medium
- Story Points: 6
- Dependencies: Employee Master Data, RBAC
- Notes: PDF export supported; role-based visibility; comments and ratings tracked
