Title: Register and Track Equipment Assets

User Story: As an asset manager I want to register and track equipment assets so that accountability and maintenance are ensured

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Registering a new asset
Given I have a new piece of equipment
When I enter its details into the system
Then it is added to the asset registry and available for assignment

Additional Details:
- Priority: Medium
- Story Points: 2
- Dependencies: Employee Master Data, Certification Tracking
- Notes: Asset types include scanners, forklifts, PPE

---

Title: Assign Equipment to Employees

User Story: As a supervisor I want to assign equipment to employees so that usage and responsibility are tracked

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Assigning equipment
Given an asset is available
When I assign it to an employee
Then the assignment is logged and the employee's profile is updated

Additional Details:
- Priority: Medium
- Story Points: 2
- Dependencies: Asset registry, Employee data
- Notes: Certification check required for certain equipment

---

Title: Prevent Assignment if Certification Missing

User Story: As a scheduler I want the system to block assignment of equipment if required certifications are missing so that only qualified employees use sensitive assets

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Assignment with missing certification
Given an employee lacks the required certification
When I attempt to assign restricted equipment
Then the system blocks the assignment and notifies me

Additional Details:
- Priority: Medium
- Story Points: 2
- Dependencies: Certification tracking, Asset assignment
- Notes: Applies to forklifts and other regulated assets

---

Title: Track Asset Condition and Maintenance

User Story: As an asset manager I want to track the condition and maintenance history of equipment so that assets are kept in safe working order

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Updating asset condition
Given an asset is returned or inspected
When I update its condition status
Then the maintenance history is logged and alerts are generated for required service

Additional Details:
- Priority: Medium
- Story Points: 2
- Dependencies: Asset registry
- Notes: Condition states: Good, Needs Service, Out of Order

Title: Asset Check-In/Check-Out Workflow

User Story: As an employee I want to check in and out equipment so that usage is tracked and overdue returns are flagged

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Checking out an asset
Given I am assigned equipment
When I check it out
Then the system logs the event and tracks the expected return date

Additional Details:
- Priority: Medium
- Story Points: 2
- Dependencies: Asset registry, Employee data
- Notes: Overdue returns trigger notifications
