#### User Story 16: Assign Shifts to Employees
**As a** supervisor  
**I want** to bulk-assign shifts to employees  
**so that** staffing is managed effectively.

**Acceptance Criteria:**
- **Given** a list of employees and a shift template,  
  **When** I assign shifts,  
  **Then** assignments are created and conflicts are detected/prevented.

**Additional Details:**  
Priority: High  
Story Points: 3  
Dependencies: E05  
Notes: Generate audit entries for assignments.
