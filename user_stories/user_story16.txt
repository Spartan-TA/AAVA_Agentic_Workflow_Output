Title: Assign Equipment to Employees
User Story: As an asset manager I want to assign equipment to employees So that asset tracking is accurate

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Equipment assignment
Given equipment inventory exists
When I assign equipment to an employee
Then the assignment is logged and visible in the employee profile

Additional Details:
- Priority: Medium
- Story Points: 2
- Dependencies: Employee Master Data (CRUD)
- Notes: Track assignment and return dates
