Title: Assign Equipment to Employee
User Story: As a supervisor I want to assign equipment and PPE to employees So that accountability and safety are maintained
Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Assign equipment Given an employee is qualified and available When I assign equipment Then the asset is checked out and linked to the employee
Additional Details:
- Priority: Medium
- Story Points: 2
- Dependencies: Employee Master Data, RBAC, Certification Tracking
- Notes: Blocks assignment if certs invalid

Additional Details:
- Priority: Medium
- Story Points: 1

- Notes: History log per asset
