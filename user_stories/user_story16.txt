Title: Create Performance Review Cycle
User Story: As an HR manager I want to create quarterly and annual review cycles so that structured feedback is collected
Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Review cycle creation
Given I am on the review management page
When I create a new cycle
Then it is available for assignment to employees
Additional Details:
- Priority: Medium
- Story Points: 2
- Dependencies: E02, E03
- Notes: Supports templates

Title: Assign Reviews to Employees
User Story: As a supervisor I want to assign performance reviews to my team so that goals and competencies are tracked
Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Assign review
Given a review cycle is active
When I assign reviews to employees
Then they receive notifications and can submit responses
Additional Details:
- Priority: Medium
- Story Points: 2
- Dependencies: E02, E03
- Notes: Role-based visibility

Title: Submit and Acknowledge Review
User Story: As an employee I want to submit my review and acknowledge supervisor feedback so that the process is transparent
Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Submit/acknowledge review
Given a review is assigned
When I submit my self-assessment and acknowledge feedback
Then the review is marked complete and history is saved
Additional Details:
- Priority: Medium
- Story Points: 2
- Dependencies: E02, E03
- Notes: Immutable after sign-off
