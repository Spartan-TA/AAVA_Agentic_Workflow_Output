### Epic E05: Shift & Schedule Management

**User Story 1**
Title: Create Shift Templates
User Story: As an HR user, I want to create recurring shift templates and rotations so that scheduling is efficient.
Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Shift Template Creation
Given shift details
When I POST to /shifts/templates
Then a new template is created and available for assignment
Priority: High
Story Points: 3
Dependencies: E02, E03, E04
Notes: Supports rotations.

**User Story 2**
Title: Assign Shifts to Employees
User Story: As a supervisor, I want to assign shifts to employees and handle blackout dates so that staffing is adequate.
Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Shift Assignment
Given a shift template and employee
When I assign a shift
Then conflicts are detected and prevented
Priority: High
Story Points: 3
Dependencies: E02, E03, E04
Notes: Blackout dates enforced.

**User Story 3**
Title: Bulk Shift Assignment
User Story: As a supervisor, I want to bulk-assign shifts to multiple employees so that scheduling is faster.
Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Bulk Assignment
Given a list of employees
When I bulk-assign a shift
Then all assignments are created and conflicts logged
Priority: High
Story Points: 2
Dependencies: E02, E03, E04
Notes: Audit entries generated.

**User Story 4**
Title: View Personal Upcoming Shifts
User Story: As a worker, I want to view my upcoming shifts so that I can plan my attendance.
Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Shift Viewing
Given assigned shifts
When I access my schedule
Then I see all upcoming shifts
Priority: High
Story Points: 2
Dependencies: E02, E03, E04
Notes: Role-based visibility.
