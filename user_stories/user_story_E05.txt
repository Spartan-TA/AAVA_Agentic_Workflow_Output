### E05 - Shift & Schedule Management

**User Story 1: CRUD for Shift Templates**
- Title: Manage Shift Templates
- Narrative: As a supervisor, I want to create, update, and delete shift templates so that scheduling is efficient.
- Acceptance Criteria:
  ```
  Given shift templates,
  When I perform CRUD operations,
  Then templates are created, updated, and deleted as needed.
  ```
- Priority: High
- Story Points: 3
- Dependencies: E02, E03, E04
- Notes: Scheduling foundation.

**User Story 2: Assign Shifts to Employees**
- Title: Assign Shifts to Employees
- Narrative: As a supervisor, I want to assign shifts to employees so that staffing is planned.
- Acceptance Criteria:
  ```
  Given a shift template,
  When I assign employees,
  Then assignments are saved and visible to workers.
  ```
- Priority: High
- Story Points: 2
- Dependencies: Story 1
- Notes: Staffing.

**User Story 3: Conflict Detection and Prevention**
- Title: Detect and Prevent Scheduling Conflicts
- Narrative: As a scheduler, I want conflicts detected and prevented so that no employee is double-booked.
- Acceptance Criteria:
  ```
  Given shift assignments,
  When a conflict is detected,
  Then the system prevents the assignment and notifies the user.
  ```
- Priority: High
- Story Points: 2
- Dependencies: Story 2
- Notes: Reliability.

**User Story 4: Bulk Assignment and Audit Trail**
- Title: Bulk Assign Shifts and Generate Audit Entries
- Narrative: As a supervisor, I want to bulk-assign shifts and have audit entries generated so that changes are tracked.
- Acceptance Criteria:
  ```
  Given multiple employees,
  When I bulk-assign shifts,
  Then assignments are made and audit entries are logged.
  ```
- Priority: High
- Story Points: 2
- Dependencies: Story 2
- Notes: Efficiency and compliance.

**User Story 5: Personal Shift Views**
- Title: Personal Upcoming Shift Views
- Narrative: As a worker, I want to view my upcoming shifts so that I can plan my schedule.
- Acceptance Criteria:
  ```
  Given my user account,
  When I view my schedule,
  Then I see all upcoming shifts assigned to me.
  ```
- Priority: High
- Story Points: 1
- Dependencies: Story 2
- Notes: Worker usability.
