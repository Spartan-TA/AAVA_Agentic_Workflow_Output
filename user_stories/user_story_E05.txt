===========================================
User Story 1: Create and Manage Shift Templates
===========================================

Title: Create and Manage Shift Templates

User Story: As an HR manager, I want to create, edit, and delete recurring shift templates so that I can standardize scheduling across the warehouse.

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Shift template creation
Given I am on the shift management page
When I enter shift details (start/end time, recurrence, role requirements) and save
Then the new template appears in the list and is available for assignment

Additional Details:
- Priority: High
- Story Points: 5
- Dependencies: Employee CRUD, RBAC, Time & Attendance
- Notes: Templates support daily/weekly recurrences

===========================================
User Story 2: Assign Shifts to Employees
===========================================

Title: Assign Shifts to Employees

User Story: As a supervisor, I want to assign shifts to employees individually or in bulk so that I can ensure adequate staffing.

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Bulk shift assignment
Given a list of employees and a shift template
When I select employees and assign the template
Then all selected employees receive the shift and conflicts are detected/prevented

Additional Details:
- Priority: High
- Story Points: 8
- Dependencies: Employee CRUD, RBAC, Time & Attendance
- Notes: Prevents double-booking and respects blackout dates

===========================================
User Story 3: View Personal Upcoming Shifts
===========================================

Title: View Personal Upcoming Shifts

User Story: As a worker, I want to view my upcoming shifts so that I can plan my attendance.

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Viewing schedule
Given I am logged in
When I navigate to my schedule
Then I see a list/calendar of my assigned shifts

Additional Details:
- Priority: High
- Story Points: 3
- Dependencies: Employee CRUD, RBAC
- Notes: Mobile-friendly view required

===========================================
User Story 4: Manage Blackout Dates and Warehouse Calendars
===========================================

Title: Manage Blackout Dates and Warehouse Calendars

User Story: As an HR manager, I want to define blackout dates and operation calendars so that shifts are not scheduled during closures.

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Blackout date enforcement
Given a defined blackout date
When a supervisor attempts to assign a shift on that date
Then the system blocks the assignment and displays an error

Additional Details:
- Priority: Medium
- Story Points: 5
- Dependencies: Employee CRUD, RBAC
- Notes: Blackout dates visible to all schedulers

===========================================
User Story 5: Audit Shift Assignments
===========================================

Title: Audit Shift Assignments

User Story: As an auditor, I want all shift assignments and changes to be logged so that I can review scheduling history for compliance.

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Audit entry creation
Given a shift assignment or change
When the action is performed
Then an audit entry is generated with actor, timestamp, and before/after details

Additional Details:
- Priority: High
- Story Points: 3
- Dependencies: Audit Trail & Compliance
- Notes: Audit logs exportable by date/employee
