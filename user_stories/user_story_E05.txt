### E05 - Shift & Schedule Management

**User Story 5.1: Create Recurring Shift Templates**
User Story: As an HR user I want to create recurring shift templates So that I can efficiently manage schedules.

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Shift template creation
Given valid shift details
When I POST to /shifts/templates
Then a recurring shift template is created

Additional Details:
- Priority: High
- Story Points: 3
- Dependencies: E02, E03, E04
- Notes: Supports daily, weekly, and custom recurrences.

---

**User Story 5.2: Assign Shifts to Employees**
User Story: As a supervisor I want to assign shifts to employees So that staffing is ensured.

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Shift assignment
Given a shift template and employee list
When I assign shifts
Then employees receive their schedules and conflicts are detected/prevented

Additional Details:
- Priority: High
- Story Points: 3
- Dependencies: E05.1
- Notes: Bulk assignment supported.

---

**User Story 5.3: Handle Blackout Dates and Calendars**
User Story: As a scheduler I want to define blackout dates and operation calendars So that shifts are not assigned on holidays or closures.

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Blackout date handling
Given defined blackout dates
When generating schedules
Then no shifts are assigned on those dates

Additional Details:
- Priority: High
- Story Points: 2
- Dependencies: E05.1
- Notes: Blackout dates visible to all users.

---

**User Story 5.4: Employee Shift View**
User Story: As a worker I want to view my upcoming shifts So that I can plan my attendance.

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: View personal shifts
Given I am logged in
When I access my schedule
Then I see all my upcoming assigned shifts

Additional Details:
- Priority: High
- Story Points: 2
- Dependencies: E05.2
- Notes: Mobile-friendly view required.

---

**User Story 5.5: Audit Trail for Schedule Changes**
User Story: As an admin I want audit entries for all schedule changes So that compliance is maintained.

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Schedule change audit
Given a shift is created/modified/deleted
When the action occurs
Then an audit entry is generated with actor, timestamp, and details

Additional Details:
- Priority: High
- Story Points: 2
- Dependencies: E05.1
- Notes: Integrates with centralized audit log.
