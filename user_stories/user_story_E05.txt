### EPIC E05 - Shift & Schedule Management

**User Story 1**

Title: Create Recurring Shift Templates

User Story: As an HR user, I want to create recurring shift templates so that scheduling is consistent and efficient.

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Shift Template Creation
Given shift parameters
When I create a template
Then it is available for assignment and recurrence

Additional Details:
- Priority: High
- Story Points: 3
- Dependencies: E02, E03, E04
- Notes: Templates must support weekly/daily recurrence

---

**User Story 2**

Title: Assign Shifts to Employees

User Story: As a supervisor, I want to assign shifts to employees in bulk so that staffing is managed efficiently.

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Bulk Assignment
Given a list of employees and a shift template
When I assign shifts
Then assignments are created and audit entries generated

Additional Details:
- Priority: High
- Story Points: 5
- Dependencies: E02, E03, E04
- Notes: Audit trail required

---

**User Story 3**

Title: Detect and Prevent Scheduling Conflicts

User Story: As a scheduler, I want the system to detect and prevent scheduling conflicts so that employees are not double-booked.

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Conflict Detection
Given existing shift assignments
When I attempt to assign a conflicting shift
Then the system blocks the assignment and notifies me

Additional Details:
- Priority: High
- Story Points: 3
- Dependencies: E02, E03, E04
- Notes: Conflict rules must be configurable

---

**User Story 4**

Title: Personal Upcoming Shift View

User Story: As a worker, I want to view my upcoming shifts so that I can plan my schedule.

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Shift View
Given my employee ID
When I access my schedule
Then I see all upcoming shifts assigned to me

Additional Details:
- Priority: High
- Story Points: 2
- Dependencies: E02, E03, E04
- Notes: Mobile-friendly view required

---

**User Story 5**

Title: Blackout Dates and Operation Calendar Management

User Story: As an HR user, I want to manage blackout dates and warehouse operation calendars so that shifts are not scheduled during non-operational periods.

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Calendar Management
Given blackout dates
When I create or edit the operation calendar
Then shifts are prevented from being scheduled on those dates

Additional Details:
- Priority: High
- Story Points: 3
- Dependencies: E02, E03, E04
- Notes: Calendar must be visible to all users
