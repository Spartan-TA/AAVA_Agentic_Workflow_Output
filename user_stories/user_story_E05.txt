### E05 - Shift & Schedule Management

**User Story 1**
Title: CRUD Shift Templates  
User Story: As an HR user, I want to create, update, and delete shift templates so that recurring schedules are easy to manage.  
Acceptance Criteria:  
Scenario: Shift Template CRUD  
Given the shift template API  
When I perform CRUD operations  
Then templates are correctly managed  
Additional Details:  
- Priority: High  
- Story Points: 5  
- Dependencies: E02, E03, E04  
- Notes: Templates support rotation rules

**User Story 2**
Title: Assign Shifts to Employees  
User Story: As a SUPERVISOR, I want to assign shifts to employees in bulk so that scheduling is efficient.  
Acceptance Criteria:  
Scenario: Bulk Assignment  
Given a list of employees  
When I assign shifts  
Then assignments are created and audit entries generated  
Additional Details:  
- Priority: High  
- Story Points: 5  
- Dependencies: E02, E03, E04  
- Notes: Audit trail required

**User Story 3**
Title: Conflict Detection and Prevention  
User Story: As an HR user, I want to detect and prevent scheduling conflicts so that employees are not double-booked.  
Acceptance Criteria:  
Scenario: Conflict Detection  
Given existing schedules  
When I assign a new shift  
Then conflicts are flagged and prevented  
Additional Details:  
- Priority: High  
- Story Points: 5  
- Dependencies: E02, E03, E04  
- Notes: Includes blackout dates

**User Story 4**
Title: Personal Upcoming Shifts View  
User Story: As a WORKER, I want to view my upcoming shifts so that I can plan my work schedule.  
Acceptance Criteria:  
Scenario: Upcoming Shifts  
Given my employee ID  
When I access my schedule  
Then my upcoming shifts are displayed  
Additional Details:  
- Priority: High  
- Story Points: 3  
- Dependencies: E02, E03, E04  
- Notes: Mobile-friendly view
