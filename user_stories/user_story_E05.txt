### E05 - Shift & Schedule Management

**User Story 1**
Title: Create and Manage Shift Templates
User Story: As a scheduler, I want to create, update, and delete recurring shift templates so that scheduling is efficient.
Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Shift Template CRUD
Given I am an ADMIN or SUPERVISOR
When I POST/GET/PUT/DELETE shift templates
Then the templates are managed accordingly
Additional Details:
- Priority: High
- Story Points: 5
- Dependencies: E02, E03, E04
- Notes: Templates should support rotations and overtime rules.

**User Story 2**
Title: Assign Shifts to Employees and Detect Conflicts
User Story: As a supervisor, I want to assign shifts and prevent scheduling conflicts so that staffing is accurate.
Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Shift Assignment
Given an employee is already scheduled
When I assign an overlapping shift
Then the system prevents the assignment and displays a conflict message
Additional Details:
- Priority: High
- Story Points: 5
- Dependencies: E02, E03, E04
- Notes: Blackout dates and operation calendars must be respected.

**User Story 3**
Title: View Personal Upcoming Shifts
User Story: As a worker, I want to view my upcoming shifts so that I can plan my schedule.
Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: View Shifts
Given I am logged in
When I access my schedule
Then I see a list of my upcoming shifts
Additional Details:
- Priority: High
- Story Points: 2
- Dependencies: E02, E03, E04
- Notes: Should be mobile-friendly.

**User Story 4**
Title: Bulk Assign Shifts and Generate Audit Entries
User Story: As a supervisor, I want to bulk-assign shifts and have audit entries generated so that changes are tracked.
Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Bulk Assignment
Given I select multiple employees
When I assign a shift to all
Then the assignments are created and audit entries are logged
Additional Details:
- Priority: High
- Story Points: 3
- Dependencies: E02, E03, E04
- Notes: Audit entries must include actor, timestamp, and affected records.
