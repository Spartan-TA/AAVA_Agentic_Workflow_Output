### E05 - Shift & Schedule Management

**User Story 1**
Title: Create and Manage Shift Templates
User Story: As an HR user I want to define recurring shift templates So that I can efficiently schedule employees.
Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Shift template CRUD
Given access to scheduling module
When I create, update, or delete a shift template
Then the template is persisted and available for assignment
Additional Details:
- Priority: High
- Story Points: 2
- Dependencies: E02, E03, E04
- Notes: Templates include start/end time, role, and location.

**User Story 2**
Title: Assign Shifts to Employees
User Story: As a supervisor I want to assign shifts to employees individually or in bulk So that staffing is efficient.
Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Shift assignment
Given a list of employees
When I assign a shift or rotation
Then assignments are created and visible to employees
Additional Details:
- Priority: High
- Story Points: 3
- Dependencies: E05-1
- Notes: Bulk assignment supported.

**User Story 3**
Title: Detect and Prevent Scheduling Conflicts
User Story: As a system I want to detect scheduling conflicts So that employees are not double-booked or assigned during blackout dates.
Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Conflict detection
Given existing assignments
When a new shift is assigned
Then conflicts are detected and prevented
Additional Details:
- Priority: High
- Story Points: 2
- Dependencies: E05-2
- Notes: Includes blackout dates and operation calendar.

**User Story 4**
Title: Display Upcoming Shifts to Workers
User Story: As a worker I want to view my upcoming shifts So that I can plan my schedule.
Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: View personal schedule
Given I am logged in
When I access my schedule
Then I see a list of my upcoming shifts
Additional Details:
- Priority: High
- Story Points: 1
- Dependencies: E05-2
- Notes: Mobile-friendly view.

**User Story 5**
Title: Generate Audit Entries for Schedule Changes
User Story: As a compliance officer I want all schedule changes to be logged So that I can audit staffing decisions.
Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Schedule change audit
Given a shift assignment is created, updated, or deleted
When I review the audit log
Then the change is recorded with actor and timestamp
Additional Details:
- Priority: High
- Story Points: 2
- Dependencies: E14
- Notes: Immutable audit trail.
