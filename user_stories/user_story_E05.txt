### E05 - Shift & Schedule Management

#### User Story 1
Title: Create Recurring Shift Templates
User Story: As an HR, I want to create recurring shift templates so that scheduling is efficient.
Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Shift Template Creation
Given HR access
When HR creates a template
Then it is saved and available for assignment
Additional Details:
- Priority: High
- Story Points: 3
- Dependencies: E02, E03, E04
- Notes: Templates must support recurrence

#### User Story 2
Title: Assign Shifts to Employees
User Story: As a SUPERVISOR, I want to assign shifts to employees in bulk so that staffing is managed easily.
Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Bulk Assignment
Given a list of employees
When SUPERVISOR assigns shifts
Then assignments are created and conflicts are detected
Additional Details:
- Priority: High
- Story Points: 2
- Dependencies: E02, E03, E04
- Notes: Conflict prevention required

#### User Story 3

Additional Details:
- Priority: High
- Story Points: 1

#### User Story 5
Title: Manage Blackout Dates and Operation Calendars
User Story: As an HR, I want to manage blackout dates and operation calendars so that shifts are not scheduled during warehouse closures.
Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Blackout Date Management
Given HR access
When HR adds blackout dates
Then shifts cannot be assigned during those dates
Additional Details:
- Priority: High
- Story Points: 2
- Dependencies: E02, E03, E04
- Notes: Calendar integration
