### E05: Shift & Schedule Management

**User Story 1: Create and Manage Shift Templates**
- User Story: As an HR user, I want to create recurring shift templates and assign them to employees So that scheduling is efficient and consistent.
- Acceptance Criteria:
  - Scenario: Create shift template
    - Given shift details
    - When I POST to /shifts/templates
    - Then the template is created and available for assignment
  - Scenario: Assign shift to employee
    - Given a template and employee
    - When I assign the shift
    - Then the employee sees the upcoming shift in their schedule
- Priority: High
- Story Points: 5
- Dependencies: E02, E03, E04
- Notes: Bulk assignment supported.

**User Story 2: Conflict Detection and Audit Logging**
- User Story: As a scheduler, I want to detect and prevent scheduling conflicts So that employees are not double-booked.
- Acceptance Criteria:
  - Scenario: Conflict detection
    - Given overlapping shifts
    - When I attempt to assign both to an employee
    - Then the system prevents the assignment and shows an error
  - Scenario: Audit log
    - Given a schedule change
    - When the change is made
    - Then an audit entry is generated
- Priority: High
- Story Points: 3
- Dependencies: E02, E03, E04
- Notes: Blackout dates and warehouse calendars must be supported.
