### E05 - Shift & Schedule Management

**Title:** Create Recurring Shift Templates  
User Story: As a scheduler, I want to create recurring shift templates So that shifts can be reused and assigned efficiently.  
Acceptance Criteria:  
Scenario: Shift Template Creation  
Given template details  
When I POST to /shifts/templates  
Then a new template is created  
Additional Details:  
- Priority: High  
- Story Points: 3  
- Dependencies: Employee Master Data, RBAC, Attendance  
- Notes: Supports rotations and overtime rules

---

**Title:** Assign Shifts to Employees  
User Story: As a supervisor, I want to assign shifts to employees So that staffing is planned and tracked.  
Acceptance Criteria:  
Scenario: Shift Assignment  
Given a shift template  
When I assign employees  
Then assignments are visible in their schedules  
Additional Details:  
- Priority: High  
- Story Points: 2  
- Dependencies: Create Recurring Shift Templates  
- Notes: Bulk assignment supported

---

**Title:** Detect & Prevent Schedule Conflicts  
User Story: As a scheduler, I want to detect and prevent scheduling conflicts So that no employee is double-booked.  
Acceptance Criteria:  
Scenario: Conflict Detection  
Given existing assignments  
When I assign a new shift  
Then conflicts are flagged and prevented  
Additional Details:  
- Priority: High  
- Story Points: 2  
- Dependencies: Assign Shifts to Employees  
- Notes: Audit entries generated

---

**Title:** Blackout Dates & Operation Calendar  
User Story: As a warehouse manager, I want to manage blackout dates and operation calendars So that shifts are not scheduled during closures.  
Acceptance Criteria:  
Scenario: Blackout Date Management  
Given a calendar  
When I add blackout dates  
Then shifts are not assigned during those periods  
Additional Details:  
- Priority: High  
- Story Points: 2  
- Dependencies: Create Recurring Shift Templates  
- Notes: Calendar visible to all users
