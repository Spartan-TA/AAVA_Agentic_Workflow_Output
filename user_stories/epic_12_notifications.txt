Title: Notifications & Announcements - Send Shift Change Notification
User Story: As a supervisor I want to notify employees of shift changes via app, email, or SMS So that everyone is informed promptly

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Notify shift change
Given a shift is changed
When notification is triggered
Then employees receive notifications via selected channels

Additional Details:
- Priority: Medium
- Story Points: 2
- Dependencies: RBAC, Shift management, Leave management, Certification tracking
- Notes: Quiet hours configurable
