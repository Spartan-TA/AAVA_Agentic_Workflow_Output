Title: Delete Employee Record

User Story: As an HR administrator I want to delete an employee record So that I can remove staff who are no longer employed

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Successful employee deletion
Given I am viewing an employee's profile
When I choose to delete the employee and confirm the action
Then the employee record should be removed from the system and no longer appear in searches or lists

Additional Details:
- Priority: High
- Story Points: 2
- Dependencies: E01
- Notes: Implement safeguards to prevent accidental deletion and ensure compliance with data retention policies.
