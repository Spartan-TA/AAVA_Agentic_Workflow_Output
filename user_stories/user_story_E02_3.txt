Title: Delete (Soft) Employee Record

User Story: As an HR manager I want to soft-delete employee records So that I can remove inactive staff without losing historical data

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Soft delete employee
Given I am viewing an employee profile
When I choose to delete the record
Then the employee should be marked inactive and excluded from active lists

Additional Details:
- Priority: Medium
- Story Points: 2
- Dependencies: Employee record exists
- Notes: Preserve audit trail
