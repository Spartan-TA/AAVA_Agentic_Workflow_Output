Title: Implement Employee List Pagination and Filtering

User Story: As an HR Manager I want to view employee records with pagination and filtering options So that I can efficiently manage large employee datasets and quickly find relevant records

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Paginated results
  Given there are more than 50 employees
  When I GET /employees?page=1&size=20
  Then I receive 20 employee records and pagination metadata
Scenario: Filtering
  Given employees have different departments and statuses
  When I GET /employees?department=Shipping&status=Active
  Then I receive only active employees in the Shipping department

Additional Details:
- Priority: High
- Story Points: 5
- Dependencies: E01, E02_1
- Notes: Filtering options should include department, role, status, and hireDate
