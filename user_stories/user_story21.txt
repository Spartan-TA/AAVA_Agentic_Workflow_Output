Title: Safety Incident Recording
User Story: As a supervisor I want to record safety incidents and near-misses So that investigations and corrective actions can be tracked
Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Incident recording
Given an incident occurs
When I submit the incident details
Then the incident is logged with status Open

Additional Details:
- Priority: High
- Story Points: 3
- Dependencies: Employee CRUD, RBAC
- Notes: Severity, location, and involved employees required
