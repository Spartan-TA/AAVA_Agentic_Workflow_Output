Title: Clock-In Endpoint
User Story: As a warehouse worker I want to clock in at the start of my shift So that my working hours are accurately recorded
Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Successful clock-in
Given I am at the warehouse and logged into the system
When I submit a clock-in event
Then my clock-in time is recorded and associated with my shift
Additional Details:
- Priority: High
- Story Points: 2
- Dependencies: Employee Master Data, RBAC
- Notes: Must validate employee status and shift assignment

Title: Clock-Out Endpoint
User Story: As a warehouse worker I want to clock out at the end of my shift So that my working hours are accurately calculated
Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Successful clock-out
Given I am clocked in and my shift is ending
When I submit a clock-out event
Then my clock-out time is recorded and my total hours for the shift are calculated
Additional Details:
- Priority: High
- Story Points: 2
- Dependencies: Employee Master Data, RBAC
- Notes: Must prevent clock-out without prior clock-in

Title: Geofence Validation
User Story: As a supervisor I want clock-in/out events to validate employee location So that only on-premises events are accepted
Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Geofence validation

Additional Details:
- Priority: Medium
- Story Points: 3
- Dependencies: Device Capture, Location Services
- Notes: Optional feature, configurable per site
