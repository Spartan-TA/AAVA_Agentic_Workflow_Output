Title: Supervisor Approval and Balance Update
User Story: As a SUPERVISOR I want to approve or deny leave requests So that employee balances and schedules are accurately updated
Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Approve or deny leave request
Given a leave request is pending approval
When I review and approve or deny the request
Then the employee's accrual balance and schedule are updated accordingly
Additional Details:
- Priority: Medium
- Story Points: 3
- Dependencies: E02, E03, E05, E06
- Notes: Approval actions must trigger notifications and audit entries.
