Title: Approve or Deny Leave Requests

User Story: As a supervisor I want to approve or deny employee leave requests So that I can manage team coverage and ensure compliance with leave policies

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Leave approval workflow
Given I have pending leave requests in my dashboard
When I review and take action on a request
Then the employee is notified and the schedule is updated accordingly

Additional Details:
- Priority: Medium
- Story Points: 2
- Dependencies: Leave Request Submission, Shift & Schedule Management
- Notes: Approval/denial should trigger notifications and update accrual balances.
