Title: Create Shift Schedules

User Story: As a scheduler I want to create shift schedules for employees So that work coverage is planned and visible in advance

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Creating a new shift schedule
Given I am on the scheduling page
When I enter shift details and assign employees
Then the schedule should be saved and visible to assigned employees

Additional Details:
- Priority: High
- Story Points: 3
- Dependencies: Employee list
- Notes: Support recurring and one-time shifts.
