Title: Reporting & Analytics

User Story: As an operations manager I want to generate and export reports on attendance, overtime, leave balances, certifications, and safety KPIs So that I can make data-driven decisions for staffing, safety, and compliance

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Filter and export report
Given I select filters for date, department, and shift
When I generate a report
Then the system provides CSV/PDF export within 10 seconds for up to 50,000 rows

Scenario: Metrics dashboard access
Given I have the appropriate role
When I access the dashboard
Then I see relevant metrics and analytics endpoints

Additional Details:
- Priority: Medium
- Story Points: 7
- Dependencies: Time & Attendance, Shift & Schedule Management, Leave & Absence Management, Training & Certification Tracking, Safety Incidents, Audit Trail
- Notes: Role-based dashboards; metrics endpoints for BI; access controlled
