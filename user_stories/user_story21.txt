Title: Record Safety Incidents

User Story: As a supervisor I want to record safety incidents in the system So that workplace safety can be monitored and improved

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Safety incident recorded
  Given an incident occurs
  When I submit a safety incident report
  Then the incident should be logged and available for review

Additional Details:
- Priority: High
- Story Points: 3
- Dependencies: Employee entity, reporting module
- Notes: Capture incident details, location, and involved employees.
