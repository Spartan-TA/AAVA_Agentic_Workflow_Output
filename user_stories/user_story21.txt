Title: Bulk Assignment of Shifts and Audit Logging

User Story:
As a supervisor, I want to bulk-assign shifts to multiple employees and have all actions logged for audit so that scheduling is efficient and compliant.

Acceptance Criteria:
- Scenario: Supervisor bulk-assigns shifts
  Given a supervisor selects multiple employees and shifts
  When they perform a bulk assignment
  Then all assignments are created and logged

- Scenario: Audit log of bulk assignments
  Given bulk assignments are made
  When an auditor reviews the logs
  Then each assignment action is traceable by supervisor, employee, and timestamp

Additional Details:
- Bulk assignment must prevent conflicts and respect blackout dates
- Audit log must be exportable and filterable
- Supervisors must only assign shifts to their own teams
