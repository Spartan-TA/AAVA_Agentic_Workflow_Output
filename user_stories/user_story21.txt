Title: Employee Views Training Requirements
User Story: As an employee I want to view my required training and certifications So that I can stay compliant and up-to-date
Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: View training requirements
Given I am logged into the system
When I navigate to the training section
Then I should see a list of required trainings and certifications with due dates
Additional Details:
- Priority: High
- Story Points: 2
- Dependencies: Training data, user authentication
- Notes: Highlight overdue or expiring items.
