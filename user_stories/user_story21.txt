Title: Record Safety Incidents and Near-Misses

User Story: As a safety officer, I want to log incidents and near-misses with all relevant details so that safety trends can be analyzed.

Acceptance Criteria:

Scenario: Record incident with details
Given the incident form
When I submit valid data
Then the incident is recorded with severity, location, and involved employees

Scenario: Validation for missing fields
Given an invalid submission
When required fields are missing
Then validation errors are shown

Additional Details:
- Priority: High
- Story Points: 3
- Dependencies: E02, E03
- Notes: OSHA compliance
