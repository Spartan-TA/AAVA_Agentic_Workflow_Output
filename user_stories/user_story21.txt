Title: Employee Upload Training Completion Evidence

User Story: As an employee I want to upload certificates or evidence of completed training so that my compliance status is updated

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Uploading training evidence
Given I have completed a training module
When I upload a certificate or completion document
Then my training record is updated and my manager is notified

Additional Details:
- Priority: Medium
- Story Points: 2
- Dependencies: Notification module, document storage
- Notes: Support for PDF, image, and document uploads.
