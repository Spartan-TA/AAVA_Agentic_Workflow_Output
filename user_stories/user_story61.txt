Title: In-App and Email/SMS Notifications for Shift Changes and Approvals

User Story: As an employee I want to receive notifications via app, email, or SMS for shift changes and approvals So that I am always informed about my work schedule

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Notification delivery
Given my shift or approval status changes
When the system triggers a notification
Then I receive the update via my selected channels

Additional Details:
- Priority: Medium
- Story Points: 4
- Dependencies: E03, E05, E06, E07
- Notes: Users must be able to opt-in/out per channel.
