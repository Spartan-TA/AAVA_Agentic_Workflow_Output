Title: Production Deployment with Approval

User Story: As a DevOps engineer I want production deployments to require approval So that releases are controlled and compliant with change management policies

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Deployment approval workflow
Given a release is ready for production
When approval is granted by authorized personnel
Then the deployment should proceed and be logged for audit

Additional Details:
- Priority: High
- Story Points: 5
- Dependencies: CI/CD platform, approval workflow
- Notes: Support for multi-level approval and deployment notifications.
