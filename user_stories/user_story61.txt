Title: Track Improvement Initiatives and Outcomes

User Story: As a product manager I want to track continuous improvement initiatives and their outcomes so that I can measure impact and share results with stakeholders

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Tracking improvement initiatives
Given an improvement initiative is launched
When I update its status and outcomes in the platform
Then stakeholders can view progress, results, and lessons learned

Additional Details:
- Priority: Medium
- Story Points: 3
- Dependencies: Feedback module, reporting module
- Notes: Support for attaching documents and linking to related feedback.
