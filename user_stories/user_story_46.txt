Title: Notification Delivery Status Tracking

User Story: As a supervisor I want to view the delivery status of notifications sent to my team so that I can confirm important messages were received

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Viewing notification delivery status
Given notifications have been sent to team members
When I check the notification log
Then I see the delivery status (delivered, failed, pending) for each recipient

Additional Details:
- Priority: Medium
- Story Points: 2
- Dependencies: Notification service, logging module
- Notes: Delivery status should be updated in real time where possible
