Title: HRIS Sync Job for Employee Creation and Updates

User Story: As an HR admin I want the system to automatically create or update employee records from the HRIS so that our workforce data stays current without manual entry

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Employee data is synchronized from HRIS
  Given a new hire or termination event in the HRIS
  When the sync job runs
  Then the employee record is created, updated, or deactivated in the system accordingly

Additional Details:
- Priority: High
- Story Points: 3
- Dependencies: E01, E02, E03
- Notes: Sync job must handle idempotency and error reporting.
