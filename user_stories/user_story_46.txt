Title: Announcements and Dashboard Visibility

User Story: As a supervisor I want to post announcements that are visible on the dashboard so that important updates reach all relevant employees

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Post and view announcement
  Given I am a supervisor
  When I post an announcement
  Then it appears on the dashboard for all targeted users and is tracked for delivery

Additional Details:
- Priority: Medium
- Story Points: 2
- Dependencies: E03 (Scheduling), E05 (Attendance), E06 (Leave), E07 (Certifications)
- Notes: Announcements must respect notification preferences
