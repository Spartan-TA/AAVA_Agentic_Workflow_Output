Title: Bulk Assign Shifts to Employees
User Story: As a supervisor I want to bulk-assign shifts to multiple employees So that I can efficiently manage staffing for large teams
Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Bulk shift assignment
Given I have selected multiple employees and a shift template
When I confirm the assignment
Then all selected employees should have the shift added to their schedules and audit entries generated
Additional Details:
- Priority: High
- Story Points: 3
- Dependencies: Shift template CRUD, employee module
- Notes: Must prevent assignment conflicts and log all actions.
