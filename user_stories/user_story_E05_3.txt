Title: Overtime Calculation and Approval Workflow

User Story: As a Payroll Administrator I want overtime hours to be calculated and routed for approval so that payroll processing is accurate and compliant with labor regulations.

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Calculating overtime
Given employees work beyond their scheduled hours
When the system processes attendance data
Then overtime hours are calculated and flagged for approval

Scenario: Approving overtime
Given overtime hours are pending
When the Supervisor reviews and approves them
Then the approved hours are included in payroll export

Additional Details:
- Priority: High
- Story Points: 5
- Dependencies: Attendance module, Payroll integration
- Notes: Overtime rules must be configurable by HR.
