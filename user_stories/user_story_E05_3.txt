Title: Shift Conflict Detection and Prevention

User Story: As a scheduler I want the system to detect and prevent shift conflicts so that employees are not double-booked and staffing is compliant

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Detecting a scheduling conflict
Given an employee is already assigned to a shift
When I attempt to assign them to an overlapping shift
Then the system should prevent the assignment and display a conflict warning

Additional Details:
- Priority: High
- Story Points: 3
- Dependencies: E02, E03, E04
- Notes: Conflicts must be checked for time overlap, blackout dates, and overtime rules.
