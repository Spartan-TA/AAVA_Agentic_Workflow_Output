### E08 - Safety Incidents & OSHA Reporting

#### User Story 1: Record Safety Incidents
- **Title:** Record Safety Incidents
- **User Story:** As a SUPERVISOR, I want to record safety incidents and near-misses so that risks are documented.
- **Acceptance Criteria:**
    - Given a new incident,
      When I POST to /safety/incidents,
      Then the incident is saved with severity, location, description, and involved employees.
- **Priority:** High
- **Story Points:** 3
- **Dependencies:** E02, E03
- **Notes:** Validation required.

#### User Story 2: Incident Investigation Workflow
- **Title:** Incident Investigation Workflow
- **User Story:** As a SAFETY OFFICER, I want to manage the investigation workflow for incidents so that corrective actions are tracked.
- **Acceptance Criteria:**
    - Given an open incident,
      When I update its status,
      Then it moves through Open, Investigating, Resolved.
- **Priority:** High
- **Story Points:** 2
- **Dependencies:** E08-1
- **Notes:** Workflow states.

#### User Story 3: OSHA Report Generation
- **Title:** Generate OSHA Summary Reports
- **User Story:** As an ADMIN or SAFETY OFFICER, I want to generate OSHA 300/300A summary reports so that regulatory requirements are met.
- **Acceptance Criteria:**
    - Given incident data,
      When I request an OSHA report,
      Then a summary is generated with required fields.
- **Priority:** High
- **Story Points:** 2
- **Dependencies:** E08-1, E08-2
- **Notes:** Compliance.

#### User Story 4: Metrics Dashboard for Safety KPIs
- **Title:** Metrics Dashboard for Safety KPIs
- **User Story:** As a MANAGER, I want to view a dashboard of safety KPIs so that I can monitor trends and take action.
- **Acceptance Criteria:**
    - Given incident data,
      When I access the dashboard,
      Then I see metrics such as incident rate, severity, and resolution time.
- **Priority:** High
- **Story Points:** 2
- **Dependencies:** E08-1
- **Notes:** For continuous improvement.
