Title: Timezone-Aware Scheduling

User Story: As a warehouse supervisor I want schedules to be displayed and managed in the local timezone of each warehouse so that shift times are accurate for all locations.

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Viewing schedules in local timezone
Given I am assigned to a warehouse in a different timezone
When I view or edit schedules
Then the system should automatically convert and display times in the warehouse's local timezone

Additional Details:
- Priority: Low
- Story Points: 2
- Dependencies: E01, E02, E05
- Notes: Users should be able to set their preferred timezone for notifications.
