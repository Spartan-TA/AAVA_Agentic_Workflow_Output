Title: User Registration - Email Verification
User Story: As a new user I want to verify my email address So that I can activate my account and ensure security
Acceptance Criteria: 
Scenario: Email verification link
Given I have registered with a valid email
When I receive the verification email and click the link
Then my account is activated and I can log in
Additional Details: 
Priority: High
Story Points: 2
Dependencies: Email service, Registration module
Notes: Verification link should expire after 24 hours
