Title: Supervisor and Employee Acknowledgement Workflow

User Story: As a supervisor I want to acknowledge and sign-off on employee performance reviews So that both parties confirm the review outcomes

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Review acknowledgement
Given a review has been submitted by the employee
When I review the submission and acknowledge it
Then the review status should update to 'Acknowledged' and become immutable

Additional Details:
- Priority: Medium
- Story Points: 3
- Dependencies: E02, E03
- Notes: Both employee and supervisor must sign-off before review is locked.
