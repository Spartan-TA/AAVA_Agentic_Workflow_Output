Title: View Assigned Equipment & Assets

User Story: As an employee I want to view my assigned equipment and assets So that I am aware of my responsibilities and can report issues

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Viewing assigned assets
Given I am logged into the system
When I navigate to my asset page
Then I should see all equipment currently assigned to me

Additional Details:
- Priority: Medium
- Story Points: 1
- Dependencies: Asset assignment logic
- Notes: Display asset status and history.
