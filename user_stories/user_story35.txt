Title: Asset History Log

User Story: As an asset manager I want to view the history log of each asset and employee So that I can track usage, condition changes, and accountability

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Asset history display
Given I am viewing an asset or employee profile
When I access the history log
Then I see all check-in/out events, condition updates, and overdue returns

Additional Details:
- Priority: Medium
- Story Points: 2
- Dependencies: Asset Registry, Asset Check-In/Out
- Notes: History should be exportable and filterable by date.
