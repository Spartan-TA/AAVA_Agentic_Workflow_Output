Title: Block Asset Assignment for Invalid Certifications

User Story: As a scheduler I want the system to prevent assignment of equipment to employees with missing or expired certifications so that we ensure safety and regulatory compliance.

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Asset assignment blocked for invalid certification
Given an employee's certification is missing or expired
When I attempt to assign them an asset requiring certification
Then the system blocks the assignment and displays a warning

Additional Details:
- Priority: Medium
- Story Points: 5
- Dependencies: E02, E03, E07
- Notes: Applies to forklifts, PPE, and other regulated assets.
