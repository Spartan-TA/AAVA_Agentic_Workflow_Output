Title: Bulk-Assign Shifts to Employees

User Story: As a supervisor I want to bulk-assign shifts to multiple employees So that I can efficiently manage large teams and schedule coverage

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Bulk shift assignment
Given I have selected multiple employees
When I assign a shift template to the group
Then all selected employees are scheduled for the shift and conflicts are checked

Additional Details:
- Priority: High
- Story Points: 5
- Dependencies: E02, E03, E04
- Notes: Audit entries should be generated for each assignment.
