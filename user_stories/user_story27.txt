Title: Track Certification Expiry

User Story: As an HR MANAGER I want to track employee certification expiry dates So that I can ensure compliance and schedule renewals

Acceptance Criteria:

Scenario: Certification expiry tracked
Given certifications are entered for employees
When a certification is nearing expiry
Then the employee and manager are notified

Scenario: Certification renewal recorded
Given an employee renews a certification
When the new expiry date is entered
Then the system updates the record and clears notifications

Additional Details:
- Priority: High
- Story Points: 5
- Dependencies: E07-1
- Notes: Expiry notifications should be configurable.
