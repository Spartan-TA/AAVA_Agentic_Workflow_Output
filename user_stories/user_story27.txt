Title: Leave Policy Configuration and Employee Visibility

User Story:
As an HR administrator, I want to configure leave accrual policies and allow employees to view their balances and policy details so that expectations are clear and disputes are minimized.

Acceptance Criteria:
- Scenario: HR configures leave policy
  Given the HR admin is authenticated
  When they update leave accrual rates or rules
  Then the changes are saved and applied to all affected employees

- Scenario: Employee views leave balance and policy
  Given an employee is authenticated
  When they access their leave dashboard
  Then their current balances and policy details are displayed

Additional Details:
- Policies must support different rules by employee type or department
- Policy changes must be versioned and auditable
- UI must clearly distinguish between available, pending, and taken leave
