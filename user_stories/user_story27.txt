Title: Certification Status on Employee Profile

User Story: As a supervisor I want to view the certification status of each employee on their profile So that I can quickly verify qualifications and compliance

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Certification status display
Given I am viewing an employee profile
When certifications are present or expired
Then the status (valid/expiring/expired) is clearly displayed with relevant details

Additional Details:
- Priority: High
- Story Points: 1
- Dependencies: Certification CRUD
- Notes: Display should include expiry dates and document links.
