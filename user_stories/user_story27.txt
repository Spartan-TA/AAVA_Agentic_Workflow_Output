User Story 27: As an HR manager, I want to create quarterly and annual performance review cycles and manage goal tracking, so that employee development and feedback are structured and documented.

Acceptance Criteria:
- Review cycle templates can be created with competencies, goals, ratings, and comment fields.
- Reviews are assigned to employees and supervisors for completion.
- Submission and acknowledgement workflow ensures both parties sign off before review is finalized.
- PDF export of completed reviews is available for records.
- Role-based visibility ensures only authorized users can view or edit reviews.
- Immutable history is maintained after sign-off.
