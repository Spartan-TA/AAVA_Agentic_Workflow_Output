Title: Employee Views Assigned Equipment
User Story: As an employee I want to view my assigned equipment and assets So that I can ensure I have the necessary resources for my tasks
Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: View assigned equipment
Given I am logged into the system
When I navigate to the equipment section
Then I should see a list of all equipment and assets assigned to me
Additional Details:
- Priority: Medium
- Story Points: 2
- Dependencies: Asset management module
- Notes: Include asset status and return dates.
