Title: Employee View Assigned Equipment

User Story: As an employee I want to view a list of my assigned equipment and assets so that I am aware of my responsibilities

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Viewing assigned equipment
Given I am logged in as an employee
When I navigate to my equipment page
Then I see a list of all assets currently assigned to me

Additional Details:
- Priority: Medium
- Story Points: 2
- Dependencies: Asset assignment
- Notes: Include asset details such as serial number and assignment date.
