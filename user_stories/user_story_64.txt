Title: Offline Queue for Clock Events with Conflict Resolution

User Story: As a worker I want my clock-in/out events to be queued offline and synced when online so that my attendance is accurately recorded even with poor connectivity

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Offline clock event sync
Given I am offline and clock in/out
When I regain connectivity
Then my events are synced and any conflicts are resolved according to business rules

Additional Details:
- Priority: Medium
- Story Points: 3
- Dependencies: PWA framework, sync engine
- Notes: User must be notified of any conflicts or errors
