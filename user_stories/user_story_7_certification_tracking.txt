Title: Certification Tracking
User Story: As a supervisor I want to track employee certifications and expirations So that compliance is maintained
Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Certification tracking
Given employee certifications are recorded
When a certification is nearing expiration
Then the system should notify the employee and supervisor
Additional Details:
- Priority: High
- Story Points: 3
- Dependencies: Employee domain, RBAC
- Notes: Block assignments for expired certifications
