### E06 â Leave & Absence Management

**User Story 1: Request Leave**
- **As a** employee  
- **I want** to request PTO, sick, or unpaid leave  
- **So that** my time off is tracked  
- **Acceptance Criteria:**  
  - Given available leave balance  
    When I submit a request  
    Then it is pending approval  
  - Given insufficient balance  
    When I request leave  
    Then I am notified  
- **Additional Details:**  
  - Priority: Medium  
  - Story Points: 2  
  - Dependencies: E02, E03, E05  
  - Notes: Supports multiple leave types

**User Story 2: Approve or Deny Leave Requests**
- **As a** supervisor  
- **I want** to approve or deny leave requests  
- **So that** staffing is maintained  
- **Acceptance Criteria:**  
  - Given a pending request  
    When I approve  
    Then the employee is notified and balance updated  
  - Given a denial  
    When I deny  
    Then the employee is notified  
- **Additional Details:**  
  - Priority: Medium  
  - Story Points: 2  
  - Dependencies: E02, E03, E05  
  - Notes: Approval workflow

**User Story 3: Update Leave Balances**
- **As a** payroll admin  
- **I want** leave balances to update automatically  
- **So that** accruals and usage are accurate  
- **Acceptance Criteria:**  
  - Given approved leave  
    When processed  
    Then the balance is reduced  
  - Given accrual policies  
    When a period ends  
    Then balances are updated  
- **Additional Details:**  
  - Priority: Medium  
  - Story Points: 2  
  - Dependencies: E02, E03, E05  
  - Notes: Policy-driven accruals

**User Story 4: Exclude Approved Leave from Scheduling**
- **As a** scheduler  
- **I want** approved leaves to block shift assignments  
- **So that** coverage gaps are flagged  
- **Acceptance Criteria:**  
  - Given an approved leave  
    When scheduling shifts  
    Then conflicts are flagged  
- **Additional Details:**  
  - Priority: Medium  
  - Story Points: 2  
  - Dependencies: E02, E03, E05  
  - Notes: Integration with shift management

**User Story 5: Export Leave Reports**
- **As a** HR admin  
- **I want** to export leave reports  
- **So that** I can analyze absence trends  
- **Acceptance Criteria:**  
  - Given leave data  
    When I export  
    Then a CSV file is generated  
- **Additional Details:**  
  - Priority: Medium  
  - Story Points: 1  
  - Dependencies: E02, E03, E05  
  - Notes: Includes all leave types
