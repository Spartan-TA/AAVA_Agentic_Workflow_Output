Title: Certification CRUD
User Story: As an HR manager I want to create, update, and delete employee certifications So that compliance records are accurate and up to date
Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Manage certifications Given I am on the employee profile page When I add, edit, or remove a certification Then the changes are saved and visible on the profile
Additional Details:
- Priority: High
- Story Points: 2
- Dependencies: Employee master data
- Notes: Certification records should support document uploads and expiry dates.
