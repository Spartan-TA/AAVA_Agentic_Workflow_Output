Title: Certification Expiry Alerts for Employees and Supervisors

User Story: As an employee or supervisor I want to receive alerts before my or my team's certifications expire so that renewals can be completed on time and compliance is maintained

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Certification expiry alert
Given a certification is set to expire in 30 or 7 days
When the system checks certification statuses
Then an alert is sent to the employee and supervisor with renewal instructions

Additional Details:
- Priority: High
- Story Points: 2
- Dependencies: Notification System, Employee Master Data
- Notes: Alerts should be sent via in-app and email/SMS if enabled.
