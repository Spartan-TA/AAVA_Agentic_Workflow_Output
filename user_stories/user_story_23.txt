Title: Manager Assigns Training

User Story: As a manager I want to assign required training modules to employees So that compliance and skill development are ensured

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Assigning a training module
Given I am logged in as a manager
When I select an employee and assign a training module
Then the employee should be notified and the assignment should appear in their training dashboard

Additional Details:
- Priority: High
- Story Points: 3
- Dependencies: Employee master data, notifications
- Notes: Track assignment and completion status.
