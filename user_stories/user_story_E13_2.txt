Title: WMS Connector for Department/Location Sync

User Story: As a warehouse manager I want the EMS system to link departments and locations with WMS So that employee assignments and operational data are consistent

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: WMS link for department/location
Given department and location data in WMS
When the connector syncs
Then EMS reflects accurate department/location assignments for employees

Additional Details:
- Priority: High
- Story Points: 3
- Dependencies: E01, E02, E03
- Notes: Sync must be idempotent and support error handling.
