Title: WMS Integration for Department/Location Sync

User Story: As a warehouse manager I want the EMS to synchronize department and location data with our WMS so that employee assignments and schedules are always up to date.

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: WMS sync
Given the WMS system sends updated department/location data
When the EMS receives the update
Then employee records and schedules should reflect the new department/location assignments

Additional Details:
- Priority: High
- Story Points: 4
- Dependencies: Project Scaffolding (E01), Employee Master Data (E02), RBAC (E03)
- Notes: Sync must be idempotent and support webhooks for real-time updates.
