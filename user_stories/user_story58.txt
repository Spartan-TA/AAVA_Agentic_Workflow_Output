Title: Mobile Announcements and PWA Lighthouse Score

User Story: As a warehouse worker I want to view announcements on my mobile device and have a high-performing PWA So that I stay informed and enjoy a reliable mobile experience

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Mobile announcement and PWA performance
Given announcements are published and PWA is installed
When I access the dashboard
Then announcements are visible and the PWA achieves a Lighthouse score of at least 80

Additional Details:
- Priority: Medium
- Story Points: 5
- Dependencies: E04, E05, E06, E12
- Notes: Announcements must be responsive and localized
