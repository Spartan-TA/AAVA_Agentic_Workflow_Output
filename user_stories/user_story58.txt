Title: AI-Powered Schedule Conflict Detection

User Story: As a scheduler I want the system to detect and alert me to potential schedule conflicts using AI so that I can resolve issues before publishing the schedule

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Detecting schedule conflicts
Given I am preparing a new schedule
When I review the AI analysis
Then I am alerted to conflicts such as double-bookings, overtime, or compliance violations

Additional Details:
- Priority: High
- Story Points: 4
- Dependencies: Scheduling module, AI engine
- Notes: Alerts should include suggested resolutions.
