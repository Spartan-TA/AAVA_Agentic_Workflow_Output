Title: Capture Clock-In Event

User Story: As a worker I want to clock in at the start of my shift using my device and location So that my attendance is accurately recorded

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Successful clock-in
Given I am at the worksite and logged into the app
When I clock in with my device
Then my clock-in event should be recorded with timestamp and geolocation

Additional Details:
- Priority: High
- Story Points: 3
- Dependencies: Employee Master Data (E02), RBAC (E03)
- Notes: Enforce geofence validation and device capture.
