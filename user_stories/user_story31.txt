Title: Asset Condition Tracking

User Story: As a supervisor I want to track the condition of assets So that maintenance needs are identified and assets remain safe for use

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Record asset condition
  Given I am authenticated as a supervisor
  When I update the condition of an asset (e.g., good, needs repair)
  Then the condition should be saved and timestamped
Scenario: View asset condition history
  Given an asset has condition updates
  When I view its record
  Then I should see a history of all condition changes
Scenario: Notify maintenance
  Given an asset is marked as needing repair
  When the condition is updated
  Then a notification should be sent to the maintenance team

Additional Details:
- Priority: Medium
- Story Points: 2
- Dependencies: Asset management module, notification system
- Notes: Support for attachments and comments
