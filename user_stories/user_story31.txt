Title: Upload Proof of Certification Documents

User Story:
As an Employee, I want to upload proof of my certifications (e.g., scanned certificates) so that my compliance status can be verified by HR and supervisors.

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario 1: Upload certification document
  Given I am on my profile page
  When I upload a certification document
  Then the document is stored securely and linked to my certification record

Scenario 2: HR reviews uploaded documents
  Given a document is uploaded
  When HR reviews the document
  Then they can approve or reject the proof

Scenario 3: Notify employee of approval status
  Given HR has reviewed the document
  When the status is updated
  Then I receive a notification of approval or rejection

Priority: High
Story Points: 5
Dependencies: E02, E03
Notes: Documents must be stored securely and be auditable.
