Title: Supervisor Conducts Performance Review
User Story: As a supervisor I want to conduct and record performance reviews for employees So that feedback and goals are documented for future reference
Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Conduct performance review
Given I am logged into the system as a supervisor
When I select an employee and complete the review form
Then the review is saved and the employee is notified
Additional Details:
- Priority: High
- Story Points: 3
- Dependencies: Performance management module, notifications
- Notes: Review history should be accessible to both supervisor and employee.
