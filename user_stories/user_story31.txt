Title: Report Safety Incident

User Story: As an employee I want to report a safety incident So that hazards and injuries are documented and addressed promptly

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Reporting an incident
Given I witness or experience a safety incident
When I submit an incident report with details
Then the safety officer should be notified and the report logged

Additional Details:
- Priority: High
- Story Points: 3
- Dependencies: Notification system, incident log
- Notes: Support attachments (photos, documents) in reports.
