Title: Safety Manager Reviews Incident

User Story: As a SAFETY MANAGER I want to review and investigate reported incidents So that corrective actions can be taken and compliance maintained

Acceptance Criteria:

Scenario: Safety manager reviews incident
Given an incident is reported
When I log in as SAFETY MANAGER
Then I can view details, assign investigation, and update status

Scenario: Corrective action recorded
Given an investigation is complete
When corrective actions are taken
Then the incident record is updated and closed

Additional Details:
- Priority: High
- Story Points: 5
- Dependencies: E08-1
- Notes: All actions must be logged and linked to incident records.
