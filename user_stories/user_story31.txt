Title: Track Leave Accrual Balances and Policies

User Story: As an HR manager I want to track leave accrual balances and policies So that employees' time off is calculated accurately and complies with company rules

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Updating leave accrual
Given an employee earns leave based on tenure
When a leave request is approved
Then the system should deduct the appropriate amount from their accrual balance and update their record

Additional Details:
- Priority: Medium
- Story Points: 4
- Dependencies: Employee Master Data, Leave Request
- Notes: Accrual policies should support carryover, caps, and pro-rated calculations.
