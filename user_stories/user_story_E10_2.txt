Title: Track Goals, Competencies, Ratings, and Comments

User Story: As a supervisor I want to track employee goals, competencies, ratings, and comments during performance reviews So that progress and feedback are documented and actionable

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Complete performance review
Given I am conducting a performance review
When I enter ratings, comments, and update goals and competencies
Then the review should be saved to the employee's record and visible to HR and the employee

Additional Details:
- Priority: Medium
- Story Points: 3
- Dependencies: E02 (Employee Profiles), E03 (Scheduling)
- Notes: Reviews should support supervisor and employee acknowledgements.
