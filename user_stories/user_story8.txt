Title: Safety Incidents & OSHA Reporting

User Story: As a safety officer I want to record and manage safety incidents so that compliance and corrective actions are tracked.

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Incident workflow
Given a new incident is reported
When I submit the incident details
Then the workflow status updates, OSHA export fields are available, and metrics are shown on dashboard

Additional Details:
- Priority: High
- Story Points: 5
- Dependencies: Employee Master Data, RBAC
- Notes: Support near-misses, investigation workflow, and export OSHA 300/300A
