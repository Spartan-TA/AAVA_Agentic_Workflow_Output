### E08: Safety Incidents & OSHA Reporting

#### User Story 1
Title: Record Safety Incident

User Story: As a supervisor, I want to record safety incidents with severity, location, and involved employees So that safety data is captured.

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Incident recording
Given the incident form
When I submit incident details
Then the incident is recorded with validation

Additional Details:
- Priority: High
- Story Points: 3
- Dependencies: E02, E03
- Notes: Near-miss supported
