Title: Time & Attendance Clock In/Out

User Story: As a warehouse worker, I want to clock in and out via an endpoint, optionally capturing geofence and device info, so that my hours are accurately tracked.

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Clock in/out event recording
Given I am authenticated
When I POST to /attendance/clock-in or /clock-out
Then my attendance event is recorded with timestamp, location, and device info
And my shift association is updated

Additional Details:
- Priority: High
- Story Points: 5
- Dependencies: Employee CRUD API, RBAC
- Notes: Validate input and handle edge cases.
