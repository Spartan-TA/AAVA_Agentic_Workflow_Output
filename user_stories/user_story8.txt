Title: Safety Incidents & OSHA Reporting

User Story: As a safety officer I want to record and manage safety incidents and near-misses so that investigations can be tracked and OSHA reporting requirements are met.

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Record incident
  Given an incident occurs
  When I submit details (severity, location, description, involved employees)
  Then the incident is logged and status is set to Open

Scenario: Incident workflow
  Given an incident is logged
  When investigation progresses
  Then status transitions through Investigating and Resolved

Scenario: OSHA export
  Given incidents are recorded
  When I request an OSHA summary
  Then I receive exportable OSHA 300/300A fields

Scenario: Metrics dashboard
  Given incident data exists
  When I access the dashboard endpoint
  Then safety KPIs are displayed

Additional Details:
- Priority: High
- Story Points: 8
- Dependencies: Employee Master Data (E02), RBAC (E03)
- Notes: Validation required; workflow for investigation and corrective actions.
