Title: Time & Attendance - Clock In/Out

User Story: As a worker, I want to clock in and out via an endpoint with optional geofence and device capture so that my attendance is accurately recorded.

Acceptance Criteria:

Scenario: Successful clock-in with valid location
Given I am within the geofence area
When I POST to /attendance/clock-in with my device info
Then my clock-in event is recorded

Scenario: Clock-in outside geofence is rejected
Given I am outside the geofence
When I attempt to clock in
Then the request is rejected with an error

Scenario: Clock-out event recorded
Given I am clocked in
When I POST to /attendance/clock-out
Then my clock-out event is recorded

Additional Details:
- Priority: High
- Story Points: 8
- Dependencies: Employee CRUD, RBAC
- Notes: Device info should be stored with event.
