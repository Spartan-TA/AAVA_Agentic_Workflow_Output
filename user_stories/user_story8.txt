Title: Support Pagination and Filtering in Employee List APIs

User Story: As an HR manager I want to paginate and filter employee lists by department, role, and status So that I can efficiently manage large numbers of employee records.

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Paginated employee list
  Given more than 20 employees exist
  When I GET /employees?page=1&size=10
  Then I receive the first 10 employees and metadata for pagination

Scenario: Filter by department
  Given employees in multiple departments
  When I GET /employees?department=Shipping
  Then only employees in the Shipping department are returned

Scenario: Filter by status
  Given active and inactive employees
  When I GET /employees?status=active
  Then only active employees are returned

Additional Details:
- Priority: High
- Story Points: 3
- Dependencies: Employee CRUD APIs
- Notes: Filtering should support role, department, shiftGroup, status.
