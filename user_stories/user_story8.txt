Title: Record Safety Incident
User Story: As a safety manager I want to record a safety incident with details on severity, location, and involved personnel so that incidents are documented for compliance and follow-up.
Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Recording a new incident
Given I am on the incident reporting page
When I enter incident details and submit the form
Then the incident should be saved and visible in the incident log
Additional Details:
- Priority: High
- Story Points: 3
- Dependencies: E02, E03
- Notes: Must support attachment of photos and documents

Title: Initiate Incident Investigation Workflow
User Story: As a safety manager I want to initiate an investigation workflow for recorded incidents so that corrective actions can be tracked and completed.
Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Starting investigation
Given an incident is recorded
When I click 'Start Investigation'
Then an investigation task list should be created and assigned to relevant personnel
Additional Details:
- Priority: High
- Story Points: 5
- Dependencies: E02, E03
- Notes: Workflow should support status updates and comments

Title: Generate OSHA Report
User Story: As a compliance officer I want to generate OSHA-compliant reports from incident data so that regulatory requirements are met.
Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Generating OSHA report
Given incidents are recorded in the system
When I select 'Generate OSHA Report'
Then a formatted report should be produced for download
Additional Details:
- Priority: High
- Story Points: 2
- Dependencies: E02, E03
- Notes: Report format must match OSHA standards

Title: Notify Stakeholders of Safety Incident
User Story: As a safety manager I want stakeholders to be notified when a new safety incident is recorded so that immediate action can be taken.
Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Incident notification
Given a new incident is recorded
When the incident is submitted
Then notifications should be sent to designated stakeholders
Additional Details:
- Priority: High
- Story Points: 2
- Dependencies: E02, E03
- Notes: Notification channels include email and in-app alerts
