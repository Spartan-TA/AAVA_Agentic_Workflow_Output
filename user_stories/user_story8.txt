Title: Record and Manage Safety Incidents & OSHA Reporting

User Story: As a safety officer I want to record safety incidents and near-misses, manage investigation workflows, and generate OSHA-compliant reports so that safety culture is improved and regulatory requirements are met.

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Record incident
  Given a safety incident occurs
  When I submit an incident report with severity, location, description, and involved employees
  Then the incident is recorded and validated

Scenario: Incident workflow
  Given an incident is recorded
  When I update the status
  Then the workflow transitions through Open, Investigating, and Resolved

Scenario: OSHA export
  Given incidents are recorded
  When I export OSHA 300/300A data
  Then the export contains all required fields and is available for download

Scenario: Metrics dashboard
  Given incidents exist
  When I access the dashboard endpoints
  Then I see safety KPIs and incident metrics

Additional Details:
- Priority: High
- Story Points: 8
- Dependencies: Employee Master Data, Role-Based Access Control
- Notes: All fields must be validated; workflow status must be tracked; metrics endpoints required.
