Title: Manager Assigns Shifts

User Story: As a manager I want to assign shifts to employees So that work coverage is ensured for all operational hours

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Assigning a shift
Given I am logged in as a manager and have access to the scheduling module
When I select an employee and assign a shift for a specific date and time
Then the employee's schedule is updated and a notification is sent

Additional Details:
- Priority: High
- Story Points: 5
- Dependencies: Employee Master Data, Notifications
- Notes: Prevent overlapping shifts and ensure compliance with labor regulations.
