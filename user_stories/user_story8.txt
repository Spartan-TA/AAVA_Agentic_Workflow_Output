Title: View Attendance History
User Story: As an employee I want to view my attendance history So that I can verify my work hours

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Attendance history retrieval
Given I am logged in
When I request my attendance history
Then I see a list of my clock in/out events

Additional Details:
- Priority: Medium
- Story Points: 2
- Dependencies: Clock In/Out Endpoint for Employees
- Notes: Show date, time, and status
