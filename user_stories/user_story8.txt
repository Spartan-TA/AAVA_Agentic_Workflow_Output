Title: Employee Clock In
User Story: As an employee I want to clock in at the start of my shift so that my working hours are accurately recorded
Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Successful clock in
  Given I am logged into the system and my shift has started
  When I click the 'Clock In' button
  Then my clock in time is recorded and confirmed
Scenario: Attempt to clock in outside scheduled shift
  Given I am logged in but my shift has not started
  When I attempt to clock in
  Then I receive an error message preventing clock in
Additional Details:
- Priority: High
- Story Points: 3
- Dependencies: Shift & Schedule Management
- Notes: Integrate with scheduling to validate shift times

Title: Employee Clock Out
User Story: As an employee I want to clock out at the end of my shift so that my working hours are accurately tracked
Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Successful clock out
  Given I am logged into the system and my shift is ending
  When I click the 'Clock Out' button
  Then my clock out time is recorded and confirmed
Scenario: Attempt to clock out without clocking in
  Given I have not clocked in
  When I attempt to clock out
  Then I receive an error message preventing clock out
Additional Details:
- Priority: High
- Story Points: 3
- Dependencies: Shift & Schedule Management
- Notes: Ensure clock out is only available after clock in

Title: Supervisor Attendance Review
User Story: As a supervisor I want to review employee attendance records so that I can monitor punctuality and compliance
Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: View attendance dashboard
  Given I am logged in as a supervisor
  When I access the attendance dashboard
  Then I see clock in/out times for all team members
Scenario: Filter attendance by date
  Given I am on the attendance dashboard
  When I select a date range
  Then I see attendance records for the selected period
Additional Details:
- Priority: Medium
- Story Points: 2
- Dependencies: Employee Master Data CRUD
- Notes: Dashboard should support filtering and exporting
