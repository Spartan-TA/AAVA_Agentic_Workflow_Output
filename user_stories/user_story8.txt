Title: Create Shift Templates
User Story: As an HR manager I want to define recurring shift templates So that I can standardize scheduling across the warehouse
Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Create shift template Given I have shift details When I submit the template form Then a new shift template is saved and available for assignment
Additional Details:
- Priority: High
- Story Points: 2
- Dependencies: Employee Master Data CRUD (E02), RBAC (E03), Time & Attendance (E04)
- Notes: Supports rotations and overtime rules

Additional Details:
- Priority: High
- Story Points: 3
- Dependencies: Shift & Schedule Management (E05)

- Priority: High
- Story Points: 2
