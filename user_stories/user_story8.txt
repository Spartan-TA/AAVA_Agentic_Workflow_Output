Title: Time & Attendance Clock In/Out
User Story: As a warehouse worker I want to clock in and out using the system So that my working hours are accurately recorded
Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Successful clock-in
Given I am scheduled for a shift
When I POST to /attendance/clock-in with valid credentials
Then my clock-in event is recorded and associated with my shift

Scenario: Missed punch correction
Given I missed a clock-out
When I submit a correction request
Then an approval task is created for my supervisor

Additional Details:
- Priority: High
- Story Points: 5
- Dependencies: Employee CRUD API, RBAC
- Notes: Optionally capture device and geofence data.
