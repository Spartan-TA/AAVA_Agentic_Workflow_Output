Title: Safety Incidents & OSHA Reporting

User Story: As a safety manager I want to record and track safety incidents and near-misses So that investigations and OSHA reporting are streamlined and compliance is maintained

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Incident workflow
Given a new safety incident is reported
When the status changes from Open to Investigating to Resolved
Then all required fields are captured and OSHA summary is exportable

Additional Details:
- Priority: High
- Story Points: 8
- Dependencies: Employee Master Data, RBAC
- Notes: Severity, location, description, involved employees tracked; metrics dashboard endpoints available
