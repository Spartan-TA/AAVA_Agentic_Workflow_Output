EPIC E08 - Safety Incidents & OSHA Reporting

===========================================

User Story 1: Record Safety Incidents
---------------------------------------------
Title: Record Safety Incidents and Near-Misses

User Story: As a safety officer, I want to record incidents and near-misses so that safety data is tracked.

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Incident recording
  Given the incident endpoint
  When incident is reported
  Then severity, location, description, and involved employees are captured

Additional Details:
- Priority: High
- Story Points: 3
- Dependencies: E02, E03
- Notes: Validate required fields.
