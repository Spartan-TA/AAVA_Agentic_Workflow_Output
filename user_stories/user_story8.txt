Title: Safety Incidents & OSHA Reporting

User Story: As a safety officer I want to record and manage safety incidents and near-misses so that investigations and OSHA reporting are streamlined and compliance is ensured.

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Incident recording and workflow
Given an incident occurs in the warehouse
When I submit a safety incident report
Then the report is validated and includes severity, location, description, and involved employees
And the status workflow progresses from Open to Investigating to Resolved
And OSHA 300/300A fields are exportable
And metrics dashboard endpoints are available

Additional Details:
- Priority: High
- Story Points: 8
- Dependencies: Employee Master Data (CRUD), Role-Based Access Control (RBAC)
- Notes: Support for corrective actions and regulatory reporting.
