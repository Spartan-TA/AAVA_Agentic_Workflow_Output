Title: Attendance Corrections Workflow
User Story: As a supervisor I want to approve or reject missed punch corrections So that attendance records are accurate and auditable
Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Correction approval
Given an employee submits a correction for a missed punch
When I review the request
Then I can approve or reject it, and the attendance record updates accordingly
Additional Details:
- Priority: High
- Story Points: 3
- Dependencies: E02, E03
- Notes: Approval tasks must be tracked
