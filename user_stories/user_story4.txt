Title: Time & Attendance (Clock In/Out)

User Story: As a warehouse worker I want to clock in and out with device and geofence capture So that my worked hours are accurately recorded for payroll and compliance

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Clock In
Given I am scheduled for a shift
When I clock in using the endpoint with device and location
Then my attendance is recorded and associated with the correct shift

Scenario: Clock Out
Given I am clocked in
When I clock out
Then my total hours for the shift are calculated

Scenario: Missed Punch Correction
Given I missed a clock event
When I submit a correction request
Then an approval task is created for my supervisor

Scenario: Attendance Report Export
Given attendance data exists
When I request an export
Then I receive a CSV file with daily totals

Additional Details:
- Priority: High
- Story Points: 8
- Dependencies: Employee Master Data (E02), Role-Based Access Control (E03)
- Notes: Support automatic shift association and corrections workflow.
