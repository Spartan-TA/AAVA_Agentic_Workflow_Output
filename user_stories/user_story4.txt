Title: Time & Attendance (Clock In/Out)

User Story: As a warehouse worker I want to clock in and out with device and location capture so that my worked hours are accurately recorded for payroll and compliance.

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Clock in/out event
Given I am scheduled for a shift
When I clock in or out using the provided endpoint
Then my event is validated and associated with my shift
And daily totals are computed
And missed punches trigger a correction workflow
And attendance reports are exportable as CSV

Additional Details:
- Priority: High
- Story Points: 8
- Dependencies: Employee Master Data (CRUD), Role-Based Access Control (RBAC)
- Notes: Geofence and device capture are optional; corrections require supervisor approval.
