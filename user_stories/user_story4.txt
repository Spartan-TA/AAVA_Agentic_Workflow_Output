Title: Time & Attendance (Clock In/Out)

User Story: As a warehouse worker I want to clock in and out with device and location capture so that my worked hours are accurately recorded for payroll and compliance.

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Clock in
  Given I am scheduled for a shift
  When I clock in using an approved device within the geofence
  Then my clock-in event is recorded with timestamp and location

Scenario: Clock out
  Given I am clocked in
  When I clock out
  Then my clock-out event is recorded and hours worked are calculated

Scenario: Missed punch correction
  Given I missed a clock event
  When I submit a correction request
  Then a supervisor receives an approval task

Scenario: Attendance reporting
  Given attendance data exists
  When I export a report
  Then I receive a CSV file with daily totals

Additional Details:
- Priority: High
- Story Points: 8
- Dependencies: Employee Master Data (E02), Role-Based Access Control (E03)
- Notes: Automatic shift association required; corrections must trigger approval workflow.
