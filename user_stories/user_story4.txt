Title: Time & Attendance - Clock In

User Story: As a worker I want to clock in at the start of my shift so that my attendance is recorded.

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Clock in with valid badgeId
Given I am a worker with a valid badgeId
When I POST to /attendance/clock-in
Then my clock-in event is recorded with timestamp

Scenario: Geofence validation (optional)
Given geofencing is enabled
When I clock in outside the allowed area
Then the request is rejected

Additional Details:
- Priority: High
- Story Points: 3
- Dependencies: E02, E03
- Notes: Device capture optional.

Title: Time & Attendance - Clock Out

User Story: As a worker I want to clock out at the end of my shift so that my hours are calculated.

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Clock out with valid badgeId
Given I am clocked in
When I POST to /attendance/clock-out
Then my clock-out event is recorded and hours worked are calculated

Additional Details:
- Priority: High
- Story Points: 2
- Dependencies: E02, E03
- Notes: Shift association is automatic.

Title: Time & Attendance - Missed Punch Correction

User Story: As a worker I want to request a correction for missed punches so that my attendance is accurate.

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Submit missed punch correction
Given I missed a clock-in/out
When I submit a correction request
Then an approval task is created for my supervisor

Scenario: Supervisor approves correction
Given a correction request is pending
When my supervisor approves it
Then my attendance record is updated

Additional Details:
- Priority: High
- Story Points: 3
- Dependencies: E02, E03
- Notes: Approval workflow required.
