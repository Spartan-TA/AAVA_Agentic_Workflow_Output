Title: Time & Attendance (Clock In/Out)
User Story: As a warehouse worker I want to clock in and out with device and location capture so that my hours are accurately tracked for payroll and compliance.
Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Clock in/out event
Given I am authenticated as a worker
When I submit a clock-in or clock-out event
Then the system validates my location and device (if enabled)
And associates the event with my scheduled shift
And computes daily totals
And missed punches trigger a corrections workflow
And attendance reports are exportable as CSV
Additional Details:
- Priority: High
- Story Points: 8
- Dependencies: Employee Master Data (CRUD), Role-Based Access Control (RBAC)
- Notes: Corrections must create approval tasks; support for geofence is optional but preferred.
