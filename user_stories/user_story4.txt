### E04 - Time & Attendance (Clock In/Out)

**Title:** Employee Clock-In
**User Story:** As a worker, I want to clock in at the start of my shift so that my attendance is recorded.
**Acceptance Criteria:**
Scenario: Valid clock-in
Given I am scheduled for a shift
When I POST to `/attendance/clock-in`
Then my clock-in event is recorded with timestamp and device info
**Additional Details:** Priority: High, Story Points: 3, Dependencies: E02, E03, Notes: Optionally capture geofence.

**Title:** Employee Clock-Out
**User Story:** As a worker, I want to clock out at the end of my shift so that my worked hours are calculated.
**Acceptance Criteria:**
Scenario: Valid clock-out
Given I am clocked in
When I POST to `/attendance/clock-out`
Then my clock-out event is recorded and hours are computed
**Additional Details:** Priority: High, Story Points: 2, Dependencies: E02, E03, Notes: Handle missed punches.

**Title:** Attendance Correction Workflow
**User Story:** As a supervisor, I want to approve corrections for missed punches so that attendance data remains accurate.
**Acceptance Criteria:**
Scenario: Correction approval
Given a correction request exists
When I approve it
Then the attendance record is updated and an audit entry is created
**Additional Details:** Priority: High, Story Points: 3, Dependencies: E02, E03, Notes: Correction tasks visible in dashboard.
