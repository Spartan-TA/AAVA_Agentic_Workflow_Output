Title: Time & Attendance (Clock In/Out)

User Story: As a warehouse worker I want to clock in and out for my shifts so that my worked hours are accurately recorded for payroll and compliance.

Acceptance Criteria:

Scenario: Clock in
Given I am scheduled for a shift
When I submit a clock-in event
Then my attendance is recorded with device and (optional) geofence data

Scenario: Clock out
Given I am clocked in
When I submit a clock-out event
Then my shift duration is calculated and stored

Scenario: Missed punch correction
Given I missed a clock event
When I request a correction
Then an approval task is created for my supervisor

Scenario: Attendance reporting
Given attendance data exists
When I request a report
Then I can export daily totals as CSV

Additional Details:
- Priority: High
- Story Points: 8
- Dependencies: Employee Master Data (E02), Role-Based Access Control (E03)
- Notes: Automatic shift association and validation required.
