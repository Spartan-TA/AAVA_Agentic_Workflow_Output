Title: Time & Attendance (Clock In/Out)

User Story: As a warehouse worker I want to clock in and out with device and location capture so that my worked hours are accurately recorded for payroll and compliance.

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Clock-in event
  Given I am scheduled for a shift
  When I POST to /attendance/clock-in with valid credentials
  Then my clock-in is recorded with timestamp and device info

Scenario: Clock-out event
  Given I am clocked in
  When I POST to /attendance/clock-out
  Then my clock-out is recorded and hours are calculated

Scenario: Missed punch correction
  Given I missed a clock event
  When I submit a correction request
  Then an approval task is created for my supervisor

Scenario: Attendance reporting
  Given attendance data exists
  When I request a report
  Then I can export daily totals as CSV

Additional Details:
- Priority: High
- Story Points: 8
- Dependencies: Employee Master Data (CRUD) (E02), Role Based Access Control (RBAC) (E03)
- Notes: Automatic shift association and geofence/device capture are optional but recommended.
