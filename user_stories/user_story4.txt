Title: Employee Clock In/Out with Validation

User Story:
As a warehouse worker, I want to clock in and out via an endpoint with optional geofence and device capture so that my attendance is accurately recorded for payroll and compliance.

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario 1: Clock in
  Given I am at the warehouse
  When I POST to /attendance/clock-in with valid data
  Then my clock-in event is recorded and associated with my shift

Scenario 2: Clock out
  Given I am clocked in
  When I POST to /attendance/clock-out
  Then my clock-out event is recorded and hours worked are calculated

Scenario 3: Missed punch correction
  Given I missed a clock event
  When I submit a correction request
  Then an approval task is created for my supervisor

Scenario 4: Export attendance
  Given attendance data exists
  When I request a CSV export
  Then I receive a report of attendance records

Additional Details:
Priority: High
Story Points: 8
Dependencies: E02, E03
Notes: Support device and geofence validation.
