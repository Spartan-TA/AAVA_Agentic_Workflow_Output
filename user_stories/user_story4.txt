Title: Time & Attendance (Clock In/Out)

User Story: As a warehouse employee I want to clock in and out of my shifts So that my working hours are accurately recorded for payroll and compliance

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Successful clock-in
Given I am scheduled for a shift and at the warehouse
When I clock in using the provided endpoint
Then my attendance is recorded and associated with my shift

Additional Details:
- Priority: High
- Story Points: 5
- Dependencies: Employee Master Data, RBAC
- Notes: Geofence and device capture optional; missed punches trigger correction workflow; reports exportable as CSV
