Title: Time & Attendance - Clock In/Out Events
User Story: As a worker, I want to clock in and out via endpoints with optional geofence and device capture so that my attendance is accurately recorded.
Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Clock in
  Given I am at the warehouse
  When I POST to /attendance/clock-in with my device
  Then my clock-in event is recorded with location

Scenario: Clock out
  Given I am clocked in
  When I POST to /attendance/clock-out
  Then my clock-out event is recorded

Additional Details:
Priority: High
Story Points: 3
Dependencies: E02, E03
Notes: Supports compliance and payroll
