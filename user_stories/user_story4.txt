Title: Time & Attendance (Clock In/Out)

User Story: As a warehouse worker I want to clock in and out using my device So that my worked hours are accurately captured for payroll and compliance

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Clock-in and clock-out event
Given I am scheduled for a shift
When I clock in or out using the endpoint
Then my attendance is recorded, hours are calculated, and corrections can be submitted for approval

Additional Details:
- Priority: High
- Story Points: 8
- Dependencies: Employee Master Data (CRUD), Role-Based Access Control (RBAC)
- Notes: Geofence and device capture optional; daily totals computed; corrections workflow; exportable reports (CSV).
