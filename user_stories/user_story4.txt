Title: Record Time & Attendance Events

User Story: As a warehouse worker I want to clock in and out using secure endpoints So that my working hours are accurately captured for payroll and compliance

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Clock-in and clock-out event
Given I am scheduled for a shift and have access to the attendance system
When I clock in or out using the provided endpoint
Then my event is validated, associated with my shift, and daily totals are computed; missed punches trigger a correction workflow

Additional Details:
- Priority: High
- Story Points: 8
- Dependencies: Employee Master Data, Role-Based Access Control
- Notes: Geofence and device capture optional; corrections create approval tasks; reports exportable in CSV format.
