Title: Time & Attendance (Clock In/Out)

User Story: As a warehouse worker I want to clock in and out of my shifts so that my working hours are accurately recorded for payroll and compliance.

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Clock-in event
  Given I am scheduled for a shift
  When I POST to /attendance/clock-in with valid credentials
  Then my clock-in time and device are recorded and associated with my shift

Scenario: Clock-out event
  Given I am clocked in
  When I POST to /attendance/clock-out
  Then my clock-out time is recorded and total hours are calculated

Scenario: Missed punch correction
  Given I missed a clock event
  When I submit a correction request
  Then an approval task is created for my supervisor

Scenario: Export attendance report
  Given attendance data exists
  When I request a CSV export
  Then the report is generated with daily totals

Additional Details:
- Priority: High
- Story Points: 8
- Dependencies: Employee Master Data (E02), Role Based Access Control (E03)
- Notes: Optional geofence/device capture; corrections workflow required; automatic shift association.
