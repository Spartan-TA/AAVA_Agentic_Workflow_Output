Title: Time & Attendance (Clock In/Out)

User Story: As a warehouse worker I want to clock in and out with device and location capture so that my worked hours are accurately recorded for payroll and compliance.

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Clock In/Out Event
Given I am scheduled for a shift
When I submit a clock-in or clock-out event
Then the system validates the event, associates it with my shift, computes daily totals, creates approval tasks for corrections, and allows export of attendance reports in CSV format.

Additional Details:
- Priority: High
- Story Points: 8
- Dependencies: Employee Master Data, Role Based Access Control
- Notes: Support geofence and device capture; handle missed punches and corrections workflow.
