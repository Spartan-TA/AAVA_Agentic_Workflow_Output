Title: Time & Attendance (Clock In/Out)
User Story: As a warehouse worker I want to clock in and out with device and location validation so that my worked hours are accurately captured for payroll and compliance.
Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Clock in/out event
Given I am scheduled for a shift
When I clock in or out using the endpoint
Then my hours are calculated, corrections create approval tasks, and reports are exportable as CSV
Additional Details:
- Priority: High
- Story Points: 8
- Dependencies: Employee Master Data (CRUD), Role Based Access Control (RBAC)
- Notes: Geofence and device capture are optional; automatic shift association required.
