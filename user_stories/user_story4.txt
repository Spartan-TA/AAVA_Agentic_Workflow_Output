Title: Clock In/Out Attendance

User Story: As a warehouse worker I want to clock in and out with device and location capture so that my worked hours are accurately tracked.

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Clock in/out event
Given I am scheduled for a shift
When I clock in/out via the endpoint
Then my attendance is recorded, shift association is automatic, and missed punches trigger correction workflow

Additional Details:
- Priority: High
- Story Points: 5
- Dependencies: Employee Master Data, RBAC
- Notes: Support corrections approval, CSV export, and geofence validation
