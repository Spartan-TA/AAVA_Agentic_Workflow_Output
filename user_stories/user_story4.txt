Title: Time & Attendance (Clock In/Out)
User Story: As a warehouse worker I want to clock in and out with device and location capture so that my worked hours are accurately recorded for payroll and compliance.
Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Clock in/out and corrections
Given I am an authenticated worker
When I clock in or out via the endpoint
Then my shift is automatically associated, daily totals are computed, and missed punches can be corrected via approval workflow
And reports are exportable as CSV
Additional Details:
- Priority: High
- Story Points: 8
- Dependencies: Employee Master Data (CRUD), Role Based Access Control (RBAC)
- Notes: Optional geofence/device capture; corrections create approval tasks.
