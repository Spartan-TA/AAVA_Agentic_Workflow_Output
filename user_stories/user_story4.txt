Title: Time & Attendance Clock In/Out

User Story: As a warehouse worker I want to clock in and out for my shifts So that my working hours are accurately recorded for payroll and compliance

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Clock in/out event
Given I am authenticated and on the attendance page
When I submit a clock-in or clock-out event
Then the system validates the entry, associates it with my shift, and computes daily totals

Additional Details:
- Priority: High
- Story Points: 5
- Dependencies: Employee Master Data (E02), RBAC (E03)
- Notes: Handle missed punches and corrections workflow; reports must be exportable as CSV.
