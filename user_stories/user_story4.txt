Title: Time & Attendance (Clock In/Out)

User Story: As a warehouse worker I want to clock in and out with device and location validation so that my worked hours are accurately captured for payroll and compliance.

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Clock in/out and calculate hours
  Given I am an authenticated employee
  When I submit a clock-in or clock-out event
  Then the system validates the event (including optional geofence and device capture)
  And associates the event with my shift
  And computes daily totals
  And missed punches or corrections trigger an approval workflow
  And reports are exportable in CSV format

Additional Details:
- Priority: High
- Story Points: 8
- Dependencies: Employee Master Data (E02), Role Based Access Control (E03)
- Notes: Ensure endpoints for clock-in/out, corrections, and reporting are implemented.
