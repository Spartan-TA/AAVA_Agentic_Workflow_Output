Title: Time & Attendance (Clock In/Out)

User Story: As a warehouse worker I want to clock in and out using endpoints with device and optional geofence capture so that my hours are accurately recorded for payroll and compliance.

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Accurate time capture
Given I am scheduled for a shift
When I clock in or out using the provided endpoint
Then my attendance is validated, shift association is automatic, daily totals are computed, missed punches trigger correction workflow, and reports are exportable as CSV.

Additional Details:
- Priority: High
- Story Points: 8
- Dependencies: Employee Master Data (E02), Role-Based Access Control (E03)
- Notes: Ensure device and geofence capture is optional and corrections create approval tasks.
