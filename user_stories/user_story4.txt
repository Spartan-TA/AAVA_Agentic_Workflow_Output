Title: Support for Multiple Weeks and Topics in MCQ Assessments

User Story: As a curriculum planner I want to organize MCQ assessments by week and topic So that assessments align with the teaching schedule

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Organizing Assessments by Week and Topic
  Given I am creating or editing an assessment
  When I assign a week and topic to each question
  Then the assessment should be categorized accordingly for easy retrieval

Additional Details:
Priority: Medium
Story Points: 2
Dependencies: Assessment metadata tagging
Notes: Enables longitudinal tracking of student progress
