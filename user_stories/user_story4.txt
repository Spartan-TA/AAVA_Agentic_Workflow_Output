Title: Time & Attendance (Clock In/Out) - Clock In
User Story: As a worker, I want to clock in with location and device capture so that my attendance is accurately recorded.
Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Clock in
  Given I am at the warehouse
  When I POST to /attendance/clock-in
  Then my clock-in event is recorded with location and device info
Additional Details:
Priority: High
Story Points: 3
Dependencies: E02, E03
Notes: Geofence optional.
