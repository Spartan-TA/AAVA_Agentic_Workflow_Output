Title: Assign Reviews and Submission/Acknowledgement Workflow

User Story: As a supervisor I want to assign performance reviews to employees and manage the submission and acknowledgement workflow so that evaluations are completed and tracked.

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Review assignment and workflow
Given a review cycle is active
When I assign reviews to employees and they submit their self-assessment
Then supervisors review, rate, and both parties acknowledge completion

Additional Details:
- Priority: Medium
- Story Points: 5
- Dependencies: E02, E03
- Notes: Workflow includes notifications and immutable history after sign-off.
