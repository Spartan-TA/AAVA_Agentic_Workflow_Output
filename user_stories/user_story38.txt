Title: Employee Views Announcement History
User Story: As an employee I want to view past company announcements So that I can reference important updates and policies
Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: View announcement history
Given I am logged into the system
When I navigate to the announcements section
Then I should see a list of all past announcements with dates and details
Additional Details:
- Priority: Medium
- Story Points: 2
- Dependencies: Announcement module
- Notes: Announcements should be searchable by keyword and date.
