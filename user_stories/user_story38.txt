Title: Update Leave Balances

User Story: As an employee I want my leave balances to update automatically after leave approval or denial So that I always see an accurate record of my available time off

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Leave balance update
Given my leave request is approved or denied
When the status changes
Then my accrual balances are updated accordingly and reflected in my profile

Additional Details:
- Priority: Medium
- Story Points: 2
- Dependencies: E02, E03, E05
- Notes: Balances should be recalculated for partial approvals and policy changes.
