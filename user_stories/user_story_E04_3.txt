Title: Missed Punches and Corrections Workflow

User Story: As a warehouse worker I want to submit corrections for missed clock-in/out punches So that my attendance record is accurate and my pay is not affected by errors

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Submit missed punch correction
Given I missed a clock-in or clock-out event
When I submit a correction request
Then the system creates an approval task for my supervisor

Scenario: Supervisor reviews correction
Given a correction request is pending
When my supervisor reviews and approves or denies it
Then the attendance record is updated or the correction is rejected with a reason

Additional Details:
- Priority: High
- Story Points: 5
- Dependencies: E02, E03
- Notes: Correction requests must be tracked with status and audit log; notifications sent to both worker and supervisor.
