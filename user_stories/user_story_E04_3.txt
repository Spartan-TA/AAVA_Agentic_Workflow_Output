Title: Track Breaks and Pauses

User Story: As an employee I want to record breaks and pauses during my shift So that my working hours are calculated accurately

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Break tracking
Given I am clocked in
When I start and end a break
Then the break duration should be logged and deducted from total hours

Additional Details:
- Priority: Medium
- Story Points: 2
- Dependencies: Attendance record
- Notes: Support multiple breaks per shift
