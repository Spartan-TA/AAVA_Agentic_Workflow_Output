Title: Approve/Edit Attendance Records

User Story: As an HR manager I want to approve or edit attendance records So that corrections can be made for missed or incorrect punches

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Attendance approval/edit
Given an attendance record requires review
When I approve or edit the record
Then the changes should be saved and reflected in payroll calculations

Additional Details:
- Priority: High
- Story Points: 3
- Dependencies: Time & attendance module, payroll
- Notes: Maintain an audit trail of changes.
