Title: Onboarding Task Generation for Training and Asset Assignment

User Story: As a supervisor I want onboarding tasks for required training and asset assignment to be automatically generated for new hires so that nothing is missed during onboarding

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Generating onboarding tasks
Given a new hire is provisioned
When the onboarding workflow starts
Then tasks for required training and asset assignment are created and assigned

Additional Details:
- Priority: Medium
- Story Points: 2
- Dependencies: Task management, training module, asset management
- Notes: Task completion must be tracked and reported
