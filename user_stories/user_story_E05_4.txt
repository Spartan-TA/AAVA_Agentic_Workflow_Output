Title: Blackout Dates and Operation Calendars

User Story: As an HR manager I want to define blackout dates and warehouse operation calendars so that shifts are not scheduled during holidays or maintenance periods

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Defining blackout dates
Given I am logged in as HR manager
When I add a blackout date to the operation calendar
Then the system should prevent shift assignments on those dates

Additional Details:
- Priority: High
- Story Points: 2
- Dependencies: E02, E03, E04
- Notes: Blackout dates must be visible to all schedulers; audit entries generated for changes.
