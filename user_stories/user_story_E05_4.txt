Title: Ad-Hoc Shift Assignment for Coverage

User Story: As a Supervisor I want to assign ad-hoc shifts to employees to cover absences or increased demand so that warehouse operations remain uninterrupted.

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Assigning an ad-hoc shift
Given an employee is available
When I assign an ad-hoc shift
Then the employee is notified and the schedule is updated

Scenario: Tracking ad-hoc assignments
Given ad-hoc shifts are assigned
When I view the schedule
Then I see all ad-hoc assignments for the period

Additional Details:
- Priority: Medium
- Story Points: 3
- Dependencies: Scheduling module, Notification service
- Notes: Ad-hoc assignments should be tracked for overtime and compliance.
