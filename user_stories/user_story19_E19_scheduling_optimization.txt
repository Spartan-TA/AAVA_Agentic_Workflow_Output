Epic E19: Advanced Scheduling Optimization

---

User Story 1: Constraint-Based Scheduling Engine
As a scheduler, I want a scheduling engine that considers skills, availability, and labor cost so that assignments are optimal.

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Optimal assignment
  Given employee constraints
  When I generate schedule
  Then assignments are optimal per rules

Additional Details:
- Priority: Low
- Story Points: 8
- Dependencies: E02, E05, E06, E07
- Notes: Engine supports shift swaps

User Story 2: Shift Swap and Coverage Requests
As a worker, I want to request shift swaps and coverage so that my schedule is flexible.

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Shift swap request
  Given my assigned shift
  When I request a swap
  Then approval workflow is triggered

Additional Details:
- Priority: Low
- Story Points: 3
- Dependencies: E02, E05, E06, E07
