Title: Employee Reports Safety Incident
User Story: As an employee I want to report a safety incident So that the organization can take corrective action and comply with OSHA regulations
Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Report safety incident
Given I am logged into the system
When I submit a safety incident report
Then the incident is logged and the safety officer is notified
Additional Details:
- Priority: High
- Story Points: 3
- Dependencies: Incident reporting module, notifications
- Notes: Incident details should be editable until submitted.
