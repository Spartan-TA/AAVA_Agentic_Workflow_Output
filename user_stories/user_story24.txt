Title: Detect and Prevent Schedule Conflicts

User Story: As a supervisor I want the system to detect and prevent schedule conflicts So that I can avoid double-booking employees and ensure compliance with labor rules

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Assigning a shift with a conflict
Given an employee is already scheduled for a shift at a given time
When I attempt to assign them to another overlapping shift
Then the system should display a conflict warning and block the assignment

Additional Details:
- Priority: High
- Story Points: 5
- Dependencies: Shift Templates, Employee Master Data
- Notes: Conflicts should include overlapping shifts, overtime rules, and blackout dates.
