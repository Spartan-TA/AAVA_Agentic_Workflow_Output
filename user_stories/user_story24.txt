User Story: As a safety officer I want to record safety incidents in the system So that all workplace accidents and hazards are documented for compliance and analysis

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Incident recording
Given a workplace incident
When I submit an incident report
Then the system should store the details and notify relevant personnel

Additional Details:

- Notes: Support attachment of photos and documents.
