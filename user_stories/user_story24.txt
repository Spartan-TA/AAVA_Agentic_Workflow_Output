Title: Export Payroll Data
User Story: As a payroll administrator I want to export payroll data So that I can process payments and maintain records
Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Payroll export
Given I am logged in as payroll administrator
When I select a pay period and export payroll data
Then a file should be generated in the required format and available for download
Additional Details:
- Priority: High
- Story Points: 2
- Dependencies: Time & Attendance, Leave Management
- Notes: Support CSV and Excel formats.
