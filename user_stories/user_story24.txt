Title: Certification CRUD and Expiry Alerts
User Story: As an HR manager I want to manage employee certifications and receive alerts before expiry So that compliance is maintained and renewals are timely
Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Certification expiry alert
Given a certification is nearing expiry (30/7 days)
When the system detects the approaching date
Then alerts are sent to the employee and supervisor
Additional Details:
- Priority: High
- Story Points: 5
- Dependencies: E02, E03
- Notes: CRUD operations must support document upload and audit logging.
