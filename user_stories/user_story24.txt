Title: Safety Officer Review and Close Incident

User Story: As a safety officer I want to review reported safety incidents and close them with resolution notes so that corrective actions are documented and tracked

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Reviewing and closing an incident
Given a safety incident is reported
When I review the details and enter resolution notes
Then the incident status is updated to closed and all stakeholders are notified

Additional Details:
- Priority: High
- Story Points: 3
- Dependencies: Notification module, incident log
- Notes: Closed incidents should remain available for audit and reporting.
