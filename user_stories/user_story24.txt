Title: Supervisor Approves Shift Swap Requests

User Story: As a supervisor I want to review and approve shift swap requests So that shift changes do not disrupt operational coverage

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Approving a shift swap
Given I am a supervisor and there are pending swap requests
When I review and approve a request
Then the schedule should be updated and both employees notified

Additional Details:
- Priority: Medium
- Story Points: 2
- Dependencies: Shift swap workflow
- Notes: Approval actions should be logged for compliance.
