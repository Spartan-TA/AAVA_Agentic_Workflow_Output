Title: Track Leave Balances

User Story: As an EMPLOYEE I want to view my leave balances So that I can plan time off and avoid exceeding my allowance

Acceptance Criteria:

Scenario: Employee views leave balance
Given I am logged in as EMPLOYEE
When I navigate to my leave dashboard
Then I see my current leave balances by type

Scenario: Leave balance updates
Given I take approved leave
When my leave is processed
Then my balance is updated accordingly

Additional Details:
- Priority: Medium
- Story Points: 3
- Dependencies: E06-1, E06-2
- Notes: Balances should update in real time and support multiple leave types.
