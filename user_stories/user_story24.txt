Title: Automate Onboarding & Offboarding Workflow

User Story: As an HR manager I want automated provisioning and deprovisioning of employee accounts and assets So that onboarding and offboarding processes are efficient and secure

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Complete onboarding workflow
Given a new employee is added to the system
When the onboarding workflow is triggered
Then accounts, roles, and equipment are provisioned automatically and notifications are sent

Additional Details:
- Priority: Medium
- Story Points: 7
- Dependencies: E02 (Employee Master Data CRUD), E03 (RBAC), E05 (Shift & Schedule Management), E07 (Training & Certification Tracking), E09 (Equipment & Asset Assignment), E13 (Integration Layer)
- Notes: Include checklist tracking and automated deprovisioning for offboarding.
