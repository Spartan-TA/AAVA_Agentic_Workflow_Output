Title: Manage Employee Certifications

User Story: As an HR manager I want to create, update, and delete employee certifications So that I can ensure compliance and track required training

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Certification CRUD
Given I am on the employee profile page
When I add, edit, or remove a certification
Then the changes are reflected in the employee's record and alerts are triggered for expirations

Additional Details:
- Priority: High
- Story Points: 2
- Dependencies: Employee Master Data, RBAC
- Notes: Include upload of proof documents for each certification.
