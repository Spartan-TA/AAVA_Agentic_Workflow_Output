Title: CRUD for Certifications and Upload Proof Documents

User Story: As an HR manager I want to create, update, and delete employee certifications and upload proof documents So that compliance with training requirements is tracked and auditable

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Add certification with proof
Given I am authenticated as HR or ADMIN
When I add a new certification for an employee and upload a proof document
Then the certification and document are saved and visible on the employee profile

Scenario: Delete expired certification
Given a certification is no longer valid
When I delete it
Then the system removes the certification and logs the action

Additional Details:
- Priority: High
- Story Points: 5
- Dependencies: E02, E03
- Notes: Proof documents must be stored securely and accessible only to authorized roles.
