Title: Track Employee Certifications

User Story: As an HR manager I want to track employee certifications and their expiration dates So that I can ensure compliance and prevent assignment to tasks requiring valid certifications

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Certification tracking and expiration alert
Given an employee has a required certification
When the certification is nearing expiration
Then the system should notify the employee and HR manager and block assignment to relevant tasks if expired

Additional Details:
- Priority: High
- Story Points: 3
- Dependencies: E02 (Employee Profiles), E03 (Scheduling)
- Notes: Certification records should include type, issue/expiration dates, and status (valid/expired).
