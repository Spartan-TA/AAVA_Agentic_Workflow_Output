Title: Certification CRUD and Assignment

User Story: As an HR manager I want to create, update, and assign certifications to employees so that compliance and qualification records are maintained

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Manage certifications
Given I am logged in as HR
When I create, update, or assign a certification to an employee
Then the certification record is saved and visible on the employee profile

Additional Details:
- Priority: High
- Story Points: 2
- Dependencies: Employee Master Data, RBAC
- Notes: Certification types configurable; assignment triggers notification to employee.
