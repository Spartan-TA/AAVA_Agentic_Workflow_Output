Title: Track Goals, Competencies, and Ratings

User Story: As an employee I want to track my goals, competencies, and ratings during performance reviews So that I can monitor my progress and receive actionable feedback

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Tracking goals and competencies
Given I am assigned to a review cycle
When I access my review form and update my goals and competencies
Then my ratings and comments should be saved and visible to my supervisor

Additional Details:
- Priority: Medium
- Story Points: 4
- Dependencies: E02, E03
- Notes: Ratings must be locked after supervisor sign-off.
