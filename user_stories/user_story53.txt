Title: Mobile App Push Notifications

User Story: As a mobile user I want to receive push notifications for important events So that I am informed in real time about updates and alerts

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Push notification delivery
Given a relevant event occurs
When I am logged into the mobile app
Then a push notification should be sent to my device

Additional Details:
- Priority: Medium
- Story Points: 3
- Dependencies: Notification Preferences Management
- Notes: Support opt-in and opt-out for notification types.
