Title: Notification Rate Limits and Quiet Hours Configuration

User Story:
As a user, I want to configure quiet hours and have rate limits applied to notifications so that I am not disturbed outside of working hours or overwhelmed by repeated alerts.

Acceptance Criteria:
- Scenario: Quiet hours suppress notifications
  Given a user has set quiet hours
  When a notification event occurs during those hours
  Then the notification is delayed until quiet hours end

- Scenario: Rate limits prevent repeated alerts
  Given multiple notification events occur in a short period
  When rate limits are reached
  Then only the first notification is delivered and others are suppressed or batched

Additional Details:
- Quiet hours and rate limits must be configurable per user
- Suppressed notifications must be logged and viewable in the app
- Admins can override for critical alerts
