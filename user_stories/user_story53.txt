Title: Attendance and Overtime Reporting

User Story: As a supervisor I want to generate attendance and overtime reports filtered by date, department, and shift So that I can monitor workforce utilization and compliance

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Attendance report generation
Given attendance and overtime data exists
When I apply filters and request a report
Then the report is generated within 10 seconds for up to 50,000 rows and access is controlled by role

Additional Details:
- Priority: Medium
- Story Points: 8
- Dependencies: E04, E05, E06, E07, E08, E14
- Notes: Reports must be exportable to CSV and PDF
