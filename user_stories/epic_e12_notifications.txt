EPIC E12: NOTIFICATIONS & ANNOUNCEMENTS

===========================================

Title: Configure Notification Preferences

User Story: As a warehouse employee I want to configure my notification preferences So that I only receive relevant updates via my preferred channels

Acceptance Criteria:

Scenario: Opt-in/Opt-out for channels
Given I am on the notification settings page
When I select or deselect notification channels (in-app, email, SMS)
Then my preferences are saved and notifications are sent according to my choices

Additional Details:
- Priority: Medium
- Story Points: 3
- Dependencies: User profile, messaging service
- Notes: Channel templates should support localization

---

Title: Shift Change Notification Delivery

User Story: As a supervisor I want to notify employees of shift changes So that they are informed in real time and can adjust accordingly

Acceptance Criteria:

Scenario: Shift change triggers notification
Given a shift assignment is updated
When the change is saved
Then affected employees receive notifications via their selected channels

Additional Details:
- Priority: High
- Story Points: 2
- Dependencies: Scheduling module
- Notes: Delivery status must be tracked

---

Title: Certification Expiry Alerts

User Story: As an employee I want to receive alerts when my certifications are about to expire So that I can renew them in time

Acceptance Criteria:

Scenario: Certification nearing expiry
Given my certification is expiring within 30 days
When the system checks certification status
Then I receive an alert via my preferred channel

Additional Details:
- Priority: Medium
- Story Points: 2
- Dependencies: Certification tracking
- Notes: Rate limits applied to prevent spamming

---

Title: Announcements Dashboard Widget

User Story: As a warehouse worker I want to view company announcements on my dashboard So that I stay informed about important updates

Acceptance Criteria:

Scenario: Announcement visibility
Given a new announcement is published
When I log in to my dashboard
Then the announcement is visible until dismissed or expired

Additional Details:
- Priority: Medium
- Story Points: 2
- Dependencies: Dashboard module
- Notes: Announcements should be localized
