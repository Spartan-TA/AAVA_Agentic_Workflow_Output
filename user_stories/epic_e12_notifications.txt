## User Story 1: In-App and Email/SMS Notifications
**User Story:**  
As a user, I want to receive notifications for shift changes, approvals, and announcements via my preferred channel.

**Acceptance Criteria (Gherkin):**  
Given a notification event  
When triggered  
Then I receive it via in-app, email, or SMS as per my settings

**Additional Details:**  
- Priority: Medium  
- Story Points: 2  
- Dependencies: E03, E05, E06, E07  
- Notes: Channel preferences stored per user

---

## User Story 2: Notification Templates and Localization
**User Story:**  
As an admin, I want to manage notification templates and localize them so that messages are clear and in the user's language.

**Acceptance Criteria (Gherkin):**  
Given a template  
When I edit or localize it  
Then notifications use the correct version

**Additional Details:**  
- Priority: Medium  
- Story Points: 1  
- Dependencies: E12-1  
- Notes: Support for multiple languages

---

## User Story 3: Notification Delivery Status Tracking
**User Story:**  
As an admin, I want to track delivery status of notifications so that failures are visible.

**Acceptance Criteria (Gherkin):**  
Given a sent notification  
When I view status  
Then delivered, failed, or pending is shown

**Additional Details:**  
- Priority: Medium  
- Story Points: 1  
- Dependencies: E12-1  
- Notes: Retry failed deliveries

---

## User Story 4: Quiet Hours and Rate Limiting
**User Story:**  
As a user, I want to configure quiet hours and rate limits so that I am not disturbed outside work hours.

**Acceptance Criteria (Gherkin):**  
Given my settings  
When a notification is triggered during quiet hours  
Then it is delayed or suppressed

**Additional Details:**  
- Priority: Low  
- Story Points: 1  
- Dependencies: E12-1  
- Notes: Quiet hours configurable per user

---
