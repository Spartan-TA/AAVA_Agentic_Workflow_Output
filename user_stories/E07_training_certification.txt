EpicID: E07
Title: Training & Certification Tracking
User Story: As a safety manager, I want to track employee certifications and expirations so that only qualified staff are assigned to sensitive equipment.
Acceptance Criteria:
  Scenario: Add Certification
    Given certification details
    When I POST to /certifications
    Then the certification is added to the employee profile
  Scenario: Expiry Alerts
    Given a certification nearing expiry
    When within 30/7 days of expiration
    Then an alert is sent to the employee and supervisor
  Scenario: Block Assignment
    Given an expired certification
    When scheduling a shift requiring it
    Then assignment is blocked
  Scenario: Upload Proof
    Given a certification
    When I upload a document
    Then proof is attached to the record
Additional Details:
  Priority: High
  Story Points: 8
  Dependencies: E02, E03
  Notes: Certification status must be visible on profile
