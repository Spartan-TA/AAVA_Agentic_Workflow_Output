Title: Pagination and Filtering of Employee Records

User Story:
As an HR manager, I want to paginate and filter employee records by department, role, status, and shiftGroup so that I can efficiently manage large numbers of employees.

Acceptance Criteria:
Given a large employee dataset,
When I GET /employees with query parameters,
Then the results are paginated and can be filtered by department, role, status, and shiftGroup.

Additional Details:
Priority: High
Story Points: 3
Dependencies: Employee records must exist
Notes: Pagination and filtering should be documented in the OpenAPI schema.
