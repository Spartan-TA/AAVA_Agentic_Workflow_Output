EPIC E07: Training & Certification Tracking

===========================================

Title: Track Employee Certifications

User Story: As an HR I want to track employee certifications and expiration dates So that compliance is maintained

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Certification entry
Given an employee completes a certification
When I add it to their record
Then the expiration date is tracked

Scenario: Expired certification
Given a certification has expired
When I view assignments
Then the employee is flagged as unqualified

Additional Details:
- Priority: High
- Story Points: 2
- Dependencies: Employee Master Data, RBAC
- Notes: Support multiple certification types

===========================================

Title: Block Unqualified Assignments

User Story: As a Supervisor I want the system to block shift assignments for employees with expired certifications So that safety and compliance are ensured

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Assignment to unqualified employee
Given an employee's certification is expired
When I assign a shift requiring certification
Then the system prevents assignment

Scenario: Assignment to qualified employee
Given an employee's certification is valid
When I assign a shift
Then the assignment is allowed

Additional Details:
- Priority: High
- Story Points: 2
- Dependencies: Certification Tracking, Shift Management
- Notes: Notify HR of blocked assignments

===========================================

Title: Certification Renewal Notifications

User Story: As an HR I want to receive notifications for expiring certifications So that renewals are managed proactively

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Notification for upcoming expiration
Given a certification expires in 30 days
When I view notifications
Then I see a renewal reminder

Scenario: No notification for valid certifications
Given all certifications are valid
When I view notifications
Then no reminders are shown

Additional Details:
- Priority: High
- Story Points: 2
- Dependencies: Certification Tracking
- Notes: Support email and in-app notifications
