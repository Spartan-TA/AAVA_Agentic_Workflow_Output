Title: Configure Audit Retention Policies

User Story: As a compliance officer I want to configure audit retention policies So that audit logs are retained according to legal and organizational requirements

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Audit retention policy configuration
Given I am logged in as a compliance officer
When I set up or modify retention policies
Then the system should retain or purge audit logs according to the policy

Additional Details:
- Priority: Medium
- Story Points: 2
- Dependencies: Audit trail module
- Notes: Support for policy versioning
