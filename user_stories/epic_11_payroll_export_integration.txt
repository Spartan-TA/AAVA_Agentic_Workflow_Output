Title: Retry Failed Payroll Deliveries
User Story: As a payroll admin I want failed payroll exports retried with backoff So that delivery errors are minimized
Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Export Retry Workflow
Given a failed delivery
When the system retries
Then delivery is attempted with backoff and audit log is updated
Additional Details:
- Priority: High
- Story Points: 2
- Dependencies: Payroll export
- Notes: Audit log for every export
