### EPIC E07: Training & Certification Tracking

**User Story 30**

**Title:** Add Certification to Employee

**User Story:** As an HR manager, I want to record certifications for employees So that I can track compliance requirements.

**Acceptance Criteria:**

Scenario: Adding a certification
Given I am on an employee's profile
When I add a certification with type and expiry date
Then it appears in the employee's record

**Additional Details:**
- Priority: High
- Story Points: 2
- Dependencies: E02, E03
- Notes: Supports file upload for proof.

---

**User Story 31**

**Title:** Alert on Expiring Certifications

**User Story:** As a supervisor, I want to be alerted before certifications expire So that I can ensure renewals are completed.

**Acceptance Criteria:**

Scenario: Certification nearing expiry
Given a certification expires in 30 or 7 days
When the system checks expirations
Then an alert is sent to the employee and supervisor

**Additional Details:**
- Priority: High
- Story Points: 2
- Dependencies: E02, E03
- Notes: Email and in-app notifications.
