Title: Detect and Prevent Scheduling Conflicts

User Story: As a scheduler I want the system to detect and prevent scheduling conflicts So that employees are not double-booked or assigned overlapping shifts

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Scheduling conflict detection
Given I am assigning a shift
When the selected employee already has a conflicting shift
Then the system should alert me and block the assignment

Additional Details:
- Priority: High
- Story Points: 3
- Dependencies: Shift assignment logic
- Notes: Include visual conflict indicators and override option with justification.
