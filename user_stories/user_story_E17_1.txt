Title: Automated Onboarding from HRIS

User Story: As an HR manager I want new hires to appear in EMS automatically from HRIS So that onboarding tasks and schedules are generated without manual entry

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: New hire onboarding
Given a new hire is created in HRIS
When the sync job runs
Then the employee is provisioned in EMS and onboarding tasks are generated

Additional Details:
- Priority: Medium
- Story Points: 4
- Dependencies: E02, E03, E05, E07, E09, E13
- Notes: Onboarding tasks include training and asset assignment.
