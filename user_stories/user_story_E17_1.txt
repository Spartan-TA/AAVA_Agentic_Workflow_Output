Title: Automated Provisioning from HRIS

User Story: As an HR manager I want new hires to be automatically provisioned in the EMS from the HRIS So that onboarding is fast and error-free

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: HRIS provisioning
Given a new hire is added to the HRIS
When the EMS sync job runs
Then the employee record is created in EMS with initial schedule and required training tasks

Additional Details:
- Priority: Medium
- Story Points: 5
- Dependencies: E02, E03, E05, E07, E09, E13
- Notes: Sync must be idempotent and handle duplicate entries.
