Title: Certification Expiry Alerts
User Story: As an employee I want to receive alerts before my certifications expire So that I can renew them and remain eligible for assignments
Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Expiry alert notification
Given my certification is expiring within 30 or 7 days
When the system detects the upcoming expiry
Then I should receive an in-app and email/SMS notification
Additional Details:
- Priority: High
- Story Points: 1
- Dependencies: Notification system, certification tracking
- Notes: Alerts must be configurable and logged for compliance.
