Title: Upload Certification Proof Documents

User Story: As an employee I want to upload proof of my certifications So that HR can verify my credentials and keep records up to date

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Upload certification document
Given I am logged in as an employee
When I upload a certification document
Then the document should be stored securely and linked to my certification record for HR review

Additional Details:
- Priority: High
- Story Points: 2
- Dependencies: E02 (Employee Profiles), E03 (Scheduling)
- Notes: Supported file types should be specified and documents should be accessible only to authorized users.
