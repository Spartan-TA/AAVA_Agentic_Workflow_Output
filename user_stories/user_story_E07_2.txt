Title: Certification Expiry Alerts

User Story: As a Training Coordinator I want to receive alerts for certifications expiring in 30 and 7 days so that I can proactively manage renewals and maintain compliance

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: 30-day expiry alert
Given an employee has a certification expiring in 30 days
When the system checks certification dates
Then an alert is sent to the Training Coordinator and the employee

Scenario: 7-day expiry alert
Given an employee has a certification expiring in 7 days
When the system checks certification dates
Then a high-priority alert is sent to the Training Coordinator and the employee

Scenario: No alert for expired certifications already renewed
Given a certification has been renewed
When the expiry date is updated
Then no further expiry alerts are sent for the old expiry date

Additional Details:
- Priority: High
- Story Points: 2
- Dependencies: Employee Master Data (E02), RBAC (E03)
- Notes: Alerts should be configurable by role and channel (email/SMS/in-app)
