Title: Certification Expiry Alerts and Scheduling Checks

User Story: As a supervisor I want to receive alerts before employee certifications expire and have scheduling checks block unqualified assignments So that only qualified employees are assigned to safety-sensitive tasks

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Certification expiry alert
Given an employee's certification is expiring in 30 or 7 days
When the system runs the daily check
Then an alert is sent to the employee and supervisor

Scenario: Scheduling check blocks expired cert
Given an employee's certification is expired
When a supervisor attempts to assign them to a task requiring that certification
Then the system blocks the assignment and displays an error

Additional Details:
- Priority: High
- Story Points: 5
- Dependencies: E02, E03
- Notes: Alerts must be sent via in-app and email; scheduling checks must be enforced at assignment and shift creation.
