Title: Certification Expiry Alerts

User Story: As an employee I want to receive alerts before my certifications expire so that I can renew them and remain qualified for my role

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Alert for expiring certification
Given my certification is set to expire in 30 or 7 days
When the system detects the upcoming expiry
Then I receive an in-app and email/SMS notification with renewal instructions

Additional Details:
- Priority: High
- Story Points: 2
- Dependencies: Employee Master Data, Notifications & Announcements
- Notes: Alert timing configurable; supervisor also notified for direct reports.
