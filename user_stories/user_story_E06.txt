### E06 - Leave & Absence Management

**User Story 1: Leave Request Submission**
- Title: Submit Leave Requests
- Narrative: As an employee, I want to request PTO, sick, or unpaid leave so that my absences are tracked.
- Acceptance Criteria:
  ```
  Given my account,
  When I submit a leave request,
  Then the request is recorded and pending approval.
  ```
- Priority: Medium
- Story Points: 2
- Dependencies: E02, E03, E05
- Notes: Absence tracking.

**User Story 2: Leave Approval Workflow**
- Title: Approve or Deny Leave Requests
- Narrative: As a supervisor, I want to approve or deny leave requests so that staffing is managed.
- Acceptance Criteria:
  ```
  Given a pending leave request,
  When I approve or deny,
  Then the request status updates and the employee is notified.
  ```
- Priority: Medium
- Story Points: 2
- Dependencies: Story 1
- Notes: Staffing control.

**User Story 3: Accrual Balance Updates**
- Title: Update Leave Accrual Balances
- Narrative: As an HR user, I want leave balances updated automatically so that accruals are accurate.
- Acceptance Criteria:
  ```
  Given an approved leave,
  When processed,
  Then the employee's accrual balance is updated.
  ```
- Priority: Medium
- Story Points: 2
- Dependencies: Story 2
- Notes: Compliance.

**User Story 4: Scheduling Integration for Leave**
- Title: Integrate Leave with Scheduling
- Narrative: As a scheduler, I want approved leaves to flag scheduled shifts for coverage so that staffing gaps are filled.
- Acceptance Criteria:
  ```
  Given an approved leave,
  When a scheduled shift overlaps,
  Then the shift is flagged for coverage.
  ```
- Priority: Medium
- Story Points: 2
- Dependencies: Story 2
- Notes: Operational continuity.

**User Story 5: Export Approved Leaves**
- Title: Export Approved Leaves
- Narrative: As an HR user, I want to export approved leaves so that reporting is simplified.
- Acceptance Criteria:
  ```
  Given approved leaves,
  When I request an export,
  Then a file is generated with leave details.
  ```
- Priority: Medium
- Story Points: 1
- Dependencies: Story 2
- Notes: Reporting.
