Title: Clock In and Out

User Story: As an employee I want to clock in and out of my shift So that my working hours are accurately recorded

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Successful clock in/out
Given I am scheduled for a shift
When I clock in at the start and clock out at the end
Then my attendance record should reflect the correct times

Additional Details:
- Priority: High
- Story Points: 3
- Dependencies: Shift schedule
- Notes: Support for mobile and desktop clock-in
