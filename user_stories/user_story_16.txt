Title: Implement Clock-Out Endpoint

User Story: As a warehouse worker I want to clock out at the end of my shift so that my work hours are accurately calculated for payroll.

Acceptance Criteria:

Scenario: Successful clock-out
Given I am clocked in
When I POST to /api/v1/attendance/clock-out with my employee ID and timestamp
Then I should receive a 200 OK response
And my clock-in record should be updated with clock-out time
And total hours worked should be calculated

Scenario: Clock-out without clock-in
Given I have not clocked in today
When I attempt to clock out
Then I should receive a 400 Bad Request response
And the error should indicate no active clock-in found

Scenario: Calculate hours worked
Given I clocked in at 8:00 AM
When I clock out at 5:00 PM
Then the system should calculate 9 hours worked
And break time should be deducted if applicable
And the total should be stored in the attendance record

Scenario: Overtime detection
Given I work more than 8 hours in a shift
When I clock out
Then the system should flag overtime hours
And overtime hours should be calculated separately

Additional Details:
- Priority: High
- Story Points: 3
- Dependencies: Clock-in endpoint (E04), Employee entity (E02)
- Notes: Handle break time deductions. Flag overtime for supervisor review. Support manual corrections workflow.
