Title: Create and Manage Employee Shifts
User Story: As a shift supervisor I want to create and manage employee shifts So that I can ensure optimal coverage and operational efficiency
Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Shift creation and assignment
Given I am on the shift management page
When I create a new shift and assign employees
Then the shift should be visible in the schedule and employees notified
Additional Details:
- Priority: High
- Story Points: 5
- Dependencies: Employee database, notification module
- Notes: Support recurring and ad-hoc shifts.
