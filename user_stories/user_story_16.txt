Title: Employee Clock-In Event Capture

User Story: As an employee I want to clock in at the start of my shift So that my working hours are accurately recorded for payroll and compliance

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Successful clock-in
Given I am on the clock-in page and my shift is scheduled
When I submit my clock-in event
Then the system records my start time and associates it with my shift

Scenario: Geofence validation
Given geofence is enabled
When I attempt to clock in outside the allowed location
Then the system rejects the clock-in and displays an error

Additional Details:
- Priority: High
- Story Points: 3
- Dependencies: Employee Master Data, RBAC
- Notes: Device capture and location validation must be logged.
