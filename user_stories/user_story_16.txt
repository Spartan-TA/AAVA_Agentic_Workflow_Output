Title: Employee Clock In Endpoint

User Story: As an employee I want to clock in at the start of my shift So that my working hours are accurately recorded

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Successful clock in
  Given I am scheduled for a shift and within allowed clock-in time
  When I submit a clock-in request
  Then my clock-in time should be recorded and confirmed
Scenario: Attempt to clock in outside allowed time
  Given I am not scheduled or outside allowed window
  When I submit a clock-in request
  Then I should receive an error message

Additional Details:
- Priority: High
- Story Points: 2
- Dependencies: Shift schedule, employee profile
- Notes: Endpoint should validate shift assignment and time window.
