Title: Asset Assignment and Tracking
User Story: As an asset manager I want to assign equipment and PPE to employees and track their condition So that assets are accounted for and safely used.
Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Assign asset to employee
  Given a valid asset and employee
  When I check out the asset
  Then the assignment is recorded

Scenario: Prevent assignment if certification missing
  Given an employee without required cert
  When I assign restricted equipment
  Then the system blocks the assignment

Scenario: Asset check-in/out history
  Given asset assignments
  When I view asset or employee history
  Then all check-in/out events are visible

Additional Details:
- Priority: Medium
- Story Points: 5
- Dependencies: E02, E03, E07
- Notes: Overdue returns are reported.
