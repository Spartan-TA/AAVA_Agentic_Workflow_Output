Title: Create Recurring Shift Templates and Rotations

User Story: As an HR manager I want to define recurring shift templates and rotations so that warehouse staffing is consistent and scheduling conflicts are minimized

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Creating a shift template
Given the HR manager is logged in
When they create a new shift template with recurrence and rotation rules
Then the template is saved and available for assignment to employees

Additional Details:
- Priority: High
- Story Points: 3
- Dependencies: Employee Master Data, RBAC, Time & Attendance
- Notes: Must support blackout dates and warehouse operation calendars.
