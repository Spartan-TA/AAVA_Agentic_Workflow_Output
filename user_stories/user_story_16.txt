Title: Manager Review of Attendance Records

User Story: As a manager I want to review my team's clock in and clock out records So that I can verify attendance and address discrepancies

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Reviewing attendance records
Given I am logged in as a manager
When I access the attendance dashboard
Then I should see accurate clock in/out times for my direct reports

Additional Details:
- Priority: Medium
- Story Points: 2
- Dependencies: Employee clock in/out data
- Notes: Provide export option for attendance reports.
