Title: Create Shift Template

User Story: As an HR manager I want to define recurring shift templates with start/end times and roles so that I can efficiently schedule employees and ensure coverage

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Create new shift template
Given I have access to the scheduling module
When I enter shift details (name, start/end, role, recurrence)
Then the shift template is saved and available for assignment

Additional Details:
- Priority: High
- Story Points: 3
- Dependencies: Employee Master Data, RBAC
- Notes: Templates should support daily, weekly, and custom recurrence patterns
