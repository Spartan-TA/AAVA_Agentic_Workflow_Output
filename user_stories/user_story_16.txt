Title: Shift Template Creation
User Story: As an HR manager I want to create and manage recurring shift templates So that scheduling is efficient and consistent across the warehouse
Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Create shift template Given I am on the shift management page When I enter shift details and save Then the template is available for assignment and visible in the schedule list
Additional Details:
- Priority: High
- Story Points: 3
- Dependencies: Employee master data, scheduling module
- Notes: Templates should support rotations and overtime rules.
