Title: Safety Officer Reviews and Closes Incident Reports

User Story: As a safety officer I want to review and close safety incident reports So that incidents are investigated and resolved appropriately

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Closing an incident report
Given there are open incident reports
When I review and close a report with resolution details
Then the report status should be updated and relevant parties notified

Additional Details:
- Priority: High
- Story Points: 2
- Dependencies: Incident reporting workflow
- Notes: Closure actions should be logged for compliance.
