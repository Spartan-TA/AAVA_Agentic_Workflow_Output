User Story 32: As an operations manager, I want to generate operational reports (attendance, overtime, leave balances, certification status, safety KPIs) and export them in CSV/PDF formats, so that I can analyze workforce and safety metrics efficiently.

Acceptance Criteria:
- Reports are filterable by date, department, and shift.
- Export of up to 50,000 rows completes within 10 seconds.
- Role-based access controls restrict report visibility.
- Metrics endpoints are available for BI integration.
- All report exports are logged in the audit trail.
