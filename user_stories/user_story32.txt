Title: Manage Asset Registry

User Story: As an asset manager I want to create, update, and delete assets in the registry So that I can track equipment and PPE assigned to employees

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Asset registry CRUD
Given I am on the asset management page
When I add, edit, or remove an asset
Then the asset registry is updated and changes are logged

Additional Details:
- Priority: Medium
- Story Points: 2
- Dependencies: Employee Master Data, RBAC
- Notes: Assets should include type, condition, and assignment status.
