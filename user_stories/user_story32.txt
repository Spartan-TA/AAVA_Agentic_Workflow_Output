Title: Create Recurring Shift Templates

User Story: As a supervisor I want to create recurring shift templates So that I can efficiently schedule employees for regular operations

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Create recurring shift template
Given I am on the shift management page
When I define a new recurring shift template with start/end times and recurrence pattern
Then the template is saved and available for future scheduling

Additional Details:
- Priority: High
- Story Points: 5
- Dependencies: E02, E03, E04
- Notes: Support daily, weekly, and custom recurrence options.
