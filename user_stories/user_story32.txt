Title: Exclude Approved Leave from Scheduling and Payroll

User Story: As a system administrator I want approved leave to be excluded from scheduling and payroll calculations So that staffing plans and payroll exports are accurate

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Approved leave integration
Given an employee has approved leave for specific dates
When schedules and payroll are generated
Then those dates should be excluded from shift assignments and payroll hours

Additional Details:
- Priority: High
- Story Points: 4
- Dependencies: Leave Request, Shift Assignment, Payroll Export Integration
- Notes: Integration hooks should support third-party payroll and scheduling systems.
