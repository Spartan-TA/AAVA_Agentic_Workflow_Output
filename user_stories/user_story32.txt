Title: Employee Updates Goal Progress
User Story: As an employee I want to update the progress of my performance goals So that my supervisor can monitor my achievements and provide timely feedback
Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Update goal progress
Given I am logged into the system and have active goals
When I update the status or add comments to a goal
Then the changes are saved and visible to my supervisor
Additional Details:
- Priority: Medium
- Story Points: 2
- Dependencies: Performance management module
- Notes: Supervisor should receive notification of updates.
