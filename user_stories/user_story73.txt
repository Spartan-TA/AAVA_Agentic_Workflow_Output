Title: New Hire Provisioning from HRIS

User Story:
As an HR administrator, I want new hires from the HRIS to be automatically provisioned with accounts and initial schedules so that onboarding is fast and error-free.

Acceptance Criteria:
- Scenario: New hire appears from HRIS
  Given a new employee is added in the HRIS
  When the sync job runs
  Then the employee is created in the warehouse system with an account and initial schedule

- Scenario: Provisioning tasks are generated
  Given a new hire is created
  When onboarding begins
  Then tasks for training and asset assignment are generated

Additional Details:
- Provisioning must include role, department, and shift group
- All onboarding actions must be logged and auditable
- Errors must trigger alerts to HR
