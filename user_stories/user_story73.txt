Title: Assign Onboarding Buddy

User Story:
As an HR administrator, I want to assign an onboarding buddy to new employees so that they have a point of contact for questions and support during their first weeks.

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario 1: Assign onboarding buddy
  Given a new employee is added
  When I select an onboarding buddy from existing staff
  Then the buddy is notified and the assignment is recorded in the employee's profile

Scenario 2: Track buddy interactions
  Given a buddy is assigned
  When I view onboarding status
  Then I see a log of interactions and feedback between the employee and buddy

Additional Details:
Priority: Medium
Story Points: 2
Dependencies: Employee profile, notification system
Notes: Buddy assignment should be visible to both employee and buddy.
