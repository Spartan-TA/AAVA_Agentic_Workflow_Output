Title: Initiate Employee Onboarding
User Story: As an HR manager I want to initiate onboarding for new employees So that they are set up with required accounts and resources
Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Onboarding initiation
Given I am logged in as HR manager
When I create a new employee record and start onboarding
Then the employee should receive a welcome email and onboarding checklist
Additional Details:
- Priority: High
- Story Points: 2
- Dependencies: Employee CRUD, Notifications
- Notes: Checklist items configurable per role.
