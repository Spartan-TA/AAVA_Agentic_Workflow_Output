Title: Asset History Logs and Overdue Return Reports

User Story: As an asset manager I want to view asset history logs and generate reports of overdue returns so that asset usage and compliance can be monitored and enforced.

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Asset history and overdue report
Given assets are checked in and out
When I view the asset or employee profile
Then I see a log of all transactions and can generate a report of overdue returns

Additional Details:
- Priority: Medium
- Story Points: 4
- Dependencies: E02, E03, E07
- Notes: Reports filter by asset type, employee, and due date.
