Title: Return Equipment and Update Asset Status

User Story: As an employee I want to return equipment and update its status So that asset inventory remains accurate and available for reassignment

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Returning equipment
Given I have equipment assigned to me
When I initiate a return and submit the asset
Then the asset status should be updated and the asset manager notified

Additional Details:
- Priority: Medium
- Story Points: 2
- Dependencies: Asset assignment logic, notification system
- Notes: Support condition reporting on return.
