Title: Certification Expiration Alerts

User Story: As an HR administrator I want to receive alerts when employee certifications are approaching expiration so that I can ensure timely renewals and maintain compliance.

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: 30-day expiration alert
Given an employee has a certification expiring in 30 days
When the system checks certification statuses
Then an alert should be sent to HR and the employee

Scenario: 7-day expiration alert
Given an employee has a certification expiring in 7 days
When the system checks certification statuses
Then an urgent alert should be sent to HR and the employee

Scenario: No alert for expired certifications
Given a certification has already expired
When the system checks certification statuses
Then no expiration alert should be sent, but the status should be flagged as expired

Additional Details:
- Priority: High
- Story Points: 2
- Dependencies: Certification CRUD (E07), Notifications (E12)
- Notes: Alerts should be configurable for timing and delivery channel.
