Title: Employee Receives Shift Notification
User Story: As an employee I want to receive notifications about upcoming shifts So that I am reminded to report on time
Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Receive shift notification
Given I have an upcoming shift
When the notification time is reached
Then I receive a notification with shift details
Additional Details:
- Priority: High
- Story Points: 2
- Dependencies: Notification module, shift schedule
- Notes: Support push, email, and SMS notifications.
