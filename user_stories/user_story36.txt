Title: Request Leave (PTO, Sick, Unpaid)

User Story: As an employee I want to request PTO, sick, or unpaid leave So that I can manage my time off and ensure proper coverage

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Employee requests leave
Given I am logged into my account
When I submit a leave request with type, dates, and reason
Then the request is sent to my supervisor for approval and my accrual balance is checked

Additional Details:
- Priority: Medium
- Story Points: 3
- Dependencies: E02, E03, E05
- Notes: Leave types and balances should be visible to the employee.
