Title: View Assigned Equipment

User Story:
As an employee, I want to view a list of equipment and assets assigned to me so that I am aware of my responsibilities and can report any issues.

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario 1: View assigned assets
  Given I am logged into my employee portal
  When I navigate to the 'My Assets' section
  Then I see a list of all equipment currently assigned to me

Scenario 2: Asset details
  Given I am viewing my assigned assets
  When I click on an asset
  Then I see detailed information about the asset including serial number, status, and return date

Additional Details:
Priority: Medium
Story Points: 2
Dependencies: Employee portal, asset assignment
Notes: Should support mobile and desktop views.
