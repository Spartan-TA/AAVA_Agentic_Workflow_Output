EPIC E17: Onboarding & Offboarding Workflow

---

Title: Automate Onboarding from HRIS
User Story: As an HR admin I want new hires from HRIS to be onboarded automatically So that provisioning is efficient
Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Automated onboarding
Given a new hire appears in HRIS
When the sync job runs
Then the employee is provisioned with account, schedule, and training tasks

Additional Details:
- Priority: Medium
- Story Points: 3
- Dependencies: E02, E03, E05, E07, E09, E13
- Notes: Tasks generated for required training and asset assignment

---

Title: Automate Offboarding on Termination
User Story: As an HR admin I want terminated employees to be offboarded automatically So that access and assets are recovered
Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Automated offboarding
Given an employee is terminated in HRIS
When the sync job runs
Then access is revoked, assets are collected, and schedules updated

Additional Details:
- Priority: Medium
- Story Points: 3
- Dependencies: E02, E03, E05, E07, E09, E13
- Notes: Deprovisioning workflow

---

- Story Points: 2
