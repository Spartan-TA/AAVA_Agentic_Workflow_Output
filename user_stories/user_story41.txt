Title: Manage Employee Certifications (CRUD)

User Story: As an HR administrator I want to create, update, and delete employee certifications So that I can ensure compliance and track qualification status

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Certification CRUD operations
Given I am on the certification management page
When I add, edit, or remove a certification for an employee
Then the changes are saved and reflected in the employee's profile

Additional Details:
- Priority: High
- Story Points: 4
- Dependencies: E02, E03
- Notes: Include fields for certification type, issue/expiry dates, and proof documents.
