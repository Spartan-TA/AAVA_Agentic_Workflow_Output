Title: Barcode Scanning for Receiving

User Story: As a warehouse associate I want to scan barcodes when receiving shipments So that inventory is updated quickly and accurately

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Barcode scan on receiving
Given a shipment arrives
When I scan its barcode
Then the system should update inventory and confirm receipt

Additional Details:
- Priority: High
- Story Points: 4
- Dependencies: Equipment Asset Registry CRUD
- Notes: Support integration with mobile devices and handheld scanners.
