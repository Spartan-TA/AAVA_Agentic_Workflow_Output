Title: Asset and Employee History Log Visibility

User Story:
As an asset manager, I want to view the complete history of check-in/out events for each asset and employee so that accountability and usage patterns are transparent.

Acceptance Criteria:
- Scenario: View asset history log
  Given an asset is selected
  When the manager views its history
  Then all check-in/out events, users, and timestamps are displayed

- Scenario: View employee asset history
  Given an employee is selected
  When the manager views their asset history
  Then all assets checked out/returned by the employee are displayed

Additional Details:
- History logs must be filterable by date, asset, and employee
- All events must be exportable for audit
- Overdue returns must be highlighted
