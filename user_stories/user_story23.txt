Title: AI-Optimized Shift Scheduling
User Story: As a scheduler I want to generate AI-optimized shift schedules so that coverage and employee preferences are balanced
Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Generate optimized schedule
  Given I am logged in as a scheduler
  When I run the AI scheduler
  Then shifts are assigned based on coverage and preferences
Scenario: Review optimization results
  Given an optimized schedule is generated
  When I review the results
  Then I see coverage, overtime, and preference metrics
Additional Details:
- Priority: High
- Story Points: 4
- Dependencies: Shift & Schedule Management
- Notes: Support manual overrides

Title: Employee Shift Preference Submission
User Story: As an employee I want to submit my shift preferences so that the AI scheduler can consider them
Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Submit preferences
  Given I am logged in as an employee
  When I enter my preferred shifts
  Then my preferences are saved for scheduling
Additional Details:
- Priority: Medium
- Story Points: 2
- Dependencies: Advanced Scheduling
- Notes: Preferences should be editable

- Priority: Medium
