Title: Manage Blackout Dates for Scheduling

User Story:
As a Scheduling Manager, I want to define blackout dates (e.g., holidays, maintenance) so that no shifts are assigned or scheduled during those periods.

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario 1: Add a new blackout date
  Given I am on the blackout dates management page
  When I add a new blackout date
  Then the system prevents shift assignments on that date

Scenario 2: Edit or remove a blackout date
  Given a blackout date exists
  When I edit or remove it
  Then the scheduling calendar updates to reflect the change

Scenario 3: Notify affected employees
  Given a blackout date is added or changed
  When employees have shifts assigned on that date
  Then they receive a notification of the change

Priority: High
Story Points: 3
Dependencies: E02, E03, E04
Notes: Must integrate with notifications and leave management.
