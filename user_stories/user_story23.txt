Title: Onboarding & Offboarding Workflow

User Story: As an HR manager I want to automate onboarding and offboarding tasks So that employee lifecycle changes are handled efficiently and securely

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Automated onboarding
Given a new hire appears from HRIS
When onboarding tasks are generated
Then accounts, initial schedule, and required training are provisioned

Scenario: Automated offboarding
Given an employee is terminated
When offboarding tasks are triggered
Then access is revoked, assets are collected, and schedules updated

Additional Details:
- Priority: Medium
- Story Points: 7
- Dependencies: Employee Master Data, RBAC, Shift & Schedule Management, Training & Certification Tracking, Equipment & Asset Assignment, Integration Layer
- Notes: Reduces cycle time and risk; tasks tracked for completion
