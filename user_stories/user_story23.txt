Title: Employee Uploads Certification
User Story: As an employee I want to upload proof of completed certifications So that my compliance records are up-to-date
Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Upload certification
Given I am logged into the system
When I upload a certification document
Then the document is stored and marked as verified by the supervisor
Additional Details:
- Priority: Medium
- Story Points: 2
- Dependencies: Document storage, supervisor verification
- Notes: Expiry dates should be tracked for certifications.
