Title: Mobile Clock In/Out
User Story: As a warehouse worker I want to clock in and out from my mobile device So that I can record attendance conveniently
Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Mobile clock-in Given I am scheduled for a shift When I use the mobile app to clock in Then my attendance is recorded and synced
Additional Details:
- Priority: Medium
- Story Points: 2
- Dependencies: Attendance, Shift Management, Leave Management, Notifications
- Notes: Offline queue for clock events
