Title: Supervisor Approves/Rejects Leave

User Story: As a SUPERVISOR I want to approve or reject leave requests So that I can manage team coverage and ensure fairness

Acceptance Criteria:

Scenario: Supervisor reviews leave request
Given I am logged in as SUPERVISOR
When I view pending leave requests
Then I can approve or reject each request

Scenario: Employee notified of decision
Given a decision is made
When the supervisor submits approval or rejection
Then the employee is notified of the outcome

Additional Details:
- Priority: High
- Story Points: 5
- Dependencies: E06-1
- Notes: Approval/rejection actions must be logged for audit.
