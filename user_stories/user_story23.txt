Title: Report Safety Incident

User Story: As an employee I want to report a safety incident so that hazards are documented and addressed promptly

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Submitting a safety incident report
Given I witness or experience a safety incident
When I fill out and submit the incident report form
Then the safety officer is notified and the incident is logged

Additional Details:
- Priority: High
- Story Points: 3
- Dependencies: Notification module, incident log
- Notes: Support for attaching photos and documents to the report.
