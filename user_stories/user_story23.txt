Title: Submit Performance Review Feedback
User Story: As a reviewer I want to submit feedback for an employee's performance review So that evaluations are documented and actionable
Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Feedback submission
Given I am logged in as reviewer
When I complete a performance review form and submit feedback
Then the employee and HR manager should be notified and the feedback stored
Additional Details:
- Priority: Medium
- Story Points: 2
- Dependencies: Notifications, Review module
- Notes: Feedback must be confidential and accessible only to authorized roles.
