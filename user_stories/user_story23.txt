Title: Enable Mobile Access via PWA

User Story: As an employee I want to access the system on my mobile device with offline support So that I can clock in/out and view schedules even without internet connectivity

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Use PWA offline
Given I have installed the PWA on my mobile device
When I lose internet connectivity
Then I can still view my schedule and clock in/out, with data syncing when connection is restored

Additional Details:
- Priority: Medium
- Story Points: 8
- Dependencies: E04 (Time & Attendance), E05 (Shift & Schedule Management), E06 (Leave & Absence Management), E12 (Notifications & Announcements)
- Notes: Must be responsive and support push notifications.
