Title: Assign Shifts to Employees

User Story: As a supervisor I want to assign shifts to employees So that I can ensure adequate staffing for each warehouse operation

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Assigning a shift
Given I have created a shift template
When I select employees and assign them to a shift
Then the system should notify the employees and update their schedules

Additional Details:
- Priority: High
- Story Points: 4
- Dependencies: Employee Master Data, Shift Templates
- Notes: Bulk assignment should be supported for efficiency.
