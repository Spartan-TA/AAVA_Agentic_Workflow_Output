### E19 - Advanced Scheduling Optimization

**User Story 1: On-Demand Scheduling Solver**
- Title: Run Constraint-Based Scheduling Solver
- Narrative: As a scheduler, I want to run a solver for shift assignments so that skills, preferences, and fairness are considered.
- Acceptance Criteria:
  ```
  Given scheduling constraints,
  When solver is run,
  Then feasible schedules are generated within 30 seconds for 500 employees.
  ```
- Priority: Low
- Story Points: 5
- Dependencies: E05, E07
- Notes: Optimization.

**User Story 2: Manual Override of Solver Results**
- Title: Manual Override of Schedules
- Narrative: As a scheduler, I want to manually tweak solver results so that exceptions are handled.
- Acceptance Criteria:
  ```
  Given solver output,
  When I make manual changes,
  Then overrides are saved and tracked.
  ```
- Priority: Low
- Story Points: 2
- Dependencies: Story 1
- Notes: Flexibility.

**User Story 3: Coverage Metrics for Schedules**
- Title: Display Coverage Metrics
- Narrative: As a manager, I want coverage metrics for schedules so that staffing adequacy is visible.
- Acceptance Criteria:
  ```
  Given a schedule,
  When viewed,
  Then coverage metrics (skills, demand) are displayed.
  ```
- Priority: Low
- Story Points: 2
- Dependencies: Story 1
- Notes: Decision support.

**User Story 4: Performance Testing for Solver**
- Title: Test Solver Performance
- Narrative: As a QA engineer, I want tests for solver performance so that SLAs are met.
- Acceptance Criteria:
  ```
  Given solver tests,
  When run,
  Then schedules for 500 employees are generated in under 30 seconds.
  ```
- Priority: Low
- Story Points: 1
- Dependencies: Story 1
- Notes: Reliability.
