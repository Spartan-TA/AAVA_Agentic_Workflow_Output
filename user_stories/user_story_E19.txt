### E19: Advanced Scheduling (AI-Assisted)

**User Story 1: AI-Driven Shift Suggestions**
- User Story: As a scheduler, I want the system to suggest optimal shift assignments based on historical data and forecasted demand So that staffing is efficient.
- Acceptance Criteria:
  - Scenario: Generate suggestions
    - Given historical attendance and demand data
    - When I request shift suggestions
    - Then the system proposes optimal assignments
- Priority: Low
- Story Points: 8
- Dependencies: E02, E05, E07
- Notes: Manual override allowed.

**User Story 2: Accept/Reject AI Suggestions**
- User Story: As a scheduler, I want to review and accept or reject AI-generated shift assignments So that I retain control over scheduling.
- Acceptance Criteria:
  - Scenario: Accept suggestion
    - Given a proposed assignment
    - When I accept it
    - Then the shift is assigned to the employee
  - Scenario: Reject suggestion
    - Given a proposed assignment
    - When I reject it
    - Then no changes are made
- Priority: Low
- Story Points: 3
- Dependencies: E02, E05, E07
- Notes: Audit log of decisions.
