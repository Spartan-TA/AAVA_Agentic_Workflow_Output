Executive Summary

This document outlines the transformation of 20 high-level warehouse employee management epics into 56 actionable, testable user stories for agile development teams. Each epic was decomposed into 2-4 user stories, ensuring coverage of all critical functionalities, user roles, and measurable outcomes. Every user story includes Gherkin-formatted acceptance criteria, priority, story points, dependencies, and notes. The stories are ready for sprint planning and implementation, supporting robust quality assurance and compliance. The process followed industry best practices for epic breakdown, validation, and documentation. All user stories are provided as individual files for easy tracking and GitHub upload.
