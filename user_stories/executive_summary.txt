Executive Summary

This deliverable transforms 20 high-level warehouse employee management epics into 80 detailed, actionable user stories for agile development teams. Each epic was decomposed into 4 user stories, covering core functionalities, workflows, validation, and reporting. Every user story includes a clear title, user narrative, Gherkin-formatted acceptance criteria, and additional details (priority, story points, dependencies, notes). The process ensures coverage of all major modules: employee master data, scheduling, attendance, safety, compliance, integrations, and mobile access. This comprehensive breakdown enables teams to plan, estimate, and deliver features iteratively, with traceable acceptance criteria and testability.
