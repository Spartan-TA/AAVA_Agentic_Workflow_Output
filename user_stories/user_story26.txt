Title: Assign Training Modules

User Story: As a TRAINING MANAGER I want to assign training modules to employees So that compliance and skill development are tracked

Acceptance Criteria:

Scenario: Training manager assigns module
Given I am logged in as TRAINING MANAGER
When I select employees and assign a module
Then employees are notified and module is added to their dashboard

Scenario: Employee views assigned training
Given a module is assigned
When I log in as EMPLOYEE
Then I see the training module and due date

Additional Details:
- Priority: High
- Story Points: 3
- Dependencies: E01, E02
- Notes: Assignment should support bulk actions and notifications.
