Title: Certification Expiry Alerts

User Story: As an HR manager I want to receive alerts when employee certifications are about to expire so that I can proactively manage renewals and maintain compliance.

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Alert for expiring certifications
Given an employee has a certification expiring in 30 or 7 days
When the system detects the upcoming expiry
Then an alert is sent to the HR manager and the employee

Additional Details:
- Priority: High
- Story Points: 3
- Dependencies: E02, E03
- Notes: Alerts should be configurable for timing and delivery channel.
