Title: Block Assignment to Tasks Requiring Expired Certifications

User Story: As a supervisor I want the system to prevent assignment of employees to tasks requiring expired certifications So that I can ensure only qualified staff operate equipment and maintain compliance

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Assignment block for expired certifications
Given I am assigning an employee to a task requiring certification
When the employee's certification is expired
Then the system blocks the assignment and displays a warning

Additional Details:
- Priority: High
- Story Points: 2
- Dependencies: Certification CRUD, Shift & Schedule Management
- Notes: Block should apply to equipment-sensitive roles and tasks.
