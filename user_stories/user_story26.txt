Title: Supervisor Approves Leave Requests

User Story: As a supervisor I want to review and approve employee leave requests So that absences are managed and do not disrupt operations

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Approving a leave request
Given I am a supervisor and there are pending leave requests
When I review and approve a request
Then the employee should be notified and the schedule updated

Additional Details:
- Priority: High
- Story Points: 2
- Dependencies: Leave request workflow
- Notes: Approval actions should be logged for audit.
