User Story 26: As a supervisor, I want to view reports of overdue asset returns and access history logs for each asset and employee, so that accountability and asset management are improved.

Acceptance Criteria:
- System tracks due dates for asset returns and flags overdue items in supervisor dashboard and reports.
- History log is available for each asset, showing all check-in/out events, condition updates, and assigned employees.
- Employees have a personal asset history log in their profile.
- Reports of overdue returns are exportable in CSV format.
- All asset history and overdue events are logged in the audit trail.
