Title: Assign Equipment to Employees

User Story: As an asset manager I want to assign equipment and assets to employees so that usage and responsibility are tracked

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Assigning equipment
Given I am logged in as an asset manager
When I assign an asset to an employee
Then the assignment is recorded and the employee is notified

Additional Details:
- Priority: High
- Story Points: 3
- Dependencies: Employee master data, notification module
- Notes: Support for bulk assignment and asset categories.
