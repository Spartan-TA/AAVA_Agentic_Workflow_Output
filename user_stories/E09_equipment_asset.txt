EpicID: E09
Title: Equipment & Asset Assignment
User Story: As an asset manager, I want to assign and track equipment to employees so that accountability and safe operation are ensured.
Acceptance Criteria:
  Scenario: Register Asset
    Given asset details
    When I POST to /assets
    Then the asset is added to the registry
  Scenario: Assign Asset
    Given a qualified employee
    When I assign an asset
    Then assignment is recorded and history updated
  Scenario: Block Assignment
    Given missing certification
    When assigning equipment
    Then assignment is blocked
  Scenario: Overdue Returns
    Given overdue assets
    When I generate a report
    Then overdue items are listed
Additional Details:
  Priority: Medium
  Story Points: 8
  Dependencies: E02, E03, E07
  Notes: History log must be visible per asset and employee
