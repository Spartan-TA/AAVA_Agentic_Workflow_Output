Title: Record Clock-Out Event

User Story: As a warehouse worker I want to clock out at the end of my shift so that my total hours worked are accurately captured for payroll and compliance

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Successful clock-out
Given I am clocked in for my scheduled shift
When I access the clock-out endpoint and provide my badge ID
Then my clock-out event is recorded with timestamp, and my daily total hours are computed

Additional Details:
- Priority: High
- Story Points: 2
- Dependencies: Employee Master Data, RBAC
- Notes: Should handle overnight shifts and validate against shift schedule
