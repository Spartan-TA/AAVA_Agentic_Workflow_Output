Title: Record Employee Clock-In Event

User Story: As a warehouse worker I want to clock in at the start of my shift So that my working hours are accurately tracked for payroll

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Successful clock-in
Given I am scheduled for a shift today
When I submit a clock-in event via the system
Then my attendance record is updated and the shift association is logged

Additional Details:
- Priority: High
- Story Points: 2
- Dependencies: Employee Master Data, RBAC
- Notes: Geofence validation is optional but should be supported if enabled.
