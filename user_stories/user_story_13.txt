Title: Clock-Out Event API and Shift Association

User Story: As a warehouse worker I want to clock out for my shift and have my hours automatically calculated So that my attendance and payroll are accurate

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Successful clock-out
  Given I am authenticated and have clocked in for my shift
  When I submit a POST request to /attendance/clock-out
  Then my clock-out event is recorded and hours worked are calculated

Scenario: Automatic shift association
  Given my clock-in and clock-out events are recorded
  When the system processes attendance
  Then my events are linked to the correct shift and daily totals are computed

Additional Details:
- Priority: High
- Story Points: 2
- Dependencies: E02, E03
- Notes: Calculation must handle overnight shifts and time zone differences.
