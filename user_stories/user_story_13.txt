Title: Automated Attendance Alerts

User Story: As an HR administrator I want to receive automated alerts for attendance anomalies So that I can proactively address issues like absenteeism and tardiness

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Alert for attendance anomaly
Given an employee's attendance record triggers a predefined rule
When the anomaly is detected
Then an alert should be sent to HR and the supervisor

Additional Details:
- Priority: Medium
- Story Points: 5
- Dependencies: Attendance module, notification system
- Notes: Configurable alert thresholds.
