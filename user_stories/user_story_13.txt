Title: Record Clock-In/Out Events with Geofence and Device Capture

User Story: As a warehouse employee I want to clock in and out using my device so that my attendance is accurately recorded for payroll and compliance

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Employee clocks in with geofence
Given the employee is within the allowed geofence area and has a registered device
When they submit a clock-in event
Then the system records the event with location and device details

Additional Details:
- Priority: High
- Story Points: 3
- Dependencies: Employee Master Data, RBAC
- Notes: Must validate geofence and device; support for optional geofence.
