Title: Clock In/Out with Geofence Validation

User Story: As a warehouse worker I want to clock in and out using my device with geofence validation So that my attendance is accurately recorded only when I am on site

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Valid clock-in
Given I am within the warehouse geofence
When I clock in via the app
Then my attendance is recorded with timestamp and location

Scenario: Invalid clock-in
Given I am outside the warehouse geofence
When I attempt to clock in
Then the request is rejected with an error message

Additional Details:
- Priority: High
- Story Points: 5
- Dependencies: Employee CRUD, RBAC
- Notes: Device and location capture required
