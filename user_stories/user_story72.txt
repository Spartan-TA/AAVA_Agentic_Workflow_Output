Title: Complete Digital Document Collection

User Story:
As a new employee, I want to complete all required onboarding documents digitally so that my paperwork is processed efficiently and securely.

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario 1: Fill out onboarding documents
  Given onboarding is initiated
  When I access my onboarding checklist
  Then I can fill out and submit all required documents online

Scenario 2: Track document status
  Given documents are submitted
  When I view my onboarding status
  Then I see which documents are completed and which are pending

Additional Details:
Priority: High
Story Points: 2
Dependencies: Onboarding workflow, document management
Notes: Documents should support e-signature and secure storage.
