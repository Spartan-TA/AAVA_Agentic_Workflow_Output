Title: Submit and Acknowledge Performance Reviews
User Story: As a supervisor I want to submit performance reviews and have employees acknowledge them so that the review process is complete and documented
Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Submit review
Given a review cycle is active
When I complete ratings, goals, and comments for an employee
Then the review is submitted and locked for editing
Scenario: Employee acknowledges review
Given a review is submitted
When the employee reviews and acknowledges it
Then the acknowledgement is recorded and the review is finalized
Additional Details:
- Priority: Medium
- Story Points: 2
- Dependencies: E02, E03
- Notes: Reviews are immutable after sign-off
