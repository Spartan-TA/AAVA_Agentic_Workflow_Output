Title: Employee Views Assigned Assets

User Story: As an employee I want to view all assets assigned to me So that I am aware of my responsibilities and can report issues

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Employee checks assigned assets
Given I am logged into the system
When I navigate to my asset list
Then I should see all currently assigned equipment and assets with details

Additional Details:
- Priority: Medium
- Story Points: 2
- Dependencies: Employee Master Data
- Notes: Asset list should include assignment date and condition.
