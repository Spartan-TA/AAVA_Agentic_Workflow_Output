Title: Corrective Action Tracking

User Story: As a safety officer I want to assign and track corrective actions for safety incidents So that issues are resolved and compliance is maintained

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Assigning a corrective action
Given an incident investigation is complete
When I create a corrective action and assign it to a responsible party
Then the action should be tracked until completion

Scenario: Marking corrective action as complete
Given a corrective action is assigned
When the responsible party marks it as complete
Then the status should update and notify the safety officer

Additional Details:
- Priority: High
- Story Points: 3
- Dependencies: Incident investigation workflow
- Notes: Corrective actions should have due dates and support attachments.
