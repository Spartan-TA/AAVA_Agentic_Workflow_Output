Title: Role-Based Visibility and Immutable Review History

User Story: As an HR manager I want completed performance reviews to be visible only to authorized roles and immutable after sign-off so that confidentiality and auditability are maintained.

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Review visibility and immutability
Given a review is signed off by both employee and supervisor
When I view the review as an authorized user
Then the review is visible and cannot be edited or deleted

Additional Details:
- Priority: Medium
- Story Points: 4
- Dependencies: E02, E03
- Notes: Access control enforced by role; audit log for all actions.
