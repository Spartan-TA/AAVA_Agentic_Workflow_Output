### E19 - Advanced Scheduling (AI/Optimization)

#### User Story 1: Forecast Demand for Scheduling
- **Title:** Forecast Demand for Scheduling
- **User Story:** As a SCHEDULER, I want the system to forecast staffing demand so that schedules match workload.
- **Acceptance Criteria:**
    - Given historical data,
      When I run forecasting,
      Then predicted demand is shown for each shift.
- **Priority:** Low
- **Story Points:** 3
- **Dependencies:** E02, E05, E06, E07
- **Notes:** Data-driven scheduling.

#### User Story 2: Auto-Generate Schedules with AI Optimization
- **Title:** Auto-Generate Schedules with AI Optimization
- **User Story:** As a SCHEDULER, I want the system to auto-generate optimized schedules so that coverage and preferences are balanced.
- **Acceptance Criteria:**
    - Given demand forecasts and constraints,
      When I generate a schedule,
      Then the system creates an optimized plan.
- **Priority:** Low
- **Story Points:** 5
- **Dependencies:** E19-1
- **Notes:** AI/ML algorithms.

#### User Story 3: Handle Employee Preferences and Constraints
- **Title:** Handle Employee Preferences and Constraints
- **User Story:** As a WORKER, I want my shift preferences and constraints considered in scheduling so that my needs are respected.
- **Acceptance Criteria:**
    - Given my preferences,
      When schedules are generated,
      Then my constraints are considered where possible.
- **Priority:** Low
- **Story Points:** 2
- **Dependencies:** E19-2
- **Notes:** Fairness.

#### User Story 4: Review and Adjust AI-Generated Schedules
- **Title:** Review and Adjust AI-Generated Schedules
- **User Story:** As a SUPERVISOR, I want to review and adjust AI-generated schedules so that exceptions can be handled.
- **Acceptance Criteria:**
    - Given an AI-generated schedule,
      When I review it,
      Then I can make manual adjustments before publishing.
- **Priority:** Low
- **Story Points:** 2
- **Dependencies:** E19-2
- **Notes:** Human oversight.
