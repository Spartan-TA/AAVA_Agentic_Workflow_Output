Title: Shift & Schedule Management

User Story: As a supervisor I want to create and assign shift schedules to employees So that staffing is optimized and scheduling conflicts are minimized

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Create recurring shift template
Given I have access to the scheduling module
When I define a new shift template and assign employees
Then the system saves the template and detects any conflicts

Scenario: Bulk assignment
Given multiple employees are selected
When I assign them to a shift rotation
Then all assignments are created and audit entries generated

Additional Details:
- Priority: High
- Story Points: 8
- Dependencies: Employee Master Data, RBAC, Time & Attendance
- Notes: Blackout dates and operation calendars supported; workers see personal shifts; supervisors can bulk-assign
