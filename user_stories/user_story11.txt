Title: Employee Clock In/Out

User Story: As an employee I want to clock in and out of my shift so that my working hours are accurately recorded

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Successful clock in/out
Given I am logged into the system
When I select 'Clock In' or 'Clock Out' during my scheduled shift
Then my timestamp is recorded and visible in my attendance history

Additional Details:
- Priority: High
- Story Points: 3
- Dependencies: Shift schedule, user authentication
- Notes: Support for web and mobile clock in/out.
