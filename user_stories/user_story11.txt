Title: Clock In/Out Endpoints for Attendance

User Story: As a warehouse worker I want to clock in and out using a secure endpoint so that my work hours are accurately recorded for payroll and compliance

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Successful clock in
Given I am authenticated and within my scheduled shift
When I submit a clock-in request with valid credentials
Then my clock-in event is recorded with timestamp and device details

Scenario: Successful clock out
Given I am authenticated and have clocked in
When I submit a clock-out request
Then my clock-out event is recorded and my shift hours are calculated

Additional Details:
- Priority: High
- Story Points: 3
- Dependencies: Employee Master Data, RBAC
- Notes: Support optional geofence and device capture; validate shift association.
