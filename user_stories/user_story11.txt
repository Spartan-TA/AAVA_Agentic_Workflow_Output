Title: Clock In/Out Endpoint
User Story: As a warehouse worker I want to clock in and out via an endpoint with optional geofence and device capture So that my attendance is accurately recorded.
Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Clock in with geofence
Given I am within the warehouse geofence
When I POST to /attendance/clock-in
Then my clock-in event is recorded with device info
Additional Details:
- Priority: High
- Story Points: 3
- Dependencies: Employee CRUD, RBAC
- Notes: Geofence is optional
