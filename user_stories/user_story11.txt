Title: Employee Clock-In Event

User Story: As a warehouse worker I want to clock in at the start of my shift So that my worked hours are accurately recorded

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Successful clock-in
Given I am scheduled for a shift and on site
When I submit a clock-in event
Then my attendance record is created and associated with my shift

Additional Details:
- Priority: High
- Story Points: 2
- Dependencies: E02, E03
- Notes: Geofence validation optional; device ID captured for audit
