Title: Shift Template Management

User Story: As a scheduler, I want to create, update, and delete recurring shift templates and assign them to employees so that scheduling is efficient and consistent.

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Shift template CRUD operations
Given I am authenticated as a scheduler
When I CRUD shift templates
Then templates are saved and available for assignment

Scenario: Template assignment
When I assign templates to employees
Then their schedules are updated accordingly

Additional Details:
- Priority: High
- Story Points: 5
- Dependencies: Employee CRUD API, RBAC, Time & Attendance
- Notes: Support bulk assignment and audit entries.
