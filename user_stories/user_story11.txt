Title: Enable Time & Attendance Clock In/Out

User Story: As an employee I want to clock in and out using my mobile device with geofence validation So that my working hours are accurately recorded and verified

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Successful clock-in with geofence
Given I am within the designated worksite geofence
When I submit a clock-in request
Then my attendance is recorded and my location is logged

Additional Details:
- Priority: High
- Story Points: 5
- Dependencies: E02 (Employee Master Data CRUD), E03 (RBAC)
- Notes: Must calculate total hours and flag anomalies for review.
