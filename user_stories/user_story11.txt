Title: Time & Attendance Clock-Out

User Story: As a warehouse worker I want to clock out so that my work hours are tracked.

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Clock-out event
Given I am clocked in
When I POST to /attendance/clock-out
Then my clock-out is validated and hours are computed

Additional Details:
- Priority: High
- Story Points: 2
- Dependencies: Clock-In
- Notes: Shift association automatic.
