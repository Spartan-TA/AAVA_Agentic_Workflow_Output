Title: Leave & Absence Request
User Story: As an employee I want to request PTO, sick, or unpaid leave So that my time off is tracked and approved
Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Submit leave request
Given I have available leave balance
When I submit a leave request
Then my supervisor receives an approval task

Scenario: Leave approval
Given a pending leave request
When my supervisor approves it
Then my leave balance is updated and scheduled shifts are flagged for coverage

Additional Details:
- Priority: Medium
- Story Points: 5
- Dependencies: Employee CRUD API, RBAC, Schedule Management
- Notes: Integrate with payroll and scheduling.
