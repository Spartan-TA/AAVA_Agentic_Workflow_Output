Title: Payroll Export Integration

User Story: As a payroll administrator I want to generate payroll-ready files from approved attendance and leave so that manual processing and errors in payroll runs are reduced.

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Payroll export
Given approved attendance and leave records
When I generate a payroll export
Then the export matches the provider schema
And totals reconcile with attendance and leave reports
And failed deliveries are retried with backoff
And an audit log is created for every export

Additional Details:
- Priority: High
- Story Points: 5
- Dependencies: Time & Attendance (Clock In/Out), Leave & Absence Management
- Notes: Support secure delivery via SFTP/API; mapping to external payroll provider formats.
