Title: Clock In/Out Endpoint

User Story: As a warehouse worker I want to clock in and out using a web/mobile endpoint So that my attendance is accurately recorded

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Clock In
  Given I am scheduled for a shift
  When I POST to /attendance/clock-in
  Then my clock-in event is recorded

Scenario: Clock Out
  Given I am clocked in
  When I POST to /attendance/clock-out
  Then my clock-out event is recorded

Additional Details:
- Priority: High
- Story Points: 5
- Dependencies: user_story5, user_story8
- Notes: Capture device and optional geofence.
