Title: Record Employee Clock-Out

User Story: As an employee I want to clock out at the end of my shift So that my working hours are accurately tracked

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Clock-out
Given I am on the time & attendance page
When I click the clock-out button
Then my clock-out time should be recorded in the system

Additional Details:
- Priority: High
- Story Points: 2
- Dependencies: Time & Attendance module
- Notes: Prevent clock-out without prior clock-in.
