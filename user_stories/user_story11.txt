Title: Manager Approves Leave

User Story: As a manager I want to review and approve or reject leave requests So that team availability is maintained and leave policies are enforced

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Approving leave
Given I am logged in as a manager and have pending leave requests
When I review a request and select approve or reject
Then the employee is notified of the decision and the schedule is updated accordingly

Additional Details:
- Priority: High
- Story Points: 3
- Dependencies: Notifications, Shift & Schedule Management
- Notes: Provide reason for rejection and update leave balance if approved.
