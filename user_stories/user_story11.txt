Title: Record Training Completion
User Story: As a training coordinator I want to record employee training completions so that compliance and skill tracking are maintained
Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Record training for employee
  Given I am logged in as a training coordinator
  When I select an employee and mark training as completed
  Then the training record is updated and visible in the employee profile
Scenario: Duplicate training prevention
  Given an employee has completed a training
  When I attempt to record the same training again
  Then I receive a warning and cannot duplicate the entry
Additional Details:
- Priority: High
- Story Points: 3
- Dependencies: Employee Master Data CRUD
- Notes: Training records should be auditable

Title: Certification Expiry Notification
User Story: As an employee I want to be notified before my certification expires so that I can renew it on time
Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Upcoming expiry notification
  Given my certification is expiring within 30 days
  When I log in or receive notifications
  Then I am alerted to renew my certification
Additional Details:
- Priority: Medium
- Story Points: 2
- Dependencies: Notifications & Announcements
- Notes: Notification timing configurable
