Title: SUPERVISOR Role Constraints

User Story: As a SUPERVISOR I want to manage only the records of employees in my assigned team So that I can perform my duties without accessing unrelated sensitive data

Acceptance Criteria:

Scenario: SUPERVISOR can access team records
Given I am authenticated as a SUPERVISOR
When I access employee records
Then I should only see and manage employees assigned to my team

Scenario: SUPERVISOR cannot access other teams
Given I am authenticated as a SUPERVISOR
When I attempt to access records outside my team
Then access should be denied with a 403 Forbidden response

Additional Details:
- Priority: High
- Story Points: 2
- Dependencies: Employee domain (E02), RBAC setup (E03)
- Notes: Team assignment logic must be enforced in all relevant endpoints.
