Title: Alerting for Critical Errors

User Story: As a support engineer I want automated alerts for critical errors and outages So that I can respond quickly to incidents

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Alert trigger
Given a critical error occurs
When alerting rules are matched
Then notifications are sent to the support team

Additional Details:
- Priority: High
- Story Points: 2
- Dependencies: Logging, monitoring
- Notes: Integrate with email/SMS/Slack
