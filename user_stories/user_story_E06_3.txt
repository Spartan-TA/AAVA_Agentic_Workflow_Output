Title: Request Sick or Unpaid Leave

User Story: As an employee I want to request sick or unpaid leave So that my absence is properly recorded and does not affect my payroll incorrectly

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Submit sick/unpaid leave request
Given I am logged in as an employee
When I select sick or unpaid leave type and submit my request
Then my supervisor should be notified and the system should update my leave balance and payroll eligibility

Additional Details:
- Priority: Medium
- Story Points: 2
- Dependencies: E02 (Employee Profiles), E03 (Scheduling), E05 (Attendance Tracking)
- Notes: Sick/unpaid leave requests should follow company policy and be excluded from payroll calculations as appropriate.

Title: View Leave Accrual Balances

User Story: As an employee I want to view my current leave accrual balances So that I can plan my time off according to available leave

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: View accrual balances
Given I am logged in as an employee
When I navigate to my leave dashboard
Then I should see my up-to-date PTO, sick, and unpaid leave balances

Additional Details:
- Priority: Medium
- Story Points: 1
- Dependencies: E02 (Employee Profiles), E05 (Attendance Tracking)
- Notes: Accrual balances should reflect approved and pending leave requests.
