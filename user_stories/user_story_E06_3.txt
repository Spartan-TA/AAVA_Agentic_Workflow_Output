Title: Integration Hooks to Exclude Approved Leave from Scheduling and Payroll

User Story: As a payroll administrator I want approved leave to automatically exclude employees from shift scheduling and payroll hours So that payroll and coverage are accurate without manual intervention

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Approved leave blocks scheduling
Given an employee has approved leave for a date
When a supervisor attempts to assign a shift for that date
Then the system blocks the assignment and notifies the supervisor

Scenario: Payroll export excludes leave
Given an employee has approved unpaid leave
When payroll data is exported
Then the leave days are excluded from payable hours

Additional Details:
- Priority: Medium
- Story Points: 5
- Dependencies: E02, E03, E05
- Notes: Integration hooks must be available for both scheduling and payroll modules; all exclusions must be auditable.
