Title: Leave Accrual Balance Tracking

User Story: As an Employee I want to view my current leave accrual balances so that I can plan my time off and avoid exceeding my limits.

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Viewing leave balances
Given I am logged into the portal
When I access my leave dashboard
Then I see my current PTO and sick leave accrual balances

Scenario: Updating balances after leave
Given I take approved leave
When the leave is processed
Then my accrual balances are updated accordingly

Additional Details:
- Priority: Medium
- Story Points: 2
- Dependencies: Leave module, Payroll integration
- Notes: Accrual rules must be configurable by HR.
