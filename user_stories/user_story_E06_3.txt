Title: Update Leave Accrual Balances
User Story: As an HR admin I want leave accrual balances to update automatically So that employee records remain accurate and compliant
Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Accrual balance update
Given an approved leave request
When the leave period starts
Then the employee's accrual balance should decrease by the appropriate amount
Additional Details:
- Priority: Medium
- Story Points: 2
- Dependencies: Leave policy engine, payroll integration
- Notes: Balance updates must be reflected in exports and employee profiles.
