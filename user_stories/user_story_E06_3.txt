Title: Track Leave Accrual Balances

User Story: As an employee I want to view my leave accrual balances So that I know how much leave I have available

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Viewing leave balance
Given I am logged into my profile
When I navigate to the leave section
Then I should see my current accruals for PTO, sick, and unpaid leave

Additional Details:
- Priority: Medium
- Story Points: 2
- Dependencies: Payroll integration
- Notes: Support real-time updates and historical balance view.
