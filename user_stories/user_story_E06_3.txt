Title: Leave Accrual and Balance Management

User Story: As an HR manager I want leave balances to automatically accrue and update based on company policies so that employees and supervisors have accurate, real-time information

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Accrual and balance update
Given an employee's leave accrual policy is set
When a leave request is approved or a new accrual period begins
Then the employee's leave balance should be updated and visible in their profile

Additional Details:
- Priority: Medium
- Story Points: 2
- Dependencies: Employee Master Data, RBAC
- Notes: Accrual rules configurable per department; audit log for changes to balances.
