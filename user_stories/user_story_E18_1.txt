Title: Multi-Site and Time Zone Support

User Story: As a warehouse supervisor I want to manage multiple sites with their own time zones and policies So that operations are tailored to each location

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Site and time zone assignment
Given multiple warehouse sites are configured
When I assign employees and shifts to a site
Then date/time is displayed in the correct site time zone and site-specific policies apply

Additional Details:
- Priority: Low
- Story Points: 3
- Dependencies: E01, E02, E05
- Notes: Employees must be assigned to a site; shift templates per site.
