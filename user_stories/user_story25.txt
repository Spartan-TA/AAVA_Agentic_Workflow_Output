Title: Safety Officer Reviews Incident
User Story: As a safety officer I want to review and investigate reported safety incidents So that corrective actions can be taken and OSHA compliance is maintained
Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Review safety incident
Given a safety incident is reported
When I review the incident details and add investigation notes
Then the incident status is updated and corrective actions are logged
Additional Details:
- Priority: High
- Story Points: 3
- Dependencies: Incident reporting module, corrective action tracking
- Notes: Investigation notes should be timestamped.
