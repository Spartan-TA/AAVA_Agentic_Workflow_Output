Title: Manage Employee Certifications

User Story: As an HR manager I want to create, update, and delete employee certifications so that I can ensure compliance and track required qualifications.

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Certification CRUD operations
Given I am on the employee profile page
When I add, edit, or remove a certification
Then the changes are saved and reflected in the employee's certification list

Additional Details:
- Priority: High
- Story Points: 5
- Dependencies: E02, E03
- Notes: Must support multiple certification types and upload proof documents.
