Title: Export Approved Leaves and Audit Trail

User Story:
As a compliance officer, I want to export all approved leave records and their audit trail so that I can demonstrate compliance with labor regulations and internal policies.

Acceptance Criteria:
- Scenario: Export approved leave records
  Given approved leave requests exist
  When the officer exports the leave report
  Then all relevant fields and approval history are included in the export

- Scenario: Audit trail is complete
  Given leave requests are processed
  When the audit log is reviewed
  Then all actions (request, approve, deny) are traceable by user and timestamp

Additional Details:
- Export must be available in CSV format
- Audit log must be filterable by date, user, and leave type
- Exported data must match internal records
