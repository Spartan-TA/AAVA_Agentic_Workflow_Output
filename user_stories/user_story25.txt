Title: Manage Blackout Dates and Operation Calendars

User Story: As an HR manager I want to define blackout dates and operation calendars So that shifts are not scheduled during holidays or maintenance periods

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Adding a blackout date
Given I am on the calendar management page
When I add a new blackout date
Then the system should prevent shift assignments on that date and notify affected supervisors

Additional Details:
- Priority: Medium
- Story Points: 3
- Dependencies: Shift Templates, Employee Master Data
- Notes: Blackout dates should support recurring holidays and one-off events.
