Title: Support Localization & Multi-Warehouse

User Story: As a warehouse supervisor I want to manage multiple warehouse locations and switch between English and Spanish interfaces So that operations are streamlined across sites and language barriers are reduced

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Switch warehouse and language
Given I am logged in as a supervisor
When I select a different warehouse and change the language preference
Then the system updates the interface and data context accordingly

Additional Details:
- Priority: Low
- Story Points: 6
- Dependencies: E01 (Project Scaffolding & Domain Setup), E02 (Employee Master Data CRUD), E05 (Shift & Schedule Management)
- Notes: Support dynamic language switching and warehouse-specific data.
