Title: Upload and Store Employee Documents
User Story: As an HR manager I want to upload and store employee documents So that records are securely maintained and accessible
Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Document upload
Given I have a document to upload
When I submit it via the system
Then it is stored and linked to the employee profile
Additional Details:
- Priority: Medium
- Story Points: 2
- Dependencies: Employee Master Data, Document Storage
- Notes: File types and size limits enforced

Additional Details:
- Priority: Medium
- Story Points: 2
