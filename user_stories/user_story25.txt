Title: Certification Expiry Alerts

User Story: As an employee I want to receive alerts before my certifications expire So that I can renew them on time and avoid assignment blocks

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Certification expiry notification
Given my certification is expiring in 30 or 7 days
When the system detects the upcoming expiry
Then I receive an alert via my preferred notification channel

Additional Details:
- Priority: High
- Story Points: 2
- Dependencies: Certification CRUD, Notifications
- Notes: Alerts should be configurable for timing and channel.
