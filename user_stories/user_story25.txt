Title: Support Multiple Warehouse Locations

User Story: As an admin I want to configure and manage multiple warehouse locations so that employee data and operations are separated by site

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Managing warehouse locations
Given I am an admin
When I add or update a warehouse location
Then the location is available for assignment to employees and operations

Additional Details:
- Priority: Medium
- Story Points: 2
- Dependencies: Employee Master Data, Scheduling
- Notes: Location assigned to employees and shifts

Scenario: Filtering reports by warehouse

When I select a warehouse location
Then the data and reports are filtered accordingly

Additional Details:
- Priority: Medium
- Story Points: 1

Title: Assign Employees to Warehouses

User Story: As an HR manager I want to assign employees to specific warehouses so that scheduling and compliance are managed per site

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Assigning employees to a warehouse
Given I am managing employee records
When I assign a warehouse location
Then the employee is linked to that site for all relevant operations

Additional Details:
- Priority: Medium
- Story Points: 1
- Dependencies: Employee Master Data, Warehouse data
- Notes: Employees may have primary and secondary sites
