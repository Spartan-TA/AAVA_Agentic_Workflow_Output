Title: Generate OSHA-Compliant Reports

User Story: As a compliance officer I want to generate OSHA-compliant safety incident reports so that regulatory requirements are met

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Generating OSHA report
Given I am logged in as a compliance officer
When I select a reporting period and generate a report
Then I receive a formatted OSHA-compliant report including all required incident data

Additional Details:
- Priority: High
- Story Points: 3
- Dependencies: Incident log, reporting module
- Notes: Reports must be exportable in PDF and Excel formats.
