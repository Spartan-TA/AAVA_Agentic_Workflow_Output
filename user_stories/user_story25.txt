Title: Safety Incident Investigation Workflow

User Story: As a safety officer I want to initiate and track investigations for recorded safety incidents So that root causes are identified and corrective actions are documented

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Investigation initiation
Given a recorded safety incident
When I start an investigation
Then the system should create a workflow and assign tasks to relevant personnel

Scenario: Investigation closure
Given an ongoing investigation
When all tasks are completed
Then the investigation should be marked as closed and a summary report generated

Additional Details:
- Priority: High
- Story Points: 6
- Dependencies: Safety Incident Recording
- Notes: Support linking corrective actions to incident records.
