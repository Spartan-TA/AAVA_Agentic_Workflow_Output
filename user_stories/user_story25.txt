Title: List and Search Employees

User Story: As an HR administrator or supervisor I want to list and search employee records by name, badgeId, department, or status So that I can quickly find and manage employee information

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Successful employee search
Given I am on the employee list page
When I enter search criteria or filters
Then only matching employee records should be displayed

Additional Details:
- Priority: High
- Story Points: 2
- Dependencies: Project Scaffolding & Domain Setup (E01)
- Notes: Support pagination and sorting for large datasets.
