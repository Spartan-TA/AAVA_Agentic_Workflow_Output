EPIC E06: LEAVE & ABSENCE MANAGEMENT

===========================================

Title: Request Paid Time Off (PTO)

User Story: As an employee I want to request PTO So that I can plan personal time in advance

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Requesting PTO
Given I am logged in
When I submit a PTO request with dates
Then my supervisor should receive a notification for approval

Additional Details:
- Priority: High
- Story Points: 2
- Dependencies: Notifications & Announcements
- Notes: Show remaining PTO balance

===========================================

Title: Approve Leave Requests

User Story: As a supervisor I want to approve or reject leave requests So that team coverage is maintained

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Approving leave
Given a pending leave request
When I review and approve or reject it
Then the employee should be notified and the schedule updated

Additional Details:
- Priority: High
- Story Points: 2
- Dependencies: Shift & Schedule Management
- Notes: Capture reason for rejection

===========================================

Title: Track Sick Leave Usage

User Story: As an HR manager I want to track sick leave usage So that compliance and reporting are maintained

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Tracking sick leave
Given employees have taken sick leave
When I generate a report
Then I should see usage by employee and department

Additional Details:
- Priority: Medium
- Story Points: 3
- Dependencies: Reporting & Analytics
- Notes: Export to CSV

===========================================

Title: Handle Unpaid Leave Requests

User Story: As an employee I want to request unpaid leave So that I can manage personal emergencies

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Requesting unpaid leave
Given I am logged in
When I submit an unpaid leave request
Then my supervisor should receive a notification and approve/reject

Additional Details:
- Priority: Medium
- Story Points: 2
- Dependencies: Notifications & Announcements
- Notes: Unpaid leave does not affect PTO balance
