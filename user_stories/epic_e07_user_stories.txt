### Epic E07: Training & Certification Tracking

**User Story 1**
Title: Track Employee Certifications
User Story: As a safety manager, I want to record employee certifications so that compliance is maintained.
Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Certification CRUD
Given valid certification data
When I create, update, or delete a certification
Then the employee's profile reflects the change
Additional Details:
- Priority: High
- Story Points: 2
- Dependencies: Employee API, RBAC
- Notes: Forklift, PPE, etc.

**User Story 2**
Title: Certification Expiry Alerts
User Story: As a supervisor, I want alerts for expiring certifications so that renewals are not missed.
Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Expiry Alert
Given a certification expires in 30 or 7 days
When the system checks expiry dates
Then an alert is sent to the employee and supervisor
Additional Details:
- Priority: High
- Story Points: 2
- Dependencies: Certification tracking
- Notes: Alert channels configurable

**User Story 3**
Title: Block Assignment for Expired Certifications
User Story: As a scheduler, I want to prevent assignment of tasks to employees with expired certifications so that safety is ensured.
Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Assignment Block
Given an employee's certification is expired
When I assign them to a restricted task
Then the system blocks the assignment
Additional Details:
- Priority: High
- Story Points: 2
- Dependencies: Certification tracking, shift assignment
- Notes: Status visible on profile

**User Story 4**
Title: Upload Certification Proof
User Story: As an employee, I want to upload proof documents for certifications so that records are complete.
Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Upload Document
Given a valid certification
When I upload a document
Then it is attached to my profile
Additional Details:
- Priority: Medium
- Story Points: 1
- Dependencies: Certification tracking
- Notes: Document storage required
