EPIC E07: Training & Certification Tracking

Title: Track Certifications
User Story: As a safety officer I want to track employee certifications and expirations So that compliance is maintained
Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Certification tracking
Given certifications are entered
When I view employee profiles
Then certification status and expiry are visible
Additional Details:
- Priority: High
- Story Points: 2
- Dependencies: E02, E03
- Notes: Block expired assignments

---

Title: Alert on Expiring Certifications
User Story: As a supervisor I want alerts for certifications expiring soon So that renewals are managed proactively
Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Expiry alerts
Given a certification is expiring in 30/7 days
When I view alerts
Then I am notified to renew
Additional Details:
- Priority: High
- Story Points: 1
- Dependencies: E02, E03
- Notes: Email/SMS notification

---

Title: Upload Proof Documents
User Story: As an employee I want to upload proof of certification So that my qualifications are validated
Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Document upload
Given I have a certification
When I upload proof
Then it is stored and linked to my profile
Additional Details:
- Priority: High
- Story Points: 1
- Dependencies: E02, E03
- Notes: Secure storage
