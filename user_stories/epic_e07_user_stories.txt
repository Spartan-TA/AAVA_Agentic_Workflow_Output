### Epic E07: Training & Certification Tracking

**User Story 1**
Title: Track Employee Certifications
User Story: As a safety manager I want to record and track required certifications for employees So that only qualified staff are assigned to sensitive tasks
Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Certification CRUD
Given I have access
When I create, update, or delete a certification
Then the certification is reflected on the employee profile
Additional Details:
- Priority: High
- Story Points: 2
- Dependencies: E02, E03
- Notes: Certification types and expiration dates required

**User Story 2**
Title: Alert on Expiring Certifications
User Story: As a supervisor I want to receive alerts before certifications expire So that renewals are not missed
Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Expiry alert
Given a certification is expiring in 30 or 7 days
When I view alerts
Then I am notified of the upcoming expiration
Additional Details:
- Priority: High
- Story Points: 2
- Dependencies: E02, E03
- Notes: Alerts sent via in-app and email

**User Story 3**
Title: Block Assignment to Tasks Requiring Valid Certification
User Story: As a scheduler I want to prevent assignment of tasks to employees with expired certifications So that safety is ensured
Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Assignment block
Given an employee's certification is expired
When I assign them to a restricted task
Then the system blocks the assignment and shows an error
Additional Details:
- Priority: High
- Story Points: 3
- Dependencies: E02, E03
- Notes: Scheduling checks must be enforced

**User Story 4**
Title: Upload Proof of Certification Documents
User Story: As an employee I want to upload proof of my certifications So that my records are complete
Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Document upload
Given I am authenticated
When I upload a certification document
Then it is stored and linked to my profile
Additional Details:
- Priority: High
- Story Points: 2
- Dependencies: E02, E03
- Notes: Supported formats: PDF, JPG, PNG
