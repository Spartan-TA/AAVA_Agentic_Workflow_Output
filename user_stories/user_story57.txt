Title: AI-Based Shift Swap Recommendations

User Story: As an employee I want the system to recommend optimal shift swaps based on availability and preferences so that I can easily find suitable replacements

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Receiving shift swap recommendations
Given I request a shift swap
When I submit my availability and preferences
Then the system suggests coworkers who are most likely to accept the swap

Additional Details:
- Priority: Medium
- Story Points: 3
- Dependencies: Scheduling module, AI engine
- Notes: Recommendations should consider compliance with labor rules.
