Title: Alerting for SLOs

User Story: As a DevOps engineer I want to receive alerts when Service Level Objectives (SLOs) are breached So that I can respond quickly to incidents and maintain reliability

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: SLO breach alerting
Given SLO thresholds are configured
When a metric exceeds the threshold
Then an alert should be sent to the designated channels

Additional Details:
- Priority: High
- Story Points: 3
- Dependencies: Monitoring module, alerting system
- Notes: Support for email, SMS, and Slack notifications.
