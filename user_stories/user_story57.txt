Title: Mobile Schedule Viewing and Leave Request

User Story: As a warehouse worker I want to view my schedule and request leave from my mobile device So that I can manage my work-life balance conveniently

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Mobile schedule and leave request
Given I have installed the PWA
When I access my schedule or submit a leave request
Then the information is displayed responsively and leave requests are queued offline if needed

Additional Details:
- Priority: Medium
- Story Points: 5
- Dependencies: E04, E05, E06, E12
- Notes: Must support offline queue and conflict resolution
