Title: HRIS Sync Job Creates and Updates Employee Records

User Story: As an HR manager I want the HRIS sync job to automatically create and update employee records So that onboarding and personnel changes are reflected in real time

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: HRIS sync job
Given new hires or terminations are processed in HRIS
When the sync job runs
Then employee records are created, updated, or deactivated in the EMS system

Additional Details:
- Priority: High
- Story Points: 5
- Dependencies: E01, E02, E03
- Notes: Sync job must be idempotent and log all changes.
