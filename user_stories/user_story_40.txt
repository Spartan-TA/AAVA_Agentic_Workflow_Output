Title: Generate Performance Review Report

User Story: As an HR administrator I want to generate reports on performance review completion and ratings So that I can monitor compliance and identify high/low performers

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Generate review report
Given I am logged into the system
When I select a review cycle and department
Then I should receive a report of completion rates and rating distributions

Additional Details:
- Priority: Medium
- Story Points: 2
- Dependencies: Reporting & Analytics module
- Notes: Reports should be exportable to CSV.
