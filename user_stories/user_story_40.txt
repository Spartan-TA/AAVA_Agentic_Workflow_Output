Title: Notify Users of Expiring Certifications

User Story: As an employee I want to receive notifications when my certifications are about to expire so that I can renew them in time and avoid work interruptions.

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Certification expiration notification
Given my certification is set to expire within 30 days
When the system checks certification statuses
Then I should receive a notification with renewal instructions

Additional Details:
- Priority: Medium
- Story Points: 2
- Dependencies: E03, E05, E06, E07
- Notes: Notification timing should be configurable (e.g., 30, 15, 7 days before expiry).
