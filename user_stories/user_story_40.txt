Title: PDF Export and Role-Based Visibility for Reviews

User Story: As an HR administrator I want to export completed performance reviews as PDFs and control who can view them so that records are secure and accessible for audits

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Export and restrict review access
  Given a performance review is completed
  When I export the review or set visibility permissions
  Then the PDF is generated and only authorized roles can access the review

Additional Details:
- Priority: Medium
- Story Points: 2
- Dependencies: E02 (User Management), E03 (Scheduling)
- Notes: History must be immutable after sign-off
