Title: Notify Expiring Certifications

User Story: As an employee I want to receive notifications when my certifications are about to expire So that I can renew them and remain eligible for assignments

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Notify certification expiration
Given my certification is nearing expiration
When the expiration date is within the notification window
Then I should receive a reminder via email and/or mobile app

Additional Details:
- Priority: High
- Story Points: 2
- Dependencies: Certification tracking, notification system
- Notes: Support configurable notification windows.
