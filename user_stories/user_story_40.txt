Title: Assign Equipment to Employees

User Story: As an equipment manager I want to assign equipment to employees So that asset tracking and accountability are maintained

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Equipment assignment
Given I am logged in as an equipment manager
When I assign equipment to an employee
Then the assignment should be recorded and the employee notified

Additional Details:
- Priority: High
- Story Points: 3
- Dependencies: Equipment inventory, notifications
- Notes: Support for bulk assignment
