Title: Offboarding Audit Log and Compliance Validation

User Story: As a compliance officer I want all offboarding actions to be logged and validated for compliance So that I can ensure terminated employees are properly removed and assets accounted for

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Offboarding audit
Given an employee is offboarded
When the process completes
Then all actions (access revocation, asset collection, schedule update) are logged in the audit trail and validated against compliance requirements

Additional Details:
- Priority: Medium
- Story Points: 3
- Dependencies: E02, E03, E05, E07, E09, E13
- Notes: Audit log must be exportable for compliance review.
