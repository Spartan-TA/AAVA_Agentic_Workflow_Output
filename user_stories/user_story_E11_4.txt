Title: Audit Log for Payroll Exports

User Story: As a compliance officer I want every payroll export to be logged with actor, timestamp, and delivery status so that we can trace and audit all payroll activities.

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Export audit logging
Given a payroll export is initiated
When the export is completed or fails
Then an audit log entry should be created with all relevant details

Additional Details:
- Priority: High
- Story Points: 3
- Dependencies: Time & Attendance (E04), Leave & Absence Management (E06)
- Notes: Audit logs must be tamper-evident and exportable by date or user.
