### E08: Safety Incidents & OSHA Reporting

**User Story 1: Record Safety Incident**
User Story: As a supervisor I want to record incidents and near-misses So that safety data is captured
Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Submit incident
Given an incident occurs
When I POST to /safety/incidents
Then the incident is recorded with required fields
Additional Details:
- Priority: High
- Story Points: 2
- Dependencies: E02, E03
- Notes: Severity, location, description required.
