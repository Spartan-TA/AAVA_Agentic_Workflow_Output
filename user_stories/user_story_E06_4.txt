Title: Export Approved Leaves for Payroll
User Story: As a payroll specialist I want to export approved leaves So that payroll calculations exclude non-working hours and remain accurate
Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Leave export
Given there are approved leave requests for the pay period
When I generate the payroll export
Then all approved leaves should be included and mapped to the provider format
Additional Details:
- Priority: Medium
- Story Points: 2
- Dependencies: Payroll integration, leave approval workflow
- Notes: Export failures should be retried and logged for audit.
