Title: Exclude Approved Leave from Scheduling

User Story: As a scheduler I want approved leave to be excluded from shift assignments So that employees are not scheduled when unavailable

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Scheduling with approved leave
Given an employee has approved leave
When I assign shifts for that period
Then the system should prevent assignment and alert me

Additional Details:
- Priority: High
- Story Points: 2
- Dependencies: Leave approval workflow
- Notes: Support override with justification and audit entry.
