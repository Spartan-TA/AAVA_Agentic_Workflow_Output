Title: Certification Tracking and Alerts

User Story: As a compliance officer, I want to track employee certifications, expirations, and upload proof documents so that only qualified staff are assigned to sensitive roles.

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Expiration alerts
Given a certification is expiring in 30 or 7 days
When the system checks certification status
Then an alert is generated for the employee and supervisor

Scenario: Assignment validation
Given an assignment requires a valid certification
When the certification is expired
Then the assignment is blocked

Additional Details:
- Priority: High
- Story Points: 5
- Dependencies: Employee CRUD API, RBAC
- Notes: Certification status visible on employee profile.
