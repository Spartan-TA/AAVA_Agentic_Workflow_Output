Title: Shift Assignment and Conflict Detection

User Story: As a scheduler I want to assign shifts to employees and detect scheduling conflicts So that no employee is double-booked or assigned overlapping shifts

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Shift assignment
Given an employee and available shift
When I assign the shift
Then the system should validate for conflicts and confirm assignment

Scenario: Conflict detection
Given an employee with existing shifts
When I attempt to assign an overlapping shift
Then the system should prevent the assignment and display a warning

Additional Details:
- Priority: High
- Story Points: 5
- Dependencies: Shift Template Management
- Notes: Support bulk assignment and conflict reporting.
