Title: Detect and Prevent Scheduling Conflicts

User Story: As a supervisor I want the system to automatically detect and prevent scheduling conflicts so that employees are not double-booked or assigned to overlapping shifts

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Schedule conflict detection
Given an employee is assigned to a shift
When a new shift overlaps with an existing assignment
Then the system blocks the assignment and notifies the supervisor

Additional Details:
- Priority: High
- Story Points: 2
- Dependencies: Shift templates, Employee schedules
- Notes: Must handle blackout dates and warehouse operation calendars.
