Title: Clock-In/Out Endpoint

User Story: As a WORKER I want to clock in and out via a mobile or web endpoint so that my attendance is accurately recorded for payroll and compliance

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Successful clock-in
Given I am scheduled for a shift and at the warehouse
When I submit a clock-in event with valid credentials
Then my attendance is recorded and associated with my shift

Additional Details:
- Priority: High
- Story Points: 3
- Dependencies: E02, E03
- Notes: Support geofence/device capture; validate against shift schedule.
