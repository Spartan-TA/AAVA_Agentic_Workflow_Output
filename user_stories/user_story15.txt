Title: Reporting & Analytics

User Story: As an operations manager I want to generate and export operational reports (attendance, overtime, leave, certifications, safety KPIs) so that I can make data-driven decisions for staffing, safety, and compliance.

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Filter and export reports
  Given operational data exists
  When I filter by date, department, or shift and request an export
  Then I receive a CSV or PDF within 10 seconds for up to 50,000 rows

Scenario: Access control
  Given reports are available
  When I log in with appropriate role
  Then I can access only the reports I am authorized to view

Scenario: Metrics endpoints
  Given BI tools are integrated
  When I access metrics endpoints
  Then data is available for dashboards and analytics

Additional Details:
- Priority: Medium
- Story Points: 8
- Dependencies: Time & Attendance (E04), Shift & Schedule Management (E05), Leave & Absence Management (E06), Training & Certification Tracking (E07), Safety Incidents & OSHA Reporting (E08), Audit Trail & Compliance (E14)
- Notes: Role-based dashboards; export performance; BI integration.
