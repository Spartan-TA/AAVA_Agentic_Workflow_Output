Title: Reporting & Analytics - Generate Operational Reports
User Story: As an operations analyst, I want to generate reports on attendance, overtime, leave, certs, and safety KPIs so that I can make data-driven decisions.
Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Generate report
  Given data exists
  When I filter by date, department, or shift
  Then the report is generated within 10 seconds for up to 50k rows

Priority: Medium
Story Points: 3
Dependencies: E04, E05, E06, E07, E08, E14
Notes: Supports large-scale reporting
