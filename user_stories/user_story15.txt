Title: Supervisor Approves Attendance

User Story: As a SUPERVISOR I want to review and approve employee attendance records So that I can ensure accuracy before payroll processing

Acceptance Criteria:

Scenario: Supervisor reviews attendance
Given I am logged in as SUPERVISOR
When I view attendance records for my team
Then I can approve or flag records for correction

Scenario: Attendance flagged for correction
Given a record is flagged
When I submit a correction request
Then the employee is notified to review and amend their record

Additional Details:
- Priority: High
- Story Points: 5
- Dependencies: E04-1
- Notes: Approval workflow must be auditable.
