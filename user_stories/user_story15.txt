#### User Story 15: Create Recurring Shift Templates
**As an** HR user  
**I want** to create and manage recurring shift templates  
**so that** scheduling is efficient and consistent.

**Acceptance Criteria:**
- **Given** shift details,  
  **When** I create a template,  
  **Then** it is saved and available for assignment.

**Additional Details:**  
Priority: High  
Story Points: 2  
Dependencies: E02, E03, E04  
Notes: Support weekly, bi-weekly, and custom recurrence.
