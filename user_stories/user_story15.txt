Title: Record Safety Incidents & OSHA Reporting

User Story: As a safety officer I want to record workplace incidents and manage investigation workflows So that compliance with OSHA regulations is maintained and corrective actions are tracked

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Record and investigate safety incident
Given an incident is reported
When the safety officer logs the incident and initiates an investigation
Then the workflow tracks progress and generates OSHA-compliant reports

Additional Details:
- Priority: High
- Story Points: 7
- Dependencies: E02 (Employee Master Data CRUD), E03 (RBAC)
- Notes: Must support attachments and incident categorization.
