Title: Reporting & Analytics
User Story: As an operations manager I want to generate operational reports and dashboards for attendance, overtime, leave, certifications, and safety so that data-driven staffing and compliance decisions can be made.
Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Report generation and export
Given I am authenticated as an operations manager
When I filter and export reports by date, department, or shift
Then exports complete within 10s for up to 50k rows
And access is controlled by role
And metrics endpoints are available for BI
Additional Details:
- Priority: Medium
- Story Points: 8
- Dependencies: Time & Attendance (Clock In/Out), Shift & Schedule Management, Leave & Absence Management, Training & Certification Tracking, Safety Incidents & OSHA Reporting, Audit Trail & Compliance
- Notes: Support for CSV/PDF export and role-based dashboards.
