Title: Equipment & Asset Assignment

User Story: As a warehouse supervisor I want to assign and track equipment and assets to employees So that accountability is maintained and only qualified staff use sensitive equipment

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Asset check-out
Given an employee is qualified and available
When I assign an asset to them
Then the system records the check-out and updates asset status

Scenario: Block assignment for invalid certification
Given an employee lacks required certification
When I attempt to assign restricted equipment
Then the system blocks the assignment and logs the attempt

Additional Details:
- Priority: Medium
- Story Points: 5
- Dependencies: Employee Master Data, RBAC, Training & Certification Tracking
- Notes: Asset registry CRUD; overdue returns report; history log per asset and employee
