### E15 - Reporting & Analytics

**User Story 1:**
Title: Generate Operational Reports
User Story: As a manager, I want reports for attendance, overtime, leave, certifications, and safety so that I can monitor operations.
Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Generate comprehensive report
  Given operational data
  When I request a report
  Then it is generated with relevant metrics
Additional Details:
- Priority: Medium
- Story Points: 3
- Dependencies: E04, E05, E06, E07, E08, E14
- Notes: Data-driven

**User Story 2:**

Acceptance Criteria:

- Priority: Medium
