User Story: As an HR manager I want to create recurring shift templates So that I can efficiently schedule employees for regular warehouse operations

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Create shift template
Given I am in the shift management module
When I define a new recurring shift template
Then the template is saved and available for assignment

Additional Details:
- Priority: High
- Story Points: 2
- Dependencies: E02, E03, E04
- Notes: Templates support daily, weekly, and custom recurrence
