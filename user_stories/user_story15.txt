Title: Manager Submits OSHA Report

User Story: As a manager I want to review safety incidents and submit OSHA reports So that regulatory compliance is maintained

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Submitting OSHA report
Given I am logged in as a manager and have access to incident reports
When I select incidents and generate an OSHA report
Then the report is formatted according to OSHA standards and submitted to authorities

Additional Details:
- Priority: High
- Story Points: 5
- Dependencies: Safety Incident Reports, Reporting Module
- Notes: Include export to PDF and digital signature functionality.
