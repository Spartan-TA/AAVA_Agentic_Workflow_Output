Title: Attendance Report and CSV Export
User Story: As an HR manager I want to export attendance reports in CSV format So that I can analyze shift totals and compliance for payroll and audits
Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Export attendance report
Given I have access to the attendance reporting dashboard
When I select a date range and export as CSV
Then the report is generated with daily totals and corrections, and is downloadable within 10 seconds
Additional Details:
- Priority: High
- Story Points: 5
- Dependencies: E02, E03, E04
- Notes: Export must include approved corrections and be auditable.
