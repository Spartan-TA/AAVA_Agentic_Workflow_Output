Title: Safety Incident Reporting
User Story: As a supervisor I want to record safety incidents and near-misses So that investigations and corrective actions can be tracked
Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Submit incident report
Given a workplace incident
When I POST to /safety/incidents with required details
Then the incident is logged and status set to Open
Additional Details:
- Priority: High
- Story Points: 3
- Dependencies: E02, E03
- Notes: OSHA exportable
