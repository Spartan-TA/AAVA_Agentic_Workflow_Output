Title: Certification CRUD & Alerts
User Story: As a supervisor, I want to manage employee certifications and receive alerts before expiration so that compliance is maintained.
Acceptance Criteria:
  Given a certification is expiring in 30 or 7 days,
  When I view alerts,
  Then I am notified.

  Given a certification is expired,
  When I assign a task requiring it,
  Then the system blocks the assignment.

  Given a certification document,
  When I upload it,
  Then it is stored and visible on the employee profile.

Additional Details:
  Priority: High
  Story Points: 5
  Dependencies: Employee CRUD, RBAC
  Notes: Scheduling checks enforced.
