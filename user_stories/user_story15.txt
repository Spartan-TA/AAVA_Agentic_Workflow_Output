Title: View Shift Schedule
User Story: As an employee I want to view my upcoming shift schedule So that I can plan my availability accordingly
Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: View shift schedule
Given I am logged into the system
When I navigate to the shift schedule page
Then I should see a list of my upcoming shifts with dates and times
Additional Details:
- Priority: High
- Story Points: 2
- Dependencies: Shift data, user authentication
- Notes: Display shifts in calendar and list formats.
