EPIC E15 - Reporting & Analytics

===========================================

User Story 1: Filterable Operational Reports
---------------------------------------------
Title: Generate Filterable Operational Reports

User Story: As a manager, I want reports filtered by date, department, and shift so that data-driven decisions are possible.

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Report filtering
  Given report endpoint
  When filters are applied
  Then results match criteria

Additional Details:
- Priority: Medium
- Story Points: 3
- Dependencies: E04, E05, E06, E07, E08, E14
- Notes: Support multiple report types.

===========================================

User Story 2: Export Reports as CSV/PDF
---------------------------------------------
