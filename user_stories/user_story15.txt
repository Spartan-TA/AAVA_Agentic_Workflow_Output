Title: Bulk Assign Shifts and Generate Audit Entries

User Story: As a supervisor I want to bulk-assign shifts to multiple employees and generate audit entries So that scheduling is efficient and changes are tracked

Acceptance Criteria:

Scenario: Bulk assignment of shifts
Given a list of employees and a shift template
When I perform a bulk assignment
Then all selected employees receive the shift

Scenario: Audit entry is generated for each assignment
Given a shift is assigned
When the assignment is saved
Then an audit entry records the actor, timestamp, and details

Additional Details:
- Priority: Medium
- Story Points: 5
- Dependencies: Shift Templates and Schedules
- Notes: Ensure atomicity of bulk operations; audit log schema defined
