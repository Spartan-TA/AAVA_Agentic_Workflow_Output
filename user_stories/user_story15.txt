Title: Employee View Schedule

User Story: As an employee I want to view my assigned shifts and schedule so that I can plan my work and attendance

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Viewing schedule
Given I am logged in as an employee
When I navigate to my schedule page
Then I see a calendar or list of all my assigned shifts

Additional Details:
- Priority: High
- Story Points: 2
- Dependencies: Shift assignment
- Notes: Schedule should be accessible on both web and mobile.
