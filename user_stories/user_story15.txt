Title: Reporting & Analytics

User Story: As an operations manager I want to generate and export operational reports and dashboards so that staffing, safety, and compliance decisions are data-driven.

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Report generation and export
Given attendance, overtime, leave, certification, and safety data is available
When I filter and export reports by date, department, or shift
Then exports complete within 10 seconds for up to 50,000 rows
And access is controlled by role
And metrics endpoints are available for BI

Additional Details:
- Priority: Medium
- Story Points: 8
- Dependencies: Time & Attendance (Clock In/Out), Shift & Schedule Management, Leave & Absence Management, Training & Certification Tracking, Safety Incidents & OSHA Reporting, Audit Trail & Compliance
- Notes: Support CSV/PDF export and role-based dashboards.
