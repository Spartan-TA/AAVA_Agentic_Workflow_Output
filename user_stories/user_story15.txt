Title: Record Safety Incident
User Story: As a supervisor I want to record safety incidents and near-misses So that we can investigate and improve safety
Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Record incident Given an incident occurs When I submit details via the safety module Then the incident is logged with severity, location, and involved employees
Additional Details:
- Priority: High
- Story Points: 2
- Dependencies: Employee Master Data, RBAC
- Notes: Validation on required fields

Title: Investigate Safety Incident

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Investigation workflow Given an incident is logged When I update status to Investigating Then tasks are generated for corrective actions
Additional Details:
- Priority: High
- Story Points: 2
- Dependencies: Employee Master Data, RBAC
- Notes: Status workflow (Open, Investigating, Resolved)
