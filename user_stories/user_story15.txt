### E15: Reporting & Analytics

#### User Story 1
Title: Generate Attendance and Overtime Reports

User Story: As an HR user, I want to generate attendance and overtime reports So that staffing decisions are data-driven.

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Attendance report generation
Given attendance data
When I request a report
Then data is filtered by date, department, shift and exported within 10s

Additional Details:
- Priority: Medium
- Story Points: 3
- Dependencies: E04, E05, E06, E07, E08, E14
- Notes: Export CSV/PDF supported
