Title: Employee Clock-Out Event

User Story: As a Warehouse Worker I want to clock out at the end of my shift So that my attendance and hours worked are accurately recorded for payroll and compliance

Acceptance Criteria:

Scenario: Successful clock-out
  Given my shift is scheduled for today
  When I submit a clock-out event via /attendance/clock-out
  Then my attendance is recorded and associated with my shift

Scenario: Missed punch correction workflow
  Given I missed a clock-out
  When I request a correction
  Then a supervisor is notified and can approve or deny the correction

Scenario: Daily totals are computed
  Given my clock-in and clock-out events
  When the system processes attendance
  Then my total hours worked for the day are calculated

Additional Details:
- Priority: High
- Story Points: 5
- Dependencies: E02, E03
- Notes: Support correction workflow; ensure accurate calculation of hours for payroll.
