Title: Shift Conflict Detection

User Story: As a scheduler I want the system to detect and prevent shift conflicts so that employees are not double-booked.

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Assign shift
Given an employee has a shift
When I assign an overlapping shift
Then the system blocks the assignment

Additional Details:
- Priority: High
- Story Points: 3
- Dependencies: Shift Template Management
- Notes: Blackout dates handled.
