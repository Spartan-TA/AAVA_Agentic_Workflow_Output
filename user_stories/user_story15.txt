Title: Reporting & Analytics

User Story: As an operations manager I want to generate and export operational reports and dashboards so that I can make data-driven decisions for staffing, safety, and compliance.

Acceptance Criteria:

Scenario: Filter and export reports
Given attendance, overtime, leave, certification, and safety data
When I filter by date, department, or shift
Then the report is generated and exportable as CSV/PDF within 10 seconds for up to 50,000 rows

Scenario: Role-based dashboards
Given I am logged in
When I access the dashboard
Then I see metrics relevant to my role

Scenario: Metrics endpoints
Given BI tools are integrated
When I access metrics endpoints
Then data is available for further analysis

Additional Details:
- Priority: Medium
- Story Points: 8
- Dependencies: Time & Attendance (E04), Shift & Schedule Management (E05), Leave & Absence Management (E06), Training & Certification Tracking (E07), Safety Incidents & OSHA Reporting (E08), Audit Trail & Compliance (E14)
- Notes: Access control required for reports and dashboards.
