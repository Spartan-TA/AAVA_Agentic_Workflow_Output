Epic: E15 - Reporting & Analytics

User Story 1: Operational Reports
Title: Generate Operational Reports
User Story: As a manager, I want reports for attendance, overtime, leave balances, certification status, and safety KPIs so that decisions are data-driven.
Acceptance Criteria (Gherkin):
Given operational data exists,
When I generate a report,
Then the report includes all relevant metrics.
Additional Details:
Priority: Medium
Story Points: 2
Dependencies: E04, E05, E06, E07, E08, E14
Notes: Supports analytics.

User Story 2: Export Reports
Title: Export Reports as CSV/PDF
User Story: As a manager, I want to export reports as CSV/PDF so that data can be shared.
Acceptance Criteria (Gherkin):

Additional Details:
Priority: Medium
Story Points: 2
