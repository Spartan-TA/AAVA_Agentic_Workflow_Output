Title: Performance Reviews & Goals
User Story: As a supervisor I want to conduct and record performance reviews for my team So that feedback and goals are documented
Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Create review cycle
Given a new review period
When I create a review template
Then it is available for assignment to employees

Scenario: Submit and acknowledge review
Given a completed review
When both supervisor and employee acknowledge
Then the review is locked and archived

Additional Details:
- Priority: Medium
- Story Points: 5
- Dependencies: Employee CRUD API, RBAC
- Notes: PDF export; immutable after sign-off.
