**E09: Equipment & Asset Assignment**

1. Title: Register Equipment and Assets
User Story: As an asset manager, I want to register scanners, forklifts, and PPE so that all assets are tracked in the system.
Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Asset Registration
Given asset details
When I POST to /assets
Then the asset is added to the registry
Additional Details:
- Priority: Medium
- Story Points: 2
- Dependencies: E02, E03, E07
- Notes: Include asset condition.

2. Title: Assign Assets to Employees
User Story: As a supervisor, I want to assign assets to employees so that accountability is maintained.
Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Asset Assignment
Given an asset and employee
When I assign the asset
Then the assignment is logged
Additional Details:
- Priority: Medium
- Story Points: 2
- Dependencies: Story 1
- Notes: Block if certs invalid.

3. Title: Track Asset Check-In/Out
User Story: As an employee, I want to check in and out assets so that usage and returns are recorded.
Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Asset Check-In/Out
Given an assigned asset
When I check in/out
Then the event is logged and overdue returns are flagged
Additional Details:
- Priority: Medium
- Story Points: 2
- Dependencies: Story 2
- Notes: Notify on overdue.

4. Title: Asset History Log
User Story: As an asset manager, I want to view the history of asset assignments and condition so that audits are possible.
Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: History Log
Given an asset
When I view its history
Then all assignments and condition changes are listed
Additional Details:
- Priority: Medium
- Story Points: 2
- Dependencies: Story 1
- Notes: Support export.
