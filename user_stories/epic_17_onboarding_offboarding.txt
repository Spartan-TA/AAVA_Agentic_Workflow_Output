### E17: Onboarding & Offboarding Workflow

**User Story 1**
- Title: Automated Onboarding from HRIS
- User Story: As an HR user, I want new hires from HRIS to trigger onboarding tasks so that provisioning is automated.
- Acceptance Criteria in Gherkin format:
  Scenario: New hire onboarding
  Given a new hire appears in HRIS
  When the system syncs
  Then accounts, schedules, and training tasks are created
- Additional Details:
  * Priority: Medium
  * Story Points: 3
  * Dependencies: E02, E03, E05, E07, E09, E13
  * Notes: Asset assignment included.

**User Story 2**
- Title: Automated Offboarding Workflow
- User Story: As an HR user, I want offboarding to revoke access and collect assets so that terminations are secure.
- Acceptance Criteria in Gherkin format:
  Scenario: Offboarding process
  Given an employee is terminated
  When offboarding is triggered
  Then access is revoked, assets are collected, and schedules updated
- Additional Details:
  * Priority: Medium
  * Story Points: 3
  * Dependencies: E02, E03, E05, E07, E09, E13
  * Notes: Integration with asset registry.
