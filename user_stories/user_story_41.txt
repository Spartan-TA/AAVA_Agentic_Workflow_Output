Title: Notification Channel Preferences

User Story: As a user I want to opt-in or opt-out of in-app, email, and SMS notifications so that I can control how I receive important updates about my shifts and certifications

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: User manages notification preferences
  Given I am on the notification settings page
  When I select or deselect notification channels for shift changes, expiring certifications, approvals, and announcements
  Then my preferences are saved and notifications are sent only via my selected channels

Additional Details:
- Priority: Medium
- Story Points: 3
- Dependencies: E03, E05, E06, E07
- Notes: Preferences should be persisted and respected for all notification types.
