Title: View Assigned Equipment

User Story: As an employee I want to view the equipment assigned to me So that I can manage my responsibilities and report any issues

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Assigned equipment viewing
Given equipment is assigned to me
When I access my equipment dashboard
Then I should see a list of all assigned equipment with details

Additional Details:
- Priority: Medium
- Story Points: 2
- Dependencies: Equipment assignment
- Notes: Support for reporting equipment issues
