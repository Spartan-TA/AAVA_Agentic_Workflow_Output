### E06: Leave & Absence Management

**Epic Description:**
Request/approve PTO, sick, unpaid leave; accrual balances and policies; integration hooks to exclude from scheduling and payroll hours.

---

**User Story 1: Employee Leave Request Submission**

Title: Employee Leave Request Submission

User Story: As an employee, I want to submit requests for PTO, sick, or unpaid leave so that my absences are properly recorded and considered in scheduling.

Acceptance Criteria:

Scenario: Submit leave request
  Given I am logged in as an employee
  When I fill out and submit a leave request form specifying type, dates, and reason
  Then the request is saved with status "Pending" and my supervisor is notified

Additional Details:
- Priority: High
- Story Points: 3
- Dependencies: E02, E03
- Notes: Request types and policies configurable.

---

**User Story 2: Supervisor Leave Approval Workflow**

Title: Supervisor Leave Approval Workflow

User Story: As a supervisor, I want to review, approve, or deny leave requests so that staffing levels are maintained.

Acceptance Criteria:

Scenario: Approve or deny leave
  Given I am a supervisor with pending leave requests
  When I review a request and select approve or deny
  Then the employee is notified and the request status is updated accordingly

Additional Details:
- Priority: High
- Story Points: 2
- Dependencies: E02, E03, E05
- Notes: None

---

**User Story 3: Leave Accrual and Balance Tracking**

Title: Leave Accrual and Balance Tracking

User Story: As an employee, I want to view my current leave balances so that I know how much time off I have available.

Acceptance Criteria:

Scenario: View leave balances
  Given I am logged in
  When I navigate to my leave balance page
  Then I see up-to-date balances for PTO, sick, and unpaid leave

Additional Details:
- Priority: Medium
- Story Points: 2
- Dependencies: E02
- Notes: None

---

**User Story 4: Integration with Scheduling and Payroll**

Title: Integration with Scheduling and Payroll

User Story: As a system, I want approved leaves to automatically update schedules and payroll exports so that absences are accurately reflected.

Acceptance Criteria:

Scenario: Approved leave updates schedule and payroll
  Given a leave request is approved
  When the schedule and payroll export are generated
  Then the employee is marked as absent and hours are adjusted accordingly

Additional Details:
- Priority: High
- Story Points: 3
- Dependencies: E05, E11
- Notes: None

---

**User Story 5: Leave Export and Reporting**

Title: Leave Export and Reporting

User Story: As an HR user, I want to export leave records for a given period so that I can process compliance and payroll.
