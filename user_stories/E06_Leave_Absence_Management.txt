### E06: Leave & Absence Management

#### Title: Leave Request Workflow
User Story: As an employee I want to request PTO, sick, or unpaid leave So that my time off is tracked and approved
Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Leave request submitted
Given I am authenticated
When I submit a leave request
Then a supervisor receives approval task

Scenario: Leave approved/denied
Given a leave request exists
When supervisor acts
Then balances update and scheduled shifts flagged

Additional Details:
- Priority: Medium
- Story Points: 5
- Dependencies: E02, E03, E05
- Notes: Integration hooks for scheduling/payroll

#### Title: Accrual Balances & Policies
User Story: As an HR user I want to manage leave accrual balances and policies So that leave is compliant and accurate
Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Accrual balance updated
Given leave is approved
When accrual policy applies
Then balances update per policy

Additional Details:
- Priority: Medium
- Story Points: 3
- Dependencies: Leave Request Workflow
- Notes: Exports show approved leaves
