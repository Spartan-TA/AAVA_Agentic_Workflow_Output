EPIC E07: TRAINING & CERTIFICATION TRACKING

===========================================

Title: Track Employee Certifications

User Story: As an HR manager I want to track employee certifications So that compliance is maintained

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Tracking certifications
Given certification data is entered
When I view the certification dashboard
Then I should see status and expiration dates for all employees

Additional Details:
- Priority: High
- Story Points: 3
- Dependencies: Employee Master Data
- Notes: Highlight expired or expiring certifications

===========================================

Title: Notify Expiring Certifications

User Story: As an employee I want to receive notifications for expiring certifications So that I can renew them on time

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Certification expiration notification
Given my certification is expiring soon
When the system detects expiration within 30 days
Then I should receive an email and in-app notification

Additional Details:
- Priority: High
- Story Points: 2
- Dependencies: Notifications & Announcements
- Notes: Configurable notification window

===========================================

Title: Assign Mandatory Training

User Story: As a supervisor I want to assign mandatory training to team members So that compliance requirements are met

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Assigning training
Given a training module exists
When I assign it to selected employees
Then they should receive a notification and due date

Additional Details:
- Priority: Medium
- Story Points: 2
- Dependencies: Notifications & Announcements
- Notes: Track completion status

===========================================

Title: Upload Certification Documents

User Story: As an employee I want to upload certification documents So that my records are up to date

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Uploading documents
Given I have a valid certification
When I upload the document
Then it should be stored and linked to my profile

Additional Details:
- Priority: Medium
- Story Points: 2
- Dependencies: Employee Master Data
- Notes: Support PDF and image formats
