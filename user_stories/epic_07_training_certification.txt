### E07: Training & Certification Tracking

**User Story 1**
- Title: Manage Certifications
- User Story: As an HR user, I want to add, update, and remove employee certifications so that compliance is maintained.
- Acceptance Criteria in Gherkin format:
  Scenario: CRUD certifications
  Given I have certification details
  When I create, update, or delete a certification
  Then the changes are reflected in the employee's profile
- Additional Details:
  * Priority: High
  * Story Points: 2
  * Dependencies: E02, E03
  * Notes: Upload proof documents.

**User Story 2**
- Title: Certification Expiry Alerts
- User Story: As a supervisor, I want alerts for expiring certifications so that renewals are not missed.
- Acceptance Criteria in Gherkin format:
  Scenario: Alert before certification expiry
  Given a certification is expiring in 30 or 7 days
  When the system checks for expirations
  Then an alert is sent to the employee and supervisor
- Additional Details:
  * Priority: High
  * Story Points: 2
  * Dependencies: E02, E03
  * Notes: Notification integration.

**User Story 3**
- Title: Block Assignment for Expired Certifications
- User Story: As a scheduler, I want to prevent assignment of tasks to employees with expired certifications so that safety is ensured.
- Acceptance Criteria in Gherkin format:
  Scenario: Block assignment if certification expired
  Given an employee's certification is expired
  When I attempt to assign them to a restricted task
  Then the assignment is blocked and a warning is shown
- Additional Details:
  * Priority: High
  * Story Points: 3
  * Dependencies: E02, E03
  * Notes: Certification status visible on profile.
