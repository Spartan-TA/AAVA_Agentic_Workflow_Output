Title: Training & Certification Tracking - Add Certification
User Story: As an HR manager I want to add certifications to employee profiles So that compliance is tracked

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Add certification
Given valid certification details
When I POST to /certifications
Then the certification is added to the employee profile

Additional Details:
- Priority: High
- Story Points: 2
- Dependencies: Employee CRUD, RBAC
- Notes: Proof documents uploadable

Title: Training & Certification Tracking - Alert on Expiring Certification
User Story: As a supervisor I want to receive alerts for expiring certifications So that renewals are managed proactively

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Certification expiry alert
Given a certification expires in 30 or 7 days
When the system checks certification status
Then an alert is sent to the supervisor and employee

Additional Details:
- Priority: High
- Story Points: 2
- Dependencies: Certification tracking
- Notes: Alerts must be visible on dashboard

Title: Training & Certification Tracking - Block Assignment to Unqualified Tasks
User Story: As a scheduler I want to prevent assignment of tasks to employees with expired certifications So that safety and compliance are maintained

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Block assignment
Given an employee's certification is expired
When I try to assign them to a task requiring certification
Then the assignment is blocked

Additional Details:
- Priority: High
- Story Points: 2
- Dependencies: Certification tracking, Shift management
- Notes: Certification status visible on profile
