Title: Automate Provisioning of Accounts and Initial Schedules for New Hires

User Story: As an HR manager I want new hires to be automatically provisioned with accounts and initial schedules So that onboarding is efficient and error-free

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Automated onboarding
Given a new hire appears from HRIS
When onboarding is initiated
Then accounts are provisioned and initial schedules assigned automatically

Additional Details:
- Priority: Medium
- Story Points: 5
- Dependencies: E02, E03, E05, E07, E09, E13
- Notes: Onboarding tasks must be tracked and completed before start date.
