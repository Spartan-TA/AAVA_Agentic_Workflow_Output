Title: View Leave Balances

User Story: As an employee I want to view my leave balances So that I can plan my time off and ensure I have sufficient leave available

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Leave balance viewing
Given I am logged in as an employee
When I access my leave dashboard
Then I should see my current leave balances by type

Additional Details:
- Priority: Medium
- Story Points: 2
- Dependencies: Leave accrual
- Notes: Support for multiple leave types
