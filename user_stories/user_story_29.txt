Title: Safety Metrics Dashboard Endpoints

User Story: As a safety officer or supervisor I want to access safety metrics dashboards with incident trends and KPIs so that I can monitor safety performance and identify areas for improvement

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Viewing safety metrics dashboard
Given I am logged in as a safety officer or supervisor
When I navigate to the safety dashboard
Then I see charts and KPIs for incident frequency, severity, and resolution times, filterable by date and department

Additional Details:
- Priority: Medium
- Story Points: 3
- Dependencies: Incident Recording, Reporting Module
- Notes: Endpoints should support export to CSV/PDF.
