Title: Investigate Safety Incidents

User Story: As a safety officer I want to assign and track investigations for reported incidents So that resolution and compliance are documented

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Assign investigation
Given a safety incident is logged
When I assign an investigator and set a due date
Then the investigation status should be tracked and updated until closure

Additional Details:
- Priority: High
- Story Points: 3
- Dependencies: Incident management, notification system
- Notes: Should support uploading investigation reports.
