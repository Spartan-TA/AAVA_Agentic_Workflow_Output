Title: Report Safety Incident

User Story: As an employee I want to report a safety incident So that the organization can take corrective action and comply with OSHA regulations

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Employee submits safety incident
Given I am logged into the system
When I fill out and submit the incident report form
Then the safety officer should be notified and the incident logged for review

Additional Details:
- Priority: High
- Story Points: 3
- Dependencies: Notifications module
- Notes: Form should support attachments (photos, documents).
