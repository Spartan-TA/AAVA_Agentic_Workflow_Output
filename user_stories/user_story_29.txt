Title: Certification Renewal Tracking

User Story: As an HR administrator I want to track certification renewals So that compliance is maintained and employees are not assigned to roles without valid certifications

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Recording a renewal
Given an employee submits renewal documentation
When I update the certification record
Then the new expiration date should be reflected and alerts reset

Scenario: Viewing renewal history
Given I am viewing an employee's certification
When I click on the renewal history
Then I should see all previous renewal dates and documents

Additional Details:
- Priority: Medium
- Story Points: 2
- Dependencies: Certification CRUD, document upload
- Notes: Renewal actions should be auditable.
