Title: Asset Registry CRUD
User Story: As an asset manager I want to create, update, and delete asset records for equipment and PPE so that all assets are tracked and managed centrally
Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Add new asset
Given I have new equipment or PPE
When I create an asset record with type, serial number, and condition
Then the asset is saved in the registry
Scenario: Edit asset details
Given an asset exists
When I update its information
Then the changes are reflected and logged
Scenario: Delete asset
Given an asset exists
When I delete it
Then it is removed from the registry
Additional Details:
- Priority: Medium
- Story Points: 3
- Dependencies: E02, E03, E07
- Notes: Asset types include scanners, forklifts, PPE
