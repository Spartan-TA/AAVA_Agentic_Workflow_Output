Title: Record Safety Incident or Near-Miss

User Story: As a supervisor I want to record details of safety incidents and near-misses so that investigations and OSHA reporting are accurate and complete

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Submit safety incident
  Given I am logged in as a supervisor
  When I submit a new safety incident with required fields (severity, location, employees, description)
  Then the incident is saved and visible in the safety dashboard

Additional Details:
- Priority: High
- Story Points: 3
- Dependencies: E02 (User Management), E03 (Scheduling)
- Notes: Validation required for all mandatory fields
