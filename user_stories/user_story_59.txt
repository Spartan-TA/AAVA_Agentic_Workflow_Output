Title: Export Reports to CSV and PDF

User Story: As a manager I want to export operational and compliance reports to CSV or PDF so that I can share and archive them easily

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Exporting reports
Given a report is generated
When I select export as CSV or PDF
Then the file is generated and downloaded within 10 seconds for up to 50,000 rows

Additional Details:
- Priority: Medium
- Story Points: 2
- Dependencies: Export module, reporting service
- Notes: Export must preserve all filters and formatting
