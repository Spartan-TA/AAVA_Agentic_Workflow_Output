Title: Automated Onboarding for New Hires

User Story: As an HR administrator I want new hires from the HRIS to be automatically provisioned with accounts, initial schedules, and required training so that onboarding is efficient and consistent

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Onboard new hire from HRIS
  Given a new hire appears in the HRIS
  When the onboarding workflow runs
  Then the employee is provisioned with an account, assigned a schedule, and training tasks are generated

Additional Details:
- Priority: Medium
- Story Points: 3
- Dependencies: E02 (User Management), E03 (Scheduling), E05 (Attendance), E07 (Certifications), E09 (Assets), E13 (Integration)
- Notes: Onboarding tasks must be tracked to completion
