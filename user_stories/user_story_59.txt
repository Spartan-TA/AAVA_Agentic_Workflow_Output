Title: Metrics, Alerting, and Dashboards for Observability

User Story: As a DevOps engineer I want system metrics exported to Prometheus, critical errors to trigger alerts, and dashboards for latency, errors, and throughput so that I can proactively monitor and respond to issues

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Metrics and alerting integration
  Given the system is running
  When a critical error or threshold breach occurs
  Then metrics are available on /actuator/prometheus, alerts are sent to PagerDuty/Slack, and dashboards display key performance indicators

Additional Details:
- Priority: High
- Story Points: 5
- Dependencies: E01
- Notes: Metrics and alerting must be tested; dashboards must be accessible to authorized users.
