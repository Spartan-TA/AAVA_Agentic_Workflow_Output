Title: Assign Review Cycles to Employees

User Story: As a supervisor I want to assign review cycles to my team members So that performance reviews are conducted on schedule

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Assigning review cycles
Given I have access to the review cycle management page
When I select employees and assign them to a review cycle
Then the employees should be notified and the cycle should be tracked in the system

Additional Details:
- Priority: Medium
- Story Points: 3
- Dependencies: E02, E03
- Notes: Review cycles must support recurring schedules and notifications.
