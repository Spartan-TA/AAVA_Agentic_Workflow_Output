Title: Employee Completes Onboarding Task
User Story: As a new employee I want to complete onboarding tasks assigned to me So that I am fully prepared for my role
Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Complete onboarding task
Given onboarding tasks are assigned
When I mark a task as complete
Then the task status is updated and HR is notified
Additional Details:
- Priority: High
- Story Points: 2
- Dependencies: Onboarding module, notifications
- Notes: Task completion should be tracked for compliance.
