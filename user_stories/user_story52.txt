Title: Announcement Archive & Search

User Story:
As an employee, I want to access an archive of past announcements and search for specific messages so that I can reference important information when needed.

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario 1: View announcement archive
  Given I am logged into my employee portal
  When I access the announcement archive
  Then I see a list of all past announcements with dates and subjects

Scenario 2: Search announcements
  Given the archive contains multiple announcements
  When I enter a search term
  Then only matching announcements are displayed

Additional Details:
Priority: Medium
Story Points: 2
Dependencies: Announcement module, employee portal
Notes: Archive should be searchable by date, subject, and keywords.
