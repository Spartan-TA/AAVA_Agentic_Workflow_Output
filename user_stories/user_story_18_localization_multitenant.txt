Title: Multi-Warehouse & Localization Support
User Story: As a system administrator I want support for multiple warehouses and UI localization So that the system is usable across regions
Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Multi-tenant and localization
Given multiple warehouses and languages are configured
When users log in
Then they should see data and UI in their warehouse context and preferred language
Additional Details:
- Priority: Low
- Story Points: 4
- Dependencies: Project scaffolding, Employee domain, Shift management
- Notes: Support timezone-aware scheduling
