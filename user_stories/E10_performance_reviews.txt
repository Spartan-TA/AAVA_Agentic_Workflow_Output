EpicID: E10
Title: Performance Reviews & Goals
User Story: As a supervisor, I want to conduct structured performance reviews and set goals so that employee feedback and development are tracked.
Acceptance Criteria:
  Scenario: Create Review Cycle
    Given review template
    When I create a cycle
    Then employees are assigned reviews
  Scenario: Submit Review
    Given a review in progress
    When supervisor submits ratings and comments
    Then review is saved and employee notified
  Scenario: Acknowledge Review
    Given a completed review
    When employee acknowledges
    Then review is locked and history is immutable
  Scenario: Export Review
    Given completed reviews
    When I export
    Then a PDF is generated
Additional Details:
  Priority: Medium
  Story Points: 8
  Dependencies: E02, E03
  Notes: Role-based visibility required
