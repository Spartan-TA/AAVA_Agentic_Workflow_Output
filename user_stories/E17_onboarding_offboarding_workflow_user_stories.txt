### E17: Onboarding & Offboarding Workflow

#### 1. Automate New Hire Provisioning
User Story: As an HR user I want new hires from HRIS to be provisioned with accounts and initial schedules So that onboarding is efficient.
Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: New hire provisioned
Given a new hire appears in HRIS
When the integration runs
Then an account is created, initial schedule assigned, and required training tasks generated
Additional Details:
- Priority: Medium
- Story Points: 2
- Dependencies: E02, E03, E05, E07, E09, E13
- Notes: Notify supervisor.

#### 2. Generate Training and Asset Assignment Tasks
User Story: As a supervisor I want onboarding tasks for training and asset assignment So that new hires are ready to work.
Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Tasks generated
Given a new hire is provisioned
When onboarding starts
Then tasks for required training and asset assignment are created
Additional Details:
- Priority: Medium
- Story Points: 2
- Dependencies: E02, E03, E05, E07, E09, E13
- Notes: Track task completion.

#### 3. Automate Offboarding on Termination
User Story: As an HR user I want access and assets revoked automatically on termination So that risk is minimized.
Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Offboarding triggered
Given an employee is terminated in HRIS
When the integration runs
Then access is revoked, assets collected, and schedules updated
Additional Details:
- Priority: Medium
- Story Points: 2
- Dependencies: E02, E03, E05, E07, E09, E13
- Notes: Notify IT and asset manager.

#### 4. Update Schedules and Training Status on Offboarding
User Story: As a scheduler I want terminated employees removed from future schedules and training So that active rosters are accurate.
Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Remove from schedules
Given an employee is offboarded
When the process completes
Then they are removed from all future shifts and training assignments
Additional Details:
- Priority: Medium
- Story Points: 1
- Dependencies: E02, E03, E05, E07, E09, E13
- Notes: Archive historical data.
