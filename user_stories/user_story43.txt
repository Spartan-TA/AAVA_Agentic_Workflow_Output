Title: Payroll Export Retry and Audit Logging

User Story: As a payroll administrator I want failed payroll export deliveries to be retried with exponential backoff and every export to be logged So that exports are reliably delivered and all actions are auditable

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Failed export delivery retry
Given a payroll export delivery fails
When the system detects the failure
Then it retries delivery with exponential backoff and logs every attempt in the audit trail

Additional Details:
- Priority: High
- Story Points: 8
- Dependencies: E04, E06
- Notes: Audit log must include timestamp, actor, export details, and delivery status
