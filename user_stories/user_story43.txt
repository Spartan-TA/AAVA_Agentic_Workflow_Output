Title: Configure Audit Retention Policy

User Story: As a compliance officer I want to configure audit log retention policies so that the system meets regulatory and business requirements

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Setting retention policy
Given I am logged in as a compliance officer
When I set the retention period for audit logs
Then the system retains and deletes logs according to the policy

Additional Details:
- Priority: Medium
- Story Points: 2
- Dependencies: Audit trail module
- Notes: System should warn before deleting expiring logs.
