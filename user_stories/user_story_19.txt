Title: Employee Clock Out

User Story: As an employee I want to clock out at the end of my shift So that my work hours are tracked

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Successful clock out
  Given I am logged in and clocked in
  When I click the clock out button
  Then my end time should be recorded

Additional Details:
- Priority: High
- Story Points: 2
- Dependencies: Attendance module
- Notes: Calculate total hours
