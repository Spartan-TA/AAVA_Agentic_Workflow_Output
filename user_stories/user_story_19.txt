Title: Create Recurring Shift Templates

User Story: As an HR manager I want to create recurring shift templates So that I can efficiently schedule employees and reduce manual entry

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Shift template creation
Given I am on the shift management page
When I create a new recurring shift template
Then the template is saved and available for assignment

Scenario: Blackout date handling
Given blackout dates are configured
When I assign a shift template
Then the system prevents scheduling on blackout dates

Additional Details:
- Priority: High
- Story Points: 4
- Dependencies: Employee Master Data, Attendance module
- Notes: Templates must support rotations and overtime rules.
