Title: Notification and Announcement Delivery
User Story: As a warehouse worker I want to receive notifications about shifts, certifications, and announcements So that I stay informed in real time.
Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Opt-in/out per channel
  Given notification preferences
  When I update my settings
  Then I receive only selected notifications

Scenario: Delivery status tracked
  Given a notification is sent
  When I check status
  Then delivery is confirmed or retried

Scenario: Quiet hours enforced
  Given quiet hours are set
  When a notification is triggered
  Then it is delayed until allowed

Additional Details:
- Priority: Medium
- Story Points: 5
- Dependencies: E03, E05, E06, E07
- Notes: Templates localized; announcements on dashboard.
