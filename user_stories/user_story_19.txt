Title: Detect and Prevent Shift Scheduling Conflicts

User Story: As a scheduler I want the system to automatically detect and prevent overlapping or conflicting shift assignments So that employees are not double-booked

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Assigning a conflicting shift
Given an employee already has a scheduled shift
When I attempt to assign an overlapping shift
Then the system blocks the assignment and displays a conflict warning

Additional Details:
- Priority: High
- Story Points: 3
- Dependencies: Employee Master Data, RBAC, Time & Attendance
- Notes: Conflicts should be logged for review and reporting.
