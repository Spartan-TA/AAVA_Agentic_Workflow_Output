Title: Personal Shift View
User Story: As a warehouse worker I want to view my upcoming shifts So that I can plan my work schedule and avoid conflicts
Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: View personal shifts Given I am logged into the system When I navigate to my schedule page Then I see a list of all upcoming assigned shifts with details
Additional Details:
- Priority: Medium
- Story Points: 1
- Dependencies: Shift assignment, employee master data
- Notes: View should be mobile-friendly and filterable by date.
