Title: Auto-Flag Scheduled Shifts for Coverage

User Story: As a scheduler I want the system to auto-flag scheduled shifts that lack coverage due to approved leave so that I can fill gaps proactively

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Auto-flag uncovered shifts
Given an approved leave overlaps with a scheduled shift
When the system processes the leave approval
Then the affected shift should be flagged for coverage

Additional Details:
- Priority: Medium
- Story Points: 3
- Dependencies: Leave management, Scheduling module
- Notes: Notify scheduler of flagged shifts.
