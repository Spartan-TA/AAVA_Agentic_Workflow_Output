Title: Workers View Upcoming Shifts and Supervisors Bulk-Assign

User Story: As a WORKER I want to view my upcoming shifts and as a SUPERVISOR I want to bulk-assign shifts to my team so that scheduling is transparent and efficient

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Viewing and assigning shifts
Given shifts are scheduled
When a worker logs in
Then they see their personal upcoming shifts
And when a supervisor bulk-assigns shifts
Then assignments are created and audit entries generated

Additional Details:
- Priority: High
- Story Points: 2
- Dependencies: E02, E03, E04, E05
- Notes: Bulk assignment should support audit logging.
