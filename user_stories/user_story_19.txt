Title: Track Absence History

User Story: As an HR administrator I want to track employee absence history So that I can identify patterns and support workforce planning

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: View absence history
Given I am in the employee profile
When I view the absence history tab
Then I should see all approved and rejected leave requests with dates and reasons

Additional Details:
- Priority: Medium
- Story Points: 3
- Dependencies: Leave management module
- Notes: Exportable for reporting.
