Title: Automated Hours Calculation

User Story: As a payroll manager I want employee hours to be automatically calculated from clock in/out events So that payroll processing is accurate and efficient

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Calculating regular hours
  Given an employee has clocked in and out for a shift
  When the system processes attendance records
  Then total hours worked should be calculated and stored
Scenario: Handling overtime
  Given an employee exceeds standard shift duration
  When hours are calculated
  Then overtime should be flagged and separated from regular hours

Additional Details:
- Priority: High
- Story Points: 3
- Dependencies: Attendance records, payroll module
- Notes: Calculation logic should support overtime, breaks, and shift exceptions.
