Title: Supervisor Reviews and Approves/Deny Leave Requests

User Story: As a supervisor I want to review and approve or deny leave requests from my team so that staffing levels are maintained and leave policies are enforced

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Approving a leave request
Given I am logged in as a supervisor and have pending leave requests
When I review a request and select approve or deny with optional comments
Then the employee is notified of the decision and the leave balance and schedule are updated accordingly

Additional Details:
- Priority: High
- Story Points: 3
- Dependencies: Employee Master Data, RBAC, Scheduling
- Notes: Approval/denial reasons should be auditable.
