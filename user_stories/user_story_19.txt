Title: AI-Suggested Shift Assignments
User Story: As a scheduling manager I want the system to suggest optimal shift assignments based on demand, skills, and preferences So that staffing is efficient and balanced
Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: AI shift suggestion
  Given employee profiles and demand forecasts are available
  When I request shift suggestions
  Then the system should propose assignments optimized for coverage and employee preferences
Additional Details:
- Priority: High
- Story Points: 4
- Dependencies: Scheduling module, AI engine
- Notes: Suggestions editable by manager

Title: Skill-Based Assignment Engine
User Story: As a manager I want the scheduling engine to prioritize employees with required skills for each shift So that operational needs are met
Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Skill-based assignment
  Given shift requirements include specific skills
  When the engine generates assignments
  Then employees with matching skills are prioritized
Additional Details:
- Priority: High
- Story Points: 3
- Dependencies: Employee profile, AI engine
- Notes: Skill matrix maintained in profiles

Title: Employee Preference Integration
User Story: As an employee I want my shift preferences to be considered in scheduling So that my work-life balance is supported
Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Preference-based scheduling
  Given I have set my shift preferences
  When schedules are generated
  Then my preferences are factored into assignment suggestions
Additional Details:
- Priority: Medium
- Story Points: 2
- Dependencies: Employee profile, scheduling module
- Notes: Preferences may be overridden for operational needs
