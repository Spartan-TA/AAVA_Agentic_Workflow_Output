Title: Report Safety Incident
User Story: As an employee I want to report a safety incident So that hazards are documented and addressed promptly
Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Incident reporting
Given I am logged in as an employee
When I submit a safety incident report
Then the safety officer should be notified and the incident logged for review
Additional Details:
- Priority: High
- Story Points: 2
- Dependencies: Notifications, Incident module
- Notes: Allow attachments (photos, documents) in report.
