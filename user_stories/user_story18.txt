Title: Notifications & Announcements

User Story: As a warehouse worker I want to receive notifications about shift changes, expiring certifications, and company announcements So that I am informed in real time and can take necessary actions

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Opt-in notification channels
Given I am a registered user
When I configure my notification preferences
Then I only receive notifications via my selected channels

Scenario: Announcement delivery
Given a new announcement is published
When I log in to the dashboard
Then the announcement is visible and delivery status is tracked

Additional Details:
- Priority: Medium
- Story Points: 5
- Dependencies: RBAC, Shift & Schedule Management, Leave & Absence Management, Training & Certification Tracking
- Notes: In-app, email, SMS supported; quiet hours configuration; templates localized
