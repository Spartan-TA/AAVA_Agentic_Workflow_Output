Title: Training & Certification Tracking - Certification CRUD

User Story: As an HR user, I want to create, update, and delete employee certifications so that training records are maintained.

Acceptance Criteria:

Scenario: Add certification to employee
Given an employee exists
When I POST a certification with type and expiry date
Then the certification is added to the employee profile

Scenario: Update certification
Given a certification exists
When I PATCH the certification
Then the record is updated

Scenario: Delete certification
Given a certification exists
When I DELETE it
Then the record is removed

Additional Details:
- Priority: High
- Story Points: 5
- Dependencies: Employee CRUD, RBAC
- Notes: Certification status must be visible on profile.
