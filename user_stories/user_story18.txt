Title: Supervisor Approve Leave Requests

User Story: As a supervisor I want to review and approve or reject employee leave requests so that staffing levels are maintained and leave policies are enforced

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Approving a leave request
Given an employee has submitted a leave request
When I review the request and select approve or reject
Then the employee is notified and the leave calendar is updated

Additional Details:
- Priority: High
- Story Points: 3
- Dependencies: Notification module, employee schedule
- Notes: All actions should be logged for audit and compliance.
