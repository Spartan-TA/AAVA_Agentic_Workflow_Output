Title: Track Employee Certifications

User Story: As an HR manager I want to record and track employee certifications So that compliance and training requirements are met

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Employee certifications tracked
  Given I add certification records for employees
  When I view an employee profile
  Then I should see all active and expired certifications

Additional Details:
- Priority: High
- Story Points: 3
- Dependencies: Employee entity, profile management
- Notes: Support uploading certification documents and expiry dates.
