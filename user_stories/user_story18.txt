Title: Bulk Assign Shifts to Employees

User Story: As a supervisor I want to bulk assign shifts to multiple employees So that I can efficiently manage large teams and reduce manual effort

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Bulk shift assignment
Given I have selected multiple employees
When I assign a shift template to the group
Then all selected employees should be scheduled for the shift and audit entries generated

Additional Details:
- Priority: High
- Story Points: 2
- Dependencies: Shift Templates, Employee Master Data
- Notes: Audit log must capture bulk actions for compliance.
