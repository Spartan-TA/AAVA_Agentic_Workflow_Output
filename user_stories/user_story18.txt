Title: Employee Clock Out

User Story: As an employee I want to clock out at the end of my shift So that my working hours are accurately completed and recorded

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Successful clock out
Given I am clocked in and my shift is ending
When I click the clock out button
Then my end time should be recorded and my attendance log updated

Additional Details:
- Priority: High
- Story Points: 2
- Dependencies: Clock in functionality
- Notes: Prevent clock out before scheduled end time unless approved.
