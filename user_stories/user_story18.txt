Title: Accrual Balances and Scheduling Integration

User Story: As an HR manager I want leave accrual balances to update automatically and scheduled shifts to be flagged for coverage when leave is approved so that staffing plans remain accurate and compliant

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Accrual balance update
Given an employee's leave request is approved
When the leave is processed
Then the employee's accrual balance is updated and scheduled shifts are flagged for coverage

Additional Details:
- Priority: Medium
- Story Points: 2
- Dependencies: Leave request/approval, Shift Management
- Notes: Approved leaves must be reflected in exports and scheduling views.
