Title: View Personal Upcoming Shifts
User Story: As a WORKER I want to view my upcoming scheduled shifts So that I can plan my attendance and manage my time
Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: View upcoming shifts
Given I am logged in as a worker
When I access my schedule dashboard
Then I see a list of my upcoming shifts with dates, times, and locations
Additional Details:
- Priority: High
- Story Points: 3
- Dependencies: E02, E03, E04, E05
- Notes: Dashboard must update in real-time with schedule changes.
