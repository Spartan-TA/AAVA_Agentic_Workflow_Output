Title: Auto-Flag Scheduled Shifts for Coverage

User Story: As a scheduler I want scheduled shifts to be auto-flagged for coverage when an employee is on approved leave So that staffing gaps are addressed

Acceptance Criteria:

Scenario: Shift auto-flagged for coverage
Given an employee has approved leave overlapping a scheduled shift
When the leave is approved
Then the shift is flagged for coverage

Scenario: Coverage report export
Given flagged shifts exist
When I export the coverage report
Then I receive a list of shifts needing coverage

Additional Details:
- Priority: Medium
- Story Points: 5
- Dependencies: Leave Management, Shift Management
- Notes: Integrate with notifications for open shifts
