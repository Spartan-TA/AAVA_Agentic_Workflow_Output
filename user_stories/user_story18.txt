Title: Automatic Shift Association and Daily Totals

User Story: As a WORKER I want my clock-in/out events to be automatically associated with my scheduled shift so that my daily hours are accurately computed

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Daily totals computation
Given I have clocked in and out for a shift
When the system processes my attendance
Then my hours worked are calculated and visible in my profile

Additional Details:
- Priority: High
- Story Points: 2
- Dependencies: E02, E03
- Notes: Handle edge cases for missed punches and overtime; display totals in worker dashboard.
