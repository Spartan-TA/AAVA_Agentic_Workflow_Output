Title: Bulk Shift Assignment

User Story: As a scheduler I want to assign shifts to multiple employees in bulk So that I can efficiently manage large teams and recurring schedules

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Bulk assign shifts
  Given I have selected multiple employees
  When I apply a shift template
  Then all selected employees should be assigned the shift
Scenario: Bulk assignment validation
  Given some employees have conflicting assignments
  When I attempt bulk assignment
  Then the system should notify me of conflicts and prevent double-booking

Additional Details:
- Priority: High
- Story Points: 3
- Dependencies: Scheduling module, shift templates
- Notes: Support for CSV upload and UI selection
