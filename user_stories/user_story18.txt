Title: Localization & Multi-Site Support

User Story: As a supervisor I want support for multiple warehouses/sites with distinct calendars, policies, and supervisors, and UI localization for English/Spanish So that our platform is usable across diverse locations and languages.

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Multi-site and localization configuration
Given multiple warehouses/sites are configured
When I access the platform as a supervisor or worker
Then I see site-specific calendars, policies, and supervisors, and can select English or Spanish for the UI.

Additional Details:
- Priority: Low
- Story Points: 5
- Dependencies: E01, E02, E05
- Notes: Ensure translation accuracy and allow future language additions.
