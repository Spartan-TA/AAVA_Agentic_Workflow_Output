EPIC E15: Reporting & Analytics

===========================================

Title: Reporting & Analytics

User Story: As a manager I want operational reports on attendance, overtime, leave, certifications, and safety So that I can make data-driven decisions

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Report generation
Given data exists
When I request a report
Then it is filtered and exported within 10s

Additional Details:
- Priority: Medium
- Story Points: 3
- Dependencies: Attendance, Schedule, Leave, Certification, Safety, Audit
- Notes: Role-based dashboards
