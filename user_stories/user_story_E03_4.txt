Title: Audit Role Assignments and Changes

User Story: As a compliance officer I want to audit role assignments and changes So that all access modifications are tracked for security and compliance

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Role assignment audit
Given role assignments or changes occur
When I review the audit log
Then I should see details of who made the change, when, and what was changed

Additional Details:
- Priority: High
- Story Points: 2
- Dependencies: RBAC module, audit log
- Notes: Ensure logs are immutable and exportable.
