## E07 - Training & Certification Tracking

### Story 1: CRUD for Certifications
- **User Story:** As an HR user, I want to create, update, and delete certifications for employees so that compliance is tracked.
- **Acceptance Criteria:**
    ```
    Given a valid certification
    When I add it to an employee
    Then the certification appears on their profile

    Given an expired certification
    When I attempt to assign equipment
    Then the system blocks the assignment
    ```
- **Additional Details:**  
    Priority: High  
    Story Points: 4  
    Dependencies: E02, E03  
    Notes: Include document upload.

### Story 2: Expiry Alerts for Certifications
- **User Story:** As a Supervisor, I want to receive alerts before certifications expire so that renewals are planned.
- **Acceptance Criteria:**
    ```
    Given a certification expiring in 30 or 7 days
    When the system runs the alert job
    Then an alert is sent to the employee and supervisor
    ```
- **Additional Details:**  
    Priority: High  
    Story Points: 2  
    Dependencies: E02, E03  
    Notes: Email and in-app notifications.

### Story 3: Block Unqualified Assignments
- **User Story:** As a System, I want to prevent assignment of tasks or equipment to employees with expired or missing certifications so that safety is ensured.
- **Acceptance Criteria:**
    ```
    Given an employee with expired certification
    When I attempt to assign restricted equipment
    Then the system blocks the assignment and notifies the user
    ```
- **Additional Details:**  
    Priority: High  
    Story Points: 3  
    Dependencies: E02, E03  
    Notes: Integrate with equipment assignment.

### Story 4: Certification Status on Employee Profile
- **User Story:** As a Worker, I want to view my certification status on my profile so that I know when renewals are needed.
- **Acceptance Criteria:**
    ```
    Given certifications assigned to me
    When I view my profile
    Then I see all certifications with status and expiry dates
    ```
- **Additional Details:**  
    Priority: Medium  
    Story Points: 2  
    Dependencies: E02, E03  
    Notes: Accessible via web/mobile.
