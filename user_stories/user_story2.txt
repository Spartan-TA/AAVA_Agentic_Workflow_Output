Title: Employee Master Data (CRUD)

User Story: As an HR manager I want to manage employee records with CRUD operations so that we maintain a single source of truth for warehouse staff.

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Employee record management
Given the employee management interface
When I create, update, or delete an employee
Then the badgeId is unique and soft-delete is supported
And pagination and filtering are available
And OpenAPI schemas include examples

Additional Details:
- Priority: High
- Story Points: 8
- Dependencies: Project Scaffolding & Domain Setup
- Notes: Ensure all fields (name, badgeId, role, department, shiftGroup, hireDate, status) are present and validated.
