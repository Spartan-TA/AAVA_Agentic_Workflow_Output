Title: Employee Master Data (CRUD)

User Story: As an HR manager I want to create, update, and manage employee records So that there is a single source of truth for warehouse employee data

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Employee CRUD operations
Given I have HR manager access
When I POST, GET, PUT, PATCH, or DELETE /employees
Then employee records are created, updated, retrieved, or soft-deleted with unique badgeId enforced and pagination/filtering available

Additional Details:
- Priority: High
- Story Points: 8
- Dependencies: Project Scaffolding & Domain Setup
- Notes: OpenAPI schemas with examples; soft-delete supported; ensure unique badgeId.
