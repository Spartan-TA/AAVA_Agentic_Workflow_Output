Title: Employee Master Data (CRUD)

User Story: As an HR manager I want to create, read, update, and delete employee records so that I can maintain a single source of truth for warehouse employee data.

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Employee CRUD operations
Given I have HR manager access
When I POST/GET/PUT/PATCH/DELETE /employees
Then employee records are created, retrieved, updated, or deleted as requested
And badgeId is unique and enforced
And soft-delete is supported
And pagination and filtering are available
And OpenAPI schemas include examples

Additional Details:
- Priority: High
- Story Points: 8
- Dependencies: Project Scaffolding & Domain Setup
- Notes: Ensure badgeId uniqueness and support for soft-delete; provide pagination/filtering and OpenAPI documentation.
