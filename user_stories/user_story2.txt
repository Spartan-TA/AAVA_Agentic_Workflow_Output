Title: Employee Master Data CRUD Operations

User Story: As an HR administrator I want to manage employee records with CRUD APIs So that I can maintain a single source of truth for warehouse employee data

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Employee Record Management
Given the employee management interface
When I create, update, retrieve, or delete employee records
Then unique badgeId is enforced, soft-delete is supported, pagination and filtering are available, and OpenAPI schemas with examples are provided

Additional Details:
- Priority: High
- Story Points: 5
- Dependencies: Project Scaffolding & Domain Setup
- Notes: Employee domain includes name, badgeId, role, department, shiftGroup, hireDate, status.
