Title: Employee Master Data (CRUD)

User Story: As an HR manager I want to create, update, and manage employee records with unique badge IDs so that we maintain a single source of truth for all warehouse employees.

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Employee record management
Given I have access to the employee management interface
When I POST, GET, PUT, PATCH, or DELETE employee records
Then the system enforces unique badge IDs, supports soft-delete, and provides pagination and filtering
And OpenAPI schemas are available with examples

Additional Details:
- Priority: High
- Story Points: 8
- Dependencies: Project Scaffolding & Domain Setup (E01)
- Notes: Ensure all CRUD operations are covered; badgeId uniqueness enforced; soft-delete for compliance.
