Title: Manage Employee Master Data

User Story: As an HR manager I want to create, update, and manage employee records So that I have a single source of truth for all warehouse employee information

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Employee CRUD operations
Given I have HR manager access
When I use the employee management APIs
Then I can POST, GET, PUT, PATCH, and DELETE employee records with unique badgeId enforced and support for soft-delete, pagination, and filtering

Additional Details:
- Priority: High
- Story Points: 8
- Dependencies: Project Scaffolding & Domain Setup
- Notes: OpenAPI schemas with examples must be provided; ensure badgeId uniqueness and soft-delete support.
