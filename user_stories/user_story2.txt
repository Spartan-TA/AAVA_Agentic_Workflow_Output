Title: Employee Master Data (CRUD)

User Story: As an HR administrator I want to manage employee records (create, read, update, delete) with unique badge IDs and relevant attributes so that employee data remains accurate and consistent across systems.

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Create employee
  Given I am an HR administrator
  When I submit a new employee with all required fields
  Then the employee is created and assigned a unique badgeId

Scenario: Update employee
  Given an employee exists
  When I update their department or status
  Then the changes are saved and reflected in the system

Scenario: Delete employee (soft-delete)
  Given an employee exists
  When I delete the employee
  Then the record is soft-deleted and not shown in active lists

Scenario: List and filter employees
  Given multiple employees exist
  When I request a paginated list with filters
  Then I receive the correct subset of employees

Additional Details:
- Priority: High
- Story Points: 8
- Dependencies: Project Scaffolding & Domain Setup (E01)
- Notes: OpenAPI schemas with examples must be provided; badgeId must be unique; support PATCH for partial updates.
