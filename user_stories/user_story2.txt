Title: Employee Master Data (CRUD)
User Story: As an HR manager I want to manage employee records with CRUD APIs so that we have a single source of truth for warehouse employees.
Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Employee record management
Given I have HR permissions
When I create, update, or delete an employee
Then badgeId is unique, soft-delete is supported, and records are paginated and filterable
Additional Details:
- Priority: High
- Story Points: 5
- Dependencies: Project Scaffolding & Domain Setup
- Notes: OpenAPI schemas must include examples.
