EPIC E15: Reporting & Analytics

---

Title: Reporting - Generate Attendance Report
User Story: As an HR I want to generate attendance reports filtered by date, department, and shift So that I can analyze staffing trends.
Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Generate attendance report
Given attendance data exists
When I filter and generate a report
Then the report is available within 10 seconds for up to 50k rows
Additional Details:
- Priority: Medium
- Story Points: 5
- Dependencies: E04, E05, E06, E07, E08, E14
- Notes: Exportable as CSV/PDF
