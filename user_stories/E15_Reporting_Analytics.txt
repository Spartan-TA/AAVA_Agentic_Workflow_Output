### E15: Reporting & Analytics

#### 1. Title: Attendance and Overtime Reports
User Story: As a manager I want to generate attendance and overtime reports So that I can monitor workforce utilization
Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Generate report
Given attendance data
When I filter by date and department
Then the report is generated and exportable

- Priority: Medium
- Story Points: 3
- Dependencies: E04, E05, E06, E07, E08, E14
