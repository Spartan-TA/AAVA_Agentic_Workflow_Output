Title: Operational Reports for Attendance, Overtime, Leave Balances, Certification Status, and Safety KPIs

User Story: As a supervisor I want to view operational reports for attendance, overtime, leave balances, certification status, and safety KPIs So that I can make informed staffing and safety decisions

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Report generation
Given I have access to the reporting dashboard
When I select a report type and apply filters
Then the report displays accurate data for the selected period, department, and shift

Additional Details:
- Priority: Medium
- Story Points: 5
- Dependencies: E04, E05, E06, E07, E08, E14
- Notes: Reports must be role-based and update in real time.
