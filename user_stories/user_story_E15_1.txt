Title: Operational Attendance and Overtime Reports

User Story: As an operations manager I want to generate attendance and overtime reports filtered by date, department, and shift So that I can monitor staffing and compliance

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Generate attendance/overtime report
Given attendance and overtime data is available
When I apply filters and request a report
Then the system generates a CSV/PDF export within 10 seconds for up to 50,000 rows

Additional Details:
- Priority: Medium
- Story Points: 4
- Dependencies: E04, E05, E06, E07, E08, E14
- Notes: Access must be controlled by user role.
