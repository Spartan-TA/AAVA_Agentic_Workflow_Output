Title: Quiet Hours Configuration for Notifications

User Story: As a worker I want to configure quiet hours for notifications so that I am not disturbed by non-critical alerts outside my preferred time window.

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Quiet hours setup
Given I am logged in
When I set my quiet hours preferences
Then notifications should be suppressed or delayed during those hours except for critical alerts

Additional Details:
- Priority: Medium
- Story Points: 2
- Dependencies: RBAC (E03), Notifications & Announcements (E12)
- Notes: Quiet hours must be customizable per user and channel.
