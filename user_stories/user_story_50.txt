Title: HRIS Sync Job for Employee Creation and Updates

User Story: As an HR manager I want new hires and terminations from the HRIS to automatically create or update employee records so that our workforce data is always current

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Automatic employee sync
Given a new hire or termination is processed in the HRIS
When the sync job runs
Then the corresponding employee record is created, updated, or deactivated in the scheduling system

Additional Details:
- Priority: High
- Story Points: 3
- Dependencies: HRIS API, employee database
- Notes: Sync must be idempotent and handle errors gracefully
