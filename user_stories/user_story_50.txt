Title: View Schedules on Mobile

User Story: As an employee I want to view my shift schedule on my mobile device So that I can plan my work and personal time easily

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: View schedule on mobile
Given I am logged into the mobile app
When I access the schedule section
Then I should see my upcoming shifts and details

Additional Details:
- Priority: High
- Story Points: 2
- Dependencies: Mobile app, scheduling module
- Notes: Support calendar integration and offline access.
