## E19 - Advanced Scheduling Optimization

### Story 1: Shift Rotations and Overtime Rules
- **User Story:** As a Scheduler, I want to define shift rotations and overtime rules so that complex schedules are supported.
- **Acceptance Criteria:**
    ```
    Given rotation and overtime parameters
    When I create a schedule
    Then the system applies rules and calculates overtime
    ```
- **Additional Details:**  
    Priority: Medium  
    Story Points: 5  
    Dependencies: E05  
    Notes: Policy-driven logic.

### Story 2: Blackout Dates and Operation Calendars
- **User Story:** As a Scheduler, I want to define blackout dates and operation calendars so that scheduling conflicts are avoided.
- **Acceptance Criteria:**
    ```
    Given blackout dates
    When I assign shifts
    Then the system prevents assignments on those dates
    ```
- **Additional Details:**  
    Priority: Medium  
    Story Points: 3  
    Dependencies: E05  
    Notes: Calendar UI.

### Story 3: Bulk Schedule Assignment
- **User Story:** As a Supervisor, I want to assign schedules in bulk so that large teams are managed efficiently.
- **Acceptance Criteria:**
    ```
    Given a list of employees and shifts
    When I perform a bulk assignment
    Then all assignments are created and conflicts are flagged
    ```
- **Additional Details:**  
    Priority: Medium  
    Story Points: 3  
    Dependencies: E05  
    Notes: Error reporting for conflicts.
