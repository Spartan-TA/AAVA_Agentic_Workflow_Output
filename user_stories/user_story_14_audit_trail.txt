Title: Audit Trail & Compliance - Audit Logging

User Story: As a compliance officer, I want centralized audit logging for sensitive changes so that actions are traceable.

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Audit log entry
Given a create/update/delete
When it occurs
Then actor, timestamp, before/after are logged

Additional Details:
- Priority: High
- Story Points: 2
- Dependencies: RBAC
- Notes: Tamper-evident storage
