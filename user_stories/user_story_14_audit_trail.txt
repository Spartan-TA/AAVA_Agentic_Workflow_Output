Title: Audit Trail Logging
User Story: As a compliance officer I want centralized audit logging for sensitive changes So that regulatory requirements are met
Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Audit logging
Given sensitive actions are performed
When changes are made
Then audit logs should capture user, action, timestamp, and details
Additional Details:
- Priority: High
- Story Points: 2
- Dependencies: RBAC
- Notes: Logs must be immutable and searchable
