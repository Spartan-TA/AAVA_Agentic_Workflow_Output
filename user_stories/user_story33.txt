Title: Asset Registry CRUD Operations

User Story: As an asset manager I want to create, update, and delete asset records for equipment and PPE so that we can maintain an accurate inventory and assignment history.

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Asset registry management
Given I am on the asset management page
When I add, edit, or remove an asset record
Then the changes are reflected in the asset registry and history log

Additional Details:
- Priority: Medium
- Story Points: 5
- Dependencies: E02, E03, E07
- Notes: Must support asset types, condition state, and assignment status.
