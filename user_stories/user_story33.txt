Title: Auto-Flag Scheduled Shifts for Coverage When Leave is Approved

User Story: As a supervisor I want scheduled shifts to be auto-flagged for coverage when leave is approved So that I can quickly identify and fill gaps in the schedule

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Leave approval triggers coverage flag
Given an employee's leave request is approved
When their scheduled shifts overlap with the leave dates
Then those shifts should be flagged for coverage and notifications sent to supervisors

Additional Details:
- Priority: Medium
- Story Points: 4
- Dependencies: Leave Request, Shift Assignment
- Notes: Coverage flags should be visible in the scheduling dashboard and support bulk reassignment.
