Title: Certification Dashboard for Compliance Monitoring

User Story:
As a Compliance Officer, I want a dashboard that shows the certification status of all employees so that I can quickly identify compliance risks and take corrective action.

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario 1: View certification status by department
  Given I am on the certification dashboard
  When I filter by department
  Then I see the certification status for all employees in that department

Scenario 2: Export certification report
  Given I am on the dashboard
  When I click 'Export'
  Then I receive a CSV or PDF report of current certification statuses

Scenario 3: Highlight expired or expiring certifications
  Given certifications are tracked
  When a certification is expired or expiring within 30 days
  Then it is highlighted on the dashboard

Priority: High
Story Points: 8
Dependencies: E02, E03
Notes: Dashboard must support export and real-time updates.
