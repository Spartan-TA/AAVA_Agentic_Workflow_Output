Title: Record and Validate Safety Incidents and Near-Misses

User Story:
As a safety officer, I want to record safety incidents and near-misses with validation so that all required details are captured for investigation and compliance.

Acceptance Criteria:
- Scenario: Submit valid safety incident
  Given a safety officer is authenticated
  When they POST to /safety/incidents with all required fields
  Then the incident is recorded and status is set to Open

- Scenario: Submit invalid or incomplete incident
  Given required fields are missing
  When the officer submits the incident
  Then the response is 400 Bad Request

Additional Details:
- Required fields: severity, location, description, involved employees
- All submissions must be timestamped and logged
- Near-misses must be tracked separately from incidents
