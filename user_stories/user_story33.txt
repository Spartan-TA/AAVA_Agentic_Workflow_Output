Title: Detect and Prevent Schedule Conflicts

User Story: As a supervisor I want the system to detect and prevent scheduling conflicts So that employees are not double-booked or assigned during blackout dates

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Schedule conflict detection
Given I am assigning a shift to an employee
When the employee is already scheduled for another shift or a blackout date
Then the system blocks the assignment and displays a conflict warning

Additional Details:
- Priority: High
- Story Points: 8
- Dependencies: E02, E03, E04
- Notes: Include checks for overtime rules and warehouse operation calendars.
