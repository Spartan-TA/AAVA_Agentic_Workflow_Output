Title: Schedule Automated Report Delivery
User Story: As a manager I want to schedule automated report delivery So that I receive regular updates without manual intervention
Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Automated report scheduling
Given I am logged in as manager
When I configure report delivery schedule
Then reports should be generated and sent to my email at the specified intervals
Additional Details:
- Priority: Medium
- Story Points: 2
- Dependencies: Reporting module, Notifications
- Notes: Support daily, weekly, and monthly schedules.
