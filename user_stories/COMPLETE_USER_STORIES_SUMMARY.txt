Project Overview:
This document contains the complete transformation of 20 high-level epics into detailed, 
actionable user stories for a Warehouse Employee Management System. Each user story 
includes a title, narrative in the format 'As a [role] I want [action] so that [benefit]', 
Gherkin-formatted acceptance criteria (Given/When/Then), and additional specifications 
including priority, story points, dependencies, and notes.

EPIC BREAKDOWN SUMMARY
===================================================================================

E01: Project Scaffolding & Domain Setup (5 stories, 9 story points)
- Foundation for Spring Boot application with Maven, base packages, DB migrations, 
  Actuator, and documentation

E02: Employee Master Data (CRUD) (5 stories, 14 story points)
- Complete employee management with CRUD operations, unique constraints, soft delete, 
  pagination, and filtering

E03: Role Based Access Control (RBAC) (5 stories, 13 story points)
- Security implementation with role-based access, row-level security, authentication 
  options, and automated testing

E04: Time & Attendance (Clock In/Out) (5 stories, 12 story points)
- Attendance tracking with clock-in/out, shift association, corrections workflow, 
  daily totals, and CSV exports

E05: Shift & Schedule Management (5 stories, 12 story points)
- Scheduling system with templates, assignments, conflict detection, bulk operations, 
  and audit trails

E06: Leave & Absence Management (5 stories, 9 story points)
- Leave management with requests, approvals, accrual tracking, coverage flagging, 
  and exports

E07: Training & Certification Tracking (5 stories, 9 story points)
- Certification management with CRUD, expiry alerts, assignment blocking, document 
  uploads, and profile display

E08: Safety Incidents & OSHA Reporting (4 stories, 9 story points)
- Safety tracking with incident recording, investigation workflow, OSHA exports, 
  and metrics dashboard

E09: Equipment & Asset Assignment (5 stories, 8 story points)
- Asset management with registry, checkout/return tracking, certification validation, 
  condition tracking, and overdue reports

E10: Performance Reviews & Goals (5 stories, 9 story points)
- Performance management with templates, assignments, goal tracking, acknowledgement 
  workflow, and PDF exports

E11: Payroll Export Integration (5 stories, 10 story points)
- Payroll integration with export generation, format mapping, secure delivery, 
  retry logic, and audit logging

E12: Notifications & Announcements (5 stories, 9 story points)
- Communication system with multi-channel notifications, opt-in/out, localization, 
  delivery tracking, and dashboard display

E13: Integration Layer (HRIS/WMS APIs) (5 stories, 10 story points)
- API integration with REST endpoints, JWT/OAuth2 security, HRIS sync, idempotent 
  webhooks, and OpenAPI documentation

E14: Audit Trail & Compliance (4 stories, 7 story points)
- Compliance system with comprehensive logging, tamper-evident storage, export 
  capabilities, and automated testing

E15: Reporting & Analytics (5 stories, 9 story points)
- Reporting system with operational reports, CSV/PDF exports, filtering, access 
  control, and BI integration

E16: Mobile Access (PWA) (5 stories, 11 story points)
- Mobile-first design with responsive views, PWA installation, offline queue, 
  conflict resolution, and Lighthouse optimization

E17: Onboarding & Offboarding Workflow (5 stories, 9 story points)
- Lifecycle management with account provisioning, schedule/training assignment, 
  asset management, deprovisioning, and task tracking

E18: Localization & Multi-Tenant (5 stories, 9 story points)
- Scalability features with tenant isolation, UI localization, timezone handling, 
  query scoping, and isolation testing

E19: Automated Testing & CI/CD (5 stories, 12 story points)
- Quality assurance with code coverage, contract tests, GitHub Actions pipeline, 
  Docker image management, and automated deployment

E20: Documentation & Developer Onboarding (5 stories, 6 story points)
- Developer enablement with comprehensive README, architecture diagrams, OpenAPI 
  specs, runbooks, and setup guides
