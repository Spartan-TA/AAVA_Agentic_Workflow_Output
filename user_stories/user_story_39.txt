Title: Track Goals, Competencies, and Comments

User Story: As an employee I want to view and update my performance goals, competencies, and comments during the review cycle so that I can participate actively in my evaluation

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Update goals and comments
  Given a performance review cycle is active
  When I add or update goals and comments
  Then the changes are saved and visible to my supervisor until the review is acknowledged

Additional Details:
- Priority: Medium
- Story Points: 2
- Dependencies: E02 (User Management), E03 (Scheduling)
- Notes: Employees and supervisors can both add comments
