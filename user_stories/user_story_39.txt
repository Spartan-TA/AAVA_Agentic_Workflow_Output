Title: Notify Shift Changes

User Story: As an employee I want to receive notifications when my shift is changed So that I am always aware of my current schedule

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Notify shift change
Given my shift assignment is updated
When the change is saved
Then I should receive a notification via email and/or mobile app

Additional Details:
- Priority: High
- Story Points: 2
- Dependencies: Notification system, shift scheduling
- Notes: Support push notifications and SMS.
