Title: Notify Users of Shift Changes and Approvals

User Story: As an employee I want to receive in-app and email/SMS notifications for shift changes and approvals so that I am always informed and can adjust my plans accordingly.

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Receiving a shift change notification
Given my shift has been changed or approved
When the change is finalized in the system
Then I should receive a notification via my preferred channel (in-app, email, or SMS)

Additional Details:
- Priority: Medium
- Story Points: 3
- Dependencies: E03, E05, E06, E07
- Notes: Notification preferences should be configurable by the user.
