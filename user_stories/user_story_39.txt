Title: Configure Safety Incident Categories

User Story: As a safety officer I want to configure safety incident categories So that incidents are classified consistently for reporting and analysis

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Safety incident category configuration
Given I am logged in as a safety officer
When I add or modify incident categories
Then the system should apply these categories to all new incident reports

Additional Details:
- Priority: Medium
- Story Points: 2
- Dependencies: Incident management
- Notes: Support for custom and standard categories
