Title: HRIS Sync for New Hires and Terminations

User Story: As an HR administrator I want the system to automatically sync new hires and terminations from the HRIS so that employee records are always up to date

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: HRIS sync job runs
  Given new hires or terminations are recorded in the HRIS
  When the sync job runs
  Then employee records are created, updated, or deactivated in the EMS

Additional Details:
- Priority: High
- Story Points: 2
- Dependencies: E01 (Core), E02 (User Management), E03 (Scheduling)
- Notes: Sync must be idempotent and log all changes
