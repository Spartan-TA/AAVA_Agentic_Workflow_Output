Title: Export OSHA Summary Reports

User Story: As a compliance officer I want to export OSHA 300/300A summary reports So that I can meet regulatory requirements and submit accurate data

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: OSHA report export
Given I have recorded safety incidents
When I generate an OSHA summary report
Then the report includes all required fields and is formatted for submission

Additional Details:
- Priority: High
- Story Points: 2
- Dependencies: Safety Incident Recording, Reporting & Analytics
- Notes: Export should support CSV and PDF formats.
