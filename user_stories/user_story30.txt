Title: Track Certification Expirations and Renewals

User Story:
As a Training Coordinator, I want the system to track certification expiration dates and send renewal reminders so that employees remain compliant and assignments are not disrupted.

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario 1: Display expiration dates on employee profiles
  Given certifications are tracked
  When I view an employee profile
  Then their certification expiration dates are displayed

Scenario 2: Send renewal reminders
  Given a certification is expiring within 30 days
  When the date approaches
  Then the employee and supervisor receive a renewal notification

Scenario 3: Block assignments for expired certifications
  Given a certification is expired
  When I attempt to assign the employee to a restricted role
  Then the system prevents the assignment and displays a warning

Priority: High
Story Points: 8
Dependencies: E02, E03
Notes: Renewal reminders must integrate with notifications and audit trail.
