Title: Manager Complete Employee Evaluation

User Story: As a manager I want to complete performance evaluations for my direct reports so that feedback and development plans are documented

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Completing an evaluation
Given a review cycle is active
When I fill out and submit an employee evaluation form
Then the employee is notified and the evaluation is stored in their record

Additional Details:
- Priority: High
- Story Points: 3
- Dependencies: Notification module, employee master data
- Notes: Support for rating scales, comments, and goal setting.
