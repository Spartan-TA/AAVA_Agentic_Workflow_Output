Title: Incident Investigation Workflow

User Story: As a safety manager I want to progress safety incidents through investigation and resolution statuses so that corrective actions are tracked and compliance is maintained.

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Incident status workflow
Given an incident is recorded
When I update the status to Investigating or Resolved
Then the system logs the change and requires documentation of actions taken

Additional Details:
- Priority: High
- Story Points: 5
- Dependencies: E02, E03
- Notes: Status transitions must be auditable; notifications sent on status change.
