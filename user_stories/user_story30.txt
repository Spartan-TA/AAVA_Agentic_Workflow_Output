Title: Employee Views Performance Goals
User Story: As an employee I want to view my performance goals and review history So that I can track my progress and prepare for evaluations
Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: View performance goals
Given I am logged into the system
When I navigate to the performance section
Then I should see my current goals and past review feedback
Additional Details:
- Priority: High
- Story Points: 2
- Dependencies: Performance management module
- Notes: Display goal status and review dates.
