Title: Supervisors Approve or Deny Leave Requests

User Story: As a supervisor I want to approve or deny leave requests So that I can manage team coverage and ensure operational continuity

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Approving a leave request
Given a leave request is pending approval
When I review and approve or deny the request
Then the employee should be notified and the leave balance updated accordingly

Additional Details:
- Priority: High
- Story Points: 3
- Dependencies: Employee Master Data, Leave Request
- Notes: Approval workflow should support comments and escalation for overlapping requests.
