Title: Automated Certification Expiry Reminders

User Story: As an employee I want to receive automated reminders before my certifications expire So that I can renew them on time and remain compliant

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Receiving expiry reminders
Given my certification is expiring soon
When the system detects the expiry date
Then I should receive a notification with renewal instructions

Additional Details:
- Priority: Medium
- Story Points: 2
- Dependencies: Notification system, certification tracking
- Notes: Reminders should be sent at configurable intervals.
