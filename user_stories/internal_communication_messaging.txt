Title: Internal Communication Messaging

User Story: As a warehouse employee, I want to send and receive messages with colleagues and managers So that I can coordinate tasks and shifts.

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Messaging
Given I am logged in
When I send a message to a colleague or manager
Then the recipient receives a notification and can reply

Additional Details:
- Priority: Medium
- Story Points: 3
- Dependencies: Employee Profile Management
- Notes: Supports group and individual messaging
