Executive Summary

This document presents a comprehensive breakdown of 20 high-level epics for the Warehouse Employee Management System (EMS) into actionable, testable user stories. Each epic has been decomposed into multiple user stories, each formatted to industry best practices, including Gherkin acceptance criteria, priority, story points, dependencies, and contextual notes. The user stories are organized by epic and are designed to be independent, properly sized, and aligned with the INVEST principles, ensuring clarity and readiness for agile development teams.
