### E12 - Notifications & Announcements

#### User Story 1
Title: Configure Notification Channels
User Story: As a WORKER, I want to opt-in/out of notification channels so that I receive updates in my preferred way.
Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Channel Configuration
Given notification settings
When WORKER updates preferences
Then notifications are sent only to selected channels
Additional Details:
- Priority: Medium
- Story Points: 1
- Dependencies: E03, E05, E06, E07
- Notes: In-app, email, SMS supported
