### E12 - Notifications & Announcements

**User Story 1: Opt-In/Out Per Notification Channel**
- Title: Manage Notification Preferences
- Narrative: As a user, I want to opt-in or out of notification channels so that I control how I am contacted.
- Acceptance Criteria:
  ```
  Given notification settings,
  When I update preferences,
  Then my choices are saved and respected.
  ```
- Priority: Medium
- Story Points: 2
- Dependencies: E03, E05, E06, E07
- Notes: User control.

**User Story 2: Localized Notification Templates**
- Title: Localize Notification Templates
- Narrative: As a user, I want notifications in my preferred language so that communication is clear.
- Acceptance Criteria:
  ```
  Given my locale,
  When a notification is sent,
  Then it uses the correct language template.
  ```
- Priority: Medium
- Story Points: 2
- Dependencies: Story 1
- Notes: Accessibility.

**User Story 3: Delivery Status Tracking**
- Title: Track Notification Delivery Status
- Narrative: As an admin, I want to track notification delivery status so that I know if messages are received.
- Acceptance Criteria:
  ```
  Given a sent notification,
  When delivered,
  Then status is updated and visible on dashboard.
  ```
- Priority: Medium
- Story Points: 1
- Dependencies: Story 1
- Notes: Reliability.

**User Story 4: Rate Limits for Notifications**
- Title: Apply Rate Limits to Notifications
- Narrative: As an admin, I want rate limits on notifications so that users are not spammed.
- Acceptance Criteria:
  ```
  Given notification traffic,
  When limits are exceeded,
  Then excess notifications are queued or dropped.
  ```
- Priority: Medium
- Story Points: 1
- Dependencies: Story 1
- Notes: User experience.

**User Story 5: Announcements Dashboard Visibility**
- Title: Announcements on Dashboard
- Narrative: As a user, I want to see announcements on my dashboard so that I am informed of important updates.
- Acceptance Criteria:
  ```
  Given active announcements,
  When I view my dashboard,
  Then announcements are displayed.
  ```
- Priority: Medium
- Story Points: 1
- Dependencies: Story 1
- Notes: Communication.
