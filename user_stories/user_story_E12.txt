### E12: Notifications & Announcements

#### User Story 1
Title: User Notification Channel Preferences
User Story: As a user, I want to opt in or out of in-app, email, and SMS notifications so that I receive updates in my preferred way.
Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Update preferences
  Given I am logged in
  When I update my notification preferences
  Then my choices are saved and respected for future notifications
Additional Details:
Priority: Medium
Story Points: 3
Dependencies: E03
Notes: Preferences should be editable at any time.

#### User Story 2
Title: Automated Shift and Approval Notifications
User Story: As an employee, I want to receive notifications for shift changes and approvals so that I stay informed.
Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Shift change notification
  Given my shift is changed or a leave is approved
  When the event occurs
  Then I receive a notification via my preferred channel
Additional Details:
Priority: Medium
Story Points: 5
Dependencies: E05, E06
Notes: Quiet hours must be respected.

#### User Story 3
Title: Certification Expiry and Announcement Notifications
User Story: As a supervisor, I want to receive notifications about expiring certifications and company announcements so that I can take action.
Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Certification expiry alert
  Given a certification is expiring
  When the alert is triggered
  Then I receive a notification
Scenario: Announcement visibility
  Given an announcement is posted
  When I log in
  Then it is visible on my dashboard
Additional Details:
Priority: Medium
Story Points: 3
Dependencies: E07
Notes: Announcements should be dismissible.

#### User Story 4
Title: Notification Delivery Tracking and Rate Limiting
User Story: As an admin, I want to track notification delivery status and apply rate limits so that users are not spammed and issues are visible.
Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Delivery tracking
  Given notifications are sent
  When I view delivery logs
  Then I see status (delivered, failed, pending) for each user
Scenario: Rate limiting
  Given a user receives many notifications
  When rate limit is exceeded
  Then further notifications are delayed or batched
Additional Details:
Priority: Medium
Story Points: 5
Dependencies: E12
Notes: Logs must be exportable.
