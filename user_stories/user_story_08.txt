Title: Role Assignment to Employees

User Story: As an HR administrator I want to assign roles to employees So that I can control their access and permissions in the system

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Assign role to employee
Given I am on the employee profile page
When I select a role and assign it to the employee
Then the employee should have the corresponding permissions immediately

Additional Details:
- Priority: High
- Story Points: 5
- Dependencies: Role management module
- Notes: Changes should be audit logged.
