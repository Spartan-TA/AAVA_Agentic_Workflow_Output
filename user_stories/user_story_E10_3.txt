Title: Supervisor and Employee Acknowledgements

User Story: As an employee I want to acknowledge my performance review So that I can confirm receipt and understanding of feedback

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Employee acknowledges review
Given a performance review has been completed
When I log in and view my review
Then I should be able to acknowledge receipt and add optional comments

Additional Details:
- Priority: Medium
- Story Points: 1
- Dependencies: E02 (Employee Profiles), E03 (Scheduling)
- Notes: Acknowledgement status should be tracked and visible to HR and supervisors.
