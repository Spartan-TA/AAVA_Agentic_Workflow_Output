Title: Supervisor and Employee Acknowledgements

User Story: As a Supervisor I want to acknowledge and sign off on completed performance reviews so that both parties confirm the review has been discussed and finalized.

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Supervisor Acknowledgement
Given a performance review is completed
When I click the acknowledge button
Then my acknowledgement is recorded and visible to the employee

Scenario: Employee Acknowledgement
Given the supervisor has acknowledged the review
When the employee clicks the acknowledge button
Then the review is marked as finalized and locked for further edits

Additional Details:
- Priority: Medium
- Story Points: 3
- Dependencies: E02, E03
- Notes: Acknowledgement timestamps must be stored and displayed in the review history.
