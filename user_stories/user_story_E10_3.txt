Title: Supervisor/Employee Acknowledgement Workflow

User Story: As an employee I want to acknowledge my performance review after my supervisor submits it so that both parties confirm the review is complete and accurate.

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Review acknowledgement
Given a review has been submitted by the supervisor
When I log in and view my review
Then I should be able to acknowledge receipt, add optional comments, and the review should be locked from further edits

Additional Details:
- Priority: Medium
- Story Points: 3
- Dependencies: Employee Master Data (E02), RBAC (E03)
- Notes: Acknowledgement timestamp and PDF export required for compliance.
