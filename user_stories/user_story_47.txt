Title: Quiet Hours Configuration for Notifications

User Story: As a worker I want to set quiet hours for notifications so that I am not disturbed outside my preferred time window

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Configuring quiet hours
Given I am on the notification settings page
When I set a start and end time for quiet hours
Then notifications are suppressed during that period except for critical alerts

Additional Details:
- Priority: Medium
- Story Points: 2
- Dependencies: Notification scheduler
- Notes: Quiet hours must be respected for all non-critical notifications
