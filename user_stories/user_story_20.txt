Title: Assign Employees to Shifts

User Story: As a supervisor I want to assign employees to shifts so that I can ensure adequate staffing for warehouse operations.

Acceptance Criteria:

Scenario: Assign employee to single shift
Given an employee and a shift template exist
When I assign the employee to a specific date and shift
Then the assignment should be created
And the employee should see the shift in their schedule

Scenario: Bulk assign employees to shifts
Given I have multiple employees to schedule
When I bulk assign them to shifts for a week
Then all assignments should be created
And conflicts should be detected and reported

Scenario: Detect scheduling conflicts
Given an employee is already assigned to a shift
When I try to assign them to an overlapping shift
Then the system should prevent the assignment
And display a conflict error message

Scenario: View employee schedule
Given an employee has shift assignments
When they view their schedule
Then they should see all upcoming shifts
And shifts should show date, time, and location

Scenario: Generate audit entries
Given a shift assignment is created or modified
When the change is saved
Then an audit entry should be created
And it should capture who made the change and when

Additional Details:
- Priority: High
- Story Points: 5
- Dependencies: Shift templates (E05), Employee entity (E02), RBAC (E03)
- Notes: Implement conflict detection. Support drag-and-drop scheduling UI. Allow shift swaps with approval.
