Title: Request and Approve Leave

User Story: As an employee I want to request PTO, sick, or unpaid leave and as a supervisor I want to approve or deny leave requests so that leave balances and coverage are managed

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Requesting and approving leave
Given I am an employee
When I submit a leave request
Then my supervisor receives an approval task
And when my supervisor approves or denies
Then my leave balance updates and scheduled shifts are flagged for coverage

Additional Details:
- Priority: Medium
- Story Points: 3
- Dependencies: E02, E03, E05
- Notes: Leave requests and approvals must be tracked and reflected in scheduling.
