Title: Export Approved Leaves

User Story: As an HR administrator I want to export a list of approved leaves so that I can process payroll and compliance reporting

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Export approved leaves
Given I am on the leave management page
When I select a date range and export format
Then a file with all approved leaves should be generated and downloaded

Additional Details:
- Priority: Medium
- Story Points: 2
- Dependencies: Leave management module, Reporting module
- Notes: Support CSV and PDF export formats.
