Title: Audit Shift Assignments

User Story: As an administrator I want to view a log of all shift assignments So that I can audit scheduling decisions and ensure compliance

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: View shift assignment audit log
Given I am on the audit log page
When I filter by date or employee
Then I should see all relevant shift assignment actions with timestamps and user details

Additional Details:
- Priority: Medium
- Story Points: 3
- Dependencies: Shift assignment, logging module
- Notes: Audit log should be exportable.
