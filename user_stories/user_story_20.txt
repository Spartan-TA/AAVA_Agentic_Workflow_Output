Title: Leave Request Submission
User Story: As an employee I want to submit leave requests for PTO, sick, or unpaid leave So that my time off is tracked and approved according to company policy
Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Submit leave request Given I am logged in and have available leave balance When I fill out and submit a leave request form Then the request is recorded and routed for supervisor approval
Additional Details:
- Priority: Medium
- Story Points: 2
- Dependencies: Employee master data, leave policies
- Notes: Request form should validate leave type and balance.
