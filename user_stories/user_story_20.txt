Title: View Personal Upcoming Shifts

User Story: As a warehouse worker I want to view my upcoming shifts So that I can plan my work schedule and avoid confusion

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: View upcoming shifts
Given I am logged in as a worker
When I access my schedule
Then I see a list of all upcoming assigned shifts

Scenario: No assigned shifts
Given I have no upcoming shifts
When I access my schedule
Then I see a message indicating no shifts are scheduled

Additional Details:
- Priority: High
- Story Points: 2
- Dependencies: Employee CRUD, RBAC, Shift Assignments
- Notes: View must be mobile-friendly
