Title: Employee Submits Leave Request

User Story: As an employee I want to submit a leave request So that I can formally notify my manager of planned absences

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Submitting a leave request
Given I am logged in as an employee
When I fill out and submit the leave request form
Then my manager should be notified and the request should appear in their approval queue

Additional Details:
- Priority: High
- Story Points: 3
- Dependencies: Employee master data, notifications
- Notes: Prevent submission for dates overlapping with approved leave.
