Title: View Upcoming Shifts

User Story: As a warehouse worker I want to view my upcoming scheduled shifts So that I can plan my attendance and personal commitments

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Viewing personal shift schedule
Given I am logged into the system
When I navigate to my schedule
Then I see a list of all upcoming shifts assigned to me

Additional Details:
- Priority: High
- Story Points: 1
- Dependencies: Employee Master Data, RBAC, Time & Attendance
- Notes: Display should be mobile-friendly and support calendar export.
