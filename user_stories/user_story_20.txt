Title: Accrual Balances Update and Shift Auto-Flagging

User Story: As an employee I want my leave accrual balances to update automatically and my scheduled shifts to be flagged when leave is approved so that my records and coverage are accurate

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Leave approval updates balances and flags shifts
Given a leave request is approved
When the approval is processed
Then the employee's accrual balance is reduced and any scheduled shifts during the leave period are flagged for coverage

Additional Details:
- Priority: Medium
- Story Points: 5
- Dependencies: Scheduling, Payroll, Employee Master Data
- Notes: Flagged shifts should trigger notifications for coverage.
