Title: Assign Training Courses

User Story: As a training coordinator I want to assign training courses to employees So that I can ensure compliance and skill development

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Assign training course
Given I am in the training management module
When I select a course and assign it to employees
Then the assignments should be saved and employees notified

Additional Details:
- Priority: High
- Story Points: 5
- Dependencies: Training management module, notification system
- Notes: Support for mandatory and optional courses.
