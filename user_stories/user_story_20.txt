Title: Missed Punches & Corrections Workflow

User Story: As an employee I want to submit corrections for missed clock in/out punches So that my attendance records are accurate and payroll is correct

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Submitting a missed punch correction
  Given I missed a clock in/out event
  When I submit a correction request with supporting details
  Then my manager should be notified and able to approve or reject the correction
Scenario: Approved correction
  Given my correction is approved
  When the attendance record is updated
  Then my hours should be recalculated and reflected in payroll
Scenario: Rejected correction
  Given my correction is rejected
  When I view my attendance record
  Then I should see the rejection reason

Additional Details:
- Priority: High
- Story Points: 3
- Dependencies: Attendance records, approval workflow
- Notes: Corrections should be logged and auditable for compliance.
