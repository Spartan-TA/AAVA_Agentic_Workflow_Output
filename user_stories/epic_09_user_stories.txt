### E09: Equipment & Asset Assignment

**User Story 9.1**
Title: Asset Registry Management

User Story: As an Asset Manager I want to add, edit, or remove equipment and PPE records So that asset inventory is accurate.

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Asset management
Given I manage assets
When I update the registry
Then changes are reflected and audit logged

Additional Details:
- Priority: Medium
- Story Points: 3
- Dependencies: E02, E03, E07
- Notes: Asset types, serial numbers, and condition required

---

**User Story 9.2**
Title: Asset Check-In/Check-Out

User Story: As a Supervisor I want to assign and track equipment issued to employees So that accountability is maintained.

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Asset checkout
Given an asset is available
When I check it out to an employee
Then the assignment is recorded and the asset status updated

Additional Details:
- Priority: Medium
- Story Points: 3
- Dependencies: E02, E03, E07
- Notes: Check-in/out history visible per asset and employee

---

**User Story 9.3**
Title: Certification Validation for Asset Use

User Story: As a System I want to block asset assignment if required certifications are missing or expired So that only qualified staff operate equipment.

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Certification check
Given an employee lacks valid certification
When attempting to assign restricted equipment
Then the system blocks the assignment and notifies the supervisor

Additional Details:
- Priority: Medium
- Story Points: 5
- Dependencies: E02, E03, E07
- Notes: Certification-asset mapping enforced

---

**User Story 9.4**
Title: Overdue Asset Return Reporting

User Story: As an Asset Manager I want to generate reports of overdue asset returns So that follow-up actions can be taken.

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Overdue report
Given assets are checked out
When the due date passes
Then the asset appears in the overdue report

Additional Details:
- Priority: Medium
- Story Points: 2
- Dependencies: E02, E03, E07
- Notes: Report exportable to CSV
