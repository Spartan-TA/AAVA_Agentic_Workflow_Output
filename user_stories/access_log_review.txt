Title: Access Log Review

User Story: As a warehouse manager, I want to review inventory access logs So that I can monitor who accessed inventory and when.

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Log review
Given access logs are available
When I filter logs by employee or date
Then I see detailed access records

Additional Details:
- Priority: Medium
- Story Points: 2
- Dependencies: Inventory Access Control
- Notes: Supports export to CSV
