Title: Mobile Clock-In/Out and Schedule Viewing

User Story: As a warehouse worker I want to clock in/out and view my schedule from my mobile device so that I can manage my shifts conveniently

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Use mobile PWA for clock-in/out
  Given I have installed the PWA on my mobile device
  When I open the app and clock in or view my schedule
  Then the actions are processed and my schedule is displayed responsively

Additional Details:
- Priority: Medium
- Story Points: 3
- Dependencies: E04 (Attendance), E05 (Scheduling), E06 (Leave), E12 (Notifications)
- Notes: Must work on iOS and Android browsers
