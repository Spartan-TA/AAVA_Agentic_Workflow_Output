Title: Track Notification Delivery Status and Apply Rate Limits

User Story: As a system administrator I want notification delivery status to be tracked and rate limits applied So that users are not spammed and delivery issues are visible

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Delivery tracking and rate limiting
Given notifications are sent to users
When delivery status is updated
Then the system logs delivery success/failure and enforces rate limits per user/channel

Additional Details:
- Priority: Medium
- Story Points: 4
- Dependencies: E03, E05, E06, E07
- Notes: Rate limits configurable by admin; delivery logs exportable.
