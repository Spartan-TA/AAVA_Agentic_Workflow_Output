### E10 - Performance Reviews & Goals

**User Story 1: Create Review Templates**
- Title: Create Performance Review Templates
- Narrative: As an HR user, I want to create review templates so that evaluations are standardized.
- Acceptance Criteria:
  ```
  Given review templates,
  When I create or edit,
  Then templates are saved and available for assignment.
  ```
- Priority: Medium
- Story Points: 2
- Dependencies: E02, E03
- Notes: Standardization.

**User Story 2: Assign Reviews to Employees**
- Title: Assign Reviews to Employees
- Narrative: As a supervisor, I want to assign reviews to employees so that performance is tracked.
- Acceptance Criteria:
  ```
  Given a review template,
  When I assign to employees,
  Then assignments are visible to both parties.
  ```
- Priority: Medium
- Story Points: 2
- Dependencies: Story 1
- Notes: Tracking.

**User Story 3: Submit and Acknowledge Reviews**
- Title: Submit and Acknowledge Performance Reviews
- Narrative: As an employee, I want to submit and acknowledge reviews so that feedback is formalized.
- Acceptance Criteria:
  ```
  Given an assigned review,
  When submitted and acknowledged,
  Then the review is locked and history is immutable.
  ```
- Priority: Medium
- Story Points: 2
- Dependencies: Story 2
- Notes: Compliance.

**User Story 4: PDF Export of Reviews**
- Title: Export Reviews to PDF
- Narrative: As an HR user, I want to export reviews to PDF so that records are easily shared.
- Acceptance Criteria:
  ```
  Given a completed review,
  When I request export,
  Then a PDF is generated.
  ```
- Priority: Medium
- Story Points: 1
- Dependencies: Story 3
- Notes: Portability.

**User Story 5: Role-Based Visibility of Reviews**
- Title: Role-Based Review Visibility
- Narrative: As a supervisor, I want review visibility controlled by role so that privacy is maintained.
- Acceptance Criteria:
  ```
  Given a review,
  When accessed by different roles,
  Then only authorized users can view details.
  ```
- Priority: Medium
- Story Points: 1
- Dependencies: Story 3
- Notes: Privacy.
