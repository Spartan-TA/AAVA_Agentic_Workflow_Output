EPIC E10: Performance Reviews & Goals

===========================================

Title: Manage Performance Review Cycles
User Story: As an HR manager, I want to create quarterly and annual review cycles and templates so that performance reviews are standardized.
Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Creating a review cycle
Given I am on the review management page
When I create a new cycle with a template
Then the cycle is available for assignment to employees
Additional Details:
- Priority: Medium
- Story Points: 5
- Dependencies: E02, E03
- Notes: Supports multiple review types.

===========================================

Title: Assign Reviews to Employees
User Story: As a supervisor, I want to assign performance reviews to my team and track completion so that feedback is timely.
Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Assigning a review
Given a review cycle is active
When I assign it to an employee
Then the employee receives a notification and can complete their self-assessment
Additional Details:
- Priority: Medium
- Story Points: 3
- Dependencies: E12
- Notes: Tracks completion status.

===========================================

Title: Submit and Acknowledge Performance Reviews
User Story: As an employee, I want to submit my self-assessment and acknowledge supervisor feedback so that the review process is complete.
Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Submitting self-assessment
Given a review is assigned
When I submit my self-assessment
Then my supervisor can provide ratings and comments
Scenario: Acknowledging review
Given my supervisor has submitted feedback
When I acknowledge the review
Then the review is marked as complete and locked
Additional Details:
- Priority: Medium
- Story Points: 5
- Dependencies: E03
- Notes: Digital signature for acknowledgement.

===========================================

Title: Export Performance Reviews
User Story: As an HR manager, I want to export completed reviews as PDF so that I can archive and share them.
Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Exporting review
Given a review is complete
When I export as PDF
Then a formatted PDF is generated with all ratings and comments
Additional Details:
- Priority: Low
- Story Points: 2
- Dependencies: E10
- Notes: Template-based PDF generation.

===========================================

Title: Lock Completed Reviews
User Story: As a system, I want completed reviews to be immutable so that the integrity of the review process is maintained.
Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Locking review
Given a review is marked complete
When any user attempts to edit it
Then the system prevents changes and displays a message
Additional Details:
- Priority: Medium
- Story Points: 2
- Dependencies: E14
- Notes: Audit trail for lock events.
