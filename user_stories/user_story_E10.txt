### E10: Performance Reviews & Goals

#### User Story 1
Title: Performance Review Template Management
User Story: As an HR admin, I want to create and manage review templates so that reviews are consistent and role-appropriate.
Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Create template
  Given I am an HR admin
  When I create a template
  Then it includes goals, competencies, and rating scales
Additional Details:
Priority: Medium
Story Points: 5
Dependencies: E02, E03
Notes: Templates should be versioned.

#### User Story 2
Title: Assign and Complete Performance Reviews
User Story: As a supervisor, I want to assign reviews to employees and complete them so that feedback is documented.
Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Assign review
  Given a review cycle is open
  When I assign a review
  Then the employee is notified and can acknowledge
Scenario: Complete review
  Given a review is completed
  When both supervisor and employee acknowledge
  Then the review is locked and archived
Additional Details:
Priority: Medium
Story Points: 8
Dependencies: E03
Notes: Reviews must be immutable after sign-off.

#### User Story 3
Title: Export Performance Reviews as PDF
User Story: As an HR admin, I want to export completed reviews as PDF so that I can share or archive them.
Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: PDF export
  Given a review is completed
  When I export it
  Then I receive a PDF with all ratings, comments, and signatures
Additional Details:
Priority: Low
Story Points: 3
Dependencies: E15
Notes: PDF must be formatted per company branding.
