### E10: Performance Reviews & Goals

**User Story 1: Create and Assign Review Templates**
- User Story: As an HR user, I want to create performance review templates and assign them to employees So that reviews are standardized.
- Acceptance Criteria:
  - Scenario: Create template
    - Given review criteria
    - When I create a template
    - Then it is available for assignment
  - Scenario: Assign review
    - Given a template and employee
    - When I assign the review
    - Then the employee and supervisor can access it
- Priority: Medium
- Story Points: 5
- Dependencies: E02, E03
- Notes: Quarterly/annual cycles.

**User Story 2: Review Submission and Acknowledgement**
- User Story: As a supervisor, I want to submit reviews and have employees acknowledge them So that feedback is documented.
- Acceptance Criteria:
  - Scenario: Submit review
    - Given a review in progress
    - When I submit it
    - Then the employee is notified to acknowledge
  - Scenario: Acknowledge review
    - Given a submitted review
    - When the employee acknowledges
    - Then the review is locked and history is immutable
- Priority: Medium
- Story Points: 3
- Dependencies: E02, E03
- Notes: PDF export required.
