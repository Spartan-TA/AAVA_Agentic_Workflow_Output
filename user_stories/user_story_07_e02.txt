Title: Delete (Soft-Delete) Employee

User Story: As an HR manager I want to soft-delete employee records So that terminated employees are deactivated but their history is retained for compliance

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Employee soft-deletion
Given an active employee record
When I choose to delete the employee
Then the record is marked as inactive and hidden from active lists but retained in the database

Additional Details:
- Priority: Medium
- Story Points: 2
- Dependencies: Employee domain module, audit logging
- Notes: Ensure deleted employees cannot log in or be assigned shifts.
