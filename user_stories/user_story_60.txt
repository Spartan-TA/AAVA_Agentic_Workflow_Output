Title: Role-Based Dashboards for Reporting

User Story: As a department head I want to view a dashboard with key metrics relevant to my role so that I can quickly assess operational performance

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Viewing a role-based dashboard
Given I am assigned a department head role
When I access the reporting dashboard
Then I see attendance, overtime, and safety KPIs for my department only

Additional Details:
- Priority: Medium
- Story Points: 2
- Dependencies: Dashboard module, role management
- Notes: Access must be controlled by user role
