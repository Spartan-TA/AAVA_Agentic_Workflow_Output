Title: Automated Offboarding for Terminations

User Story: As an HR administrator I want terminated employees to be deprovisioned from all systems, assets collected, and schedules updated so that offboarding is secure and complete

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Offboard terminated employee
  Given an employee is terminated in the HRIS
  When the offboarding workflow runs
  Then access is revoked, assets are collected, and future schedules are cleared

Additional Details:
- Priority: Medium
- Story Points: 3
- Dependencies: E02 (User Management), E03 (Scheduling), E05 (Attendance), E07 (Certifications), E09 (Assets), E13 (Integration)
- Notes: Offboarding tasks must be tracked and auditable
