### E04: Time & Attendance (Clock In/Out)

**User Story 4.1**
Title: Clock-In/Clock-Out Endpoints

User Story: As a warehouse worker I want to clock in and out via API So that my working hours are accurately recorded.

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Clock in/out
Given a valid employee
When I POST to /attendance/clock-in or /clock-out
Then my event is recorded with timestamp

Additional Details:
- Priority: High
- Story Points: 2
- Dependencies: E02, E03
- Notes: Device and location capture optional

---

**User Story 4.2**
Title: Calculate Hours Worked Per Shift

User Story: As a supervisor I want the system to calculate hours worked per shift So that payroll and compliance are accurate.

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Shift total calculation
Given clock-in and clock-out events
When I view shift summary
Then total hours are computed correctly

Additional Details:
- Priority: High
- Story Points: 2
- Dependencies: E04.1
- Notes: Handle overnight shifts

---

**User Story 4.3**
Title: Handle Missed Punches and Corrections

User Story: As a worker I want to request corrections for missed punches So that my attendance is accurate.

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Missed punch correction
Given a missed clock-in/out
When I submit a correction request
Then a supervisor is notified for approval

Additional Details:
- Priority: High
- Story Points: 2
- Dependencies: E04.1
- Notes: Correction workflow required

---

**User Story 4.4**
Title: Export Attendance Reports

Acceptance Criteria:
