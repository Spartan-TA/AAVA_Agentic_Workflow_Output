### E10 - Performance Reviews & Goals

#### User Story 1: Create Review Templates
- **Title:** Create Performance Review Templates
- **User Story:** As an HR, I want to create templates for quarterly and annual reviews so that evaluations are standardized.
- **Acceptance Criteria:**
    - Given a template,
      When I create or update it,
      Then it is available for review cycles.
- **Priority:** Medium
- **Story Points:** 2
- **Dependencies:** E02, E03
- **Notes:** Standardization.

#### User Story 2: Assign Reviews to Employees
- **Title:** Assign Reviews to Employees
- **User Story:** As a SUPERVISOR, I want to assign performance reviews to employees so that feedback is collected on schedule.
- **Acceptance Criteria:**
    - Given a review cycle,
      When I assign it,
      Then employees receive review tasks.
- **Priority:** Medium
- **Story Points:** 2
- **Dependencies:** E10-1
- **Notes:** Timely feedback.

#### User Story 3: Submit and Acknowledge Reviews
- **Title:** Submit and Acknowledge Performance Reviews
- **User Story:** As a SUPERVISOR or EMPLOYEE, I want to submit and acknowledge reviews so that the process is complete and transparent.
- **Acceptance Criteria:**
    - Given a review,
      When it is submitted,
      Then both supervisor and employee can acknowledge.
    - Given a completed review,
      When acknowledged,
      Then it becomes immutable.
- **Priority:** Medium
- **Story Points:** 3
- **Dependencies:** E10-2
- **Notes:** Immutable after sign-off.

#### User Story 4: Track Goals and Competencies
- **Title:** Track Goals and Competencies in Reviews
- **User Story:** As a SUPERVISOR, I want to track goals and competencies in reviews so that employee development is monitored.
- **Acceptance Criteria:**
    - Given a review,
      When I add goals and competencies,
      Then they are saved and visible in the review.
- **Priority:** Medium
- **Story Points:** 2
- **Dependencies:** E10-1
- **Notes:** Development focus.

#### User Story 5: Export Reviews as PDF
- **Title:** Export Performance Reviews as PDF
- **User Story:** As an HR, I want to export completed reviews as PDF so that records are archived and shared.
- **Acceptance Criteria:**
    - Given a completed review,
      When I request an export,
      Then a PDF file is generated.
- **Priority:** Medium
- **Story Points:** 1
- **Dependencies:** E10-3
- **Notes:** For record-keeping.
