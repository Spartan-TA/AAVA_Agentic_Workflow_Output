Title: Audit Payroll Exports

User Story: As a compliance officer I want to audit payroll exports So that I can ensure accuracy and traceability of payroll processing

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Payroll export audit
Given payroll exports are performed
When I access the audit log
Then I should see details of who exported, when, and what data was included

Additional Details:
- Priority: Medium
- Story Points: 2
- Dependencies: Payroll export module, audit trail
- Notes: Audit logs should be immutable
