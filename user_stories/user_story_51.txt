Title: Operational Reports with Filters and Exports

User Story: As a manager I want to generate attendance, overtime, leave, certification, and safety reports with filters so that I can analyze workforce metrics and export them for further review

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Generate and export operational reports
  Given I have access to the reporting module
  When I filter by date, department, or shift and export the report
  Then I receive a CSV or PDF file within 10 seconds for up to 50,000 rows

Additional Details:
- Priority: Medium
- Story Points: 5
- Dependencies: E04, E05, E06, E07, E08, E14
- Notes: Export must be access controlled and support large datasets efficiently.
