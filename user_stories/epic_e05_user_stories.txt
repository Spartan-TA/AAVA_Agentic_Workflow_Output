EPIC E05: Shift & Schedule Management

===========================================

Title: Shift & Schedule Management

User Story: As a supervisor I want to create recurring shift templates and assign employees So that staffing is planned efficiently

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Shift template creation
Given I have supervisor role
When I create a shift template
Then it is available for assignment

Additional Details:
- Priority: High
- Story Points: 3
- Dependencies: Employee CRUD, Attendance
- Notes: Rotations and overtime rules

---

Title: Detect and Prevent Scheduling Conflicts

User Story: As a supervisor I want the system to detect scheduling conflicts So that double-booking is avoided

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Conflict detection
Given an employee is assigned a shift
When I try to assign another overlapping shift
Then the system blocks the assignment

Additional Details:
- Priority: High
- Story Points: 2
- Dependencies: Schedule management
- Notes: Conflicts flagged in UI

---

Title: Bulk Assign Shifts

User Story: As a supervisor I want to bulk-assign shifts to employees So that scheduling is faster

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Bulk assignment
Given multiple employees selected
When I assign a shift
Then all receive the assignment

Additional Details:
- Priority: Medium
- Story Points: 2
- Dependencies: Schedule management
- Notes: Audit entries generated
