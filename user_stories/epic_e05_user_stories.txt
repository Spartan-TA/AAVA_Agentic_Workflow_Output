### Epic E05: Shift & Schedule Management

**User Story 1**
Title: Create Shift Templates
User Story: As an HR manager, I want to define recurring shift templates so that scheduling is efficient.
Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Shift Template Creation
Given valid shift details
When I POST to /shifts/templates
Then a new template is created
Additional Details:
- Priority: High
- Story Points: 2
- Dependencies: Employee API, RBAC, Attendance
- Notes: Templates reusable

**User Story 2**
Title: Assign Shifts to Employees
User Story: As a supervisor, I want to assign shifts to employees so that staffing is planned.
Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Bulk Assignment
Given a list of employees
When I assign a shift
Then all selected employees are scheduled
Additional Details:
- Priority: High
- Story Points: 2
- Dependencies: Shift templates
- Notes: Audit entries generated

**User Story 3**
Title: Detect Scheduling Conflicts
User Story: As a scheduler, I want the system to detect and prevent shift conflicts so that double-booking is avoided.
Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Conflict Detection
Given overlapping shifts
When I attempt to assign
Then the system blocks the assignment
And notifies the user
Additional Details:
- Priority: High
- Story Points: 2
- Dependencies: Shift assignment
- Notes: Blackout dates handled

**User Story 4**
Title: Personal Upcoming Shifts View
User Story: As a worker, I want to view my upcoming shifts so that I can plan my schedule.
Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: View Shifts
Given I am logged in
When I access my schedule
Then my assigned shifts are displayed
Additional Details:
- Priority: Medium
- Story Points: 1
- Dependencies: Shift assignment
- Notes: Role-based access
