Title: Employee Self-Service Profile Viewing

User Story: As a warehouse employee, I want to view my profile So that I can verify my personal and employment information.

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Profile viewing
Given I am logged in as a warehouse employee
When I navigate to my profile page
Then I can see my personal and employment details

Additional Details:
- Priority: Medium
- Story Points: 1
- Dependencies: Employee Profile Creation
- Notes: Read-only access for employees
