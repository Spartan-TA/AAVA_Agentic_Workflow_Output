Title: Automated Provisioning for New Hires
User Story: As an HR manager I want new hires to be automatically provisioned with accounts, schedules, and training tasks So that onboarding is efficient and consistent
Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: New hire provisioning
Given a new hire appears from HRIS
When onboarding is triggered
Then accounts, schedules, and training tasks are created
Additional Details:
- Priority: Medium
- Story Points: 2
- Dependencies: HRIS, Scheduling, Training
- Notes: Provisioning workflow documented

Title: Training and Certification Assignment During Onboarding
User Story: As a supervisor I want required training and certifications to be assigned during onboarding So that compliance is ensured from day one
Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Training assignment
Given onboarding is in progress
When training tasks are assigned
Then the employee receives notifications and deadlines
Additional Details:
- Priority: Medium
- Story Points: 1
- Dependencies: Training, Notifications
- Notes: Block asset assignment until training complete

Title: Asset Assignment for New Employees
User Story: As an admin I want assets to be assigned to new employees during onboarding So that equipment and PPE are tracked from the start
Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Asset assignment
Given onboarding is in progress
When assets are assigned
Then the asset registry is updated and check-out logged
Additional Details:
- Priority: Medium
- Story Points: 1
- Dependencies: Asset Registry, Onboarding
- Notes: Certification validation required

Title: Automated Deprovisioning on Termination
User Story: As an HR manager I want accounts, schedules, and assets to be deprovisioned automatically on termination So that risk is reduced and compliance maintained
Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Deprovisioning workflow
Given an employee is terminated
When offboarding is triggered
Then access is revoked, assets collected, and schedules updated
Additional Details:
- Priority: Medium
- Story Points: 2
- Dependencies: HRIS, Asset Registry, Scheduling
- Notes: Deprovisioning workflow documented
