Title: Leave Balance Management

User Story: As an employee I want to view my leave balances So that I can plan my time off and avoid exceeding available leave

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: View leave balance
  Given I am authenticated
  When I access my leave dashboard
  Then I should see my current leave balances by type
Scenario: Balance update after leave
  Given I have taken approved leave
  When the leave period ends
  Then my leave balance should be updated accordingly

Additional Details:
- Priority: High
- Story Points: 2
- Dependencies: Leave management module
- Notes: Support for multiple leave types and accrual policies
