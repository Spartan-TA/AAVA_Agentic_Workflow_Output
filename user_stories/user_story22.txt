Title: Generate Operational Reports & Analytics

User Story: As an operations manager I want to view reports on attendance, overtime, and safety KPIs So that I can monitor workforce trends and make data-driven decisions

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: View overtime report
Given attendance and shift data are available
When the manager requests an overtime report
Then the system displays aggregated overtime hours by employee and department

Additional Details:
- Priority: Medium
- Story Points: 7
- Dependencies: E04 (Time & Attendance), E05 (Shift & Schedule Management), E06 (Leave & Absence Management), E07 (Training & Certification Tracking), E08 (Safety Incidents & OSHA Reporting), E14 (Audit Trail & Compliance)
- Notes: Support export to CSV and scheduled report delivery.
