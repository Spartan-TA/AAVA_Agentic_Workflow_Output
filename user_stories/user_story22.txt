Title: Auto-Flag Scheduled Shifts for Coverage
User Story: As a SUPERVISOR I want scheduled shifts to be auto-flagged for coverage when an employee's leave is approved So that I can ensure all shifts are staffed and operational needs are met
Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Auto-flag shifts for coverage
Given an employee's leave request is approved
When the system updates the schedule
Then any affected shifts are flagged for coverage and notifications are sent to supervisors
Additional Details:
- Priority: Medium
- Story Points: 5
- Dependencies: E02, E03, E05, E06
- Notes: Coverage flags must integrate with scheduling and notification modules.
