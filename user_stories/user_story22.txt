Title: Safety Incidents & OSHA Reporting - Record Incident

User Story: As a supervisor, I want to record safety incidents and near-misses with details so that safety data is tracked.

Acceptance Criteria:

Scenario: Submit incident report
Given I am authenticated as supervisor
When I POST to /safety/incidents with required fields
Then the incident is recorded with status "Open"

Scenario: Validation of required fields
Given I submit an incomplete incident report
When required fields are missing
Then the request is rejected with validation errors

Additional Details:
- Priority: High
- Story Points: 5
- Dependencies: Employee CRUD, RBAC
- Notes: Fields: severity, location, description, involved employees.
