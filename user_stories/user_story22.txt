Title: Schedule Performance Review
User Story: As an HR manager I want to schedule performance reviews So that employee evaluations are conducted regularly
Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Review scheduling
Given I am logged in as HR manager
When I select an employee and schedule a review
Then the employee and reviewer should be notified of the scheduled date
Additional Details:
- Priority: Medium
- Story Points: 2
- Dependencies: Notifications, Calendar module
- Notes: Allow recurring review schedules.
