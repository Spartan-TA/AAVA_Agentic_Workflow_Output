Title: Auto-Flag Scheduled Shifts for Coverage

User Story: As a scheduling coordinator I want the system to automatically flag scheduled shifts affected by approved leave So that I can quickly identify coverage gaps and reassign staff

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Shift auto-flagging
Given an employee's leave request is approved
When the system detects scheduled shifts during the leave period
Then those shifts are flagged for coverage and notifications sent to the coordinator

Additional Details:
- Priority: Medium
- Story Points: 2
- Dependencies: Leave Approval, Shift & Schedule Management
- Notes: Flagged shifts should be visible in the scheduling dashboard.
