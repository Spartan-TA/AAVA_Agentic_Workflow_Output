Title: Mobile Access (PWA)

User Story: As a warehouse worker I want to access core EMS features on my mobile device So that I can clock in/out, view schedules, request leave, and see announcements from anywhere

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Mobile clock-in/out
Given I have installed the PWA on my device
When I clock in or out
Then my attendance is recorded even if offline, and synced when online

Scenario: Offline queue conflict resolution
Given I have offline events queued
When the device reconnects
Then conflicts are resolved and events are synced

Additional Details:
- Priority: Medium
- Story Points: 8
- Dependencies: Time & Attendance, Shift & Schedule Management, Leave & Absence Management, Notifications & Announcements
- Notes: Responsive views; installable manifest; Lighthouse PWA score; core flows usable on mobile
