Title: View Assigned Shift Schedule

User Story: As an employee I want to view my assigned shift schedule So that I know when I am expected to work

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Viewing schedule
Given I am logged into the system
When I navigate to my schedule page
Then I should see all upcoming assigned shifts

Additional Details:
- Priority: High
- Story Points: 1
- Dependencies: Shift schedule creation
- Notes: Display schedule in calendar and list formats.
