Title: Create Recurring Shift Templates

User Story: As a supervisor I want to create recurring shift templates So that I can efficiently schedule employees for regular warehouse operations

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Creating a recurring shift template
Given I am on the shift management page
When I select 'Create Template' and define recurrence rules
Then the system should save the template and display it in the list of available templates

Additional Details:
- Priority: High
- Story Points: 5
- Dependencies: Employee Master Data, Time & Attendance
- Notes: Recurrence rules should support daily, weekly, and custom intervals.
