Title: Assign Shifts to Employees

User Story:
As a Scheduling Manager, I want to assign shifts (including recurring and rotation-based) to employees so that everyone knows their work schedule in advance.

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario 1: Assign a shift to an individual employee
  Given a shift is available
  When I select an employee and assign the shift
  Then the employee receives a notification and the shift appears on their calendar

Scenario 2: Assign a shift template to a group
  Given a recurring shift template exists
  When I assign it to a group of employees
  Then all group members see the assigned shifts in their schedules

Scenario 3: Prevent assignment conflicts
  Given an employee already has a shift assigned
  When I attempt to assign an overlapping shift
  Then the system displays a conflict warning

Priority: High
Story Points: 5
Dependencies: E02, E03, E04
Notes: Must integrate with notifications and handle exceptions for blackout dates.
