Title: Certification Blocking for Assignments

User Story: As a scheduler I want to prevent shift or asset assignments to employees with expired or missing certifications So that compliance and safety standards are enforced

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Assignment blocking
Given an employee with an expired certification
When I attempt to assign a shift or asset
Then the system should block the assignment and display a warning

Additional Details:
- Priority: High
- Story Points: 4
- Dependencies: Certification CRUD and Expiry Alerts
- Notes: Support override by authorized personnel with justification.
