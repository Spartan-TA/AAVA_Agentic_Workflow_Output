Title: Track Certification Expirations

User Story: As a manager I want to track employee certification expiration dates so that I can ensure compliance and schedule renewals

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Viewing certification expirations
Given I am logged in as a manager
When I view the certification dashboard
Then I see upcoming and expired certifications for my team

Additional Details:
- Priority: High
- Story Points: 3
- Dependencies: Employee master data, notification module
- Notes: System should send reminders before expiration.
