Title: Supervisor Assigns Training
User Story: As a supervisor I want to assign training modules to employees So that they can complete required certifications
Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Assign training
Given I am logged into the system as a supervisor
When I select an employee and assign a training module
Then the employee receives a notification and the assignment is logged
Additional Details:
- Priority: High
- Story Points: 2
- Dependencies: Training module data, notifications
- Notes: Assignment history should be tracked.
