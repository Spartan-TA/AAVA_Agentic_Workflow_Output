### E06: Leave & Absence Management

**User Story 1: Request Leave**
User Story: As an employee I want to request PTO or sick leave So that my absences are tracked
Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Submit leave request
Given I have available leave balance
When I request PTO
Then my supervisor receives an approval task
Additional Details:
- Priority: Medium
- Story Points: 2
- Dependencies: E02, E03, E05
- Notes: Accrual policies enforced.

**User Story 2: Approve or Deny Leave**
User Story: As a supervisor I want to approve or deny leave requests So that staffing is maintained
Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Approve leave
Given a pending leave request
When I approve it
Then the employee's balance updates and the schedule is flagged for coverage
Additional Details:
- Priority: Medium
- Story Points: 2
- Dependencies: E02, E03, E05
- Notes: Denials notify employee.

**User Story 3: Exclude Approved Leave from Scheduling**
User Story: As a scheduler I want approved leaves to auto-flag shifts So that coverage gaps are visible
Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Auto-flag scheduled shifts
Given an approved leave overlaps a shift
When I view the schedule
Then the shift is flagged for coverage
Additional Details:
- Priority: Medium
- Story Points: 2
- Dependencies: E02, E03, E05
- Notes: Export includes approved leaves.
