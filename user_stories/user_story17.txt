Title: Automate Employee Onboarding
User Story: As an HR manager I want to automate the onboarding workflow so that new employees are provisioned with required access and equipment.
Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Onboarding automation
Given a new employee is added
When onboarding is initiated
Then tasks for access, equipment, and training should be assigned automatically
Additional Details:
- Priority: Medium
- Story Points: 4
- Dependencies: E02, E03, E05, E07, E09, E13
- Notes: Checklist configurable by role

- Story Points: 4
