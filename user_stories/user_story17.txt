Title: Notifications & Announcements
User Story: As a supervisor I want to send shift change notifications and announcements to my team So that everyone is informed in real time
Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Send notification
Given a shift change occurs
When I trigger a notification
Then affected users receive in-app and email/SMS alerts

Scenario: Opt-in/out and quiet hours
Given a user configures notification preferences
When an event occurs during quiet hours
Then notifications are delayed or suppressed

Additional Details:
- Priority: Medium
- Story Points: 5
- Dependencies: RBAC, Schedule Management, Leave Management, Certification Tracking
- Notes: Localized templates; delivery status tracked.
