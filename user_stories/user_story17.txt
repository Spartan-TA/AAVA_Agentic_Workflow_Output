Title: Personal Upcoming Shifts View

User Story: As an employee I want to view my upcoming shifts on a dashboard So that I can plan my work schedule in advance

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Upcoming shifts display
Given I am authenticated
When I access my dashboard
Then my scheduled shifts for the next 30 days should be displayed

Additional Details:
- Priority: Medium
- Story Points: 2
- Dependencies: Shift Assignment and Conflict Detection
- Notes: Support mobile and desktop views.
