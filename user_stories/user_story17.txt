### E17: Onboarding & Offboarding Workflow

#### User Story 1
Title: Automated Account Provisioning for New Hires

User Story: As an HR user, I want automated account provisioning for new hires So that onboarding is efficient.

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Account provisioning
Given a new hire from HRIS
When onboarding is triggered
Then accounts are provisioned automatically

Additional Details:
- Priority: Medium
- Story Points: 3
- Dependencies: E02, E03, E05, E07, E09, E13
- Notes: Initial schedule and training tasks generated

#### User Story 4
Title: Automated Deprovisioning on Termination

User Story: As an HR user, I want automated deprovisioning of access and assets on termination So that risk is reduced.

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Deprovisioning workflow
Given a terminated employee
When offboarding is triggered
Then access is revoked, assets collected, and schedules updated

Additional Details:
- Priority: Medium
- Story Points: 3
- Dependencies: E02, E03, E05, E07, E09, E13
- Notes: Offboarding tasks tracked
