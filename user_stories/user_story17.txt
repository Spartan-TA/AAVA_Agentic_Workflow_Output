Title: Generate Operational Attendance Reports

User Story: As an HR manager I want to generate attendance reports so that I can monitor employee presence and compliance

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Attendance report generation
Given attendance data exists for a date range
When I request a report
Then the system generates a CSV or PDF with attendance details

Additional Details:
- Priority: Medium
- Story Points: 2
- Dependencies: Attendance, Shift management, Leave management
- Notes: Reports filter by date, department, shift
