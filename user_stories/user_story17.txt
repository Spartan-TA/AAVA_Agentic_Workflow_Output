Title: Certification Renewal Reminder
User Story: As an employee I want to receive reminders for certification renewal So that I can maintain compliance
Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Automated renewal reminder
Given my certification is expiring within 30 days
When the system checks certification statuses
Then I should receive a notification with renewal instructions
Additional Details:
- Priority: Medium
- Story Points: 1
- Dependencies: Notifications module
- Notes: Reminder frequency configurable by HR.
