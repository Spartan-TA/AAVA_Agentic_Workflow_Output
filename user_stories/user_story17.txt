Title: Onboarding & Offboarding Workflow

User Story: As an HR manager I want automated onboarding and offboarding workflows so that employee lifecycle changes are efficient and risk is reduced.

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Onboarding and offboarding automation
Given new hires or terminations occur
When workflows are triggered
Then tasks are generated for training and asset assignment, offboarding revokes access, collects assets, and updates schedules

Additional Details:
- Priority: Medium
- Story Points: 8
- Dependencies: Employee Master Data, RBAC, Shift & Schedule Management, Training & Certification Tracking, Equipment & Asset Assignment, Integration Layer
- Notes: HRIS integration for new hires
