Title: Employee Clock In

User Story: As an employee I want to clock in at the start of my shift So that my working hours are accurately recorded

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Successful clock in
Given I am logged into the system and my shift is scheduled
When I click the clock in button
Then my start time should be recorded and visible in my attendance log

Additional Details:
- Priority: High
- Story Points: 2
- Dependencies: Shift schedule
- Notes: Prevent clock in outside scheduled hours.
