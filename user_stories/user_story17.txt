Title: Equipment Assignment and Validation

User Story: As an asset manager, I want to assign equipment to employees, track check-in/out, and block use if required certifications are missing so that asset accountability and safety are maintained.

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Certification validation
Given an employee lacks a required certification
When I attempt to assign equipment
Then the assignment is blocked

Scenario: Check-in/out tracking
When equipment is checked out or returned
Then the history log is updated

Additional Details:
- Priority: Medium
- Story Points: 5
- Dependencies: Employee CRUD API, RBAC, Certification Tracking
- Notes: Reports of overdue returns required.
