Title: Bulk Shift Assignment and Conflict Detection
User Story: As a SUPERVISOR I want to assign shifts to multiple workers and detect scheduling conflicts So that I can efficiently manage team schedules and avoid overlaps
Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Bulk assign shifts
Given I am on the shift assignment page
When I select multiple workers and assign shifts
Then any conflicts are detected and prevented, and audit entries are generated
Additional Details:
- Priority: High
- Story Points: 5
- Dependencies: E02, E03, E04, E05
- Notes: Audit trail must log all bulk assignments and conflict resolutions.
