Title: Manage Performance Reviews & Goals

User Story: As a manager I want to create review templates and track employee goals and ratings So that performance management is standardized and progress is measurable

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Complete performance review
Given a review cycle is active
When the manager fills out a review template for an employee
Then the system records ratings and updates goal progress

Additional Details:
- Priority: Medium
- Story Points: 6
- Dependencies: E02 (Employee Master Data CRUD), E03 (RBAC)
- Notes: Support self-assessment and multi-rater reviews.
