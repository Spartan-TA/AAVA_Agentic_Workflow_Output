### E17 - Onboarding & Offboarding Workflow

**Title:** Automate New Hire Provisioning
**User Story:** As an HR user, I want new hires to be provisioned automatically so that onboarding is efficient.
**Acceptance Criteria:**
Scenario: New hire provisioning
Given a new hire appears from HRIS
When onboarding starts
Then accounts, initial schedule, and required training tasks are generated
**Additional Details:** Priority: Medium, Story Points: 3, Dependencies: E02, E03, E05, E07, E09, E13, Notes: Track completion status.

**Title:** Automate Offboarding
**User Story:** As an HR user, I want offboarding to revoke access and collect assets so that terminations are secure.
**Acceptance Criteria:**
Scenario: Offboarding workflow
Given an employee is terminated
When offboarding starts
Then access is revoked, assets are collected, and schedules are updated
**Additional Details:** Priority: Medium, Story Points: 3, Dependencies: E02, E03, E05, E07, E09, E13, Notes: Update all linked systems.
