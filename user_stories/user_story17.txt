Title: Request and Approve Leave

User Story: As an employee I want to request PTO, sick, or unpaid leave So that my absences are tracked and approved

Acceptance Criteria:

Scenario: Employee submits leave request
Given I am an employee
When I submit a leave request
Then the request is recorded and routed to my supervisor

Scenario: Supervisor approves or denies leave
Given a leave request is pending
When my supervisor reviews it
Then they can approve or deny the request

Scenario: Leave balances update
Given a leave is approved
When the approval is recorded
Then my leave balance is updated accordingly

Additional Details:
- Priority: Medium
- Story Points: 8
- Dependencies: Employee CRUD APIs, RBAC, Shift Management
- Notes: Support for different leave types; notify employee of decision
