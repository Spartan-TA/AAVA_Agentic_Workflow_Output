Title: Manager Conducts Performance Review

User Story: As a manager I want to conduct performance reviews for employees So that feedback and goals are documented and tracked

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Conducting performance review
Given I am logged in as a manager and have access to employee profiles
When I initiate a performance review and enter feedback and ratings
Then the review is saved and the employee is notified

Additional Details:
- Priority: High
- Story Points: 4
- Dependencies: Employee Master Data, Notifications
- Notes: Allow attaching supporting documents and setting future goals.
