Title: Payroll Export Integration

User Story: As a payroll administrator I want to export payroll-ready files from attendance and leave data So that payroll runs are accurate and manual errors are reduced

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Generate payroll export
Given approved attendance and leave records exist
When I initiate a payroll export
Then the system generates files matching provider schema and delivers securely

Scenario: Failed delivery retry
Given a payroll export fails to deliver
When the system detects the failure
Then it retries delivery with backoff and logs the attempt

Additional Details:
- Priority: High
- Story Points: 6
- Dependencies: Time & Attendance, Leave & Absence Management
- Notes: SFTP/API delivery; audit log for every export; totals reconcile with reports
