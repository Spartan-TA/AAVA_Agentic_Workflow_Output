Title: Detect and Prevent Shift Conflicts

User Story: As a supervisor I want the system to detect and prevent scheduling conflicts So that I can ensure adequate staffing and avoid double-booking employees

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Scheduling conflict detection
Given I am assigning shifts to employees
When a conflict is detected (overlapping shifts or blackout dates)
Then the system should prevent the assignment and display a clear error message

Additional Details:
- Priority: High
- Story Points: 2
- Dependencies: Shift Templates, Employee Master Data
- Notes: Include blackout dates and warehouse operation calendars in conflict checks.
