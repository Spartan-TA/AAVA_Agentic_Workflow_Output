Title: Onboarding & Offboarding Workflow

User Story: As an HR manager I want to automate onboarding and offboarding workflows so that new employees are provisioned efficiently and departing employees have access and assets revoked promptly.

Acceptance Criteria:

Scenario: New hire onboarding
Given a new hire appears from HRIS
When onboarding tasks are generated
Then accounts, initial schedule, and required training are provisioned automatically

Scenario: Offboarding
Given an employee is terminated
When offboarding is triggered
Then access is revoked, assets are collected, and schedules are updated

Scenario: Task tracking
Given onboarding/offboarding tasks
When I review the workflow
Then all tasks are tracked to completion

Additional Details:
- Priority: Medium
- Story Points: 8
- Dependencies: Employee Master Data (E02), Role-Based Access Control (E03), Shift & Schedule Management (E05), Training & Certification Tracking (E07), Equipment & Asset Assignment (E09), Integration Layer (E13)
- Notes: Reduce cycle time and risk during employee lifecycle changes.
