Title: Employee Leave Request and Approval

User Story: As an employee I want to request paid or unpaid leave and have my supervisor approve or deny it so that my time off is managed transparently and in compliance with policy

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Submit leave request
Given I am an employee with available leave balance
When I submit a PTO or sick leave request
Then my supervisor is notified for approval

Scenario: Supervisor approval
Given a leave request is pending
When my supervisor reviews and approves or denies the request
Then my leave balance is updated and my schedule is flagged for coverage

Additional Details:
- Priority: Medium
- Story Points: 3
- Dependencies: Employee Master Data, RBAC, Shift Management
- Notes: Must support accrual policies and integration with scheduling and payroll.
