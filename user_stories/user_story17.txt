Title: Create Performance Review Templates

User Story: As an HR manager I want to create performance review templates so that reviews are standardized across the organization

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Creating a review template
Given I am on the performance review admin page
When I define a new template with goals and competencies
Then the template is saved and available for assignment

Additional Details:
- Priority: Medium
- Story Points: 2
- Dependencies: Employee Master Data, RBAC
- Notes: Support quarterly and annual cycles

Title: Assign Reviews to Employees

User Story: As a supervisor I want to assign performance reviews to employees so that structured feedback is collected

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Assigning a review
Given a review cycle is active
When I assign a review to an employee
Then the employee is notified and can complete their self-assessment

Additional Details:
- Priority: Medium
- Story Points: 2
- Dependencies: Review templates, Notifications
- Notes: Role-based assignment

Title: Submit and Acknowledge Reviews

User Story: As an employee I want to submit my review and acknowledge my supervisor's feedback so that the review process is complete

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Submitting and acknowledging a review
Given a review is assigned
When I submit my self-assessment and my supervisor completes their review
Then I can acknowledge the feedback and the review is finalized

Additional Details:
- Priority: Medium
- Story Points: 2
- Dependencies: Review assignment
- Notes: Immutable history after sign-off
