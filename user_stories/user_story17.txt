Title: Equipment Assignment & Tracking
User Story: As a supervisor, I want to assign and track equipment to employees so that accountability is maintained.
Acceptance Criteria:
  Given an asset registry,
  When I check out equipment,
  Then it is assigned to the employee.

  Given a certification is missing,
  When I assign equipment,
  Then the system blocks the assignment.

  Given equipment is overdue,
  When I view reports,
  Then overdue items are listed.

Additional Details:
  Priority: Medium
  Story Points: 5
  Dependencies: Employee CRUD, RBAC, Certification
  Notes: History log per asset.
