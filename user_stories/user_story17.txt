Title: Onboarding & Offboarding Workflow

User Story: As an HR manager I want to automate onboarding and offboarding workflows so that employee lifecycle changes are handled efficiently and securely.

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Onboarding new hire
  Given a new hire appears from HRIS
  When onboarding tasks are generated
  Then accounts, initial schedule, and required training are provisioned

Scenario: Asset assignment
  Given onboarding is in progress
  When assets are required
  Then asset assignment tasks are created and tracked

Scenario: Offboarding termination
  Given an employee is terminated
  When offboarding is triggered
  Then access is revoked, assets are collected, and schedules are updated

Additional Details:
- Priority: Medium
- Story Points: 8
- Dependencies: Employee Master Data (E02), RBAC (E03), Shift & Schedule Management (E05), Training & Certification Tracking (E07), Equipment & Asset Assignment (E09), Integration Layer (E13)
- Notes: Reduce cycle time and risk; automate provisioning and deprovisioning.
