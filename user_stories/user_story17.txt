Title: Integrate Leave with Scheduling

User Story: As a scheduler I want approved leave to automatically block out employee availability So that scheduling conflicts are prevented

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Leave blocks scheduling
  Given an employee's leave is approved
  When I assign shifts
  Then the system should prevent assignments during the leave period

Additional Details:
- Priority: High
- Story Points: 3
- Dependencies: Leave approval workflow, scheduling module
- Notes: Show leave blocks visually on the scheduling calendar.
