Title: Onboarding & Offboarding Workflow
User Story: As an HR manager I want automated onboarding and offboarding workflows so that employee lifecycle changes are efficient and risk is reduced.
Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Automated onboarding and offboarding
Given a new hire appears from HRIS
When onboarding tasks are generated
Then initial schedule and required training are assigned
And asset provisioning is triggered
And offboarding revokes access, collects assets, and updates schedules
Additional Details:
- Priority: Medium
- Story Points: 8
- Dependencies: Employee Master Data (CRUD), Role-Based Access Control (RBAC), Shift & Schedule Management, Training & Certification Tracking, Equipment & Asset Assignment, Integration Layer (HRIS/WMS APIs)
- Notes: Deprovisioning must update schedules and asset registry; onboarding tasks must be tracked.
