Title: Onboarding & Offboarding Workflow

User Story: As an HR manager I want to automate onboarding and offboarding workflows so that employee lifecycle changes are efficient and risk is minimized.

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Onboarding and offboarding automation
Given a new hire appears from HRIS
When onboarding tasks are generated
Then accounts, initial schedule, and required training are provisioned
And asset assignment tasks are created
And on termination, access is revoked, assets are collected, and schedules are updated

Additional Details:
- Priority: Medium
- Story Points: 8
- Dependencies: Employee Master Data (CRUD), Role-Based Access Control (RBAC), Shift & Schedule Management, Training & Certification Tracking, Equipment & Asset Assignment, Integration Layer (HRIS/WMS APIs)
- Notes: Reduce cycle time and risk during employee lifecycle changes.
