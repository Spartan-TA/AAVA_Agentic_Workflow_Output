### E17 â Onboarding & Offboarding Workflow

**User Story 1: Automate New Hire Provisioning**
- **User Story:** As an HR user, I want new hires from HRIS to be provisioned automatically so that onboarding is fast.
- **Acceptance Criteria:**
  - Given a new hire in HRIS,
  - When sync runs,
  - Then an account and initial schedule are created.
- **Additional Details:** Priority: Medium; Story Points: 2; Dependencies: E02, E03, E05, E07, E09, E13; Notes: Notify supervisor.

**User Story 2: Assign Required Training**
- **User Story:** As a supervisor, I want onboarding tasks for required training so that compliance is ensured.
- **Acceptance Criteria:**
  - Given a new hire,
  - When onboarding starts,
  - Then training tasks are generated and tracked.
- **Additional Details:** Priority: Medium; Story Points: 2; Dependencies: E07, E17-1; Notes: Track completion.

**User Story 3: Assign Initial Assets**
- **User Story:** As an asset manager, I want to assign assets during onboarding so that new hires have required equipment.
- **Acceptance Criteria:**
  - Given a new hire,
  - When onboarding,
  - Then assets are assigned and logged.
- **Additional Details:** Priority: Medium; Story Points: 2; Dependencies: E09, E17-1; Notes: Track asset status.

**User Story 4: Offboarding Workflow**
- **User Story:** As an HR user, I want offboarding to revoke access and collect assets so that terminations are secure.
- **Acceptance Criteria:**
  - Given a termination,
  - When offboarding runs,
  - Then access is revoked, assets collected, and schedules updated.
- **Additional Details:** Priority: Medium; Story Points: 2; Dependencies: E02, E09, E13; Notes: Notify IT and asset manager.
