Title: Onboarding & Offboarding Workflow

User Story: As an HR manager I want automated workflows for provisioning accounts, initial schedules, required training, and deprovisioning access/assets on termination So that onboarding and offboarding are efficient and secure.

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Automated onboarding and offboarding
Given a new hire or termination event occurs
When the workflow is triggered
Then accounts, schedules, and training are provisioned for new hires, and access/assets are deprovisioned for terminations.

Additional Details:
- Priority: Medium
- Story Points: 8
- Dependencies: E02, E03, E05, E07, E09, E13
- Notes: Include notifications to relevant stakeholders and audit logging of all actions.
