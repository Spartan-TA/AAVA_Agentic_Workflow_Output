### E16: Mobile Access (PWA)

**User Story 16.1**
Title: Mobile Clock-In/Out

User Story: As a Worker I want to clock in and out from my mobile device So that my attendance is recorded conveniently.

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Mobile clock event
Given I am on mobile
When I clock in/out
Then the event is queued if offline and synced when online

Additional Details:
- Priority: Medium
- Story Points: 5
- Dependencies: E04
- Notes: Offline queue with conflict resolution

---

**User Story 16.2**
Title: Mobile Schedule and Leave Access

User Story: As a Worker I want to view my schedule and request leave from my mobile device So that I can manage my work-life balance.

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Mobile access
Given I am on mobile
When I access schedules or leave
Then the views are responsive and fully functional

Additional Details:
- Priority: Medium
- Story Points: 3
- Dependencies: E05, E06
- Notes: PWA manifest required

---

**User Story 16.3**
Title: Mobile Announcements and Notifications

User Story: As a Worker I want to receive and view announcements and notifications on my mobile device So that I stay informed.

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Mobile notifications
Given announcements exist
When I log in on mobile
Then I see all relevant notifications

Additional Details:
- Priority: Medium
- Story Points: 2
- Dependencies: E12
- Notes: Push notifications supported

---

**User Story 16.4**
Title: PWA Performance and Installability

User Story: As a Product Owner I want the mobile app to meet PWA standards So that users have a reliable experience.

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: PWA audit
Given the app is audited
When tested with Lighthouse
Then the PWA score is at least 80

Additional Details:
- Priority: Medium
- Story Points: 2
- Dependencies: E04, E05, E06, E12
- Notes: Must be installable on Android/iOS
