**E17: Onboarding & Offboarding Workflow**

1. Title: Automate New Hire Provisioning
User Story: As an HR user, I want new hires from HRIS to trigger account provisioning, initial schedule, and required training so that onboarding is efficient.
Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: New Hire Workflow
Given a new hire in HRIS
When the sync job runs
Then account, schedule, and training tasks are created
Additional Details:
- Priority: Medium
- Story Points: 3
- Dependencies: E02, E03, E05, E07, E09, E13
- Notes: Notify stakeholders.

2. Title: Automate Offboarding
User Story: As an HR user, I want offboarding to revoke access, collect assets, and update schedules so that terminated employees are properly deprovisioned.
Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Offboarding Workflow
Given a termination event
When offboarding is triggered
Then access is revoked, assets collected, and schedules updated
Additional Details:
- Priority: Medium
- Story Points: 3
- Dependencies: Story 1
- Notes: Audit all actions.

3. Title: Track Onboarding/Offboarding Tasks
User Story: As a supervisor, I want to track onboarding and offboarding tasks so that nothing is missed during transitions.
Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Task Tracking
Given onboarding/offboarding events
When I view tasks
Then all required actions are listed and status is tracked
Additional Details:
- Priority: Medium
- Story Points: 2
- Dependencies: Story 1
- Notes: Support reminders.
