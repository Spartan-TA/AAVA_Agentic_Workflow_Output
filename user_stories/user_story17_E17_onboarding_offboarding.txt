Epic E17: Onboarding & Offboarding Workflow

---

User Story 1: Automated Account Provisioning
As HR, I want new hire accounts provisioned automatically so that onboarding is efficient.

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: HRIS sync
  Given new hire data from HRIS
  When sync job runs
  Then account is created

Additional Details:
- Priority: Medium
- Story Points: 3
- Dependencies: E02, E03, E05, E07, E09, E13
- Notes: Initial schedule assigned

---

User Story 2: Training and Asset Assignment Tasks
As a supervisor, I want onboarding tasks generated for training and asset assignment so that new hires are prepared.

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Task generation
  Given a new hire
  When onboarding starts
  Then training and asset tasks are created

Additional Details:
- Priority: Medium
- Story Points: 3
- Dependencies: E02, E03, E05, E07, E09, E13
- Notes: Tasks tracked to completion

---

User Story 3: Offboarding Workflow
As HR, I want access deprovisioned and assets collected on termination so that risk is minimized.

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Offboarding
  Given a terminated employee
  When offboarding starts
  Then access is revoked, assets collected, schedules updated

Additional Details:
- Priority: Medium
- Story Points: 3
- Dependencies: E02, E03, E05, E07, E09, E13
- Notes: Offboarding checklist
