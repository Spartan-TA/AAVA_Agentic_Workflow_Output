### Epic E17: Onboarding & Offboarding Workflow

#### 1. Automate Provisioning of Accounts for New Hires
User Story: As an HR user I want accounts provisioned automatically for new hires So that onboarding is fast.
Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Account provisioning
Given a new hire in HRIS
When the sync job runs
Then an account is created in the system
Additional Details:
- Priority: Medium
- Story Points: 3
- Dependencies: E02, E03, E13
- Notes: Email notification sent.

#### 2. Assign Initial Schedule and Required Training
User Story: As a supervisor I want initial schedule and required training tasks assigned to new hires So that onboarding tasks are clear.
Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Onboarding task assignment
Given a new hire
When onboarding starts
Then schedule and training tasks are generated
Additional Details:
- Priority: Medium
- Story Points: 2
- Dependencies: Account provisioning
- Notes: Due dates tracked.

#### 3. Automate Deprovisioning of Access and Assets on Termination
User Story: As an HR user I want access and asset deprovisioning automated on termination So that offboarding is secure.
Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Offboarding automation
Given a termination in HRIS
When the sync job runs
Then access is revoked and asset collection tasks are created
Additional Details:
- Priority: Medium
- Story Points: 3
- Dependencies: Account provisioning, E09
- Notes: Audit log of actions.

#### 4. Update Schedules and Training Status on Lifecycle Changes
User Story: As a supervisor I want schedules and training status updated on onboarding/offboarding So that records are current.
Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Lifecycle update
Given onboarding/offboarding event
When it occurs
Then schedules and training status are updated
Additional Details:
- Priority: Medium
- Story Points: 2
- Dependencies: Onboarding/offboarding automation
- Notes: Notifications sent.
