## User Story 1: Attendance and Overtime Reports
**User Story:**  
As a manager, I want reports on attendance and overtime so that I can monitor workforce utilization.

**Acceptance Criteria (Gherkin):**  
Given attendance data  
When I generate a report  
Then totals and trends are shown

**Additional Details:**  
- Priority: Medium  
- Story Points: 2  
- Dependencies: E04, E05, E14  
- Notes: Filter by date, department

## User Story 5: Export Reports as CSV/PDF
**User Story:**  
As a user, I want to export any report as CSV or PDF.

**Acceptance Criteria (Gherkin):**  
Given a report  
When I export  
Then the file is generated within 10 seconds for up to 50k rows

**Additional Details:**  
- Priority: Medium  
- Story Points: 1  
- Dependencies: E15-1  
- Notes: Export includes applied filters
