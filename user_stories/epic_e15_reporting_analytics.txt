EPIC E15: REPORTING & ANALYTICS

===========================================

Title: Attendance & Overtime Reports

User Story: As a supervisor I want to generate attendance and overtime reports So that I can monitor staffing and compliance

Acceptance Criteria:

Scenario: Report generation
Given I select date and department filters
When I request a report
Then attendance and overtime data is exported in CSV/PDF within 10 seconds for up to 50k rows

Additional Details:
- Priority: Medium
- Story Points: 3
- Dependencies: Attendance, scheduling modules
- Notes: Access controlled by role

---

Title: Certification Status Dashboard

User Story: As a manager I want a dashboard showing certification status across my team So that I can ensure compliance and schedule renewals

Acceptance Criteria:

Scenario: Dashboard view
Given I access the dashboard
When certification data is loaded
Then I see status, expiry dates, and alerts for my team

Additional Details:
- Priority: Medium
- Story Points: 2
- Dependencies: Certification tracking
- Notes: Metrics endpoint available for BI
