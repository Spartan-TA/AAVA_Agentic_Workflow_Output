Title: Shift & Schedule Management - Create Shift Template
User Story: As an HR manager I want to create recurring shift templates So that scheduling is efficient and consistent

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Create shift template
Given valid shift details
When I POST to /shifts/templates
Then a new template is created

Additional Details:
- Priority: High
- Story Points: 2
- Dependencies: Employee CRUD, RBAC, Attendance
- Notes: Templates support rotation and overtime rules
