EPIC 05: SHIFT & SCHEDULE MANAGEMENT

===========================================

Title: Create Shift Templates
User Story: As a SUPERVISOR I want to create recurring shift templates So that scheduling is efficient and consistent
Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Shift template creation
Given I am authenticated as supervisor
When I create a new shift template
Then the template is saved and available for assignment

Additional Details:
- Priority: High
- Story Points: 3
- Dependencies: Employee CRUD API, Role-Based Access Control, Clock In/Out
- Notes: Templates support rotations and overtime rules

---

Title: Assign Shifts to Employees
User Story: As a SUPERVISOR I want to assign shifts to employees in bulk So that staffing is managed effectively
Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Bulk shift assignment
Given shift templates exist
When I select employees and assign shifts
Then assignments are created and audit entries generated

Additional Details:
- Priority: High
- Story Points: 3
- Dependencies: Shift Templates
- Notes: Conflicts detected and prevented

---

Title: Detect and Prevent Scheduling Conflicts
User Story: As a SUPERVISOR I want the system to detect and prevent scheduling conflicts So that employees are not double-booked
Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Conflict detection
Given an employee has an existing shift
When I try to assign an overlapping shift
Then the system blocks the assignment and shows an error

Additional Details:
- Priority: High
- Story Points: 2
- Dependencies: Assign Shifts to Employees
- Notes: Blackout dates and operation calendars considered

---

Title: View Personal Upcoming Shifts
User Story: As a WORKER I want to view my upcoming shifts So that I can plan my schedule
Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: View shifts
Given I am logged in as a worker
When I access my schedule
Then my upcoming shifts are displayed

Additional Details:
- Priority: Medium
- Story Points: 2
- Dependencies: Assign Shifts to Employees
- Notes: Mobile access supported
