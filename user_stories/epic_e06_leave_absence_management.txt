EPIC E06: LEAVE & ABSENCE MANAGEMENT

===========================================

Title: Leave & Absence Management - Request Leave
User Story: As a worker, I want to request PTO, sick, or unpaid leave so that my absences are tracked.
Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Leave Request
Given leave balances
When leave is requested
Then balances update and request is logged.
Additional Details:
- Priority: Medium
- Story Points: 2
- Dependencies: E02, E03, E05
- Notes: Leave types.

===========================================

Title: Leave & Absence Management - Approve/Deny Leave
User Story: As a supervisor, I want to approve or deny leave requests so that staffing is managed.
Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Leave Approval
Given a leave request
When supervisor acts
Then request is approved/denied and balances update.
Additional Details:
- Priority: Medium
- Story Points: 2
- Dependencies: E02, E03, E05
- Notes: Approval workflow.

===========================================

Additional Details:
- Priority: Medium
- Story Points: 3
- Dependencies: E02, E03, E05
