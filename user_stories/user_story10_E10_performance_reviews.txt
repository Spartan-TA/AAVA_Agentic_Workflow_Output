Epic E10: Performance Reviews & Goals

---

User Story 1: Create Review Templates
As HR, I want to create quarterly and annual review templates so that performance reviews are standardized.

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Template creation
  Given valid review criteria
  When I create a template
  Then it is saved and reusable

Additional Details:
- Priority: Medium
- Story Points: 3
- Dependencies: E02, E03
- Notes: Templates support competencies and goals

---

User Story 2: Assign Reviews to Employees
As a supervisor, I want to assign reviews to employees so that feedback cycles are initiated.

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Review assignment
  Given a review cycle
  When I assign to employees
  Then reviews are created

Additional Details:
- Priority: Medium
- Story Points: 2
- Dependencies: E02, E03
- Notes: Assignment workflow logged

---

User Story 3: Submit and Acknowledge Reviews
As an employee, I want to submit my self-review and acknowledge supervisor feedback so that the process is collaborative.

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Self-review submission
  Given a review assigned
  When I submit my self-review
  Then it is saved

Scenario: Supervisor feedback
  Given a completed self-review
  When supervisor submits feedback
  Then employee can acknowledge

Additional Details:
- Priority: Medium
- Story Points: 3
- Dependencies: E02, E03
- Notes: Immutable after sign-off

---

User Story 4: PDF Export of Reviews
As HR, I want to export reviews as PDF so that records are archived.

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: PDF export
  Given completed reviews
  When I request export
  Then PDF files are generated

Additional Details:
- Priority: Medium
- Story Points: 2
- Dependencies: E02, E03

User Story 5: Role-Based Visibility
As a supervisor, I want visibility of reviews only for my team so that privacy is maintained.

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Visibility check
  Given a supervisor user
  When I access reviews
  Then only team reviews are visible

Additional Details:
- Priority: Medium
- Story Points: 2
- Dependencies: E02, E03
- Notes: Access rules enforced
