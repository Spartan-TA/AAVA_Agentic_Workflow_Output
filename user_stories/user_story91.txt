Title: Localized Date/Time Formats and Policy Scoping by Warehouse

User Story: As an employee I want date/time formats and policies to be localized and scoped to my assigned warehouse So that I see accurate schedules and rules for my location

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Localization and policy scoping
Given I am assigned to a warehouse
When I view schedules and policies
Then date/time formats and rules reflect my warehouse's configuration and locale

Additional Details:
- Priority: Low
- Story Points: 4
- Dependencies: E01, E02, E05
- Notes: Support US and international formats; policies editable by warehouse admin.
