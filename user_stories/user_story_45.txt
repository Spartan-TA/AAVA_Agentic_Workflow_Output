Title: Localized Notification Templates and Delivery Status

User Story: As a system administrator I want notification templates to be localized and delivery status tracked so that users receive messages in their preferred language and delivery issues are visible

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Send localized notification and track status
  Given a notification is triggered
  When the template is sent in the user's preferred language
  Then the delivery status is logged and visible to admins

Additional Details:
- Priority: Medium
- Story Points: 2
- Dependencies: E03 (Scheduling), E05 (Attendance), E06 (Leave), E07 (Certifications)
- Notes: Delivery failures must be retried
