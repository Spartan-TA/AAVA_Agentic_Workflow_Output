Title: Offline Queue and Conflict Resolution for Clock Events

User Story: As a worker I want my clock-in/out events to be queued offline and synced with conflict resolution so that my attendance is accurately recorded even without network

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Offline clock event queue and sync
  Given I am offline and clock in or out
  When I regain connectivity
  Then my events are synced to the server and any conflicts are resolved according to business rules

Additional Details:
- Priority: Medium
- Story Points: 3
- Dependencies: E04, E05, E06, E12
- Notes: User notified of sync status and any conflicts; must not lose events.
