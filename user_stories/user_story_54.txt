Title: View Notification History

User Story: As an employee I want to view my notification history So that I can reference past updates and ensure I did not miss important information

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Notification history viewing
Given notifications are sent to me
When I access my notification dashboard
Then I should see a list of all past notifications with details

Additional Details:
- Priority: Medium
- Story Points: 2
- Dependencies: Notifications module
- Notes: Support for filtering by date and type
