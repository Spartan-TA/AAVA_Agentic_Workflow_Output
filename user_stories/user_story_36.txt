Title: Immutable Review History After Sign-Off
User Story: As an HR manager I want performance reviews to become immutable after employee and supervisor sign-off so that the review history is preserved for compliance
Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Lock review after sign-off
Given a review is signed off by both supervisor and employee
When I attempt to edit the review
Then the system blocks changes and logs any access
Scenario: Export immutable review history
Given reviews are locked
When I export review history
Then all records are complete and uneditable
Additional Details:
- Priority: Medium
- Story Points: 1
- Dependencies: E02, E03
- Notes: History must be auditable
