Title: Report Safety Incidents

User Story: As an employee I want to report safety incidents So that hazards are addressed and compliance is maintained

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Safety incident reporting
Given I witness a safety incident
When I submit a report
Then the safety officer should be notified and the incident logged for investigation

Additional Details:
- Priority: High
- Story Points: 3
- Dependencies: Notifications, incident management
- Notes: Support for anonymous reporting
