Title: Asset and Employee History Logs & Overdue Reports

User Story: As an asset manager I want to view the history of asset assignments and generate reports of overdue returns so that accountability and asset utilization are transparent

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: View asset and employee history
  Given assets are checked in and out by employees
  When I view an asset or employee profile
  Then I see a log of all assignments and returns, and can export overdue return reports

Additional Details:
- Priority: Medium
- Story Points: 2
- Dependencies: E02 (User Management), E03 (Scheduling), E07 (Certifications)
- Notes: Overdue reports must be exportable as CSV
