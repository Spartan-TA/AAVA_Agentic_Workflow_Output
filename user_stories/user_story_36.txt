Title: Manager Review and Feedback Submission

User Story: As a manager I want to provide feedback and ratings for my direct reports during the performance review so that I can support their professional development

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Manager submits review
Given my direct report has completed their self-assessment
When I access their review form and enter my feedback and ratings
Then my responses should be saved and visible to HR and the employee after finalization

Additional Details:
- Priority: High
- Story Points: 2
- Dependencies: Review cycle management, Employee records
- Notes: Allow managers to save drafts and submit final reviews
