Title: Immutable Review History

User Story: As an HR administrator I want completed performance reviews to be immutable So that historical records are preserved for compliance and audits

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Lock completed review
Given a performance review is marked complete
When I view the review history
Then the review should be read-only and show a timestamp of completion

Additional Details:
- Priority: Medium
- Story Points: 2
- Dependencies: Performance management, audit logging
- Notes: Support digital signatures for completed reviews.
