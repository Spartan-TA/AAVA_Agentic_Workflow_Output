Title: Create Shift Templates

User Story: As a scheduler I want to create reusable shift templates So that I can quickly assign common shift patterns to employees

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Shift template creation
Given I am in the scheduling module
When I define a new shift template with start/end times and roles
Then the template is saved and available for future assignments

Additional Details:
- Priority: Medium
- Story Points: 2
- Dependencies: Scheduling module, UI forms
- Notes: Support overnight and multi-day shifts.
