Title: Record Safety Incidents
User Story: As a supervisor I want to record safety incidents with severity and investigation workflow So that OSHA compliance is ensured
Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Log incident
  Given an incident occurs
  When I submit a report
  Then the incident is logged with severity and details
Scenario: Investigation workflow
  Given an incident is logged
  When I assign an investigator
  Then the workflow should track status and resolution
Additional Details:
- Priority: High
- Story Points: 5
- Dependencies: Employee CRUD, RBAC
- Notes: Generate OSHA reports
