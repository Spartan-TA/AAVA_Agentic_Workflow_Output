EPIC E05: Shift & Schedule Management

---

Title: Create Shift Templates
User Story: As an HR manager, I want to create recurring shift templates so that I can standardize scheduling across the warehouse.

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Create shift template
  Given I am authenticated as HR
  When I create a new shift template with start/end times and recurrence
  Then the template is saved and available for assignment

Additional Details:
- Priority: High
- Story Points: 5
- Dependencies: E02, E03, E04
- Notes: Templates support daily/weekly recurrence

---

Title: Assign Shifts to Employees
User Story: As a supervisor, I want to assign shifts to employees so that staffing needs are met.

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Assign shift to employee
  Given I am a supervisor
  When I select employees and assign them to a shift
  Then the system validates for conflicts and saves the assignment

Additional Details:
- Priority: High
- Story Points: 5
- Dependencies: E02, E03, E04
- Notes: Bulk assignment supported

---

Title: Detect Scheduling Conflicts
User Story: As a supervisor, I want the system to detect scheduling conflicts so that I avoid double-booking employees.

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Prevent overlapping shifts
  Given an employee has an existing shift
  When I attempt to assign an overlapping shift
  Then the system prevents the assignment and shows an error

Additional Details:
- Priority: High
- Story Points: 3
- Dependencies: E02, E03, E04
- Notes: Includes blackout dates

---

Title: View Personal Upcoming Shifts
User Story: As a worker, I want to view my upcoming shifts so that I can plan my schedule.

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: View my shifts
  Given I am logged in
  When I navigate to my schedule
  Then I see a list of my upcoming shifts

Additional Details:
- Priority: High
- Story Points: 2
- Dependencies: E02, E03, E04
- Notes: Mobile-friendly

---

Title: Audit Shift Assignments
User Story: As an auditor, I want all shift assignments to be logged so that compliance is maintained.

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: View audit log
  Given a shift assignment is made
  When I view the audit log
  Then I see the actor, timestamp, and before/after state

Additional Details:
- Priority: High
- Story Points: 2
- Dependencies: E14
- Notes: Tamper-evident storage
