Title: Enforce Unique BadgeId and Soft-Delete for Employees

User Story: As a System Administrator I want to enforce unique badgeId assignment and support soft-delete for employee records so that data integrity is maintained and inactive employees are not permanently removed.

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Unique BadgeId Enforcement
  Given an employee record is being created
  When I assign a badgeId
  Then the system validates that the badgeId is unique and rejects duplicates

Scenario: Soft-Delete Employee
  Given an employee record exists
  When I perform a delete operation
  Then the record is marked as inactive and excluded from active lists, but retained in the database

Additional Details:
- Priority: High
- Story Points: 3
- Dependencies: Employee CRUD APIs (E02)
- Notes: Soft-deleted records should be retrievable for audit and reporting purposes.
