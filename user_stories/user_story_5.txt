Title: Implement Shift & Schedule Management

User Story: As a Supervisor I want to create and assign recurring shift templates and schedules so that warehouse staffing is optimized and scheduling conflicts are minimized.

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Create shift template
  Given I have access to scheduling
  When I create a new shift template
  Then the template is saved and available for assignment
Scenario: Assign shifts to employees
  Given shift templates exist
  When I assign shifts to employees
  Then assignments are reflected in their personal schedules
Scenario: Detect scheduling conflicts
  Given employees have assigned shifts
  When I attempt to assign overlapping shifts
  Then the system prevents the conflict and notifies me
Scenario: Bulk-assign shifts
  Given multiple employees
  When I use bulk-assign
  Then all selected employees receive the assigned shifts
Scenario: Audit entry generation
  Given a shift assignment is made
  When I review the audit log
  Then the assignment action is recorded

Additional Details:
- Priority: High
- Story Points: 8
- Dependencies: Employee CRUD (E02), RBAC (E03), Attendance (E04)
- Notes: Support blackout dates and warehouse operation calendars.
