Title: Record Safety Incident Workflow

User Story: As a supervisor I want to record safety incidents and near-misses with severity, location, and involved employees so that the organization can track and investigate workplace safety issues

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Submit safety incident
Given I am logged in as supervisor
When I submit a safety incident form with required details
Then the incident is logged and assigned a status of Open

Additional Details:
- Priority: High
- Story Points: 2
- Dependencies: Employee Master Data, RBAC
- Notes: Required fields validated; incident ID generated automatically.
