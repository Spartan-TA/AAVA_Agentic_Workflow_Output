Title: Record Safety Incident

User Story: As a supervisor I want to record safety incidents and near-misses with details So that we can investigate, take corrective actions, and comply with OSHA reporting requirements

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Record safety incident
Given I am logged in as a supervisor
When I submit a new safety incident report with severity, location, description, and involved employees
Then the incident should be logged and trigger the investigation workflow

Additional Details:
- Priority: High
- Story Points: 3
- Dependencies: E02 (Employee Profiles), E03 (Scheduling)
- Notes: Incident records should be tamper-evident and support attachment of photos/documents.
