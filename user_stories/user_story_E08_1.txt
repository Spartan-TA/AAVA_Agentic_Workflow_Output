Title: Record Safety Incidents and Near-Misses

User Story: As a safety officer I want to record safety incidents and near-misses So that workplace hazards are tracked and mitigated

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Logging an incident
Given I am on the safety reporting page
When I submit an incident report
Then it should be stored with details, location, and involved parties

Additional Details:
- Priority: High
- Story Points: 3
- Dependencies: Incident database
- Notes: Support attachments and categorization.
