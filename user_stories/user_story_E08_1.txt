Title: Record Safety Incidents

User Story: As a Safety Officer I want to record safety incidents and near-misses with severity, location, and involved employees so that we can track and investigate workplace hazards

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Record new incident
Given I am logged in as Safety Officer
When I submit a new incident with all required fields
Then the incident is saved and visible in the safety dashboard

Scenario: Validation of required fields
Given I attempt to submit an incident without severity or location
When I submit the form
Then the system displays validation errors and does not save the incident

Scenario: Link involved employees
Given an incident involves multiple employees
When I add them to the incident record
Then each employee is linked and notified

Additional Details:
- Priority: High
- Story Points: 3
- Dependencies: Employee Master Data (E02), RBAC (E03)
- Notes: Severity levels and locations should be configurable
