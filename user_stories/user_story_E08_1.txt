Title: Record Safety Incident
User Story: As a supervisor I want to record safety incidents and near-misses So that we can investigate and improve warehouse safety
Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Incident recording
Given an incident occurs in the warehouse
When I submit the incident details (severity, location, involved employees)
Then the system should store the record and initiate the investigation workflow
Additional Details:
- Priority: High
- Story Points: 3
- Dependencies: Employee module, incident workflow
- Notes: All records must be validated and exportable for OSHA reporting.
