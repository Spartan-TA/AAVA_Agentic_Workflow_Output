### E07 - Training & Certification Tracking

**User Story 1: Certification CRUD**
- Title: Manage Employee Certifications
- Narrative: As an HR user, I want to create, update, and delete certifications for employees so that compliance is tracked.
- Acceptance Criteria:
  ```
  Given an employee,
  When I perform CRUD on certifications,
  Then the changes are reflected in their profile.
  ```
- Priority: High
- Story Points: 3
- Dependencies: E02, E03
- Notes: Safety compliance.

**User Story 2: Expiry Alerts for Certifications**
- Title: Certification Expiry Alerts
- Narrative: As a supervisor, I want alerts for certifications expiring in 30/7 days so that renewals are timely.
- Acceptance Criteria:
  ```
  Given a certification nearing expiry,
  When within 30 or 7 days,
  Then an alert is sent to the employee and supervisor.
  ```
- Priority: High
- Story Points: 2
- Dependencies: Story 1
- Notes: Proactive compliance.

**User Story 3: Block Unqualified Assignments**
- Title: Block Assignments for Expired Certifications
- Narrative: As a scheduler, I want to prevent assignment of tasks to employees with expired certifications so that safety is ensured.
- Acceptance Criteria:
  ```
  Given an expired certification,
  When assigning a task requiring it,
  Then the system blocks the assignment and notifies the user.
  ```
- Priority: High
- Story Points: 2
- Dependencies: Story 1
- Notes: Safety enforcement.

**User Story 4: Upload Proof Documents**
- Title: Upload Certification Proof Documents
- Narrative: As an employee, I want to upload proof documents for certifications so that records are complete.
- Acceptance Criteria:
  ```
  Given a certification,
  When I upload a document,
  Then it is stored and linked to my profile.
  ```
- Priority: High
- Story Points: 1
- Dependencies: Story 1
- Notes: Documentation.

**User Story 5: Certification Status Visibility**
- Title: View Certification Status on Profile
- Narrative: As an employee, I want to view my certification status on my profile so that I know my compliance.
- Acceptance Criteria:
  ```
  Given my profile,
  When I view certifications,
  Then I see status, expiry, and proof documents.
  ```
- Priority: High
- Story Points: 1
- Dependencies: Story 1
- Notes: Transparency.
