### E07: Training & Certification Tracking

**User Story 1: Certification CRUD and Alerts**
- User Story: As an HR user, I want to track employee certifications and receive alerts before expiration So that compliance is maintained.
- Acceptance Criteria:
  - Scenario: Add certification
    - Given an employee and certification details
    - When I add a certification
    - Then it appears on the employee profile
  - Scenario: Expiry alert
    - Given a certification expiring in 30 or 7 days
    - When the date approaches
    - Then an alert is sent to the employee and supervisor
- Priority: High
- Story Points: 5
- Dependencies: E02, E03
- Notes: Proof documents uploadable.

**User Story 2: Block Unqualified Assignments**
- User Story: As a scheduler, I want to prevent assignment of tasks to employees with expired or missing certifications So that only qualified staff are assigned.
- Acceptance Criteria:
  - Scenario: Assignment check
    - Given an expired certification
    - When I assign a restricted task
    - Then the system blocks the assignment and shows an error
- Priority: High
- Story Points: 3
- Dependencies: E02, E03
- Notes: Certification status visible on profile.
