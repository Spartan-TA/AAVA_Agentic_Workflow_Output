### E07 - Training & Certification Tracking

**User Story 1**
Title: Manage Employee Certifications
User Story: As an HR user, I want to CRUD certifications for employees so that compliance is tracked.
Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Certification CRUD
Given an employee
When I add or update a certification
Then the certification is visible on their profile
Priority: High
Story Points: 2
Dependencies: E02, E03
Notes: Include expiration date and document upload.

**User Story 2**
Title: Alert on Expiring Certifications
User Story: As a supervisor, I want alerts 30 and 7 days before a certification expires so that renewals are not missed.
Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Expiry alert
Given a certification expiring in 30 or 7 days
When the date is reached
Then an alert is sent to the employee and supervisor
Priority: High
Story Points: 2
Dependencies: E02, E03
Notes: Support email and in-app notifications.

**User Story 3**
Title: Block Assignment to Tasks Requiring Valid Certification
User Story: As a scheduler, I want to prevent assignment of tasks to employees with expired certifications so that safety is ensured.
Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Assignment block
Given an expired certification
When I assign a restricted task
Then the system blocks the assignment and shows an error
Priority: High
Story Points: 3
Dependencies: E02, E03
Notes: Integrate with scheduling and asset modules.

**User Story 4**
Title: Upload Proof of Certification
User Story: As an employee, I want to upload proof documents for certifications so that my records are complete.
Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Upload document
Given a certification
When I upload a file
Then the document is stored and linked to my certification
Priority: High
Story Points: 2
Dependencies: E02, E03
Notes: Support PDF/JPG/PNG formats.
