Title: Create Recurring Shift Templates

User Story: As a scheduling manager I want to create recurring shift templates So that I can efficiently plan and reuse standard shift patterns

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Creating a recurring shift template
Given I am on the shift management page
When I select 'Create Template' and define recurrence rules
Then the template should be saved and available for future scheduling

Additional Details:
- Priority: High
- Story Points: 3
- Dependencies: Calendar module
- Notes: Support daily, weekly, and custom recurrence options.
