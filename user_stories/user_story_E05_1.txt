Title: Create Recurring Shift Templates

User Story: As an HR administrator I want to create recurring shift templates So that I can efficiently schedule employees for regular work periods

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Successful shift template creation
Given I am on the shift management page
When I define a new shift template with start/end times and recurrence pattern
Then the template should be saved and available for assignment

Additional Details:
- Priority: High
- Story Points: 3
- Dependencies: E02, E03, E04
- Notes: Support daily, weekly, and custom recurrence.
