Title: Create Shift Templates

User Story: As an HR manager I want to create recurring shift templates so that scheduling is standardized and efficient across the warehouse

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Creating a shift template
Given I am logged in as HR manager
When I define a new shift template with start/end times and recurrence
Then the template should be saved and available for assignment

Additional Details:
- Priority: High
- Story Points: 2
- Dependencies: E02, E03, E04
- Notes: Templates must support daily, weekly, and custom recurrence.
