Title: Create Recurring Shift Templates

User Story: As an HR Manager I want to create and manage recurring shift templates so that scheduling is efficient and consistent across pay periods.

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Creating a recurring shift template
Given I am on the shift management page
When I define a new recurring template with start/end times and days of week
Then the template is saved and available for assignment

Scenario: Editing a shift template
Given a template exists
When I update its parameters
Then the changes are reflected in future schedules

Additional Details:
- Priority: High
- Story Points: 4
- Dependencies: Scheduling module
- Notes: Support for holidays and exceptions is required.
