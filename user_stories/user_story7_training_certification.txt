EPIC E07: Training & Certification Tracking

---

Title: Track Employee Certifications
User Story: As an HR admin I want to record employee certifications and expirations So that compliance is maintained
Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Add certification to employee
Given an employee profile
When I add a certification with expiry date
Then it is saved and visible on the profile

Additional Details:
- Priority: High
- Story Points: 2
- Dependencies: E02, E03
- Notes: Support multiple cert types

---

Title: Alert on Expiring Certifications
User Story: As a supervisor I want to receive alerts before certifications expire So that renewals are not missed
Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Certification expiry alert
Given a certification is expiring in 30 or 7 days
When the system checks expirations
Then an alert is sent to the employee and supervisor

Additional Details:
- Priority: High
- Story Points: 2
- Dependencies: E02, E03
- Notes: Email and in-app notifications

---

Title: Block Assignment to Tasks Requiring Certification
User Story: As a scheduler I want to prevent assignment of tasks to employees with expired certifications So that only qualified staff are assigned
Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Block unqualified assignment
Given an employee's certification is expired
When I try to assign them to a restricted task
Then the system blocks the assignment and shows an error

Additional Details:
- Priority: High
- Story Points: 2
- Dependencies: E02, E03
- Notes: Integrate with scheduling and asset assignment

---

Title: Upload Proof of Certification
User Story: As an employee I want to upload proof documents for certifications So that records are complete
Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Upload certification document
Given I have a valid certification
When I upload a document
Then it is attached to my profile and viewable by HR

Additional Details:
- Priority: Medium
- Story Points: 2
- Dependencies: E02, E03
- Notes: Support PDF/JPG/PNG uploads

---

Title: Show Certification Status on Profile
User Story: As a supervisor I want to view certification status on employee profiles So that I can verify qualifications
Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: View certification status
Given I view an employee profile
When I check certifications
Then I see current status and expiry dates

Additional Details:
- Priority: Medium
- Story Points: 1
- Dependencies: E02, E03
- Notes: Status color-coded (valid/expiring/expired)
