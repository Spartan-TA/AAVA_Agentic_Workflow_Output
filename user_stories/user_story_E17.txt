### E17 - Onboarding & Offboarding Workflow

**User Story 1**
Title: Automate Account Provisioning for New Hires
User Story: As an HR I want new hires from HRIS to trigger account provisioning So that onboarding is streamlined.
Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Account Provisioning
Given new hire data from HRIS
When onboarding workflow starts
Then accounts are provisioned automatically

Additional Details:
- Priority: Medium
- Story Points: 3
- Dependencies: E02, E03, E05, E07, E09, E13
- Notes: Workflow tasks generated

**User Story 2**
Title: Initial Schedule and Training Assignment
User Story: As an HR I want initial schedules and required training assigned to new hires So that onboarding is complete.
Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Schedule and Training Assignment
Given new hire onboarding
When workflow runs
Then schedule and training tasks are assigned

Additional Details:
- Priority: Medium
- Story Points: 2
- Dependencies: User Story 1
- Notes: Training requirements checked

**User Story 3**
Title: Offboarding Workflow for Terminations
User Story: As an HR I want offboarding to revoke access, collect assets, and update schedules So that terminations are handled securely.
Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Offboarding Execution
Given a termination event
When offboarding workflow runs
Then access is revoked, assets collected, and schedules updated

Additional Details:
- Priority: Medium
- Story Points: 3
- Dependencies: User Story 1, User Story 2
- Notes: Asset return tracked
