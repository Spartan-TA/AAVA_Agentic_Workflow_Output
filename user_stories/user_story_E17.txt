### E17: Onboarding & Offboarding Workflow

#### User Story 1
Title: New Hire Onboarding Automation
User Story: As an HR admin, I want new hires from HRIS to trigger onboarding tasks so that setup is consistent and fast.
Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Onboarding trigger
  Given a new hire appears from HRIS
  When onboarding starts
  Then tasks are generated for account setup, schedule, and training
Additional Details:
Priority: Medium
Story Points: 8
Dependencies: E02, E03, E05, E07, E13
Notes: Onboarding checklist must be configurable.

#### User Story 2
Title: Assign Equipment During Onboarding
User Story: As an asset manager, I want onboarding to include asset assignment so that new hires have what they need.
Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Asset assignment
  Given onboarding is in progress
  When asset assignment is due
  Then assets are assigned and logged
Additional Details:
Priority: Medium
Story Points: 3
Dependencies: E09
Notes: Asset checklist must be tracked.

#### User Story 3
Title: Automated Offboarding on Termination
User Story: As an HR admin, I want terminations to trigger offboarding so that access and assets are revoked.
Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Offboarding trigger
  Given an employee is terminated
  When offboarding starts
  Then access is revoked, assets are collected, and schedules are updated
Additional Details:
Priority: Medium
Story Points: 8
Dependencies: E09, E13
Notes: Offboarding must be auditable.

#### User Story 4
Title: Assign Required Training to New Hires
User Story: As a supervisor, I want onboarding to assign required training so that compliance is ensured.
Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Training assignment
  Given onboarding is in progress
  When training is due
  Then training tasks are assigned and tracked
Additional Details:
Priority: Medium
Story Points: 3
Dependencies: E07
Notes: Training status must be visible to supervisors.
