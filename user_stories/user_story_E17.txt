### E17: Onboarding & Offboarding Workflow

**User Story 1**
- Title: Automate New Hire Provisioning from HRIS
- User Story: As an HR user, I want new hires from HRIS to trigger account, schedule, and training provisioning so that onboarding is seamless.
- Acceptance Criteria:
    - Given a new hire in HRIS,
      When the sync runs,
      Then an account, initial schedule, and required training tasks are created.
- Additional Details: Priority: Medium, Story Points: 3, Dependencies: E02, E03, E05, E07, E09, E13, Notes: For automation.

**User Story 2**
- Title: Assign Required Training and Assets on Onboarding
- User Story: As a supervisor, I want onboarding to generate tasks for training and asset assignment so that compliance is ensured.
- Acceptance Criteria:
    - Given a new hire,
      When onboarding starts,
      Then training and asset tasks are assigned.
- Additional Details: Priority: Medium, Story Points: 2, Dependencies: E02, E03, E05, E07, E09, E13, Notes: For compliance.

**User Story 3**
- Title: Automate Offboarding Workflow
- User Story: As an HR user, I want offboarding to revoke access, collect assets, and update schedules so that terminations are handled securely.
- Acceptance Criteria:
    - Given a termination event,
      When offboarding runs,
      Then access is revoked, assets are collected, and schedules are updated.
- Additional Details: Priority: Medium, Story Points: 3, Dependencies: E02, E03, E05, E07, E09, E13, Notes: For security.

**User Story 4**
- Title: Track Onboarding/Offboarding Task Completion
- User Story: As a manager, I want to track completion of onboarding/offboarding tasks so that nothing is missed.
- Acceptance Criteria:
    - Given a workflow,
      When tasks are completed,
      Then the status is updated and visible.
- Additional Details: Priority: Medium, Story Points: 2, Dependencies: E02, E03, E05, E07, E09, E13, Notes: For accountability.
