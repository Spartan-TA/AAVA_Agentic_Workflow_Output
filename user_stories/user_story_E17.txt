### E17 - Onboarding & Offboarding Workflow

**User Story 1: Automate Account Provisioning from HRIS**
- Title: Automate Account Provisioning
- Narrative: As an HR user, I want accounts provisioned automatically from HRIS so that onboarding is fast.
- Acceptance Criteria:
  ```
  Given a new hire in HRIS,
  When synced,
  Then an account is created and initial schedule assigned.
  ```
- Priority: Medium
- Story Points: 3
- Dependencies: E02, E03, E05, E07, E09, E13
- Notes: Efficiency.

**User Story 2: Assign Required Training and Assets**
- Title: Assign Training and Assets on Onboarding
- Narrative: As a supervisor, I want training and asset tasks generated for new hires so that compliance is ensured.
- Acceptance Criteria:
  ```
  Given a new hire,
  When onboarded,
  Then training and asset assignment tasks are created.
  ```
- Priority: Medium
- Story Points: 2
- Dependencies: Story 1
- Notes: Compliance.

**User Story 3: Automate Access Revocation on Termination**
- Title: Automate Access Revocation
- Narrative: As an HR user, I want access revoked and assets collected on termination so that risk is reduced.
- Acceptance Criteria:
  ```
  Given a termination in HRIS,
  When synced,
  Then access is revoked and asset collection tasks are generated.
  ```
- Priority: Medium
- Story Points: 2
- Dependencies: Story 1
- Notes: Security.

**User Story 4: Update Schedules on Offboarding**
- Title: Update Schedules for Offboarded Employees
- Narrative: As a scheduler, I want schedules updated for offboarded employees so that coverage is maintained.
- Acceptance Criteria:
  ```
  Given an offboarding event,
  When processed,
  Then scheduled shifts are flagged for coverage.
  ```
- Priority: Medium
- Story Points: 1
- Dependencies: Story 3
- Notes: Staffing.
