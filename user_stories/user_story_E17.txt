### E17: Onboarding & Offboarding Workflow

**User Story 1: Automated Onboarding Tasks**
- User Story: As an HR user, I want new hires to trigger onboarding tasks for accounts, schedules, training, and asset assignment So that onboarding is consistent and timely.
- Acceptance Criteria:
  - Scenario: New hire onboarding
    - Given a new hire from HRIS
    - When onboarding is triggered
    - Then tasks are generated for account provisioning, schedule assignment, required training, and asset allocation
- Priority: Medium
- Story Points: 8
- Dependencies: E02, E03, E05, E07, E09, E13
- Notes: Tasks tracked to completion.

**User Story 2: Automated Offboarding Tasks**
- User Story: As an HR user, I want terminations to trigger offboarding tasks for access revocation, asset collection, and schedule update So that offboarding is secure and complete.
- Acceptance Criteria:
  - Scenario: Employee termination
    - Given a terminated employee
    - When offboarding is triggered
    - Then access is revoked, assets are collected, and schedules are updated
- Priority: Medium
- Story Points: 5
- Dependencies: E02, E03, E05, E07, E09, E13
- Notes: Status visible to HR.
