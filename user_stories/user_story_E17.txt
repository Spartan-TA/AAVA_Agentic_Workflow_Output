### E17 - Onboarding & Offboarding Workflow

**User Story 1**
- Title: Automate Provisioning of Accounts for New Hires
- User Story: As an HR user, I want accounts provisioned automatically for new hires so that onboarding is efficient.
- Acceptance Criteria:
  - Given a new hire appears from HRIS,
    When onboarding tasks are generated,
    Then accounts are provisioned and initial schedule assigned.
- Additional Details: Priority: Medium, Story Points: 2, Dependencies: E02, E03, E05, E07, E09, E13, Notes: Efficiency.

**User Story 2**
- Title: Assign Required Training and Assets
- User Story: As a supervisor, I want required training and asset assignment tasks generated for new hires so that compliance is ensured.
- Acceptance Criteria:
  - Given a new hire is onboarded,
    When tasks are generated,
    Then training and asset assignments are scheduled.
- Additional Details: Priority: Medium, Story Points: 2, Dependencies: E02, E03, E05, E07, E09, E13, Notes: Compliance.

**User Story 3**
- Title: Automate Offboarding to Revoke Access and Collect Assets
- User Story: As an HR user, I want offboarding to revoke access and collect assets so that terminations are secure.
- Acceptance Criteria:
  - Given an employee is terminated,
    When offboarding runs,
    Then access is revoked, assets are collected, and schedules updated.
- Additional Details: Priority: Medium, Story Points: 2, Dependencies: E02, E03, E05, E07, E09, E13, Notes: Security.
