Title: Asset Registry CRUD

User Story: As an asset manager I want to create, update, and delete asset records So that I can maintain an accurate inventory of equipment and PPE

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Asset registry management
Given I am on the asset management page
When I add, edit, or remove an asset record
Then the changes are saved and reflected in the asset registry

Additional Details:
- Priority: Medium
- Story Points: 4
- Dependencies: E02, E03, E07
- Notes: Include fields for asset type, serial number, condition, and assignment status.
