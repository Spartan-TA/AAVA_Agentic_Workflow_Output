Title: Employee Receives Mobile Notifications
User Story: As an employee I want to receive notifications on my mobile device So that I am informed about schedule changes and announcements in real time
Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Receive mobile notifications
Given I have a mobile device and notifications enabled
When a schedule change or announcement is published
Then I receive a push notification with relevant details
Additional Details:
- Priority: High
- Story Points: 2
- Dependencies: Mobile app (PWA), notification module
- Notes: Support push and SMS notifications.
