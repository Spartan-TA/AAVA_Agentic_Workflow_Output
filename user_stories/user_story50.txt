Title: Initiate Onboarding Workflow

User Story: As an HR manager I want to initiate an onboarding workflow for new hires so that all required tasks are tracked and completed

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Starting onboarding
Given I am logged in as an HR manager
When I add a new employee and start onboarding
Then the system generates a checklist and assigns tasks to relevant stakeholders

Additional Details:
- Priority: High
- Story Points: 4
- Dependencies: Employee master data, task management
- Notes: Support for custom onboarding templates by role or location.
