### EPIC E05: Shift & Schedule Management

**User Story 20**

**Title:** Create Shift Template

**User Story:** As an HR manager, I want to create reusable shift templates So that I can efficiently assign standard schedules to employees.

**Acceptance Criteria:**

Scenario: Creating a new shift template
Given I am an HR manager
When I provide shift details (start/end time, break, recurrence) and save
Then the template is available for assignment and visible in the template list

**Additional Details:**
- Priority: High
- Story Points: 3
- Dependencies: E02, E03, E04
- Notes: Templates support daily/weekly recurrence.

---

**User Story 21**

**Title:** Assign Shift to Employee

**User Story:** As a supervisor, I want to assign shifts to employees using templates So that I can quickly schedule my team.

**Acceptance Criteria:**

Scenario: Assigning a shift
Given I have selected a shift template and an employee
When I assign the shift
Then the employee's schedule updates and conflicts are checked

**Additional Details:**
- Priority: High
- Story Points: 3
- Dependencies: E02, E03, E04
- Notes: Bulk assignment supported.

---

**User Story 22**

**Title:** Detect Scheduling Conflicts

**User Story:** As a supervisor, I want the system to detect scheduling conflicts So that I can avoid double-booking employees.

**Acceptance Criteria:**

Scenario: Assigning overlapping shifts
Given an employee is already scheduled for a shift
When I attempt to assign an overlapping shift
Then the system blocks the assignment and displays an error

**Additional Details:**
- Priority: High
- Story Points: 2
- Dependencies: E02, E03, E04
- Notes: Includes overtime and blackout dates.

---

**User Story 23**

**Title:** View Personal Shift Calendar

**User Story:** As a worker, I want to view my upcoming shifts So that I can plan my attendance.

**Acceptance Criteria:**

Scenario: Viewing shift calendar
Given I am logged in as a worker
When I access my schedule
Then I see all assigned shifts for the next 30 days

**Additional Details:**
- Priority: High
- Story Points: 2
- Dependencies: E02, E03, E04
- Notes: Calendar and list views.

---

**User Story 24**

**Title:** Bulk Shift Assignment

**User Story:** As a supervisor, I want to assign shifts to multiple employees at once So that I can efficiently manage large teams.

**Acceptance Criteria:**

Scenario: Bulk assigning shifts
Given I have selected multiple employees and a shift template
When I confirm the assignment
Then all selected employees receive the shift if no conflicts exist

**Additional Details:**
- Priority: High
- Story Points: 3
- Dependencies: E02, E03, E04
