Title: Employee Clock-In

User Story: As an employee I want to clock in at the start of my shift So that my attendance is accurately recorded

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Successful clock-in
Given I am scheduled for a shift
When I clock in via the web or mobile app
Then my clock-in time is recorded and visible to my manager

Additional Details:
- Priority: High
- Story Points: 2
- Dependencies: Attendance module, user authentication
- Notes: Prevent multiple clock-ins for the same shift.
