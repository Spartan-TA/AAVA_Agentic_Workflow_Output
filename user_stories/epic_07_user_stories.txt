### E07: Training & Certification Tracking

**User Story 7.1**
Title: Certification Record Management

User Story: As an HR Admin I want to add, edit, or remove employee certifications So that compliance records are up to date.

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Certification management
Given I am an HR Admin
When I manage certifications
Then the changes are reflected in the employee's profile and audit logged

Additional Details:
- Priority: High
- Story Points: 3
- Dependencies: E02, E03
- Notes: Certification types and expiry dates required

---

**User Story 7.2**
Title: Certification Expiry Alerts

User Story: As a Supervisor I want to receive alerts when certifications are nearing expiry So that renewals can be scheduled.

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Expiry alert
Given an employee's certification is expiring in 30 or 7 days
When the system runs the expiry check
Then an alert is sent to the employee and supervisor

Additional Details:
- Priority: High
- Story Points: 2
- Dependencies: E02, E03
- Notes: Alert channels configurable

---

**User Story 7.3**
Title: Block Assignment for Expired Certifications

User Story: As a Scheduler I want to prevent assignment of tasks requiring valid certifications to employees with expired or missing certs So that safety is ensured.

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Assignment block
Given an employee's certification is expired
When assigning them to a restricted task
Then the system blocks the assignment and displays an error

Additional Details:
- Priority: High
- Story Points: 5
- Dependencies: E02, E03
- Notes: Certification-task mapping required

---

**User Story 7.4**
Title: Upload Certification Proof Documents

User Story: As an Employee I want to upload proof of certification So that my records are complete.

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Document upload
Given I have a new or renewed certification
When I upload the document
Then it is stored securely and visible to HR and my supervisor

Additional Details:
- Priority: Medium
- Story Points: 2
- Dependencies: E02, E03
- Notes: File type and size restrictions apply
