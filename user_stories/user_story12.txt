Title: Approve or Reject Leave Requests
User Story: As a manager I want to approve or reject leave requests So that team availability is managed

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Leave approval workflow
Given I have pending leave requests
When I approve or reject a request
Then the employee is notified of the decision

Additional Details:
- Priority: High
- Story Points: 2
- Dependencies: Request Paid Time Off (PTO)
- Notes: Record reason for rejection
