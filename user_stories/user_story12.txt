Title: Create Performance Review Cycles

User Story: As an HR manager I want to create quarterly and annual performance review cycles so that structured feedback can be collected

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Creating a review cycle
Given I am logged in as HR manager
When I create a new review cycle and assign it to employees
Then the cycle is visible to supervisors and employees

Additional Details:
- Priority: Medium
- Story Points: 2
- Dependencies: Employee master data, RBAC
- Notes: Templates must support goals, competencies, ratings

---

Title: Submit and Acknowledge Performance Reviews

User Story: As a supervisor I want to submit reviews and as an employee I want to acknowledge them so that feedback is documented and confirmed

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Submitting and acknowledging reviews
Given a review cycle is active
When a supervisor submits a review and employee acknowledges
Then the review is locked and history is immutable

Additional Details:
- Priority: Medium
- Story Points: 2
- Dependencies: Review cycle
- Notes: PDF export required

---

Title: Role-Based Visibility of Reviews

User Story: As an HR manager I want reviews to be visible only to authorized roles so that privacy and compliance are maintained

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Viewing reviews
Given I am logged in as HR manager or supervisor
When I access the review dashboard
Then I see only reviews for my scope

Additional Details:
- Priority: Medium
- Story Points: 1
- Dependencies: RBAC
- Notes: Employees see only their own reviews

---

Title: Track Goals and Competencies

User Story: As a supervisor I want to track employee goals and competencies so that development plans can be monitored

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Tracking goals
Given a review cycle is active
When I update goals and competencies for an employee
Then the updates are saved and visible in the review history

Additional Details:
- Priority: Medium
- Story Points: 1
- Dependencies: Review cycle
- Notes: History must be immutable after sign-off
