Title: Create Shift Templates

User Story: As a scheduler I want to define reusable shift templates So that I can quickly assign standard shifts to employees

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Shift templates created
  Given I am on the shift management page
  When I create a new shift template
  Then it should be available for future shift assignments

Additional Details:
- Priority: Medium
- Story Points: 2
- Dependencies: Employee entity, scheduling module
- Notes: Templates should include start/end times, breaks, and role requirements.
