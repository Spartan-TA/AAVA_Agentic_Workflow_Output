Title: Employee Clock-Out Event

User Story: As a warehouse worker I want to clock out at the end of my shift So that my worked hours are accurately recorded and payroll is correct

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Successful clock-out
Given I am scheduled for a shift and have clocked in
When I submit a clock-out event
Then my attendance record is updated and total hours are calculated

Additional Details:
- Priority: High
- Story Points: 2
- Dependencies: E02, E03
- Notes: Device ID captured for audit; automatic shift association
