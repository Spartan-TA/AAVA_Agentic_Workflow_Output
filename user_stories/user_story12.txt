Title: Training & Certification Tracking
User Story: As a safety manager I want to track employee certifications and expirations So that only qualified staff are assigned to sensitive tasks
Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Certification expiring alert
Given a certification expires in 30 days
When I view the dashboard
Then I see an alert for upcoming expiration

Scenario: Block assignment with expired cert
Given an employee's certification is expired
When I assign them to a restricted task
Then the assignment is blocked

Additional Details:
- Priority: High
- Story Points: 5
- Dependencies: Employee CRUD API, RBAC
- Notes: Upload proof documents; status visible on profile.
