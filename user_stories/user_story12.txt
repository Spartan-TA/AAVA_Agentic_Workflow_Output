Title: Notification Channel Preferences

User Story: As a user I want to opt-in or out of in-app, email, and SMS notifications So that I receive updates in my preferred way.

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Notification preference management
Given notification channels are available
When I update my preferences
Then notifications are sent only via selected channels

Additional Details:
- Priority: Medium
- Story Points: 2
- Dependencies: E03, E05, E06, E07
- Notes: Templates localized; delivery status tracked.

When I log in to my dashboard

Additional Details:
- Priority: Medium
- Story Points: 1
- Dependencies: E03, E05, E06, E07
