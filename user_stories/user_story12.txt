EPIC E12 - Notifications & Announcements

===========================================

User Story 1: Notification Channel Opt-In/Out
---------------------------------------------
Title: Users Opt-In/Out of Notification Channels

User Story: As a user, I want to opt-in or out of notification channels so that I control how I receive updates.

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Channel preference
  Given user settings
  When channel is toggled
  Then notifications are sent only to selected channels

Additional Details:
- Priority: Medium
- Story Points: 2
- Dependencies: E03, E05, E06, E07
- Notes: Support in-app, email, SMS.

===========================================
