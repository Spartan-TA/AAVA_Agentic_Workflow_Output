Title: Notifications & Announcements

User Story: As a warehouse worker I want to receive timely notifications and announcements via in-app, email, or SMS so that I am informed about shift changes, expiring certifications, approvals, and important updates.

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Opt-in/out notification channels
  Given notification channels are available
  When I configure my preferences
  Then I receive notifications only via selected channels

Scenario: Localized templates
  Given notification templates exist
  When I receive a notification
  Then the message is localized to my language preference

Scenario: Delivery status tracking
  Given a notification is sent
  When I check delivery status
  Then the system shows whether it was delivered or failed

Scenario: Announcement dashboard
  Given announcements are published
  When I log in
  Then I see announcements on my dashboard

Additional Details:
- Priority: Medium
- Story Points: 5
- Dependencies: RBAC (E03), Shift & Schedule Management (E05), Leave & Absence Management (E06), Training & Certification Tracking (E07)
- Notes: Quiet hours configuration; rate limits applied; delivery status tracked.
