Title: Clock-In Event Recording
User Story: As a WORKER I want to clock in at the start of my shift So that my attendance and hours worked are accurately tracked
Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Successful clock-in
Given I am scheduled for a shift and on the clock-in page
When I submit a clock-in event with valid device and (optional) geofence
Then my clock-in is recorded, associated with my shift, and reflected in daily totals
Additional Details:
- Priority: High
- Story Points: 3
- Dependencies: E02, E03
- Notes: Device and geofence validation must be logged for audit.
