Title: Shift Conflict Detection

User Story: As a system, I want to detect and prevent scheduling conflicts so that employees are not double-booked or assigned during blackout dates.

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Conflict detection
Given an employee has an existing shift
When a conflicting shift is assigned
Then the system rejects the assignment with a conflict error

Additional Details:
- Priority: High
- Story Points: 3
- Dependencies: Shift Template Management
- Notes: Integrate with warehouse operation calendars.
