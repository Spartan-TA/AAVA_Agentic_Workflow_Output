Title: Notifications & Announcements
User Story: As a warehouse worker I want to receive in-app and email/SMS notifications for shift changes, expiring certifications, approvals, and announcements so that I am informed in real time.
Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Notification delivery and management
Given I am authenticated as a user
When a relevant event occurs (shift change, cert expiry, approval, announcement)
Then I receive notifications via my chosen channels
And I can opt-in or out per channel
And templates are localized
And delivery status is tracked
And announcements are visible on my dashboard
Additional Details:
- Priority: Medium
- Story Points: 5
- Dependencies: Role-Based Access Control (RBAC), Shift & Schedule Management, Leave & Absence Management, Training & Certification Tracking
- Notes: Quiet hours configuration and rate limits must be supported.
