Title: Shift & Schedule Management - Assign Shifts to Employees

User Story: As a supervisor, I want to assign shifts to employees and manage rotations so that staffing is planned efficiently.

Acceptance Criteria:

Scenario: Assign shift to employee
Given a shift template exists
When I assign it to an employee for a date range
Then the employee's schedule is updated

Scenario: Bulk assign shifts
Given multiple employees and a shift template
When I bulk assign the template
Then all selected employees receive the schedule

Scenario: Conflict detection
Given an employee has a scheduled shift
When I attempt to assign an overlapping shift
Then the system prevents the conflict

Additional Details:
- Priority: High
- Story Points: 8
- Dependencies: Shift template CRUD
- Notes: Blackout dates and warehouse calendars must be respected.
