Title: Notifications & Announcements

User Story: As a supervisor I want to send notifications and announcements to staff so that everyone is informed of important updates.

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Notification delivery
Given a shift change or announcement
When I send a notification
Then users receive it via their chosen channel, delivery status is tracked, and rate limits are enforced

Additional Details:
- Priority: Medium
- Story Points: 3
- Dependencies: RBAC, Shift & Schedule Management, Leave & Absence Management, Training & Certification Tracking
- Notes: In-app, email, SMS; templates localized; quiet hours config
