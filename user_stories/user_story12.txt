### E12 â Notifications & Announcements

**User Story 1: In-App Notifications**
- **User Story:** As a worker, I want to receive in-app notifications for shift changes and approvals so that I am always informed.
- **Acceptance Criteria:**
  - Given a relevant event,
  - When it occurs,
  - Then a notification appears in my dashboard.
- **Additional Details:** Priority: Medium; Story Points: 2; Dependencies: E03, E05, E06, E07; Notes: Real-time delivery.

**User Story 2: Email/SMS Notifications**
- **User Story:** As a worker, I want to receive email/SMS alerts for critical updates so that I don't miss important info.
- **Acceptance Criteria:**
  - Given a notification,
  - When I am opted in,
  - Then I receive an email/SMS.
- **Additional Details:** Priority: Medium; Story Points: 2; Dependencies: E12-1; Notes: Support opt-in/out.

**User Story 3: Localized Notification Templates**
- **User Story:** As an admin, I want notification templates localized so that users receive messages in their language.
- **Acceptance Criteria:**
  - Given a user's locale,
  - When a notification is sent,
  - Then the template matches their language.
- **Additional Details:** Priority: Medium; Story Points: 2; Dependencies: E18; Notes: Support EN/ES at minimum.

**User Story 4: Delivery Status Tracking**
- **User Story:** As an admin, I want to track notification delivery status so that failures are visible.
- **Acceptance Criteria:**
  - Given a notification,
  - When it is sent,
  - Then delivery status is updated and visible.
- **Additional Details:** Priority: Medium; Story Points: 1; Dependencies: E12-1; Notes: Retry failed sends.

**User Story 5: Announcements Dashboard**
- **User Story:** As a worker, I want to see company announcements on my dashboard so that I stay informed.
- **Acceptance Criteria:**
  - Given an announcement,
  - When I log in,
  - Then it is visible until dismissed or expired.
- **Additional Details:** Priority: Medium; Story Points: 1; Dependencies: E12-1; Notes: Role-based targeting.
