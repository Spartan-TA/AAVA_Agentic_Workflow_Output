Title: View Attendance History

User Story: As an employee I want to view my attendance history So that I can verify my working hours and absences

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: View attendance
Given I am on the attendance history page
When I select a date range
Then I should see a list of my clock-in and clock-out records for that period

Additional Details:
- Priority: Medium
- Story Points: 2
- Dependencies: Time & Attendance module
- Notes: Records should be exportable to CSV.
