Title: Leave & Absence Management

User Story: As an employee I want to request and track leave So that my time off is managed according to company policy and scheduling needs

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Request leave
Given I have available leave balance
When I submit a leave request
Then my supervisor receives the request for approval and my balance updates upon approval

Scenario: Scheduled shift auto-flag
Given I have approved leave overlapping with scheduled shifts
When the leave is approved
Then the system flags those shifts for coverage

Additional Details:
- Priority: Medium
- Story Points: 5
- Dependencies: Employee Master Data, RBAC, Shift & Schedule Management
- Notes: PTO, sick, unpaid leave supported; integration hooks for payroll and scheduling
