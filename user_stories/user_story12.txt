Title: Notifications & Announcements

User Story: As a warehouse worker or supervisor I want to receive in-app and email/SMS notifications for important events so that I stay informed about shift changes, expiring certifications, approvals, and announcements.

Acceptance Criteria:

Scenario: Notification preferences
Given I am a user
When I configure my notification settings
Then I can opt-in or out per channel (in-app, email, SMS)

Scenario: Localized templates
Given a notification is sent
When my preferred language is set
Then the notification is localized accordingly

Scenario: Delivery status tracking
Given notifications are sent
When I review my notification history
Then the delivery status is visible

Scenario: Announcements dashboard
Given announcements are published
When I log in
Then I see all relevant announcements on my dashboard

Additional Details:
- Priority: Medium
- Story Points: 5
- Dependencies: Role-Based Access Control (E03), Shift & Schedule Management (E05), Leave & Absence Management (E06), Training & Certification Tracking (E07)
- Notes: Support quiet hours configuration and rate limits.
