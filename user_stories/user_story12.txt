Title: Send Notifications & Announcements

User Story:
As a warehouse worker, I want to receive in-app and email/SMS notifications for shift changes, expiring certifications, approvals, and announcements so that I stay informed in real time.

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario 1: Opt-in/out channels
  Given notification channels are available
  When I manage my preferences
  Then I can opt in or out per channel

Scenario 2: Localized templates
  Given notifications are sent
  When my language preference is set
  Then I receive messages in my language

Scenario 3: Track delivery status
  Given notifications are sent
  When I view my notification history
  Then I see delivery status for each

Scenario 4: Rate limits
  Given many notifications are triggered
  When rate limits are exceeded
  Then excess notifications are queued or dropped

Scenario 5: Announcements dashboard
  Given announcements are published
  When I log in
  Then I see them on my dashboard

Additional Details:
Priority: Medium
Story Points: 8
Dependencies: E03, E05, E06, E07
Notes: Quiet hours must be configurable.
