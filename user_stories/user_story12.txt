Title: Employee Views Required Training

User Story: As an employee I want to view my required training and certifications So that I can complete them on time and remain compliant

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Viewing required training
Given I am logged into the system
When I navigate to the 'Training & Certifications' section
Then I see a list of all required and completed training modules

Additional Details:
- Priority: Medium
- Story Points: 2
- Dependencies: Employee Master Data, Training Module
- Notes: Highlight overdue or expiring certifications.
