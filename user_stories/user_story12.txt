Title: Notifications & Announcements

User Story: As a warehouse worker or supervisor I want to receive in-app and email/SMS notifications for shift changes, expiring certifications, approvals, and announcements So that I am always informed of important updates and can act promptly.

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Notification delivery
Given a shift change, expiring certification, approval, or announcement occurs
When the event is triggered in the system
Then the relevant users receive notifications via their preferred channel (in-app, email, SMS) respecting configured quiet hours.

Additional Details:
- Priority: Medium
- Story Points: 5
- Dependencies: E03, E05, E06, E07
- Notes: Include configuration for quiet hours and notification preferences.
