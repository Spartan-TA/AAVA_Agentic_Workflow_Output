#### User Story 12: Clock-Out Endpoint and Shift Calculation
**As a** warehouse worker  
**I want** to clock out and have my worked hours calculated  
**so that** my payroll and compliance records are accurate.

**Acceptance Criteria:**
- **Given** a clock-out event,  
  **When** I POST `/attendance/clock-out`,  
  **Then** my shift hours are computed and stored.

**Additional Details:**  
Priority: High  
Story Points: 2  
Dependencies: E04  
Notes: Handle overnight shifts and edge cases.
