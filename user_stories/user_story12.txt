Title: Shift Assignment & Conflict Detection
User Story: As a supervisor, I want to assign shifts to employees and detect conflicts so that staffing is adequate and errors are prevented.
Acceptance Criteria:
  Given overlapping shifts,
  When I assign to an employee,
  Then the system blocks the conflict.

  Given a worker,
  When I view their schedule,
  Then all upcoming shifts are visible.

Additional Details:
  Priority: High
  Story Points: 3
  Dependencies: Shift Template Management
  Notes: Bulk-assign supported.
