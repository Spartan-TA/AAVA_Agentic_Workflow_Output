Title: Manage Shifts & Schedules

User Story: As a scheduler I want to create shift templates and assign rotations So that employee schedules are managed efficiently and overtime rules are enforced

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Create and assign shift template
Given I have access to the scheduling module
When I create a new shift template and assign it to employees
Then the schedule is updated and overtime rules are applied automatically

Additional Details:
- Priority: High
- Story Points: 8
- Dependencies: E02 (Employee Master Data CRUD), E03 (RBAC), E04 (Time & Attendance)
- Notes: Must support recurring shifts and custom overtime rules.
