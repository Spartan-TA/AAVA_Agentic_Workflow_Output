Title: Notifications - In-App Notifications

User Story: As a worker I want to receive in-app notifications for shift changes, expiring certs, and approvals so that I stay informed.

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Receive notification for shift change
Given my shift is changed
When the change is saved
Then I receive an in-app notification

Scenario: Receive notification for expiring cert
Given my certification is expiring soon
When the system checks expirations
Then I receive an in-app notification

Additional Details:
- Priority: Medium
- Story Points: 2
- Dependencies: E03, E05, E06, E07
- Notes: Users can opt-in/out per channel.
