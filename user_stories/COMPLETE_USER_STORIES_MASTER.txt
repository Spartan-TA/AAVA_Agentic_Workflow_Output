WAREHOUSE EMPLOYEE MANAGEMENT SYSTEM - COMPLETE USER STORIES
Transformation of 20 Epics into 100 Detailed User Stories
===================================================================================

EXECUTIVE SUMMARY:
-----------------
This document contains the complete transformation of 20 high-level epics from the warehouse employee management system into 100 detailed, actionable user stories. Each epic has been systematically decomposed into 3-8 user stories, covering all functional requirements across:

- Project Scaffolding & Infrastructure (E01)
- Employee Master Data Management (E02)
- Role-Based Access Control (E03)
- Time & Attendance Tracking (E04)
- Shift & Schedule Management (E05)
- Leave & Absence Management (E06)
- Training & Certification Tracking (E07)
- Safety Incidents & OSHA Reporting (E08)
- Equipment & Asset Assignment (E09)
- Performance Reviews & Goals (E10)
- Payroll Export Integration (E11)
- Notifications & Announcements (E12)
- Integration Layer (HRIS/WMS/IDP) (E13)
- Audit Trail & Compliance (E14)
- Reporting & Analytics (E15)
- Mobile Access (PWA) (E16)
- Onboarding & Offboarding Workflow (E17)
- Localization & Multi-Tenant (E18)
- Advanced Scheduling (E19)
- CI/CD & Observability (E20)

All user stories follow the standard format with Gherkin-style acceptance criteria, priority levels, story point estimates, dependencies, and implementation notes.
