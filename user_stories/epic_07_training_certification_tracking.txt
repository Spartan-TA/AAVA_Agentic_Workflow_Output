Title: Track Training & Certifications
User Story: As a safety officer I want to track employee certifications and expirations So that only qualified staff are assigned to equipment-sensitive roles
Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Certification Tracking
Given employee certification data
When a certification is about to expire
Then alerts are sent 30/7 days before expiry
Additional Details:
- Priority: High
- Story Points: 3
- Dependencies: Employee CRUD, RBAC
- Notes: Upload proof documents
