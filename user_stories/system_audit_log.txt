Title: System Audit Log

User Story: As a system administrator, I want to maintain audit logs of all employee management actions So that I can ensure accountability and traceability.

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Audit log maintenance
Given employee management actions occur
When actions are performed (create, update, delete)
Then audit logs are generated and accessible for review

Additional Details:
- Priority: High
- Story Points: 3
- Dependencies: All modules
- Notes: Logs are immutable and exportable
