Title: Request Leave and View Announcements on Mobile

User Story: As a warehouse worker I want to request leave and view company announcements from my mobile device So that I can manage my work-life balance and stay informed

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Mobile leave request and announcements
Given I am logged in on my mobile device
When I request leave or view announcements
Then the actions are processed and displayed responsively

Additional Details:
- Priority: Medium
- Story Points: 4
- Dependencies: E04, E05, E06, E12
- Notes: Leave requests must sync with desktop; announcements localized.
