Title: Asset Registry CRUD and Check-In/Out Endpoints

User Story: As a warehouse supervisor I want to manage the asset registry and track check-in/out of equipment So that asset usage and responsibility are clearly recorded

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Register new asset
Given I am authenticated as a supervisor or ADMIN
When I add a new asset to the registry
Then the asset is saved with unique ID, type, and condition

Scenario: Check-out asset
Given an asset is available
When I assign it to an employee
Then the system records the check-out event with timestamp and employee ID

Additional Details:
- Priority: Medium
- Story Points: 5
- Dependencies: E02, E03, E07
- Notes: Asset registry must support all equipment types; check-in/out must be available via API and UI.
