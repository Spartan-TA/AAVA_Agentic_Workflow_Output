Title: Asset Registry CRUD

User Story: As an Asset Manager I want to create, update, and delete asset records for scanners, forklifts, and PPE so that all equipment is tracked and managed centrally

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Add asset
Given I am logged in as Asset Manager
When I add a new asset with type, serial number, and condition
Then the asset appears in the registry and is available for assignment

Scenario: Update asset
Given an asset exists
When I update its condition or location
Then the changes are saved and visible in the registry

Scenario: Delete asset
Given an asset is no longer in use
When I delete the asset
Then it is removed from the registry and audit log records the deletion

Additional Details:
- Priority: Medium
- Story Points: 3
- Dependencies: Employee Master Data (E02), RBAC (E03), Certification Tracking (E07)
- Notes: Asset types should be configurable; deletion must be logged
