Title: Assign Equipment to Employees

User Story: As a warehouse manager I want to assign equipment such as scanners, forklifts, and PPE to employees So that asset usage is tracked and compliance is maintained

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Assign equipment
Given I am logged in as a warehouse manager
When I assign equipment to an employee
Then the assignment should be recorded and linked to the employee profile

Additional Details:
- Priority: Medium
- Story Points: 2
- Dependencies: E02 (Employee Profiles), E03 (Scheduling), E07 (Training & Certification Tracking)
- Notes: Assignment should check for required certifications before completion.
