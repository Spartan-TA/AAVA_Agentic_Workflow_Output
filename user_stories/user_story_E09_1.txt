Title: CRUD for Asset Registry

User Story: As an asset manager I want to create, read, update, and delete assets in the registry So that all equipment and assets are tracked accurately

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Managing asset registry
Given I am on the asset management page
When I add, edit, or remove an asset
Then the changes should be reflected in the registry and available for assignment

Additional Details:
- Priority: High
- Story Points: 3
- Dependencies: Asset database
- Notes: Support asset types, serial numbers, and location tracking.
