## User Story 1: Create and Manage Shift Templates
**User Story:**  
As an HR user, I want to define recurring shift templates so that I can standardize scheduling.

**Acceptance Criteria (Gherkin):**  
Given the scheduling module  
When I create/edit/delete a shift template  
Then the template is saved and available for assignment

**Additional Details:**  
- Priority: High  
- Story Points: 2  
- Dependencies: E02, E03, E04  
- Notes: Templates include start/end time, break rules

---

## User Story 2: Assign Shifts to Employees
**User Story:**  
As a supervisor, I want to assign shifts to employees so that staffing is planned in advance.

**Acceptance Criteria (Gherkin):**  
Given available shift templates  
When I assign a shift to an employee  
Then the assignment is saved and visible to the employee

**Additional Details:**  
- Priority: High  
- Story Points: 2  
- Dependencies: E05-1  
- Notes: Bulk assignment supported

---

## User Story 3: Detect and Prevent Scheduling Conflicts
**User Story:**  
As a scheduler, I want the system to detect conflicts (overlaps, double-booking) so that errors are prevented.

**Acceptance Criteria (Gherkin):**  
Given existing assignments  
When I assign a new shift  
Then conflicts are detected and prevented

**Additional Details:**  
- Priority: High  
- Story Points: 2  
- Dependencies: E05-2  
- Notes: Includes blackout dates and holidays

---

## User Story 4: Display Personal Upcoming Shifts
**User Story:**  
As a worker, I want to view my upcoming shifts so that I can plan accordingly.

**Acceptance Criteria (Gherkin):**  
Given I am logged in  
When I view my schedule  
Then I see all upcoming assigned shifts

**Additional Details:**  
- Priority: Medium  
- Story Points: 1  
- Dependencies: E05-2  
- Notes: Mobile-friendly view

---

## User Story 5: Generate Audit Entries for Shift Changes
**User Story:**  
As an auditor, I want all shift assignments and changes logged so that I can review scheduling history.

**Acceptance Criteria (Gherkin):**  
Given a shift assignment is created/updated/deleted  
When I view the audit log  
Then the change is recorded with actor and timestamp

**Additional Details:**  
- Priority: Medium  
- Story Points: 1  
- Dependencies: E14  
- Notes: Link to audit trail module
