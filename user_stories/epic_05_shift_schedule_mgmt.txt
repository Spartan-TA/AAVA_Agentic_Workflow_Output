### E05: Shift & Schedule Management

**User Story 1**
- Title: Create Shift Templates
- User Story: As an HR user, I want to define recurring shift templates so that scheduling is efficient.
- Acceptance Criteria in Gherkin format:
  Scenario: Create and retrieve shift templates
  Given I have shift details
  When I POST to /shifts/templates
  Then a new template is created and retrievable
- Additional Details:
  * Priority: High
  * Story Points: 3
  * Dependencies: E02, E03, E04
  * Notes: Support for rotations and overtime rules.

**User Story 2**
- Title: Assign Shifts to Employees
- User Story: As a supervisor, I want to assign shifts to employees so that staffing needs are met.
- Acceptance Criteria in Gherkin format:
  Scenario: Assign shift to employee
  Given a shift template exists
  When I assign it to an employee
  Then the employee's schedule is updated and conflicts are checked
- Additional Details:
  * Priority: High
  * Story Points: 3
  * Dependencies: E02, E03, E04
  * Notes: Detect and prevent conflicts.

**User Story 3**
- Title: Bulk Shift Assignment
- User Story: As a supervisor, I want to bulk-assign shifts so that I can efficiently schedule my team.
- Acceptance Criteria in Gherkin format:
  Scenario: Bulk assignment with audit
  Given multiple employees are selected
  When I assign a shift template in bulk
  Then all assignments are created and audit entries logged
- Additional Details:
  * Priority: High
  * Story Points: 2
  * Dependencies: E02, E03, E04
  * Notes: Audit trail required.

**User Story 4**
- Title: View Personal Upcoming Shifts
- User Story: As a worker, I want to see my upcoming shifts so that I can plan accordingly.
- Acceptance Criteria in Gherkin format:
  Scenario: View personal schedule
  Given I am logged in as a worker
  When I view my schedule
  Then I see all upcoming assigned shifts
- Additional Details:
  * Priority: High
  * Story Points: 2
  * Dependencies: E02, E03, E04
  * Notes: Mobile-friendly view.
