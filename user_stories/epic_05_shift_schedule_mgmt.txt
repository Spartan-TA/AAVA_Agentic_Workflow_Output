EPIC 05: SHIFT & SCHEDULE MANAGEMENT

===========================================

Title: Shift Template Management

User Story: As a supervisor I want to create and manage shift templates So that I can efficiently schedule employees

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Create shift template
Given I am authenticated as SUPERVISOR
When I POST to /shifts/templates with valid data
Then a new shift template is created

Scenario: Conflict detection
Given overlapping shifts exist
When I assign a new shift
Then the system detects and prevents conflicts

Additional Details:
- Priority: High
- Story Points: 5
- Dependencies: Employee CRUD API, RBAC, Attendance
- Notes: Blackout dates and operation calendars supported

---

Title: Shift Assignment

User Story: As a supervisor I want to bulk-assign shifts to employees So that staffing is adequate and scheduling conflicts are minimized

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Bulk assignment
Given multiple employees are selected
When I assign a shift template
Then all employees are scheduled accordingly

Scenario: Audit entry generation
Given a shift assignment occurs
When I view audit logs
Then the assignment is recorded with actor and timestamp

Additional Details:
- Priority: High
- Story Points: 8
- Dependencies: Employee CRUD API, RBAC, Attendance
- Notes: Workers see personal upcoming shifts
