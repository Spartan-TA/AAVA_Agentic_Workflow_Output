Title: Certification Tracking and Alerts
User Story: As a safety manager I want to track employee certifications and receive expiry alerts So that only qualified staff are assigned to sensitive tasks.
Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Add certification to employee
  Given valid certification data
  When I POST to /certifications
  Then the certification is linked to the employee

Scenario: Expiry alerts
  Given a certification expiring in 30 or 7 days
  When the date approaches
  Then an alert is sent to the employee and supervisor

Scenario: Block assignment if expired
  Given an expired certification
  When I assign the employee to a restricted task
  Then the system blocks the assignment

Additional Details:
- Priority: High
- Story Points: 5
- Dependencies: E02, E03
- Notes: Proof documents can be uploaded.
