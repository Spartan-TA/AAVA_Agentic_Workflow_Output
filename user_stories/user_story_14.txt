Title: Employee Clock In

User Story: As an employee I want to clock in at the start of my shift So that my attendance is accurately recorded

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Successful clock in
Given I am scheduled for a shift and have not yet clocked in
When I access the clock in page and submit my clock in
Then my attendance record should show the correct clock in time

Additional Details:
- Priority: High
- Story Points: 3
- Dependencies: Shift scheduling
- Notes: Prevent multiple clock-ins without a clock-out.
