Title: Clock-In/Out Event Endpoints

User Story: As a WORKER I want to clock in and out using a secure endpoint so that my attendance is accurately recorded

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Clocking in for a shift
Given I am scheduled for a shift
When I submit a clock-in event via the endpoint
Then my attendance record is created and associated with my shift

Additional Details:
- Priority: High
- Story Points: 3
- Dependencies: E02, E03
- Notes: Geofence and device capture are optional but should be supported.
