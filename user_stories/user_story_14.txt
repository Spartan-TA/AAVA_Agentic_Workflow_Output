Title: Calculate Hours Worked Per Shift and Daily Totals

User Story: As a payroll administrator I want the system to automatically compute hours worked per shift and daily totals so that payroll and compliance reports are accurate

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Automatic calculation of hours
Given clock-in and clock-out events are recorded for an employee
When the payroll administrator views the attendance report
Then the system displays computed hours for each shift and daily totals

Additional Details:
- Priority: High
- Story Points: 2
- Dependencies: Clock-in/out event recording
- Notes: Must handle overnight shifts and time zone differences.
