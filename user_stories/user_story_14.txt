Title: Assign Shifts to Employees

User Story: As a scheduler I want to assign shifts to employees so that work coverage is ensured

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Assign shift
Given I have created shift templates
When I select employees and assign shifts
Then the assignments should appear on the schedule and notify employees

Additional Details:
- Priority: High
- Story Points: 3
- Dependencies: Shift templates, Notification module
- Notes: Support bulk assignment and editing.
