Title: Create Shift Schedules

User Story: As a shift manager I want to create shift schedules for employees So that I can ensure adequate coverage and operational efficiency

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Create new shift schedule
Given I am in the shift scheduling module
When I create a new shift and assign employees
Then the schedule should be saved and visible to assigned employees

Additional Details:
- Priority: High
- Story Points: 8
- Dependencies: Shift scheduling module
- Notes: Support for recurring and ad-hoc shifts.
