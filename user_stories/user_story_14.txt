Title: Record Employee Clock-Out Event

User Story: As a warehouse worker I want to clock out at the end of my shift So that my total hours worked are accurately calculated

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Successful clock-out
Given I am clocked in for a shift
When I submit a clock-out event via the system
Then my attendance record is updated and the total hours for the shift are computed

Additional Details:
- Priority: High
- Story Points: 2
- Dependencies: Employee Master Data, RBAC
- Notes: System should handle missed punches gracefully.
