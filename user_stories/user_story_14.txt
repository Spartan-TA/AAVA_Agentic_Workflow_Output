Title: Automatic Shift Association for Attendance

User Story: As a supervisor I want clock-in/out events to be automatically associated with scheduled shifts So that attendance and payroll calculations are accurate

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Clock-in during scheduled shift
Given a scheduled shift for an employee
When the employee clocks in
Then the attendance record is linked to the correct shift

Scenario: Clock-in outside scheduled shift
Given no scheduled shift
When the employee clocks in
Then the event is flagged for supervisor review

Additional Details:
- Priority: High
- Story Points: 3
- Dependencies: Employee CRUD, RBAC, Shift Management
- Notes: Attendance must support shift association logic
