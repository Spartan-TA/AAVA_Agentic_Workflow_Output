EPIC E10: Performance Reviews & Goals

---

Title: Create Review Templates

User Story: As an HR user I want to create quarterly/annual review templates So that reviews are standardized

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Template creation
Given I define a review template
When I save it
Then it is available for assignment

Additional Details:
- Priority: Medium
- Story Points: 2
- Dependencies: Employee Master Data, RBAC
- Notes: Goals, competencies, ratings

---

Title: Assign Reviews to Employees

User Story: As a supervisor I want to assign reviews to employees So that performance is evaluated

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Review assignment
Given a template exists
When I assign it to employees
Then they are notified

Additional Details:
- Priority: Medium
- Story Points: 1
- Dependencies: Create Review Templates
- Notes: Notification sent

---

Title: Submit and Acknowledge Reviews

User Story: As an employee I want to submit and acknowledge reviews So that feedback is documented

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Review submission
Given a review is assigned
When I submit my self-assessment
Then my supervisor is notified

Additional Details:
- Priority: Medium
- Story Points: 2
- Dependencies: Assign Reviews to Employees
- Notes: Acknowledgement workflow

---

Title: PDF Export of Reviews

User Story: As an HR user I want to export reviews as PDF So that records are archived

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: PDF export
Given a review is complete
When I export it
Then a PDF is generated

Additional Details:
- Priority: Low
- Story Points: 1
- Dependencies: Submit and Acknowledge Reviews
- Notes: Formatted PDF

---

Title: Role-Based Visibility and Immutable History

User Story: As a compliance officer I want role-based visibility and immutable history for reviews So that integrity is maintained

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Immutable history
Given a review is signed off
When I view it
Then no edits are allowed

Additional Details:
- Priority: Medium
- Story Points: 1
- Dependencies: Submit and Acknowledge Reviews
- Notes: Audit trail
