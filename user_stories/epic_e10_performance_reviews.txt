## User Story 1: Create Review Templates
**User Story:**  
As an HR user, I want to define quarterly and annual review templates with goals and competencies.

**Acceptance Criteria (Gherkin):**  
Given the review module  
When I create/edit/delete a template  
Then it is available for assignment

**Additional Details:**  
- Priority: Medium  
- Story Points: 2  
- Dependencies: E02, E03  
- Notes: Templates versioned

---

## User Story 2: Assign Reviews to Employees
**User Story:**  
As a supervisor, I want to assign reviews to employees so that performance is tracked.

**Acceptance Criteria (Gherkin):**  
Given a review template  
When I assign it to an employee  
Then the review cycle is started

**Additional Details:**  
- Priority: Medium  
- Story Points: 2  
- Dependencies: E10-1  
- Notes: Assignment triggers notification

---

## User Story 3: Submit and Acknowledge Reviews
**User Story:**  
As an employee and supervisor, I want to submit and acknowledge reviews so that feedback is documented.

**Acceptance Criteria (Gherkin):**  
Given an assigned review  
When both parties submit and acknowledge  
Then the review is marked complete and locked

**Additional Details:**  
- Priority: Medium  
- Story Points: 2  
- Dependencies: E10-2  
- Notes: Immutable after sign-off

---

## User Story 4: Export Reviews as PDF
**User Story:**  
As an HR user, I want to export completed reviews as PDF for record-keeping.

**Acceptance Criteria (Gherkin):**  
Given a completed review  
When I request export  
Then a PDF is generated with all details

**Additional Details:**  
- Priority: Low  
- Story Points: 1  
- Dependencies: E10-3  
- Notes: PDF includes signatures

---

## User Story 5: Role-Based Review Visibility
**User Story:**  
As a user, I want to see only reviews relevant to my role (self, direct reports, HR) for privacy.

**Acceptance Criteria (Gherkin):**  
Given logged-in user  
When accessing reviews  
Then only authorized reviews are visible

**Additional Details:**  
- Priority: Medium  
- Story Points: 1  
- Dependencies: E10-2  
- Notes: RBAC enforced
