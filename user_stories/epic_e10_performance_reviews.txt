Epic E10: Performance Reviews & Goals

---

Title: Create Performance Review Templates

User Story: As an HR manager I want to create quarterly and annual review templates So that performance evaluations are standardized

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Creating a review template
Given I am on the review management page
When I define competencies, ratings, and comments
Then the template is saved for future reviews

Additional Details:
- Priority: Medium
- Story Points: 3
- Dependencies: E02, E03
- Notes: Support multiple review cycles

---

Title: Track Employee Goals and Competencies

User Story: As a supervisor I want to track goals and competencies for employees So that development and progress are monitored

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Adding a goal
Given an employee profile
When I add a goal and target date
Then the goal is tracked and progress updated

Additional Details:
- Priority: Medium
- Story Points: 3
- Dependencies: E02, E03
- Notes: Goals visible to employee and supervisor

---

Title: Supervisor and Employee Acknowledgements

User Story: As a supervisor I want both parties to acknowledge performance reviews So that feedback is documented and agreed upon

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Review acknowledgement
Given a completed review
When both supervisor and employee acknowledge
Then the review is finalized and stored

Additional Details:
- Priority: Medium
- Story Points: 3
- Dependencies: E02, E03
- Notes: Digital signatures supported
