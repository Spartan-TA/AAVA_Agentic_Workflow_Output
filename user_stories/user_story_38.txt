Title: View Safety Incident History

User Story: As a manager I want to view the history of safety incidents So that I can identify trends and take preventive measures

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Safety incident history viewing
Given safety incidents are reported
When I access the incident history dashboard
Then I should see a list of incidents with details and status

Additional Details:
- Priority: Medium
- Story Points: 2
- Dependencies: Incident reporting
- Notes: Support for filtering by date, type, and location
