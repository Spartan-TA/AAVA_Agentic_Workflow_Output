Title: Submit and Acknowledge Performance Review

User Story: As a supervisor I want to submit performance reviews and have employees acknowledge them so that the review process is documented and transparent

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Submit and acknowledge review
  Given a review cycle is active
  When I submit a completed review and the employee acknowledges it
  Then the review is marked as complete and the history is locked

Additional Details:
- Priority: Medium
- Story Points: 3
- Dependencies: E02 (User Management), E03 (Scheduling)
- Notes: Reviews are immutable after acknowledgement
