Title: HR Review and Finalize Performance Results

User Story: As an HR specialist I want to review and finalize all submitted performance reviews so that results are consistent and ready for record-keeping and compensation decisions

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: HR finalizes review cycle
Given all manager and employee reviews are submitted
When I review the results and approve the cycle
Then the results should be locked, notifications sent, and data archived for reporting

Additional Details:
- Priority: Medium
- Story Points: 2
- Dependencies: Review cycle management, Notification service
- Notes: Allow HR to send feedback or request revisions before finalization
