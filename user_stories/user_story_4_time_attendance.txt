Title: Time & Attendance (Clock In/Out) - Clock In Endpoint

User Story: As a worker, I want to clock in via an endpoint with geofence and device capture so that attendance is accurately recorded.

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Clock in event
Given valid badgeId and location
When I POST to /attendance/clock-in
Then my clock-in is recorded with device and geofence info

Additional Details:
- Priority: High
- Story Points: 3
- Dependencies: Employee CRUD, RBAC
- Notes: Optional geofence
