Title: Automated Onboarding from HRIS and Task Generation

User Story: As an HR admin I want new hires from HRIS to be onboarded automatically with accounts, initial schedules, and required training tasks so that onboarding is efficient and consistent

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Automated onboarding workflow
  Given a new hire appears in the HRIS
  When the onboarding workflow is triggered
  Then the user account is provisioned, an initial schedule is assigned, and training/asset tasks are generated

Additional Details:
- Priority: Medium
- Story Points: 5
- Dependencies: E02, E03, E05, E07, E09, E13
- Notes: All onboarding steps must be tracked and auditable.
