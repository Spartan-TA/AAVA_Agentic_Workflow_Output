Title: WMS Department/Location Sync and Webhooks

User Story: As a warehouse manager I want department and location data to sync from the WMS and receive webhooks for key events so that the EMS reflects the latest operational structure

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: WMS sync and webhook delivery
  Given departments or locations are updated in the WMS
  When the sync job or webhook fires
  Then the EMS updates department/location data and triggers downstream events

Additional Details:
- Priority: High
- Story Points: 2
- Dependencies: E01 (Core), E02 (User Management), E03 (Scheduling)
- Notes: Webhooks must be idempotent and documented
