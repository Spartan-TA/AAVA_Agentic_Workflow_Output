Title: Mobile Clock-In/Out

User Story: As an employee I want to clock in and out using my mobile device So that attendance is tracked conveniently

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Mobile clock-in
Given I am scheduled for a shift
When I open the mobile app and clock in
Then my attendance should be recorded in the system

Additional Details:
- Priority: High
- Story Points: 3
- Dependencies: Mobile app, time tracking
- Notes: Support geolocation and offline mode.
