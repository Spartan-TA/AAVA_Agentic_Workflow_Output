Title: Audit and Export of Onboarding/Offboarding Actions

User Story:
As a compliance officer, I want all onboarding and offboarding actions to be logged and exportable so that I can demonstrate compliance and investigate lifecycle changes.

Acceptance Criteria:
- Scenario: All onboarding/offboarding actions are logged
  Given onboarding or offboarding occurs
  When the audit log is reviewed
  Then all actions (provisioning, training, asset assignment, access revocation) are traceable by user and timestamp

- Scenario: Export onboarding/offboarding log
  Given logs exist
  When the officer exports the log
  Then all relevant actions and details are included in the export

Additional Details:
- Export must be available in CSV and PDF formats
- Logs must be filterable by date, user, and action type
- Exported data must match internal records
