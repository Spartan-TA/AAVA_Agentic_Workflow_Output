Title: Clock-In/Out Event Endpoints with Validation and Geofence/Device Capture

User Story: As a warehouse worker I want to clock in and out using a secure endpoint that validates my location and device So that my attendance is accurately recorded and compliant with company policy

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Successful clock-in
Given I am within the warehouse geofence and using a registered device
When I POST to /attendance/clock-in with valid credentials
Then my clock-in event is recorded with timestamp, location, and device info

Scenario: Clock-in attempt outside geofence
Given I am outside the warehouse geofence
When I attempt to clock in
Then the system rejects the request with an error message

Additional Details:
- Priority: High
- Story Points: 5
- Dependencies: E02, E03
- Notes: Device registration and geofence validation must be enforced for all clock events.
