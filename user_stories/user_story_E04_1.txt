Title: Capture Clock-In/Out Event with Geofence and Device

User Story: As a worker I want to clock in and out using my mobile device within a geofenced area So that my attendance is accurately recorded and verified

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Successful clock-in with geofence
Given I am within the designated worksite geofence
When I use my device to clock in
Then my clock-in event is recorded with location and device details

Additional Details:
- Priority: High
- Story Points: 3
- Dependencies: E02, E03
- Notes: Prevent clock-in/out outside geofence; log device ID.
