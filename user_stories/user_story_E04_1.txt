Title: Clock-In/Out with Geofence Validation

User Story: As a Warehouse Worker I want to clock in and out only when I am within the designated geofence so that attendance records are accurate and compliant.

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Clock-in within geofence
Given I am physically present within the warehouse geofence
When I attempt to clock in
Then my attendance is recorded successfully

Scenario: Clock-in outside geofence
Given I am outside the warehouse geofence
When I attempt to clock in
Then the system denies the clock-in and displays an error message

Additional Details:
- Priority: High
- Story Points: 5
- Dependencies: Geolocation service, Attendance module
- Notes: Geofence boundaries must be configurable by ADMIN.
