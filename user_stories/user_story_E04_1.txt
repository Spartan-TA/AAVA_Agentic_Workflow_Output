Title: Record Employee Clock-In

User Story: As an employee I want to record my clock-in time So that my attendance is accurately tracked

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Employee clock-in
Given I am scheduled to work
When I clock in via the time & attendance module
Then my clock-in time should be recorded and visible to HR

Additional Details:
- Priority: High
- Story Points: 2
- Dependencies: Time & attendance module
- Notes: Support multiple clock-in methods (web, mobile, kiosk).
