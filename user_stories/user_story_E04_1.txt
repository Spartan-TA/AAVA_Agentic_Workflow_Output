Title: Record Employee Clock-In

User Story: As an employee I want to clock in at the start of my shift So that my attendance is accurately tracked

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Employee clock-in
Given I am scheduled for a shift
When I clock in using the system
Then my attendance should be recorded with timestamp

Additional Details:
- Priority: High
- Story Points: 2
- Dependencies: Shift schedule
- Notes: Support mobile and desktop clock-in
