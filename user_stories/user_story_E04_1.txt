Title: Employee Clock-In

User Story: As a warehouse worker I want to clock in at the start of my shift so that my working hours are accurately recorded for payroll and compliance

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Successful clock-in
Given I am scheduled for a shift and at the warehouse location
When I submit a clock-in event via the app
Then my attendance record should show the clock-in time and associate it with my shift

Additional Details:
- Priority: High
- Story Points: 2
- Dependencies: E02, E03
- Notes: Geofence validation optional; device ID captured for audit.
