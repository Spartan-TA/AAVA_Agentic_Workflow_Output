# Epic E15: Reporting & Analytics

## User Story 1: Operational Reports for Attendance, Overtime, Leave
**Title:** Generate Operational Reports  
**Narrative:** As a manager, I want reports for attendance, overtime, and leave balances so that staffing decisions are data-driven.  
**Acceptance Criteria (Gherkin):**
```
Given attendance and leave data
When I generate a report
Then metrics for attendance, overtime, and leave are included
```
**Priority:** Medium  
**Story Points:** 3  
**Dependencies:** E04, E05, E06  
**Notes:** Support filtering by date, department, shift.
