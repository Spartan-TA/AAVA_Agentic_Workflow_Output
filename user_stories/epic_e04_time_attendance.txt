Epic E04: Time & Attendance (Clock In/Out)

---

Title: Employee Clock-In with Geofence Validation

User Story: As a worker I want to clock in only when within the designated geofence So that attendance is accurately tracked and location compliance is enforced

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Successful clock-in within geofence
Given I am within the warehouse geofence
When I submit a clock-in request
Then my attendance is recorded

Scenario: Clock-in attempt outside geofence
Given I am outside the geofence
When I try to clock in
Then I receive an error and attendance is not recorded

Additional Details:
- Priority: High
- Story Points: 5
- Dependencies: E02, E03
- Notes: Geofence coordinates configurable per site

---

Title: Capture Device Information on Clock-In/Out

User Story: As a supervisor I want device information captured during clock-in/out So that I can audit attendance and detect fraudulent activity

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Device info logged on clock-in
Given I clock in using my mobile device
When my attendance is recorded
Then the device ID and type are stored with the record

Additional Details:
- Priority: High
- Story Points: 3
- Dependencies: E02, E03
- Notes: Device info includes device ID, type, and OS

---

Title: Calculate Hours Worked Per Shift

User Story: As an HR manager I want total hours worked calculated from clock-in/out records So that payroll and overtime can be accurately determined

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Standard shift calculation
Given valid clock-in and clock-out times
When I view the attendance report
Then the total hours worked are displayed

Scenario: Missed punch correction
Given a missed clock-out
When a correction is submitted and approved
Then the hours worked are updated accordingly

Additional Details:
- Priority: High
- Story Points: 5
- Dependencies: E02, E03
- Notes: Handle overnight and split shifts
