EPIC E04: TIME & ATTENDANCE (CLOCK IN/OUT)

===========================================

Title: Time & Attendance (Clock In/Out) - Clock In
User Story: As a worker, I want to clock in with device and geofence capture so that attendance is accurate.
Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Clock In
Given a worker at location
When POST /attendance/clock-in
Then event is validated and shift association is automatic.
Additional Details:
- Priority: High
- Story Points: 3
- Dependencies: E02, E03
- Notes: Geofence optional.

===========================================

Title: Time & Attendance (Clock In/Out) - Clock Out
User Story: As a worker, I want to clock out so that hours worked are recorded.
Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Clock Out
Given a worker

Additional Details:
- Priority: High
- Story Points: 2
- Dependencies: E02, E03
- Notes: Shift association.
