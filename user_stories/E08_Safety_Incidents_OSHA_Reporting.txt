## E08 - Safety Incidents & OSHA Reporting

### Story 1: Record Safety Incident
- **User Story:** As a Worker, I want to report safety incidents or near-misses so that hazards are documented.
- **Acceptance Criteria:**
    ```
    Given a valid incident report (severity, location, description)
    When I POST to '/safety/incidents'
    Then the incident is recorded with status 'Open'
    ```
- **Additional Details:**  
    Priority: High  
    Story Points: 3  
    Dependencies: E02, E03  
    Notes: Validation required.

### Story 2: Incident Investigation Workflow
- **User Story:** As a Safety Officer, I want to manage the investigation workflow for incidents so that corrective actions are tracked.
- **Acceptance Criteria:**
    ```
    Given an open incident
    When I update the status to 'Investigating' or 'Resolved'
    Then the workflow state is updated and actions are logged
    ```
- **Additional Details:**  
    Priority: High  
    Story Points: 4  
    Dependencies: E02, E03  
    Notes: Status transitions enforced.

### Story 3: OSHA Summary Export
- **User Story:** As an Admin, I want to export OSHA 300/300A summary reports so that regulatory requirements are met.
- **Acceptance Criteria:**
    ```
    Given incident records
    When I export OSHA summaries
    Then I receive files with all required fields
    ```
- **Additional Details:**  
    Priority: Medium  
    Story Points: 2  
    Dependencies: E02, E03  
    Notes: Format per OSHA guidelines.

### Story 4: Safety Metrics Dashboard
- **User Story:** As a Supervisor, I want to view safety KPIs on a dashboard so that I can monitor trends.
- **Acceptance Criteria:**
    ```
    Given incident data
    When I access the dashboard endpoint
    Then I see metrics such as incident rate, days since last incident, etc.
    ```
- **Additional Details:**  
    Priority: Medium  
    Story Points: 3  
    Dependencies: E02, E03  
    Notes: Role-based access.
