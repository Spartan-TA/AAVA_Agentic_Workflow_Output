[E08] Safety Incidents & OSHA Reporting

Title: Record Safety Incidents and Near-Misses
User Story: As a safety officer I want to record incidents and near-misses with details So that safety data is captured for analysis.
Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Incident recording
Given the safety module is available
When I submit an incident report
Then severity, location, description, and involved employees are stored

Additional Details:
Priority: High
Story Points: 3
Dependencies: E02, E03
Notes: Validate required fields.

---

Title: Workflow for Investigation and Corrective Actions
User Story: As a safety officer I want a workflow for investigating incidents and tracking corrective actions So that issues are resolved.
Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Investigation workflow
Given an incident is recorded
When investigation status changes
Then the workflow updates from Open to Investigating to Resolved

Additional Details:
Priority: High
Story Points: 3
Dependencies: Record Safety Incidents and Near-Misses
Notes: Track timestamps and responsible users.
