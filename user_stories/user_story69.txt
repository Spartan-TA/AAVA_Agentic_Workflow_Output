Title: Mobile Clock-In/Out with Offline Queue

User Story:
As a warehouse worker, I want to clock in and out from my mobile device, even when offline, so that my attendance is accurately recorded and synced when connectivity is restored.

Acceptance Criteria:
- Scenario: Clock-in/out while online
  Given the worker is online
  When they clock in or out
  Then the event is immediately recorded and synced

- Scenario: Clock-in/out while offline
  Given the worker is offline
  When they clock in or out
  Then the event is queued locally and synced when back online

- Scenario: Conflict resolution on sync
  Given conflicting events are queued
  When the device syncs
  Then the user is prompted to resolve conflicts

Additional Details:
- Offline queue must be encrypted and tamper-evident
- All sync and conflict actions must be logged
- Mobile UI must indicate sync status
