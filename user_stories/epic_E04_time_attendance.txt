EPIC E04: TIME & ATTENDANCE (CLOCK IN/OUT)

===========================================

Title: Time & Attendance - Clock In
User Story: As a worker, I want to clock in with device and optional geofence capture so that my attendance is accurately recorded.
Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Clock in event
  Given I am at the warehouse
  When I POST to /attendance/clock-in
  Then my clock-in is recorded with device and location (if enabled)

Additional Details:
Priority: High
Story Points: 3
Dependencies: E02, E03
Notes: Validate against shift schedule.

---

Title: Time & Attendance - Clock Out
User Story: As a worker, I want to clock out so that my work hours are tracked.
Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Clock out event
  Given I am clocked in
  When I POST to /attendance/clock-out
  Then my clock-out is recorded and hours are calculated

Additional Details:
Priority: High
Story Points: 2
Dependencies: E02, E03
Notes: Handle missed punches gracefully.

---

Title: Time & Attendance - Missed Punch Correction
User Story: As a worker, I want to request a correction for missed punches so that my attendance is accurate.
Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Missed punch correction
  Given I missed a clock event
  When I submit a correction request
  Then an approval task is created for my supervisor

Additional Details:
Priority: High
Story Points: 3
Dependencies: E02, E03
Notes: Track correction status.

---

Title: Time & Attendance - Attendance Reports
User Story: As a supervisor, I want to export attendance reports in CSV so that I can review and share attendance data.
Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Export attendance report
  Given attendance data exists
  When I request a report export
  Then a CSV file is generated with daily totals

Additional Details:
Priority: High
Story Points: 2
Dependencies: E02, E03
Notes: Include filters for date and employee.

---

Title: Time & Attendance - Shift Association
User Story: As a system, I want to automatically associate clock events with scheduled shifts so that hours worked are calculated correctly.
Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Automatic shift association
  Given a clock-in/out event
  When the event is processed
  Then it is linked to the correct shift

Additional Details:
Priority: High
Story Points: 2
Dependencies: E02, E03
Notes: Handle overnight and split shifts.
