## E12 - Notifications & Announcements

### Story 1: In-App and Email/SMS Notifications
- **User Story:** As a Worker, I want to receive notifications for shift changes, expiring certs, and approvals so that I stay informed.
- **Acceptance Criteria:**
    ```
    Given a shift change or expiring cert
    When the event occurs
    Then I receive a notification via my chosen channels
    ```
- **Additional Details:**  
    Priority: Medium  
    Story Points: 4  
    Dependencies: E03, E05, E06, E07  
    Notes: Channel preferences per user.

### Story 2: Notification Preferences and Quiet Hours
- **User Story:** As a Worker, I want to configure notification preferences and quiet hours so that I am not disturbed outside work hours.
- **Acceptance Criteria:**
    ```
    Given notification settings
    When I update my preferences
    Then only selected channels are used

    Given quiet hours are set
    When a notification is triggered during quiet hours
    Then delivery is delayed until allowed
    ```
- **Additional Details:**  
    Priority: Medium  
    Story Points: 3  
    Dependencies: E03, E05, E06, E07  
    Notes: Quiet hours per user.

### Story 3: Announcement Dashboard Widget
- **User Story:** As a Worker, I want to see company announcements on my dashboard so that I am aware of important updates.
- **Acceptance Criteria:**
    ```
    Given active announcements
    When I log in
    Then I see all current announcements on my dashboard
    ```
- **Additional Details:**  
    Priority: Low  
    Story Points: 2  
    Dependencies: E03, E05, E06, E07  
    Notes: Announcements managed by Admin.

### Story 4: Notification Delivery Status Tracking
- **User Story:** As an Admin, I want to track the delivery status of notifications so that I can troubleshoot issues.
- **Acceptance Criteria:**
    ```
    Given sent notifications
    When I view the delivery log
    Then I see status (delivered, failed, pending) for each notification
    ```
- **Additional Details:**  
    Priority: Low  
    Story Points: 2  
    Dependencies: E03, E05, E06, E07  
    Notes: Rate limits applied.
