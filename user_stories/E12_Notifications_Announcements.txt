EPIC E12: Notifications & Announcements

---

Title: Notifications - Notification Preferences
User Story: As a WORKER I want to opt-in/out of notification channels So that I control how I am notified.
Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Manage notification preferences
Given I am logged in
When I update my preferences
Then notifications are sent only via selected channels
Additional Details:
- Priority: Medium
- Story Points: 2
- Dependencies: E03, E05, E06, E07
- Notes: In-app, email, SMS supported

---

Title: Notifications - Localized Templates
User Story: As a System I want to send notifications using localized templates So that users receive messages in their preferred language.
Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Send localized notification
Given a user has a preferred language
When a notification is sent
Then the message uses the correct template
Additional Details:
- Priority: Medium
- Story Points: 3
- Dependencies: E03, E05, E06, E07
- Notes: Templates managed by locale

---

Title: Notifications - Delivery Status Tracking
User Story: As an ADMIN I want to track notification delivery status So that I can troubleshoot delivery issues.
Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Track notification delivery
Given notifications are sent
When I view delivery status
Then I see sent, delivered, failed statuses
Additional Details:
- Priority: Medium
- Story Points: 2
- Dependencies: E03, E05, E06, E07
- Notes: Rate limits applied

---

Title: Notifications - Announcements Dashboard
User Story: As a SUPERVISOR I want to post announcements visible to my team So that important updates are communicated.
Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Post announcement
Given I am a supervisor
When I post an announcement
Then it is visible on the dashboard to my team
Additional Details:
- Priority: Medium
- Story Points: 2
- Dependencies: E03, E05, E06, E07
- Notes: Announcements expire after configurable period
