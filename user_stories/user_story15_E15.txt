### E15 - Reporting & Analytics

**Title:** Attendance & Overtime Reports
User Story: As an HR user I want attendance and overtime reports So that staffing and compliance are monitored
Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Filter by date/department
Given attendance data
When I filter reports
Then only matching records are shown
Scenario: Export report
Given report data
When I export
Then CSV/PDF is generated within 10s for up to 50k rows
Additional Details:
- Priority: Medium
- Story Points: 4
- Dependencies: E04, E05, E06, E07, E08, E14
- Notes: Access controlled
