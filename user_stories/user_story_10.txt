Title: Support Soft-Delete and Employee Status Management

User Story: As an HR Manager I want to soft-delete employees and manage their status So that historical records are preserved and inactive employees are not shown in active lists

Acceptance Criteria:

Scenario: Soft-delete employee
Given an employee exists
When I DELETE /employees/{id}
Then the employee's status is set to 'Inactive' and record is not removed from the database

Scenario: Exclude inactive employees from active lists
Given some employees are inactive
When I GET /employees?status=Active
Then only active employees are returned

Scenario: Restore employee
Given an employee is inactive
When I PATCH /employees/{id} with status 'Active'
Then the employee is restored to active status

Additional Details:
- Priority: High
- Story Points: 3
- Dependencies: E01, E02
- Notes: Ensure audit trail is maintained for status changes and deletions.
