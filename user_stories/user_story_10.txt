Title: User Authentication - Logout
User Story: As a logged-in user I want to log out of my account So that I can secure my session when finished
Acceptance Criteria: 
Scenario: Successful logout
Given I am logged in
When I click the logout button
Then my session is terminated and I am redirected to the login page
Additional Details: 
Priority: High
Story Points: 1
Dependencies: Authentication module
Notes: Clear all session data on logout
