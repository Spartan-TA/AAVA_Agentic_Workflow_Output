Title: Enforce Row-Level Security for Employee Data

User Story: As a Supervisor I want to access only the employee records for my team So that I cannot view or modify data outside my scope of responsibility.

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Supervisor accesses team records
Given I am authenticated as SUPERVISOR
When I GET /employees
Then only records for my assigned team are returned

Scenario: Supervisor attempts to access other teams
Given I am authenticated as SUPERVISOR
When I GET /employees for another team
Then I am denied with a 403 error

Additional Details:
- Priority: High
- Story Points: 3
- Dependencies: Employee domain, Role-based endpoint security
- Notes: Row-level constraints must be enforced in all relevant queries.
