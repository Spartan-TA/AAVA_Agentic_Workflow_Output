Title: View Employee List

User Story: As an HR manager I want to view a list of all employees So that I can manage and review staff information easily

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Employee list viewing
Given I am on the employee management screen
When I open the employee list
Then I should see all current employees with key details displayed

Additional Details:
- Priority: High
- Story Points: 2
- Dependencies: Employee record creation
- Notes: Support search and filter options
