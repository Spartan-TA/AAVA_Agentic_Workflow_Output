Title: Enforce Row-Level Security for Supervisors

User Story: As a supervisor I want to access only the records of my assigned team So that I cannot view or modify data outside my scope

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Supervisor data access
Given a supervisor is logged in
When they attempt to view employee records
Then only records for their team are visible and editable

Additional Details:
- Priority: High
- Story Points: 3
- Dependencies: Employee Master Data
- Notes: Team assignments must be up-to-date for correct filtering.
