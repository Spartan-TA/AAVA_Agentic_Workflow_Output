EPIC E05: SHIFT & SCHEDULE MANAGEMENT

===========================================

Title: Create Recurring Shift Templates

User Story: As a scheduler I want to create recurring shift templates So that I can efficiently assign shifts for multiple weeks

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Creating a recurring shift template
Given I am on the shift management page
When I select "Create Template" and define recurrence rules
Then the system should save the template and display it in the template list

Additional Details:
- Priority: High
- Story Points: 5
- Dependencies: Employee Master Data
- Notes: Support daily, weekly, and custom recurrence

===========================================

Title: Assign Shift Rotations

User Story: As a supervisor I want to assign shift rotations to teams So that coverage is maintained and fatigue is minimized

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Assigning shift rotation
Given a shift template exists
When I assign it to a team for a defined period
Then the system should update the schedule and notify affected employees

Additional Details:
- Priority: High
- Story Points: 3
- Dependencies: Notifications & Announcements
- Notes: Include rotation preview before confirmation

===========================================

Title: Configure Overtime Rules

User Story: As an admin I want to configure overtime rules So that overtime is calculated accurately for payroll

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Setting overtime rules
Given I am in the schedule settings
When I define overtime thresholds and rates
Then overtime should be flagged in the schedule and exported for payroll

Additional Details:
- Priority: Medium
- Story Points: 3
- Dependencies: Payroll Export Integration
- Notes: Support state and federal compliance

===========================================

Title: View Shift Coverage Calendar

User Story: As a manager I want to view a calendar of shift coverage So that I can identify gaps and overlaps

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Viewing shift coverage
Given shifts are assigned
When I open the coverage calendar
Then I should see color-coded coverage and alerts for gaps

Additional Details:
- Priority: Medium
- Story Points: 2
- Dependencies: Time & Attendance
- Notes: Export calendar as PDF
