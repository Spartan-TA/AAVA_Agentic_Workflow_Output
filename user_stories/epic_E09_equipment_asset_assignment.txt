EPIC E09: EQUIPMENT & ASSET ASSIGNMENT

===========================================

Title: Assign Equipment to Employee

User Story: As a supervisor I want to assign equipment to employees So that asset usage is tracked

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Assigning equipment
Given equipment is available
When I assign it to an employee
Then the assignment should be logged and visible in their profile

Additional Details:
- Priority: High
- Story Points: 2
- Dependencies: Employee Master Data
- Notes: Support bulk assignment

===========================================

Title: Track PPE Issuance

User Story: As a safety officer I want to track PPE issuance So that compliance is maintained

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Tracking PPE
Given PPE inventory is available
When I issue PPE to an employee
Then the issuance should be logged and tracked for expiration

Additional Details:
- Priority: Medium
- Story Points: 2
- Dependencies: Training & Certification Tracking
- Notes: Notify for PPE replacement

===========================================

Title: Manage Equipment Inventory

User Story: As an admin I want to manage equipment inventory So that assets are accounted for

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Managing inventory
Given equipment is added or removed
When I update inventory records
Then the system should reflect current asset status

Additional Details:
- Priority: Medium
- Story Points: 3
- Dependencies: Reporting & Analytics
- Notes: Support barcode scanning

===========================================

Title: Reassign Equipment

User Story: As a supervisor I want to reassign equipment between employees So that asset allocation remains optimal

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Reassigning equipment
Given equipment is assigned
When I select "Reassign" and choose a new employee
Then the assignment should update and notify both parties

Additional Details:
- Priority: Low
- Story Points: 2
- Dependencies: Notifications & Announcements
- Notes: Log reassignment history
