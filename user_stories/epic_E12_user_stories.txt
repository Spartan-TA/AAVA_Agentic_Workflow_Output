### E12: Notifications & Announcements

**User Story 1: In-App and Email/SMS Notifications**
User Story: As a worker I want to receive notifications for shift changes and approvals So that I am always informed
Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Receive notification
Given a shift is changed
When the change is approved
Then I receive a notification via my preferred channel
Additional Details:
- Priority: Medium
- Story Points: 2
- Dependencies: E03, E05, E06, E07
- Notes: Opt-in/out per channel.

**User Story 2: Localized Notification Templates**
User Story: As an admin I want notification templates to be localized So that users receive messages in their language
Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Localized delivery
Given my profile language is Spanish
When I receive a notification
Then the message is in Spanish
Additional Details:
- Priority: Medium
- Story Points: 1
- Dependencies: E03, E05, E06, E07
- Notes: Support for at least 2 languages.

**User Story 3: Announcements Dashboard Widget**
User Story: As a worker I want to see company announcements on my dashboard So that I stay up to date
Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: View announcement
Given an active announcement
When I log in
Then the announcement is visible on my dashboard
Additional Details:
- Priority: Medium
- Story Points: 1
- Dependencies: E03, E05, E06, E07
- Notes: Rate limits applied.
