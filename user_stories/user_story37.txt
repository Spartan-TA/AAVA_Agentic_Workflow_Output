Title: Approve or Deny Leave Requests

User Story: As a supervisor I want to approve or deny employee leave requests So that I can manage team coverage and ensure policy compliance

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Supervisor reviews leave request
Given I have received a leave request notification
When I review the request details and employee accrual balance
Then I can approve or deny the request and the employee is notified

Additional Details:
- Priority: Medium
- Story Points: 2
- Dependencies: E02, E03, E05
- Notes: Approval/denial actions should update the leave status and trigger notifications.
