Title: Asset Assignment History

User Story:
As an HR administrator, I want to view the assignment history of each asset so that I can track usage, identify patterns, and ensure accountability.

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario 1: View asset history
  Given an asset exists in the inventory
  When I view the asset details
  Then I see a chronological list of all assignments and returns for that asset

Scenario 2: Filter history by employee
  Given I am viewing asset assignment history
  When I filter by employee name
  Then only assignments related to that employee are shown

Additional Details:
Priority: Medium
Story Points: 2
Dependencies: Asset inventory, assignment records
Notes: Should support export to CSV for audit purposes.
