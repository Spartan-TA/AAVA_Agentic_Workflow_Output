Title: Complete Employee Offboarding
User Story: As an HR manager I want to complete offboarding for departing employees So that access is revoked and exit procedures are followed
Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Offboarding completion
Given I am logged in as HR manager
When I mark an employee as departing and complete offboarding steps
Then the employee's system access should be revoked and exit checklist completed
Additional Details:
- Priority: High
- Story Points: 2
- Dependencies: Employee CRUD, Access Control
- Notes: Checklist items configurable per role.
