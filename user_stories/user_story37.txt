Title: Create Review Cycles and Templates

User Story: As an HR manager I want to create quarterly and annual performance review cycles and templates so that employee evaluations are standardized and scheduled.

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Review cycle and template creation
Given I am on the performance review setup page
When I create a new review cycle and template
Then the cycle and template are saved and available for assignment

Additional Details:
- Priority: Medium
- Story Points: 5
- Dependencies: E02, E03
- Notes: Templates support goals, competencies, and rating scales.
