Title: Employee Clock In/Out

User Story: As an employee I want to clock in and out of my shift So that my attendance is accurately tracked

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Successful clock in/out
Given I am logged into the EMS
When I clock in or out
Then my attendance record should be updated with timestamp and status

Additional Details:
- Priority: High
- Story Points: 5
- Dependencies: Attendance module
- Notes: Support for mobile and desktop clock in/out.
