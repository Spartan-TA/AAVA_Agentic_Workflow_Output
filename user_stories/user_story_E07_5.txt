Title: Display Certification Status on Employee Profile

User Story: As an employee I want to see my certification status on my profile So that I am aware of my current qualifications and expirations

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Viewing certification status
Given I am on my profile page
When I view the certifications section
Then I should see all active, expired, and pending certifications

Additional Details:
- Priority: Medium
- Story Points: 2
- Dependencies: Employee profile UI
- Notes: Include color-coded status and renewal links.
