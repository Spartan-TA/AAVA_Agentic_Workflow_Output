EPIC E09 - Equipment & Asset Assignment

===========================================

User Story 1: Asset Registry CRUD
---------------------------------------------
Title: Manage Asset Registry (CRUD)

User Story: As an asset manager, I want to add, update, and delete assets so that equipment inventory is accurate.

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Asset management
  Given the asset endpoint
  When CRUD operations are performed
  Then assets are updated accordingly

Additional Details:
- Priority: Medium
- Story Points: 3
- Dependencies: E02, E03, E07
- Notes: Include asset condition.
