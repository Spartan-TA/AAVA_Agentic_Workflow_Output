Title: Attendance Clock In/Out
User Story: As a worker, I want to clock in and out with device and location capture so that my hours are accurately recorded.
Acceptance Criteria:
  Given I am at the warehouse,
  When I clock in/out,
  Then my event is recorded with timestamp, device, and (if enabled) geofence.

  Given I miss a punch,
  When I submit a correction,
  Then an approval task is created.

  Given a shift is ongoing,
  When I clock in/out,
  Then the event is associated with the correct shift.

Additional Details:
  Priority: High
  Story Points: 5
  Dependencies: Employee CRUD API, RBAC
  Notes: Reports exportable as CSV.
