### E09 - Equipment & Asset Assignment

**Title:** Assign Asset to Employee
**User Story:** As a supervisor, I want to assign equipment to employees so that asset usage is tracked.
**Acceptance Criteria:**
Scenario: Asset assignment
Given an available asset
When I assign it to an employee
Then the assignment is recorded and visible
**Additional Details:** Priority: Medium, Story Points: 2, Dependencies: E02, E03, E07, Notes: Block if certification invalid.

**Title:** Track Asset Check-In/Out
**User Story:** As an employee, I want to check out and return assets so that accountability is maintained.
**Acceptance Criteria:**
Scenario: Asset check-in/out
Given I have an assigned asset
When I check it in or out
Then the event is logged with timestamp
**Additional Details:** Priority: Medium, Story Points: 2, Dependencies: E02, E03, E07, Notes: Overdue returns flagged.

**Title:** Asset Condition Update
**User Story:** As a supervisor, I want to update asset condition so that maintenance needs are tracked.
**Acceptance Criteria:**
Scenario: Condition update
Given an asset is returned
When I update its condition
Then the new status is saved and visible
**Additional Details:** Priority: Medium, Story Points: 2, Dependencies: E02, E03, E07, Notes: History log per asset.
