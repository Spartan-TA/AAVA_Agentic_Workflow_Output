Title: Time & Attendance - Clock In/Out

User Story: As a worker, I want to clock in and out with geofence and device capture so that my attendance is accurately recorded.

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Clock In
Given a worker at the warehouse
When they POST /attendance/clock-in with location and device info
Then the event is recorded and validated

Scenario: Clock Out
Given a worker who has clocked in
When they POST /attendance/clock-out
Then the event is recorded and shift hours calculated

Additional Details:
- Priority: High
- Story Points: 5
- Dependencies: E02, E03
- Notes: Automatic shift association
