#### User Story 9: Row-Level Security for Supervisors
**As a** supervisor  
**I want** to access only my team's employee records  
**so that** data privacy is maintained.

**Acceptance Criteria:**
- **Given** a supervisor user,  
  **When** they list employees,  
  **Then** only their team's records are visible.

**Additional Details:**  
Priority: High  
Story Points: 2  
Dependencies: E02, E03  
Notes: Enforce row-level constraints in queries.
