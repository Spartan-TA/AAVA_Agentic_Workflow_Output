Title: Equipment & Asset Assignment

User Story: As an asset manager I want to assign and track equipment and PPE to employees so that accountability and safe equipment operation are ensured.

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Asset assignment and tracking
Given an asset registry
When I check out or return an asset to an employee
Then the system blocks assignment if required certifications are invalid
And maintains asset condition state
And logs history per asset and employee
And reports overdue returns

Additional Details:
- Priority: Medium
- Story Points: 5
- Dependencies: Employee Master Data (CRUD), Role-Based Access Control (RBAC), Training & Certification Tracking
- Notes: Support scanners, forklifts, PPE; CRUD for assets; check-in/out endpoints.
