Title: Equipment & Asset Assignment

User Story: As a warehouse supervisor I want to assign equipment and assets to employees So that accountability and safe operation are ensured

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Asset checkout
Given an employee with valid certification
When the employee checks out an asset
Then the asset is assigned and usage is tracked; blocks if certification is missing

Additional Details:
- Priority: Medium
- Story Points: 5
- Dependencies: Employee Master Data, RBAC, Training & Certification Tracking
- Notes: Asset registry CRUD; check-in/out endpoints; history log per asset and employee; overdue return reports
