Title: Track Employee Certifications

User Story: As an HR manager I want to record and track employee certifications so that compliance and qualification requirements are met

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Adding a certification
Given an employee profile exists
When I add a certification with type and expiration date
Then the certification is visible on the employee profile

Additional Details:
- Priority: High
- Story Points: 2
- Dependencies: Employee master data, RBAC
- Notes: Certification types include forklift, PPE, etc.

---

Title: Alert on Expiring Certifications

User Story: As a supervisor I want to receive alerts when certifications are about to expire so that renewals can be scheduled proactively

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Expiry alert
Given a certification is expiring in 30 or 7 days
When the system runs the alert job
Then an alert is sent to the supervisor and employee

Additional Details:
- Priority: High
- Story Points: 1
- Dependencies: Certification tracking
- Notes: Alerts must be visible in dashboard and notifications

---

Title: Block Assignment to Tasks Requiring Valid Certifications

User Story: As a scheduler I want to prevent assignment of employees to equipment-sensitive roles if their certifications are expired so that safety and compliance are enforced

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Assignment block
Given an employee's certification is expired
When I attempt to assign them to a forklift shift
Then the system blocks the assignment and notifies me

Additional Details:
- Priority: High
- Story Points: 2
- Dependencies: Shift management, Certification tracking
- Notes: Must be enforced for all equipment-sensitive tasks

---

Title: Upload Proof of Certification Documents

User Story: As an employee I want to upload proof documents for my certifications so that HR can verify my qualifications

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Uploading certification proof
Given I have a new certification
When I upload a document to my profile
Then HR can view and verify the document

Additional Details:
- Priority: High
- Story Points: 1
- Dependencies: Certification tracking
- Notes: Document storage must be secure
