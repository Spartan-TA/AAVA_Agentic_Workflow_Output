Title: Equipment & Asset Assignment

User Story: As a supervisor I want to assign and track equipment to employees so that asset accountability and safety are ensured.

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Asset check-out
Given an employee with valid certification
When I assign an asset
Then the assignment is logged, invalid certs block assignment, and overdue returns are reported

Additional Details:
- Priority: Medium
- Story Points: 3
- Dependencies: Employee Master Data, RBAC, Training & Certification Tracking
- Notes: Track asset condition and history per asset/employee
