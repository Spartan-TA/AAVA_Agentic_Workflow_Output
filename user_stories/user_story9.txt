Title: Soft-Delete Employee Record
User Story: As an HR user, I want to soft-delete an employee record so that terminated employees are not removed from history but are excluded from active lists.
Acceptance Criteria: Given I am an authenticated HR user, When I DELETE /employees/{id}, Then the employee's status is set to "inactive" or "deleted", And the record is excluded from active queries but retained in the database.
Additional Details: Priority: High, Story Points: 2, Dependencies: E01, Notes: Soft-delete only; no hard deletes.
