Title: Employee Views Schedule

User Story: As an employee I want to view my assigned shifts So that I can plan my availability and attendance

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Viewing schedule
Given I am logged into the system
When I navigate to the 'My Schedule' section
Then I see a calendar view of all my upcoming shifts

Additional Details:
- Priority: Medium
- Story Points: 2
- Dependencies: Shift & Schedule Management
- Notes: Display both current and future shifts, highlight any changes or updates.
