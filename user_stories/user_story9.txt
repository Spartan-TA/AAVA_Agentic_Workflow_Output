Title: Equipment & Asset Assignment

User Story: As an asset manager I want to assign and track warehouse equipment and PPE to employees so that asset accountability and safe operation are ensured.

Acceptance Criteria:

Scenario: Assign asset
Given an available asset and a qualified employee
When I check out the asset
Then the assignment is recorded and the employee's certification is validated

Scenario: Return asset
Given an asset is checked out
When it is returned
Then the asset's condition and history log are updated

Scenario: Block assignment for invalid certs
Given an employee with expired or missing certification
When I attempt to assign restricted equipment
Then the assignment is blocked

Scenario: Overdue report
Given assets are overdue for return
When I generate a report
Then all overdue assets are listed by employee

Additional Details:
- Priority: Medium
- Story Points: 5
- Dependencies: Employee Master Data (E02), Role-Based Access Control (E03), Training & Certification Tracking (E07)
- Notes: Track asset condition and maintain history log per asset and employee.
