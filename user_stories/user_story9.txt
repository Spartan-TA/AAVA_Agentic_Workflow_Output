Title: Implement Clock In/Out Endpoints

User Story: As an employee I want to clock in and out via the system So that my working hours are accurately recorded

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Clock in/out endpoints available
  Given I am authenticated
  When I call the clock in or clock out endpoint
  Then my attendance record should be updated accordingly

Additional Details:
- Priority: High
- Story Points: 3
- Dependencies: Employee entity, authentication
- Notes: Prevent duplicate clock-ins or outs within the same shift.
