Title: Equipment & Asset Assignment
User Story: As an asset manager I want to assign and track equipment and PPE to employees so that asset accountability and safe operation are ensured.
Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Asset assignment and tracking
Given I am authenticated as an asset manager
When I assign or check out equipment to an employee
Then the system validates required certifications
And blocks assignment if certifications are invalid
And maintains asset condition state
And history log is visible per asset and employee
And reports of overdue returns are available
Additional Details:
- Priority: Medium
- Story Points: 5
- Dependencies: Employee Master Data (CRUD), Role-Based Access Control (RBAC), Training & Certification Tracking
- Notes: Asset registry CRUD and check-in/out endpoints must be implemented.
