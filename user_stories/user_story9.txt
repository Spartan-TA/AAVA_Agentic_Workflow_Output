Title: Time & Attendance Clock-In/Out
User Story: As a warehouse worker, I want to clock in and out via API so that my working hours are accurately recorded.
Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Valid clock-in
Given my badgeId is valid
When I POST to /attendance/clock-in
Then my clock-in event is recorded and associated with my shift

Scenario: Valid clock-out
Given I am clocked in
When I POST to /attendance/clock-out
Then my clock-out event is recorded and hours are calculated

Scenario: Geofence validation
Given geofence is enabled
When I clock in outside the allowed area
Then the request is rejected

Additional Details:
- Priority: High
- Story Points: 5
- Dependencies: Employee CRUD, RBAC
- Notes: Device capture optional
