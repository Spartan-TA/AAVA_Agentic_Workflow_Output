Title: Offline Queue for Clock Events and Conflict Resolution

User Story: As a warehouse worker I want my clock-in/out events to be queued offline and resolved when reconnected So that my attendance is accurately recorded even without network access

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Offline clock event queue
Given I am offline and clock-in/out
When I reconnect to the network
Then my events are synced and any conflicts are resolved automatically

Additional Details:
- Priority: Medium
- Story Points: 5
- Dependencies: E04, E05, E06, E12
- Notes: Conflict resolution must notify user of any discrepancies.
