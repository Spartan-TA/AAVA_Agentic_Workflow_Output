### E06: Leave & Absence Management

**User Story 6.1**
Title: Employee Leave Request Submission

User Story: As an Employee I want to submit requests for PTO, sick, or unpaid leave So that my time off is accurately tracked and approved.

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Leave request submission
Given I am logged in as an Employee
When I navigate to the Leave Request form and submit a new leave request
Then the request is saved with a pending status and my supervisor is notified

Additional Details:
- Priority: Medium
- Story Points: 5
- Dependencies: E02, E03, E05
- Notes: Leave types and accruals must be validated

---

**User Story 6.2**
Title: Supervisor Leave Approval Workflow

User Story: As a Supervisor I want to review and approve or deny leave requests So that staffing levels are maintained.

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Leave approval
Given I am a Supervisor with pending leave requests
When I approve or deny a request
Then the employee is notified and the leave balance is updated accordingly

Additional Details:
- Priority: Medium
- Story Points: 3
- Dependencies: E02, E03, E05
- Notes: Approval/denial reasons must be captured

---

**User Story 6.3**
Title: Leave Accrual and Balance Management

User Story: As an HR Admin I want leave balances to update automatically based on approved requests and company policy So that records remain accurate.

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Balance update
Given a leave request is approved
When the leave period starts
Then the employee's leave balance is decremented per policy

Additional Details:
- Priority: Medium
- Story Points: 3
- Dependencies: E02, E03, E05
- Notes: Accrual rules configurable by HR

---

**User Story 6.4**
Title: Integration with Scheduling and Payroll

User Story: As a System I want approved leaves to automatically flag scheduled shifts and payroll hours So that coverage and pay are accurate.

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Leave integration
Given an employee has approved leave overlapping scheduled shifts
When the leave is approved
Then the affected shifts are flagged for coverage and payroll exports reflect the absence

Additional Details:
- Priority: Medium
- Story Points: 5
- Dependencies: E02, E03, E05
- Notes: Integration hooks for scheduling and payroll modules
