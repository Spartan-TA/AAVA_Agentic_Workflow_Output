Title: Retry Failed Payroll Exports and Maintain Audit Log

User Story: As a payroll administrator I want failed payroll exports to be retried automatically and every export logged So that delivery reliability and traceability are ensured

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Export retry and audit
Given a payroll export fails
When the system detects the failure
Then it retries delivery with exponential backoff and logs the attempt in the audit log

Additional Details:
- Priority: High
- Story Points: 5
- Dependencies: E04, E06
- Notes: Audit log must include timestamp, actor, and status for each export.
