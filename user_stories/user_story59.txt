Title: Automated Onboarding from HRIS

User Story: As an HR manager I want new hires to appear automatically from the HRIS So that onboarding tasks are triggered without manual entry

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: New hire onboarding
Given a new hire is created in the HRIS
When the sync job runs
Then the employee appears in the system and onboarding tasks are generated

Additional Details:
- Priority: Medium
- Story Points: 8
- Dependencies: E02, E03, E05, E07, E09, E13
- Notes: Tasks must include training and asset assignment
