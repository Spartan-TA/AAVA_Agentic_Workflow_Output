Title: Safety Incident Recording
User Story: As a safety officer I want to record safety incidents with severity and investigation workflow So that OSHA reporting is accurate
Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Incident recording
Given an incident occurs
When I log the incident details
Then the system should capture severity, investigation steps, and generate OSHA reports
Additional Details:
- Priority: High
- Story Points: 3
- Dependencies: Employee domain, RBAC
- Notes: Support attachments and workflow tracking
