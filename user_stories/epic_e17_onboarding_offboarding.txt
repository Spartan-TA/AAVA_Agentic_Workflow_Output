EPIC E17: ONBOARDING & OFFBOARDING WORKFLOW

===========================================

Title: Automated Account Provisioning for New Hires

User Story: As an HR admin I want new hire accounts to be provisioned automatically So that onboarding is efficient and error-free

Acceptance Criteria:

Scenario: New hire detected
Given a new hire appears from HRIS
When onboarding is triggered
Then user accounts and initial schedules are created

Additional Details:
- Priority: Medium
- Story Points: 3
- Dependencies: HRIS integration, scheduling module
- Notes: Tasks generated for training and asset assignment

---

Title: Training Assignment During Onboarding

User Story: As a supervisor I want required training tasks assigned to new employees So that compliance is ensured before work begins

Acceptance Criteria:

Scenario: Onboarding workflow
Given a new employee is onboarded
When onboarding tasks are generated
Then required training is assigned and tracked

Additional Details:
- Priority: Medium
- Story Points: 2
- Dependencies: Training module, onboarding workflow
- Notes: Certification status updated upon completion

---

Title: Asset Assignment for New Employees

User Story: As an asset manager I want assets (PPE, scanners) assigned to new hires during onboarding So that equipment is tracked from day one

Acceptance Criteria:

Scenario: Asset assignment
Given a new employee is onboarded
When onboarding tasks are generated
Then required assets are assigned and logged

Additional Details:
- Priority: Medium
- Story Points: 2
- Dependencies: Asset management, onboarding workflow
- Notes: Asset history log updated

---

Title: Automated Offboarding and Access Revocation

User Story: As an HR admin I want terminated employees to have access revoked and assets collected So that offboarding is secure and complete

Acceptance Criteria:

Scenario: Termination detected
Given an employee is terminated in HRIS
When offboarding is triggered
Then system access is revoked and asset collection tasks are generated

Additional Details:
- Priority: Medium
- Story Points: 3
- Dependencies: HRIS integration, asset management
- Notes: Schedules updated to remove terminated employees
