## User Story 1: Automated Provisioning for New Hires
**User Story:**  
As an HR user, I want new hires from HRIS to be provisioned with accounts, schedules, and training tasks.

**Acceptance Criteria (Gherkin):**  
Given a new hire in HRIS  
When synced  
Then an account, initial schedule, and training tasks are created

**Additional Details:**  
- Priority: Medium  
- Story Points: 2  
- Dependencies: E02, E03, E05, E07, E13  
- Notes: Welcome notification sent

---

## User Story 2: Asset Assignment Tasks for Onboarding
**User Story:**  
As a supervisor, I want onboarding tasks to include asset assignment.

**Acceptance Criteria (Gherkin):**  
Given a new hire  
When onboarding tasks are generated  
Then asset assignment is included

**Additional Details:**  
- Priority: Medium  
- Story Points: 1  
- Dependencies: E17-1, E09  
- Notes: Track asset status

---

## User Story 3: Automated Offboarding for Terminations
**User Story:**  
As an HR user, I want terminated employees to have access revoked, assets collected, and schedules updated.

**Acceptance Criteria (Gherkin):**  
Given a termination in HRIS  
When synced  
Then access is revoked, assets flagged for return, and future shifts removed

**Additional Details:**  
- Priority: Medium  
- Story Points: 2  
- Dependencies: E17-1, E09, E05  
- Notes: Offboarding checklist generated

---

## User Story 4: Track Onboarding/Offboarding Progress
**User Story:**  
As an HR user, I want to track progress of onboarding/offboarding tasks.

**Acceptance Criteria (Gherkin):**  
Given an employee  
When I view their profile  
Then all onboarding/offboarding tasks and status are visible

**Additional Details:**  
- Priority: Low  
- Story Points: 1  
- Dependencies: E17-1  
- Notes: Dashboard widget
