Title: Required Training and Certification Tasks for Onboarding

User Story:
As a supervisor, I want onboarding to automatically generate required training and certification tasks for new hires so that compliance and safety requirements are met before work begins.

Acceptance Criteria:
- Scenario: Training tasks are generated for new hire
  Given a new employee is provisioned
  When onboarding begins
  Then required training and certification tasks are assigned based on role and department

- Scenario: Certification status is tracked
  Given training is completed
  When the supervisor reviews the employee profile
  Then certification status and proof documents are visible

Additional Details:
- Training requirements must be configurable by role and department
- All training and certification actions must be logged and auditable
- Incomplete tasks must block assignment to restricted shifts or equipment
