Title: Offboarding Checklist & Exit Survey

User Story:
As an HR administrator, I want to provide departing employees with an offboarding checklist and exit survey so that all exit tasks are completed and feedback is collected for continuous improvement.

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario 1: Provide offboarding checklist
  Given offboarding is initiated
  When the employee accesses their portal
  Then they see a checklist of required exit tasks and documentation

Scenario 2: Submit exit survey
  Given the employee is completing offboarding
  When they fill out the exit survey
  Then their feedback is securely submitted and stored for analysis

Additional Details:
Priority: Medium
Story Points: 2
Dependencies: Offboarding workflow, survey module
Notes: Exit survey responses should be confidential and accessible only to HR.
