### E19: Advanced Scheduling (AI-Assisted)

#### User Story 1
**Title:** AI-Suggested Shift Assignments
**User Story:** As a SUPERVISOR I want AI to suggest optimal shift assignments So that staffing is efficient
**Acceptance Criteria (Gherkin):**
```
Given scheduling data
When I request AI suggestions
Then I receive optimal assignments based on rules and availability

Given conflicting constraints
When AI suggests
Then conflicts are flagged for manual review
```
**Additional Details:** Priority: Low, Story Points: 5, Dependencies: E05, E15, Notes: AI engine integration

#### User Story 2
**Title:** Supervisor Override of AI Suggestions
**User Story:** As a SUPERVISOR I want to override AI suggestions So that I retain control over final assignments
**Acceptance Criteria (Gherkin):**
```
Given AI-suggested assignments
When I override
Then manual assignments are saved and logged

Given no override
When I accept suggestions
Then assignments are finalized
```
**Additional Details:** Priority: Low, Story Points: 2, Dependencies: E05, E15, Notes: Audit override actions
