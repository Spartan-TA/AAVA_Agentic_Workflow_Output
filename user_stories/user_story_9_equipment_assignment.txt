Title: Equipment Assignment
User Story: As a supervisor I want to assign equipment (scanners, forklifts, PPE) to employees So that asset usage is tracked
Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Equipment assignment
Given equipment inventory exists
When I assign equipment to an employee
Then the system should track checkout and return status
Additional Details:
- Priority: Medium
- Story Points: 2
- Dependencies: Employee domain, RBAC, Certification tracking
- Notes: Prevent assignment if certification expired
