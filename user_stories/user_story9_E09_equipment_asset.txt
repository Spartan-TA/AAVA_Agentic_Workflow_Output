[E09] Equipment & Asset Assignment

Title: CRUD for Asset Registry
User Story: As an asset manager I want to create, read, update, and delete assets so that equipment inventory is maintained.
Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Asset Registry CRUD
Given the asset module
When I perform CRUD operations
Then assets are correctly managed

Additional Details:
- Priority: Medium
- Story Points: 2
- Dependencies: E02, E03, E07
- Notes: Include asset condition field
