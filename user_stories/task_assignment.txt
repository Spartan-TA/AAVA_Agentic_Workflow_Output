Title: Task Assignment

User Story: As a warehouse manager, I want to assign tasks to employees So that daily operations are organized and efficient.

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Task assignment
Given a list of tasks and available employees
When I assign tasks to employees
Then employees receive task notifications and can view their assignments

Additional Details:
- Priority: High
- Story Points: 3
- Dependencies: Employee Profile Management
- Notes: Tasks can be prioritized and tracked
