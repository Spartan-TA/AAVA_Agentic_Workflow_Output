Title: Shift Scheduling

User Story: As a warehouse manager, I want to schedule shifts for employees So that I can ensure adequate staffing for each shift.

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Shift assignment
Given a list of employees and shift requirements
When I assign employees to shifts and publish the schedule
Then employees are notified of their assigned shifts

Additional Details:
- Priority: High
- Story Points: 5
- Dependencies: Employee Profile Management
- Notes: Supports recurring and one-time shifts
