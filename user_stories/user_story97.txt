Title: Provide Kubernetes Manifests for Dev/Staging/Prod Deployment

User Story: As a DevOps engineer I want Kubernetes manifests for deploying EMS to dev, staging, and prod environments So that deployments are automated and environment-specific

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: K8s manifest deployment
Given manifests are configured
When I deploy to an environment
Then the EMS app is deployed with correct settings for that environment

Additional Details:
- Priority: High
- Story Points: 5
- Dependencies: E01, E19
- Notes: Manifests must support blue-green and canary strategies.
