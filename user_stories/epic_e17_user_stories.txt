EPIC E17: Onboarding & Offboarding Workflow

===========================================

Title: Onboarding & Offboarding Workflow

User Story: As an HR manager I want to automate provisioning and deprovisioning of accounts, schedules, training, and assets So that employee lifecycle changes are efficient

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Onboarding workflow
Given a new hire appears from HRIS
When onboarding tasks are generated
Then accounts, schedules, and training are provisioned

Additional Details:
- Priority: Medium
- Story Points: 3
- Dependencies: Employee CRUD, RBAC, Schedule, Certification, Asset, Integration
- Notes: Offboarding revokes access and collects assets
