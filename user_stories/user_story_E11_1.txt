Title: Generate Payroll-Ready Files from Approved Attendance and Leave

User Story: As a payroll administrator I want to generate payroll-ready files from approved attendance and leave records so that payroll processing is accurate and efficient.

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Payroll file generation
Given all attendance and leave records are approved
When I initiate a payroll export
Then the system should generate a file matching the external provider's schema and flag any discrepancies

Additional Details:
- Priority: High
- Story Points: 5
- Dependencies: Time & Attendance (E04), Leave & Absence Management (E06)
- Notes: Export must be auditable and retried on failure.
