EPIC E17: Onboarding & Offboarding Workflow

---

Title: Onboarding - Automate New Hire Provisioning
User Story: As an HR I want new hires to be provisioned automatically from HRIS So that onboarding is efficient.
Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: New hire provisioning
Given a new hire appears in HRIS
When the sync job runs
Then an account, initial schedule, and required training tasks are created
Additional Details:
- Priority: Medium
- Story Points: 5
- Dependencies: E02, E03, E05, E07, E09, E13
- Notes: Asset assignment tasks generated

---

Title: Onboarding - Offboarding Workflow
User Story: As an HR I want to automate offboarding to revoke access and collect assets So that terminations are risk-free.
Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Offboarding automation
Given an employee is terminated
When the offboarding workflow runs
Then access is revoked, assets are collected, and schedules updated
Additional Details:
- Priority: Medium
- Story Points: 5
- Dependencies: E02, E03, E05, E07, E09, E13
- Notes: Deprovisioning tasks tracked

---

Title: Onboarding - Training Assignment
User Story: As a SUPERVISOR I want required training assigned to new hires So that compliance is ensured.
Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Assign training
Given a new hire is provisioned
When I review onboarding tasks
Then required training is assigned and tracked
Additional Details:
- Priority: Medium
- Story Points: 3
- Dependencies: E02, E03, E05, E07, E09, E13
- Notes: Training status visible to supervisor

---

Title: Onboarding - Asset Assignment
User Story: As an ADMIN I want to assign assets to new hires during onboarding So that they have required equipment.
Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Assign onboarding assets
Given a new hire is provisioned
When I assign assets
Then the assignments are tracked and history logged
Additional Details:
- Priority: Medium
- Story Points: 3
- Dependencies: E02, E03, E05, E07, E09, E13
- Notes: Asset assignment from onboarding workflow
