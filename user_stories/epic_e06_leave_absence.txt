## User Story 1: Request Leave (PTO, Sick, Unpaid)
**User Story:**  
As an employee, I want to request different types of leave so that my absences are tracked.

**Acceptance Criteria (Gherkin):**  
Given I am logged in  
When I submit a leave request  
Then the request is saved and pending approval

**Additional Details:**  
- Priority: Medium  
- Story Points: 2  
- Dependencies: E02, E03, E05  
- Notes: Leave types configurable

---

## User Story 2: Approve or Deny Leave Requests
**User Story:**  
As a supervisor, I want to approve or deny leave requests so that staffing is managed.

**Acceptance Criteria (Gherkin):**  
Given a pending leave request  
When I approve or deny it  
Then the employee is notified and balances are updated

**Additional Details:**  
- Priority: Medium  
- Story Points: 2  
- Dependencies: E06-1  
- Notes: Approval workflow with notifications

---

## User Story 3: Update Leave Balances and Policies
**User Story:**  
As an HR user, I want leave balances to update automatically based on accrual policies so that records are accurate.

**Acceptance Criteria (Gherkin):**  
Given an approved leave  
When the leave period starts  
Then the employee's balance is reduced accordingly

**Additional Details:**  
- Priority: Medium  
- Story Points: 2  
- Dependencies: E06-2  
- Notes: Accrual rules configurable

---

## User Story 4: Exclude Approved Leaves from Scheduling and Payroll
**User Story:**  
As a scheduler, I want approved leaves to block shift assignments and payroll calculations so that absences are handled correctly.

**Acceptance Criteria (Gherkin):**  
Given an approved leave  
When generating schedules or payroll  
Then the leave period is excluded

**Additional Details:**  
- Priority: Medium  
- Story Points: 2  
- Dependencies: E06-2, E05  
- Notes: Integration hooks for scheduling/payroll

---

## User Story 5: Export Leave Reports
**User Story:**  
As an HR user, I want to export leave data for reporting and compliance.

**Acceptance Criteria (Gherkin):**  
Given leave records  
When I request an export  
Then a CSV file is generated with approved leaves

**Additional Details:**  
- Priority: Low  
- Story Points: 1  
- Dependencies: E06-3  
- Notes: Include filters for type, date, employee
