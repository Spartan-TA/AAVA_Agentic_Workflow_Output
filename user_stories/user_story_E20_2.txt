Title: Shift Assignment Performance Metrics and Manual Override

User Story: As a supervisor I want to track performance metrics for AI-assisted scheduling and manually override assignments So that I can ensure optimal staffing and accountability

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Metrics tracking and manual override
Given shift assignments are suggested by the system
When I review and accept or modify the assignments
Then the rationale is logged and performance metrics are updated for reporting

Additional Details:
- Priority: Low
- Story Points: 3
- Dependencies: E02, E05, E15
- Notes: Fallback to manual assignment must be seamless; metrics tracked for BI.
