Title: Filter Reports by Date, Department, and Shift

User Story: As an HR manager I want to filter operational reports by date, department, and shift So that I can analyze trends and compliance across different teams and time periods

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Report filtering
Given I am on the reporting dashboard
When I apply filters for date range, department, or shift
Then the displayed report updates to show only matching records

Additional Details:
- Priority: Medium
- Story Points: 3
- Dependencies: E04, E05, E06, E07, E08, E14
- Notes: Filtering must be fast and support large datasets.
