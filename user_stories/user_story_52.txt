Title: Announcements on Mobile

User Story: As an employee I want to receive company announcements on my mobile device So that I am informed of important updates wherever I am

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: View announcements on mobile
Given I am logged into the mobile app
When I access the announcements section
Then I should see a list of current company announcements

Additional Details:
- Priority: Medium
- Story Points: 2
- Dependencies: Mobile app, announcement module
- Notes: Support push notifications and attachments.
