[E05] Shift & Schedule Management

Title: Create Shift Templates and Rotations
User Story: As a supervisor I want to create recurring shift templates and rotations So that scheduling is efficient and consistent.
Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Shift template creation
Given the scheduling module is available
When I create a new template
Then it can be assigned to employees and reused

Additional Details:
Priority: High
Story Points: 3
Dependencies: E02, E03, E04
Notes: Support multiple shift patterns.

---

Title: Assign Shifts to Employees
User Story: As a supervisor I want to assign shifts to employees in bulk So that staffing is managed effectively.
Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Bulk shift assignment
Given shift templates exist
When I select employees and assign shifts
Then assignments are created and visible to workers

Additional Details:
Priority: High
Story Points: 3
Dependencies: Create Shift Templates and Rotations
Notes: Prevent scheduling conflicts.

---

Title: Detect and Prevent Scheduling Conflicts
User Story: As a supervisor I want the system to detect and prevent scheduling conflicts So that employees are not double-booked.
Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Conflict detection
Given shifts are being assigned
When a conflict is detected
Then the system blocks the assignment and shows an error

Additional Details:
Priority: High
Story Points: 3
Dependencies: Assign Shifts to Employees
Notes: Include blackout dates and operation calendars.

---

Title: Display Upcoming Shifts to Workers
User Story: As a worker I want to see my upcoming shifts So that I can plan my work schedule.
Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: View personal shifts
Given shifts are assigned
When I log in to my dashboard
Then my upcoming shifts are displayed

Additional Details:
Priority: Medium
Story Points: 2
Dependencies: Assign Shifts to Employees
Notes: Show shift details and location.

---

Title: Generate Audit Entries for Scheduling Actions
User Story: As a compliance officer I want audit entries for all scheduling actions So that changes are traceable.
Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Audit entry generation
Given a scheduling action occurs
When shifts are created, updated, or deleted
Then an audit entry is logged with actor and timestamp

Additional Details:
Priority: Medium
Story Points: 2
Dependencies: Assign Shifts to Employees
Notes: Integrate with centralized audit trail.
