### E05: Shift & Schedule Management

**Epic Description:**
Create recurring shift templates, rotations, overtime rules, and assignment to employees; handle blackout dates and warehouse operation calendars.

---

**User Story 1: Shift Template CRUD**

Title: Manage Shift Templates

User Story: As an HR user, I want to create, update, and delete shift templates so that I can standardize scheduling.

Acceptance Criteria:

Scenario: Create shift template
  Given I have shift details
  When I POST to /shifts/templates
  Then a new shift template is created

Additional Details:
- Priority: High
- Story Points: 3
- Dependencies: E02, E03, E04
- Notes: Support recurring patterns.

---

**User Story 2: Schedule Assignment**

Title: Assign Schedules to Employees

User Story: As a supervisor, I want to assign schedules to employees so that shifts are covered.

Acceptance Criteria:

Scenario: Assign schedule to employee
  Given a shift template exists
  When I assign it to an employee
  Then the employee's schedule is updated

Additional Details:
- Priority: High
- Story Points: 2
- Dependencies: E02, E03, E04
- Notes: Bulk assignment supported.

---

**User Story 3: Conflict Detection**

Title: Detect and Prevent Scheduling Conflicts

User Story: As a scheduler, I want the system to detect scheduling conflicts so that employees are not double-booked.

Acceptance Criteria:

Scenario: Scheduling conflict detected
  Given an employee has an existing shift
  When I try to assign an overlapping shift
  Then the system prevents the assignment and shows an error

Additional Details:
- Priority: High
- Story Points: 3
- Dependencies: E02, E03, E04
- Notes: Include blackout dates and operation calendars.

---

**User Story 4: Personal Shift View**

Title: View Upcoming Shifts

User Story: As a worker, I want to view my upcoming shifts so that I can plan my work schedule.

Acceptance Criteria:

Scenario: Worker views personal shift schedule
  Given I am logged in as a worker
  When I access my schedule
  Then I see a list of my upcoming shifts

Additional Details:
- Priority: High
- Story Points: 2
- Dependencies: E02, E03, E04
- Notes: Mobile-friendly view.

---

**User Story 5: Bulk Assignment**

Title: Bulk Assign Shifts to Multiple Employees

User Story: As a supervisor, I want to bulk-assign shifts to multiple employees so that scheduling is efficient.

Acceptance Criteria:

Scenario: Bulk shift assignment
  Given I select multiple employees
  When I assign a shift template
  Then all selected employees receive the assigned shift

Additional Details:
- Priority: High
- Story Points: 2
- Dependencies: E02, E03, E04
- Notes: Audit entries generated for each assignment.

---

**User Story 6: Audit Trail for Scheduling**

Title: Generate Audit Entries for Schedule Changes

User Story: As a compliance officer, I want audit entries for schedule changes so that I can track who made what changes.

Acceptance Criteria:

Scenario: Audit entry created for schedule change
  Given a schedule is created or modified

  Then an audit entry is generated with actor and timestamp

Additional Details:
- Priority: High
- Story Points: 2
- Dependencies: E02, E03, E04
- Notes: Integrate with audit trail module.
