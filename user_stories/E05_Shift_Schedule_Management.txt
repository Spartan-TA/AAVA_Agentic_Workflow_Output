### E05: Shift & Schedule Management

#### Title: Create Shift Templates
User Story: As an HR user I want to create recurring shift templates So that scheduling is consistent and efficient
Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Shift template created
Given I have shift details
When I POST to `/shifts/templates`
Then a new template is created

Additional Details:
- Priority: High
- Story Points: 3
- Dependencies: E02, E03, E04
- Notes: Support blackout dates

#### Title: Assign Shifts to Employees
User Story: As a supervisor I want to assign shifts to employees So that staffing is adequate
Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Bulk assignment
Given a shift template exists
When I assign to multiple employees
Then assignments are created and conflicts detected/prevented

Scenario: Worker views shifts
Given I am a worker
When I view my schedule
Then I see upcoming shifts

Additional Details:
- Priority: High
- Story Points: 5
- Dependencies: Create Shift Templates
- Notes: Audit entries generated

#### Title: Overtime Rules & Rotations
User Story: As an HR user I want to define overtime rules and shift rotations So that compliance and fairness are maintained
Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Overtime rule applied
Given overtime rules exist
When an employee exceeds hours
Then overtime is flagged

Scenario: Rotation applied
Given rotation schedule exists
When shifts are assigned
Then rotation is followed

Additional Details:
- Priority: High
- Story Points: 3
- Dependencies: Create Shift Templates
- Notes: Warehouse operation calendars supported
