EPIC E05: Shift & Schedule Management

---

Title: Shift Management - Create Shift Template
User Story: As an HR I want to create recurring shift templates So that scheduling is efficient and consistent.
Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Create shift template
Given I have shift details
When I POST to /shifts/templates
Then a new template is created and available for assignment
Additional Details:
- Priority: High
- Story Points: 3
- Dependencies: E02, E03, E04
- Notes: Supports daily/weekly recurrences

---

Title: Shift Management - Assign Shifts
User Story: As a SUPERVISOR I want to assign shifts to employees in bulk So that staffing is managed efficiently.
Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Bulk assign shifts
Given a list of employees
When I assign a shift template
Then all selected employees receive the shift
Additional Details:
- Priority: High
- Story Points: 5
- Dependencies: E02, E03, E04
- Notes: Audit entry generated for each assignment

---

Title: Shift Management - Conflict Detection
User Story: As an HR I want the system to detect scheduling conflicts So that double-booking is prevented.
Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Detect shift conflicts
Given an employee has an existing shift
When I assign an overlapping shift
Then the system prevents the assignment and shows an error
Additional Details:
- Priority: High
- Story Points: 5
- Dependencies: E02, E03, E04
- Notes: Includes blackout dates and operation calendars

---

Title: Shift Management - Worker Shift View
User Story: As a WORKER I want to view my upcoming shifts So that I can plan my attendance.
Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: View personal shifts
Given I am logged in
When I view my schedule
Then I see all upcoming assigned shifts
Additional Details:
- Priority: High
- Story Points: 2
- Dependencies: E02, E03, E04
- Notes: Role-based visibility
