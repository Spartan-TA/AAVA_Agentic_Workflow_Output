EPIC E10: Performance Reviews & Goals

---

Title: Performance Reviews - Create Review Cycle
User Story: As an HR I want to create quarterly/annual performance review cycles So that structured feedback is collected.
Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Create review cycle
Given review period details
When I create a cycle
Then employees and supervisors are notified and assigned
Additional Details:
- Priority: Medium
- Story Points: 3
- Dependencies: E02, E03
- Notes: Supports multiple templates

---

Title: Performance Reviews - Submit Review
User Story: As a SUPERVISOR I want to submit performance reviews for my team So that feedback is documented.
Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Submit review
Given a review cycle is active
When I submit a review
Then the review is saved and visible to HR and the employee
Additional Details:
- Priority: Medium
- Story Points: 3
- Dependencies: E02, E03
- Notes: Ratings, comments, goals required

---

Title: Performance Reviews - Employee Acknowledgement
User Story: As a WORKER I want to acknowledge my performance review So that the process is complete.
Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Acknowledge review
Given a review is submitted
When I acknowledge it
Then the review is marked as acknowledged and history is immutable
Additional Details:
- Priority: Medium
- Story Points: 2
- Dependencies: E02, E03
- Notes: PDF export available

---

Title: Performance Reviews - Review Visibility
User Story: As an HR I want to control review visibility by role So that privacy is maintained.
Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Role-based review access
Given reviews exist
When a user accesses reviews
Then they see only reviews they are authorized to view
Additional Details:
- Priority: Medium
- Story Points: 2
- Dependencies: E02, E03
- Notes: Immutable after sign-off
