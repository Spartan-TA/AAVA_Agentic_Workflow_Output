## E10 - Performance Reviews & Goals

### Story 1: Create Review Templates
- **User Story:** As an HR user, I want to create performance review templates so that reviews are consistent.
- **Acceptance Criteria:**
    ```
    Given valid template details
    When I create a review template
    Then it is available for assignment
    ```
- **Additional Details:**  
    Priority: Medium  
    Story Points: 3  
    Dependencies: E02, E03  
    Notes: Templates include goals, competencies.

### Story 2: Assign Reviews to Employees
- **User Story:** As a Supervisor, I want to assign performance reviews to my team so that feedback cycles are managed.
- **Acceptance Criteria:**
    ```
    Given a review cycle
    When I assign employees
    Then review tasks are created for each
    ```
- **Additional Details:**  
    Priority: Medium  
    Story Points: 2  
    Dependencies: E02, E03  
    Notes: Role-based assignment.

### Story 3: Submit and Acknowledge Reviews
- **User Story:** As a Worker, I want to submit self-reviews and acknowledge supervisor reviews so that the process is collaborative.
- **Acceptance Criteria:**
    ```
    Given a pending review
    When I submit my self-review
    Then it is saved and visible to my supervisor

    Given a completed supervisor review
    When I acknowledge it
    Then the review is marked as acknowledged
    ```
- **Additional Details:**  
    Priority: Medium  
    Story Points: 3  
    Dependencies: E02, E03  
    Notes: Workflow must enforce order.

### Story 4: PDF Export of Reviews
- **User Story:** As an HR user, I want to export completed reviews as PDFs so that they can be archived.
- **Acceptance Criteria:**
    ```
    Given a completed review
    When I export it
    Then I receive a PDF file with all review details
    ```
- **Additional Details:**  
    Priority: Low  
    Story Points: 2  
    Dependencies: E02, E03  
    Notes: Immutable after sign-off.
