### Epic E04: Time & Attendance (Clock In/Out)

**US-011**
- Title: Clock In Endpoint with Geofence
- User Story: As a Warehouse Worker, I want to clock in only when within the warehouse geofence so that attendance is accurate.
- Acceptance Criteria:
    - Given I am within the geofence,
      When I POST to /attendance/clock-in,
      Then my clock-in is recorded.
    - Given I am outside the geofence,
      When I attempt to clock in,
      Then I receive an error.
- Additional Details: Priority: High, Story Points: 5, Dependencies: E02, E03, Notes: Geofence coordinates configurable.

**US-012**
- Title: Clock Out Endpoint and Hours Calculation
- User Story: As a Warehouse Worker, I want to clock out and have hours worked calculated so that my attendance is tracked.
- Acceptance Criteria:
    - Given I am clocked in,
      When I POST to /attendance/clock-out,
      Then my clock-out is recorded and hours are calculated.
- Additional Details: Priority: High, Story Points: 3, Dependencies: US-011, Notes: Handles overnight shifts.

**US-013**
- Title: Handle Missed Punches
- User Story: As a Supervisor, I want to correct missed punches for my team so that attendance records are accurate.
- Acceptance Criteria:
    - Given a missed punch,
      When I submit a correction,
      Then the record is updated and flagged as corrected.
- Additional Details: Priority: High, Story Points: 3, Dependencies: US-011, US-012, Notes: Audit trail required.

### Epic E06: Leave & Absence Management

**US-018**
- Title: Request Paid Time Off (PTO)
- User Story: As a Warehouse Worker, I want to request PTO so that my absences are tracked and approved.
- Acceptance Criteria:
    - Given available PTO balance,
      When I submit a request,
      Then the request is pending approval.
    - When I lack sufficient balance,
      Then I receive an error.
- Additional Details: Priority: Medium, Story Points: 3, Dependencies: E02, E03, E05, Notes: PTO balance visible to user.

**US-019**
- Title: Approve or Reject Leave Requests
- User Story: As a Supervisor, I want to approve or reject leave requests so that team coverage is maintained.
- Acceptance Criteria:
    - Given a pending request,
      When I approve it,
      Then the employee is notified and schedule updated.
    - When I reject it,
      Then the employee is notified with reason.
- Additional Details: Priority: Medium, Story Points: 2, Dependencies: US-018, Notes: Approval workflow.

**US-020**
- Title: Track Leave Accrual Balances
- User Story: As an HR Manager, I want to track accrual of PTO and sick leave so that balances are accurate.
- Acceptance Criteria:
    - Given accrual rules,
      When time passes,
      Then balances are updated automatically.
- Additional Details: Priority: Medium, Story Points: 3, Dependencies: US-018, Notes: Configurable accrual rates.
