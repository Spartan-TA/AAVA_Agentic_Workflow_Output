### E20 - Self-Service Portal

**User Story 1**
Title: Employee Profile Updates
User Story: As an employee, I want to update my contact info so that my records are current.
Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Profile update
Given my profile
When I edit and save contact info
Then the changes are reflected in my record
Priority: Medium
Story Points: 2
Dependencies: E02, E03, E06, E10, E11
Notes: Audit all changes.

**User Story 2**
Title: Upload Personal Documents
User Story: As an employee, I want to upload documents (e.g., tax forms) so that my records are complete.
Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Document upload
Given my profile
When I upload a document
Then it is stored and linked to my profile
Priority: Medium
Story Points: 2
Dependencies: E02, E03, E06, E10, E11
Notes: Support PDF/JPG/PNG formats.

**User Story 3**
Title: View Pay Stubs and Tax Forms
User Story: As an employee, I want to view my pay history and tax forms so that I can access my financial records.
Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Pay stub access
Given my employment
When I view pay stubs
Then all historical pay and tax forms are available
Priority: Medium
Story Points: 2
Dependencies: E02, E03, E06, E10, E11
Notes: Secure access to sensitive documents.

**User Story 4**
Title: Benefits Enrollment
User Story: As an employee, I want to enroll in benefits so that my selections are recorded.
Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Benefits enrollment
Given open enrollment
When I select benefits
Then my choices are saved and confirmation is provided
Priority: Medium
Story Points: 2
Dependencies: E02, E03, E06, E10, E11
Notes: Integrate with benefits provider if applicable.

**User Story 5**
Title: Supervisor Team Management Portal
User Story: As a supervisor, I want to view my team roster and pending approvals so that I can manage my team efficiently.
Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Team management
Given my supervisor role

Priority: Medium
Story Points: 2
Dependencies: E02, E03, E06, E10, E11
Notes: Audit all actions.
