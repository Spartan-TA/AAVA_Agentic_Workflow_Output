### E20: Self-Service Portal

**User Story 1: Employee Pay Stub and Tax Form Access**
- User Story: As an employee, I want to view and download my pay stubs and tax forms So that I can manage my personal records.
- Acceptance Criteria:
  - Scenario: View pay stub
    - Given available pay stubs
    - When I access the portal
    - Then I can view and download my documents
- Priority: Low
- Story Points: 5
- Dependencies: E02, E03, E11
- Notes: Secure document storage.

**User Story 2: Update Contact Info and View Policies**
- User Story: As an employee, I want to update my contact info and view company policies So that my records are current and I am informed.
- Acceptance Criteria:
  - Scenario: Update contact info
    - Given my profile
    - When I submit changes
    - Then my contact info is updated after validation
  - Scenario: View policies
    - Given company policies
    - When I access the portal
    - Then I can view all current policies
- Priority: Low
- Story Points: 3
- Dependencies: E02, E03, E11
- Notes: All changes audited.
