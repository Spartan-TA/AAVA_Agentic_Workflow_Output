### E20 - Continuous Improvement & Feedback

**User Story 1: Feedback Form CRUD**
- Title: Manage Feedback Forms
- Narrative: As a user, I want to submit, edit, and delete feedback forms so that my suggestions are heard.
- Acceptance Criteria:
  ```
  Given feedback forms,
  When I perform CRUD,
  Then forms are managed accordingly.
  ```
- Priority: Low
- Story Points: 2
- Dependencies: E03, E12
- Notes: Engagement.

**User Story 2: Anonymous Feedback Option**
- Title: Submit Anonymous Feedback
- Narrative: As a user, I want to submit feedback anonymously so that I feel safe sharing concerns.
- Acceptance Criteria:
  ```
  Given feedback submission,
  When I choose anonymous,
  Then my identity is not stored.
  ```
- Priority: Low
- Story Points: 1
- Dependencies: Story 1
- Notes: Privacy.

**User Story 3: Voting and Commenting on Suggestions**
- Title: Vote and Comment on Suggestions
- Narrative: As a user, I want to upvote and comment on suggestions so that popular ideas are prioritized.
- Acceptance Criteria:
  ```
  Given a suggestion,
  When I upvote or comment,
  Then my input is recorded and visible.
  ```
- Priority: Low
- Story Points: 2
- Dependencies: Story 1
- Notes: Collaboration.

**User Story 4: Export Feedback**
- Title: Export Feedback Data
- Narrative: As a product manager, I want to export feedback so that analysis is possible.
- Acceptance Criteria:
  ```
  Given feedback data,
  When exported,
  Then a file is generated with all details.
  ```
- Priority: Low
- Story Points: 1
- Dependencies: Story 1
- Notes: Analysis.

**User Story 5: Status Notifications for Feedback**
- Title: Notify Users of Feedback Status
- Narrative: As a user, I want notifications when my feedback status changes so that I am informed.
- Acceptance Criteria:
  ```
  Given feedback status changes,
  When updated,
  Then a notification is sent to the user.
  ```
- Priority: Low
- Story Points: 1
- Dependencies: Story 1
- Notes: Engagement.
