EPIC E05: SHIFT & SCHEDULE MANAGEMENT

===========================================

Title: Shift Management - Create Shift Templates
User Story: As an HR user, I want to create recurring shift templates so that scheduling is efficient.
Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Create shift template
  Given I have shift details
  When I POST to /shifts/templates
  Then a new template is created

Additional Details:
Priority: High
Story Points: 3
Dependencies: E02, E03, E04
Notes: Support weekly and custom recurrence.

---

Title: Shift Management - Assign Shifts
User Story: As a supervisor, I want to assign shifts to employees in bulk so that scheduling is streamlined.
Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Bulk assign shifts
  Given a list of employees and a shift template
  When I assign shifts
  Then all selected employees receive the shift assignment

Additional Details:
Priority: High
Story Points: 3
Dependencies: E02, E03, E04
Notes: Prevent conflicts and double-booking.

---

Title: Shift Management - Conflict Detection
User Story: As a system, I want to detect and prevent scheduling conflicts so that employees are not double-booked.
Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Conflict detection
  Given existing shift assignments
  When a new shift is assigned
  Then conflicts are flagged and prevented

Additional Details:
Priority: High
Story Points: 2
Dependencies: E02, E03, E04
Notes: Include blackout dates and warehouse calendar.

---

Title: Shift Management - View Personal Shifts
User Story: As a worker, I want to view my upcoming shifts so that I can plan my work.
Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: View shifts
  Given I am logged in
  When I access my schedule
  Then I see all upcoming assigned shifts

Additional Details:
Priority: High
Story Points: 2
Dependencies: E02, E03, E04
Notes: Show shift details and location.

---

Title: Shift Management - Audit Entries
User Story: As an auditor, I want audit entries for shift assignments so that changes are traceable.
Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Audit log for shifts
  Given a shift assignment is created or modified
  When I review the audit log
  Then I see who made the change and when

Additional Details:
Priority: High
Story Points: 2
Dependencies: E02, E03, E04
Notes: Store before/after state.
