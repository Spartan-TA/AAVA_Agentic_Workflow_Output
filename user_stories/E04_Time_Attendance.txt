EPIC E04: Time & Attendance (Clock In/Out)

---

Title: Time & Attendance - Clock In
User Story: As a WORKER I want to clock in at the start of my shift So that my work hours are accurately recorded.
Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Clock in event
Given my shift is scheduled
When I POST to /attendance/clock-in
Then my clock-in time is recorded and associated with my shift
Additional Details:
- Priority: High
- Story Points: 3
- Dependencies: E02, E03
- Notes: Device and location capture optional

---

Title: Time & Attendance - Clock Out
User Story: As a WORKER I want to clock out at the end of my shift So that my work hours are complete.
Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Clock out event
Given I am clocked in
When I POST to /attendance/clock-out
Then my clock-out time is recorded and daily total is computed
Additional Details:
- Priority: High
- Story Points: 3
- Dependencies: E02, E03
- Notes: Automatic shift association

---

Title: Time & Attendance - Missed Punch Correction
User Story: As a WORKER I want to request correction for missed punches So that my attendance is accurate.
Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Missed punch correction
Given I missed a clock-in/out
When I submit a correction request
Then an approval task is created for my supervisor
Additional Details:
- Priority: High
- Story Points: 5
- Dependencies: E02, E03
- Notes: Correction free-text reason required

---

Title: Time & Attendance - Attendance Report Export
User Story: As an HR I want to export attendance reports in CSV format So that I can process payroll and audits.
Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Export attendance report
Given attendance data exists
When I request a CSV export
Then the file is generated with all relevant fields
Additional Details:
- Priority: High
- Story Points: 2
- Dependencies: E02, E03
- Notes: Export must match report schema
