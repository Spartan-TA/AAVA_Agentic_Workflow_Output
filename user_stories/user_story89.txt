Title: Assign Employees to Warehouses

User Story: As an HR manager I want to assign employees to specific warehouses So that scheduling and policy enforcement are accurate for each location

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Employee warehouse assignment
Given I am managing employee records
When I assign an employee to a warehouse
Then the employee is linked to that warehouse and inherits its policies

Additional Details:
- Priority: Low
- Story Points: 4
- Dependencies: E01, E02, E05
- Notes: Employees may be reassigned between warehouses as needed.
