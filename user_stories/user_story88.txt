Title: Support Multiple Warehouses with Distinct Calendars and Policies

User Story: As an operations manager I want to configure multiple warehouses with unique calendars and policies So that each warehouse operates according to its local requirements

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Warehouse configuration
Given I am in the warehouse admin panel
When I create or edit a warehouse
Then I can set its calendar, shift templates, and policies independently

Additional Details:
- Priority: Low
- Story Points: 5
- Dependencies: E01, E02, E05
- Notes: Policies must be scoped to warehouse entity.
