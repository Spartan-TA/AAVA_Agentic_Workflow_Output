### E15: Reporting & Analytics

**User Story 15.1**
Title: Attendance and Overtime Reports

User Story: As a Supervisor I want to generate attendance and overtime reports by date, department, and shift So that I can manage staffing.

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Report generation
Given attendance data exists
When I filter and export
Then the report is generated within 10 seconds for up to 50k rows

Additional Details:
- Priority: Medium
- Story Points: 5
- Dependencies: E04, E05, E06, E14
- Notes: Export formats: CSV, PDF

---
