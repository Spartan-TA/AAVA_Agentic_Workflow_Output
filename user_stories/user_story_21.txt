Title: Leave Approval Workflow
User Story: As a supervisor I want to review and approve or deny employee leave requests So that staffing levels are maintained and leave policies are enforced
Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Approve leave request Given a pending leave request is assigned to me When I review the request and take action Then the employee is notified and balances are updated accordingly
Additional Details:
- Priority: Medium
- Story Points: 2
- Dependencies: Leave request submission, notification system
- Notes: Approval actions should be logged and auditable.
