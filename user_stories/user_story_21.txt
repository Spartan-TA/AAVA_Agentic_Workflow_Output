Title: Create Shift Templates

User Story: As a scheduling manager I want to create reusable shift templates So that I can efficiently assign standard shift patterns to employees

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Creating a new shift template
  Given I am on the shift management page
  When I enter shift details and save as a template
  Then the template should be available for future assignments
Scenario: Editing a shift template
  Given a shift template exists
  When I modify its details
  Then changes should be reflected for future assignments

Additional Details:
- Priority: High
- Story Points: 2
- Dependencies: Schedule module
- Notes: Templates should support start/end times, breaks, and overtime rules.
