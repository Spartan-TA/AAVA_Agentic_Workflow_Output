Title: Track Certification Expiry

User Story: As a training coordinator I want to track employee certification expiry dates So that I can ensure compliance and timely renewals

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Certification expiry tracking
Given certifications are assigned to employees
When a certification is nearing expiry
Then an alert should be sent to the employee and coordinator

Additional Details:
- Priority: High
- Story Points: 5
- Dependencies: Training management module, notification system
- Notes: Support for multiple certification types.
