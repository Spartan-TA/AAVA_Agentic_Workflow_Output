Title: Create Shift Schedules

User Story: As a scheduler I want to create shift schedules for employees So that work coverage is ensured and employees know their assignments

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Shift schedule creation
Given I am logged in as a scheduler
When I create a new shift schedule and assign employees
Then the schedule should be visible to assigned employees

Additional Details:
- Priority: High
- Story Points: 4
- Dependencies: Employee database
- Notes: Support for recurring and ad-hoc shifts
