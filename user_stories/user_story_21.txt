Title: Submit Leave Request

User Story: As an employee I want to submit a request for paid time off, sick leave, or unpaid leave so that my supervisor can review and approve my absence in accordance with company policy

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Employee submits leave request
  Given I am logged in and viewing my leave balance
  When I select leave type, enter dates, and submit the request
  Then the request appears in my pending requests and my supervisor is notified

Additional Details:
- Priority: Medium
- Story Points: 3
- Dependencies: E02 (User Management), E03 (Scheduling), E05 (Attendance)
- Notes: Validate leave type and date range against accrual policy
