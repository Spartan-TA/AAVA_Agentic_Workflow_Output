Title: Rollback Strategy and Pipeline Validation

User Story: As a DevOps engineer I want the CI/CD pipeline to support rollback and validate all steps so that failed deployments can be reverted safely

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Rollback and pipeline validation
  Given a deployment fails or is rolled back
  When the rollback is triggered
  Then the previous stable version is restored and all pipeline steps are validated for success before merge

Additional Details:
- Priority: High
- Story Points: 3
- Dependencies: E01
- Notes: Rollback must be tested in staging and production; rollback logs must be available.
