### E10: Performance Reviews & Goals

**User Story 1**
- Title: Create Review Templates
- User Story: As an HR user, I want to create performance review templates so that reviews are standardized.
- Acceptance Criteria in Gherkin format:
  Scenario: Create and retrieve review templates
  Given I have template details
  When I create a template
  Then it is available for assignment
- Additional Details:
  * Priority: Medium
  * Story Points: 2
  * Dependencies: E02, E03
  * Notes: Support for quarterly/annual cycles.

**User Story 2**
- Title: Assign Reviews to Employees
- User Story: As a supervisor, I want to assign reviews to employees so that feedback is structured.
- Acceptance Criteria in Gherkin format:
  Scenario: Assign review cycle
  Given a review template exists
  When I assign it to an employee
  Then the employee is notified and can participate
- Additional Details:
  * Priority: Medium
  * Story Points: 2
  * Dependencies: E02, E03
  * Notes: Role-based visibility.

**User Story 3**
- Title: Submit and Acknowledge Reviews
- User Story: As an employee, I want to submit and acknowledge reviews so that feedback is documented.
- Acceptance Criteria in Gherkin format:
  Scenario: Submit and acknowledge review
  Given a review is assigned
  When the supervisor submits and the employee acknowledges
  Then the review is marked complete and history is immutable
- Additional Details:
  * Priority: Medium
  * Story Points: 2
  * Dependencies: E02, E03
  * Notes: PDF export available.
