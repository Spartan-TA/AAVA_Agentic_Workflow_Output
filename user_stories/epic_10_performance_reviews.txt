Title: Performance Reviews & Goals - Create Review Cycle
User Story: As an HR manager I want to create quarterly and annual review cycles So that structured feedback is collected

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Create review cycle
Given valid review template
When I POST to /reviews/cycles
Then the cycle is created and assigned to employees

Additional Details:
- Priority: Medium
- Story Points: 2
- Dependencies: Employee CRUD, RBAC
- Notes: Templates include goals, competencies

Title: Performance Reviews & Goals - Submit and Acknowledge Review
User Story: As a supervisor I want to submit reviews and employees to acknowledge them So that feedback is documented

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Submit review
Given a review cycle is active
When supervisor submits ratings and comments
Then the review is saved

Scenario: Employee acknowledgment
Given a review is submitted
When employee acknowledges
Then the review is marked as acknowledged

Additional Details:
- Priority: Medium
- Story Points: 2
- Dependencies: Review cycle
- Notes: PDF export supported

Title: Performance Reviews & Goals - Immutable History After Sign-Off
User Story: As an HR manager I want review history to be immutable after sign-off So that records are auditable

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Sign-off review
Given a review is acknowledged
When sign-off is completed
Then the review cannot be edited

Additional Details:
- Priority: Medium
- Story Points: 1
- Dependencies: Review submission
- Notes: Role-based visibility enforced
