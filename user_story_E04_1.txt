Title: Clock-In Endpoint

User Story: As a warehouse worker I want to clock in at the start of my shift So that my attendance and hours worked are accurately recorded

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Successful clock-in
Given I am scheduled for a shift and within the geofence
When I submit a clock-in request via /attendance/clock-in
Then my clock-in time is recorded and associated with my shift

Additional Details:
- Priority: High
- Story Points: 2
- Dependencies: E02, E03
- Notes: Validate geofence and device capture.
