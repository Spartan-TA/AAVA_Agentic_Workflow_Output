Title: Email Verification

User Story Narrative:
As a new user, I want to verify my email address after registration so that I can activate my account.

Acceptance Criteria (Gherkin):
Given I have registered,
When I receive a verification email and click the link,
Then my account is activated,
And I can log in.

Additional Details:
- Priority: High
- Story Points: 2
- Dependencies: User Registration
- Notes: Verification link expires in 48 hours.
