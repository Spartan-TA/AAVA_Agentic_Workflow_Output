Title: Equipment Assignment - Assign Asset

User Story: As a SUPERVISOR I want to assign equipment (scanner, forklift, PPE) to employees So that asset usage is tracked.

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Assign asset
Given an available asset and a qualified employee
When I assign the asset
Then the checkout is logged and asset status is updated

Additional Details:
- Priority: Medium
- Story Points: 2
- Dependencies: E02, E03, E07
- Notes: Prevents assignment if certification is missing

---

Title: Equipment Assignment - Asset Check-In/Out

User Story: As a WORKER I want to check in and out equipment So that asset usage and condition are recorded.

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Check-in asset
Given I have an assigned asset
When I check it in
Then the return is logged and asset condition is updated

Additional Details:
- Priority: Medium
- Story Points: 2
- Dependencies: E02, E03, E07
- Notes: Overdue returns are reported

---

Title: Equipment Assignment - Asset History Log

User Story: As an ADMIN I want to view the history of asset assignments So that accountability is maintained.

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: View asset history
Given an asset
When I view its history
Then I see all check-in/out events and assigned employees

Additional Details:
- Priority: Medium
- Story Points: 2
- Dependencies: E02, E03, E07
- Notes: History is exportable
