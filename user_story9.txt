### E09: Equipment & Asset Assignment

**User Story 1**
Title: Asset Registry Management

User Story: As an ADMIN I want to manage the asset registry So that equipment assignments are tracked.

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Asset CRUD
Given asset details
When I create, update, or delete an asset
Then the registry reflects the changes

Additional Details:
- Priority: Medium
- Story Points: 2
- Dependencies: E02, E03, E07
- Notes: Includes scanners, forklifts, PPE

---

**User Story 2**
Title: Asset Check-In/Check-Out

User Story: As a SUPERVISOR I want to check assets in and out to employees So that accountability is maintained.

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Asset Assignment
Given an available asset and qualified employee
When I assign the asset
Then the check-out is recorded and history updated

Additional Details:
- Priority: Medium
- Story Points: 2
- Dependencies: Story 1
- Notes: Blocks assignment if certification invalid

---

**User Story 3**
Title: Asset Condition Tracking

User Story: As a WORKER I want to report asset condition So that maintenance needs are flagged.

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Report Asset Condition
Given a checked-out asset
When I submit a condition report
Then the asset state is updated

Additional Details:
- Priority: Medium
- Story Points: 1
- Dependencies: Story 2
- Notes: Condition history visible per asset

---

**User Story 4**
Title: Overdue Asset Return Reporting

User Story: As an ADMIN I want to generate reports of overdue asset returns So that lost or unreturned equipment is identified.

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Overdue Asset Report
Given asset assignment data
When I request a report
Then overdue returns are listed

Additional Details:
- Priority: Medium
- Story Points: 1
- Dependencies: Story 2
- Notes: History log per asset and employee
