Title: Support Filtering and Searching Employee Records

User Story: As an HR user I want to filter and search employee records by name, badgeId, department, role, and status so that I can quickly find relevant employees.

Acceptance Criteria:

Scenario: Filter by department and role
Given multiple employees exist
When I GET /employees?department=Shipping&role=WORKER
Then I receive only employees in Shipping with the WORKER role

Scenario: Search by name or badgeId
Given multiple employees exist
When I GET /employees?search=Smith
Then I receive employees whose name or badgeId matches 'Smith'

Scenario: Filter by status
Given employees with different statuses exist
When I GET /employees?status=active
Then I receive only active employees

Additional Details:
- Priority: High
- Story Points: 3
- Dependencies: E01
- Notes: Improves usability for HR and supervisors.
