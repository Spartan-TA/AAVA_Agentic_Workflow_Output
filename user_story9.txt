Title: Equipment & Asset Assignment

User Story: As an asset manager I want to assign and track equipment to employees So that asset accountability and safe operation are ensured.

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Asset check-out
Given an employee is certified
When I assign equipment to them
Then the check-out is recorded and asset status updated

Scenario: Certification block
Given an employee lacks required certification
When I attempt asset assignment
Then the system blocks the transaction

Additional Details:
- Priority: Medium
- Story Points: 8
- Dependencies: E02, E03, E07
- Notes: History log per asset/employee; overdue return reports.
