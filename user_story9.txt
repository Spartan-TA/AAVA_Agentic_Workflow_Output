Title: Employee Pagination and Filtering
User Story: As an HR manager, I want to paginate and filter employee records so that I can efficiently manage large datasets and quickly find relevant employees.
Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Paginate and filter employees
Given there are more than 50 employee records
When I request a paginated and filtered list via the API
Then I receive only the relevant subset of employee records based on my criteria
Additional Details:
- Priority: High
- Story Points: 2
- Dependencies: E01
- Notes: Filtering should support department, role, status, and hire date.
