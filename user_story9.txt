Title: Create Shift Templates and Assignments

User Story: As a scheduler I want to create recurring shift templates and assign them to employees So that staffing is planned efficiently

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Shift template creation Given I have scheduler access When I create and assign shift templates Then employees see their upcoming shifts and conflicts are prevented

Additional Details:
- Priority: High
- Story Points: 3
- Dependencies: Employee CRUD, RBAC, Attendance
- Notes: Blackout dates and operation calendars supported; audit entries generated
