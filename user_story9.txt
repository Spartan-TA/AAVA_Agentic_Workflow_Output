Title: Equipment & Asset Assignment

User Story: As an asset manager I want to assign and track equipment and PPE to employees So that accountability and safe operation are ensured

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Assign asset to employee
Given an asset is available and employee is certified
When I assign the asset
Then the assignment is recorded and asset status is updated

Scenario: Block assignment for missing certification
Given an employee lacks required certification
When I attempt to assign an asset
Then the system blocks the assignment and notifies me

Scenario: Track asset condition
Given an asset is assigned
When I update its condition
Then the condition is logged and visible in asset history

Scenario: Overdue asset return report
Given assets are overdue for return
When I request a report
Then I see a list of overdue assets and responsible employees

Additional Details:
- Priority: Medium
- Story Points: 5
- Dependencies: Employee Master Data CRUD APIs, Role Based Access Control (RBAC), Training & Certification Tracking
- Notes: Asset registry CRUD; check-in/out endpoints; blocks when certs invalid; history log per asset and employee.
