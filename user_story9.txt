Title: Equipment & Asset Assignment
User Story: As an asset manager I want to assign and track equipment and PPE to employees so that accountability and safe operation are ensured in the warehouse.
Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Asset assignment and tracking
Given an asset registry and employee certifications
When I assign or check out equipment to an employee
Then blocks occur if certifications are invalid, check-in/out endpoints are available, history log is visible per asset and employee, and reports of overdue returns are generated.
Additional Details:
- Priority: Medium
- Story Points: 5
- Dependencies: Employee Master Data (CRUD), Role Based Access Control (RBAC), Training & Certification Tracking
- Notes: Track asset condition state; prevent use if certification missing.
