Title: Equipment & Asset Assignment
User Story: As an asset manager I want to assign and track equipment So that accountability and safe operation are ensured
Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Asset check-in/out and certification validation
Given an employee checks out equipment
When their certification is invalid
Then the system blocks the assignment and logs the event
Additional Details:
- Priority: Medium
- Story Points: 5
- Dependencies: Employee Master Data, RBAC, Training & Certification Tracking
- Notes: Asset condition state, overdue reports
