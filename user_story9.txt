Title: Equipment Assignment

User Story: As a warehouse supervisor I want to assign equipment to employees So that asset usage is tracked and accountability is maintained

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Assigning equipment
Given I am logged in as a supervisor
When I assign equipment to an employee
Then the assignment is recorded and the employee is notified

Additional Details:
- Priority: Medium
- Story Points: 5
- Dependencies: Asset management module, notification module
- Notes: Support for equipment return and maintenance tracking
