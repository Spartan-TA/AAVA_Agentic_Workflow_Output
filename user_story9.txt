Title: Equipment & Asset Assignment

User Story: As an asset manager I want to assign and track equipment and PPE to employees so that asset accountability and safe equipment operation are ensured.

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Asset assignment and validation
  Given an asset is assigned to an employee
  When the employee checks out or returns the asset
  Then the system blocks use if certifications are invalid, maintains asset condition state, and logs history per asset and employee
  And reports of overdue returns are available

Additional Details:
- Priority: Medium
- Story Points: 6
- Dependencies: Employee Master Data (CRUD), Role Based Access Control (RBAC), Training & Certification Tracking
- Notes: Asset registry CRUD and check-in/out endpoints required.
