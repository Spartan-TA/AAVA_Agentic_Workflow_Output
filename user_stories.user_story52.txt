Title: Policy Scoping by Warehouse

User Story: As a compliance manager I want to scope policies and permissions to specific warehouses So that local regulations and practices are enforced appropriately

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Assigning policies to warehouses
Given I have defined policies and multiple warehouses
When I assign a policy to a warehouse
Then only users and assets in that warehouse are affected by the policy

Additional Details:
- Priority: High
- Story Points: 5
- Dependencies: Policy engine, warehouse entity
- Notes: Support for both global and local policies.
