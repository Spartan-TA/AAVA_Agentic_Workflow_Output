Title: Notification Opt-In/Out and Quiet Hours Configuration

User Story: As a warehouse worker I want to opt-in or out of notification channels and configure quiet hours so that I am not disturbed outside of working hours.

Acceptance Criteria:

Scenario: Opt-in/out per channel
Given notification channels are available
When I update my preferences
Then I can enable or disable in-app, email, or SMS notifications

Scenario: Configure quiet hours
Given notification preferences
When I set quiet hours
Then notifications are suppressed during the configured period

Scenario: Delivery status tracked
Given notifications are sent
When I view my notification history
Then I see the delivery status for each notification

Additional Details:
- Priority: Medium
- Story Points: 3
- Dependencies: E03, E05, E06, E07
- Notes: Improves user experience and reduces notification fatigue.
