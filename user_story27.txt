Title: Leave Management - Supervisor Approval Workflow
User Story: As a supervisor, I want to approve or deny leave requests so that team coverage and policy compliance are maintained.
Acceptance Criteria:
  Given I am authenticated as a supervisor
  When I review a leave request
  Then I can approve or deny the request, and the employee is notified
Additional Details:
  Priority: Medium
  Story Points: 5
  Dependencies: E02, E03, E05
  Notes: Approval/denial actions must update leave balances and trigger notifications.
