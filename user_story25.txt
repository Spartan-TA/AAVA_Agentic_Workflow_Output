Title: Shift Management - Blackout Dates and Warehouse Calendars
User Story: As an operations manager, I want to configure blackout dates and warehouse operation calendars so that shifts are not scheduled during non-operational periods.
Acceptance Criteria:
  Given I am authenticated as an operations manager
  When I configure blackout dates or update the warehouse calendar
  Then the system prevents shift assignments during those periods
Additional Details:
  Priority: High
  Story Points: 5
  Dependencies: E02, E03, E04
  Notes: Calendar updates must be reflected in scheduling and conflict detection.
