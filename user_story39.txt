Title: Safety Incidents - Metrics Dashboard Endpoints
User Story: As a safety manager, I want to access dashboard endpoints showing safety KPIs and incident metrics so that I can monitor trends and take proactive actions.
Acceptance Criteria:
  Given incidents are recorded
  When I access the safety metrics dashboard
  Then I see KPIs such as incident rate, severity distribution, and corrective actions
Additional Details:
  Priority: High
  Story Points: 5
  Dependencies: E02, E03
  Notes: Dashboard endpoints must be role-based and exportable.
