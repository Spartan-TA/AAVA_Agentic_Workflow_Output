Title: Leave Management - Scheduling and Payroll Integration
User Story: As a payroll administrator, I want approved leaves to be excluded from scheduling and payroll hours so that payroll is accurate and shifts are properly covered.
Acceptance Criteria:
  Given a leave request is approved
  When payroll and scheduling processes run
  Then the approved leave is excluded from scheduled hours and payroll calculations
Additional Details:
  Priority: Medium
  Story Points: 5
  Dependencies: E02, E03, E05
  Notes: Integration hooks must be available for both scheduling and payroll systems.
