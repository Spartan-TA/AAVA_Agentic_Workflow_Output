Title: Asset Registry CRUD and Condition Tracking

User Story: As an asset manager I want to create, update, and delete asset records and track their condition so that the asset registry is accurate and assets are maintained properly.

Acceptance Criteria:

Scenario: Create asset record
Given asset details
When I POST to /assets
Then a new asset is added to the registry

Scenario: Update asset condition
Given an existing asset
When I PATCH the asset with condition details
Then the condition is updated and history is logged

Scenario: Delete asset record
Given an asset is decommissioned
When I DELETE the asset
Then it is removed from the registry

Additional Details:
- Priority: Medium
- Story Points: 3
- Dependencies: E02, E03, E07
- Notes: Ensures accurate tracking and maintenance.
