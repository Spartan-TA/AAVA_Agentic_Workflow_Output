Title: Performance Reviews - PDF Export and Role-Based Visibility
User Story: As an HR manager, I want completed reviews to be exportable as PDF and visible only to authorized roles so that confidentiality and compliance are maintained.
Acceptance Criteria:
  Given a review cycle is completed
  When I export the review
  Then the PDF includes all ratings, comments, and acknowledgements and is accessible only to authorized users
Additional Details:
  Priority: Medium
  Story Points: 5
  Dependencies: E02, E03
  Notes: Export must be available after sign-off and history must be immutable.
