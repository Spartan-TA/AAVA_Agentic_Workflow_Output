Title: Review Submission, Workflow, and Acknowledgements

User Story: As a supervisor I want to submit performance reviews, track workflow status, and collect acknowledgements from employees so that the review process is transparent and complete.

Acceptance Criteria:

Scenario: Supervisor submits review
Given a review is assigned
When the supervisor completes and submits it
Then the review status updates to 'Pending Employee Acknowledgement'

Scenario: Employee acknowledges review
Given a review is pending acknowledgement
When the employee acknowledges it
Then the review status updates to 'Completed' and is locked

Scenario: Review workflow visible
Given a review is in progress
When I view the review details
Then the current status and next steps are visible

Additional Details:
- Priority: Medium
- Story Points: 5
- Dependencies: E02, E03
- Notes: Ensures accountability and transparency in performance management.
