Title: Leave & Absence Management

User Story: As an employee I want to request and track PTO, sick, and unpaid leave so that my time off is managed accurately and in compliance with company policies.

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Leave request and approval
Given I have accrued leave balances
When I submit a leave request
Then my supervisor can approve or deny, balances update accordingly, scheduled shifts are auto-flagged for coverage, and exports show approved leaves.

Additional Details:
- Priority: Medium
- Story Points: 5
- Dependencies: Employee Master Data (E02), Role Based Access Control (E03), Shift & Schedule Management (E05)
- Notes: Integration hooks to exclude from scheduling and payroll hours.
