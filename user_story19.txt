Title: Attendance - Daily Totals and Report Export
User Story: As a supervisor, I want to view daily attendance totals and export attendance reports in CSV format so that I can monitor team hours and maintain records.
Acceptance Criteria:
  Given I am authenticated as a SUPERVISOR
  When I access attendance reports
  Then I can view daily totals and export the report as CSV
Additional Details:
  Priority: High
  Story Points: 5
  Dependencies: E02, E03
  Notes: Export must include all relevant fields and be available for selected date ranges.
