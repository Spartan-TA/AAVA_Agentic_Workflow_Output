Title: Training & Certification - Certification Status Visibility
User Story: As a supervisor, I want to view certification status on employee profiles so that I can quickly verify qualifications.
Acceptance Criteria:
  - Given an employee profile, When I view it, Then I see all certifications and their status (valid/expiring/expired).
Additional Details:
  Priority: High
  Story Points: 1
  Dependencies: Training & Certification
  Notes: Status visible to supervisors.
