### E19: Advanced Scheduling (AI-Assisted)

**User Story 1**
Title: AI-Based Shift Assignment Suggestions

User Story: As a SUPERVISOR I want AI to suggest optimal shift assignments So that staffing is efficient and fair.

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: AI Shift Suggestion
Given employee and shift data
When I request assignment suggestions
Then AI provides recommended assignments

Additional Details:
- Priority: Medium
- Story Points: 3
- Dependencies: E05
- Notes: Considers availability, skills, preferences

---

**User Story 2**
Title: Supervisor Override of AI Suggestions

User Story: As a SUPERVISOR I want to override AI suggestions So that I retain control over final assignments.

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Override AI Assignment
Given AI suggestions
When I make manual changes
Then overrides are applied and logged

Additional Details:
- Priority: Medium
- Story Points: 1
- Dependencies: Story 1
- Notes: Audit log for overrides

---

**User Story 3**
Title: AI Assignment Audit Trail

User Story: As an ADMIN I want an audit trail of AI-based assignments So that decisions are transparent.

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Audit AI Assignments
Given AI assignment history
When I review the audit trail
Then all suggestions and overrides are logged

Additional Details:
- Priority: Medium
- Story Points: 1
- Dependencies: Story 1, Story 2
- Notes: Includes rationale for suggestions
