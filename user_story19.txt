Title: Advanced Scheduling - AI Shift Suggestions

User Story: As a SCHEDULER I want the system to suggest optimal shift assignments based on demand and skills So that staffing is efficient.

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: AI shift suggestion
Given open shifts and employee data
When I request suggestions
Then the system proposes assignments based on demand, skills, and preferences

Additional Details:
- Priority: Low
- Story Points: 5
- Dependencies: E05, E07
- Notes: Suggestions can be accepted or overridden

Title: Advanced Scheduling - Preference-Based Scheduling

User Story: As a WORKER I want to set my shift preferences So that the system considers them in scheduling.

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Set shift preferences
Given I am a worker
When I set my preferred shifts
Then the AI considers my preferences in suggestions

Additional Details:
- Priority: Low
- Story Points: 2
- Dependencies: E05, E07
- Notes: Preferences editable anytime
