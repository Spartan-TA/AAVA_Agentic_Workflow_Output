Title: Attendance Report Generation

User Story: As a warehouse manager, I want to generate attendance reports so that I can review employee punctuality and absences.

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Generating attendance report
Given attendance data is available
When the manager requests a report
Then a summary of attendance, tardiness, and absences is generated

Additional Details:
- Priority: Medium
- Story Points: 3
- Dependencies: Time tracking module, reporting module
- Notes: Reports should support export to CSV
