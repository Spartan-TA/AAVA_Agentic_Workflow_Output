There once was a worker named Joe,
Who had a boss with a dramatic show.
In meetings, they'd clash,
Over budgets and cash,
But Joe mastered the art to go slow.

The interns all watched with delight,
As the cubicles buzzed through the night.
While memos would fly,
And egos would cry,
Office politics soared to new height.

But amidst all the paper and chatter,
Joe wondered what truly did matter.
He brewed up a plan,
Dodging power-hungry man,
Now he's the office peace-shatter!
