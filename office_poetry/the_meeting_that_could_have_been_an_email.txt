The Meeting That Could Have Been an Email

Oh, gather 'round the conference room,
A place of chatter, stress, and doom.
We sit and stare, our coffee cold,
As tales of metrics are retold.

The clock ticks slow, the time we steal,
For what could just have been an email.
The boss proclaims, "This must be heard!"
Yet no one's listening to his word.

Charts appear, and slides align,
Each one duller than the time.
"Collaborate!" they proudly hail,
But silence grips this quiet tale.

The questions asked are deep and grim,
"Can someone mute poor noisy Jim?"
And as we wrap, the hours wane,
We're left to ponder, "Was this in vain?"

So, next time when the calendar pings,
And meetings sprout their endless wings,
Remember, leaders, hear our plea:
Let emails reign, and set us free!
