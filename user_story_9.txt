Title: Soft Delete Employee Record

User Story: As an HR Manager I want to soft delete an employee record So that terminated employees are not visible but data is retained for compliance

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Employee soft delete
Given I am viewing an employee's details
When I choose to delete the employee
Then the employee is marked as inactive and hidden from active lists but remains in the database

Additional Details:
- Priority: Medium
- Story Points: 2
- Dependencies: Employee record creation
- Notes: Add 'active' flag to employee schema
