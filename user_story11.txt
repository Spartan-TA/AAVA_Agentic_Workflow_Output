Title: Payroll Export Integration
User Story: As a payroll administrator I want to generate payroll-ready files from approved attendance and leave so that manual processing and errors in payroll runs are reduced.
Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Payroll export generation
Given approved attendance and leave records
When I generate payroll exports
Then exports match provider schema, totals reconcile with reports, failed deliveries are retried with backoff, and audit log is created for every export.
Additional Details:
- Priority: High
- Story Points: 5
- Dependencies: Time & Attendance (Clock In/Out), Leave & Absence Management
- Notes: Secure delivery via SFTP/API; mapping to external payroll provider formats.
