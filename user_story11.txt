Title: Performance Review Notification

User Story: As an HR manager, I want to notify employees about upcoming performance reviews so that they are prepared.

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Sending review notifications
Given a performance review is scheduled
When the HR manager triggers notifications
Then employees receive review details and preparation guidelines

Additional Details:
- Priority: Medium
- Story Points: 2
- Dependencies: Notification system, employee database
- Notes: Notification should include review date and criteria
