Title: Leave Request and Approval

User Story: As an employee I want to request leave and have it approved or denied by my supervisor So that my time off is managed compliantly

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Leave workflow Given I submit a leave request When my supervisor reviews and approves/denies Then my leave balance updates and scheduled shifts are flagged

Additional Details:
- Priority: Medium
- Story Points: 3
- Dependencies: Employee CRUD, RBAC, Shift Management
- Notes: PTO, sick, unpaid leave supported; exports show approved leaves
