Title: Time & Attendance - Daily Totals Calculation
User Story: As a payroll manager, I want daily totals of hours worked to be automatically calculated so that payroll processing is accurate.
Acceptance Criteria:
  - Given clock-in/out events, When the day ends, Then total hours worked are computed for each employee.
  - Given overtime rules, When hours exceed thresholds, Then overtime is flagged.
Additional Details:
  Priority: High
  Story Points: 2
  Dependencies: Time & Attendance
  Notes: Supports export to CSV.
