Title: Payroll Export Integration

User Story: As a payroll administrator I want to generate payroll-ready files from approved attendance and leave data so that payroll runs are accurate and manual processing is reduced.

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Payroll export and delivery
  Given approved attendance and leave records
  When I generate a payroll export
  Then the export matches provider schema, totals reconcile with reports, failed deliveries are retried with backoff, and an audit log is created for every export

Additional Details:
- Priority: High
- Story Points: 7
- Dependencies: Time & Attendance (Clock In/Out), Leave & Absence Management
- Notes: Secure delivery via SFTP/API required; auditability is critical.
