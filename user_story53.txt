Title: Payroll Export - Failed Delivery Retry and Audit Log
User Story: As a payroll administrator, I want failed payroll export deliveries to be retried with backoff and every export to be logged for audit so that no data is lost and compliance is maintained.
Acceptance Criteria:
  Given a payroll export delivery fails
  When the system retries delivery
  Then retries use exponential backoff and all attempts are logged in the audit trail
Additional Details:
  Priority: High
  Story Points: 5
  Dependencies: E04, E06
  Notes: Audit log must include export details, delivery status, and timestamps.
