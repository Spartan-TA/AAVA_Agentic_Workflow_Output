### E06: Leave & Absence Management

**User Story 1**
Title: Leave Request Submission

User Story: As a WORKER I want to request PTO, sick, or unpaid leave So that my time off is tracked and approved.

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Submit Leave Request
Given available leave balance
When I submit a leave request
Then the request is created and pending approval

Additional Details:
- Priority: Medium
- Story Points: 2
- Dependencies: E02, E03, E05
- Notes: Accrual policies enforced

---

**User Story 2**
Title: Leave Approval Workflow

User Story: As a SUPERVISOR I want to approve or deny leave requests So that staffing plans remain accurate.

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Approve Leave
Given a pending leave request
When I approve or deny
Then the employee's leave balance updates accordingly

Additional Details:
- Priority: Medium
- Story Points: 2
- Dependencies: Story 1
- Notes: Approval triggers schedule coverage flag

---

**User Story 3**
Title: Leave Balance Tracking

User Story: As an HR I want to track leave accruals and balances So that compliance is maintained.

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Leave Balance Update
Given an approved leave
When the leave is processed
Then the employee's balance is updated

Additional Details:
- Priority: Medium
- Story Points: 1
- Dependencies: Story 1, Story 2
- Notes: Integration hooks for payroll and scheduling

---

**User Story 4**
Title: Leave Export Reporting

User Story: As an HR I want to export approved leaves for payroll So that time off is accurately reflected in payroll runs.

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Leave Export
Given approved leave records
When I request an export
Then the report includes all approved leaves

Additional Details:
- Priority: Medium
- Story Points: 1
- Dependencies: All leave stories
- Notes: Export format matches payroll requirements
