Title: Shift Attendance Confirmation

User Story: As a warehouse manager, I want to confirm employee attendance for each shift so that payroll and performance records are accurate.

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Confirming attendance
Given a shift has ended
When the manager reviews attendance records
Then attendance is confirmed and discrepancies are flagged

Additional Details:
- Priority: High
- Story Points: 2
- Dependencies: Time tracking module, shift scheduling module
- Notes: Attendance confirmation should integrate with payroll
