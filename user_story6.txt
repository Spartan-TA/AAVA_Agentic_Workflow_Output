Title: Leave Management

User Story: As an employee I want to request leave and view my leave balance So that I can plan my time off and ensure compliance with company policies

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Requesting leave
Given I am logged into the system
When I submit a leave request
Then my manager is notified and my leave balance is updated upon approval

Additional Details:
- Priority: High
- Story Points: 5
- Dependencies: Notification module, approval workflow
- Notes: Support for multiple leave types and accrual policies
