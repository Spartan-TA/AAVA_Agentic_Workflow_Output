Title: Logout

User Story Narrative:
As a logged-in user, I want to log out of my account so that I can ensure my session is secure.

Acceptance Criteria (Gherkin):
Given I am logged in,
When I click the "Logout" button,
Then I am logged out,
And redirected to the login page.

Additional Details:
- Priority: High
- Story Points: 1
- Dependencies: User Login
- Notes: Invalidate session token on logout.
