Title: Leave & Absence Management
User Story: As an employee I want to request and track leave (PTO, sick, unpaid) so that my time off is handled compliantly and my schedule is updated accurately.
Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Leave request and approval
Given I am logged in as an employee
When I request leave
Then supervisors can approve or deny, balances update, scheduled shifts are auto-flagged for coverage, and exports show approved leaves.
Additional Details:
- Priority: Medium
- Story Points: 5
- Dependencies: Employee Master Data (CRUD), Role Based Access Control (RBAC), Shift & Schedule Management
- Notes: Accrual balances and policies; integration hooks for scheduling and payroll exclusion.
