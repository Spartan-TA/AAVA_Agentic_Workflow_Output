Title: Leave & Absence Management

User Story: As an employee I want to request and track leave (PTO, sick, unpaid) so that my time off is handled compliantly and my schedule is updated accordingly.

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Leave request and approval
  Given I am an employee with available leave balance
  When I submit a leave request
  Then my supervisor can approve or deny the request, balances update, scheduled shifts are auto-flagged for coverage, and exports show approved leaves

Additional Details:
- Priority: Medium
- Story Points: 5
- Dependencies: Employee Master Data (CRUD), Role Based Access Control (RBAC), Shift & Schedule Management
- Notes: Accrual policies and integration hooks to scheduling and payroll must be supported.
