Title: Leave & Absence Management

User Story: As an employee I want to request PTO, sick, or unpaid leave So that my time off is tracked and staffing plans remain accurate.

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Leave request
Given I have available leave balance
When I submit a leave request
Then my supervisor can approve or deny it

Scenario: Leave balance update
Given my leave is approved
When the request is processed
Then my accrual balance is updated and scheduled shifts flagged for coverage

Additional Details:
- Priority: Medium
- Story Points: 8
- Dependencies: E02, E03, E05
- Notes: Integration hooks for scheduling/payroll; exports show approved leaves.
