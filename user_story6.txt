Title: Leave Management - Request PTO

User Story: As a WORKER I want to request paid time off So that my leave is tracked and approved.

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Submit PTO request
Given I have available PTO balance
When I submit a request
Then my supervisor is notified and the request is pending approval

Additional Details:
- Priority: Medium
- Story Points: 2
- Dependencies: E02, E03, E05
- Notes: Accrual balances update on approval

Title: Leave Management - Approve/Deny Leave

User Story: As a SUPERVISOR I want to approve or deny leave requests So that staffing is maintained.

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Approve leave request
Given a pending leave request
When I approve it
Then the employee's balance is updated and the shift is flagged for coverage

Additional Details:
- Priority: Medium
- Story Points: 2
- Dependencies: E02, E03, E05
- Notes: Denials notify employee

Title: Leave Management - Exclude from Scheduling

User Story: As an HR I want approved leaves to automatically exclude employees from scheduling So that coverage gaps are visible.

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Exclude from schedule
Given an approved leave
When the schedule is generated
Then the employee is not assigned to shifts during the leave period

Additional Details:
- Priority: Medium
- Story Points: 2
- Dependencies: E02, E03, E05
- Notes: Integration with shift management
