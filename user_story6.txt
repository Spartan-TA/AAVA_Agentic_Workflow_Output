Title: Leave & Absence Management

User Story: As an employee I want to request and track leave So that my time off is accurately recorded and approved according to policy

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Request leave
Given I have available leave balance
When I submit a leave request
Then the request is sent to my supervisor for approval

Scenario: Approve/deny leave
Given a pending leave request
When my supervisor reviews the request
Then they can approve or deny it and my balance updates accordingly

Scenario: Auto-flag scheduled shifts
Given approved leave overlaps with scheduled shifts
When the leave is approved
Then those shifts are flagged for coverage

Scenario: Export approved leaves
Given approved leave records exist
When I request an export
Then I receive a report showing all approved leaves

Additional Details:
- Priority: Medium
- Story Points: 5
- Dependencies: Employee Master Data CRUD APIs, Role Based Access Control (RBAC), Shift & Schedule Management
- Notes: Support PTO, sick, unpaid leave; accrual balances and policies; integration hooks for scheduling and payroll.
