Title: Row-Level Security for Supervisors

User Story: As a supervisor I want to only see and manage records for my team So that I do not access data outside my scope

Acceptance Criteria:
Scenario: Supervisor data access Given I am logged in as a supervisor When I access employee data Then only my team's records are visible and manageable

Additional Details:
- Priority: High
- Story Points: 2
- Dependencies: RBAC
- Notes: Row-level constraints enforced
